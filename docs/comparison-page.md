# The "Kaizena Flow compared with Shopify" page

Since 5 November 2026 the Shopify page is one of the six comparison pages made by one generator: **`docs/comparison-pages.md`** says how it
is made, what each line rests on and what to check before it is published. The facts about Shopify are in
`docs/comparison-evidence/shopify.md` (read on 4 and 5 November 2026), and no longer in `docs/parity/rows` alone.

  were not true yet (reviews, back-in-stock emails, pre-orders, tiered discounts, scheduled report emails, Vipps and MobilePay). The row was
  inserted already published by mistake and unpublished by SQL the same day.
- **29 October 2026:** published from the builder.
- **5 November 2026:** made again with the design of `docs/site-design.md` and what Flow does now, saved as the row's draft; the version

  claimed), and the reading of Shopify corrected three lines in Shopify's favour: its own VAT invoices for EU and UK orders, several stores
  grouped on any plan, and a weekly summary email.
