# Squarespace facts, re-checked 4 November 2026 (raw reads 23:52 to 00:01 UTC; the last listing read just after midnight is dated 5 November)

Method: each help-centre article's own text was downloaded from Squarespace's help centre (support.squarespace.com/api/v2/help_center/..., the same articles' raw text, so quotes are exact words). WebFetch was used on five pages as a cross-check (46757991027213, 360053592211, 215074868, 360002116567, squarespace.com/pricing); its summaries matched the raw text. Extension listings were read on squarespace.com/extensions. Forum posts (forum.squarespace.com) and sellers' own *.squarespace.com sites were ignored: they are not Squarespace's statements. "Edited" = the article's own last-updated date. No prices.

T1 Invoices and credit notes for store orders: native for a VAT receipt; credit notes unverified. Your order confirmation email and printed receipt can show your VAT number and a tax breakdown ("In many regions, your order confirmation email may need to include specific details, like a tax registration ID (VAT/GST) and a full tax breakdown, to serve as an invoice or receipt"), printed receipts are fixed ("It's not possible to customize or edit receipts"), and no page read describes invoice numbering or credit notes for shop orders (a refund sends an "Order refunded" email); the separate Invoices tool is for billing clients by hand. Source: https://support.squarespace.com/hc/en-us/articles/215074868 (edited 20 Mar 2026), https://support.squarespace.com/hc/en-us/articles/206540967 (edited 13 Mar 2026), https://support.squarespace.com/hc/en-us/articles/360049390031 (read 2026-11-04 by WebFetch for 215074868; others by download of the article text). same

Source: https://support.squarespace.com/hc/en-us/articles/206540707 (edited 26 Oct 2026; read 2026-11-04 by download of the article text; search by search excerpt). same

Source: https://support.squarespace.com/hc/en-us/articles/46757991027213 (edited 2 Nov 2026; read 2026-11-04 by WebFetch and by download of the article text). same (page re-edited 2 Nov 2026, same steps)

T5 EU VAT by destination and OSS: higher-plan (Core, Plus, Advanced; not Basic). Automated tax works out "EU and UK Value-Added Tax (VAT), including EU One-Stop Shop" once you add the places you are registered, for a per-order fee, and "Automated tax features are available on the Core, Plus, Advanced, Business, Commerce Basic and Commerce Advanced plans"; filing stays with you ("You're also responsible for ensuring your business collects, files, and pays the correct taxes"), and no OSS return or report is described. Source: https://support.squarespace.com/hc/en-us/articles/360053592211 (edited 28 Oct 2026; read 2026-11-04 by WebFetch and by download of the article text); pricing page row "Automated global tax rate calculations" shows Basic "Not included" (https://www.squarespace.com/pricing, read 2026-11-04 by WebFetch). same (but see Cautions: the plan guide lists it under Basic too)
T6 A business buyer's VAT number, reverse charge: not-possible-per-source. Squarespace's VAT guide says "It's not possible to exempt certain customers from paying VAT." (and the manual-rates guide: "It's not possible to exempt certain customers from paying sales tax."); the automated-tax guide says nothing about a buyer's VAT number or reverse charge. Source: https://support.squarespace.com/hc/en-us/articles/215074868 (edited 20 Mar 2026; read 2026-11-04 by WebFetch and by download of the article text), https://support.squarespace.com/hc/en-us/articles/206540947 (edited 22 Jul 2026). same (the sentence stands word for word; October's second quote "You can't create tax exemptions for specific customers" was not in the pages read today)

Source: https://support.squarespace.com/hc/en-us/articles/45175469686541 (edited 26 Oct 2026), https://support.squarespace.com/hc/en-us/articles/360034944391 (read 2026-11-04 by download of the article text). same
T8 Subscriptions (subscription products): native (Basic, Core, Plus, Advanced). You can sell physical or service products on repeat, but "You need to be connected to Squarespace Payments or Stripe. You can't sell subscription products using PayPal, Apple Pay, or ACH payments." and customer accounts must be on; download products can't be subscriptions. Source: https://support.squarespace.com/hc/en-us/articles/360002012247 (edited 30 Sep 2026; read 2026-11-04 by download of the article text). same
T9 Bundles: extension (Trunk); built-in bundles unverified. Source: https://www.squarespace.com/extensions/details/trunk (read 2026-11-05 by download), https://support.squarespace.com/hc/en-us/articles/21149527560205 (read 2026-11-04 by download of the article text). new topic (not in the Oct file)

T16 Product reviews: native (Basic, Core, Plus, Advanced). "Customer review request emails are available on the Basic, Core, Plus, and Advanced plans"; buyers get an email 14 days after you fulfil the order and can give one to five stars and a written review, but in-person sales get no request and you can't collect reviews of the business as a whole. Source: https://support.squarespace.com/hc/en-us/articles/4413194180365 (edited 28 Oct 2026; read 2026-11-04 by download of the article text). same
T17 Back-in-stock: higher-plan (Core, Plus, Advanced). "Waitlists are available in the Core, Plus, Advanced, Commerce Basic, and Commerce Advanced plans." and "Waitlists are only available for physical and service products."; shoppers leave an email on a sold-out product and get a message when it's back. Source: https://support.squarespace.com/hc/en-us/articles/360035947211 (edited 1 Oct 2026; read 2026-11-04 by download of the article text); pricing page shows Basic "Not included" for waitlists (read 2026-11-04 by WebFetch). same

## Strengths

- Design and editing: templates and drag-and-drop editing on every plan ("With all of our plans, you can use our drag-and-drop editing system to lay out your content"). Source: https://support.squarespace.com/hc/en-us/articles/29215717722637
- Reviews and subscriptions on the cheapest plan: review requests and subscription products are both on Basic, Core, Plus and Advanced. Source: https://support.squarespace.com/hc/en-us/articles/4413194180365, https://support.squarespace.com/hc/en-us/articles/360002012247
- EU VAT done for you from Core up: automated tax covers "EU and UK Value-Added Tax (VAT), including EU One-Stop Shop" (per-order fee). Source: https://support.squarespace.com/hc/en-us/articles/360053592211
- Appointments: Acuity Scheduling brings reminders, intake forms, deposits and several providers (a separate subscription). Source: https://support.squarespace.com/hc/en-us/articles/45175469686541
- AI across the editor: Clarkson AI chat edits pages, products, discounts, campaigns and contacts and summarises analytics (rolling out), alongside an AI site builder and Image Studio; Clarkson costs no credits. Source: https://support.squarespace.com/hc/en-us/articles/16282290976013, https://support.squarespace.com/hc/en-us/articles/46668956855053
- Email marketing in the same place: Email Campaigns sends blasts and automations from the site's own contacts (add-on subscription). Source: https://support.squarespace.com/hc/en-us/articles/360002077567

## Cautions

- Never write "Squarespace has no X" for any unverified topic: Omnibus 30-day price (T2), GPSR (T4), OSS returns, stays and rentals (T7), built-in bundles (T9), company accounts and price lists (T10), marketplace (T11), loyalty (T13), wishlists (T15), tiered pricing (T19), scheduled reports (T21), Vipps and MobilePay (T23), credit notes (T1). Say "not described in Squarespace's help centre (read 4 Nov 2026)" at most, and better leave them out.
- Only three topics rest on Squarespace's own "can't" words; quote them exactly and date them: T6 "It's not possible to exempt certain customers from paying VAT." (guide edited 20 Mar 2026), T18 "Squarespace doesn't have a dedicated pre-order feature" (edited 12 Oct 2026; the old October sentence is gone, so don't use it), and the US-only labels in T24.
- T6 is a sentence in the VAT guide, not a statement about reverse charge as such; the automated-tax guide (28 Oct 2026) is silent. Write "Squarespace's VAT guide says customers can't be exempted from VAT", not "Squarespace can't handle B2B".
- Plans conflict: the plan guide (29215717722637) lists "Automatic tax rate calculations" under Basic, while the tax guide and the pricing page say Core and up. Use "Core and higher" with the tax guide as source. Likewise the discounts guide's eligibility line does not name Basic, but the pricing page was not checked for discounts, so say nothing about discounts on Basic.
- Don't use squarespace.com/assets/pricing.txt: its plan descriptions (for example subscriptions and abandoned-cart recovery under Plus) contradict the help centre and the pricing page.
- T3: describe Squarespace's guide as advice to build a form-block button that the owner answers by hand; don't say Squarespace is non-compliant. The guide's sentence "As of June 19, 2026, sellers affected by the cooling-off period to create a prominent, easily accessible way…" is garbled as published; don't quote it. The article was edited 2 Nov 2026, so check it again before publishing.
- T22: Clarkson is "rolling out … in waves" and "may not be available on your site yet"; don't say every store has it, and don't say it runs on credits. Don't equate it with a store-operations agent.
- T25: the data page was last edited 4 May 2023; quote it with that date. Don't add that Squarespace has no EU hosting beyond what it says.
- T27: Squarespace Payments isn't open to sellers based in Norway, Sweden or Denmark, but those sellers can still take cards through another processor (Stripe, PayPal); don't write "Squarespace can't take card payments in Norway". Point of sale is US and UK only by Squarespace's words; don't write "no point of sale".
- T24: the Sendcloud listing doesn't name Bring, PostNord or other Nordic carriers; don't claim them through Sendcloud from Squarespace's pages.
- T26: don't promise a one-click Shopify import; Squarespace says to download Shopify's .csv and import it, and its CSV guide works from its own template.
- Extensions: name one listing, never a price, and say it's a third-party extension.
- Forum posts on forum.squarespace.com and sellers' sites on *.squarespace.com are not Squarespace's statements; nothing here rests on them.
- No prices; date every claim ("read 4 November 2026").
