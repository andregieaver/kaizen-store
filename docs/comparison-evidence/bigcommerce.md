T1 Legal invoices and credit notes for orders: native (printable invoices) + app (credit notes). BigCommerce has four editable invoice templates (email, merchant printable, customer printable, detailed merchant printable), and the detailed one is marked "Supported by UK, France, and Poland". The page says nothing about credit notes, sequential numbering, PDFs or VAT on invoices. For credit notes there is the third-party app Sufio Invoices: "Create other types of documents like pro forma invoices, credit notes and quotes." Source: https://docs.bigcommerce.com/docs/store-operations/orders/invoice-templates ; https://www.bigcommerce.com/apps/sufio-automatic-invoices/ (read 2026-11-04 by WebFetch). same

T2 30-day lowest price shown on a reduction (Omnibus): app. A third-party app, Omnibus Pricing by Sniffie, will "Show the 30-day lowest price when products are discounted to comply with the EU Omnibus Directive". BigCommerce's own pages that I read show no built-in way to do this. Source: https://www.bigcommerce.com/apps/omnibus-pricing (read 2026-11-04 by WebFetch). same

## Strengths (where BigCommerce is honestly the better fit)

- Headless builds: Catalyst is BigCommerce's own open-source (MIT) storefront on Next.js, React and the GraphQL Storefront API, for a team that wants to own its front end. Source: https://docs.bigcommerce.com/developer/docs/storefront/catalyst/overview
- Several brands or regions from one back end: extra storefronts can be added on every plan, capped at 3, 5 or 8 (Performance by arrangement), with "one backend" for all of them. Source: https://www.bigcommerce.com/pricing/ ; https://www.bigcommerce.com/solutions/multi-store/
- Deep B2B on its top plan: B2B Edition, included with new Performance stores, gives company hierarchies with roles, quotes, purchase orders and net terms per company account, plus SKU-level Price Lists. Source: https://www.bigcommerce.com/b2b-edition/ ; https://support.bigcommerce.com/articles/en_US/Knowledge/B2B-Edition-User-Guide ; https://www.bigcommerce.com/pricing/
- A free, large migration: the Data Migration App moves products, customers and orders from Shopify and others for free, up to 500,000 records. Source: https://www.bigcommerce.com/migration/
- Wide audited security credentials: PCI DSS v4.0 Level 1 as merchant and service provider, plus a long ISO and SOC list. Source: https://security.bigcommerce.com/
- Pre-orders and quantity price tiers built in, with no app needed. Source: https://support.bigcommerce.com/articles/en_US/Knowledge/Product-Availability ; https://support.bigcommerce.com/articles/en_US/Knowledge/Possible-Discounts-Offer

## Cautions (what the page must not say)

- No prices. BigCommerce's pricing page is in USD; do not quote, convert or compare amounts. The October file's plan prices, auto-upgrade by sales and "Open Payment Provider Fee" were not re-read today, so do not state them.
- Withdrawal: say only "BigCommerce's own blog post of 1 July 2026 says it 'doesn't offer a native withdrawal function yet'", give its date, and add that the post says BigCommerce is "actively exploring" it. Do not say BigCommerce stores cannot comply. The 15 June 2026 Help Center thread was not readable, so re-check both before publishing.
- Do not say GPSR, OSS/IOSS, native VIES checks, native bundles, native scheduled reports or native Nordic carriers are unavailable. They are unverified, and the page should say "not verified" or leave them out.
- "App" is not "impossible". Name the vendor as listed in BigCommerce's App Marketplace, say it is a third party, and treat what the listing says as the vendor's own claim.
- B2B: do not say company accounts, price lists or payment terms come on every plan. Price Lists are on Performance only, and B2B Edition is "included with new Performance stores or activated in existing stores" (which plans can activate it is not stated). Customer groups are Growth and up, not Core.
- Multi-storefront is a paid add-on per storefront with a cap on Core, Growth and Scale; do not call it free or unlimited.
- Abandoned-cart emails are not on Core; do not say "every plan".
- Vipps works through Adyen only. MobilePay needs a Danish or Finnish billing address and does not take recurring payments. Do not say BigCommerce "has no Vipps".
- A printable invoice is not evidence of EU-legal numbering or credit notes, and BigCommerce's page settles neither. Do not call its invoices non-compliant either.
- Data location: do not claim or deny EU data residency, because the trust centre states no region.
- Avalara's listing says "VAT" and "VAT reporting" but not OSS or IOSS. Do not credit or deny OSS/IOSS through it.
- Companion: do not list "issues store credit" as a feature (it appears only as an example prompt). It is included at no extra cost, so do not call it a paid add-on.
- Migration: say "up to 500,000 records" carefully, because the same page's FAQ says "products". Do not say it migrates reviews or coupons (not on the page today).
- Name: the trust centre is branded "Commerce.com", and other pages show Commerce.com only in links. The platform is still called BigCommerce on its pages, so use "BigCommerce".
- Fetch summaries can misquote. Have a person check every quoted sentence against the live page on the day the comparison is published.
