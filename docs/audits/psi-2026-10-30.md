## What the review found that needs action at once

- **Kaizena Flow's own privacy notice, terms and DPA are not published.** `/privacy` serves a page with no text (one row, last changed
  5 October), and `/terms` and `/data-processing-agreement` are 404. The writer exists (Platform → Pages, *Write the legal pages*,
  `src/server/platform-legal.ts`) and has never been run on production. Publishing is the owner's decision; it is the largest single
  Privacy gain (REG-02, and REG-12 through the DPA stores need).
- **Events that stop arriving are not noticed.** The `webhooks` live drill found Stripe events had not reached production for 13 days
  while `/status` said Payments was up. A check that turns Payments red when no event has arrived for N hours while payments are made,
  and the drill on a schedule, would have caught it (REL-09, REL-02, DATA-03).
- **The gating finding stands**: no restore drill is recorded (`docs/runbooks/restore.md`; postponed by the owner for cost,
  `docs/consolidation.md`), so Reliability stays capped at 40 (about +2.4 overall when lifted).
- **Direct deploys are not gated by the type check.** D378 turned the type check off on Vercel, and a push without a migration deploys at
  once, so the "Decided" reason that a deploy is safe because Vercel type-checks is no longer true (REL-03, CODE-07).
- **D390 lowered contrast in dark mode** on Kaizena Flow's front page (headings 2.6–2.8:1) and pricing page (filled button 3.66:1).
