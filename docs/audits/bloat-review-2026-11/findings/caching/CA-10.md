# CA-10 ✓ Pages are rebuilt every hour and dropped after a quiet day, though every write already refreshes a tag

Caching and rendering (`README.md` beside this file), part of `docs/audits/bloat-review-2026-11.md`.

- **Kind:** eager. **Priority:** P2. **Effort:** S. **Confidence:** medium. **Step:** 2.15.
- **Second check:** confirmed.
- **Where:** `src/server/stores.ts:218`, `src/server/pages.ts:666`, `src/server/platform-navigation.ts:77`, `src/server/campaign-notices.ts:28`, `src/server/store-writers.scan.test.ts:1`, `next.config.ts:41`, `node_modules/next/dist/docs/01-app/03-api-reference/04-functions/cacheLife.md:124`, `node_modules/next/dist/docs/01-app/03-api-reference/04-functions/cacheLife.md:176`, `.next/prerender-manifest.json`
- **Evidence:** Re-counted in this pass: 70 of 85 cached reads use cacheLife('hours'): revalidate after 1 hour, expire after 1 day (Next's table, cacheLife.md:138-146). 82 of 85 also carry a tag and 407 call sites refresh tags. The manifest shows revalidate 3600, expire 86400 on 538 of 772 prerendered routes; 6 are at 900 (the demo home and All products, because two reads use the default profile: campaign-notices.ts:28 and :183), 2 at 60, 226 never. 251 docs routes are among the 538: their words change only with a deploy, yet they inherit the hour from the platform's menu read (platform-navigation.ts:76-78). After an hour the next visit starts a background render; Next's doc on expire: 'after this period with no traffic, the server regenerates content synchronously on the next request'. A page's own period is the shortest of its reads. Only writers of commerce.stores are held to their tag by a scan test (store-writers.scan.test.ts).
- **Cost today:** Up to 24 background renders a day per visited page for content that did not change, each re-running the page's cached reads on a cold instance, and a blocking render for the first visitor after a quiet day. With many small stores, most pages are seen less than once a day, so most of their views would be that blocking render. Not counted in production. Paid by: The server and the database; the first shopper after a quiet day.
- **Saving:** Estimate: background renders fall from hourly to on change for those pages, and a quiet page stays served for 30 days instead of 1. Small while deploys reset everything several times a day (CA-2).
- **Risk:** A writer that forgets its tag leaves a page old for a week, not an hour: hence the scans first. A read with a clock in it moved by mistake shows a stale price or notice: step 5's list and a review of each moved read guard it. Noticed by e2e and by owners seeing an old page after a save.

## The fix

1. Hold the writers first. New scan tests modelled on src/server/store-writers.scan.test.ts: a module that writes commerce.pages refreshes pagesTag (or is on a list that may only shrink); the same for commerce.platform_settings and commerce.menus with PLATFORM_NAVIGATION_TAG and the store's menu tag.
2. next.config.ts: add a named profile beside cacheComponents: cacheLife: { tagged: { stale: 300, revalidate: 604800, expire: 2592000 } } (custom profiles: cacheLife.md:176-200). A comment says it is only for reads whose every writer refreshes a tag and that hold nothing that moves with the clock.
3. Move the platform's reads first: loadSettings (platform-navigation.ts:76-78), getPlatformPageRoles, getPlatformSeo, enabledLanguages, the platform's text styles, siteTerms(null, …), publishedPageIndex(null) from CA-3. Build and check that docs routes show the new period in the manifest.
4. Then the store's clock-free reads whose writers the scans hold: loadStore (stores.ts:216-218), siteLayoutFor, textStylesFor, siteTerms, the page reads.
5. Leave on 'hours' or shorter everything that moves with the clock: catalogue and listing reads (the 30-day reference price in commerce.current_prices changes with time alone), campaign notices, VAT labels, afterSaleOpenCached, the status page, demo and sandbox reads, redirect lookups, and anything shown by the clock (D340), which relies on the dispatcher refreshing its store's tag.

**Done when:** After a build, the docs routes that show initialRevalidateSeconds 3600 in .next/prerender-manifest.json (251 of the 538 routes at 3600 today, counted in this pass) show 604800, and the new scan tests pass.

**Tests and rules that hold today's behaviour, and change with it:**

- src/server/store-writers.scan.test.ts is the pattern; the new scans join the 22 *.scan.test.ts files in src/server.
- docs/rules/storefront.md:112-114 (Catalogue reads are cached) and the price rule in CLAUDE.md (advertised reductions use prior_30d_minor): the reason catalogue reads keep the hour; write it beside the profile.
- e2e specs that change a page or the platform's menu and read the site (menus.spec.ts, legal-pages.spec.ts, the page builder's signed-in specs) show a writer that forgot its tag: the page would stay old.
- CA-16's baseline records each route's revalidate period, so a read that falls back to a short profile is seen.

**Comes after:** CA-16 (records the periods); CA-3 (the page index read that moves)

**Notes:** The part that grows after opening is the quiet-day blocking render: many small stores means many rarely seen pages. A page gains only when every read it uses has moved, so start where the set is small (the docs and the platform's site).
