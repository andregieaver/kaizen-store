- **Evidence:** Of 1,648 selects assigned to a const, 442 carry a limit and 387 return a list with no limit, no id or any() filter and no aggregate (the verified heuristic). Most are bounded by their parent (an order's lines, a store's locations). The ten that are bounded only by how much a store has: pages.ts:136 (every page with both documents, for the admin list); pages.ts:668 (DQ-2); knowledge.ts:170 (DQ-6); accessibility.ts:120; green-claims.ts:46-55 (every active product's texts and every published page); store-translate.ts:125; analytics-speed.ts:116 (up to 28 days times 10,000 samples); activity.ts:155 (select distinct action over the audit log the viewer may see, to fill a filter); theme-store.ts:176 (every version's snapshot); owner-assistant.ts:234-235 (select * of every message and approval of a conversation, which I opened). The storefront's own unbounded read, listProducts() (catalog.ts:284), is code review step 3.9. Rows loaded only to be counted are rare: 6 hits.
- **Cost today:** Small today. Each of the ten returns rows in proportion to one store's whole content, so a large store makes its own screens slow. None costs anything for a store that does not open the screen, apart from the two already carried by DQ-2 and DQ-6. Paid by: An owner on the Pages list, the accessibility and claims screens and the activity filter; the server's memory.
- **Saving:** Estimate: these reads stop growing with a store's content; nothing measurable at today's sizes.
- **Risk:** Paging changes what a list shows at once; it follows the shared list pattern of step 1.13. A conversation that shows only its newest 200 messages must make the older ones reachable, or an owner loses sight of earlier work: the test of step 4 holds the order and the 'older' load.
- **Already tracked:** partly: code review step 1.13 'One way to search and page a list' (Open) covers paging admin lists; step 0.9 (Done) paged the storefront's listings

## The fix

1. The admin's Pages list: the slim columns of DQ-10 step 3, and paging with the shared list pattern when code review step 1.13 ('One way to search and page a list') is built; do not invent a second paging here.
2. The speed report: the percentile in SQL (DQ-10 step 5).
3. activity.ts:153-158: bound the distinct to the period the list shows (or the last 90 days), so the audit log's time index serves it instead of a pass over every row the viewer may see.
4. owner-assistant.ts:233-236: name the columns, read the newest 200 messages first (order by created_at desc, id desc limit 200, reversed in code) and load older ones when the owner scrolls up; approvals of those messages only.
5. theme-store.ts:176: the notes per version of DQ-10 step 2.
6. Leave accessibility.ts, green-claims.ts and store-translate.ts as they are: they need the content, and their cost follows the owner who opens them.
