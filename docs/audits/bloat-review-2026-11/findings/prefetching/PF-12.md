# PF-12 The first add to cart of a visit throws the router's whole prefetch cache away: 20 more requests on a phone, at the moment the shopper buys

Prefetching: what the router asks for unasked (`README.md` beside this file), part of `docs/audits/bloat-review-2026-11.md`.

- **Kind:** eager. **Priority:** P2. **Effort:** S. **Confidence:** high. **Step:** 2.3.
- **Second check:** added by the planner, not checked a second time.
- **Where:** `src/server/cart.ts:698-726`, `src/app/s/[store]/[market]/cart/actions.ts:120-142`, `node_modules/next/dist/server/app-render/action-handler.js:123-129`, `node_modules/next/dist/client/components/router-reducer/reducers/server-action-reducer.js:213-233`, `node_modules/next/dist/client/components/segment-cache/cache.js:270-274`, `node_modules/next/dist/client/components/links.js:285-306`, `src/server/b2b.ts:46`, `src/server/customers.ts:416`, `src/components/buyer.tsx:37,88,122`
- **Evidence:** Missed by the report, which read the mechanism for the admin and did not measure it. A cart is made on the first add, and making it sets the cart's cookie (cart.ts:698-726). Next answers a server action that changed a cookie as one that revalidated static and dynamic data (action-handler.js:123-126), and the router then evicts the entire prefetch cache and asks again for every link in view (server-action-reducer.js:228-229; cache.js:270-274; links.js:285-306); Next's own comment there says the eviction is wider than it should be. Measured by the planner twice, with the same numbers, on /s/demo/no/p/demo-handlenett on a phone at the top of the page (work/pfplan/addcart2.mjs): the load made 12 router prefetch requests (40.3 KB on the wire); the first press of Add to cart made 32 more request events, 20 of them on the network (28.9 KB), and its answer carried x-action-revalidated: 1. The second press carried 2 (dynamic only) and asked for nothing. At 1440x900 the first add asked again for 12 links (one of them inside PF-1's loop). The same eviction follows the buyer switch (b2b.ts:46 sets a cookie, and buyer.tsx then calls router.refresh() too), a customer's sign-in (customers.ts:416), and every admin save whose action revalidates.
- **Cost today:** Once per visit that adds to a cart: 20 requests on the network on a phone at the top of a product page, more with the footer in view (every link in view is asked again). Each is a proxy run, and they share the phone's connection with the action's own refresh of the page. Paid by: The shopper's browser at the moment of buying; the proxy and the server per request. It grows with carts started.
- **Saving:** Estimate from the measured list: from 32 request events (20 on the network) to the cart link's own, 7 events in my run (its tree, the market layout, its default and the drawer page, some asked twice).
- **Risk:** None of its own: nothing changes but a test and a sentence.

## The fix

1. No change to the cart: the cookie has to be set when the cart is made (D14: the one cookie a cart has), and the eviction is Next's. The cost is the number of links in view, so PF-2, PF-3 and PF-7 remove most of it: on a phone only the cart link keeps viewport prefetch.
2. e2e/prefetch.spec.ts: add 'the first add to cart asks for little again': on a phone at the top of /s/demo/no/p/demo-handlenett, count router prefetch requests in the 4 s after the first press of the add button: at most 10 (32 today). A second press: 0.
3. docs/performance.md, 'What the router fetches ahead': one sentence that a server action which sets a cookie or revalidates makes the router ask again for every link in view, so the count of viewport-prefetched links is also the cost of every such action, in the store and in the admin.

**Done when:** e2e/prefetch.spec.ts 'the first add to cart asks for little again' passes: at most 10 router prefetch request events after the first add on a phone (32 today, 20 of them on the network).

**Tests and rules that hold today's behaviour, and change with it:**

- e2e/cart.spec.ts, e2e/shop-components.spec.ts (Add to cart on tiles, D557), e2e/b2b.spec.ts (the buyer switch) and e2e/account.spec.ts (sign-in) exercise the actions: behaviour is unchanged.
- D14 (the cart's one strictly necessary cookie) stays as it is.

**Comes after:** PF-1; PF-3

**Notes:** Not worth a change of its own beyond the budget case: minting the cart outside a server action (a route handler) would be a larger change for 20 requests once a visit. Worth reporting upstream with PF-1: Next's TODO at server-action-reducer.js:223-227 describes the narrower eviction.
