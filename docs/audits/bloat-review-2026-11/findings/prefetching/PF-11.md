# PF-11 Links that leave their root layout are fetched ahead, and pressing them loads a whole document anyway: a multi-country store's front door fetches every country's home page for nothing

Prefetching: what the router asks for unasked (`README.md` beside this file), part of `docs/audits/bloat-review-2026-11.md`.

- **Kind:** eager. **Priority:** P0. **Effort:** S. **Confidence:** high. **Step:** 0.12.
- **Second check:** added by the planner, not checked a second time.
- **Where:** `src/app/s/[store]/(chooser)/page.tsx:94-104,119-125`, `src/app/s/[store]/(chooser)/layout.tsx`, `src/app/s/[store]/[market]/layout.tsx:88-89,147`, `src/components/platform-layout.tsx:133,176,273`, `src/components/platform-routes/home.tsx:51,55`, `src/components/site-parts.tsx:237-245`, `src/app/admin/(gated)/[store]/layout.tsx:132`, `node_modules/next/dist/docs/01-app/03-api-reference/03-file-conventions/layout.md:142-145`, `node_modules/next/dist/client/components/router-reducer/is-navigating-to-new-root-layout.js:12`
- **Evidence:** Missed by the report. The app has 14 root layouts (layout.tsx files that draw <html>, and platform-root.tsx:65 for the platform groups): a store's front door (s/[store]/(chooser)) and its market pages (s/[store]/[market]) are two of them, the admin a third, Kaizena Flow's own pages a fourth. Next's docs: navigating across root layouts causes a full page load (layout.md:145). The links between them are <Link>s all the same, so they are prefetched. Measured by the planner at 1440x900. The demo store's front door /s/demo: 12 router requests for its 3 country links, 254 KB decoded, 58.6 KB on the wire (each: the route tree, the market layout 54.7 KB, the home page 28.8 KB, the drawer's default); all 12 answered STALE. Pressing Denmark then loaded a document (21,354 B) and 56 more requests: nothing fetched ahead was used. Kaizena Flow's home page /: 8 router requests for 4 links, 4 of them for /admin and the demo store; pressing /admin loaded a document (6,256 B; 38 requests after the press), while pressing /sign-up, in the same root layout, took 4 requests. The admin's View store link: 4 requests and 62 KB decoded on the admin home (local only: in production the store is on another host). On a store's own host the front door's links are /no and /dk on the same origin, so production pays this; the front door is the x-default address of every page of a store with several countries (market layout.tsx:89).
- **Cost today:** Per visit to a multi-country store's front door: 4 requests and about 85 KB decoded per country, none usable (demo: 12 and 254 KB; ten countries would be 40 and about 850 KB, by multiplication). Each is a proxy run and, cold or stale, a render of that country's layout and home page. Per page view of Kaizena Flow's own pages: 2 wasted requests for /admin, 2 more where a store is linked. Paid by: The shopper's browser at the front door; the proxy and the server per request; visitors of kaizenaflow.com. It grows with stores that sell in several countries.
- **Saving:** Measured: 12 requests, 254 KB decoded and 58.6 KB on the wire per visit to the demo store's front door, with up to 3 cold or stale renders; 4 of 8 requests per view of Kaizena Flow's home page.
- **Risk:** Low. Nothing fetched there was ever used: the press is the same document load before and after. A wrong edit would show as a front door or sign-in link that no longer navigates.

## The fix

1. src/app/s/[store]/(chooser)/page.tsx:96 and :121: add prefetch={false} to both <Link>s (or draw plain anchors: the press is a document load either way).
2. src/components/platform-layout.tsx:133, 176 and 273 (the links to /admin) and src/components/platform-routes/home.tsx:51 (the demo store) and :55 (/admin): add prefetch={false}.
3. src/components/site-parts.tsx:241 (the house store's sign-up link, which leads into the platform's root layout): add prefetch={false}.
4. src/app/admin/(gated)/[store]/layout.tsx:132 (View store): add prefetch={false}.
5. e2e/prefetch.spec.ts: add 'the front door asks for nothing ahead' (/s/demo makes 0 router prefetch requests in the 3 s after load; 12 today) and 'the site does not fetch the admin or a store ahead' (/ makes none for /admin or /s/demo and at most 4 in all; 8 today).
6. In PF-10's policy text: a link that leaves its root layout is policy none, with the list of root layouts (the two (platform) groups, the two (docs) groups, the two (theme-preview) groups, admin, s/[store]/(chooser), s/[store]/[market], s/[store]/private/[market], holding, passport/[id], sms, supply).

**Done when:** e2e/prefetch.spec.ts passes both cases: 0 router prefetch requests on /s/demo (12 today) and at most 4 on / with none for /admin or a store (8 today, 4 of them wasted).
