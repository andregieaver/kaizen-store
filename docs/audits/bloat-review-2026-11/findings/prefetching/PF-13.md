# PF-13 A real listing prefetches every product tile, and up to six swatch links under each: nothing bounds it, and the test stores are too small to show it

Prefetching: what the router asks for unasked (`README.md` beside this file), part of `docs/audits/bloat-review-2026-11.md`.

- **Kind:** eager. **Priority:** P2. **Effort:** S. **Confidence:** medium. **Step:** 2.4.
- **Second check:** added by the planner, not checked a second time.
- **Where:** `src/components/product-card.tsx:68-77,96-118`, `src/components/product-listing.tsx:122-128,250-266`, `src/server/listing.ts:59,207`, `src/server/shop.ts:93-101`, `src/app/s/[store]/[market]/blog/blog-listing.tsx:58`, `src/app/s/[store]/[market]/term-listing.tsx:174`
- **Evidence:** Missed by the report because the test data cannot show it. The demo and test stores have 10 products each (counted in the local database), and the demo's /products and home pages are built pages whose tiles are plain anchors (PF-4), so no measurement of this review saw a real listing. A category, tag, brand or related-products listing draws ProductCard, whose title is a <Link> with no policy (product-card.tsx:74), up to 48 a page (LISTING_LIMIT, listing.ts:59), and Show more adds 48 at a time up to 20 pages (listing.ts:207). Measured by the planner per product link (the three in the header's menu, on /s/demo/no/products at 1440x900): 2 requests, the route tree (1,035 B decoded, 2.4 KB on the wire) and the product page's shell (10.1 to 10.5 KB decoded, 5.1 KB on the wire). A combined listing (D566) adds up to 6 swatch <Link>s under each card (product-card.tsx:96-118), each to another product page. Only the template store is built ahead (shop.ts:93-101), so the first ask of each product in each view renders its shell (75 to 78 ms cold, the skeptic's measure) and a stale one renders in the background (s-maxage 3,600 s).
- **Cost today:** Estimate by multiplication, not measured: a 48-tile listing read to the end is 96 requests, about 360 KB on the wire and 550 KB decoded, and up to 48 shell renders when the store has been quiet for an hour; with swatches up to 288 more links. Filter chips and Show more cost nothing (checked, see clean). Paid by: The shopper's browser on every listing; the proxy and the server (a render per product seen on a quiet store). It grows with stores times catalogue size, which is what opening brings.
- **Saving:** Swatches: all of their prefetches until one is pointed at (estimate; no combined listing in the test data). Tiles: none yet; the number becomes visible and held, and the spike decides.
- **Risk:** Low. A swatch pressed at once may wait one round trip. The seeded category makes the e2e database a little larger.
