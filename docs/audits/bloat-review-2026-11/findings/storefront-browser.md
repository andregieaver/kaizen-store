# What a shopper's browser loads (SC)

Part of `docs/audits/bloat-review-2026-11.md`, on commit 0bd814c. One reviewer's reading of this area. A tick (✓) marks a finding the lead checked a second time; the others were not checked. "Step" is the step of the plan that closes the finding.

**In short.** The storefront is leaner than the rest of the platform, but not lean. A cold home page sends 680 KB of scripts (213 KB gzipped); 63 % of that is React and Next themselves, and the store's own code is about 80 KB gzipped, with no zod, no whole-language texts, no third-party script and no large object passed into client components. The bloat is in how the rest is grouped: scripts are bundled per route, never per feature, so a store with every optional feature off downloads the same code as one with all on; eight working routes (listing, search, cart, checkout, account and more) share one 54-module bundle; about 16 KB gzipped of admin words and catalogues ride on every page; and after load each page spends another 33 to 190 KB prefetching pages nobody asked for. Chromium found 57 to 70 % of the script bytes loaded on a storefront page never ran, and the budgets meant to stop growth have 150 to 180 KB of slack and cover one storefront route besides home.

## Well made, and should stay

- No large object crosses into client components: the inline payloads of the home, product and category pages hold none of the Store's fields (paymentsOn, setupCompletedAt, customCss, localization, markets: 0 hits each); props are small labels and ids.
- The extras are server-gated and lazy: chat widget, consent manager, owner's code, referral capture, country hint and first-visit keeper are drawn only when the store has them (src/app/s/[store]/[market]/extras.tsx, src/components/site-chat.tsx, store-affiliate.tsx, consent/site-consent.tsx) and load as their own chunks, the chat and referral capture only when idle (src/components/lazy/extras.tsx). On the demo store none of their chunks is requested.
- Motion runtime: a 702-byte stub; the runtime is fetched only when the page has motion that needs it in this browser (src/components/motion-runtime.tsx).
- 3D viewer loads only on the press of View in 3D (src/components/product-gallery.tsx:341-357); web-vitals is a dynamic import after load and only with visit counting on (src/components/speed-beacon.tsx).
- No third-party script, font or beacon on a storefront page by default: 0 third-party requests on the 12 measured pages; fonts are self-hosted, kept for a year, preloaded and trimmed (docs/performance.md, Fonts found late); the demo store loads no font file.
- zod, the page model and whole-language texts stay out of the visitor's scripts (src/lib/storefront-bundle.scan.test.ts, e2e/performance.spec.ts): no such module appeared in the module-by-module split of the home, product or category chunks.
- The framework chunks hold only React 19 and Next's router (446 KB raw / 133 KB gzip); no app code leaked into them.
- Product tiles in content grids are plain anchors, never prefetched, and their stock and Add to cart come from one batched fetch with a lazy chunk (src/components/content-grid.tsx:147,310; TileShop in src/components/lazy/blocks.tsx).
- Visit and speed beacons are drawn only while the owner has visit counting on (src/components/store-visits.tsx:12); nothing was sent on the demo store.
- Weighed and left: the browser texts of all four languages load together (24,831 raw / 7,186 gzip, three of four unused; the withdraw page's four languages 31,804 raw). docs/performance.md D212 says a per-language load made the page wait and lost presses; sending one language inline instead would cost about 6 KB uncached on every page view, which is worse for a visitor of three or more pages.
- Weighed and left: filter-dialog (11,773 raw) and site-form (6,348) are in every built page's first scripts by the written rule that a form or filter pressed before its script runs is lost (docs/performance.md, lines 38-51).

## Findings

### SC-1 ✓ Every page prefetches the pages behind its links, including language and currency views nobody opens

- **Kind:** eager. **Effort:** S. **Confidence:** high. **Step:** 0.9.
- **Where:** `src/components/view-menu.tsx:56`, `src/components/store-layout.tsx:234`, `src/components/store-layout.tsx:265`, `src/components/store-layout.tsx:279`, `src/components/store-layout.tsx:491`, `src/components/cart-link.tsx:53`, `next.config.ts:41`, `node_modules/next/dist/docs/01-app/02-guides/adopting-partial-prefetching.md:74`, `evidence/routes.json`
- **Evidence:** Review's browser run (routes.json, phone, cold cache): home fires 11 RSC prefetches (32,830 bytes) and then 3 cart-drawer script chunks (24,402 bytes) after load; the content page /om-oss 34 prefetches (143,036 bytes); the empty cart 42 (184,408). Each language or currency link in view costs 3 requests and about 17,600 bytes (85,000 decoded): 70,459 bytes for 4 links on /om-oss, 104,635 for 6 on the cart. ViewMenu's link has href = the other view's front page, but its click handler calls preventDefault and pushes the current page in that view (view-menu.tsx:56-72), so the prefetched front page is used only when the shopper already is on the front page. Next's guide says that without partialPrefetching a Link prefetches the destination's cached render per link; next.config.ts does not set the option and next start warns about it (m/start.log).
- **Cost today:** 33 to 190 KB transferred and 11 to 50 requests per page view after load: 14 % of the home page's 406,542 bytes, 26 % of the content page's, 31 % of the cart's. Prefetches of per-visitor pages (cart, account, wishlist, search) also reach a function. Paid by: A shopper's browser (data, battery); the server per request for the prefetched per-visitor pages
- **Fix:** 1. prefetch={false} on the language, currency and country links (ViewMenu, LanguageLinks, MarketChoice): their href is never what a click opens. 2. prefetch={false} on the footer's cookies, withdrawal and legal links and on account and wishlist. 3. The cart link without prefetch (see SC-7). 4. Plan partialPrefetching: true as its own step with Next's adoption guide (one shared shell per route; Next makes it the default in the next major), with a test for the product link.
- **Saving:** Measured on this build: 70 to 105 KB and 12 to 18 requests per page view where the footer's language and currency links are in view. Estimate from the same file: about 57 KB and 14 requests on the home page once cart, account, wishlist and search stop prefetching.
- **Risk:** Low for steps 1 to 3: the first press of those links waits for the server instead of showing a prefetched shell. Medium for partialPrefetching: it changes what a navigation shows at once, so it needs its own tests.
