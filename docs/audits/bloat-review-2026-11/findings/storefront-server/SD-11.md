# SD-11 ✓ The same shopper facts are read several times in one request

What the server does for a storefront request (`README.md` beside this file), part of `docs/audits/bloat-review-2026-11.md`.

- **Kind:** duplicate. **Priority:** P0. **Effort:** S. **Confidence:** high. **Step:** 0.22.
- **Second check:** confirmed.
- **Where:** `src/components/store-layout.tsx:287`, `src/components/store-layout.tsx:583`, `src/components/site-parts.tsx:179`, `src/components/cart-link.tsx:26`, `src/server/cart.ts:544-556`, `src/server/customers.ts:454-463`, `src/components/product-parts.tsx:935`, `src/components/product-parts.tsx:946`, `src/components/product-parts.tsx:1006`, `src/server/express-checkout.ts:63`, `src/server/visibility.ts:62`, `src/server/request-memo.ts:13-20`
- **Evidence:** The cart link is drawn twice (the header at store-layout.tsx:287 or site-parts.tsx:179, and the phone bar at :583) and each runs getCartCount() (cart-link.tsx:26, cart.ts:544-556), so a shopper with a cart costs two identical counts on every page. getCustomer() runs a session query on each call (customers.ts:454-463); on a product page a signed-in shopper meets it in the member notice (:935), the buy part (:1006) and the express check (express-checkout.ts:63), and also at :946 when a variant is sold out. perRequest() exists (request-memo.ts) and the cart, checkout and order pages use it; visibility rules keep their own copy of the customer under their own key (visibility.ts:62). Read from the code; not measured with cookies.
- **Cost today:** 1 extra statement per view for a shopper with a cart; 2 to 4 extra on a product page for a signed-in shopper. It grows with real shoppers, who do not exist yet. Paid by: The database per request; the shoppers closest to buying.
- **Saving:** 1 statement per view for a shopper with a cart and 2 to 4 per product view for a signed-in shopper.
- **Risk:** Low. React's cache is per render, so nothing is shared between requests or between an action and the render after it.

## The fix

1. getCustomer(storeId) (src/server/customers.ts:454-463) runs its read through perRequest(`customer:${storeId}`, ...). Outside a render nothing is shared (request-memo.ts:9-11), so an action that signs someone in or out and reads again is not affected.
2. getCartCount(shop) (src/server/cart.ts:544-556) the same, keyed by store and market.
3. src/server/visibility.ts:62 calls getCustomer() directly and drops its own key.

**Done when:** With a cart cookie, one GET of a store's home page reads commerce.cart_lines once (twice today), and with a session cookie a product page reads commerce.customer_sessions once, by the table deltas of the review's working files (not kept).

**Tests and rules that hold today's behaviour, and change with it:**

- src/server/customers.int.test.ts; the unit tests that mock @/server/customers or @/server/cart are not affected: cart-contents.test.ts, bonus.test.ts, checkout-section.test.ts, order-section.test.ts, account/privacy/actions.test.ts.
- e2e/cart.spec.ts, e2e/wishlist.spec.ts and the account specs (sign in, then the header shows the account).
