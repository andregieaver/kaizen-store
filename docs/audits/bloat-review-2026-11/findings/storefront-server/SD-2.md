- **Evidence:** Measured again: one anonymous GET of the prerendered /s/demo/no/p/demo-keramikkopp on the warm local build committed 20 times in a window whose idle floor is 1. Tables read: inventory_levels, inventory_locations and inventory_reservations +8 each (stock is read by the buy part at product-parts.tsx:1012 and again by the JSON-LD part at :694), payment_providers +7, shipping_rates +4, checkout_providers +2 and vat_rates +2 (getCheckoutInfo() runs in expressOn() and in klarnaMessaging(), express-checkout.ts:62 and :155), platform_settings +2 (getCheckoutUi() at :61 and :155), price_breaks +2, and automations, order_settings, checkout_fields, payment_methods and stripe_accounts +1 each. None of these readers is cached. priceBreaksFor() runs for a guest too (price-breaks.ts:17-18 has no early return for a null customer; listPricesFor() at price-lists.ts:459 has one). The buy part waits in rounds before the button is drawn: the scheme check (:997), quote mode (:1001), the wave at :1011-1020 through a pool of 5 connections, then Klarna's states (express-checkout.ts:158). Every one of these runs for every store, whether or not it uses express checkout, Klarna, back in stock, quotes, price breaks, checkout questions or producer schemes. Next's guide says a cached read called at request time on serverless typically does not persist across requests, so the fix must carry the facts in the prerendered page, not in an instance's memory.
- **Cost today:** About 16 statements beyond the proxy's per product view. It grows one for one with shopper traffic, on the page where the buy button is: the largest per-request cost on the storefront once the platform opens. Paid by: The server and the database per request; the shopper waits on 'Checking stock' for the buy button.
- **Saving:** Estimate: from about 16 to 1 to 3 statements per anonymous product view (stock; schemes and breaks only for stores that have them), and the buy button no longer waits for payment facts. Step 1 alone: 3 statements.
- **Risk:** Medium. A writer that misses the tag shows or hides the express button or Klarna's line up to an hour late (the one-hour life stays as the net); checkout itself still checks. Noticed by buy-writers.scan.test.ts and buy-facts.int.test.ts, and in production by an owner who sees no express button after switching payments on. D373's per-shopper parts (signed in, buyer kind) stay per request; D172 (stock per request) is kept.
- **Already tracked:** No. FF4 / step 3.8 (Open) covers the cart's and checkout's reads only; FF10 / step 3.11 (Open) splits the file, not the reads.
