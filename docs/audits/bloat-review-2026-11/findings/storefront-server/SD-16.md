# SD-16 One owner saving AI settings expires the cached pages of every store

What the server does for a storefront request (`README.md` beside this file), part of `docs/audits/bloat-review-2026-11.md`.

- **Kind:** eager. **Priority:** P0. **Effort:** S. **Confidence:** high. **Step:** 0.16.
- **Second check:** added by the planner, not checked a second time.
- **Where:** `src/server/ai.ts:242`, `src/server/chat-agent.ts:135-141`, `src/app/admin/(gated)/[store]/settings/ai/actions.ts:34`, `src/app/admin/(gated)/[store]/settings/ai/actions.ts:50`, `src/app/admin/(gated)/platform/ai/actions.ts:52`, `src/server/docs-guide-settings.ts:137`, `src/server/vat-admin.ts:39-41`, `.next/server/app/s/demo/no.meta`, `node_modules/next/dist/docs/01-app/03-api-reference/04-functions/updateTag.md:18`, `docs/rules/storefront.md:384-390`
- **Evidence:** The chat button's cached read carries one tag for the whole platform, 'ai-providers' (ai.ts:242, chat-agent.ts:138). The market layout draws that read on every page, so every prerendered store page carries the tag: 319 of the build's 1,316 prerendered entries do, the template store's home, product and text pages and Kaizena Flow's own home among those I opened. A store owner's own action refreshes that tag: saveStoreAiAction and removeStoreAiAction call updateTag(AI_TAG) (settings/ai/actions.ts:34 and :50). Next's guide on updateTag: it 'immediately expires the cached data', and 'the next request will wait to fetch fresh data'. So one owner saving their own AI key expires every page of every other store, and of Kaizena Flow's site, at once. The store pages carry one more platform-wide tag, 'catalog'; only the platform's VAT tools refresh that one (vat-admin.ts:39-41, :345, :372), and that is tracked as FF3.
- **Cost today:** Per save of AI settings by any owner: every visited page of every store is drawn cold on its next visit (about 30 statements each, SD-5) and that visitor waits for it. It grows with stores x pages x owners who touch the setting. Today nobody notices: the stores are the team's. Paid by: Shoppers of other stores (first byte after the flush); the server and the database for the redraws.
- **Saving:** A platform-wide expiry per owner's save becomes one store's. Not measurable before opening; after it, stores x visited pages cold draws avoided per save by any owner.
- **Risk:** Low. A store that uses Kaizena Flow's AI still follows the platform's tag, so a platform change of AI still reaches every store, as a VAT change does (FF3). A store whose widget should appear after its first save is covered by its own tag.
- **Already tracked:** No. FF3 / step 3.10 (Open) covers the platform-wide catalogue tag and VAT changes, not this tag or a store owner's action.

## The fix

1. src/server/ai.ts:242 gains aiTag(storeId) = `ai:${storeId ?? "kaizen"}`; AI_TAG stays only as the platform's, aiTag(null).
2. src/server/chat-agent.ts:138 tags the widget with chatTag(storeId), aiTag(storeId) and aiTag(null): a store without a provider of its own answers with Kaizena Flow's (ai.ts:200-211), so the platform's tag stays on its pages.
3. saveAiSettings() and removeAiSettings() in src/server/ai.ts (the only writers of commerce.ai_providers, by rg) refresh aiTag(storeId) themselves with refreshTag(); src/app/admin/(gated)/[store]/settings/ai/actions.ts:34 and :50 drop updateTag(AI_TAG). The platform's actions (platform/ai/actions.ts:52, :66) and src/server/docs-guide-settings.ts:137 use aiTag(null).
4. hold it with the scan of SD-13 step 2: no file under src/app/admin/(gated)/[store] refreshes a tag from the list of platform-wide tags.

**Done when:** After a build, grep -rl 'ai-providers' .next/server/app --include='*.meta' | wc -l prints 0 (319 today), store pages carry ai:{storeId} and ai:kaizen, and the scan fails when a store's action names a platform-wide tag.

**Tests and rules that hold today's behaviour, and change with it:**

- docs/rules/storefront.md:388-390 names AI_TAG as the widget's tag: reword to aiTag(storeId) and the platform's.
- No test names AI_TAG (rg over *.test.* found none). New: the scan of SD-13 step 2; an int test that saving one store's AI settings leaves another store's cached widget in place is hard outside Next, so the scan and the build's tags are the check.

**Notes:** Read from the code and the build's tags; the flush itself was not triggered here, because that would write. Free to fix before opening; after SD-8 lengthens lives it would be the main cause of platform-wide redraws.
