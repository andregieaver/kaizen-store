# CR-13 ✓ Status checks are kept raw for 91 days though the page shows one bar a day

Scheduled and background work (`README.md` beside this file), part of `docs/audits/bloat-review-2026-11.md`.

- **Kind:** oversize. **Priority:** P3. **Effort:** S. **Confidence:** high. **Step:** tolerated.
- **Second check:** confirmed.
- **Where:** `src/server/status.ts:238-275`, `src/server/status.ts:340-347`, `src/server/status.ts:375-383`, `src/server/status.ts:381-383`, `src/server/status.ts:411-416`, `src/app/api/cron/cart-reminders/route.ts:263`, `src/lib/status.ts:7`, `supabase/migrations/20261111172841_baseline_3_rules.sql:12841`
- **Evidence:** Each run inserts one row per measured part (five of the six today): 1,440 rows a day. The prune keeps 91 days, so the table heads for about 131,000 rows; it has 20,547 after about 14 days. The page's read groups all 90 days into per-day counts and also runs a 'distinct on (component)' over the whole table with no time bound (status.ts:381-383), each time its cache is refilled, and the dispatcher refreshes that cache every five minutes. So far: 1,072 sequential scans reading 10.5 M rows. Only two reads need raw rows: the newest check per part, and seven days of timings for the Dublin measurement.
- **Cost today:** Estimate at steady state: about 131,000 rows and 20 MB, and two reads of the whole table on each cache refill. A fixed platform cost; it does not grow with stores. Paid by: The database, and a visitor to /status after each five-minute refresh.
- **Saving:** Estimate: about 131,000 rows to about 11,500 raw rows plus 540 day rows; the page's read from 131,000 rows to about 550.
- **Risk:** Low. The SLO minutes are computed from day counts already (dayBars), so the numbers stay the same. A wrong bound would show a part as 'unknown' on /status when its newest check is older than a day, which is also true.

## The fix

1. Small step: src/server/status.ts:381-383, bound the 'newest check per part' read to checked_at > now() - interval '1 day', so it uses status_checks_component_idx (component, checked_at).
2. Later, if the table is touched again: a table of per-day, per-part counts (ok and total) that runStatusChecks() upserts; readStatusOverview() reads the 90 days from it; raw checks kept 8 days (the Dublin timing reads 7).

**Done when:** After step 1, seq_scan on commerce.status_checks in pg_stat_user_tables rises by 300 or fewer a day; after step 2 the table holds under 12,000 rows.

**Tests and rules that hold today's behaviour, and change with it:**

- src/server/status.int.test.ts (the overview's bars and the newest state) and src/lib/status.test.ts (dayBars, the SLO minutes from day counts).
