# CR-15 The listings' check hashes every product on the platform every five minutes, and a listing the AI cannot answer for is asked again every run at the head of the queue

Scheduled and background work (`README.md` beside this file), part of `docs/audits/bloat-review-2026-11.md`.

- **Kind:** eager. **Priority:** P1. **Effort:** S. **Confidence:** high. **Step:** 0.22.
- **Second check:** added by the planner, not checked a second time.
- **Where:** `src/server/content-moderation.ts:38-39`, `src/server/content-moderation.ts:45-55`, `src/server/content-moderation.ts:152`, `src/server/content-moderation.ts:159-162`, `src/server/content-moderation.ts:202-226`, `src/server/content-moderation.ts:227-231`, `src/server/content-moderation.ts:262-266`, `src/lib/content-moderation.ts:103-110`, `src/app/api/cron/cart-reminders/route.ts:139-140`, `supabase/migrations/20261111172839_baseline_1_schema.sql:24021-24027`, `supabase/migrations/20261111172839_baseline_1_schema.sql:29715-29726`
- **Evidence:** The dispatcher's contentModeration job ends with screenListings(), Kaizena Flow's own check of listings (DSA art. 7, voluntary), on unless a platform admin turns it off (content_screening defaults to true, line 160). Its candidate statement (lines 205-225) first sorts every row of commerce.product_translations on the platform (distinct on product_id, with title, description and safety text, no store or status filter inside the CTE), then joins every active product of every live store and compares md5(title, description, safety) with the hash kept in content_screened. It is the same 'hash everything to learn nothing changed' shape as the embeddings statement in CR-4, with no store scope, and it runs every five minutes. product_translations has no updated_at, and no trigger moves products.updated_at, so there is no cheaper marker today. For each of up to ten candidates it asks the platform's model once. When the answer is null (a provider error) or cannot be read as the expected JSON (parseScreening returns null, src/lib/content-moderation.ts:103-110), line 230 says 'continue' and nothing is recorded. The candidates are ordered 'screened_at nulls first, p.id', so the same listings come back first on the next run. Triage of notices stops at the first 'no_ai' (line 152); the listings' loop does not stop on a provider error.
- **Cost today:** Database: one statement every five minutes that reads and sorts every product text on the platform; the like statement for embeddings takes 14.5 ms at today's few hundred translations and cannot use an index for 'hash differs' (estimate: at a few hundred thousand translations it nears the 15 s statement limit, and the job then fails every run). AI: one call per new or changed wording, capped at 10 a run, 2,880 a day, paid on Kaizena Flow's key for every store whatever its plan. The waste: ten listings whose answers cannot be read cost up to 2,880 calls a day for nothing and stop every other listing from being checked; while the provider is down the job makes 10 failing calls a run. How often answers are unreadable in production was not measured. Paid by: The database every five minutes, in proportion to every store's catalogue; Kaizena Flow's AI bill; and the notices' queue, which silently stops getting flags.
- **Saving:** Estimate: up to 2,880 wasted AI calls a day avoided when answers cannot be read, the queue can no longer be held by ten listings, and the platform-wide hash runs 24 times a day instead of 288 (then only over what changed).
- **Risk:** A listing recorded as tried is checked a day later, not five minutes later; for a voluntary check that is acceptable. A marker that misses a kind of change (a term rename, say) leaves a reworded listing unread until the hourly full pass, so keep that pass. It would be noticed on Platform, Notices as no new 'screening' flags.

## The fix

1. Record a try (smallest, do first). Migration: commerce.content_screened gains tries integer not null default 0 and tried_at timestamptz. In screenListings() (content-moderation.ts:227-231), when the answer is null or unreadable, upsert the row with the hash, flagged false, tries + 1 and tried_at now(), and make the candidate statement leave out a product whose hash is the same and whose tried_at is under a day old. Stop the loop at the first provider error, as triageWaitingReports() does at line 152.
2. Narrow the statement: choose the candidate products first (active products of live stores with no content_screened row, or whose products.text_changed_at is newer than screened_at), limit them, then read the words of those few with a lateral select from product_translations ordered by locale limit 1, and hash only them. products.text_changed_at is the marker of CR-4 step 4 (a trigger on product_translations and product_terms).
3. Until the marker exists: run the listings' check once an hour with a batch of 120 (the same cap of 2,880 a day) through CR-2's cadence. Split runContentModeration() (lines 262-266) so the triage of waiting notices stays at five minutes.
4. Say in docs/dsa.md what the cap means at opening: a move of 5,000 products takes about two days to be read.

**Done when:** The two new cases in content-moderation.int.test.ts pass, and in production select count(*) from commerce.ai_usage where feature = 'content_moderation' and created_at > now() - interval '1 day' is at most the products created or reworded that day plus the notices plus 10.
