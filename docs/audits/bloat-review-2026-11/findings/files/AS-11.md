# AS-11 Nothing is kept between 480 and 1600 pixels, so 3x phones and Retina laptops download the 1600 px file for every product tile

Files that ship or are stored (`README.md` beside this file), part of `docs/audits/bloat-review-2026-11.md`.

- **Kind:** oversize. **Priority:** P1. **Effort:** M. **Confidence:** medium. **Step:** 0.33.
- **Second check:** added by the planner, not checked a second time.
- **Where:** `src/lib/picture-sizes.ts:11`, `src/lib/picture-sizes.ts:25`, `src/components/product-card.tsx:16`, `src/components/product-card.tsx:57`, `src/components/sized-picture.tsx:59`, `src/components/product-gallery.tsx:12`, `src/components/product-gallery.tsx:166`, `src/components/content-grid.tsx:343`, `src/server/catalog.ts:298`, `src/server/listing.ts:59`, `src/components/admin/image-upload.tsx:28`, `src/server/media.ts:73`, `src/server/media-library.ts:120`, `e2e/performance.spec.ts:122`, `docs/media-compression.md:9`, `docs/audits/bloat-review-2026-11/scripts/sources/as-plan/srcset-test.cjs.txt`
- **Evidence:** Added by the completeness check. Every picture is kept as two files, 480 px and at most 1600 px (picture-sizes.ts:11-13), offered to the browser as 'small 480w, large Nw' (line 25). A product tile says it fills half the screen on a phone and a quarter from 768 px (product-card.tsx:16). I served that srcset and sizes to headless Chromium 153 and logged which file it asked for: 390 px wide at 2x takes the 480 file; 390 px at 3x, 360 px at 3x and 412 px at 2.625x take the 1600 file (the tile needs 540 to 585 device pixels, more than 480); 1440 px at 2x, four columns, takes the 1600 file. A full-width picture (the product page's main picture, 100vw under 768 px, product-gallery.tsx:12) takes the 1600 file on every phone tested, though the comment at product-gallery.tsx:166 says 'a phone fetches the small one'. Safari and Firefox were not run. Bytes, from the 24 theme art files at least 1500 px wide: 71 KB on average as stored, 8 KB as a 480 px copy, 18 KB at 800 px (sharp in memory). The speed budget cannot see this: e2e/performance.spec.ts runs at 1x on drawings ('the largest picture about 1 KB', line 122).
- **Cost today:** Per product tile on a 3x phone or a 2x laptop: the whole large file where a file of about 800 px would do, about 53 KB more for the art measured (71 against 18 KB); photographs are heavier. A listing shows up to 48 tiles (listing.ts:59), loaded as they scroll into view. The product page's main picture costs the same on a 2x phone. Paid by: A shopper's browser and data plan, in every store with product pictures, on most current phones. It grows with shoppers, not with what an owner switches on.
- **Saving:** Estimate from the theme art: about 53 KB less per product tile (71 to 18 KB) on 3x phones and 2x laptops, and the same on the product page's main picture on 2x phones. Real photographs were not measured and should save more.
- **Risk:** An upload takes a little longer in the owner's browser (a third quality search), and a picture's stored bytes grow by about a quarter. A picture without a middle copy keeps two files and behaves as today, so nothing breaks for what is already stored.
- **Already tracked:** no. D336 (wave 7 unit B) chose two files; docs/performance.md has no rule on screen density.

## The fix

1. src/db/schema.ts, table media: add mediumUrl (medium_url) and mediumPath (medium_path), text, nullable; pnpm db:generate. Add only; media is not one of the hot tables.
2. src/lib/picture-sizes.ts: MEDIUM_COPY_WIDTH = 800; pictureSources() takes an optional medium and writes 'small 480w, medium 800w, large Nw' when there is one and the picture is wider than 800 px.
3. In the owner's browser: src/components/admin/image-upload.tsx:28 and src/components/admin/media-compress.ts:44 also call compressPicture(file, 800) for a picture wider than 800 px and send it as 'medium'.
4. Storage: uploadProductImage() in src/server/media.ts takes the optional third file and stores it as {name}-800.{ext} with the same one-year cache; storePicture() in src/server/media-library.ts (line 120) writes medium_url and medium_path and counts the file in storageRefusal(); the library's copy (media-library.ts:266), its delete (716-750) and Compress (src/server/media-compress.ts:161-180) carry the third path.
5. Pictures the server makes: where a 480 px copy is made with sharp, make the 800 px one beside it: shrinkPicture() in src/server/ai-pictures.ts, storeArtPicture() in src/server/content-packs.ts, pictureToLibrary() in src/server/product-import.ts, and the callers of storePicture() in src/server/api-uploads.ts and src/server/replicate-assets.ts.
6. Readers: src/server/catalog.ts: beside image_width (line 302) read md.medium_url from the same lookup of commerce.media, for cards and for a product's gallery pictures; ProductCard, ProductGallery and content-grid's tile pass medium to SizedPicture (src/components/sized-picture.tsx). Pictures inside page content keep two files in this step.
7. e2e/performance.spec.ts: a test at viewport 390 with deviceScaleFactor 3 on a product whose picture has three files: expect the first tile's img.currentSrc to be the 800 px file. Correct the comment at product-gallery.tsx:166.
8. docs/media-compression.md and D336's text in docs/wave-7-storefront.md: three files and why; a decision row; show the owner a listing on a phone before and after.
