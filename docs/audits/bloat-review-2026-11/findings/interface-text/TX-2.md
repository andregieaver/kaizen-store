- **Second check:** confirmed.
- **Where:** `src/lib/admin-text.ts:308-310`, `src/app/admin/(gated)/layout.tsx:45`, `src/app/admin/(gated)/[store]/layout.tsx:114`, `src/app/admin/(gated)/[store]/products/[productId]/page.tsx:45`, `src/app/admin/(gated)/[store]/orders/[orderId]/page.tsx:268`, `src/app/admin/(gated)/[store]/customers/[customerId]/page.tsx:131`, `src/app/admin/(gated)/[store]/pages/editor-views.tsx:30`, `src/components/admin/rich-text-editor.tsx:155`, `src/components/admin/rich-text-editor.tsx:250`, `src/components/admin/text-styles.tsx:174`, `src/components/admin/admin-text.tsx:33-36`, `src/components/admin/admin-text-later.ts:1-20`, `docs/performance.md:156-190`
- **Evidence:** adminTexts(lang, areas) serialises each named area whole into the page. Measured documents (verified report): the product editor's holds 233 KB of whole areas in 416 KB (56%), the page builder's 304 KB in 572 KB (53%); of 195 hand-down sites with a literal list the median is 28.9 KB and 17 are over 100 KB. My own static count for three pages: the product editor hands product, products, fields, builder and website, 204,116 bytes of English JSON, and the client parts in its import graph name at most 65,307 of them. On the product, order and customer pages the only readers of the builder area are rich-text-editor.tsx and text-styles.tsx, which read its richText and textStyles groups: 2,847 of the 74,940 bytes handed. The product editor reads two groups of website (1,616 of 47,127 bytes). The order page hands 163,851 bytes and names at most 75,642. Every signed-in document carries common, setup and shell, and for an owner assistant: 34.0 KB raw / 11.7 KB gzip in English, 45.3 / 15.3 in Norwegian. No file in src calls useAdminText("setup"): its 6.9 KB is read by nothing in the browser. Across the admin, 33 files read the builder area 160 times; 115 reads name one group on the call.
- **Cost today:** Over half of the raw bytes of the product editor's and the page builder's documents and a third or more of several list pages'. A page's own areas are sent again on each navigation to it and each redraw after a save. The server serialises them on every request. Nothing measures a document's words today, so the amount grows with every text added to a large area. Paid by: An owner's browser on every admin page view; the server per request.
- **Saving:** Static estimate from the catalogues and each page's client parts, English JSON, raw; about a third of it on the wire. Product editor about 139 KB less (204 KB handed, at most 65 KB named). Order page about 88 KB less (164 to at most 76). Customer page at least 72 KB less (the builder area). Every signed-in document 6.9 KB less (setup), and an owner's a further 12 KB in English or 23 KB in Norwegian once assistantBar replaces assistant. The page builder's 304 KB needs the recording of step 5 before a figure can be given.
- **Risk:** A part that reads a group nobody handed draws English after a wait and fails admin-text-fallbacks.spec.ts. That spec visits every screen without an id and one of each kind behind its links (an order, a product), and notes what is read while the page wakes up; a part drawn only after a press (a dialog with the text editor) is not seen by it, so open those by hand or extend the spec. My read count follows static imports only; a part loaded with import() may read more of an area. The chat opening must not flash English: mount it only when its words have arrived.
- **Already tracked:** partly: PSI PERF-01 (open, docs/audits/psi-followups.md:482) and D540 cover the admin's scripts; documents have no budget and are not named
