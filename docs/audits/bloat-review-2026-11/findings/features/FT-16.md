## The fix

None as a fold: the verdict is tolerated, by the brief's rule. Its first branch does not hold whole (the number series and the e-invoice sender are shared, the VAT arithmetic is not); its second does: 0 shared windows, and the documents differ in law:

1. **Where the figures come from.** An order invoice is the order as it was paid: its VAT is the VAT the checkout charged on VAT-inclusive
   consumer prices, nothing is worked out again (`docs/wave-1b-invoices.md` 4.1), and the payment issues it (or the job, for one that
   waited), never a person (D159). A Work invoice is written by hand from net prices and hours, its VAT worked out per line at issue (D122,
   `docs/work.md` 4.3 and 4.5). One VAT engine for both would have to recompute the order's VAT, which 4.1 forbids so that the invoice
   equals what the shopper paid.
2. **Its life.** An order invoice has no draft, no status and no payment: it is frozen when issued and corrected only by a credit note the
   refund makes. A Work invoice has drafts (deleted without burning a number), payments, losses written off with their VAT taken back
   (D500, `tapPåKrav`) and recurring templates. Both are corrected only by credit notes, as the Bookkeeping Act asks (D501).
3. **Separate series.** The VAT Directive allows a sequential number "based on one or more series" (Art. 226(2)); the two engines take
   their numbers from one function in four series, one per document kind (`docs/wave-1b-invoices.md` 4.8, `docs/work.md` 4.4). Whether
   separate series for order and Work invoices are acceptable in each launch country is the accountant's question `docs/work.md` 4.4
   already marks LEGAL; this step cannot answer it, and a fold would not change it.

What a later change should do instead, when the code is open for another reason (no step added):

1. Share the half-up integer division: `divideRounded()` in `src/lib/work-calc.ts:54` and `divRound()` in `src/lib/invoice-snapshot.ts:51` are the same arithmetic. Keep both names as re-exports so no caller and no SQL mirror changes; `src/lib/work-calc.test.ts` and `src/lib/invoice-snapshot.test.ts` hold it.

When the platform gets its own sales documents for EHF (`docs/platform-parity.md`, the E-invoices row: Kaizena Flow's own documents through B1's sender), make them a third document type of `einvoice_sends` and `einvoice-sending.ts`, with its own series from `next_document_number()`, not a third engine copied from either.
3. Never merge or rename the tables of the two sides: both hold legal documents.
