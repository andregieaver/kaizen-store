## The fix

None as a fold: the verdict is tolerated, by the brief's rule. The two are different debts (a company's goods order on terms, paid on Stripe's or Mollie's page or by bank and recorded; a hand-written invoice for work paid to the owner's bank), they share the ledger's entries and the reminder mailer, and they share 0 windows. Each of the brief's fold signs, checked:

1. **Two overdue rules.** The rule is the same (past due after the due day, on the store's day): `termsState()` and `isOverdue()`, one comparison each. What differs is 'due soon': 3 days is when the terms reminder goes (D293), 7 days is the Work overview's bucket (`docs/work.md` 6.5). Not worth a module.
2. **Two reminder schedulers.** No: one scheduler. Orders on terms are reminded by the five-minute job; Work's reminder is the owner's press on purpose (`docs/work.md` 4.5 point 10: no automated interest or collection, an owner-triggered reminder).
3. **Two 'paid outside' writers with different checks.** Yes, two writers, and each check has a reason I can name:
   - The cash ceiling is the D173 rule for goods (`src/lib/cash-limits.ts:2`: hvitvaskingsloven § 5, 40,000 NOK, marked for legal review), and only goods are sold on terms (`termsOffer()`). Work sells services.
   - The 31-day window is the shop's own rule for money recorded by hand (D173, `MANUAL_RECEIVED_DAYS_MAX`); Work must record older payments (imported invoices, the whole year in the books, D498).
   - Closing an open Stripe session first exists because an order on terms can be paid online; a Work invoice cannot.
   - The writers write each document's own record (`payments` and the order's balance, `work_invoice_payments`), and the tables are not to be merged. The bank statement already reaches both through one matcher (D487).

What a later change should do instead (no step added):

1. When a write-off for orders on terms is built (D293's 'not built'), post it with `entryOfInvoiceLoss()` (`src/lib/ledger.ts:509`) and follow D500's rule (VAT taken back as a deduction with `tapPåKrav`, only a loss that is established), so the books keep one rule for a lost receivable.
2. Not sure, for the owner and the accountant: whether a cash ceiling also reaches Work's services. Norway's rule in `cash-limits.ts` is for goods; the EU's anti-money-laundering regulation (2024/1624) sets a cash limit for goods and services from July 2027, and I do not know whether or when Norway takes it in.
3. Never merge or rename the tables of the two sides.
