The built client bundles, by page (`README.md` beside this file), part of `docs/audits/bloat-review-2026-11.md`.

- **Kind:** oversize. **Priority:** P1. **Effort:** S. **Confidence:** high. **Step:** 0.16.
- **Second check:** corrected.
- **Where:** `src/lib/cookie-consent.ts:103`, `src/lib/cookie-consent.ts:156`, `src/lib/page-modal.ts:1`, `src/components/ab/ab-assign.tsx:5`, `src/components/touch-keeper.tsx:5`, `src/components/consent/consent-manager.tsx:10`, `src/components/consent/consent-manager.tsx:230`, `src/components/buyer.tsx:6`, `src/lib/b2b.ts:13`, `src/lib/b2b.ts:35`, `src/lib/store-features.ts:14`, `src/components/theme-page-parts.tsx:5`
- **Evidence:** (1) src/lib/cookie-consent.ts holds the consent helpers (lines 1 to 95) and, from line 156 to 754, KNOWN_COOKIES: every known cookie with its purpose in four languages. The skeptic measured the built module at 24,640 bytes, 23,860 of them the table (6.4 KB gzip). Client modules import the file for three small helpers (page-modal.ts:1, ab-assign.tsx:5, touch-keeper.tsx:5: consentCookieName, decodeConsent, CONSENT_CHANGED_EVENT). The one browser reader of the table is the consent manager's forget(), which tests each cookie's name against the patterns and categories (consent-manager.tsx:230); it is loaded only where a banner is drawn. (2) The header's Private or Business switch (buyer.tsx:6) imports cookie helpers from b2b.ts, which imports featureOn from store-features.ts for one function (lines 13 and 35), which imports the admin's common English for default labels (line 14). (3) theme-preview-stage.tsx, the theme store's stage, is a client root of every built page because the page renderer imports the theme store's parts (theme-page-parts.tsx:5). Ranked by what leaves if one root is dropped, the buyer switch is the third heaviest of a store home's 35 roots (14,976 bytes) and the stage is 4,884.
- **Cost today:** About 40 KB raw (about 12 KB gzip, estimate) of the roughly 190 KB that is the app's share of a store home's first scripts; on every storefront and site page. Paid by: Every shopper's browser, on the first page of each store they visit.
- **Saving:** Simulated on the real chunks (the skeptic's run): 24 KB (the cookie module), 12 KB (the feature registry and common.en) and 4.8 KB (the stage) leave the first scripts of the store home, the product page and Kaizena Flow's front page: 40 KB in all (store home 645 to 605 KB). A store that draws a consent banner still loads the table with the banner's script until step 5.
- **Risk:** Low, as long as the consent manager keeps its patterns: moving the table away from it would stop a withdrawn category's cookies from being removed, which e2e/cookies.spec.ts would show.
- **Already tracked:** No. Same change as SC-3 (steps 1, 2 and 6), TX-5 and PL-10.
