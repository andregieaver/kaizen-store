- **Kind:** no check. **Priority:** P0. **Effort:** S. **Confidence:** high. **Step:** 1.3.
- **Second check:** corrected.
- **Where:** `e2e/performance.spec.ts:11`, `e2e/performance.spec.ts:40`, `e2e/performance.spec.ts:126`, `e2e/signed-in/admin-scripts.spec.ts:18`, `e2e/signed-in/admin-scripts.spec.ts:36`, `docs/performance.md:110`, `scripts/next-build.mjs:9`, `docs/audits/bloat-review-2026-11/scripts/measure-routes.mjs:1`
- **Evidence:** The checks hold first scripts per route (e2e/performance.spec.ts:11, 40; admin-scripts.spec.ts:36) and the total transferred weight of two storefront pages, the front page and one product page (450,000 bytes each, counted until two seconds after load, set when they measured 311 and 331 KB on 27 October; performance.spec.ts:126-129). In routes.json they measure 406,542 and 423,097 bytes by a similar method: 10% and 6% of room. Nothing holds a document's raw size or payload, a count of requests after load, any admin document, a cart, a checkout or a listing, or what a server action sends back. So the admin's catalogues have ridden in the document unmeasured since October (up to 304 KB), the store layout's second copy went unseen, an Add to cart answers with 79 KB nobody has looked at (measured in this pass), and the empty cart (591,352 bytes) and checkout (613,556) transfer more than the budget the home is held to. The build's prefetching warning is printed on every build and start and fails nothing (scripts/next-build.mjs passes the output through).
- **Cost today:** Regressions in payload and request count are free, and the one weight budget is nearly used up. Paid by: Everyone the other findings name; CI stays blind to it.
- **Saving:** None by itself; it keeps the savings above from eroding and makes the next shift of bytes from one place to another visible.
- **Risk:** Low. Request counts wobble by one or two with timing (what is in view when the count is taken): set each count budget two above the measure, and keep the phone viewport fixed as SPEED_BUDGETS does.
- **Already tracked:** Partly: FIRST_SCRIPTS_BUDGETS and SPEED_BUDGETS exist (PSI PERF-11, D336) and admin-scripts.spec.ts holds the admin's first scripts; the gaps named here are in no plan.
