# What a page sends: document, payload and requests (PL)

Part of `docs/audits/bloat-review-2026-11.md`, on commit 0bd814c. Read by one reviewer, challenged by a second (who re-opened every citation, re-measured every number and applied the owner's note that the platform is not open yet) and planned by a third. A tick (✓) marks a finding the second reviewer confirmed or corrected; one the planner added has none. Each finding has a file of its own beside this one.

**In short.** On the wire a storefront document is light (13.7 to 21.3 thousand bytes gzipped); only the admin's two editors are heavy (108 and 136 thousand), and about 70% of that is message catalogues, of which the product editor's parts read a third (239 KB handed, 79 KB read). The waste is in what repeats and in what follows a load: the store's layout is rendered and sent twice for a 404 nobody sees (29.7 thousand bytes raw per document, and again inside the 79 KB every Add to cart sends back), a visitor who does nothing causes 11 to 50 prefetches and one or two uncached server calls per storefront view (the wishlist, the tiles, and on a search page a suggestion call nobody typed for), and Kaizena Flow's own pages pull about 141 KB of admin sign-in files. Fourteen findings, none refuted and none resting on 'unused so far': each is a duplicate or an idle cost per view, per press or per open tab, which is what grows when stores open. Seven are small and safe and go first (budgets, two prefetch props, the wishlist marker, the tiles' switches, the search call, the notice count); the two that restructure (the cart drawer's slot out of the root layout, the admin's words by cut) are cheapest before opening.

## Well made, and should stay

- No row of the home's or a product page's payload holds a product row, the store's settings or a message catalogue. Ranked, the largest are the grid's list (5.4 KB), the header, menu and footer trees (3.5 to 4.7 KB each, twice because of PL-2), the metadata (2.2 to 3.1 KB) and the JSON-LD (2.1 to 2.7 KB) (a scratch script).
- A store in a hand-written language sends no message catalogue: the UiTexts prop is null in both layout copies of the home document.
- Chat, referral capture, the business popup, consent and the visit, touch and speed beacons are drawn only when the store has them on; the demo's idle pages sent 0 beacons. A store with visit counting on will add its beacons per view: that is the feature working, cost following use.
- Pictures carry the right loading hints: on the home 5 are lazy and 5 eager, the first with high priority; every picture has a width and a height. Their sizes could not be judged: the demo's pictures are SVG drawings of 0.3 to 0.75 KB.
- Scripts and the stylesheet are served public, max-age=31536000, immutable. Files under public/ (the demo's pictures, the icon) have max-age=0: that is AS-5 of the assets dimension, and PL-12 here.
- Hidden markup is modest once compressed. Every admin document carries its navigation twice in the markup, the rail (9.2 to 15.4 KB raw) and the phone menu's closed dialog (5.1 to 22.0 KB raw), but the dialog is 0.6 to 2.6 KB gzipped (0.5 to 1.3 KB Brotli) of documents of 18 to 136 KB (a scratch script). On a store page the closed menu dialog is 5.6 KB raw and the filter dialog 8.5 KB on listings.
- Besides the words, the admin's largest prop is the menu's areas (12.5 KB on Orders, in the top bar's row): planned as AD-5 in the admin-data dimension, not repeated here.
- A quantity change in the cart also answers with the whole cart page; there the page's content did change, so it is not counted as waste.
- The local server sends 1,718 bytes of headers with every answer, 1.4 KB of it the report-only Content-Security-Policy on every path (src/lib/csp.ts:198). Production serves HTTP/2 or 3, where a repeated header costs a few bytes, so small answers weigh less there; request and function counts are as measured.
- Not measured, and said so: the checkout with a payment form (the demo store takes no payments, so /checkout leads back to the cart even with a product in it, by a headless run in this pass); a store with several countries (the demo sells in one); a store in an AI-translated language; and what production's Kaizena Flow front page holds in its 244 KB payload (evidence/prod-pages.md gives only totals; production was not queried).
- No written rule or decision covers prefetching (rg prefetch in docs finds only docs/plan.md:120), and no spec measures a document, its payload, an action's answer or an admin page's requests.
