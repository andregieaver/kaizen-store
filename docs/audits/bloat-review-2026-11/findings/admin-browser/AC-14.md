# AC-14 The Products list sends every product of the store to the browser: no paging, about 0.9 KB a product

What an owner's browser loads in the admin (`README.md` beside this file), part of `docs/audits/bloat-review-2026-11.md`.

- **Kind:** oversize. **Priority:** P1. **Effort:** M. **Confidence:** high. **Step:** 0.15.
- **Second check:** added by the planner, not checked a second time.
- **Where:** `src/server/products.ts:305`, `src/server/products.ts:347`, `src/app/admin/(gated)/[store]/products/page.tsx:91`, `src/app/admin/(gated)/[store]/products/page.tsx:168`, `src/components/admin/products-bulk.tsx:57`, `src/components/admin/products-bulk.tsx:251`, `src/server/pages.ts:135`, `docs/audits/code-review-2026-11.md:70`, `docs/audits/code-review-2026-11.md:305`, `docs/audits/admin-usability-2026-11.md:109`
- **Evidence:** listAdminProducts() has no limit: its statement ends at the order by (products.ts line 347) and runs several correlated subqueries per product (first picture, variants, digital variants, stock, prices). The page maps every row into the rows prop of the client component ProductsTable (page.tsx line 168), which draws a table row for each (products-bulk.tsx line 251). In the sampled list of 10 products the rows prop is 2,171 characters of flight data (217 a product) and consecutive rows are 606 to 744 characters apart in the HTML: about 0.9 KB of document per product, plus a hydrated row each. listPages() has no limit either and selects every page's whole draft and published content to make its summaries (pages.ts lines 135-139).
- **Cost today:** Grows in a straight line with the catalogue. Estimate from 10 rows: about 0.45 MB of document for 500 products and 4.5 MB for 5,000, on every visit to Products and every refresh after a bulk action. Nothing today: the test stores have about 10 products. Paid by: An owner's browser (document, DOM, hydration), the server and the database, whose read has no bound either.
- **Saving:** Estimate: at 5,000 products about 4.4 MB less document and 4,900 fewer table rows per visit; nothing at today's 10.
- **Risk:** Low to medium: a list that used to show everything now needs its search to find a product further down, so the search must land in the same change. It would be noticed as a product that cannot be found on the first page in products-bulk.spec.ts.
- **Already tracked:** Yes: docs/audits/code-review-2026-11.md step 1.13 (One way to search and page a list, FS2), Open, after step 1.12 (Open); docs/audits/admin-usability-2026-11.md row 3 (H7). Not new; listed because the report on this dimension did not count it and it is the one admin screen whose weight grows with a store's size.
