# Grade A for: AI calls

Part of `docs/audits/bloat-review-2026-11.md`. **Today: C.** C, in its lower half; the steward's grade holds against the verified findings. The base is sound and partly held: the shopper chat sends about 5,100 characters a call, the other agents are small, search answers are kept, tools of switched-off features are dropped, and a turn has ceilings of time and tokens. But the dearest call, the AI manager's, sends 161,661 characters on every round (3.3 times a lean round, up to 11 times a turn), nothing counts cached or output tokens, the only size budget is a tool count set at the provider's cap, and four upkeep jobs and 12.4 connection reads per AI call run with no model called. It is not a D because the waste sits in one agent and follows use, and not an E because nobody is hurt and the money is cents while only the team uses it.

**The bar, in a sentence.** The AI manager's first round is a third of today's size or less (at most 56,000 characters, and a median of 15,000 real tokens a call), at least half of its input comes from the provider's cache, and an eval shows it still finds its tools. Small jobs run on a small model, nothing reads the AI connection or walks the stores when no model is called, and a test or a daily figure holds every agent's size, output and ceiling.

## An A lives with

- The 12 core tools go with every round (12,700 characters), also on a turn that uses none. They answer most questions without a second round.
- The tools of the page in view and of one group matched to the question are sent before the model asks: up to about 11,500 characters each.
- The prompt carries an index of playbooks and the admin map, about 8,000 to 11,000 characters a round, so the manager can name a page or load a playbook.
- Up to 35% of turns take one more round, a second or two, because the tool was outside what was sent.
- The first call of a cold conversation is not cached. A provider with no cache is outside the cache measure.
- The chat script keeps a list of 416 page names in two languages, about 53,500 of its 125,000 bytes, so the suggested question needs no call to the server. It loads once a session, and only when the panel is opened.
- One read of the AI connection a minute per server instance and store, to know what the AI can do.
- A picture the vision model cannot describe is tried up to five times.
- Providers that do not report tokens on a streamed answer (Mistral, a custom address) are counted at four characters a token and left out of the token figures.
- Not a tolerance, by the panel's ruling: if the owner keeps the 17 plan, reminder and skill tools on every round (15,400 characters, named in the baseline), measures 1 and 2 are a B.
