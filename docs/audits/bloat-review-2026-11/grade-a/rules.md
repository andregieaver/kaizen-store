# Grade A for: The written rules

Part of `docs/audits/bloat-review-2026-11.md`. **Today: C.** C, in its lower half. The base is designed and sound: the rules in full sit under 295 unique headings that are read on demand, a test keeps them out of the always-loaded file, 5 of 3,145 cited code names are stale, and no doc reaches a deploy or a CI run. But every session and subagent loads 44 KB where about 10 KB will do, the search tool cannot show 52% of the rules or 98% of the decisions (I repeated the test: a 520-character line comes back as '[Omitted long matching line]'), 5 of 24 merges renumbered a decision, and no size is held while one test adds a line per rule (35% growth in 12 days). It is not a D because the load-on-demand mechanism exists and a test holds it, which the D areas lack; it is not a B because no budget exists and the largest cause is untouched.

**The bar, in a sentence.** A session starts with at most 11 KB of standing rules, finds any rule, decision or tracker row with one search that shows its text, and is never told to read more than 20 KB before it starts. A check on every push, docs-only ones too, holds each size, and a sample of real sessions shows a code change takes in at most 25 KB of rules and docs.
