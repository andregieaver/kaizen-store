# Grade A for: Interface text

Part of `docs/audits/bloat-review-2026-11.md`. **Today: C.** The words are in excellent order: every language is complete and held by the compiler, 34 of 31,359 keys are dead, and a shopper's document carries 7 to 32 texts. How they load is not: 135 of the 136 admin catalogue files (3.52 MB) sit behind the proxy, the visit beacon and every store page, admin documents hand two to three times the words their parts name, and 423 of 449 admin routes and 97 of 111 visitor routes carry text for someone else in their first scripts. Two of the eight ways words can leak have a test. That is a sound base with real waste, mostly unheld: a C and not a D, because the shopper's side is lean and the admin's English fallback was taken out of its scripts on 10 November and is held (D540).

**The bar, in a sentence.** A shopper's function and scripts carry no admin words, an admin screen is handed the words its parts name and its function loads only the areas it names, a save sends no words again, and a translated language costs what it uses. Each of these is a number that a test fails on, for an English and a Norwegian owner.

## An A lives with

- The four hand-written storefront languages load with every server function: 0.57 MB of source, about 10 ms and 0.3 MB of heap per cold start. Loading one at a time could show a shopper the wrong language. A ceiling of 590,000 bytes makes a fifth language force the choice.
- All four small browser-language files ride in a shopper's first scripts: 25.9 KB minified, 7.1 KB gzip, of which 4.6 KB is three languages the shopper does not read. Loading one alone once lost a press (D212). Ceiling: 34,000 source bytes.
- The 38 cookie patterns stay in the browser (2.2 KB), so deleting cookies never waits for a download.
- The common words' English stays compiled into every admin screen as a safety net for buttons: 6.2 KB minified, 2.4 KB gzip.
- A store page's function loads up to 8 catalogue files, 0.21 MB, for staff mail, order words, sign-in and customer groups: about 5 ms a cold start.
- Both languages of an area load on the server where one is shown: a factor of two, about 0.4 MB on a median admin page, while the admin has two languages.
- A group of words is handed whole, with the texts of states this visit does not reach: up to a quarter more than the parts name.
- A save's answer may carry up to 20 KB of words, and a redraw of the shell up to 30 KB of shell JSON.
- An instance asks every ten minutes whether a translated language changed: at most 144 one-row reads a day. The rows of every translated language are read at start, about 135 KB and 0.23 MB of heap each, until a warning at 1 MB.
- About 300 refusals are kept in English twice and matched to their Norwegian (D312, finding TX-10): 16.7 KB of source and no running cost. Add the coverage test when a validator is next changed.

## The owner's choices here

- Is a third language for the admin (Swedish or Danish) wanted in the first half year? If yes, step 2.25 and the added step on direct imports move before opening: until they are done each language adds about 1.5 MB to every function.
- Will the next storefront languages be hand-written or translated by AI? A fifth hand-written one adds about 125 KB to every server start and 6.4 KB to every shopper's first scripts, and loading one language at a time must be designed first. A translated one costs about 135 KB per start and nothing in the scripts.
