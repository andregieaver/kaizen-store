# Step 0.16: Send shoppers' first scripts only what the page draws

Phase 0 (Before the doors open) of `docs/audits/bloat-review-2026-11.md`. Effort M. Read [how to carry out a step](../README.md) first.

**Who:** a stronger model. Commits one to three are file moves a cheap model can do under the scan test, but the filter's button-and-panel split and the picker trial need design judgement about lost presses that the brief cannot remove.

**Goal:** A store page's first scripts hold only what the page draws: no admin words, no features table, no cookie purposes, no cart forms on the product page, and no carousel, modal helper, theme preview stage, platform language switch or filter panel on a plain page.

**Why:** Today /s/demo/no sends 680,264 bytes of first scripts and the product page 777,785; the target is at most 615,000 and 670,000 (690,000 only while a product picker is kept by the press test), with the product page's own entry chunks at most 297,000 (315,368 today).

**Closes:** [SC-3](../../findings/storefront-browser/SC-3.md), [PL-10](../../findings/page-payload/PL-10.md), [BN-10](../../findings/client-bundles/BN-10.md), [TX-5](../../findings/interface-text/TX-5.md), [TX-8](../../findings/interface-text/TX-8.md), [SC-11](../../findings/storefront-browser/SC-11.md), [SC-4](../../findings/storefront-browser/SC-4.md), [PM-11](../../findings/page-builder-model/PM-11.md), GAP-shopper-browser-s4. ## Do not

- Do not re-export the moved names from their old files (cookie-consent.ts, b2b.ts, page-modal.ts, store-features.ts, menu-drawer.ts): a re-export puts the weight back.
- Do not make a form, the cart's checkout button, the discount code form, the gift box, the delivery choice or sign-in lazy: what is typed before the script runs is lost (docs/performance.md).
- Do not load the consent manager's cookie patterns with import(): withdrawing consent must delete cookies without a second download.
