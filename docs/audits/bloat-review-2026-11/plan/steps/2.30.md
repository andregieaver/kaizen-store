# Step 2.30: Load the builder's dialogs and each block's field form when they are opened

Phase 2 (Load on demand) of `docs/audits/bloat-review-2026-11.md`. Effort L. Read [how to carry out a step](../README.md) first.

**Who:** a stronger model. Splitting the block fields into modules that bring their own words is a redesign of a 3,644-line file with a known trap (words arriving after a press); only commits 1 and 2 are mechanical.

**Goal:** The page builder loads its dialogs, its settings panel and each block type's field form when they are first opened, each bringing its own words, so the editor's first scripts are at or under 1,800,000 bytes and its document carries at most 130,000 bytes of words.

**Why:** After steps 2.26 to 2.29 the builder is still near 2,300,000 bytes of first scripts and a 205,000-byte texts object (3,303,372 and 276,922 at the review); this step brings them to 1,800,000 and 130,000, the document to 445,000 bytes and script by idle to 2,500,000 (3,454,740).

**Closes:** GAP-owner-browser-1, GAP-owner-browser-s2. Each finding's file has the evidence.

## Do not

- Do not start the code before the cut table is in the tracker.
- Do not draw a dialog or a field form before its words have arrived: a part that waits for words is not interactive and an early press is lost (docs/performance.md, 31 October 2026).
- Do not change what a block stores or how the canvas draws it; only where the editing form's code and words come from.
- Do not remove a block type, a dialog or a field because its chunk is large.
- Do not leave out the platform's builder (/admin/platform/pages) or the sandbox (/admin/try): they draw the same editor.
- Do not raise a budget to pass.
