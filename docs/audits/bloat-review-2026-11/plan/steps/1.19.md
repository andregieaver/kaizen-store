# Step 1.19: Hold every agent's round, show real tokens per call, and let the eval see whether the model finds a tool

Phase 1 (Budgets, so it stays removed) of `docs/audits/bloat-review-2026-11.md`. Effort M. Read [how to carry out a step](../README.md) first.

**Who:** a stronger model. Drawing each agent's real round, threading turn and round through the metering path of src/server/ai.ts and writing eval cases the model must find tools for all need judgement, and the recording needs a person with platform access; commit 1 is specified for a cheaper model once roundTools() is extracted.

**Goal:** Every agent's round is drawn and held in a baseline, each AI call is stored with its turn, round and cached input tokens and shown on Platform, AI as medians and 90th percentiles, and the agents' eval has at least 12 lean cases that show whether the model finds a tool that was not sent.

**Why:** The only AI budget today is a tool count set at the provider's cap of 128, while the AI manager's default round is about 161,661 characters. After this step that figure and seven other agents' rounds are numbers that fail a test when they grow, real tokens per call are on a screen, and a 'before' recording exists for the lean round of step 2.36.

**Closes:** [AI-10](../../findings/ai-calls/AI-10.md), [AI-14](../../findings/ai-calls/AI-14.md), GAP-ai-3, GAP-ai-s2, GAP-ai-s3. Each finding's file has the evidence.
