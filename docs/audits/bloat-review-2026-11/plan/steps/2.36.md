# Step 2.36: Send the AI manager a lean default round

Phase 2 (Load on demand) of `docs/audits/bloat-review-2026-11.md`. Effort M. Read [how to carry out a step](../README.md) first.

**Who:** a stronger model. Commit 1 assigns about 240 tools to groups by what pages they work on, commit 3 marks which of 38 playbooks need goods and rewrites the AI manager's tool choice and prompt, judged only by an eval the owner runs in production; commit 2 is now exact but is a permission check a reviewer reads; only commit 4, replacing the recording file with the owner's download, is mechanical. A smaller model may still do commit 4 alone. *(Rating checked twice, 12 November.)*

**Goal:** A round of the AI manager sends its core tools, the group of the page the person is on and the group a router picks from the question, with everything else one load_tools call away; its 17 plan, reminder and saved-skill tools load only when the talk turns to them or something of theirs is alive; playbooks are listed by feature, an autopilot step carries no admin map, a person's few memories cost no embedding request, and a turn's ceiling is 250,000 tokens.

**Why:** Today every AI manager call sends 124 tool definitions and a prompt that lists every playbook and the admin map: 161,661 characters whatever the question, up to 11 times a turn. After: no first round over 56,000 characters for any of the 20 planner questions on any page, and 34,000 or fewer with no page and no question.

**Decided by the owner (12 November, the recommended way):** The AI manager offers its core tools, the page's group and the group the router picks from the question; the rest is one load_tools call away. Its 17 plan, reminder and saved-skill tools load when the talk turns to them or the store has a plan, goal or reminder alive; the critic says only this is an A for the AI area and the surface.

**Closes:** [AI-1](../../findings/ai-calls/AI-1.md), [AI-9](../../findings/ai-calls/AI-9.md), [AI-11](../../findings/ai-calls/AI-11.md), [FT-4](../../findings/features/FT-4.md), [AI-6](../../findings/ai-calls/AI-6.md), GAP-ai-s1, GAP-ai-2. Each finding's file has the evidence.

- **create** `src/lib/tool-groups.ts`: Pure, no server import. A group is a part of an area: its id is '<area>.<slug>' where <area> is a ToolArea of src/lib/tool-budget.ts and <slug> the kebab-case of a group heading of that area's section in STORE_SECTIONS (src/lib/store-nav.ts: section.key is the area, section.groups[].heading names the group, group.items[].path its pages). Export const TOOL_GROUPS: Readonly<Record<string, string>>: tool name to group id, for every tool of OWNER_TOOLS (src/lib/owner-tools.ts) that is not in CORE_TOOLS and whose area is 'grouped'. An area is grouped when its tools outside the core total more than 12,000 characters of JSON.stringify(toolDefinition(tool)) (toolDefinition is in src/lib/owner-tools.ts). - Do not change what an autopilot step, a scheduled follow-up or an outside assistant (OUTSIDE_MANAGER_TOOLS) is offered, apart from the admin map leaving an autopilot step's prompt.
