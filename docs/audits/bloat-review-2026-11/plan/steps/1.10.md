# Step 1.10: Count the statements and rows of scheduled work and of a cold frame

Phase 1 (Budgets, so it stays removed) of `docs/audits/bloat-review-2026-11.md`. Effort M. Read [how to carry out a step](../README.md) first.

**Who:** a stronger model. Commits 1 and 2 are mechanical, but the 20-store seed of commit 3 must switch on fourteen features through their own savers, and choosing those calls and judging which idle counts are stable needs a model that can read each feature.

**Goal:** An integration test counts the statements and the rows read of the idle dispatcher tick job by job, of each minute route, of the daily route and of a cold page frame, with 1 and with 20 seeded stores, and keeps the counts in src/server/ratchet.baseline.json where they may only fall.

**Why:** The scheduler sends about 170,000 idle statements a day and no check holds it. After this step every job has a statement count and a rows-read count for 1 and for 20 stores, and the difference between the two is a number that only falls (0 after steps 3.2 and 3.6).

**Closes:** [DQ-11](../../findings/queries/DQ-11.md), GAP-database-2, GAP-scheduled-s5, GAP-database-s2. Each finding's file has the evidence.

## Do not

- Do not change what a job does, its order in the list or its key: the key is the job's row in commerce.cron_jobs.
- Do not add a margin to a count to make a wobbling job pass: a count that changes between runs on the same database is a finding to report.
- Do not let a job reach the network: the test database has no provider keys; a job that tries is a failure to report, not something to mock around, unless the job already takes a deps argument (runThemeShots takes a photographer: pass one that throws if called).
- Do not count with pg_stat_database's commits, and do not count time.
- Do not seed by writing a feature's secret in plain SQL; use the feature's own saver so encrypted columns hold what the app can read.
- Do not fix a job that grows with the number of stores here: steps 0.27 to 0.31, 3.2 and 3.6 do that; this step records the growth.

## Stop and report if

- countStatements is not exported from src/db/statement-count.ts: step 1.9 is not done.
- A job or route launches a browser, calls a provider or throws when there is nothing to do: report its name.
- The same job gives different statement counts on three runs in a row.
- rowsRead does not settle within 15 seconds or differs by more than 0 between runs: report the job; rows read may then be held for the 20-store run only.
- A feature of the list cannot be switched on from a server function without a real provider answer (eBay, an ad platform): leave it out, say which in SEEDED_FEATURES' comment and in your report.
- The whole file takes over 5 minutes in pnpm test:int.
