# Step 0.42: Compare by job the two pairs of engines nobody compared

Phase 0 (Before the doors open) of `docs/audits/bloat-review-2026-11.md`. Effort S. Read [how to carry out a step](../README.md) first.

**Who:** a stronger model. Counting is mechanical, but saying whether two money engines do one job or two, and what a safe fold would be, needs judgement about invoicing law and the code that the brief cannot remove.

**Goal:** docs/audits/bloat-review-2026-11/findings/features/ holds one file for each of the two pairs of engines (order invoices beside Work invoices; orders on payment terms beside Work's unpaid invoices), each with the shared and separate lines and tables counted and a verdict: fold, with a step added to the plan, or tolerated, with its numbers.

**Why:** The review compared three jobs that have two engines and never looked at these two pairs: about 3,700 lines and 4 tables of order invoicing beside about 6,500 lines and 13 tables of Work, and 737 lines of payment terms beside Work's payments. After: each pair has a written verdict, so a fold can join phase 3 while no owner has data in either.

**Closes:** GAP-surface-2. Each finding's file has the evidence.

## Stop and report if

- A file named in the two sets does not exist (the code moved): list what rg --files src | rg 'invoice|payment-terms|work-' gives and go on with that list, saying so in the finding.
