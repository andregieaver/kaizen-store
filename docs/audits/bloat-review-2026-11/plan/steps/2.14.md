# Step 2.14: Let a publish make only its own page stale

Phase 2 (Load on demand) of `docs/audits/bloat-review-2026-11.md`. Effort M. Read [how to carry out a step](../README.md) first.

**Who:** a stronger model. Which facts of a page other pages show (listFacts) is a judgement over the whole site that tests cannot fully hold, and a miss leaves a page stale for a week once step 2.15 lands; the first commit is mechanical and can go to the cheap model.

**Goal:** A read of one page's document carries the tag page:{id}; publishing a change to a page's body refreshes only that tag, a change other pages show (title, address, thumbnail, excerpt, a header or footer, a first publish, an unpublish) still refreshes pages:{store}; a product layout's document is cached once per store; and the alt-text job refreshes a store's tags at most once per pass of its queue.

**Why:** Today one tag, pages:{store}, is on all 261 built entries of the demo store and about 60 places refresh it, so one publish makes all 261 render again, and the alt-text job refreshed a store's tag 10 times in 10 runs. After: a body-only publish makes 3 of 261 entries stale (the page's own), and the job refreshes store-wide at most once in 10 runs.

**Closes:** [PM-15](../../findings/page-builder-model/PM-15.md), [PM-16](../../findings/page-builder-model/PM-16.md), GAP-page-model-2, GAP-page-model-s3. Each finding's file has the evidence.
