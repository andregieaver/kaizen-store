# Step 2.27: Hand each admin page only the words its parts read

Phase 2 (Load on demand) of `docs/audits/bloat-review-2026-11.md`. Effort L. Read [how to carry out a step](../README.md) first.

**Who:** a stronger model. Commits 1 to 3 are specified and the type check and the fallbacks spec catch a wrong edit, but choosing the cuts for the editor and for the 24 large sites, and keeping the chat from drawing before its words, needs judgement.

**Goal:** Each admin page hands its client parts typed cuts of the big word areas (only the groups those parts name), the assistant's button gets its own small cut and the chat gets its words when it opens, and every hand-down site over 50 KB in the words baseline hands at most 1.25 times what its parts name.

**Why:** The product editor is handed 238 KB of words and names about 79 KB, and every store screen carries 34.0 KB of shell words (45.3 KB in Norwegian) and reads 12; after the step shared words are at or under 15.5 KB (16 KB in Norwegian), the product editor's document is under 290,000 bytes (426,497 today) and the builder's under 480,000 (585,943).

**Closes:** [AC-4](../../findings/admin-browser/AC-4.md), [PL-1](../../findings/page-payload/PL-1.md), [TX-2](../../findings/interface-text/TX-2.md), [PM-10](../../findings/page-builder-model/PM-10.md), GAP-text-1. Each finding's file has the evidence.
