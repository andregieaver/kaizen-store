# Step 0.12: Stop prefetching what a press cannot use on stores and on Kaizena Flow's site

Phase 0 (Before the doors open) of `docs/audits/bloat-review-2026-11.md`. Effort S. Read [how to carry out a step](../README.md) first.

**Who:** a smaller model alone. About 25 one-attribute edits at named lines, with a scan test and an e2e spec that fail on a wrong or missing one.

**Goal:** Links whose press cannot use a prefetched answer are not prefetched: language, currency and country links, links that leave their root layout, the footer's legal links, and the links between Kaizena Flow's site and the admin or a store.

**Why:** Today a phone home page read to the footer makes 47 router prefetch requests, 24 of them for other views a press never opens; the /s/demo front door makes 12; and Kaizena Flow's front page fetches 2 /admin answers, 6 admin scripts and the admin's stylesheet. After: 0 for other views and at most 23 in all, 0 at the front door, and 0 requests to /admin.

**Closes:** [PF-2](../../findings/prefetching/PF-2.md), [PL-3](../../findings/page-payload/PL-3.md), [SC-1](../../findings/storefront-browser/SC-1.md), [PF-11](../../findings/prefetching/PF-11.md), [PL-4](../../findings/page-payload/PL-4.md), [BN-2](../../findings/client-bundles/BN-2.md), [CA-4](../../findings/caching/CA-4.md), [CS-8](../../findings/styles/CS-8.md). Each finding's file has the evidence.

## Do not

- Do not remove an href, turn a <Link> into a button, or change an onClick: without scripts the link must still lead somewhere (D109), and the language cookie logic of D479 stays.
- Do not add prefetch={false} to the header's search, account, wishlist and cart links, the menus, product tiles or the logo: those presses use the prefetch. Step 2.6 decides them with PolicyLink.
- Do not set a global prefetch switch in next.config.ts and do not turn on partialPrefetching here.
- Do not touch src/components/admin's rail, menus or lists: step 0.13 does the admin.
- Do not raise a number in the spec's BUDGETS to make it pass.
- Do not write the spec against `pnpm dev`: prefetching only runs in a production build.
