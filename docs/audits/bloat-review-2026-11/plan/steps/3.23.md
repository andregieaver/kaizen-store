# Step 3.23: Tripletex and Fiken skip a store with nothing new, and the registry labels the six per-store jobs

Phase 3 (One of each) of `docs/audits/bloat-review-2026-11.md`. Effort S. Read [how to carry out a step](../README.md) first.

**Who:** a smaller model; a stronger one reads the diff before the push. The list statements are named, but these are accounting syncs and a migration CI applies: an exists() on the wrong time column or a due condition copied wrongly passes the int tests and leaves an invoice unsent to Tripletex or Fiken for an hour; and each per-store job's cap and order is read from its code, which a reviewer checks against the functions. *(Rating checked twice, 12 November; first written as strong.)*

**Goal:** The Tripletex and Fiken syncs skip a store with nothing new since they last looked (looked_at) and look at an idle one hourly, and the six per-store jobs (the books, Tripletex, Fiken, auto-archive, autopilot budgets, recurring costs) carry their real cap and order in the registry, with auto-archive, autopilot budgets and recurring costs hourly.

**Why:** Tripletex and Fiken list every connected store on every five-minute tick and sweep and claim for each even when nothing was issued, and six per-store jobs sit in the registry without the cap and order that say how their work grows. After the step a connected store with nothing new costs no statement beyond the list, a new document is taken on the next tick, and the registry says each per-store job's cap and order.

**Closes:** [CR-16](../../findings/scheduled-work/CR-16.md). Split out of step 3.6 on 12 November. Each finding's file has the evidence.
