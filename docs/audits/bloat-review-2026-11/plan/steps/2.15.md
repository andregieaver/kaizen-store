# Step 2.15: Let tagged reads live until their tag is refreshed

Phase 2 (Load on demand) of `docs/audits/bloat-review-2026-11.md`. Effort M. Read [how to carry out a step](../README.md) first.

**Who:** a stronger model. Commit 1 is mechanical, but commit 2 must find which tables each tag family's reads touch and judge, for every writer the scan names, whether its callers' refresh really covers it; commits 3 and 4 then make reads live a week on the strength of that judgement, which no test can check. A smaller model may still do commit 1 alone. *(Rating checked twice, 12 November.)*

**Goal:** A cached read whose every writer refreshes a tag lives a week (a named profile, 'tagged'); only reads whose answer moves with the clock, the grid of pages and the feed keep an hour; no storefront route is redrawn every 15 minutes; and one table-driven scan holds every writer of a week-long read to its tag.

**Why:** Today 71 of 85 cached reads expire after an hour and 2 after 15 minutes, so 538 of 772 prerendered routes are redrawn hourly (251 docs routes among them) and the store home and All products every 15 minutes, though their writers already refresh tags. After: a store's own pages and the docs show 604800 s in the prerender manifest, and no storefront route shows 15m (6 today).

**Closes:** [SD-8](../../findings/storefront-server/SD-8.md), [CA-10](../../findings/caching/CA-10.md). Each finding's file has the evidence.
