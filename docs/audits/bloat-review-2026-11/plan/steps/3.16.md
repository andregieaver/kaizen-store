# Step 3.16: Draw an order's totals from one part

Phase 3 (One of each) of `docs/audits/bloat-review-2026-11.md`. Effort S. Read [how to carry out a step](../README.md) first.

**Who:** a smaller model; a stronger one reads the diff before the push. It is a move of JSX and SQL text that the section tests and e2e specs hold, but these are the sums a shopper pays by, so a stronger model reads the diff and the before and after screenshots before the push.

**Goal:** The account's order page and the order confirmation draw an order's totals with one OrderTotals part, checkout draws its delivery rows from the same file, and the two product-card reads in catalog.ts share one column list, one price join and one row mapper.

**Why:** The totals list is the same 70 lines of JSX in two order pages (988 lines together) and its delivery rows a third time in checkout; the product-card SELECT is typed twice in catalog.ts (67 identical lines). After: the two order page files total under 900 lines and src/server/catalog.ts is under 700 (750 today).

**Closes:** [CP-12](../../findings/copied-code/CP-12.md). Each finding's file has the evidence.
