# Step 2.39: Give small jobs a quick model

Phase 2 (Load on demand) of `docs/audits/bloat-review-2026-11.md`. Effort M. Read [how to carry out a step](../README.md) first.

**Who:** a stronger model. Commits 1 and 2 are mechanical and named (the column and the eval suite check, then 'main' at 31 listed sites with the cost ceiling kept whole), but commit 3 writes fixed cases that must truly separate good answers from bad for five jobs plus a runner, and commit 4 depends on a person's run and the owner's price rows. A smaller model may still do commits 1, 2 alone. *(Rating checked twice, 12 November.)*

**Goal:** Every completeText call says whether it runs on the main or the quick model, each provider has a default quick model, and each of the 8 named small jobs runs on the quick model once its fixed cases pass on it (or is named as kept on main, with the failing case).

**Why:** One text model does every job: 33 completeText sites take whatever model the platform or the store chose, and only 3 of the 8 small jobs have fixed cases; after the step 0 sites lack a tier, all 8 jobs have at least 5 cases each and those that pass show the quick model in commerce.ai_usage.

## Do not

- Do not move a job to quick before its cases pass in a committed recording, and never the AI manager's loop, the shopper chat, the reading of a notice of illegal content, legal pages or anything that writes text a customer reads at length.
- Do not put a model's price in code or a migration: prices are rows the owner adds on Platform, AI, Prices.
- Do not name a model anywhere but src/lib/ai-provider.ts.
- Do not make the quick model a required setting: empty means the default, so an owner never has to choose.
- Do not push the migration together with any other commit of this step.
- Do not change a job's prompt to make a case pass on the quick model; keep it on main and name the case.
- Do not buy a plan or a service for the eval: it runs on the platform's own AI connection.
