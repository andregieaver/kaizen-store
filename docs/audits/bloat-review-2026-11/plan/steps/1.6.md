# Step 1.6: Put a launch-theme store into the measured set

Phase 1 (Budgets, so it stays removed) of `docs/audits/bloat-review-2026-11.md`. Effort M. Read [how to carry out a step](../README.md) first.

**Who:** a stronger model. Commit 1 writes a seed that runs the platform's own content-pack, launch-theme and demo-store functions against a fresh database under a new Vitest config and a new CI step, and on a fresh database it may still have to make and publish the pack's starting content (its step 4 names functions to read, not calls), which is judgement; commit 2 is held budgets and docs with every helper named, which the cheap model can do once commit 1 is in. A smaller model may still do commit 2 alone. *(Rating checked twice, 12 November.)*

**Goal:** CI seeds one store that wears the Orbit launch theme whole (its content pack, header, footer, product layout, two font families and photographs with all their stored sizes), and e2e/performance.spec.ts prints and holds its home, a product page, a listing and a built page: document, payload, main, style text, each stylesheet, font files, picture bytes on a 3x phone and router requests; the four pages are in measure-routes.mjs.

**Why:** All 12 measured store pages are the bare template: no font file, one-row pages and pictures of about 1 KB, while every store started from a theme has a builder-made header and footer, two font families and photographs. After: four themed pages are held within 5% of their first figures, with at most 2 font files and 80 KB and at most 3 stylesheets on the themed home.

**Closes:** GAP-shopper-browser-1, GAP-page-model-3, GAP-styles-1, GAP-shopper-browser-s5. Each finding's file has the evidence.

(3) First the content packs, with their art kept as fixtures: `await syncContentPacks(admin, { storeArt: fixtureArt, apply: deps })` (src/server/content-packs.ts:231; PackDeps.storeArt at line 98 replaces storeArtPicture() entirely, as src/server/content-packs.int.test.ts:31 does) and expect its problems to be empty; fixtureArt is written in (6). ## Do not

- Do not build the themed store with hand-written SQL that copies a theme's snapshot into rows: the point is the path a new owner's store takes.
- Do not fetch fonts from Google or pictures from the internet in CI, and do not call Supabase storage.
- Do not shrink the font fixtures below what the installer would store, and do not use 1 KB drawings as the photographs.
- Do not change Orbit's theme, its pack or its version to make a budget pass: this step measures.
- Do not add the seed to the integration or signed-in jobs, and do not run it after the build.
- Do not put the generated public/e2e-orbit/ files in git.
- Do not mark the step Done at the first figures.

## Stop and report if

- createDemoStore() refuses on the seeded database (made.ok is false): report its problems; do not call self sign-up or approveAccessRequest() instead.
- syncContentPacks() does not call deps.storeArt for Orbit's art on the seeded database (fewer than 8 commerce.media rows of the pack's template store after (3)): stop and report; do not change storeArtPicture().
- The real font files are larger than 80 KB together, or the themed home loads more than 2 font files: that is a finding, not a fixture problem. Hold the measured figure, report it, and do not trim the fixtures.
- syncLaunchThemes() returns problems on the seeded database.
- The themed home's stored page is over 11,100 bytes.
- The licence of a font file is not the SIL Open Font License.
- A themed page's figures differ between two runs of the same build.
- npm pack cannot fetch @fontsource-variable/unbounded or @fontsource-variable/manrope, or the archive has no files/<family>-latin-wght-normal.woff2: stop before commit 1 and ask the owner to commit the two latin files; do not make a font file another way.
