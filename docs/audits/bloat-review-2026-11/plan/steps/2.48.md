# Step 2.48: Give a placed order a ceiling, and stage placeOrder where the first figures are over it

Phase 2 (Load on demand) of `docs/audits/bloat-review-2026-11.md`. Effort M. Read [how to carry out a step](../README.md) first.

**Who:** a stronger model. Commit 1 is now test cases alone in step 1.12's named file, with the seeding, the function timing and its superuser setting specified, so the cheap model may do it; commits 2 and 3 are made or skipped on its figures and then change checkout's line writes and the order triggers in a migration, where which loop work depends on line order and which trigger functions count as optional are readings the brief leaves to the executor. A smaller model may still do commit 1 alone. *(Rating checked twice, 12 November; first written as review.)*

**Goal:** The write budget of step 1.12 asserts five ceilings for one placed order (same statements with 10 and 2,000 earlier orders; at most 3 more per added line; triggers under a fifth of the order's database time; a counted visit at most 3 statements and 2 rows; optional features' triggers under 2 ms on a shop-only store), and placeOrder is staged only where a measured figure is over one.

**Why:** Step 1.12 counts what one placed order writes but sets no ceiling and nobody has judged the count; code review step 3.7 (Stage placeOrder) is open. After this step each figure has a line in docs/performance.md and a failing test behind it.

**Closes:** GAP-database-s7, GAP-surface-s5. Each finding's file has the evidence.

## Do not

- Do not raise or remove a ceiling to make the test pass, and do not skip a case other than case 3 on a database that cannot time functions.
- Do not stage placeOrder where no figure is over its ceiling: no multi-row insert and no migration 'while we are here'.
- Do not drop, disable or rename a trigger; do not change what a trigger writes for a store that has the feature on.
- Do not alter commerce.orders or commerce.order_lines (hot tables) and do not add an index in this step.
- Do not change insertOrderLine's signature: three other writers call it.
- Do not apply the migration to production by hand.
