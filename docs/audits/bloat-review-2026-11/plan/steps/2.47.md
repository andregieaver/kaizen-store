# Step 2.47: Take the order writer off pages that only show products

Phase 2 (Load on demand) of `docs/audits/bloat-review-2026-11.md`. Effort L. Read [how to carry out a step](../README.md) first.

**Who:** a stronger model. The first three commits are fully specified and mechanical, but they sit on the order and payment path, and the remaining cuts are found by reading the graph test's chains and choosing where an edge may safely become lazy.

**Goal:** A product page, a category page and the search load none of the order writer (src/server/checkout.ts), the Stripe SDK, the ledger, staff alerts or billing; they are loaded inside the server action that places an order, and the graph test and scripts/route-budget.mjs hold six storefront pages.

**Why:** After every other cut the product page still reaches about 1,930 modules and a listing, the search, the cart and the checkout about 1,995 (2,516 to 2,546 modules and 12.3 MB today), because the cart's actions file imports startCheckout and startExpress at its top. The target is 1,800 modules and 4.8 MB for the three product-showing pages, and 1,800 and 5.0 MB for cart, checkout and account.

**Closes:** GAP-server-s1. Each finding's file has the evidence.

- Do not change what startCheckout, startExpress or placeOrder do, their arguments or the order of checks before them. This step moves imports, not behaviour.
- Do not wrap the import() in a try/catch that swallows the error: a failed load must fail the action as a thrown error does today.
- Do not make checkout.ts smaller by moving its writers elsewhere in this step; staging placeOrder is code review step 3.7 and step 2.48.
- Do not cut more than one file's edges in one commit, and do not start the next commit before the test order of the previous deploy passed.
- Do not remove the express button's server facts (expressOn, expressAreaFor, klarnaMessaging) from the product page: they are what draws it.
- Do not add 'use server' to express-start.ts or checkout.ts: only the actions files are server actions.
