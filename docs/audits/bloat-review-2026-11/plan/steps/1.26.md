**Who:** a smaller model; a stronger one reads the diff before the push. **Goal:** No scheduled job can fail in silence: a job's entry function reports every error it catches through report(), which logs it and marks the run failed after the job's other parts have run, and the work seven routes do after answering is kept as a job of its own.

After: 0 silent catches, held by a scan test, and seven `route.<name>.work` rows in the ledger.

**Closes:** GAP-scheduled-s2. Each finding's file has the evidence.
