# Step 2.10: Let a recommending grid bring its own words

Phase 2 (Load on demand) of `docs/audits/bloat-review-2026-11.md`. Effort M. Read [how to carry out a step](../README.md) first.

**Who:** a stronger model. Commit 1 is specified and a cheap model can do it, but reaching 140,000 bytes means splitting zod and the page model out of typography, colour, theme-elements, part-css and page-content, where which symbols to move and where to stop is design judgement.

**Goal:** The grid view that a recommending grid loads after /api/recommendations answers takes its words as a prop from the browser text files and reaches no whole language, no zod and no page model, and the import scan holds that for scripts loaded later as well as first scripts.

**Why:** Today a store page with a recommending grid downloads 649,561 bytes of script once recommendations answer: all four storefront languages (318,693), zod (116,870) and the page model (74,836). After: at most 140,000 bytes, with none of the three in it.

**Closes:** [BN-16](../../findings/client-bundles/BN-16.md). Each finding's file has the evidence.
