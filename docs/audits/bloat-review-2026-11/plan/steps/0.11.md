# Step 0.11: Take two whole tables out of client scripts: the Work invoice's words and the AI manager's tool definitions

Phase 0 (Before the doors open) of `docs/audits/bloat-review-2026-11.md`. Effort S. Read [how to carry out a step](../README.md) first.

**Who:** a smaller model; a stronger one reads the diff before the push. Both changes are now specified to the prop, the import, the effect's flag and the language expression, and a unit test, the panel's byte budget and the build's chunk figures hold them, but one adds two server actions behind permission guards and the other moves props across about 20 client and server files where a Norwegian-only mistake passes the English tests, so a stronger model should read the diff. A smaller model may still do commit 1 alone. *(Rating checked twice, 12 November.)*

**Goal:** The Work invoice screen gets its words as props and the AI manager's chat gets a tool's label and the page's name without the tool definitions, the admin map or the Norwegian page list, so no first-load chunk is over 600,000 bytes and the chat's scripts are at most 130,000 bytes.

**Why:** Today the Work invoice screen imports the whole admin catalogue in both languages (one chunk of 1,704,595 bytes; the route is 4,274,839), and opening the AI manager fetches 496,206 bytes of script, about 437 KB of it three tables read for two labels. After: no chunk over 600,000, at most 26 routes over 1,300,000 (90 today), and at most 130,000 bytes on opening the chat.

**Closes:** [AC-7](../../findings/admin-browser/AC-7.md), [BN-7](../../findings/client-bundles/BN-7.md), [AC-8](../../findings/admin-browser/AC-8.md), [BN-15](../../findings/client-bundles/BN-15.md), [AI-4](../../findings/ai-calls/AI-4.md). Each finding's file has the evidence.
