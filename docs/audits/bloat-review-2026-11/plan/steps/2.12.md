# Step 2.12: Draw a listing's grid once and read its list once

Phase 2 (Load on demand) of `docs/audits/bloat-review-2026-11.md`. Effort M. Read [how to carry out a step](../README.md) first.

**Who:** a smaller model; a stronger one reads the diff before the push. The moves are specified line by line, but it changes a streaming boundary on a store's busiest page and edits two e2e locators, so a stronger model should read the diff and look at a filtered address before the push.

**Goal:** A product listing with Filter and sort (a built product grid, and the standard All products, category and tag pages) sends its unfiltered grid once, in the prerendered shell, streams only the controls, a chosen grid when the address asks for one, and Show more, and reads its list once per request.

**Why:** Today the served /s/demo/no/products holds each product's data-item-id 18 times in its markup and 18 in its payload for 9 products (the built grid is the Suspense fallback and an identical grid streams over it), the document is 170,329 bytes raw, and a standard category, tag or products page runs one uncached list query per unfiltered view. After: 9 and 9, at most 120,000 bytes, and no list query on an unfiltered first page.

**Closes:** [PL-6](../../findings/page-payload/PL-6.md), [PM-8](../../findings/page-builder-model/PM-8.md). Each finding's file has the evidence.
