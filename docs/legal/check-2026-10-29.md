## 1. No right of withdrawal for dated services and bookings

**What the code does.** Every product of kind `appointment`, `stay` or `rental` is saved with `delivery = 'service'` (`src/server/products.ts:722`). Every service line is then given the exclusion `dated_service` (`src/lib/checkout.ts:37-40`). `lineEligibility()` refuses every service line with the reason `booking` (`src/lib/withdrawal.ts:308` and `:312`). The product page shows the "no right of withdrawal" sentence for every product of those kinds (`src/components/product-parts.tsx:511-523`). Nothing checks what the service is.

**What the law says.**
- CRD Art. 16(l): no right for "the provision of accommodation other than for residential purpose, transport of goods, car rental services, catering or services related to leisure activities if the contract provides for a specific date or period of performance".
- Commission guidance 2021/C 525/01, section 5.11.6: the exception "should be interpreted narrowly". Its examples, taken from recital 49, are hotels, holiday cottages, cultural or sporting events, theatre performances on a specific date, and catering at a birthday or wedding party. It "should not apply to contracts for leisure activities that do not have a capacity limitation". Rental of trucks for carrying goods "could fall within" the words "transport of goods".
- Norway, angrerettloven § 22 m: "levering av innkvarteringstjenester for andre formål enn boligformål, transport av varer, bilutleietjenester, / catering og tjenester knyttet til fritidsaktiviteter når det i avtalen er fastsatt en bestemt dato / eller et bestemt tidsrom for utførelsen av slike tjenester".
- Sweden, 2 kap. 11 § 12 (2005:59): "kulturevenemang, idrottsevenemang eller någon annan liknande fritidsaktivitet, servering, catering eller någon annan liknande tjänst, inkvartering, varutransport eller biluthyrning, om näringsidkaren ska tillhandahålla tjänsten på en bestämd dag eller under en bestämd tidsperiod". The bill, prop. 2013/14:15, says on point 12: "Ett exempel på en fritidsaktivitet kan vara att konsumenten utövar en sport." It also says "Även biluthyrning undantas"; no other kind of rental is named.
- Denmark, forbrugeraftaleloven § 18 stk. 2 nr. 12: "levering af tjenesteydelser i form af logi undtagen til beboelsesformål, transport af varer, biludlejningsvirksomhed, forplejning eller tjenesteydelser i forbindelse med fritidstilbud, når det følger af aftalen, på hvilken dato eller inden for hvilken bestemt periode den pågældende tjenesteydelse skal udføres".
- **Services in general.** The period is 14 days from the conclusion of the contract: CRD Art. 9(2)(a); angrerettloven § 21 ("Angrefristen utløper 14 dager fra den dag avtalen om tjeneste ble inngått."); 2 kap. 12 §; § 19 stk. 2 nr. 1. The right ends once the service has been fully performed only if performance "has begun with the consumer's prior express consent and acknowledgement that he will lose his right of withdrawal once the contract has been fully performed": CRD Art. 16(a); § 22 c; 2 kap. 11 § 1; § 18 stk. 2 nr. 2. Under Art. 8(8) the trader "shall require" that express request. Without it the consumer bears no cost for a service performed during the withdrawal period: CRD Art. 14(4)(a)(ii); angrerettloven § 26 (as summarised on Lovdata, not quoted); DK § 25 stk. 1 nr. 1 requires the request before payment can be claimed.

## The 5 most important corrections

1. **Stop treating every appointment and rental as exempt.** `checkout.ts:39` marks every service line `dated_service`, and the product page says "Timer til en fast dato har ikke angrerett" / "Utleie på faste datoer har ikke angrerett". The law (CRD Art. 16(l), § 22 m, 2 kap. 11 § 12, § 18 stk. 2 nr. 12) exempts only accommodation, transport of goods, car rental, catering and leisure services on a set date, and the Commission says to read it narrowly. A massage is not shown to be "leisure" by any source I read, and the Swedish text (a leisure activity similar to cultural or sporting events) points against it. Make the exemption a per-product choice in the law's words, off by default. Holiday stays and car rental can stay exempt.
2. **Give services their right and collect the express request.** Let non-exempt services through the withdrawal function: 14 days from booking, full refund before performance. At checkout, ask for and record the express request and acknowledgement (Art. 8(8), 16(a); § 22 c) when the appointment falls within 14 days. Without it the right does not end on performance and the consumer owes nothing for what was performed (Art. 14(4)(a)(ii)). Until then, remove `excl.service_performed` from the starters.
3. **Put the withdrawal information on a durable medium.** The order email gives one line and links. Under C-49/11 a link is not enough. That extends the period by 12 months (CRD Art. 10, § 21 third paragraph, 1 year in SE). In Denmark the period does not start at all until the information is received on a durable medium (§ 19 stk. 3). The email also does not confirm the consent for downloads, which § 22 n, Art. 16(m)(iii), SE 2 kap. 11 § 11 and DK § 18 stk. 2 nr. 13 b require before the download exemption applies. Put the full information, the "no right" statements and the consent confirmation in the confirmation email itself.
4. **Fix the sealed-goods sentence.** "Denne varen er unntatt fra angreretten" is shown for sealed hygiene goods and sealed media, which keep the right until unsealed after delivery (Art. 16(e), (i); § 22 g, j). Show the existing sealed-goods line instead.
5. **Move the last day off weekends and holidays, and fix two VAT rows.** `withdrawalWindow()` closes the right on day 14 even when that day is a Saturday, Sunday or holiday. Reg. 1182/71 Art. 3(4), DK § 19 stk. 6 and SE lag 1930:173 2 § move it to the next working day. Also: Danish newspapers are 0 % (momsloven § 34 stk. 1 nr. 18), not 25 %. Norwegian periodicals are 0 % only under the conditions of mval § 6-3, not as a whole category.

Also worth doing: replace "så snart du upptäcker/opdager det" in the SV, DA and EN starters with the statutory complaint rules (SE 5 kap. 2 § and 4 kap. 14 §; DK §§ 81 and 83). In the Swedish starter, use the narrower Swedish wording of the dated-service exception.
