# Wave 10, units I and K: notes for the lead

Written by the unit's agent for `docs/decisions.md` and the parity rows; the lead copies them in. No schema or migration change.

## D372 (unit I): decision text

| D372 | **Partial fulfilment with a carrier, and a second parcel of the same order booked straight after the first (wave 10 unit I, `docs/wave-10-must-haves.md` 4); built.** The refusal "This order was just booked with Bring" was Kaizena Flow's own: `bringBook()`, `helthjemBook()` and `porterbuddyBook()` each refused any booking of an order that had a parcel of that carrier from the last two minutes, a double-click guard (D134) that also refused the second parcel of a partly sent order. Bring never refused one: its Booking request (`bookingBody()`) carries nothing of the order (no reference, no correlation id), so there was nothing of Bring's to make unique. The window is gone, replaced by what every carrier booking now shares (`src/server/carrier-booking.ts`): one booking of an order at a time, under a transaction-scoped advisory lock taken without waiting (`oneBookingAtATime()`, `carrier-book:{order}`; a second booking while the first is with the carrier is told *Another booking of this order is being made right now*, `BOOKING_BUSY`), the parcel checked under the lock before the carrier is asked or paid (`bookingPrecheck()`: `parcelPrecheck()`, and with `seen`, the order's fulfilment as the page showed it, `OrderFulfilment.basis`, a press made before the page showed the last parcel is refused as `changed`), then `markSent()` with the parcel's lines as before. The order page's three booking forms send `seen` (`BringBooking`, `ChosenDeliveryBooking`, `basis`). Reading the carriers' requests found the real cause of the same failure elsewhere: Porterbuddy's booking sent the order's number as its `Idempotency-Key`, so Porterbuddy would have answered a second parcel of the order with the first parcel's booking. A booking now carries `BookingRequest.parcelReference` (`parcelReference()`: the order's number for its first parcel, `{number}-{n}` for the n-th, undone parcels counted, read under the lock so a retry of a failed booking keeps its key), which Porterbuddy's adapter sends as the key; Helthjem's `reference` stays the order's number (it is a reference, not a key). Integration tests book part of an order with each carrier (a parcel naming only its lines and units, the order `paid` and *Partly sent* after it, the rest booked straight after as its own parcel and the order sent, a stale `seen` refused before the carrier is asked, nothing left to send refused before the carrier is asked; Bring also two bookings at once, the second told to wait; Porterbuddy each parcel's own key against a fake that answers a repeated key as Porterbuddy does): `bring-shipping.int.test.ts`, `helthjem-shipping.int.test.ts`, `porterbuddy-shipping.int.test.ts`. | 2026-10-28 | The parity row's gap named two things: no carrier booking of part of an order was tested, and two Bring parcels of one order could not be booked back to back. The spec said a second booking "waits and retries"; there was nothing at Bring to wait for, so the guard that caused it was replaced instead, keeping what it was for (no parcel paid for twice by a double press or a second tab). No migration. |

## D374 (unit K): decision text

| D374 | **Labels for several orders in one action (wave 10 unit K, `docs/wave-10-must-haves.md` 4); built.** The Orders list's bulk bar gains *Book labels* (`BULK_ACTIONS` `book_labels`, `orders:write`, at most `BULK_PRINT_MAX` 100 orders a request, ticked or all matching, as its labels are printed together and each order waits for its carrier's answer). After a confirmation that says what will happen (the carrier the customer chose at checkout, or Posten / Bring's home parcel `5600` for an order that chose none, `BULK_BRING_SERVICE`; the weight from the products; each order marked sent; nothing shipped while a carrier is in its test environment) and *Tell the customers* (off by default), `runBulk()` books each order's units still to send as one parcel, one order after another, only through the order page's own functions (`bookOrderLabel()` in `src/server/bulk-labels.ts` calls `bringBook()`, `helthjemBook()` or `porterbuddyBook()`, so D372's lock, the parcel's checks, the test environment and `markSent()` are theirs, never a second path). *Mark as sent*'s blocks come first (`markSentBlock()`: not paid, copied, already sent, withdrawn, a change waiting, units waiting for stock, a pickup order), then a local delivery (`local_order`), a carrier that cannot book (`carrier_cannot_book`, PostNord), a carrier not connected (`no_carrier`), something left to send without a weight (`no_weight`, `remainingWeights()` over `commerce.line_to_send()`), a test booking (`test_booking`: booked at the carrier's test environment, nothing sent, no label) and the carrier's own refusal (`carrier_refused`, its sentence kept as `BulkRefusal.detail` and shown with the order's number). Each refusal is listed by order number with its reason (en and nb in the `orders` catalogue). The labels booked come back as `BulkResult.labels`, in the order they were booked, and the result panel offers *Print the n labels*: `/admin/{store}/orders/labels?ids=` (`orders:read`, `labelsToPrint()`: this store's parcels with a carrier label, not undone, others counted and left out) shows them one after another, each the order's own label route (`carrierLabel()`: fetched from the carrier's own host with the store's agreement when it is shown, never kept) in the browser's PDF viewer, where it is printed. One audit entry per batch with counts (`order.bulk_labels_booked`: requested, applied, refused, labels; never an order number); each booking keeps its own `shipping.*_booked` entry. Held by `bulk-labels.int.test.ts` (faked Bring and Helthjem: a flat-rate order to Bring's home parcel, a partly sent order's remaining unit only, Helthjem for its chooser, PostNord, no weight, a carrier's refusal and an unpaid order each named, the labels in order and printable, run again books nothing twice, the test environment, a carrier not connected, more than 100 refused), `order-actions.int.test.ts` (no access without `orders:write`), and the unit and view tests (`order-bulk.test.ts`, `order-list-admin.test.ts`, `orders-views.test.ts`). | 2026-10-28 | The parity row's criterion 3: selecting several orders books and prints their labels in one action, reporting any that failed. One PDF of all labels would need a PDF library the app does not have; a page of the labels one after another, each printed from its own viewer, needs none and keeps the rule that a label is fetched when printed and never stored. No migration. |

## New `gap` sentence for `orders.shipping-label-purchase-and-printing`

Booking from the order with Bring (D134), Porterbuddy (D137) and Helthjem (D138) for parcels in Norway; the booked parcel names its lines (D174), a second parcel of the same order is booked straight after the first (D372), and the label is fetched from the carrier's own host when printed and never stored (`carrier-label.ts`). *Book labels* on the Orders list (D374) books up to 100 orders' remaining units with their carriers through the same functions, names each refusal by order number (the carrier's own sentence included) and shows the labels one after another to print (`bulk-labels.int.test.ts`); it prints them from a page of the carriers' PDFs, not as one merged PDF. PostNord cannot book (D136, waiting for its EDI specification). In the store's test environment nothing real is booked. Undoing a parcel does not cancel the carrier's booking. Nothing has been verified against a carrier's live service (bucket B).
