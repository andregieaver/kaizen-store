# D428: The chat answers from everything the site publishes

**The chat answers from everything the site publishes.** Whatever the site's own pages, articles or knowledge base speak of is on topic for its chat, a comparison with another company or product included: the chat calls `search_content` with the question before it ever says it can only help with the site, answers from what it finds and says nothing of the other company the passages do not say.
What the search does not cover stays off topic, and the rules against best-price and urgency claims stand. The agents' eval has a case for it (`chat-comparison`): its first live run failed it (the model still turned "Are you cheaper or better than Amazon?" away unsearched while "other companies" stood in the list to turn away), so the rule was made the order to search first.
The knowledge base also reads every published content entry (D326) of the site: its title and each field shown on the site with words in it ("Label: value"; never a field for staff only, a picture, a file or a field its rules hide), at its page's address where its type has pages (`entryKnowledgeText()`, `knowledge_chunks.entry_id`); a change to the type's fields cuts its entries anew. Articles are at their own blog's address (D341), cut anew when that address changes.
