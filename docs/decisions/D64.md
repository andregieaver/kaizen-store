# D64: On phones the cart slides out over the page

**On phones the cart slides out over the page.** Opening the cart from any page of a market (header, bottom bar, "Go to cart") is intercepted by the market layout's `@drawer` slot (`(.)cart`), which draws the same `CartContents` as the cart page in `CartDrawer`, a panel from the right over the page the shopper was on; the address becomes `/cart`, so back and close return to that page, and a link inside (a product, checkout) leaves through `@drawer/[...rest]`, which draws nothing.
Loading `/cart` itself, and every screen from 768 px, shows the cart page: the drawer reloads the address there. It works on stores' own hosts, whose rewrites the interception follows. The drawer is always a modal dialog, in the top layer over the header and bars; Next.js keeps a page left by a link hidden, which takes a dialog out of the top layer, so the drawer closes when hidden and opens again as a modal when shown.
Stores can choose to open it as soon as something is added (`stores.open_cart_on_add`, the Design page's Cart card; `useOpenCartAfterAdd()`), on phones only.
