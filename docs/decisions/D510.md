# D510: Roles on the platform's team

**Roles on the platform's team (C1 of `docs/platform-parity-plan.md`, its second step after D471, 9 November 2026, `accounts.platform_role`, `src/lib/platform-access.ts`, `src/server/platform-team.ts`, Platform → Settings → Team).** The owner: *start with 1 and 2*. Until now every platform admin could do everything.
Now an **owner** is a platform admin as before (`platform_admin`: everything, settings, the team, the AI manager, an owner of Kaizena Flow's store), and four other roles are kept in `platform_role` (a check keeps the two apart): **Support** (the support desk, the chat assistant, deciding requests for stores), **Content** (Kaizena Flow's website and themes), **Finance** (the plans, their prices and limits, discounts, referrals, reminders, the books and payouts) and **Read-only**.
Every role reads every page of the platform admin; each changes only its own area; settings, the team, the AI manager and drastic store actions (suspending, closing, limits, two-step resets) stay the owners'.
A guard's area is written in code by the folder it sits in (`requirePlatform(area)`; pages `requirePlatformReader()`), never read from the address a request came to, because a server action can be posted to any page's address; server modules ask `platformCan()` where they asked `platformAdmin` for something a role may do (themes, demo stores, content packs, the site's design, templates, referrals, the support desk, the page editor and its pictures), and the rest still ask for an owner, so
anything not converted stays closed to the other roles. A scan test holds every platform guard to its folder's area and keeps the readers' guard out of anything that changes something. Everyone on the team is held at two-step sign-in. In Kaizena Flow's own store a member is an admin in the store role of their work (Support agent, Content, Analytics for finance, Read-only), set by the team page in the same transaction; the database lets no one else in and never makes a member its owner.
The team page shows each person's role and what it does; an owner adds someone with a role (Read-only unless chosen), changes a role or removes someone, after a fresh sign-in, never their own place and never the last owner; the others see the list. A quiet line on a page someone may only look at says what their role changes instead. The AI manager is shown to owners only.
