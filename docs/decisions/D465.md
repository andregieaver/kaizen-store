# D465: Kaizena Flow's own integrations, and whatever stores get the platform gets

**Kaizena Flow's own integrations, and whatever stores get the platform gets (6 November 2026, `docs/platform-parity.md`, `src/lib/platform-integrations.ts`, `src/server/platform-integrations.ts`, `/admin/platform/integrations`).** The owner found that the platform had no Integrations: a store connects Zapier, Make and Slack (D41, D101), own endpoints (D354), Tripletex and Fiken, carriers, while Kaizena Flow's own site and store could connect nothing.
The owner's rule, now in CLAUDE.md (Conventions): anything built for store owners or stores is built for the platform and its own store too, in the same change or as its next step named in the decision; a feature stays store-only only where it cannot apply (Kaizena Flow sells plans through Stripe, not goods), and says why. `docs/platform-parity.md` lists what the platform lacks, from comparing the two admins' sections.
First of them: Settings → Integrations on the platform, the stores' three services for Kaizena Flow's own events (a store requested, made or opened, a plan started, changed, cancelled or not paid, a notice of illegal content), each queued by the database where it happens (`commerce.queue_platform_event()`; a new store at commit, so a demo store, a template or a sandbox flagged in its own transaction is never news, and a sandbox kept as a store is), sent every minute in the integrations cron
with the stores' sender and tries, kept 30 days. Zapier and Make get a request's name and email (the operator's own leads); Slack never an email, and a notice never its sender or words; blanking a request empties every delivery about it. Separate tables, not a null `store_id` on the stores' (their key is the store, and their sender, payloads and endpoint health are a store's), sharing only the pure parts and the sender.
