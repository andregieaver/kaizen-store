# D292: The payment-provider adapter

**The payment-provider adapter (wave 4 unit E).** `docs/wave-4-payments.md` 7 is the contract.
E1 (built first, nothing changing for shoppers or staff): a provider's answer about a payment is one shape (`PaymentAnswer`) and one function decides what becomes of the order from it (`paymentFate()`, `applyPaymentAnswer()`), so Stripe and the next provider share the order's completion, cancellation, confirmation and every step after; the late read, closing open payments and refunds go to the payment's own provider through `PaymentProvider` (`refresh()`, `close()`, `refund()`), with Stripe's
code moved behind it unchanged.
E2 (Mollie): an owner connects the store's own Mollie account by approving Kaizena Flow at Mollie (OAuth, the refresh token encrypted in `mollie_connections`) and chooses Mollie per mode (`checkout_providers`); a cart's order then asks who pays and where it goes (Mollie's page asks neither), is paid on Mollie's hosted page through the Payments API with Kaizena Flow's fee as Mollie's application fee (capped at half the payment), offers what Mollie has active less pay-later methods and what the
owner switched off for the country, and is answered by a webhook that only names the payment, which is always fetched from Mollie; refunds go to Mollie with an idempotency key and Mollie's refunds and chargebacks are recorded as Stripe's are. Subscriptions, deposits, hosts' listings, boxes and pay links stay on Stripe. Live Mollie payments wait for Kaizena Flow's Mollie app; everything is tested against a recorded fake of Mollie's API.
Migration versions (applied by CI, 20 October 2026): `20261019223435_mollie`, `20261019223525_mollie_rules`. Advisors checked after: no new warning (the standing ones only); INFO only (RLS without policies on `checkout_providers` and `mollie_connections`, their `updated_by`/`connected_by` indexes not used yet).

Date: 2026-10-19
