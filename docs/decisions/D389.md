# D389: PSI

**PSI: security and operations (wave 11 unit J, `docs/wave-11-core-and-psi.md` J): built.** **Deploys verified.** After every call of Vercel's deploy hook (the `pending`, `release` and `redeploy` jobs of `.github/workflows/ci.yml`), `scripts/await-deploy.mjs` reads `/api/health` every 15 seconds until production reports the pushed commit (`commit`, the deployment's own), or a later one whose history includes it (`deployVerdict()`, the repository's history fetched once when too shallow), and
fails the job after `DEPLOY_WAIT_SECONDS` (1,200) saying what production reports; the address is the variable `PRODUCTION_URL`, else https://kaizenaflow.com. It reads nothing secret.
Whether the deploy should also wait for the checks stays decision 6 (D5 deploys at once). **A site-wide Report-Only policy** (`siteReportOnlyCsp()` in `src/lib/csp.ts`, sent on every path by `baseRouteHeaders()` with `Reporting-Endpoints`): the origins the site loads named (Stripe's forms and Connect components, the consented ad and statistics tags, the storage host and its realtime socket, YouTube's and Vimeo's players), `object-src 'none'`, no `'unsafe-eval'`, and `report-uri`/`report-to` the
site's own `/api/csp-report`, which logs each violation as one `csp.violation` line (`cspViolations()` in `src/lib/csp-report.ts`: the directive, the blocked origin or keyword and `admin` or `site`, never the page's or the blocked address in full) under the `csp` budget, and keeps nothing.
The pay routes keep their enforced policy, which the Report-Only one allows all of, and `e2e/csp.spec.ts` counts enforced violations only. **Budgets** (SEC-10): `takeBudget()` takes a null store (Kaizena Flow's own site; the counters' key treats nulls as equal) and `budgetVisitor()` (a daily-changing hash of the address); `/api/consent` is budgeted as `consent` and `/api/ab/assign` as `ab` (event and exposure already counted). **Security logs** (SEC-11): `admin_sign_in.failed` (each wrong
password), `admin_sign_in.locked`, `admin_sign_in.two_step_failed` (account id and the way) and `auth.permission_refused` (account, store and keys, from `requirePermission()` and its siblings) through `log()`, never an email or a name. **A failing job's alert** (REL-02): when a job's runs ending in an error reach `FAILING_AFTER` (3), `record()` in `src/server/cron-runs.ts` calls `alertFailingJob()` (`src/server/cron-alerts.ts`), which emails each platform admin in their language
(`staffEmails.jobFailing`, kind `platform.job_failing`) with the job, the count, when, the last good run and the last error, once per turn (the key is the job and its last good run). **Course changes.** (1) No nonce: a nonce needs every page rendered per request, which the prerendered pages (cache components, D211) are not, so `script-src` keeps `'unsafe-inline'` as the pay routes' enforced policy does; enforcing a policy site-wide waits for what the reports show. (2) Reports are log lines, not
a table: the platform reads them in the logs, and nothing of a visitor is kept. (3) A later push's deployment that includes the commit counts as it deployed. No migration.
