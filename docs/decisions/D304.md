# D304: Accounts in depth

**Accounts in depth (wave 5 unit J, `docs/wave-5-marketing.md` 11), built in three parts; J1 saved addresses.** `commerce.customer_addresses` (store, customer, the shopper's `label`, `address` in the shape orders keep, `country`, `is_default_shipping`, `is_default_billing`; one default of each kind by partial unique indexes, at most 20 a customer by `customer_addresses_max()` under the customer's lock, deleted with the customer) is written only by `src/server/customer-addresses.ts`
(`saveAddress()`, `saveDefaultAddress()`, `removeAddress()`, `setDefaultAddress()`; another store's or customer's address is `not_found`), which keeps `customers.address` equal to the default shipping address (empty without one) so its readers keep working; the first address is the default for both, and a removed default passes to the newest left. The migration copied every saved `customers.address` with a country into a first address.
My account's details form edits the default shipping address; *Saved addresses* opens `/account/addresses` (list, add, change, remove, the defaults; new addresses in the market's country). Where checkout asks the address itself (Mollie, gift cards paying it all, payment terms) a signed-in customer picks a saved address of the market's country (`choicesFor()`, `Dropdown`), filling fields that stay editable; Stripe's hosted page asks its own address.
Personal data (export section `addresses`, source `saved`; erasure deletes), `never` copied. Words `m.account.addresses` (nb, sv, da, en by hand).
Not yet: saving a new checkout address after payment, a country choice on the Addresses page. **J2:** *Your orders* (`/account/orders`, an after-sale page; My account shows the newest five and *See all orders*) reads its address only through `parseAccountOrderParams()` (`src/lib/account-orders.ts`: `q` as D173's `searchWords()`, `state` `open`/`sent`/`delivered`/`returned`, `after` a keyset cursor) and lists only through `findCustomerOrders()` (the signed-in customer's own orders, the list's old
rules, number, line title or SKU and tracking number with every word bound and escaped, 20 a page by `(placed_at, id)`); the support tool keeps `listCustomerOrders()`.
Words `m.account.orderList` (nb, sv, da, en by hand). **J3:** a newsletter form's `incentive` (a discount with single-use codes) and `tags` (up to 5): on `confirmSignup()` only, `tagFromForm()` tags the confirmed address (no actor, audited with the count) and `issueSignupCode()` makes one code item under the discount's lock, once per store, discount and address (`commerce.signup_incentives`, written only there), and emails it (`form.welcome_code`, words `m.news.welcome*`, by hand); nothing while
the discount is off, outside its dates, full or another store's. Both fields stay on the server and are dropped when a page is copied. Personal data (export section `marketing`, erasure deletes), `never` copied.
