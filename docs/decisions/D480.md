# D480: My account in four tabs, for Homer first

**My account in four tabs, for Homer first (5 November 2026, `src/lib/account-tabs.ts` pure, `src/components/account-tabs.tsx`, `src/components/account-tabs-view.tsx`, `src/app/s/[store]/[market]/account/account-section.tsx`).** A signed-in shopper's My account had grown into one long column: up to seven cards, five orders, subscriptions, the details form, the picture, the password, marketing email and Your data, so the one thing most come for (where is my order) sat between things they rarely
touch.
It is now four tabs under the greeting and *Sign out*: **Overview** (the three latest orders, each with its state as a label, and *See all orders*; running subscriptions with *Manage*; then the store's cards for this customer in a grid: company, own catalogue, subscription box, reviews, bonus, referrals, creator), **Orders** (the 20 latest, then the order list of its own, D304 J2, to search or for older ones; with none, *Go to the shop*), **Profile** (the details form, its *Save* now the theme's
primary button, a card to Saved addresses, the picture) and **Settings** (password, marketing email, Your data).
Nothing is said of what the customer does not have: no subscriptions heading without one. **The tab is in the address** (`?tab=orders|profile|settings`, the overview none, so every earlier link to `/account` opens it; anything else, `?tab=register` included, is the overview): each tab is a real link, so it works before the script and without it, and with it a tab opens at once (`history.pushState`, which Next follows through `useSearchParams()`), so Back, a reload and a shared link open the same
tab; the server draws every tab's content, the others hidden, so what was typed in one stays on coming back, and a save's `refresh()` comes back on the same tab. A link marked `data-account-tab` (the overview's *See all orders*) opens its tab the same way. ARIA tabs once the page has woken up (arrow keys, Home, End, one tab in the Tab order); a picture over each tab's word on a phone, beside it on a wider screen, so four fit at 360 pixels.
Loaded only where drawn (D211, `lazy/shop.tsx`), with the server's tab drawn as it stands until the script is there. The pages under My account go back to their tab: the orders and an order to Orders, Saved addresses to Profile, Your data to Settings.

Date: 2026-11-05

No migration. Words in the four hand-written languages (`account.tabs`, `recentOrders`, `toShop`); the others follow from the English catalogue (D111).
