# D473: Changing the business behind a store

**Changing the business behind a store (B8 of `docs/own-books-and-sales.md` section 4.5), built in steps.** An ENK that becomes an AS (or any change of the legal business behind a store) from a date: from that date new documents carry the new business, the books and reports know which business a period belongs to, and a checklist says what must be set up again. **Step 1, the change (built).** `commerce.store_business_changes` keeps each change: the new business's legal name, form (`enk`, `as`,
`asa`, `nuf`, `da`, `ans`, `sa`, `other`), organisation number (null until it is registered: the change waits for it), VAT registration and number, address (null keeps the store's), its first day in the store's time zone, and a prefix of its own for each of the invoice, credit note, Work invoice and Work credit note series.
One is planned at a time, by the store's owner, for a day after today and within two years (`cleanBusinessChange()`, `src/lib/business-change.ts`; `planBusinessChange()`, `cancelBusinessChange()`, `src/server/business-change.ts`).
`commerce.apply_business_change(store)` makes it take effect in one transaction once its day has come and its numbers are known: the store's legal name, number and address, its tax profile's VAT registration and number (the check of the old number forgotten) and Work's, and the prefixes; what the store was is kept (`before`) and where each series stood (`series_start`, such as `AS-F-1043`). **The numbers go on** from where they are, with no gap and no number used twice; whether a new business
may continue the old one's series is still for an accountant [U], and a series starting again at 1 needs a business on each document (a later step if they say so). **Exact at the day:** every issuer (`make_order_invoice`, `make_credit_note`, `make_edit_documents`, `issue_work_invoice`, `credit_work_invoice`, patched in place) calls it before it numbers anything, so the first document of the day is the new business's whenever the dispatcher's `businessChanges` job runs; the job applies a change
nothing issued and draws each changed store again (`refreshed_at`). The sales series' guard lets a prefix change once numbers are issued only inside that function. **An order placed before and paid after** states the new business's VAT number (`commerce.invoice_seller_vat()`): the business that issues the invoice is the one it names. A credit note still names the business of the invoice it corrects.
Not changed by it: the store's country (a business moving abroad is not this), the order series, Stripe and the payment providers, the accounting connection, the bank account, legal pages (step 2's checklist says what to do about each).
Tests: `business-change.test.ts`, `src/db/business-changes.test.ts`, `business-change.int.test.ts` (the shop's first invoice of the day and Work's next under the new business, its prefix and numbers going on). **The platform:** Kaizena Flow's own store is a store like any other here; Kaizena Flow's own sales are Stripe Billing's invoices, whose seller is set in Stripe, so a change of the platform's business is Stripe's and its `platform_settings.business` (step 2 names it on the platform's
checklist). **Step 2, the owner's screen and checklist (built).** Settings → Company has a section of its own (`BusinessChangeSection`, words in the server-only area `business`): *Plan a change of business* opens the form with the name to type, a limited company and the next 1 January chosen, VAT registration ticked and the number left for later; the address and each series' prefix are one step further.
Once planned it says "Your business becomes Human Web AS on 1 January 2027", the days left (or that its day came and what is missing keeps it from taking effect), and a checklist (`businessChecklist()`) of only what applies to the store: the organisation number and VAT registration (done when given), the invoice numbers (Kaizena Flow's: they go on from the first number shown), the bank account on Work invoices, payments (Stripe and the providers), the accounting connection (done when it books
into the new company's number), the legal pages (done when none names the business as it is), Kaizena Flow's invoices for the plan, telling the clients, and e-invoices (Kaizena Flow's, when they go live). What happens elsewhere the owner ticks off (`store_business_changes.checked`, `tickBusinessStep()`), each with a link to where it is done; the change can be changed or called off until it takes effect, and the changes that took effect are listed with the business before.
Only the owner plans, ticks and calls off (`business-actions.ts`, an owner-only action in `permissions.scan.test.ts`).
`e2e/signed-in/business-change.spec.ts` plans one, ticks a step and calls it off. **Step 3, reports split at the date (built).** A tax report whose period spans a change that took effect (the VAT report's range, an OSS quarter, an IOSS month) says so and opens each business's part as a VAT report of its own days (`businessSplit()`): the documents of a day are the business's that was behind the store that day, since the issuers apply the change first, so a period cut at the day is a period cut by
business. Left as they were: a credit note issued after the change for an invoice before it names the old business but falls in the new one's days (its accountant books it), an OSS or IOSS return is drawn for the whole quarter or month (each business files its own; the note says so), and Work's reports and the ledger (B2) do not split yet. **B8 is built**; the rest of section 4.5's changes are the owner's with their adviser.
Steps: 1 the change, applied exactly at the start of its day, with its own invoice prefixes; 2 the owner's screen and the checklist; 3 reports split at the date.
