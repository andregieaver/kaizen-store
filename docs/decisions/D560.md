# D560: An Instagram feed with hashtag filtering

**An Instagram feed with hashtag filtering (10 November 2026, `src/lib/instagram.ts`, `src/server/instagram.ts`, `InstagramBlock`, `src/components/instagram-section.tsx`, `/admin/{store}/integrations/instagram`, `/admin/platform/instagram`).** The owner asked for an Instagram feed component with hashtag filtering; asked where the posts come from, they chose the owner's own account filtered by hashtags (Meta's public hashtag search needs App Review, allows 30 hashtags a week and would put
strangers' photos on an owner's site). **Connecting:** Kaizena Flow's Instagram app (`INSTAGRAM_APP_ID`, `INSTAGRAM_APP_SECRET`, "Instagram API with Instagram Login", the one permission `instagram_business_basic`, redirect `{SITE_URL}/api/integrations/instagram/callback`) signs the owner in with Instagram (a business or creator account), the code becomes a long-lived token (60 days), kept encrypted in `commerce.instagram_accounts` (one per store, or Kaizena Flow's with a null store;
`settings:write`, or the platform's `settings`; audited `store.instagram_connected`/`disconnected`, `platform.…`).
Without the app's keys the page says Instagram cannot be connected yet. **Upkeep:** the dispatcher's job `instagram` (`refreshInstagramFeeds()`, never throws, 20 accounts a run) refreshes a token with under 30 days left and reads the latest 60 posts once an hour into the row; a failure is kept in `problem`, shown on the admin page, and the posts kept stay. **Showing:** the Instagram feed component (builder palette, store pages and Kaizena Flow's own) reads only the kept posts
(`instagramPosts()`, cached under `instagram:{owner}`), keeps those with any (or all) of its hashtags (`feedPosts()`, `cleanHashtags()`: letters of any script, digits and underscores, ten at most), newest first, up to 24 in all (`limit`), as a grid or, as the owner asked during the work, a carousel (`display`, the site's own `Carousel` with its arrows, dots, rest, rewind and autoplay settings, and *part of the next* `peek`); `columns` is how many across on larger screens (a grid's columns or a
carousel's posts at a time, one to six) and `phoneColumns` on phones (one to three, two unless set).
A picture is a square linking to the post, its description the caption's words; a video post plays in its square when pressed (`<video controls muted preload="none">`, its cover as the poster, never by itself and nothing loaded before), or with *Play videos here* off shows its cover with a play mark linking to Instagram; captions under them if chosen and a *Follow @account* link unless off. **Privacy:** pictures and videos are served by the site (`/api/instagram/{owner}/{post}`, `?video` for a
video: only a kept post's, fetched only from Instagram's media hosts; a picture an image of 8 MB at most, a video streamed with the browser's `Range` passed on, 300 MB at most; cached a day by the CDN), so a visitor's browser loads nothing from Meta and no consent is needed for it.
Captions are the owner's words, drawn as text. **Not done:** no real Instagram account was connected in this change (the sandbox has no app keys): the screens were checked with a seeded account; the app must be created in Meta's developer console with the keys above before owners can connect. Platform parity: Kaizena Flow connects its own account on Platform → Instagram and its pages take the component. Tests: `instagram.test.ts`.
