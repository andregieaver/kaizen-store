# D30: Every store has a header and footer its owner edits

**Every store has a header and footer its owner edits: a logo, a header menu and a footer menu, in each of the store's languages.** Kept on the store as one JSON value (`stores.navigation`), loaded and cached with the store, so pages make no extra query. A menu link goes to the front page, a product (named by handle, so copies of the template keep working), My account, the cart, or a web address (http(s), or a path in the store); at most 8 header and 12 footer links.
A product link left without text takes the product's title. The header stays at the top and slides away while the shopper scrolls down, and comes back on the way up; it holds the logo (or name), the header menu on computers, a country chooser, My account and the cart with its count.
On phones a Menu button opens a menu that slides in from the left (a modal dialog: focus stays in it; Escape, tapping outside, choosing a link or swiping it away closes it), and a bar fixed along the bottom (Home, Menu, My account, Cart) slides away while scrolling up, when the header returns, so only one shows. On product pages that bar becomes the price, a variant choice and Add to cart, following the purchase option chosen on the page.
The footer shows the business details the law requires on every page, the footer menu and the countries. New stores start with the template's logo and menus.

Date: 2026-09-24

The logo is uploaded like product pictures (shrunk in the browser, up to 800 px) and shown up to 40 px high; PNG, JPEG or WebP, not SVG, which the browser cannot shrink. Menus are one level deep: there are no collections or content pages to group yet.
The phone menu and bottom bar are each rendered in their own Suspense boundary: React counts everything outside boundaries towards a 12.8 kB budget, beyond which a page's content is sent in a block that needs JavaScript to show; keeping these enhancements out keeps product pages readable without it. The motion is off for shoppers who ask for reduced motion.
