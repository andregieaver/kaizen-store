# D850: Subscribers show and filter by the form and page they signed up on

**Subscribers show and filter by the form and page they signed up on (asked by the owner; 20 November 2026).** A subscriber who joined
by a form kept only that it was "a form" and the form's id (`source_ref`); the page was not kept, and the list could not be narrowed.

- **Kept:** `marketing_subscribers.source_path`, the page a form's sign-up was sent from (a path on the site, as the form sent it, at most
  500 characters, only for `source = 'form'`: `marketing_subscribers_source_path`). `joinList()` takes `sourcePath`; `src/server/forms.ts`
  passes the request's path for a sign-up without confirmation and the kept send's path when a link is confirmed. An address already
  subscribed keeps where it first joined.
- **Filled for older rows** (`20261120130251_subscriber_source_path_fill`): from the newest kept response of the same form and address
  (a form record's sign-up is kept as a response with its page, D705), else from the last 30 days of a page form's sends
  (`form_submissions`). Older sign-ups whose response is gone say *Page not known*.
- **Shown:** each row of Subscribers says the form's name and the page (*Winter list · /blog/vinter*), a form since deleted as *A form
  since deleted* (a uuid no form has) and a page's own sign-up form from before forms were records as *A sign-up form on a page*; one subscriber's page names the form (opening its editor: the store's, or Kaizena Flow's own for the house store's list,
  D467) and the page. The export gains `form` and `page`, last, so readers of the older columns keep them.
- **Filtered:** Subscribers narrows to one form, one page, or both, beside the search and status (`listSubscribers()`'s `form` and `path`;
  the choices from `subscriberSources()`, each with its count, at most 50 of each, the busiest first).
- **Platform:** Kaizena Flow's own sign-ups are kept on its house store's list (D467), so its Subscribers page has the same.
- Not changed: segments, broadcasts and the Admin API do not filter by form or page yet; the forms' own responses already list each
  sign-up with its page.

Migrations `20261120130246_subscriber_source_path`, `20261120130251_subscriber_source_path_fill`, `20261120130306_subscriber_source_path_check`.
Tests: `forms-admin.int.test.ts` (a sign-up through a placed form keeps its form and page; the list narrowed by form, page and both; the
filters' choices; the export; a deleted form), `src/db/marketing-subscribers.test.ts` (the path's rule), `e2e/signed-in/subscribers.spec.ts`.
