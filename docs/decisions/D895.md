# D895: The incident log and its autopilot: one log of errors, warnings and enhancements, worked by Claude Code on the owner's s

Date: 2026-11-25

**One log, worked by Claude Code (20 to 26 November 2026, the owner).** Every error, warning and suggested enhancement on the platform
lands in one log; Claude Code sessions fix what they can through the normal CI and deploy, and an entry is done once its problem has stayed
away. The plan, its phases and what each built: `docs/audits/incident-autopilot-2026-11.md`; the run: `docs/runbooks/incident-autopilot.md`.

- **Enhancements wait for the owner's Approve**, held by the database (`platform_incidents_move`: `proposed` only becomes `approved` or
  `dismissed`). An idea the owner writes is approved when saved.
- **On the owner's Claude subscription, never on API billing.** One scheduled task, woken by the app when there is work (at most once per
  the owner's gap, 4 hours by default) and nightly at 03:00 Oslo. The platform API's scope `incidents` is its one write (D518 asked for a
  decision for any write: this is it), the incident log only.
- **Proposals** come from the nightly session (the code, the log, the figures) and from the platform's AI on Mondays (counts the platform
  already keeps about how owners use the admin, checked against the figures in code).
- **Telling the owner:** a push at once when something needs them, and a summary email every day at 07:00 Oslo.
- **To the owner instead of `main`:** payments and payouts, prices and VAT, legal texts, sign-in and security, deleting data, and a
  migration that drops or renames.
- **Admin heatmaps and recordings** are a new processing with Kaizena Flow as controller (`docs/dpia-admin-use.md`). Built locked (the
  owner, 26 November 2026): the switch opens only once a Legal account has answered every question under Legal reviews and the owner
  confirms the signed assessment; only a store's owner is asked, about their own use, never their staff; each choice is kept as a record.

Idle cost: one dispatcher job of three statements a run when nothing waits; one email a day to each platform admin; one model call a week
when something is worth reading; one read on an owner's store home and account page.
