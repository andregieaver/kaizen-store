# D895: The incident log and its autopilot: one log of errors, warnings and enhancements, worked by Claude Code on the owner's s

Date: 2026-11-25

**One log, worked by Claude Code (20 to 25 November 2026, the owner).** Every error, warning and suggested enhancement on the platform lands
in one log, and Claude Code sessions work through it on their own: fix, test, push to `main` through the normal CI and deploy, and mark the
entry done once the problem has stayed away. The plan with its steps is `docs/audits/incident-autopilot-2026-11.md`.

- **Enhancements wait for the owner's Approve.** Errors and warnings are fixed without asking; a proposal is never built until approved,
  held by the database (`platform_incidents_move`: `proposed` only becomes `approved` or `dismissed`). An idea the owner writes is approved
  when saved.
- **On the owner's subscription, never on API billing.** The sessions are one Claude Code scheduled task: woken by the app when there is
  work (at most once per the owner's gap, 4 hours by default, adjustable) and once a night at 03:00 Oslo. A quiet day is one short session.
- **Two sources of proposals:** the nightly session, from the code, the log and the platform's figures; and the platform's AI, from how
  store owners use the admin, read through the platform's own heatmaps and recordings of the admin (asked first, all text masked, data
  screens never recorded; phase 3, after the owner's privacy notice and a lawyer's answer).
- **Telling the owner:** a push at once when something needs them, and a summary email every day at 07:00 Oslo time.
- **Areas that go to the owner instead of to `main`:** payments and payouts, prices and VAT, legal texts, sign-in and security settings,
  anything that deletes data, and any migration that drops or renames.

Phase 1 (25 November 2026): the log (`platform_incidents`, its events, `platform_autopilot_runs`), its one writer
(`src/server/incidents.ts`), the daily upkeep job, and three sources inside the app (failing jobs, a stopped scheduler, failed status
checks), the page `/admin/platform/incidents` (Needs you, Being fixed, Fixed lately; Add an idea; the autopilot's switch and gap), the
instant push and the daily summary email at seven in Oslo. Store owners get no such page: the fixes are changes to the one codebase, which
only the platform owns (the plan's section 9). Idle cost: one dispatcher job of three statements a run when nothing waits, and one email
a day to each platform admin.
