# D829: Forms that answer: each problem under its field, Saved that clears on the next edit, and every admin form busy while it works

**An admin form answers where it was sent from (code review step 1.15, finding FS6; 19 November 2026).** `ActionForm` shows each problem a refusal names (`fields`) right under its field, after its hint: at the foot of the kit's `Field` (`data-field`), after a raw label, after
a group's fieldset. The field is `aria-invalid`, the line joins its `aria-describedby`, and the first in the page's order is brought into view and focused; problems that name no field stay in the list at the form's foot, scrolled to. The form owns its DOM and puts the lines there
itself (`form-answer.ts`), so a server page's field needs no state. *Saved* is a common word (`common.form.saved`, en and nb; the action's words and `successMessage` still win) and goes on the first `input` or `change` after it. While it works a form is `aria-busy`, takes no second
submission and marks the pressed button `data-submitting`: `admin.css` spins that one and lets no button of the form be pressed, so the kit's `Button` stays a server component. `SubmitButton` and `ConfirmButton` read the work from `FormWorkContext` (`form-work.ts`), as `useFormStatus`
does not see an action the form calls from its own transition. `QuietActionForm` is the form whose action answers by what it does (removes, moves, switches, connects, then refreshes or goes elsewhere): the same busy state; a refusal (`FormState`, `{ ok: false, problems }`) under its
fields or after its buttons; what was typed kept on a refusal and cleared on a success, as React clears a form's own action; a plain post without JavaScript, the server action being the form's `action`; `onSubmit` first. 77 forms in 49 files moved onto it, the store's (tags, field
groups, broadcasts, campaigns, segments, apps, hosts, carriers, calendars, bookings), the platform's (OAuth clients, referrals, plan reminders, Slack, theme variations) and the owner's level (account, connected apps, WordPress, developer), with sign-in, consent and try. The ratchet
counts per file the forms still bound to an action by hand (`adminFiles.rawActionForms`, 107 in 71 files to 29 in 21): a `<form>` whose `action` is an expression and which says no `method` (a search, a filter or a download says its method or has a string action; the media search now
says `get`). The 21 left are named with why in `ratchet.scan.test.ts`: 20 hold the answer in their own `useActionState` or transition, and Sign out, in every admin page's frame, would cost each admin route 4 KB. Against the commit before: the storefront is unchanged; Kaizena Flow's
form pages (sign-up, status, report, get-domain) and the admin's pages with an `ActionForm` carry 3 KB more, pages with a `QuietActionForm` 4 KB. Tests: `action-forms.test.ts`, `form-problems.test.ts`, the ratchet's counts and `e2e/signed-in/forms-answer.spec.ts`.
