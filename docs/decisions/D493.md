# D493: A bill read by the model into the form

**A bill read by the model into the form (B3 step 4, 8 November 2026, `src/lib/bill-reading.ts`, `src/server/bill-reading.ts`, `BillFields`, `readBillAction()`).** When the owner chooses a bill's file in *Add a bill* (D492), the store's model reads it and the form fills: who it is from, its organisation number, the invoice's number, its date and due date, the amount to pay, what it was and its VAT rate; the form says what it read (*Read from the bill: Telenor Norge AS, 88123, NOK 1,250.00.
Check it, then add it.*), and the owner checks it and presses *Add the bill* as before.
A PDF's text (its first three pages, read by `unpdf`, at most 12,000 characters) goes to the store's text model, cleaned of text addressed to an AI line by line (`withoutInstructions()` takes the rest of a value in a tool's JSON; in a document's text it would take the rest of the file, the amount to pay with it) and framed between markers it cannot close; a photo goes to the model that sees pictures (D491's way).
The answer is read by its shape into the fields only: a name holding text addressed to an AI dropped, a number that is no number, a date that is no date, a due date before the invoice's, a kind or rate outside the choices left out; a bill in another currency than the books' fills no amount and says so. Nothing is kept or posted by the reading; what is posted is the form the owner sends. A scanned PDF (no text) or a HEIC photo is filled by hand, as without AI.
Metered with the receipts (`receipt_reading`, *Reading receipts and bills*). Not an agent (one call that fills a form, as D491), so no recorded eval run.
