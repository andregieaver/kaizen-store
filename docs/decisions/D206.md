# D206: Hover takes time; buttons have hover effects; logos can take one colour

**Hover takes time; buttons have hover effects; logos can take one colour.** Hover changes were instant (or Tailwind's fixed 150 ms).
Their time (0–2 s) and easing (a closed list: Ease, Ease out, Ease in and out, Linear, Smooth, Snappy, Springy) are now two custom properties, set for the whole site in the builder's Theme tab and by any row, column or component for itself and what it holds in its Motion tab, and read by every hover on the site (links, buttons, fields, product cards, logos, the motion catalogue's hover effects); visitors who prefer less motion get every change at once.
Buttons choose how they answer the pointer from thirteen effects (Fade, as before, Darken, Lighten, Lift, Grow, Shadow, Fill sweep, Invert, Underline, Arrow, Shine, Glow, None), per button or dual button, with the theme's default for every button of the site.
Brand logos can be drawn in the store's brand colour (the theme's accent), white, black or a chosen colour (each logo's own shape filled, so best with see-through backgrounds), or inverted for dark backgrounds, with their own colours on hover if wanted.
