# D157: Tax profile and VAT engine

**Tax profile and VAT engine (wave 1, unit 1a).** VAT categories are data (`commerce.vat_categories`: standard, exempt, accommodation, food, books, periodicals, medicines, culture_events, children_goods; products reference them by foreign key).
Per-country rates have validity dates, a source, a checked-on date and a verified flag (`commerce.vat_rates`), are written only by `commerce.set_vat_rate()` (a period ends, a new one begins, audit-logged, never edited in place) and read only through `commerce.vat_rate(country, category, at)`; a missing row means the standard rate, and shipping takes the standard rate unless a person has verified a per-country rule.
A store's tax profile (`commerce.store_tax_profile`: VAT, OSS, IOSS registration) is edited by owners at `/admin/{store}/settings/tax`; `duplicate_store()` copies the choices and blanks the numbers. `vatTreatment()` (pure) decides standard, reverse_charge or ioss with a reason code, through one loader (`tax-treatment.ts`) shared by `cartSummary()` and `placeOrder()`.
VIES is reached only through an injectable fetch with a fixed host, is never called while an order is placed, and its failure never blocks and never exempts. A reverse-charge order stores the VAT not charged inside `discount_minor` (`vat_relief_minor`), as D130 does for credits, so the total checks hold. IOSS marks private buyers' shipped goods of at most 150 EUR from outside the EU.
Rows: the EU VAT row stays partial (report 1c, invoice 1b); the b2b row, `checkout.oss-and-ioss-support` and `international.ioss` become partial. Accepted limits: seeded rates are unverified and cover only NO, SE, FI and DE reduced rows; every consumer text is hand-written and needs legal review.
Known limits: VIES says a number is registered, not whose it is, so a private buyer who types a company and someone else's valid number is not charged VAT (staff see a flag on the order); the VIES budget per store and hour can be used up by many carts.
