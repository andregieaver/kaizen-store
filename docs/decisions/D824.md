# D824: Reading a page loads the page reader alone

**Reading a page loads the page reader alone (bloat review step 2.21; 19 November 2026).** The reads of pages moved from
`src/server/pages.ts` to `src/server/page-reads.ts` (published pages by id, the index, names, cards, articles, a store's
products page and role pages), with the card helpers of step 2.17 and the names query of step 2.16; `pages.ts` re-exports them,
so no caller had to change. Files that only read pages import the reader, and files that only need a tag import the tag leaf
(`src/lib/cache-tags.ts`, D816). The content types' reads moved apart from their writer the same way
(`src/server/content-types-read.ts`). The graph test forbids the page renderer (`page-article.tsx`) to reach `pages.ts`,
`design-presets.ts`, `page-ai.ts` or `src/lib/signature/`. Measured locally: the reader reaches 72 files (`pages.ts` 183),
the article 467 to 431. A store's home still reaches `pages.ts` through `seo.ts` and `platform-roles.ts`, outside this
step; its chunk did not fall (+1.9 KB in a window where main moved and unrelated routes rose as much). Commits 732da16a8,
91064b27f, fdc7d193a.

On production on 19 November the demo store's front page, blog, About us and a product, and Kaizena Flow's front page and blog,
answered 200 with their titles and their header menus, as before (no article is published yet, so the blogs list none). The step
moved the admin's client chunk split, though its changes are server imports only: from CI run 1732, 459 of 482 admin routes grew
by a median of 12.6 KB (Lucide's icon base and the link wrappers moved into their first chunks; ci-red issue 26, two routes'
budgets raised to CI's figures to go green). The follow-up landed on 20 November (980e481ef): the root admin layout used to reach,
through its server imports, server components drawing `AdminLink` and Lucide icons, so Turbopack put them in the chunk every admin
route loads; this step cut that path. `src/components/admin/shared-client-parts.ts`, imported by the root admin layout, names them on
purpose (`shared-client-parts.scan.test.ts`). Locally 481 of 482 admin routes fell by a median of 10.5 KB; the two raised budgets
were lowered again (marketing 656,843 to 649,378, support settings 668,413 to 660,791) and CI run 1757 passed on them.
