# D69: A rental's variants are each rented by whole days, half days or hours, and a resource is full only when its bookings at one moment reach its capacity

**A rental's variants are each rented by whole days, half days or hours, and a resource is full only when its bookings at one moment reach its capacity.** `product_variants.rental_period` (`day`, `half_day`, `hour`) is set per variant in the product editor, so one product offers several ways to rent at their own prices. Whole days work as in D67. A half day is the morning (pick-up to the middle of the day, on a quarter hour) or the afternoon (from there to return), quantity 1.
Hours start on the hour from pick-up and end by return time; the quantity is the hours. The product page asks for a day, then a half or a start time and how many hours (`rentalTimes()`), and a date shows as free when any of its halves or hours is. Capacity is counted at the busiest moment of the span (`peakBusy()` in code, `commerce.resource_peak()` under the lock), so three bikes rented for different hours leave room for one across all of them.

Date: 2026-09-27

Asked for the demo e-bike rental, which now offers a day (450 kr), a half day (300 kr) and an hour (120 kr). Shortest and longest stay rules apply to whole days only. Order lines and Stripe show option values in the shopper's language ("Halv dag", "Hvit"), checkout and subscription changes alike.
