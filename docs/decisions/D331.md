# D331: Reacting to events

**Reacting to events (`docs/ai-autopilot.md` unit G, section 15).** With *React to what happens* on (`autopilot_settings.planning.reacts`, off by default; `waitMinutes` 5 to 240, default 15), `runEvents()` in `/api/cron/autopilot` finds three kinds of event per store and answers each once (`commerce.autopilot_events`, unique per store, kind and reference, written only by `src/server/autopilot-events.ts`): a customer waiting for a person longer than the minutes (a headless turn that reads the
conversation and leaves one staff note with a suggested reply: never a word to the customer, `reply_to_support` not offered); an A/B test stopped by its guardrail or planned end in the last 7 days (`explain_results` and `apply_winner`, gated as ever through `autopilotDecision()`); an urgent or warning alert of `alertsFor()`, read once a day per store (`autopilot_event_checks`) and once per alert and ISO week (a goal review's read tools and at most one plan, started by `startDecision()` within
the plans a week shared with reviews). Turns run in the name of whoever last saved the settings, read again and still an owner, at most 2 a run and one per store, 12 a store a day, a pending event stale after 24 hours, a waiting conversation answered or a test decided since skipped; events kept 90 days (`pruneAutopilotEvents()`). Stores only; the platform has none.
