# D237: Shipping methods, zones and profiles, local pickup and local delivery

**Shipping methods, zones and profiles, local pickup and local delivery (wave 3).** `docs/wave-3-shipping-methods.md` is the contract. **Methods, zones and profiles.** Per market, named methods (`shipping_methods`) carry rates per zone and profile (`shipping_method_rates`). A rate is flat, by basket weight or by basket value. Tiers run from an inclusive minimum to an exclusive maximum with no gap, held by the deferred trigger `shipping_rates_tiers`.
Zones are postal codes and prefixes inside a country (`shipping_zones`); the most specific pattern wins and Rest of country takes the rest. Profiles group products (`shipping_profiles`), with General for the rest. A mixed basket is offered a method only when every profile has a matching tier, and pays their sum, free above the method's value.
Goods without a weight weigh the store's default per unit (`shipping_settings`, 500 g). **Flat rate.** The market's flat rate stays as the fallback when no method prices a basket. It is the only shipping of subscriptions, weekly boxes (`placeOrder({ flatRateOnly })`) and drafts. **Pickup and local delivery.** Pickup is set per place (`pickup_options`: price, ready time, instructions).
Local delivery is set per area (`local_delivery_areas`), by postal codes or by a radius from a place measured between GeoNames postal-code centres (`postal_points`, CC BY 4.0, NO/SE/DK/FI). An area has a price, a minimum and a free-above value, and the cheapest matching area is offered. **Cart and order.** The cart holds one choice and the postal code. `chosenDelivery()` stays the one reader for `cartSummary()` and `placeOrder()` and re-prices the store's own methods on every read.
Orders keep `delivery_method`, a snapshot in `delivery`, the place and the driver's note. **Handovers.** *Ready for pickup* emails once (`order-ready:{id}`). *Collected* records a handover (`shipments.method = 'pickup'`) and `delivered_at` together; *Out for delivery* records a `local` handover.
Neither kind of order is in *To send*. **Refunds.** The standard delivery kept for refunds is the cheapest option actually offered (`standardShippingMinor(offer)`). **Postal-code mismatch.** A priced postal code that differs from Stripe's address, or an order priced without one whose address is in a dearer zone, sets `orders.delivery_mismatch_at` and never changes the charge.
`anonymise_order()` removes the delivery note and the mismatch event's codes; `forbid_change()` is patched to allow that removal.
