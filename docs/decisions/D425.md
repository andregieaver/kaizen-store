# D425: A dual heading

**A dual heading: a heading's second part, with its own look, side by side or one under the other.** A heading has an optional `second` (`isDualHeading()`), written after the first in its settings. It is one heading (`<hN>`, one name to a screen reader) of two marked parts, `data-kz-text=first` and `second`, each with its own Typography group (*First part*, *Second part*) over the heading's; *One under the other* (`stack`, per size in `at`) puts each part on its own line.
Both parts are its words (search, excerpt, the page check's outline and a contrast check per part) and the second is translated on its own.

Date: 2026-11-02

Asked for by the owner: a heading of two parts, each with its own style, inline or stacked. *Why one heading and not two:* the two parts are one title ("We are" and "Kaizena Flow"); two headings would put a second entry in the page's outline and break its levels. *Why not edited in place:* the canvas's in-place editor writes one text; two fields in the settings say which part is which. *Why the parts' roles only while there is a second part:* a plain heading's Typography panel stays as it was.
