# D591: The admin's links are fetched on intent

**The admin's links are fetched on intent (bloat review step 0.13; 14 November 2026).** An admin screen made 23 to 46 router prefetch requests at rest (28 on a store's home, 8 for product editors on a four-row product list) and about 25 again after every save, fetching nearly the same loading skeleton many times.
`PolicyLink` (`src/components/policy-link.tsx`) gives a link a written policy: `view` (Next's default, when it scrolls into view), `intent` (when the pointer, a touch or the focus reaches it, Next's hover pattern) or `none` (never: a link that leaves its root layout or opens a file). `AdminLink` (`src/components/admin/ui/link.tsx`) is the admin's link: `intent` unless a policy is given, and a caller's `prefetch={false}` becomes `none`.
It draws the rail, the store menu, the store switcher and account menu, the frame's links, `ListRow`, `RowLead`, the card's link, the page header's and focus frame's back links, `ButtonLink`, the product list's rows, and, added in review when the home stayed at 18 and the counts followed the data, the store home's own links (`[store]/page.tsx`, `Stat` and `Attention` in `overview-parts.tsx`, the analytics alerts card), the order table's order and customer links, and the platform home's two
section links. Never fetched: View store, the capacity CSV and the focus frame's logo to the site (D590). The trade: a touch or keyboard press now waits one round trip for the skeleton, and the pending bar (`LinkPending`, D540, code review FS1) shows the press worked meanwhile. Not taken: `export const prefetch = 'force-disabled'` on the gated layout, which would also stop the fetch on hover.
`e2e/signed-in/prefetch.spec.ts` holds eight screens at the counts of 14 November, which may only fall: home 0 (28 before), orders 8, products 8 (none a product's editor; 8 before), pages 6, settings 16, a product's editor 2, the builder 0, the platform home 0; every other fetch after load named (the notices feed); hovering the rail's Products link makes 1 or 2 and pressing opens it. The rest of the admin's roughly 950 raw links keep today's behaviour. No migration.
