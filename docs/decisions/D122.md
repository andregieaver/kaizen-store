# D122: Work

**Work: clients, assignments, time and invoices in the store admin, ported from Kaizen Life's Work area (`docs/work.md`), so consultants sell and manage hours in one system.** A module switched on under Features (`stores.modules` `work`), at `/admin/{store}/work`: clients (archived, never deleted with history), assignments with tasks and estimates, time entries and one running timer per person (the SQL functions start, stop and log, minutes rounded up and capped at a day, dated in the store's
time zone), estimate alerts, invoices, and an overview.
Money is integer minor units and hours integer minutes (quantities in hundredths of an hour), never floats. **Invoices are legal documents**: a draft becomes an invoice only through `commerce.issue_work_invoice()`, which numbers it gap-free from its own series (`W-`, credits `WCN-`) at issue, checks readiness (seller and buyer details, bank account, VAT treatment), freezes seller, buyer, lines, VAT and totals as a snapshot, and from then on nothing about it can change; mistakes are corrected by
credit notes (`credit_work_invoice()`, whole or by line, releasing the time), payments are append-only and the status follows them by trigger. One draft at a time per assignment. VAT per line from a treatment the owner chooses (domestic, not registered, reverse charge, outside scope, exempt) with hand-written notes in nb, sv, da and en (`src/lib/work-invoice-text.ts`, kept out of the AI catalogue, **needs legal and accountant review before real use**).
The printable invoice and credit note come from the snapshot at `/admin/{store}/work/invoices/{id}/print`. Left out of Life on purpose: Finances links, organizations, agent personas. Not yet: emailing invoices and a hosted page for clients, recurring invoices and their cron, reports and CSV, linking clients to store customers, selling hours as products with a prepaid ledger, online payment, owner tools and events.

Date: 2026-09-30

Asked for by the owner: "clone all of its functionality" from Life. Life's own send only marked an invoice sent, numbers were typed by hand and the print was the browser's; Kaizen's rules (legal numbering, immutability, VAT, store isolation) replace those, so this is a port of the behaviour, not of the code.
