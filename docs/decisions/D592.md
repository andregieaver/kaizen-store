# D592: The router's two settings are written down

**The router's two settings are written down: route guessing off, partial prefetching a recorded false (bloat review step 0.14; 14 November 2026).** Next guesses a link's route tree from a route of the same shape, and the guess never fills the `rest` param of the `@drawer/[...rest]` slot (the TODO "This should never happen.
Bail out with null" in `next/dist/client/components/segment-cache/optimistic-routes.js`), so at /s/{store} addresses the slot's segment was asked for without end: on a local build the search page made 687 router prefetches in its first 3 s, a category 866 and the cart 873, and never stopped. `experimental.optimisticRouting: false` ends it: 25, 26 and 26, then none (the theme store's list, /themes, 10).
Production could not loop while stores are on their own hosts (behind a rewrite no shape is learned), but /s/ addresses are served too. Remove the line when the TODO is fixed upstream and `e2e/prefetch.spec.ts` 'a store page goes quiet' passes without it; `src/lib/next-config-prefetch.test.ts` holds both lines and the comment. `partialPrefetching: false` is a recorded choice, not an oversight: unset, Next treats it as false and warned on every build and start; step 2.8 decides adoption by trial.
With guessing off, a link to the same page with another query is prefetched on sight, so the admin's list tabs, filter and page links became `AdminLink`s (d5af8b4, D591's ceilings kept). Rejected: a second hunk in `patches/next.patch` with a two-line bail-out (47 requests against 55 in the finding's measurement, not worth carrying through upgrades) and removing `@drawer/[...rest]` (D64 needs it). `CLAUDE.md` (Stack) and `docs/performance.md` (The router's two settings) say it.
The platform: the theme store's list is in the same spec. No migration.
