# D437: The AI manager's quotes, price on request and price display

**The AI manager's quotes, price on request and price display.** The AI manager reads a store's requests for a quote among its drafts (`list_draft_orders` marks them, counts those waiting and takes `quotes`), types the price of an open draft's lines by SKU (`set_draft_prices`, through the draft editor's own state and `saveDraft()`, so a line it does not name keeps its price and a line priced on request keeps waiting; nothing is sent, and `send_draft_order` sends a request as a quote with the
owner's yes), declines a request with a short reply the customer is emailed (`decline_quote`, gate `send`, autonomy area messages as `decline_return`, the reply checked by `checkQuoteReply()` and `findClaims()` before the owner is asked), reads how prices show and who may request a quote (`price_display`), changes them through the pages' own writers (`set_price_display`, `set_quote_requests`, gate `public`, area setup, only what is given), and prices a product on request with its own words
around the price (`update_product`'s `price_on_request` and `price_words`, through `saveProduct()`, so D433's bundle rule holds; `get_product` says both). The shopper's note and the company they typed are never given to the model: they are read on the draft's page. `src/lib/quote-tools.ts`, `src/server/quote-tools.ts`, `quote-tools.int.test.ts`.

Date: 2026-11-03

Asked for by the owner (the items D431 left undone). *Why no shopper text:* the drafts already gave the customer's name and email; a note or a typed company is the shopper's own words, a new way outside text would reach a prompt (and an eval case with it), and the owner reads it on the page anyway. *Why `set_draft_prices` is not gated:* it changes an unsent draft only, as `create_draft_order` makes one; what reaches the customer is `send_draft_order`'s, which the owner approves with the draft's
lines and total. *Why one tool per page:* each change goes through the writer and checks of the page the owner would use. The tools changed `toolsVersion()`, so the agents' eval was recorded again on production after the deploy and its recording committed (D391).
