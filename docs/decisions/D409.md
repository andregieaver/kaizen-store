# D409: The Dream Factory

**The Dream Factory: one description to a store ready to open (`docs/dream-factory.md`).** A run (`commerce.dream_runs`, one in progress a store, written only by `src/server/dream-factory.ts`) follows thirteen steps in lanes (`DREAM_STEPS` in `src/lib/dream-factory.ts`): the brief (one answer of the model under `RULES_STAND`; the business's details kept only where the owner's words hold them, `ownBusiness()`, else asked), the owner's yes, then research, setup and brand side by side, products,
website and legal pages, the owner's payments and plan, a rehearsal, the opening and the first goals. The AI's steps are turns of the AI manager in the owner's name, limited to the step's tools (`STEP_TOOLS`) at *Just do it* for catalogue, website, design and setup, money and messages still asking and `NEVER_AUTOMATIC` never run; code's are D351's draft and publish, D311's legal writer and the store's checkup.
Every step ends on a check of the store's rows; three failed tries become a question with *Try again* (a test run fails instead). `/api/cron/dream-factory` (every minute) moves the runs on, one AI turn a run a minute; products are made one a turn, each with a picture from the store's picture model.
Owners start it with *Build my store with AI* at self-serve sign-up (`access_requests.dream`, the run started when the store is made) or on `/admin/{store}/dream-factory`, where an owner of a store not yet open lands from Home while a run goes. A run whose owner is at the drills' test address is a test: its yes is pressed by the drill and counted, it pays in test mode, stays private and ends ready to open; the live drill `dream_factory` signs up through the real path with `DRILL_PROMPT`.
Its result stays `waiting` until the run ends, then `settleDreamDrills()` (the dispatcher, with the drills) writes passed at ready or open and failed when the run failed or was stopped (`dreamDrillOutcome()`, PSI AGT-06). Platform → AI scores every run: minutes to ready without the owner's waiting, the owner's presses, steps right first time (`scoreOf()`). Self-serve sign-up switched on in production on the owner's word (30 October 2026, audited).
