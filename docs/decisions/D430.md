# D430: Quantity with − and +

**Quantity with − and +.** `QuantityStepper` is a number field between − and + buttons (44 px targets named in the shopper's language, switched off at the limits) that follows the variant's quantity rule (D323: its minimum, the line's maximum and its step, `stepUp()`/`stepDown()`/`snapQuantity()` in `src/lib/quantity-stepper.ts`); typing and the arrow keys work, a number the rule does not allow becomes the nearest one it does when the field is left or Enter pressed, and without a script it
is an ordinary number in its form.
On a product page it sits beside Add to cart (and in the phone's bar, sharing its number through `QuantityChoice`), for goods and bundles whose rule leaves a choice (never an appointment, a stay, a rental, a gift card or a variant fixed at one); the store chooses how product pages offer it (`stores.quantity_picker`: `buttons`, the default, `box` for the number alone, or `hidden`, which adds the rule's minimum as before) on *Prices and quantity*, and a product layout's Buy component may choose
otherwise (`ProductBlock.quantity`). In the cart each line has it (`CartQuantity`), saved by itself a moment after the last press (one save for several), the totals following; its Update button is drawn only without a script (kept for a stepper in plain HTML: the cart itself streams in and needs the script, as add-to-cart does, so it is not tested without one). The server holds every quantity to the rule as before (`nextQuantity()`, `quantityProblem()`).
Migration: `20261103005650_price_display` (with D429).

Date: 2026-11-03

Asked for by the owner. *Why the number stays typeable:* a buyer of 48 should not press + 47 times, and a screen reader user reads and sets a number field directly. *Why debounced in the cart:* each press saving at once would send a request per press and redraw the totals between them. *Why the store's choice and the layout's:* a shop selling single pieces wants no chooser, a wholesaler a box. *Why no chooser for bookings and gift cards:* a time, a stay or a card's amount is chosen its own way.
