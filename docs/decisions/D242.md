# D242: Seamless switching, step 1

**Seamless switching, step 1: section styles (phase 2 of `docs/theme-store.md`, `docs/theme-phase-2.md`).** A section style is a named set of theme elements (D182) in `ThemeSettings.sectionStyles`; a row names one (`PageRow.sectionStyle`, a name only, never CSS) and is drawn with the style's elements laid over the site's (`layerElement()`), its own settings winning over both.
The core names are `CORE_SECTION_STYLES` (plain, hero, feature, muted, contrast, quote); a theme may add its own (at most 12 styles); a name the live theme lacks draws as its Plain, then as the site's elements. `themedRows(rows, elements, sections)` and `themeLayersFor(owner)` read both, on the site and on the builder's canvas, so a theme's styles travel with its snapshot, versions, installs and saved themes without anything new.
The Theme tab edits the whole site's elements or one style's (*Elements for*), and a row's General tab chooses its style. The page migration (D241) compares against the section style too.
