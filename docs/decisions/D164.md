# D164: Rows of a copied page that still match badly are kept as the original's own picture, as a trial

**Rows of a copied page that still match badly are kept as the original's own picture, as a trial.** After the page is corrected by measuring (two rounds), a row under the threshold is rebuilt as the original's strip photographed twice (also with its glyphs not painted) with its words placed over it; when words over a picture would not fit the page's 50 KB of CSS, the row is picture-only with its words hidden as text.
It is a trial: the rows stay only if the page, measured again, matches no worse (`BACKDROP_WORSE`), and they go back if the look at the rebuilt page fails; a failed look is tried again once. A group of more than six cards is never tried as columns, and a row's strips are kept together (two library files a row). Phone elements are found by tag and classes when the phone page's paths are shifted (`alignTrees()`).

Date: 2026-10-05

Asked for by the owner after detnorskekaffehus.net scored 30 % and runandrelax.com 55 %; the probe (no AI) reaches 90 % or more on both and on lampan.no, and a production run of runandrelax.com failed with 'the browser stopped', which the trial and the retry guard against. The words stay text in both forms. Rows as pictures cost library files and are not editable; the report says so. Not verified in production: the AI credits were out when this shipped.
