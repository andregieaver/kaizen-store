# D298: Email automations

**Email automations (wave 5 unit D, `docs/wave-5-marketing.md` 6).** Four emails a store switches on once (`commerce.automations`, one row per store and kind, `enabled_at` set at each switch-on, the days within each kind's range and the words per language in a broadcast's shape, checked by the database).
Welcome (days after subscribing), after a purchase (days after a paid order's delivery) and win-back (days without a paid order, by lower-case address) go only to addresses on the list that are `subscribed`, once per person and trigger: a row of `automation_sends` keyed `welcome:{subscriber}`, `after_purchase:{order}` or `win_back:{subscriber}:{order}` is written before the email (`sendEmail()` under `automation:{store}:{key}`), so two runs never send twice; only a moment after `enabled_at` and
within the last seven days starts one, so switching on never writes about the past. Paid is D152's `PAID` with the order not cancelled, restricted or anonymised (copied and host orders never count). Words start from hand-written nb, sv, da and en starters (`AUTOMATION_STARTERS`), edited in the broadcast editor's fields (`EmailContentFields`), with the subscriber's unsubscribe link and `List-Unsubscribe` (`marketingEmail()`, `src/server/marketing-email.ts`, now shared with broadcasts).
Back in stock: a sold-out physical variant that does not keep selling offers *Email me when it is back* while the store has it on (`/api/stock-requests`, same site, a selling shop); a typed address confirms from its link and a page's button (`/account/stock/{token}`), a verified signed-in customer's own address waits at once; one open request per store, variant and address (`stock_requests`, status rules in the database), at most 10 a day per visitor key (daily-changing hash, never an IP) and 5
open confirmations per address; the answer never says whether the address was known. Each run, the waiting requests whose variant is in stock again by any path (`variant_availability`) and still offered are claimed and marked sent, then emailed once each (`stock.back_in_stock`); a request subscribes no one. The runner is `runAutomations()` in the every-minute marketing job beside broadcasts; only open, real stores.
Email automations under Marketing (`marketing:read`, changes `marketing:write`, audited `marketing_automation.*`, as `automation.` is wave 3's order rules): four cards with what each does, on or off, how many it sent in 30 days and the waiting requests. Sends and requests are personal data (export `marketing`, erasure, pruned at 90 days and 7/180/30 days), `automations` `NOT_PERSONAL`, none copied.
Not built: click counts for automation emails, drip sequences, browse abandonment, the live price and a cancel link in the back-in-stock email, and AI manager tools for them.
