# D501: Invoices brought in from elsewhere

**Invoices brought in from elsewhere: deleted, or merged into the one they are the same as (8 November 2026, `src/server/work-imported-invoices.ts`, `InvoiceTidy` on an imported invoice).** The owner: *we also need a way to merge invoices and be able to delete them*. Human Web's imports from Life and Tripletex can be wrong or here twice (D495 merges a pair on *Bring in*, by its rules only).
On an imported invoice (from Tripletex, Life, or recorded as issued elsewhere, D499), owners only, folded away: *Merge into it* (the invoices to this client in the same currency with the same total) or *Delete this invoice*. Only while nothing hangs on it: no payment, credit note, write-off or time logged on its lines (the database's guard holds the same, D495, now also no loss, D500; and only inside a transaction that says it is the import's work).
One in the books has its entry reversed first (`reverseLedgerEntry()`, today), so the books never hold an invoice that is gone and the reversal shows what happened; then it is deleted, its lines with it. A merge also gives the invoice kept the copy's number when that one is imported and came without one (Life's; the guard's one-time numbering, D494), with the event `invoice.merged` on it.
Audited `work.invoice.imported_deleted`, `work.invoice.imported_merged`. **An invoice issued from Kaizena Flow is never deleted or merged**: the Bookkeeping Act has a mistake corrected by a credit note, which Work already makes. Merging a copy that has a payment is refused: merge the copy without one into the one with it. No migration.
