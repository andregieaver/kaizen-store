# D308: The AI manager makes and changes products

**The AI manager makes and changes products (`docs/ai-autopilot.md` unit A1).** Five owner tools: `create_product` (a draft of physical goods with its words in every language, prices per country, options and variants, stock, weight, categories and tags by name, pictures from the media library or made by the picture model), `update_product` (only the fields given, laid over the product as the editor reads it), `set_prices`, `set_product_status` (publish or take off) and `write_product_texts`
(D76's writer from the product's own facts). Every product is saved by the editor's own `saveProduct()` with the person as actor, so its checks, price history and stock history are the editor's; an existing variant's stock is never written from here (`set_stock`). A draft is changed at once, as the owner would in the editor; a change to a live product and every publish wait for the owner's yes, checked first and described from the product's own title.
The A2 to A4 tools (website, setup, design) follow the same rules. **A2 (website):** `get_page`, `build_page`, `list_page_builds`, `edit_page`, `publish_page`, `set_page_place`, `get_menus`, `save_menu` and `set_header_footer`, for a store and for Kaizena Flow's own site, through the builder's, the Pages list's, the menu editor's and Header and footer's own savers; a page is built by the page studio after the answer, its pictures one by one, as jobs in `commerce.page_ai_jobs`; drafts and new
menus are changed at once, publishing, places, the header and a menu that is shown wait for a yes (`docs/ai-autopilot.md` 4.1). **A3 (setup):** `set_store_details`, `set_countries`, `set_languages`, `set_currencies`, `set_flat_shipping`, `save_shipping_method` and `open_store` through the wizard's and the Settings pages' own savers, at once while the store is being set up (its storefront is a preview) and waiting for a yes once it is open; opening always waits; `setup_progress` became the status
the spec calls `setup_status` (each step's tool, or the one thing only the owner can do) (`docs/ai-autopilot.md` 5.1). **A4 (design, 2026-10-25):** `list_themes` (the themes and presets on offer with what making each live costs the store, and its theme library), `add_theme` (`installDesignPreset()`, at once: visitors see nothing), `try_theme` (the install's preview address) and `make_theme_live` (`switchToInstall()` or `applyDesignPreset()`, always on the owner's yes; a `spend` when the theme
costs money, with the charge the owner was shown kept as `approved_charge` and compared when the yes runs). The autopilot may make a free theme live at the owner's Design level and never one that costs money (`costsMoney`) (`docs/ai-autopilot.md` 6.1).
