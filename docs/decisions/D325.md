# D325: Videos and 3D in the product gallery

**Videos and 3D in the product gallery (wave 6 unit G, `docs/wave-6-catalogue.md` Unit G); built.** `product_media` gains `kind` (`image`, `video`, `model`), `source` (a video's `upload`, `youtube` or `vimeo`) and `media_url` (the file or address), while `url` and `thumbnail_url` stay every item's picture (a video's or model's poster), so cards, the cart, feeds and every other reader of a product's picture are right unchanged.
At most 20 items, 5 videos and 3 models; videos as D86's (50 MB, MP4 or WebM, or YouTube and Vimeo played only on a press), models as GLB up to 20 MB in a new `product-models` bucket, each upload checked to be in the store's own folder; the product page plays videos without autoplay and loads `<model-viewer>` only when *View in 3D* is pressed. Alt text per language for every item, the other languages in the translation worklist.

Date: 2026-10-26

*Why:* `catalogue.product-media-video-and-3d`: Shopify's product media takes videos and 3D models; Kaizena Flow's gallery took pictures only. Built in three steps, all on 2026-10-26.
G1: the columns and their check (`product_media_kind`), `mediaProblems()` and `ownMediaFile()` (`src/lib/product-media.ts`) in `productProblems()` and `saveProduct()` (a file the product already had is kept), the `product-models` bucket (`startModelUpload()`, `registerModel()`), `mediaUses()` and `rewriteAddresses()` reading `media_url`, store copies carrying the files, `pictureItem()` for every writer of a plain picture.
G2: the editor's *Add video* and, one step away, *Add 3D model* (`product-media-tools.tsx`, words in other languages under *Other languages*), the product page's `GalleryVideo` and `GalleryModel` (`@google/model-viewer` loaded by `import()` on the press), `e2e/product-media.spec.ts`.
G3: items' words in the translation worklist (`media.{n}`, `MEDIA_LABELS`; a language filled from the title is offered again), `get_product` naming each item's kind, the product tools keeping videos and models when they replace pictures; the row re-rated Full. Migration versions: `20261026213800` (product_media_kinds), `20261026213829` (product_media_kinds_sql), applied by CI on 2026-10-26 22:24 UTC. Advisors checked after: INFO only (RLS on with no policy, as every private table).
