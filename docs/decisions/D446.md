# D446: Moving from WooCommerce

**Moving from WooCommerce: one button, and the WordPress site may stay (proposed 3 November 2026, `docs/moving-from-woocommerce.md`; steps 1 to 5 built 4 November 2026).** D445's move engine with WooCommerce's own Connect: a small read-only plugin of ours installed with one press (or WooCommerce's `wc-auth` read key and an application password without it, files last), reading what the REST API can't (gift card plugins, MailPoet's consent, the Redirection, Yoast and Rank Math redirects, SEO
texts, menus) and the real address of every product; one question after Copy, *Keep your WordPress site?*, where keeping it sets up the WordPress plugin (D169, D170) for the products and moves the shop to `shop.{domain}`; on move day an optional pause on selling and, for a whole move, the old site redirecting every address for 30 days.
Open for the owner: one plugin or two, an email list from Mailchimp or Klaviyo, guest buyers as customers, the pause on selling, the WordPress.org listing. *4 November 2026, step 1 built* (`docs/moving-from-woocommerce.md` 17): the owner's answers to Decisions 1, 3, 4 and 5 are in section 13 (one plugin, guests in the order history only, catalogue mode for the switch, a zip from us). *Connect WooCommerce* through WooCommerce's own read-only `wc-auth` key (`src/server/woocommerce-connect.ts`),
and D445's copy engine reading WooCommerce a page at a time into the same files and imports (`readWoo()`, `src/lib/woocommerce-copy.ts`): products with variations and categories, registered customers, past orders and coupons. A store moves from one shop at a time. `safeFetch()` sends a header of its own only to the first origin.
Migration version: `20261104161134_woocommerce_moves`. *Step 1b, reviews:* read from `wc/v3/products/reviews` and written only through the review import (`importReviews()`) as `import` reviews from WooCommerce, the approved ones published and the held ones waiting; never a verified purchase here (WooCommerce's *verified owner* is not carried, D301), and undone exactly (`shop_move_items` kind `review`).
Migration version: `20261104170733_woocommerce_reviews`. *Step 2a, the mover:* our WordPress plugin 1.3.0 (Decision 1) reads, after the owner presses Allow on his site, the plugins the shop runs, the redirect plugins' redirects, the SEO plugins' product titles and descriptions, and MailPoet's subscribed subscribers, through `GET` routes signed with a secret the plugin made and posted only to its own Kaizena Flow address (`/api/woocommerce/mover`, kept encrypted in `shop_moves.mover_secret`);
they come over through the redirect import, the product import's SEO columns and the email list's writer (`source` `woocommerce`, MailPoet's record as the consent's words). Decision 2 answered: a Mailchimp or Klaviyo export counts only with a subscribed status and its date (step 2b).

`seo`), a category's words through `updateTerm()`; Undo deletes the group.
Migration version: `20261104185119_woocommerce_seo_fields`. *Step 2b:* the gift card plugins' cards with money left (WooCommerce Gift Cards, PW Gift Cards, YITH) are read by the plugin (1.3.2) without their codes and made on move day with new codes emailed to their holders, as Shopify's (the owner's answer, 4 November 2026; move day for WooCommerce is step 5); a Mailchimp or Klaviyo export brings only its subscribed rows with their moment (`readListExport()`, Decision 2). *Undo this copy* now
removes WooCommerce's moved subscribers too. *Step 3:* WordPress's pages, posts and menus through the plugin (1.3.3; shortcodes taken out and named on the site, never run; WooCommerce's own pages left) into the page editor's and menu editor's writers as Shopify's are; a page's pictures now come over as image blocks in the store's library (`splitPictures()`, `pictureToLibrary()` exported from the product import), for Shopify's pages too. *The one question first (the owner's ask):* *Copy my shop*
on a WooCommerce shop asks *Everything* or *Only the shop* before it starts, neither chosen for the owner; only the shop leaves the site's pages, posts and menus unread; the answer is kept as `CopyOptions.wordpress` for step 4 and move day. *Step 4a:* keeping WordPress, the switch: the plugin (1.3.4) learns the new shop's address from Kaizena Flow's answer when reading is allowed, and its *Moving day* has two switches the owner presses there, *Pause buying* (Decision 4) and *Send shoppers to the
new shop* (permanent redirects of products, categories, the shop, cart and checkout; other pages stay); nothing on the site changes by Kaizena Flow's command. *Step 4b:* the plugin (1.3.5) makes views of the new shop's categories and all its products through its own D169 connection; with *only the shop* only `shop.{domain}` moves here (`namesToMove()`). *Step 5:* D445's move engine works from whichever shop is connected: the catch-up asks WooCommerce for what changed since the copy
(`modified_after`), old links are WordPress's own when it stays, gift cards are read again through the plugin and made with new codes, and the shop opens; the 14 days after's card stays Shopify's for now. *The 14 days after for WooCommerce:* the card says it of WooCommerce with its own closing steps by the owner's choice (deactivate WooCommerce, or keep a copy and cancel the hosting); each daily look reads every gift card's balance through the plugin (1.3.6, spent and switched-off ones too) and
lowers a moved card by what was spent there (`lowerSpentOnWoo()`, sharing Shopify's `lowerFrom()`). Every step of the plan is built.
