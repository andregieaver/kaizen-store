# D218: The admin's components

**The admin's components (`docs/admin-style.md` section 5, step 2), in `src/components/admin/ui/`.** The type scale and shapes are Tailwind names in `globals.css`'s `@theme` (`text-display` … `text-eyebrow` with their weight and tracking, `rounded-card`, `rounded-panel`, `rounded-pill`, `rounded-tile`); the admin's heading rules leave a heading in one of those sizes alone.
The components are server components drawn in the colour roles only: `Button`/`ButtonLink` (main, strong, secondary, quiet, icon, link, danger; header, card, dense), `Chip` (a signal's tint, a dot for a state), `TrendChip` (its signal is what the change means, the words say which way), `CountBadge` (read by what it counts), `Card` (flat in light, a line in dark and under forced colours, labelled by its title), `KpiTile` (a dash, never 0, for a figure not known), `ShareBar`/`ScoreBar` (read from
label and figure; the bar is hidden and never drawn past its track), `InsightPanel` (only for what the AI wrote, always with when), `List`/`ListRow` (a pointer colour only on a linked row) and `PageHeader`. `ui.test.ts` refuses a colour, an older token or a bold weight in them; `CONTRAST_RULES` adds the danger button under the pointer. Existing pages are not moved yet.
