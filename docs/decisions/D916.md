# D916: Kaizena Life's site moves into its Flow store, and the builder gets the parts it lacked

Date: 2026-11-28

**Kaizena Life's marketing site (kaizenalife.com, served today by the lifesaver-app repo) is rebuilt in the Flow store `kaizena-life`
from Flow's own blocks on a theme of its own, its copy and pictures taken from Life's source (the message files and Life's database),
never scraped; and where Life's layout needs something the page builder cannot hold, the builder gets it, for every store and Kaizena
Flow's own site.** Asked for by the owner: "we need to migrate ... their text copy, images ... recreate their design and layout", and
"you are allowed to create additional blocks to close that gap. This would only enrich the page builder anyway."

- **Languages:** en, nb and sv, the three the site publishes (lifesaver-app's `MARKETING_LOCALES`); English is the pages' own language.
- **The look:** Life's dark palette, Geist and pill buttons as the store's theme; pages follow it, with no page-wide CSS copies.
- **New in the builder so far:** sixteen icons Life uses (bot, video, calendar days, radio, checklist, dumbbell, plug, rocket, group,
  clipboard, activity, eye, footprints, bell, briefcase, quote). Their names for a table's screen reader (D201) move out of the storefront
  message files into `src/lib/icon-words.ts`, loaded by the table alone. The sandbox's editor loads self sign-up only when a sandbox is
  kept, so it no longer reaches most of the admin's words (0.83 MB instead of 1.64 MB), which leaves the page editors room for new parts.
- **The plan and the page sheet:** `docs/life-site-move.md`. Further parts (cards, badge, questions shown open, glow, a picture per
  language, boxed checks) are added there as they land.
- **The domain** moves only with the owner's go-ahead: Flow serves kaizenalife.com, the app moves to app.kaizenalife.com.

Idle cost: none. A table now loads its icons' names with import() when drawn.
