# D277: Demo stores

**Demo stores: live stores wearing a theme, and their look published by a copy (step 3 of `docs/themes-content-and-demo-stores.md`).** A store template now has a kind (`store_starters.kind`): `content` (starting content, frozen on Publish as before) or `demo`.
A platform admin makes a demo store at Stores → Themes → *New demo store* (name, address, a published theme or preset, the starting content or theme's content it is copied from; `createDemoStore()`), customises it in its own store admin, and publishes it as it stands: there is no frozen copy (the database refuses, `freeze_starter()`), so every change shows at once.
As a starter store it takes no orders, holds back shoppers' emails and is `noindex`; it is never offered as a new store's content (`starter_offered_source()` leaves it out, so a request naming one is approved from the Standard store), but `starter_source()` copies it as it stands, published or not, while it is open and not archived.
The look it wears is its live install; the theme's page in the theme store lists every published demo store wearing one of its looks under *See it live* (`liveDemosOf()`, cached a few minutes; its trade and picture are set on its page, else the look's colours), opening the store at its address.
Its page offers *Publish this look…*: **as a new theme** (`createDesign()` from the store and `giveThemeContent()` of the demo store, unpublished), **as a preset of the theme it wears** (its theme settings as a new draft preset, refused with the reason when the header, footer, product page or CSS differ from what the theme placed, `liveLookChanges()`, and for Kaizena Flow's own themes, whose presets are written in code), or **into that theme's next version** (`copyStoreLookToDraft()`).
Each is a copy made once; nothing stays linked. Until the demo domain (step 4) a demo store lives at its store-domain address like any store template, published or not, so *Unpublish* takes it off *See it live* and the address comes down only with step 4.
