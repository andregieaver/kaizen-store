# D291: Payment methods per country

**Payment methods per country (wave 4 unit D).** `docs/wave-4-payments.md` 6 is the contract.
Kaizena Flow asks Stripe for iDEAL, Bancontact, BLIK, EPS, Przelewy24 and SEPA Direct Debit on every store's account besides cards, Klarna, Link and MobilePay (`PAYMENT_METHODS`), after the account is made and one at a time when Stripe refuses them together, so a method Stripe does not offer never stops an account; each capability's state as Stripe reports it is kept (`stripe_accounts.method_states`) and shown on the Payments page with Stripe's reason.
The owner unticks a method for a country (`commerce.payment_methods`, `enabled = false`; no row is on, cards are always on), and that country's checkout sessions pass it in `excluded_payment_method_types`, never `payment_method_types`, so a checkout offers what is on in Stripe, suits the shopper and is on for the country.
A bank payment that arrives days later (SEPA Direct Debit) keeps the order `pending_payment` and waiting for the bank (`orders.awaiting_bank_since`, `commerce.await_bank_payment()`), its stock and booked times held for 21 days, until Stripe's asynchronous success pays it or its failure cancels it and lets the stock go; the shopper's page and the admin say so. Subscriptions take what Stripe allows for repeat payments; a subscription box's pay link stays card-only.

Excluding rather than listing methods keeps Stripe's own choice of what suits a shopper, and a method a store's Stripe has not made active is never offered whatever is ticked.
