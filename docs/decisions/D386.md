# D386: Creators

**Creators (wave 11 unit G, `docs/wave-11-core-and-psi.md` 2.G): built.** A store's creator programme (`commerce.creator_settings`: the owner's switch, a commission in basis points of goods without VAT, up to 50 %, a commission of its own for any of the store's product categories, the days a link is remembered) works while the shop is on and the switch is (`commerce.creator_program_on()`).
A signed-in customer applies from My account (`/account/creator`, a card on My account while it works; `ACCOUNT_ONLY_SEGMENTS`): the name they are known by, a link to their channel (`cleanChannelUrl()`: http or https, no password in it) and the consent, ticked, whose words are kept with the time (`creators.consent_text`, `consented_at`); the address is the account's own proven email (`email_verified_at`), never one typed, and one application per customer (`applyAsCreator()`).
Staff with `marketing:write` approve, decline or stop a creator at Marketing → Creators (`decideCreator()`; the database moves a creator only as `creators_rules()` allows and fixes a code once given); approving gives a code unique among the store's creators and referrers (both writers check the other's codes), which rides the referral program's link (`?ref=`), its visit count (`affiliate_count_visit()` counts an approved creator's code while the programme works) and its consented cookie
`kaizen_aff_{storeId}` (`affiliateSite().capture`, the longer of the two programmes' days), and is kept on the cart by `validAffiliateCode()`. `placeOrder()` attributes an order with the code in play through `commerce.creator_attribute_order()`, a guest's too, never a copied, host or exchange order, and rejects the creator's own (`creator_is_self()`: their account or their address).
The commission is the database's alone: when the order is paid with a captured payment (`creator_order_paid()`, from the order's status and from a payment captured later, so an order on terms or a card captured later earns then), each line of goods (physical or digital, never a gift card, a service or a booking) earns its rate of what it paid without VAT (the creator's own, else the highest category rate of the product, by hand or filled by rules through `product_matches_rules()`, else the
programme's), floored once, in the order's own currency, as one `earned` row of the append-only `commerce.creator_commissions` (`creator_ledger_guard()`: no update or delete); every refund by any path but a release or a void takes back the share refunded of what was paid (`creator_settle_refunds()`, all of it once all is refunded, the attribution `reversed`) and a cancelled paid order all of it (`creator_order_cancelled()`).
Nothing is ever converted: a creator's balances are per currency (`creator_owed()`). The owner records the payouts they make (`commerce.creator_payouts`, append-only, never more than owed in its currency, checked under the creator's lock; Kaizena Flow moves no money) and downloads them as CSV (`creators/csv`, a POST, `writeCsv()` and `amountCell()`), sets a creator's own rate, and changes the programme.
The creator's page shows their status, code and link, visits in 30 days, orders by state and money per currency with the payouts, never a buyer or an order. Personal data: `creators` in `PERSONAL_DATA` (section `referrals`, in the export with the consent's words; erasure clears the name, channel and tie to the customer and stops the creator, `forgetCreator()`, the ledger kept naming no one); a merge refuses to move a creator (`referrals`); all five tables are `never` in `COPY_RULES`.
Shopper words are `m.creator` (nb, sv, da, en by hand, `HAND_WRITTEN_ONLY`, for legal review); the admin's are the `loyalty` catalogue's `creators`. **Course changes.** (1) The application is on My account's page, not a page part a builder places: it needs the signed-in customer, whose proven address is the email the design asks for (a typed one is never proof); a page links to it like any other. (2) No feature switch of its own: the programme's switch is on its page, and the shop's feature
hides it. (3) Commission per category, not per product: a product's own rate is a category of one. (4) A refund that later fails leaves its reversal, as the referral program's does.
