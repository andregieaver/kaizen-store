# D559: Goes well with

**Goes well with: upsells and cross-sells from the cart (10 November 2026, `recommendForCart()` in `src/server/recommend.ts`, `src/components/cart-upsell.tsx`, `STORE_NOTES.cart_upsell`).** The owner asked for an upsell and cross-sell component based on the cart's content, on the cart, the slide-out cart and in the builder.
It is the recommendations engine (D139) anchored on the cart, which already left the cart's products out: `recommendForCart()` runs the same pipeline with the place `other`, no session signals and **no AI** (the plain ranking, no tokens), up to six products, upsells, cross-sells and complements by the owner's rules (*goes with*, *never with*, the upsell ceiling).
Each product shows its picture, title, the engine's reason, its price (`<Price>`) and the product page's Add to cart for one unit when nothing must be chosen first (`tileFacts()`, D557), else a link to its page. **Where:** a note (`cart_upsell`, routes cart and checkout) in the builder's *Basket notes* on a page chosen for the cart or checkout (`notesForRoles()`), drawn whether or not the store's recommendations are on, as placing it is the owner's choice; and in the standard cart page and the
slide-out cart (`CartContents`) only while the store's recommendations are on, so no store's cart changed by this alone. Read per request in `<Suspense>`; Add to cart's `refresh()` redraws it, the added product then leaving the list. An add from it is not yet counted as a recommendation's in the recommendations report (the tab's attribution is not sent from the cart). **Not for the platform:** Kaizena Flow has no cart. No migration.
