# D589: The Work invoice's words and the AI manager's tool definitions leave client scripts

**The Work invoice's words and the AI manager's tool definitions leave client scripts (bloat review step 0.11; 14 November 2026).** Two client parts pulled whole tables: the Work invoice's checklist and totals imported `work-text` (every Work catalogue in both languages, and through it a Norwegian builder sentence), and the AI manager's chat imported `manager-tools.ts` (every tool definition with its zod schema) for one table of labels, plus the admin map and its Norwegian page titles for one
suggestion. Now `ReadinessList` and `InvoiceTotalsParts` (`src/components/admin/work/invoice-totals-view.tsx`; the server `invoice-totals.tsx` reads its words and draws it) take their words as props: the draft editor and the issue dialog read the Work words with `useAdminText("work")`, and the invoice page hands the checklist's words from `workScreens()` in the request's language, with the problem templates only for a language other than English (the rule of `readinessText()`, kept).
The tool labels are `src/lib/tool-words.ts` (`manager-tools.ts` re-exports `TOOL_WORDS`), the skill limits `src/lib/skill-limits.ts` (re-exported by `saved-skills.ts`), and the page the owner is on is named by the server: `pageHereAction` (store, behind `requireOwner`) and `pagePlatformHereAction` (platform, behind `principal()`) take a path of at most 300 characters starting with `/admin`, read no store data, write nothing, and answer the page's title, Norwegian for a Norwegian account; the
chat asks only while it draws its suggestions and shows an answer only for the path it was asked for. `assistant-actions.scan.test.ts` lists both actions. On a local build the largest chunk went from 1,704,595 to 464,505 bytes, routes over 1.3 MB from 90 to 26 (the builder's own), the Work invoice route from 4,274,839 to 982,520, and the chat's scripts on first opening from 496,206 to 49,786 bytes.
`e2e/signed-in/ai-manager-panel.spec.ts` checks the first suggestion names the page and follows a move. No migration.
