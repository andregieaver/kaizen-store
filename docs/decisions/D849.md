# D849: A recommending grid brings its own words and reaches no zod or page model

**A recommending grid brings its own words and reaches no zod or page model (bloat review step 2.10; 20 November 2026).** A
recommending grid (`recommended-grid.tsx`) loads `ContentGridTiles` with the store's browser words (`browserText()`): the words its
tiles show live in the four browser word files at their existing paths in `Messages` (the AI-translated languages need nothing new),
among them the seven badge texts a product card shows (`cardText()`, the same strings as `noticeText()` in every language) and the
unit price line (D160). The tiles' helpers moved to modules without zod or the page model (`grid-view.ts`, `grid-list-style.ts`,
`typography-schema.ts`, `colour-schema.ts`). Locally, the scripts loaded after `/api/recommendations` answers fell from 649,561 to
98,341 bytes (52,913 of them the product page fetched ahead for the tiles' links), held at 140,000 by `e2e/recommendations.spec.ts`;
`storefront-bundle.scan.test.ts` walks from the tiles and finds no zod, page model or whole language (later chains 24 to 0). The
browser word files grew 32,165 to 38,512 bytes. Five route-load rows rose by the new leaf modules (beacon-speed, store-product,
store-layout, page-reads, page-article), written at today's counts. Live from 20 November; no store has Recommendations on yet, so
production shows nothing to read until one does. Idle cost: none. Commits 019d06a16, f036a59bf.
