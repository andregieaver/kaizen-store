# D221: A second colour scheme, `indigo`, and each person chooses theirs

**A second colour scheme, `indigo`, and each person chooses theirs (`docs/admin-style.md` 7.1 and 7.3, step 5).** `indigo` is D149's look (cool tint, navy ink, the indigo brand) set as roles in `src/app/admin/schemes/indigo.css`, with white words on its darker brand; it passes every pair of `CONTRAST_RULES` in light and dark (the danger tint under the pointer was lightened to `#fdd8d8` for 4.5:1).
The choice is the account's, like light and dark (D99): `accounts.admin_scheme` (default `meadow`, a check listing the registered schemes, which `admin-schemes.test.ts` holds to `ADMIN_SCHEMES`), saved by `saveAdminScheme()` from *Colour scheme* on Your account (`SchemeField`, a card per offered scheme with its swatch), kept in the browser as `kaizen_admin_scheme` (necessary, in `KNOWN_COOKIES`) and put on `<html>` by the admin's first script (`adminSchemeScript()`, only an offered id) so a page
never flashes the other colours; `AdminSchemeSync` brings another device in line. Stores keep their own colours.
