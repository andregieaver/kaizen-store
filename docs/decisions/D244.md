# D244: Seamless switching, step 3

**Seamless switching, step 3: try and preview (`docs/theme-phase-2.md`).** An installed theme that is not live has its own page under Design settings: its settings in the same theme editor, saved into the install (`saveInstallLook()`, the live save's checks through `checkStoreTheme()`), its own CSS, and *Edit* links to its header, footer and product page.
Its preview draws one of the store's published pages or articles, or a product, in the install's settings, section styles, layouts and CSS, inert and read per request.
It is served from the admin (`(print)` group, `website:read`, `noindex`, no cookie), not on the storefront with a signed `?theme=` link as the plan's 4.5 said, so no visitor or cached page can ever be served it and the storefront's proxy and caches are untouched; what that gives up is the store's own domain and following the site's links.
Rows take another theme through `GridPlace.theme`; headers, footers and product layouts take the store's they are drawn with, the same as before on the live site, and the design-profile preview now draws its rows in the profile's theme. *What changes* (`whatChanges()`) says in code, from facts, which layouts change, which section styles pages name and whether the other theme has them, how many settings stay the owner's own, how many only repeat the live theme and so keep today's look, the fonts
that come in and where CSS is involved.
