# D892: Legal reviews: drafts an outside lawyer reads and answers in the platform, with a Legal role that sees nothing else

Date: 2026-11-24

**A lawyer reads and answers drafts in the platform (24 November 2026, the owner).** Drafts for a lawyer, first `docs/outreach-rules.md`
(email plan P5.3), had no place in the platform: the lawyer would have needed the repository, or the owner would have copied views back by
hand.

- **Legal reviews** (`/admin/legal`) lists the drafts (`LEGAL_REVIEWS` in `src/lib/legal-reviews.ts`) with how many points have a view. A
  review shows the draft as written and, under it, one box per point (each country row and each question), where the lawyer writes a view.
  The latest view of each point is shown with who wrote it and when; earlier ones are kept (`platform_legal_views`), never edited.
- **A Legal role.** The team page (Platform → Team) offers `legal` beside the platform's roles, kept in `accounts.platform_role`. It is never
  a platform role: `platformRoleOf()` answers null for it, so every page and action of the platform admin stays closed, and it holds no seat
  in Kaizena Flow's own store (one it had is closed when the role is given). It opens Legal reviews only, and the admin's front page sends it
  there. It signs in with two steps, as the platform's team does.
- The platform's owners open Legal reviews too; the other platform roles do not.
- A view is saved by a form posted to a route handler (`/admin/legal/[review]/view`, same-site only), not a server action, and audited
  (`platform.legal_view_saved`, the review and the point, never the text).
- The code follows the draft as the lawyer leaves it: a view changes nothing by itself. The owner moves the draft's rows to match, and the
  rule in code (`src/lib/outreach-rules.ts`, P5.3) follows the rows.

Store-only? No: this is the platform's own (its rows have no store). Stores have no lawyer's reviews and no need named for one.

Idle cost: nothing; the pages read the draft from the deployment's files and one indexed read of the views when opened.
