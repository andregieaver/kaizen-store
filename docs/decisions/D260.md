# D260: A new store's demo content, removable in one press

**A new store's demo content, removable in one press (the theme showcase plan, phase 1 step 4b, `docs/theme-showcase-phase-1.md`).** When a store is made for an owner (an approved request, *Create a store*), everything its store template brought is marked, after its design is applied: products, pages and articles (not the All products page or a page chosen for a role) and categories and tags (`commerce.store_demo_items`, `markDemoContent()`). *Remove demo content* on the store's Home (owners
only, one press, the list one step away under *What it removes*) archives the products and deletes the pages, articles, categories and tags the owner has not changed since (their `updated_at` or a custom field after the mark), takes their links out of the menus (the links under one move up a level in its place, `withoutItems()`), and then forgets every mark.
What the owner changed stays, as does a page chosen for a role or in an A/B test and a category or tag their own products or pages still use; the answer names each. The setup wizard counts a marked, unchanged product as demo whatever its handle (`isDemoProduct()`), so a content pack's products are offered for removal there too.
