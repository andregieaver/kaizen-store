# D307: Find and replace in the page builder

**Find and replace in the page builder.** *Find and replace* beside Undo and Redo (and Ctrl/Cmd+Shift+H) finds words or phrases in every text of the page as it is being written (the texts `mapTexts()` walks, so every text a translator sees: the title and search texts, headings, rich text, buttons, captions, alt texts, items, FAQ answers and nested columns; in the language chosen, so a translation is replaced in that language only) and *Replace all* changes them at once, as one change of the
editor that Undo takes back and nothing on the site changes until the page is saved or published. Lisa's choices are one step away (*More choices*: match case, whole words only by letters and digits of any language, and a tick per text with the words around the first match to leave some as they are).
Only words are touched (`src/lib/page-find-replace.ts`, pure): inline markup's tags and rich text's marks stay as they are, so a match is looked for within one run of words (a phrase half bold is not found); the words are taken literally (`$` and all); a text the replacement would make longer than it may be is left as it was and the status line says how many; a rich-text run emptied goes.
