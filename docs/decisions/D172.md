# D172: Inventory with locations, an adjustment history, backorders and low-stock notices

**Inventory with locations, an adjustment history, backorders and low-stock notices (wave 3, first run).** `docs/wave-3-inventory.md` is the contract. Every change of `inventory_levels.on_hand` is a row of the append-only `commerce.inventory_movements`, written by a trigger so every writer is covered (reason, source, staff account, order, return; 24 months, except each level's newest movement, kept as the baseline). `inventory_ledger_check()` proves the movements add up to the level.
A variant can keep selling at zero (`stock_policy = 'continue'`, physical goods only, `backorder_days` 1 to 90 required, stated on the product page, cart, checkout, order page and confirmation, frozen on the order line). Only such a variant's level may go below zero, enforced by a trigger.
A checkout's claim on the stock it was told was there is kept whatever the order of payment (`inventory_reservations.backorder_quantity` marks the part beyond stock; `commerce.draw_order_stock()` draws it inside `complete_order_payment()`). Stock is read through one view, `commerce.variant_availability` (`in_stock` floors each location at zero).
Locations have a rank (`priority`); `placeOrder()` allocates with `allocate()` under row locks taken in (variant, location) order; refund, cancel and return restock to the original location or one staff choose (`restockPlan()`, `restockRoom()`). Staff add, rename and rank locations; owners deactivate after a report of the units that stop being for sale.
An Inventory page (on hand, committed to checkouts in progress, available, owed; adjust with a reason; history), stock files as D165 jobs (`inventory_import`, `inventory_export`, dry run, `on_hand_was` conflicts), and a per-variant low-stock level sending the owners one digest email per crossing (`stock_alerts`).
Rows: `catalogue.sell-when-out-of-stock` partial (D: the delivery-time sentences are unreviewed legal text), `catalogue.inventory-tracking-with-reservations` and `orders.multi-location-inventory-and-routing` stay partial (no signed-in admin fixture; a flaky performance guard).
