# D775: A page's title and flags are kept in columns

**A page's title and flags are kept in columns (bloat review step 2.16; 18 November 2026).** `commerce.pages` gets three stored generated columns, filled by the database from the published document on every write, so no writer can forget them and none may write them: `published_title` (`published ->> 'title'`), `published_titles` (the title by language, `commerce.page_titles(published)`, immutable) and `remembered_modal` (a modal remembered for a session or days and opened on exit intent or a
timer, as `remembersClose()` decides). Menus' titles (`namesQuery()` in `src/server/pages.ts`) and the check for remembered modals (`modalsQuery()` in `src/server/page-modals.ts`) read the columns instead of opening every published document (68,878 and 57,930 calls of the old statements in pg_stat_statements before; they should stop growing). Kaizena Flow's own pages are rows of the same table and get the same columns.
A page's other flags (`blog_id`, AI assistants, search engines) still read the document; step 2.17 moves lists and pickers. Migration versions (applied by CI, 18 November 2026): `20261118152833_page_titles_fn`, `20261118152842_lyrical_the_hood`. Advisors after: INFO only. In production: 1,331 published pages, all with a title, 144 with titles by language, none with a remembered modal. Commits 565bdc27, 9ba17aa4, 64e5b620.
