# D74: Search by meaning

**Search by meaning (Phase 2, S2): products are embedded by the store's AI and found by meaning as well as by their words, the two merged by reciprocal rank fusion; without AI, or when it fails, search is by words alone.** Each active product's translations are embedded (`src/server/embeddings.ts`) from their title, categories and tags, and description (at most 4,000 characters) with the store's search model (D73), and kept in `commerce.product_embeddings` (pgvector in the `extensions` schema;
no fixed length, as models differ) with the model's `space` and an md5 of what was embedded: a translation is embedded again only when its text, its terms or the model change. The five-minute cron brings every store up to date (Kaizen's AI for stores without their own, then each store's own, at most 256 texts per AI per run, 32 per request), and saving a product does its store at once.
A search (`rankedSearch()`) asks for the query's vector (`queryVector()`, cached for days per store, model and text, waiting at most 1.5 seconds), takes the 50 closest products (active, priced in the market, by their closest translation, of the same model) that reach the store's similarity limit, and merges them with the 100 best keyword results by reciprocal rank fusion (k = 60). Type-ahead stays keyword only.
Each search's log keeps how close the closest product came (`semantic_best`) and how many results only meaning found (`meaning_results`); the store's Search page shows how often meaning helped, and how close meaning came on searches that found nothing, to set the limit by.

Date: 2026-09-27

Asked for by the owner ("continue with S2"). Reciprocal rank fusion needs no tuning between keyword and vector scores, which are on different scales. An exact scan of a store's own vectors instead of an approximate (HNSW) index: an index across all stores filtered to one can miss that store's products, and stores' catalogues are small; revisit per model when one outgrows it. The table is outside the Drizzle schema, which cannot name a type in another schema.
The similarity limit is set per provider after measuring (Test on the AI pages, and the search log). Measured in production with OpenAI's `text-embedding-3-small` (2026-09-27): searches that fit came out at 0.33–0.55 and nonsense at 0.16, so new providers start at 0.3 and Kaizen's was set to 0.3; results by meaning must also come within 0.1 of the closest, so a low limit does not let in everything above it.
Searches' vectors and filters are kept in `commerce.search_cache` (shared by every server instance, keyed by an md5 of the model, market, the store's terms for filters, and the search; 30 days, pruned by the five-minute cron): with `'use cache'`, whose memory is per instance, repeated searches in production still waited 3–5 seconds for the model. CI's database is `pgvector/pgvector:pg17`; PGlite loads `@electric-sql/pglite-pgvector`.
