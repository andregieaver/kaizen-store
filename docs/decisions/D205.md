# D205: Brands

**Brands: moved by the visitor, greys, links; no Pause button.** A carousel's movement may be `none`: still, one logo at a time by arrows where there is a mouse (`(hover: hover) and (pointer: fine)`) and by swiping on a phone (scroll snap), an arrow off at its end. Logos may be drawn in greys (`greyscale`), in their colours under the pointer or focus (`colourOnHover`).
A brand may link (`href`, checked like an icon list's line); a linked brand is one link whose words are its name, or the logo's alt text when names are hidden. The owner asked for the Pause button to go: a moving carousel still pauses under the pointer or the keyboard's focus and stands still for visitors who prefer less motion, but on a touch screen it cannot be paused, which WCAG 2.2.2 asks for when movement lasts over five seconds; the editor's note says how it stops.
The second copy of a moving strip is `inert`, so its links are not reached twice.
