# D97: Every profile has a picture

**Every profile has a picture: the person's own, else their Gravatar through Kaizen, else their initials.** Accounts (owners, staff, hosts, platform admins; set under Your account) and store customers (set under My account) keep a picture in the public `avatars` bucket (`avatar_path` on `accounts` and `customers`): the browser crops it to a 256-pixel square as WebP (`squarePicture()`, no camera metadata), the server stores it under a new random name (`accounts/{account}/…`,
`customers/{store}/{customer}/…`, at most 512 KB) and removes the one it replaces; a customer's goes when their account is deleted. Pictures are personal, so they are not in the media library (D88).
`<Avatar>` (`src/components/avatar.tsx`) draws the initials (`initials()`) with the picture over them, and `avatarFor()` (`src/server/avatars.ts`) says which picture: shown in the admin's header, Team, customers (list, page and the customer bar), Kaizen's platform customers and the shopper's My account.
Without a picture of their own, pages link to `/api/gravatar/{sha256 of the email}?k=…`, which asks Gravatar on the server (`d=404`, rating G) and answers its picture, or an empty one the initials show through; the address is signed with a key derived from `SETTINGS_ENCRYPTION_KEY`, so the route serves only Kaizen's own and is no open proxy, and without that key there is no Gravatar.
Browsers never reach Gravatar, so it learns no visitor's address and no consent is needed; what it receives from Kaizen's server is the email's hash.
