# D259: Starting a store from a preset

**Starting a store from a preset (the theme showcase plan, phase 1 step 4a, `docs/theme-showcase-phase-1.md`).** An access request keeps the preset chosen beside the theme (`access_requests.design_variation`, only a key its published version has), and approval and *Create a store* apply the theme with that preset, the theme's own look when a later version dropped it.
The design cards at sign-up and *Create a store* offer each preset under its theme with its trade, and the admin's approval select lists them; a choice is `{theme}` or `{theme}:{preset}`. *Start a store with …* on the theme page opens sign-up with the look chosen and, where its content pack's template is published, that template chosen too.
