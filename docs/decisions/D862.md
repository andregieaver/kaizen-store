# D862: Contract editor: side by side or stacked, and a draft saved as a template

Date: 2026-11-20

**Asked by the owner (20 November 2026):** a switch between the draft and its preview shown side by side or stacked, side by side by
default, and a way to keep a draft's words as a template, for reuse and to save time.

- **Side by side or stacked:** `ContractEditor` sets *What it says* and *How it will look* side by side on a wide screen (`xl`, 1280 px
  and up), the preview kept in view and scrolling on its own while the words scroll; one press (*Layout*: *Side by side*, *Stacked*) stacks
  them as D857 had them, each the page's full width. The choice is the browser's own (`localStorage` `kz-contract-layout`, read through
  `useSyncExternalStore`, side by side where nothing is kept). Below `xl` the two are always stacked and the switch is not shown: side by
  side was too narrow there (D857's reason, which still holds for narrower screens).
- **Save as template:** beside Save, *Save as template* asks one thing, the template's name (the draft's title until changed), and keeps
  the words as they are on the screen, saved or not, as a new template (`templateFromDraft()`, `customers:write`, the blocks checked as a
  template's by `parseBlocks()`): in the draft's language, with its reminder days and the days to sign of the template it was made from.
  A name another template in use has is refused, so nothing is overwritten; the draft is not changed. The answer links to the new template.
  Audited as `contract.template_created` with the contract's id.
- **Platform:** Kaizena Flow's contracts are its house store's (D466), so it has both.

Tests: `src/server/contracts.int.test.ts` (the words as typed, the language and days, the draft unchanged, the name from the title, a name
taken, words that cannot be kept, a reader, a draft that is gone), `e2e/signed-in/contracts.spec.ts` (the switch kept over a reload; a
template made from the draft opens with its words).

Idle cost: none: one server action more, run only when pressed.
