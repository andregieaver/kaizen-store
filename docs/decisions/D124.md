# D124: A Work client can be filled in from Brønnøysundregistrene's open Enhetsregisteret

**A Work client can be filled in from Brønnøysundregistrene's open Enhetsregisteret.** Type a Norwegian organisation number or a company name on the client form (`BrregLookup`); the register (`data.brreg.no`, NLOD, no key) gives the registered name, business address (else the postal address) and whether the company is in the VAT register, and the form fills in the legal name, organisation number, address, country and, only for a company registered for VAT, its VAT number `NO{number}MVA`.
Pure parts in `src/lib/brreg.ts` (the mod-11 check digit is checked before any call; a name in capitals is read as a name; a bankrupt, winding-up or deleted company gets a warning, not a refusal), calls in `src/server/brreg.ts` (fixed host, 6 s timeout, a lookup cached a day, a search not cached, a failing register is `unavailable` and never blocks saving), actions `lookupCompanyAction`/`searchCompaniesAction` behind `requireMember` and the Work switch.
Only the number or the typed words are sent. The register has no bank account, email or phone, so those stay manual; the managing director and chair are not fetched (personal data, and invoices go to the company). Everything filled in stays editable.
The same panel (`src/components/admin/brreg-lookup.tsx`, taking its two actions as props so each place keeps its own rule about who may ask) is on a shop's company accounts (`CompanyFields`, `/admin/{store}/companies`): there it fills only the name (if none is typed) and the organisation number as digits, since a company account holds no address or VAT number; its actions (`lookupRegisterCompanyAction`, `searchRegisterCompaniesAction`) ask only for store membership, not for Work.
