# D226: Every admin card is `Card` or `CARD_CLASS`

**Every admin card is `Card` or `CARD_CLASS` (`docs/admin-style.md` section 8, step 10).** `Card` gained `description` (a sentence under the title on a line of its own, muted, at the 75-character measure) and `level={4}`, and its header keeps a small margin when the card lays its children out with a gap, so the two do not add up.
The 35 section cards left after D225 moved: 13 whose title sat with a hint in a wrapper by a codemod (title and hint to `title` and `description`, line breaks joined by JSX's rule), the rest by hand: the reminder switches and an order's *Edit items* and an A/B test's *Add another version* to `aside` (as `Button`s), a customer's linked name as the title, a status card inside a `role="status"` box, untitled boxes as `Card` without a title, and boxes named another way (an `aria-label`, a
screen-reader title, a fieldset, a form, a list item, a notice) keep their element with `CARD_CLASS`: 52 such class names in 42 files. Light mode's cards are flat everywhere now (D218). `admin-type.scan.test.ts` refuses the old bordered card. `PagePlaceForm` lost an unused `id`. No migration.
