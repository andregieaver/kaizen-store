# D210: A gallery fades one tile into the next, and its hover zoom is the owner's

**A gallery fades one tile into the next, and its hover zoom is the owner's.** A gallery carousel that shows one tile at every screen size can fade from tile to tile instead of sliding, with the fade's time (150 ms to 3 s); with more than one tile in view a fade would hide the others, so it slides whatever is set.
The fade follows the carousel's own settings: arrows, dots, a swipe across the tile on a phone (which opens nothing, and a swipe up or down still scrolls), going back to the first, and autoplay with its rules (never under reduced motion, stops at any interaction, waits under the pointer or with the tab hidden, a Pause button); the tiles not shown cannot be reached, and without a script the tiles are a row to scroll.
How much a tile's picture grows under the pointer (0 to 30 %, 3 unless set) and how long it takes (the site's hover time unless set, 0 to 3 s) are settings of the gallery; reduced motion keeps it still.
