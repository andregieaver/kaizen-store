# D797: The AI answers a store's form messages (100-day B2)

**The AI answers a store's form messages (`docs/100-day-challenge.md` B2; 19 November 2026).** A contact form's message was people only
(D382): the database started it in the queue and never let it go to the AI. Now, while the desk works AI first and its new
`support_settings.form_ai` is on (Support settings, *Let the AI answer messages from your contact forms first*, beside the forms'
choice), or the store is in the challenge, a form's message with an email answer starts with the AI (migration
`20261119133536_ordinary_warlock`: a form's conversation may start with the AI on a desk AI first, and still never goes to it later).

`formToInbox()` runs the AI's answer after the form's response (`after()`), through the same `answer()` as an email's: counted under
the address, handed to a person without an assistant or a model, past a limit, on failure or when the AI asks. The answer goes by
email to the form's address. A form's address is only typed, so the AI reads no orders by it; `emailCustomer()` counts a form's
conversation only once the customer wrote in it by email from that address (its DMARC check not failed), as B1 does for emails.
Without an email answer, with people only, or for a store with sensitive information, people answer as before. Tone, summaries and
the other AI helpers still leave form conversations out.

The platform: Kaizena Flow's own site's forms reach the house store's desk, which has the same switch; the platform's desk takes no
form's message (D382). Tests: `src/db/support.test.ts`, `src/server/support-forms.int.test.ts`.
