# D36: Store owners see every shopper's wishlists, what is in each, and what went from a list to the cart and was bought

**Store owners see every shopper's wishlists, what is in each, and what went from a list to the cart and was bought.** The Wishlists page shows each list with its shopper (a customer, linked to their page, or a guest not signed in), how many items it holds, how many went to the cart and how many were bought; it has figures for the store (lists, saved items, items put in the cart from a list, items bought, the share bought and their value), the most wished-for products, and a search by list,
customer or product. A list's page shows its shopper, every item with its variant, quantity, price today and stock, and each time items went from it to the cart. The record "From list to purchase" lists every item put in the cart from a list, newest first, with the list, the shopper and what came of it: bought (with its order), ordered but not paid, order cancelled, still in the cart, taken out of the cart, or left in a cart never checked out.
Each add is written with the cart line (in the same transaction), keeping the list's name, the product's title and the price as they were; whether it was bought is read from the order the cart became (the same variant, bought once), counting only what was bought, at the line's price after discounts. Order pages mark the lines that came from a wishlist, linking to the list; a customer's page links to their lists. Owners only look: the lists stay the shoppers'.

Date: 2026-09-25

Asked for by the owner. The record outlives the list, the product and the customer's account (their link is cleared, the rest kept). Items added from the product page are not wishlist adds, even if saved in a list.
