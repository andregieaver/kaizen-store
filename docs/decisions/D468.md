# D468: Activities

**Activities (S3 of `docs/own-books-and-sales.md`; `src/lib/activities.ts`, `src/server/activities.ts`, `ActivitiesCard`, `TodayCard`), built.** A call, a meeting or a task: who does it and when, about a customer, a company or a deal of the store, seen by the team. Typed as a day and a time in the store's own time zone, or a day alone (a task for Friday); a length only for a meeting.
The *Today* list on the store's home: what is late (before today, or a timed one more than an hour past), then today's with a time in order, then today's for the day, then what was done today; one person's or the team's. The dispatcher's `activities` job tells the person an activity is for on their devices (D361) when a timed one is due, once (`notified_at`, claimed so two runs never both send).
A deal's activities go with it; a person's or a company's lose the link; a merge moves them, an erasure deletes those about the person alone, the export lists them under the `sales` purpose, and the timeline shows each one done (kind `activity`).
A deal's, a person's and a company's page lists theirs (`ActivitiesCard`): what is still to do by when it is due, each done with one round button, what is done folded below, and *Add activity*, a call today for the one adding it unless they choose a kind, a day, a time, a length (a meeting only), a member of the team whose access has not ended and a note; each opens to be changed or deleted. Added on a deal, it is about the deal's person and company too, so it is on their pages.
The store's home shows *Today* (`TodayCard`, while Deals is on, to a member with `customers:read`): the viewer's, or the team's one step away (`?today=team`), each linked to its deal or person and done there with one press. The board's cards say each open deal's next step ("Next: call Mon 9 Nov", red once late) or *No next step*.
Its words are the `deals` area's (`activities`, `today`); its actions `deals/activity-actions.ts`, written by `customers:write` while Deals is on. **The platform** gets them through Kaizena Flow's own store (D466, D467) with no step of its own.
