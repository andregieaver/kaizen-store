# D18: Kaizen's plans for stores are managed in Kaizen and billed with Stripe Billing

**Kaizen's plans for stores are managed in Kaizen and billed with Stripe Billing.** Platform admins create plans (tiers) with monthly and yearly prices per currency, excluding VAT, and a fee per sale; saving copies them to Stripe as Products and Prices. Admins start, change and cancel each store's plan and can give a store its own fee.

Date: 2026-09-24

The subscription lives on Kaizen's Stripe account with the store's own connected account as the customer (`customer_account`); Stripe emails an invoice each period, due in 14 days (`send_invoice`), with 25 % Norwegian VAT for stores in Norway and none for others (reverse charge); since 5 November 2026 nothing is billed to a store without its country (`NO_COUNTRY`: a plan, a premium theme or a domain waits, and Billing says where to add it), never taken to be Norway, and the business details ask
for the country with none chosen. Store owners see their plan under Plan and open Stripe's customer portal for invoices and card details. A third platform webhook keeps `store_billing` current. The fee on a sale is the store's own fee, else its plan's while it is on the plan, else the platform default. Prices never change in place: a new amount is a new Stripe Price, and stores keep the old one until moved.
Taking fees straight from a store's Stripe balance is left for when Stripe offers it outside preview.
