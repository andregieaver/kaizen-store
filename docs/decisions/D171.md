# D171: A store can be closed, and suspended and reopened by the platform; nothing is deleted

**A store can be closed, and suspended and reopened by the platform; nothing is deleted.** `docs/store-closure.md` is the contract. An owner closes from *Settings, Close store* after a sign-in from the last ten minutes and the store's address typed in; it is blocked while paid goods are unsent or subscriptions and weekly deliveries run, and warns about the rest.
Closing ends the Kaizen plan with its period (first: if Stripe refuses, nothing is closed), cancels orders waiting for payment, releases the store's own domains and emails the owners. The owner may reopen for 30 days; the platform suspends, closes (forcibly if need be) and reopens at any time, with a reason the owners are emailed.
The database holds the steps (`stores_status_rules()`) and refuses a new order in a store that is not open (`orders_store_open()`); `memberCan()` limits every role in such a store to orders, customers and analytics and the owner's own key; jobs that email shoppers or call a model skip it (`store_is_active()`). **Permanent deletion of a store that never sold was not built:** orders, invoices and the audit log cannot be deleted and almost no foreign key cascades, so a purge would be a large, risky
ordered delete for little gain. Migration versions: `20261006061208_store_closure` and `20261006061215_store_closure_rules`, applied by CI in the `release` job on 6 October 2026 at 07:05 UTC. Advisors checked after: no new finding.
