# D842: A listing fetches its first screen of tiles ahead and the rest on intent

**A listing fetches its first screen of tiles ahead and the rest on intent (bloat review step 2.8; 20 November 2026).** The owner's
yes of 12 November: on a full listing only the first screen of product tiles (`SIGHT_TILES`, six: three rows of two on a phone) is
fetched ahead on sight, and the rest when pointed at, touched or focused; a card's swatches and a listing's chips wait for intent
(`ProductCard`'s `policy`, `src/components/product-card.tsx`; the listing, the store home and the term and blog listings). A seeded
48-product category read to the end on a phone makes 15 router requests (47 with every tile on sight; the brief guessed 96), held at
20 by `e2e/prefetch.spec.ts` (`FULL_LISTING_MOST`); `directLinks` 383 to 381. CI run 1760 passed on it. On production the demo store's
product list and front page read to the end fetch no product page ahead, only the cart.

The partial-prefetching trial (local production builds of Next 16.4.6, one with `export const prefetch = 'partial'` per destination;
raw output in `docs/audits/bloat-review-2026-11/evidence/partial-prefetching-trial.md`, table in `docs/performance.md`): only the
product page changed. On the seeded category its router requests fell 15 to 10 and bytes 70,692 to 20,481, but the shared shell holds
one tile's product, so the second tile's heading took 118 ms against 43 (1,098 against 52 with the navigation fetch held 1 s), as the
product page reads its params outside a Suspense boundary. The listing, the store home and the admin's orders page fetched the same
either way. So the first-screen rule stands: no page sets `prefetch = 'partial'` and `partialPrefetching` stays false; look again when
the product page reads its params behind Suspense or at Next's next major release. The admin page in the trial makes the choice cover
`/admin/platform`'s lists too. Idle cost: none. Commits fef414080, 66d246771, 96064a3a6.
