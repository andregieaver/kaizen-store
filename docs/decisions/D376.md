# D376: Reduced VAT rates with their sources

**Reduced VAT rates with their sources (wave 10 unit M, `docs/wave-10-must-haves.md` 4, `docs/wave-10-notes/m.md`); built.** A custom migration enters 130 reduced rates through `commerce.set_vat_rate()` for the countries and categories that had none (food, books, periodicals, medicines, culture and events, accommodation, children's goods): 23 EU countries that had none and the missing categories of Germany, Finland and Sweden, while Denmark has no reduced rate for them and Norway needed none.
Each was read on 2026-10-28 from an official source, named with its address in `source`: the European Commission's Taxes in Europe Database (its VAT search for each country's latest situation, 1 July 2026, or 1 January 2026 for Cyprus, Greece and Portugal; the Commission's VAT rates page links no other document), the Agencia Tributaria's 2026 table for Spain's books, periodicals and medicines, and Revenue's pages for Ireland's children's clothing and footwear.
Where a country has several rates for a category, the row is the one covering most of it and the note names the others; a rate for only a narrow part of a category, an exemption that depends on the supplier, or no reduced rate gives no row, so the standard rate stays the fallback (Denmark has none; the rest are listed in the notes). Rates start on 1 January 2026, except Belgian accommodation (12 %, from 1 July 2026, the first situation that shows the change from 6 %).
Every row's note begins *Unverified*: `verified_at` stays null until a platform admin checks it with an accountant, never by code, and the existing rows of Germany, Finland, Sweden and Norway agree with what was read. `checkout.eu-vat-by-destination-vat-inclusive-prices-reduced-rates` stays partial. Migration: `20261028171816_reduced_vat_rates` (applied by CI; the release job of run 710 found nothing left pending).
