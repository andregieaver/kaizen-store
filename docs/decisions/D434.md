# D434: Price on request and the words around the price in the product file

**Price on request and the words around the price in the product file.** The Kaizen product file (D165, `docs/wave-2-data.md` 4.1.2) carries, on a product's first row after `standard_category`: `price_on_request` (`true`/`false`, D431), `price_words` (`store`: the store's words, `none`: no words, `own`: the product's own, D429) and the product's own words, `price_prefix` and `price_suffix` for the main language and `price_prefix:{locale}`, `price_suffix:{locale}` for each other language of the
store, as the text columns are (`productColumns()`, `PRICE_WORDS_CELLS`, `priceWordColumn()`). Export writes the words each language shows (one without words of its own shows the main language's).
Import (`planProduct()`, `overlayPriceWords()`): a blank flag or choice keeps the product's, as `status` does; a word cell is read only for a product with its own words, replaces that word in its language (blank for none, tidied as the editor tidies) and is checked by the editor's `priceWordProblem()`, a language whose columns are absent keeping its words; the problems are per line with stable codes (`price_on_request.invalid`, `price_on_request.not_goods`, `price_words.unknown`,
`price_words.invalid` naming the column and never quoting it, `price_words.ignored` for words given with `store` or `none`), each with what to do (`FIX_FOR`). A file without the columns (an older export, Shopify's or WooCommerce's) leaves the product's flag and words as they are. The write is `saveProduct()`, so its checks and D433's refusal apply. Export then import changes nothing (`canonical()` compares the flag and the words each of the store's languages shows).

Date: 2026-11-03

Asked for by the owner. *Why `store`, `none`, `own`:* the three choices of the product editor, in words a person typing a spreadsheet understands (the editor's *inherit* is not one). *Why the words as each language shows them:* a cell that says what the shopper reads is what an owner edits; a language that followed the main one comes back with the same words, so nothing a shopper sees changes and the round trip holds. *Why blank keeps the flag:* as `status` and `active`, a blank cell in a
spreadsheet is more often an untouched one than a decision.
