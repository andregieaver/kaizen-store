# D565: SMS marketing through the store's own 46elks account

**SMS marketing through the store's own 46elks account (11 November 2026, `docs/sms-marketing.md`, `src/lib/sms.ts`, `src/server/sms.ts`, `/admin/{store}/text-messages`, `/sms/{token}`, `/api/sms/{key}/…`).** The owner chose it as the next thing to build ("go", after the list of what was still missing), so D405's "skipped for now" is reversed.
The provider is 46elks (Sweden): each store connects its own account (the OWNER's; API username and password, the password encrypted), which also tells its currency. **Consent** is its own (`sms_subscribers`, history in `sms_consent_events` by trigger): checkout's unticked box (only with SMS on) with a mobile number keeps it `pending` on the order, subscribed once paid, gone after 7 days unpaid; the database refuses a message to a number that is not subscribed. **Stopping**: the stop link in
every text (a page with one button, never on GET), a STOP reply (and STOPP, AVSLUTT, SLUTA, AFMELD, LOPETA…) on the store's 46elks number, staff, erasure. **Sending**: a text message (`sms_campaigns`, `marketing:write`) goes to every subscribed number or a segment's; each text waits for its reader's hours in their country's zone (09 to 20 unless narrowed, never 21 to 08, no Sundays in France); priced by 46elks's own dry run per country before it starts and refused past the month's cap (in the
account's currency), stopped by the job when the month's real cost reaches it; at most 4 parts; the store's name first, the stop link last. The five-minute dispatcher's `sms` job sends (`runSms()`).
Numbers, history and texts are personal data (privacy export, erasure, customer merge); `sms_settings` is a secret table (never copied, left out of the store file). **Platform parity:** Kaizena Flow's own marketing lists are its house store's (D467), whose admin has the same page; owners sign up without a checkout of goods, so their numbers would come from a sign-up field, named as the next step with a form's phone field. Kept out of the AI manager's prompt for one deploy (`NOT_YET_IN_PROMPT`).
Not built: My account's switch, a form's phone field, automations by text, public holidays, AI tools; not tried against 46elks itself (its documentation and a stand-in). Parity row `customers.sms-marketing` rated Partial (built and tested; the real provider and a legal reading of the words are left). Tests: `sms.test.ts`, `sms.int.test.ts` (a stand-in for 46elks).
