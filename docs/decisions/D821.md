# D821: Each admin page hands cuts of the word areas its parts read

**Each admin page hands cuts of the word areas its parts read (bloat review step 2.27; 19 November 2026).** A cut is some top-level
groups of an existing word area (`cut()` in `src/lib/admin-text.ts`): the same objects, no string moved, no catalogue file added,
and typed, so a part that reads a group outside the cut it is handed does not compile. A page hands the cuts its client parts name
instead of whole areas (the product, order and customer pages, the store home's drafts card, the page lists, the page editor, and
in commit 5 integrations, settings, support settings, analytics, the platform's wizards, staging, the media library, menus,
imports, the Dream Factory and the forms editor). The AI manager's button reads its own few words on every screen, and the chat's
words come with its start call. `admin-cuts.scan.test.ts` holds the cuts, and the words-handed baseline only falls.

Measured on a signed-in build: the product editor's document 431,497 to 236,060 bytes, the builder's 606,658 to 460,097 (words
handed 319,551 to 182,315), a fresh store home 165,942 to 134,689, a product save's answer 311,274 to 130,588; words handed across
the admin in English 13,219,401 to 6,132,124 bytes, and sites handing over 50 KB with more than 1.25 times what their parts name
68 to 3. No screen showed a fallback in English or Norwegian (38 screens walked as the Norwegian owner and platform admin). The
words every screen carries fell from 33,723 to 16,617 bytes in English (47,103 to 17,180 in Norwegian), not to the brief's 15.5
and 16 KB: common and shell alone are about 15 KB, so the ceiling holds today's figure with its usual room (the rule from step
0.43). Left over the rule, each needing a decision of its own: the theme editor's shell (its editors are its children, which the
scan cannot see), Work's account layout (31 groups of work read through files the store's Work screens share) and an order's page
(drafts read whole). Commits 632a5da42, 1a5d274a9, 707f4b38a, b5c6cf6de, ef0b030e5, ffec560c6, 41d065061, 947dc113e, 04943ed0e.
