# D496: The VAT return from our own books

**The VAT return from our own books (B5 step 1, 8 November 2026, `src/lib/vat-return.ts`, `src/server/vat-return.ts`, Settings → Books → *VAT return*).** A term's *mva-melding* computed from the store's ledger: the lines Skatteetaten's form asks for, from VAT by code (a sale's base, rate and VAT; a sale without VAT, codes 5, 6, 51 and 52, its base at 0 %; a purchase, codes 1 and 11 to 15, only its deduction; a reverse charge, 81 to 92, its VAT due and, where deductible, the same as a
deduction; a code the form does not take named and left out), the VAT to pay or to get back, and the due day (the 10th of the second month after, 31 August for May–June, 10 March for a year; a weekend moved to Monday, a public holiday left to the owner with a sentence). A book's terms are two months or a year (`ledger_books.vat_term`, the owner's choice under *Your VAT terms*, audited); a book that opened inside a term counts it from its opening day and says so.
Until Kaizena Flow files it, the page says to type the lines into Skatteetaten's form or give the file to the accountant: *Download the return (XML)* writes Skatteetaten's `mvaMeldingDto` (schema `skattemeldingformerverdiavgift` v1.0, github.com/Skatteetaten/mva-meldingen), validated with xmllint against the schema; the validated sample is kept (`src/data/vat-return/mva-melding-sample.xml`) and a unit test holds the writer to it, so the XML changes only with a new validation.
The return is for the books' organisation number, else the store's; with neither, no file is offered (Skatteetaten takes none without one) and the page asks for it under Company, found when the download of a test store without one failed the schema.
The term is in the page's address (`/settings/books/vat/2026-6`, the Books screen linking to today's): a page with a parameter is not in the AI manager's map of pages, so the manager's prompt stays the one its safety eval was recorded with, and the migration (which waits for every check) is never held behind a recording that can only be made once the new prompt is live; the AI manager learns the page with its next recording.
With Tripletex connected, the same days are worked out from Tripletex's postings (`GET /ledger/posting`, each posting's VAT code, net and gross, never counting the VAT postings Tripletex makes itself) and compared code by code, with what Tripletex says of the term (`GET /vatReturns/status`: sent or not, and what Skatteetaten says is owed): the pilot's exit (*one VAT period computed by us and compared with the return filed from Tripletex*).
Read only: nothing is posted, filed or written to Tripletex. Readers: anyone who sees the Books; the term length is the owner's. Not yet: filing through Altinn (needs the store's sign-in with Skatteetaten and Kaizena Flow's registration as a system), corrected returns, monthly terms, SAF-T (the next step).
