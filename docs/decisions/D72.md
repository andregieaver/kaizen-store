# D72: Phase 2

**Phase 2 (AI search and product content) is built in five parts, starting with keyword search in Postgres that needs no AI; each AI part keeps working, as keyword search, when its model is unavailable.** S1 (built): every product translation keeps a text-search document of its title (weighted A) and description (C), stemmed in its own language (`commerce.search_config()`: norwegian, swedish, danish, english and others, `simple` otherwise; a stored generated column `product_translations.search`
with a GIN index), and trigram indexes (`pg_trgm` in the `extensions` schema) on titles, SKUs and category and tag names. A search (`src/server/search.ts`) finds active products with a price in the market by their words, by titles close to what was typed (typos, and parts of compound words: "kopp" finds "keramikkopp"), by SKU (an exact SKU first) or by a category or tag of that name, in the market's language where the product has one; titles weigh most.
Shoppers search at `/s/{store}/{market}/search` (a search icon in the header; `search` is a reserved page address), with a few products suggested as they type (keyword only, so no model call slows typing; an ARIA combobox, and a plain form without JavaScript); nothing found, the store's top-level categories are offered.
Every search on the page (not type-ahead) is logged with its number of results (`search_queries`), kept 90 days because queries can hold personal data, and the store's admin shows searches, the zero-result rate, the most searched and the searches that found nothing (`/admin/{store}/search`).
Next: S2 meaning-based search (embeddings in pgvector, fused with keyword results by reciprocal rank fusion), S3 an LLM turning queries into structured filters, with an eval, S4 AI product content (descriptions, translations, attributes, FAQ) approved by staff before publishing, with a filter blocking generic green claims, fake urgency and "best price" claims, and S5 a randomised test of hybrid against keyword search.

Date: 2026-09-27

Asked for by the owner ("start on Phase 2"). Keyword search first because it needs no vendor, gives the baseline the AI parts are measured against, and is what type-ahead and the fallback use anyway. Models will go through Vercel AI Gateway with EU inference and zero data retention (already planned in the residency register); S2 needs the AI Gateway enabled on the Vercel project and pgvector in CI's Postgres image.
