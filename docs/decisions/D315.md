# D315: Domains

**Domains: buying, DNS by the AI manager, and moving away (`docs/domains.md`), built in phases (section 17 says what is built).** A domain is three layers kept apart: registration, DNS and the connection to the store (P8), each with Kaizena Flow or elsewhere and moved on its own.
Decided: the registrant is the store's owner or their company, never Kaizena Flow; generic TLDs through Vercel's Registrar API, country TLDs (which Vercel does not sell: no `.no`, `.se`, `.dk`, `.fi`, `.eu`, `.de`, read 22 October 2026) through a second registrar in the EU behind the same adapter, and `.no` through Norid (only a registrar with Norid's agreement may apply, policy 7.1), whether as Kaizena Flow's own accreditation or through one (open, 2a); a renewal not paid is not renewed, after
reminders at 30, 14, 7 and 1 days (a `.no` is deleted at expiry, and says so); the AI manager changes only DNS Kaizena Flow hosts, after the owner's yes by default, and the owner can set DNS record changes to automatic (`stores.domain_dns_auto`, never buying, renewal, nameservers or moves; unit B's autonomy levels take it over); closing a store keeps its domains with its owners (a change to D171); buying opens to every store only once moving away (phase 4) is built; with cells (D314),
registrations and DNS stay in the control plane's Vercel team and only the connection follows a store. Buying checks the plan's domain limit (D314) before anything is charged.
The rest as recommended (the owner, 23 October 2026: "build the domains.md based on your recommendations"): `.no` through the EU registrar first and Norid's own accreditation near 100 `.no` sales a year; prices are the registrar's converted at the ECB rate plus a margin, rounded, with a floor (`commerce.domain_pricing`, platform admins), on every plan, no free year; only owners buy, renew, change DNS and move; no referral credit; no premium names; under automatic a change that stops email still
asks; a bought domain takes one of the plan's domains once connected. A *Domain search* component for Kaizena Flow's own pages (`docs/domains.md` 16).
