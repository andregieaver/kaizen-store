# D685: A customer's request by email carried out once they confirm it from the order's address

**A customer's request by email carried out once they confirm it from the order's address (`docs/100-day-challenge.md` B3; 16 November 2026, `src/server/request-confirm.ts`, `src/lib/request-confirm-text.ts`, `/s/{store}/{market}/request/{token}`, `src/server/support-requests.ts`, `src/server/autopilot-events.ts`, migration `20261116151132_worthless_jimmy_woo`).** D640 carried out an address change or a cancellation only for the order's own customer signed in on the store's chat: never one asked
by email, because an email reaches the AI when its DMARC check did not *fail*, and a domain with no DMARC policy does not fail, so its From can be anyone's.
Now, when the email agent passes such a request to staff (`request_staff_action`), and the conversation's address is the order's own, the order paid, not held and nothing of it sent, and the store's autopilot reacts, `offerRequestConfirmation()` emails the order's address once (`support.request_confirm`) with code's own words for the request (and the new address for an address change) and a one-time link that works for 48 hours (`REQUEST_CONFIRM_HOURS`; the token is kept only as a sha-256 hash,
`support_actions.confirm_token_hash`, unique). The link opens a page that says what will be done; opening it changes nothing (a mail scanner may open links), only its Confirm, a POST, sets `customer_confirmed_at`, once, within the hours. A confirmed request then counts as a signed-in chat's: the `staff_request` event and `carry_out_address_change` accept it (the conversation's address must still be the order's), and a cancellation still goes through `cancel_order` and the refund queue.
The AI is told in the tool's answer when the link went, so it tells the customer to press Confirm and never says it is done. Words hand-written in Norwegian, Swedish, Danish and English (legal review pending, as the support emails).
`request` is a reserved store address in every list of them and in the database's `pages_store_slug_not_reserved` (no store had a page there), and a private one, so visit counting and recordings never keep its address. **Platform:** Kaizena Flow's own desk serves owners, not customers with orders, so store-only.
