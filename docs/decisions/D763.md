# D763: Data protection officer per account, with a store's own override

**Data protection officer per account, with a store's own override (18 November 2026; `src/lib/privacy-officer.ts`, `src/server/privacy-officers.ts`, `PrivacyOfficerForm`).** Asked for by the owner after the lawyer's review of recordings (D656): the officer is named once for the account (`commerce.organisations`, D748) on Your account, by its owner, and a store may name its own on Settings → Company.
The officer is someone on the team (an account, read each time, so a new name or address follows; one who leaves the team, a collaborator whose time ran out included, names nobody and the store falls back to its account's) or someone else (name, email, phone, firm). `commerce.privacy_officers` holds one row per account and one per store that names its own (RLS on; personal data of the owner's kind, never copied with a store).
The officer is named under Contact on the store's privacy statement starter (en, nb, sv, da; in the legal facts only when one is named, so the legal AI's upkeep rewrites nothing for a store without one), on the store's recordings assessment (D656) and in its sign-off, and Kaizena Flow's own, the platform account's, set on Platform → Pages beside the legal pages, on Kaizena Flow's privacy notice when those pages are written again (while kaizenaflow.com is served from the house store, D484, the
writer writes the house store's copies, the ones visitors read, and leaves the platform's frozen ones). Idle cost: one table and two indexes; no job, and a read only on the pages that show or use the officer (Your account, Company, the legal starters and writer, the assessment).
