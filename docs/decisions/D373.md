# D373: Express checkout on the product page and in the cart, wallets per web address, Klarna's line

**Express checkout on the product page and in the cart, wallets per web address, Klarna's line (wave 10 unit J, `docs/wave-10-must-haves.md` 4); built.** The express area (`ExpressArea`, `src/components/express-area.tsx`) is a plain button until it is pressed, so nothing of Stripe's is in a product page's first scripts: the press calls `expressCheckoutAction()` (in the cart's actions), which on a product page first puts the chosen variant in the cart through `addToCart()` itself, then
`startExpress()` (`src/server/express-checkout.ts`): the basket must be goods sent by post and bought once (`expressFits()` in `src/lib/express-checkout.ts`: a download needs the shopper's tick, a subscription its consent, a booking its time and contact, a gift card who it is for, a host's listing the host's account, which a wallet's sheet cannot ask), the delivery must be settled without a postal code the wallet would give only later (`deliveryReady()`), and then the order is placed and its
Checkout Session opened by `startCheckout()`, the cart button's own path, so price, VAT, stock held, discounts and the delivery are decided once; the open checkout's client secret goes to the browser that owns the cart, as on the checkout page.
Only then is `express-panel.tsx` loaded (Stripe's Express Checkout Element over `CheckoutElementsProvider`), whose wallet press runs the checkout page's steps (`deliveryReadyAction()`, the store's terms recorded and held while a box is unticked) before `checkout.confirm()`; Stripe returns the shopper to the order page, and the wallet's address and the cart's delivery reach the order through `applySession()` as for any payment.
It is offered only where `expressOn()` says (Kaizena Flow's own checkout page, Stripe as the store's checkout, payments on, not a store template, a signed-in shopper where accounts are required (D365), a private buyer, no checkout questions (D357)), on a product of goods or a bundle without purchase options, for its variants sent by post and in stock, and in a cart that fits; a product page's press pays for the whole cart and the panel says so when the cart holds more.
Klarna's line under a price (`KlarnaMessage`, Stripe's messaging element, `klarna-element.tsx`) loads once the price is on the screen and the browser is idle, only where `klarnaMessaging()` finds Klarna active on the store's account in the mode it takes payments in, on for the country (D291), and a currency and country the element draws.
The Payments page's *Wallets and express checkout* card (`WalletsCard`) lists Apple Pay, Google Pay, Link and Klarna with their capability on the account and their state on each web address the store's shoppers pay on (`walletHosts()`: its own hosts once it has them, else Kaizena Flow's) as Stripe's `PaymentMethodDomain` reports it, with Stripe's reason; *Check again* (owners, `checkWalletDomains()`) registers any address missing and reads them back into `stripe_accounts.wallet_domains`.
`docs/pci.md` says what the elements on a page outside the pay routes take and do not take.
