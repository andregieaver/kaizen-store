# D67: Stays and rentals are booked by whole nights or days over rooms and items; a cart line's quantity is the nights or days, so prices, discounts, VAT and deposits work as for any line

**Stays and rentals are booked by whole nights or days over rooms and items; a cart line's quantity is the nights or days, so prices, discounts, VAT and deposits work as for any line.** Products of kind `stay` (nights in a room or home, accommodation VAT by default) and `rental` (days with an item) are booked over `booking_resources` of kind `unit` or `item` (a name and how many at once, no hours).
Each has a check-in and check-out time (pick-up and return), a shortest and longest length (1–365), notice, how far ahead, and the payment and cancellation rules of D66. A stay of N nights runs from check-in on its first day to check-out N days later, so a guest may arrive the day another leaves; a rental of N days from pick-up on the first to return on the last.
The product page shows four weeks of dates, the shopper picks the first and the last, and the cart line carries the check-in instant and the length; `commerce.hold_booking` holds the whole span at checkout, as for appointments. Shoppers cancel stays and rentals themselves within the rule but do not move them.

Date: 2026-09-27

Defaults chosen for the owner: one flat price per night or day (seasonal prices and a cleaning fee come in B3c), whichever room or item is free (no choice for the shopper), check-in 15:00 and check-out 11:00, rentals 09:00–17:00. B3 is built in parts: B3a (this), B3b (blocked dates and calendar sync with Airbnb and Booking.com), B3c (seasonal prices, cleaning fee).
