# D324: Search merchandising

**Search merchandising (wave 6 unit F, `docs/wave-6-catalogue.md` Unit F); built.** Synonym groups per language (`search_synonyms`: two-way or one-way), expanded in keyword search as OR'd alternatives (`expandQuery()`) and counted as typed words when query understanding keeps a category or tag; pins, boosts, buries and search redirects (`search_rules`: a product, a category or tag, or a page, for every search or for searches holding words) applied to the search's own order after the fusion
(`merchandise()`); the zero-result report offers a synonym or a redirect. The understanding's cache key holds the synonyms, so nothing stale is served.

Date: 2026-10-26

*Why:* `catalogue.search-merchandising-synonyms-boosts-pins`: Shopify's Search & Discovery app has synonym groups and boosts; Kaizena Flow's search had none. Built in three steps (F1 synonyms, F2 rules, F3 tools), all on 2026-10-26. F1: `search_synonyms` (at most 500 a store, `search_synonyms_rules()`), `expandQuery()` in `matchingIds()` and as the understanding's `aliases`, the Search page's *Synonyms* card.
F2: `search_rules` (at most 200), `merchandise()` in `searchProducts()` (only the search's own order; a pin the search missed only without filters), `searchRedirect()` in the search section, `search_queries.redirected` left out of every zero-result count (the Search page, analytics, the AI manager's and the overview's), the zero-result list's *Add a synonym* and *Send to a page*, the *Results and pages* card; both tables copied by `clone_store()` and `duplicate_store()`, products and terms
mapped. F3: the owner tools `search_merchandising`, `save_search_synonym`, `save_search_rule`, `delete_search_merchandising` (`settings:*`, gate `public`, area `website`), checked before a yes by `preflightSearchTool()`; the row re-rated Full. Migration versions: `20261026201404` (search_synonyms), `20261026201432` (search_synonyms_sql), `20261026203218` (search_rules), `20261026203255` (search_rules_sql), applied by CI on 2026-10-26 21:15 UTC.
Advisors checked after: INFO only (RLS on with no policy, as every private table; the new tables' indexes not used yet).
