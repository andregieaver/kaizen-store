# D319: Collections in depth

**Collections in depth (wave 6 unit A, `docs/wave-6-catalogue.md` 3); A1 built: a category's own page and the owner's order of its products.** A store's product category or tag has a description (rich text), a picture from the media library and its name and description in the store's other languages (`terms.texts`, never another language's), shown above its products on its page, in its metadata and share picture where it has none of its own, in the sitemap, and by name in menus, filters and the
chat; names and descriptions are in the translation worklist. The owner puts a term's products in order (`product_terms.position`), which the term's page's *Featured* and a content grid's *Your order* follow; products added since come after, by when they were made. The owner's choices on wave 6 (2026-10-25, section 5): all as recommended.
A2 built (2026-10-25): a category or tag can be filled by rules (in a category or tag filled by hand, price in the store's own country, in stock, manufacturer, variant option, a public filter field, kind of product, new arrivals; all or any), worked out each time it is read by one SQL evaluator (`commerce.product_matches_rules()`), so it follows every change to a product with nothing re-run; listed newest first on its page, in grids and the sitemap, reached by campaigns and codes; the owner sees
a live count per condition; turning rules on takes out the products placed by hand, turning them off can keep what matched. The AI manager has `list_categories` and `update_category` (gated `public`).
