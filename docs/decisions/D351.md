# D351: AI in the builder and a store from one description

**AI in the builder and a store from one description (wave 8 unit G, `docs/wave-8-analytics-ai.md` section 4, G); built.** *Change with AI* in the page builder (stores and Kaizena Flow's own, offered while the site's AI has a text model, `canChangePages()`, `PageOwnerContext.aiChange`): the person describes a change to the open page (3 to 1,000 characters) and the routes `/admin/{store}/pages/change` (`website:write`, `storeChangeRequest`) and `/admin/platform/pages/change` run
`changePageWithAi()` (`src/server/page-change-ai.ts`, feature `page_change`) on the page as the editor holds it: the model gets the page's outline as data (`pageOutline()`, `<page-data>`, `UNTRUSTED_TEXT`), the site's own links and the studio's designs, and answers only a plan of the AI manager's `edit_page` changes (`readChangePlan()`: texts set or replaced by key, rows moved or removed, at most two new sections from `PATTERNS`, their pictures left to the person), tried first by
`dryRunEdits()`, each new section written by the studio's writer (`sectionForPage()`), and made by the builder's pure edits (`applyPageEdits()`, links only to `SiteFacts.links`, every text through `findClaims()`); the first change that cannot be made refuses them all.
The result goes back into the editor unsaved, as one Undo step (`AiChangeDialog`), and nothing is saved until the person saves. *Headers and footers*: `draft_header_footer` (ungated, a draft staff see) builds a header or footer page from the standard one's parts (`draftedLayout()`: `HEADER_LOOKS` standard or centred, `FOOTER_LOOKS` columns or simple, one of the site's menus, a section style of the live theme), checked by `siteLayoutProblem()` and saved by `savePage()`; `use_header_footer` (gated
`public`, owner and platform tools) publishes it and chooses it as Header and footer does (`chooseSiteLayout()`), or the standard one again. *A store from one description*: *Start from a description* on Home and in the setup wizard's first step (`StoreDraftPanel`) and the owner tool `draft_store` (ungated: nothing reaches visitors) take a description, the front page's purpose and up to three more pages (`draftStoreInput`), refuse a claim, and queue each page as a `build_page` job
(`page_ai_jobs`, built in the background as drafts); a theme named by id, else one the model chooses by id among the published themes the store may use without paying (`readThemeChoice()`, feature `store_draft`; no answer is no theme), is added to the library not live (`installDesignPreset()`).
The draft is `commerce.store_drafts` (one open per store, written only by `src/server/store-drafts.ts`, audited `store.draft_started`, `store.draft_published`, `store.draft_discarded`). *Your drafts* shows each page as it is built (asked again every 10 seconds) and one press, the owner's yes for every part, publishes each built page through `savePage()` (one with a blocking problem stays a draft and is named), makes the first the front page (`setFrontPage()`), adds the others after the links of
the menu the header shows (or a menu of their own the header then shows, `saveStoreMenu()`, `saveNavigation()`) and makes the theme live (`switchToInstall()`); *Discard* keeps the pages as drafts and the theme in the library. Personal data register: the owner's description of their own business (`staff`), `never` copied. Merges `ai.ai-page-and-theme-generation` into `storefront.ai-store-and-page-building` (decision 7) and closes it in the tracker.
