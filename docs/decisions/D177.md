# D177: Store templates and design profiles get a life

**Store templates and design profiles get a life: draft, publish, unpublish, archive, delete; a design profile is edited on its own pages (outside the parity waves).** `docs/store-templates.md` section 4a and `docs/design-profiles.md` section 2a are the contract.
Both: details (and a template's recommended profile) are saved as a **draft** (`draft` jsonb) and reach owners only on **Publish**; **Unpublish** takes it out of every chooser at once, and a pending access request that chose it gets the Standard store / the template's own look at approval, said to the admin (`approve_access_request()` replaced: `starter_offered_source()`, audit `starterFallback`; `ApproveResult.fellBack`); **Archive** (`archived_at`, never published while archived, a check)
hides it everywhere but an Archived filter and Restore brings it back unpublished; **Delete** only while unused, enforced by the database (`store_starters.used`/`.requested`: a store made from it or a request naming it; `design_presets.used`/`.requested`: a use or a request; a profile's delete clears it as any template's recommendation, published and drafted), with a confirmation; stores are never deleted (D171): a deleted template's store and copies, or a deleted profile's workspace, are closed
and kept, hidden. **Store templates:** Publish freezes the working store into a hidden copy with `clone_store()` (`commerce.freeze_starter()`: `{slug}-v{n}`, `starter`, `stores.starter_copy_of`, no `made_from_starter`; the copy it replaces is closed) and `starter_source()` returns that copy (or, published before D177 and not since, the working store as before), so later changes in the working store reach no new store until the next Publish; owners preview the copy, the admin both. "Changed since
published" is estimated from the working store's activity log. **Design profiles:** made from a store or from scratch (Kaizen's standard look), each with a **workspace** (`design_presets.workspace_store_id`: a hidden starter store copied from the default template, described by no template, fixed); its theme (`ThemeEditor`), header, footer, product page (`PageEditor`/`PageBuilder` in a new `draftOnly` mode, bound to profile actions that check the platform admin and the workspace) and CSS are
edited at `/admin/platform/design-profiles/{id}/…`; Publish takes the workspace's snapshot through `takeSnapshot()`/`snapshotLayout()` into `snapshot`; "Unpublished changes" compares a key of it (`workspace_key`); `applyDesignPreset()` and a new `writeWorkspaceLook()` share `placeSnapshot()`; profiles from before D177 get their workspace lazily from their published snapshot.
Hidden starters (frozen copies, workspaces, deleted templates' stores) are worked in by nobody: `loadMembership()` honours a membership of a starter only while a `store_starters` row describes it, and Work's and Kaizen Life's store lists and `snapshotSources()` leave them out; `applyDesignPreset()` refuses them.

Date: 2026-10-07

Asked for by the platform owner: create a profile from a store or from scratch and configure the whole design from the profile's page, never by going to a store; draft, publish, unpublish, archive and delete for both.
Owner's decisions: delete only while unused (else archive); draft until published (a template's content frozen by a copy, a profile's from its workspace); stores keep their copy. *Deviations:* the recommended profile is now one of a template's draft details (no separate immediate form); a profile's delete clears recommendations in the database's trigger rather than refusing; *Update from its store* became *Start again from a store* (into the draft).
