# D891: Company facts from Brønnøysund and a daily watch for bankruptcy and liquidation

Date: 2026-11-24

**What the register says of the companies we know (24 November 2026, the owner: "build 1 and 2", while Leadfeeder's API is on its way).**
Brønnøysundregistrene cannot say who visited (it has no link from an address to a company), but it says a great deal of a company once its
organisation number is known, free and open (NLOD, no key): Enhetsregisteret (form, industry, headcount, founding day, place, website, and the
flags of bankruptcy, winding up, forced dissolution and deletion, with a feed of changes) and Regnskapsregisteret (the last years' accounts).

- **Where.** A store's business customer (`customer_companies`, Customers → Companies → a company), a store's own business on Kaizena Flow's
  store page (`/admin/platform/stores/{store}`: its legal name and number were loaded and never shown), and a line on each Norwegian visiting
  company (D890). One card, `RegisterFactsCard`: a red flag first (with "turn payment terms off" when the company buys on them), then what it is
  and its latest accounts (revenue with its change on the year before, operating result, profit, equity, a note on negative equity), and the
  source with the day it was checked.
- **Kept once, platform-wide.** `commerce.register_companies`, one row per number, written only by `src/server/register-facts.ts`. A page with
  one company reads the row and asks the register when there is none or it is over 30 days old (2.5 s of patience; it draws without the facts
  rather than wait); a list reads rows only. Only the number is ever sent, to the register's fixed host.
- **Never a person.** A sole trader's entry is a person's: its row keeps only that it is one (`status` `sole_trader`, a check in the table
  holds it), so it is never asked again and nothing of the person is stored; its card says so. The register's roles (board, manager) are not
  read.
- **The daily watch** (`watchRegister()`, `daily.register-watch` in the daily run): every Norwegian number we hold in one statement, new ones
  asked with their accounts, the feed of changes read for the companies kept (100 numbers a call, three days back so a missed run is covered),
  a changed company or one over 30 days old asked again, accounts over 30 days old asked again; at most 300 calls a run, and the first failure
  stops the run's asking. A newly flagged company (`flagged_at`, set when a flag first appears) that a store sells to on payment terms is
  emailed to that store's `payment_overdue` recipients (owners by default), once per company and flag (`sendCompanyFlagAlert()`).
- **Homer sees it without looking.** A store's Home lists business customers whose company is flagged and still on payment terms, or flagged
  in the last 30 days (`companies_flagged`, one more lateral in the Home's existing order-counts statement, so no extra round trip); one
  company opens at once. Kaizena Flow's home and morning briefing say when stores' own businesses are flagged (`business_flagged`).
- **Platform first and stores alike.** Kaizena Flow's customers are the stores' businesses (the platform's card and alert); its own store's
  business customers are a store's like any other.

Idle cost: per daily run, one statement for the numbers held and a register call for each new number, each change and each month-old company;
per page of one company, one read (and a register call when the facts are missing or a month old); none per store page or beacon.
