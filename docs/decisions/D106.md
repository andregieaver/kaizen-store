# D106: Every call to an AI model is recorded, and read back as usage for the platform and for each store owner

**Every call to an AI model is recorded, and read back as usage for the platform and for each store owner.** `commerce.ai_usage` holds one row per call (`recordUsage()` in `src/server/ai-usage.ts`, called by `metered()` around every model call in `src/server/ai.ts`: text, streamed text, embeddings, speech to text, text to speech, pictures; a live voice call is one row when it starts, its length added when the page ends it): the store it was for (null for Kaizen's own AI manager and chat agent),
the store's owner account (its first owner when the call was made), the signed-in person who asked where there was one, whose key paid (`platform`: Kaizen's, or `store`: the owner's own), provider, model, kind, what in Kaizen asked (`AI_FEATURES`: AI manager, chat agent, search, catalogue vectors, chat knowledge, media library, page studio, product writer, settings tests), and what it used: requests, failed requests, input and output tokens, characters spoken, audio bytes and seconds, pictures.
Tokens are the provider's own figures from its answer (`usage`, in either name providers use; streamed answers ask for it with `stream_options` from the providers known to take it); where a provider reports none they are counted at about four characters a token and the row is marked `estimated`, which the reports say. A call tried again after a model refused a setting is one row; a refused call is a row marked failed.
Every call gets its store from `aiFor(storeId, { feature, accountId })`, so a new call site names what it is for; connections made any other way record nothing. The platform's page `/admin/platform/ai/usage` shows the total, tokens per day, per provider and model, per store owner account, per store and per feature, each account and store opening to its own providers and models, with Kaizen's key and stores' own keys apart, for 7, 30, 90 days or 12 months.
An owner's page `/admin/account/usage` shows the same for the stores they own (a store's owners each see it; one store on request): the total for their account, per provider and model, per store, per feature. The AI manager answers usage questions from the same rows (`ai_usage` for owners, `platform_ai_usage` for platform admins, sums worked out in code). Rows are kept 400 days (the daily cron).

Date: 2026-09-29

Asked for by the owner: usage per provider and model for the platform in total, per store owner account and per store, and for owners in total and per store. Recording at the one place every call passes gives complete figures whoever calls; a row per call rather than daily sums keeps every cut of the figures possible (per feature, per person, per provider) and is cheap beside a model's answer.
The owner account is fixed when the call is made, so a store changing hands does not rewrite what was used. Money is not shown: prices differ by provider and model and change, so a figure Kaizen kept would soon be wrong; the reports give tokens, requests, characters, audio and pictures to multiply by a provider's own prices. Live voice calls report their length from the person's own page, which they could misreport, and their tokens are billed by the provider and not seen.
