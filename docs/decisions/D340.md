# D340: By the clock, and links to share

**By the clock, and links to share (wave 7 unit F, `docs/wave-7-storefront.md` section 4, F); built.** A page's, article's, header's or footer's draft is published at a set time and, with a way back, the content from before is put back at another (`commerce.page_schedules`, written only by `src/server/page-schedules.ts`); an installed theme goes live at a time and the theme before comes back at another (`commerce.theme_schedules`, `src/server/theme-schedules.ts`, through `switchToInstall()`).
Both run only in `runSchedules()` in the five-minute dispatcher, in the maker's name read again, never for a store that is not open or a page in a running A/B test; each leg is settled once by its rules trigger, a way back touches only a page or look still as the schedule left it and fails with its reason otherwise, and a schedule never spends money (a premium theme the store does not own is refused). A way back keeps the person's draft.
Preview links (`preview_links`, a hashed token per page or theme install, an expiry the owner picks up to 30 days, revocable, noindex, the draft only) are made from the builder's Share button; a link that finds nothing says nothing of why. The live look's saves keep versions (`look_versions`, the newest 50), taken back as unsaved changes.
