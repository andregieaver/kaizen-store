# D831: A save keeps its redraw; the store menu sends each address once

**A save keeps its redraw; the store menu sends each address once (bloat review step 2.28; 19 November 2026).** The store's menu
sends each address once: a section's tab names its section and the browser looks its addresses up in the sidebar's prefixes
(`useIsCurrent()`), so the menu fell from 17,307 to 14,757 characters with every feature on (held at 15,000;
`store-nav.test.ts`). `e2e/signed-in/admin-scripts.spec.ts` now records three figures for a product save, a builder save and a
shell redraw (the answer, its words, its menu), each held at 4 % (the shell's whole answer 15 %, as it grows with the pages before
it). Measured locally: a product save 127,821 bytes (words 66,222, menu 13,514), a builder draft save 1,087, a builder publish
236,942 (words 184,312), the Pages list redrawn 318,490 (words 66,679).

The brief's commit 3, a save answering under 20 KB of words, is not made. A save that does not redraw needs a cache profile that
serves stale content, which a publish, a price or stock must not do: in Next 16.4.6 `revalidateTag(tag, { expire: 0 })` marks the
path revalidated and redraws just as `updateTag` does. The editors' words as client modules leave about 47,720 bytes on a publish
and do not touch the product save. A shell redraw cannot reach 30 KB while the shared words (17,735) and the menu (13,514) are
read on every screen. The figures are held where they are measured, and a later change that shrinks the shared words or the menu
lowers them. Commits c7e9edba0, 909f704fa, 49bce5200, d131fa3a1.
