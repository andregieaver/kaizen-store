# D113: A store's working pages are page-builder pages too

**A store's working pages are page-builder pages too: cart, checkout, order confirmation, My account, sign-in, wishlists, a subscription, weekly deliveries and cookies.** Each is a role in `commerce.page_roles` (D112; `PAGE_ROLES` now also `cart`, `checkout`, `order`, `account`, `sign_in`, `wishlist`, `subscription`, `deliveries`, `cookies`, the check constraint widened by a migration) chosen on the Pages list, where the special pages are now grouped (`ROLE_GROUPS`: content pages, shopping,
customer account, information).
A working page holds one *shop component* (`StorePartBlock`, `type: "storePart"`, `part` one of `STORE_PARTS` in `src/lib/store-parts.ts`; in the palette of a store's pages, not articles, headers, footers or Kaizen's: `storePartProblem()`), drawn by `StorePartSection` from the same section components the standard pages are made of (`CartContents`, `Checkout`, `OrderDetails`, `AccountSection`/`SignInForm`, `WishlistSection`, `SubscriptionSection`, `DeliveriesSection`, `CookiesSection`, each moved
out of its route's `page.tsx` into a `*-section.tsx` beside it).
A component draws only on the route it belongs to: the route hands the page `GridPlace.route` (`StoreRoute`: the part, the address's own part such as an order's id or a subscription link's secret, and the query) through `RolePage` (`src/components/role-page.tsx`, the chosen page or the standard page as its children), and a component whose part is not the route's draws nothing, so a cart component on the About page does no harm and checkout never redirects from another page.
Components that depend on the shopper's state bring their own heading (order, account, wishlists, subscription, deliveries, cookies); the cart, checkout and sign-in starters have a heading block of their own (`starterPage()`).
Signed out, My account shows the sign-in page where one is chosen (`sign_in`, drawn at `/account`), else the account page's own component, which is the sign-in form; emailed sign-in links, company pages, order details under My account, downloads and unsubscribe links keep their standard pages. A page's own address leads to its route (permanent redirect) or, where the route has none (order, subscription, 404), answers 404; the pages are left out of the sitemap as D112's are.
The cart page is built this way but phones still get the slide-out cart (D64). Cookies keep the component so the list stays reachable. Not yet: category and tag pages, the country chooser, Kaizen's own site.

Asked for by the owner: the cart, checkout, order confirmation, My account, sign-in, wishlist, subscription, delivery and cookies pages should be builder pages too. One generic component with a part, rather than a block per page, and the route-scoped drawing keep the working pages' behaviour (redirects, per-request data, secrets in addresses) in one place each while the words, layout and extra content around them are the owner's.
