# D713: Ecosystem plan phase 2

**Ecosystem plan phase 2: one front door (17 November 2026, `docs/audits/ecosystem-2026-11.md` E2.1 to E2.8).** Eight steps so an owner starts the day in one place and finds what they need where they work. **E2.1:** Home and the morning briefing count, from one source (`src/server/attention.ts`), the queues they missed: disputes waiting for the store's answer (urgent, with the days left), the AI manager's decisions (owner), new leads and today's activities (while Deals is on) and blog comments
held for a check; every `attentionFor()` item has a `kind`. **E2.2:** ⌘K finds records as well as pages: orders (by number or exact email), customers, conversations, deals and products, five a kind, each by the person's permission and the store's features, erased or restricted people never found (`src/server/finder-records.ts`); a last row asks the AI manager; the owner's level has the finder, searching every store by that store's own rights. **E2.3:** the page and product editors show how the
page performs in one line for the last 30 days (views, dead or rage clicks, how far people scroll, speed, the running test; a product's orders) with links to its heatmap, recordings, speed and the AI manager, loaded after the editor (`src/server/page-signals.ts`, `analytics:read`); a heatmap's parts table offers *Edit this part* and *A/B test this part*. **E2.4:** the customer page says whether the person is on the email list and the texts list and which saved segments they are in
(`segmentsOfPerson()`). **E2.5:** the AI manager's CRM tools: `list_deals`, `get_deal`, `move_deal`, `list_leads`, `make_deal_from_lead`, `crm_today`, `log_activity`, `customer_timeline` (`src/server/crm-tools.ts`), behind Deals but the timeline, never giving the model what people typed but a lead's name; eval case `manager-lead-name`; API contract 1.37. **E2.6:** *Make a quote* on an open deal and *Make a draft order* on a customer start a draft with the customer's details, linked to the deal.
**E2.7:** a support request is carried out from the conversation or the Requests page: cancel, address and refund through the order page's own functions, under a two-minute claim, then marked done for the customer (`src/server/support-carry-out.ts`); a person's own Done or Decline waits for a running carry-out. **E2.8:** Leads has its own page under Customers (`/leads`), each request linked to where it is kept; a form's message is read from its responses (D705), so form leads count at last.
