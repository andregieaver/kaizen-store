# D847: The platform's Stores list is the store admin's list: search by name, owner or domain, plan and state filters, a pager that keeps them; the sandbox funnel moves to Requests

**Platform → Stores finds a store by its name or address, an owner's name or email, or one of its domains, narrows the list by the
store's state, its plan and its subscription, pages a hundred at a time keeping all of them, and lists only stores (code review step
2.10, finding FS9; 20 November 2026).** Before, it had a pager (bloat step 0.19, D818, D834) and nothing to find a store with, under
the page builder sandbox's funnel. `storeBillingPage()` (`src/server/billing.ts`, beside `listStoreBilling()`) reads a page in
D834's shape: the stores the list holds, closed ones too (D171), `count(*) over ()` in the same statement and an order that ends on
the store's id. Its search, lower-cased and cut at 100 characters, matches the store's name or address (slug), the name or email of
an owner (not of other members: the list says who owns a store) and the domains it serves (`store_domains`, pending or active) or
that Kaizena Flow registered for it (`domain_registrations` not moved away), with `%`, `_` and `\` as typed (`escapeLike()`). The
filters: the store's own state (open, suspended, closed); its plan, or none; its subscription as the rows say it (on a free trial,
paying, payment overdue, ending at the period's end, cancelled), read from `store_plans`, so a store billed through its account
(D748) is filtered by the account's plan. `storeListFacts()` gives the state chips' counts and the plans in one statement. Every
value is a bound parameter; no index is added (stores are hundreds, and each subquery reaches its table by `store_id`).

The page is the store admin's list (Homer and Lisa): one `ListSearch` form, the store's state as its chips with their counts (the
form's buttons, as Products' views, so a chip keeps the search; a state with no store is left out), the plan and the subscription
one step away under *Filters* in the same form (as Customers' tags), and `Pager` keeping all four. `src/lib/platform-stores.ts`
(`storeListFilter()`) is the one parser of the address: the last state named wins, a plan is an id or `none`, anything else is the
whole list. A search that finds nothing says "No store matches …" (or "these filters") in the kit's `EmptyState`, which offers no
step because *Show every store* sits beside the bar (D845); a page past the last opens the first. The Dream Factory stays in the
header and each store is still one row of two lines on a phone (D827). The search form is lifted (`z-10`): each part of an admin
page eases in as its own layer (`admin.css`), so the list covered the *Filters* panel. The funnel (how far the sandbox's visitors went, D457 S4)
moved beside its switch on Access requests, inside that switch's phone row; its Stores-only words went. `listStoreBilling()` keeps
the AI manager's tool and the account's billing page as they were; its paging and `countStoreBilling()` went with the page. Platform:
this step is the platform's (the store admin's lists are D834's); `docs/platform-parity.md` gains the row under *Built for both*.
Ratchet: `directLinks` 379 to 378. Bundle: Stores loads what platform Customers loads (635,339 bytes, no chunk of its own); Requests
gains none. Tests: `src/lib/platform-stores.test.ts`, `src/server/platform-stores.int.test.ts` (search by
name, address, owner and domain, each filter, closed stores, paging with the count) and `e2e/signed-in/platform-stores.spec.ts` (the
platform admin finds a store by name and owner email, a chip and a plan keep the search, no match, the funnel on Requests).
