# D86: A row or column with no background or a colour can blur what lies behind it, and its colour can be see-through

**A row or column with no background or a colour can blur what lies behind it, and its colour can be see-through.** A colour background takes an opacity (`Background.opacity`, solid when left out, drawn with `color-mix`), and rows and columns a `backdropBlur` in pixels (up to `BLUR_MAX`), drawn with `backdrop-filter`: frosted glass, such as a header over a picture. With a picture or video it is not offered and is cleared, as the part's own background covers what is behind.

Date: 2026-09-28

Asked for by the owner. A setting beside the background rather than a background of its own keeps "has a background" meaning what it did (the page's room around rows, a header lying over the page, D80). Blur is only visible through a see-through colour or none, so the colour's opacity comes with it.
