# D286: Premium themes

**Premium themes: a price per store, paid when the store goes live.** `docs/theme-prices.md` is the contract. A theme is free, or premium with a price in every currency stores are billed in, without VAT (`commerce.theme_prices`, set on the theme's page by `setThemePrices()`); a store pays once and keeps the theme with every preset and update.
Trying, previewing and customising are free; a store pays when the theme goes live and the store is open: chosen before opening it is owed and becomes due when the store opens (only the one live then), made live on an open store it is due at once, after a button that says the price.
The database makes every purchase (`commerce.theme_installs_charge()` after an install goes live) and refuses a premium theme on an open store unless the transaction carries the agreed price (`kaizen.theme_charge`), so no path skips the charge and nobody pays a price they were not shown; store templates and stores that are not open are never charged, a theme a platform admin applies is given, a theme's first price leaves the stores that have it free.
`chargeDueThemePurchases()` invoices on Kaizena Flow's Stripe account to the store's account (the plan's card when it pays by card, else an invoice due in 14 days, MVA for Norway), from `after()` and the five-minute job; the billing webhook marks it paid and the referrer earns (`theme_invoice:`). Prices show in the theme store (cards, page, `offers`), at sign-up and *Create a store*, on Design settings, the setup wizard's last step and Billing.

Date: 2026-10-19

The owner: "Let's add the ability to set pricing for themes in order for us to sell them. Free should still be an option. When a user chooses a Premium theme, they should not have to pay upfront. Payment should be withdrawn when the site/store goes live." Defaults chosen and told to the owner: one price per store, per currency, without VAT; charged through Kaizena Flow's own billing like the plan.
