# D749: The surface budget

**The surface budget (bloat review step 1.18; 17 November 2026).** What every store carries whether it uses it or not is counted and held in `src/lib/surface-budget.baseline.json` by `src/lib/surface-budget.scan.test.ts` (findings FT-2, DS-12).
With the owner's yes, the test refuses a new scheduled job, table, trigger or index on a hot table, navigation link or AI tool until its decision has an `Idle cost:` line (what it costs a store that does not use it: per request, per scheduled run, per cold start; none where none); writing the line and running `node scripts/surface-budget.mjs --raise D<n>` lifts the refusal and records the raise with its decision.
Pages (+40) and route handlers (+15) have room, as everyday work adds them; a fall is a note and `node scripts/surface-budget.mjs` lowers the baseline.
Start: dispatcher jobs 83, minute routes 9, pages 591, route handlers 287, tables 519 (20 platform), store links 115 (45 in Basic), platform links 55, store tools 294 (264 shop-only), the manager's own tools 26, feature switches 14; hot tables' indexes and triggers: orders 33/27, order_lines 8/5, visits 2/0, products 13/14, product_variants 6/7, customers 8/6, carts 11/1, cart_lines 5/0; a new store gets 8 rows (`src/db/commerce.test.ts`).
Twins kept on purpose are listed with their reason: the platform's four event engines (D465), order and Work invoices, payment terms and Work's unpaid invoices. Switches are for what an owner sees, not for cost: a feature switched off still costs what its jobs, tables and tools cost (`docs/store-features.md`). In review: a fall in the admin's repeats (step 1.17) or in a new store's rows is a note. Idle cost: none (a test and a JSON file; nothing runs in production).
