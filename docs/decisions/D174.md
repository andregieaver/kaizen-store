# D174: Orders edited after purchase, sent in parts, closed units, packing slips per parcel and pick lists

**Orders edited after purchase, sent in parts, closed units, packing slips per parcel and pick lists (wave 3, run 3).** `docs/wave-3-fulfilment.md` is the contract. Every new shipment names its lines and units (`shipment_lines`, written only by `markSent()`; no lines given = everything still to send; shipments from before are `legacy` and count as everything sent).
Units still to send are only `commerce.line_to_send()` / `toSend()`: quantity − shipped − withdrawn (withdrawal returns only, `withdrawn_quantity()`) − closed. An order is *Partly sent* and still `paid` while units are left, and `fulfilled` through `refresh_fulfilment()` when none are; `markDelivered()` needs it sent in full, so the 14-day receipt (D153) counts from the last parcel.
Each parcel has its own carrier, tracking and email listing its lines; the shopper's order pages list the parcels and what is still to come.
Units that will not be sent are closed explicitly (`commerce.unsent_closures`, append-only, under the order lock, never more than is to send): the admin refund's *These units were not sent* (`refundOrder({ notSent })`) takes restocked units off what is to send, a closed unit is never in a parcel, a slip or the pick list, and is never offered for withdrawal (`loadFacts()` counts it taken); a plain restock is a sent unit that came back.
Editing (`order_edits`, E1, E2 … per order; `src/server/order-edits.ts`): only a paid order with nothing sent, standard VAT, no subscription, booking, box, host, return, venue balance or closed unit, in an open store (`editBlock()`). Kept units keep their sold price and discounts (`splitLine()`); added goods are priced by `priceOrderEdit()` with `shown()` and `decideTax()` and equal `cartSummary()`.
Lines and amounts of a settled order change only inside the edit context (`kaizen.order_edit`; triggers refuse anything else). A lower total is claimed under the order lock and refunded through `refundOrder({ within: tx })` in the same transaction; a higher total applies only when the customer pays the link (`/account/change/{token}`, Stripe's hosted page; `applySession()` branches on `payments.order_edit_id`) or when staff record it paid outside Kaizen under D173's cash ceiling.
`refundOrder()` now splits any refund over the order's captured payments (`splitRefund()`). A late change payment is refunded in full and moves no bonus or affiliate credit (`payment_of_order()`); a change refund that fails later is shown as owed (`order.edit_refund_failed`, checkup `edit_refund_failed`). The order number never changes (D141).
Documents: an invoiced order's change issues an edit credit note and an additional invoice (`kind = 'order_edit'`), both referring to the original (`issue_edit_documents()`); later credits are pooled over the order's documents; a change's refund gets no credit note and is not a refund in analytics.
Slips print what is still to send (`packingSlipData()`, up to 100 orders, each in its language, no prices) or one parcel (`parcelSlipData()`); the pick list (`pickList()`, `/admin/{store}/orders/pick-list`) sums the selected orders' units to send by product, variant and SKU or lists them by order. Tracker: `checkout.order-editing-after-purchase` was merged into `orders.edit-an-order-after-placement` (README rule 8).
Packing slips and pick lists are Full; partial fulfilment and order editing stay partial until the consumer texts (change emails, pay page, parcel and receipt words, edit documents) are reviewed by a person, a change is paid in Stripe test mode and the staff screens are checked by hand; a carrier-booked partial parcel has no test yet.
