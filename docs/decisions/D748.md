# D748: Paid extensions, account plans and standalone products

**Paid extensions, account plans and standalone products (`docs/extensions.md`); a design, built in steps E1 to E11.** The owner's choices in an interview on 17 November 2026. Five parts become paid extensions: A/B testing, the AI support desk, email automation, Work (time, invoicing and the books: ledger, VAT return, SAF-T, bank statements, year-end) and deals and contracts (with the wider CRM, as far as e-commerce stays whole in the core).
Plans move from the store to the **account**, an organisation that owns the stores, the plan and the extensions, with a store limit and usage limits counted across it; at a limit the account moves up a plan; existing owners' stores become one account on the highest plan any of them had. A plan can include extensions at no cost; four (all but A/B testing) are also sold on their own, and a standalone account sees no website or store feature (its data in a hidden workspace store).
Until an account has an extension, its links, cards, tools and connections are absent; one quiet marker per screen and the AI manager (on a given fact, at most monthly, silent 90 days after a no) offer its trial. Each extension: a 14-day trial, 14 days' grace, then read-only (nothing faces customers, export kept); existing accounts get 60 days.
Prices are settings with list and 12-month founding prices, bundles beside single prices, never charging twice (`cheapestCover()`); the AI tunes prices as far as the owner of the prices allows (off, proposes, or within a range), the same feature for stores' product prices. Bulk marketing email through Amazon SES in Ireland, an own sending domain per account. A new `extensions` pricing block for Kaizena Flow's pages and the admin. The platform always has every extension.
Built in steps, each with its migration versions and the advisors' answer in `docs/extensions.md` 13: E1 (organisations), E2 (the catalogue and `extensionState()`), E3 first part (the gates at a store's level).
Idle cost: none per request; two daily reads, the extension trials ending within 30 days (`daily.extension-trials`, E5) and the founding prices ended (`daily.extension-founding`, E6) (so far eleven tables, one view, two platform pages, one trigger on a new account and one function call more in the cached store read; limits are read in the same one query).
