# D127: Whole page layouts are templates too, and any template can be previewed before it is switched on or used

**Whole page layouts are templates too, and any template can be previewed before it is switched on or used.** A page layout (`saved_parts.kind = 'page'`, content `PageLayout`: the kind of page it is for, its rows and the page's own CSS, `src/lib/page-layout.ts`) is saved from the builder toolbar (Save as template), listed under Saved, and shared, switched on, hidden and moderated exactly like a row, column or component (D125); never global, and none of the page's own settings (title, address,
search texts, categories, translations, a header's overlay) come with it. It is only for pages of its own kind (`layoutFits()`: a header's rows hold components no article has).
Using one asks whether to **replace** the page's rows or **add** after them (`applyPageLayout()`, fresh ids, unique custom ids, row and block caps; the layout's CSS is used when the page has none, or replaces the page's on replace only if the layout has some); the builder has no undo, and the dialog says so. **Preview** (`previewTemplate()`, `/admin/account/templates/s/{store}/{id}/preview`, chrome-free in the `(print)` group) draws a template as the store's own pages are (its theme, fonts and
CSS; header and footer with the store's details, a product layout with one of its products, an article under its heading) in an inert frame the builder shows at desktop, tablet or phone width; it is view-only (nothing copied, saved or audited; pictures are still the publisher's) and follows the same `visibleTo()` rule as using. Kaizen's own builder has none of the browsing or preview, its saved page layouts being the marketplace's.
