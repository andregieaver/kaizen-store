# D491: The receipt read by the model into the purchase

**The receipt read by the model into the purchase (B3 step 2, 7 November 2026, `src/lib/receipt-reading.ts`, `src/server/receipt-reading.ts`, `ReceiptFields`, `readReceiptAction()`).** When the owner chooses a photo of a receipt under a bank line (D490), the store's model that sees pictures (D163, `seeing()`, metered as `receipt_reading`) reads it, and the form fills: what it was (one of the purchase kinds), the VAT rate (the rate most of the receipt's VAT is at; none when it shows none; left as
it was when it shows a rate the books do not post), and the supplier's name as the entry's words. It says what it read (*Read from the receipt: Clas Ohlson AS, NOK 1,000.00.
Check what it was, then post.*), or, when the receipt's total is not the bank's amount, asks *Is it the right receipt?*; the owner changes what they like and presses *Post the purchase* as before. *Grounded:* the picture is data (`UNTRUSTED_TEXT`, the prompt built in `src/lib` and sent by its delegate), the answer is read by its shape and only into the closed choices, a name holding text addressed to an AI is dropped, and nothing the model read is posted: the amount is the bank's and its VAT
worked out in code (`entryOfPurchase()`); the total read is only compared. Nothing is kept or posted by the reading itself. A PDF or a HEIC photo is not sent (the provider's chat takes JPEG, PNG and WebP pictures): the owner chooses, as without AI, and so does a store with no AI that sees pictures.
The picture goes to the store's own AI provider, or Kaizena Flow's (its EU settings apply); no new vendor. *Not an agent:* one call that fills a form (as the alt texts, D89), not one of the agents the safety eval plays (`AgentKind`) or `promptVersions()` versions, so it needs no recorded eval run; the server test sends a photo through the real request and reads the answer. *Not yet:* a receipt with goods at two rates is posted at its main rate (a split comes with supplier invoices, B3 step 3).
