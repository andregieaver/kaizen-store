# D421: Start over and discard a Dream Factory store

**Start over and discard a Dream Factory store.** On the run's page, one step away (*Not what you hoped for?*) and only before the store opens: *Start over* takes the edited description (the old one filled in), sets the newest run aside and clears what it made (`startDreamOver()`, `src/server/dream-factory.ts`): an open draft discarded, the front page unset, the run's pages deleted with the redirects their deletion left (a store never opened has no links from outside to keep), its products
archived and its goals dropped, audited `store.dream_cleared`; then a new run starts. The brand, the looks in the library, the pictures, the research notes and the business details stay, and the trial is not given again. *Discard this store* closes the store as its owner closes it (`closeStore()`, the store's address typed to confirm), so it moves to *Closed stores* on Your stores.

Date: 2026-11-02

Asked for by the owner: an easy way to try again with an adjusted description, or to throw a store away. *Why in the same store:* the address, the trial, the business details and the pictures carry over, and a new store per try would fill Your stores. *Why closing and not deleting:* a store touches over 120 tables, orders and invoices must be kept and the audit log is append-only (`docs/store-closure.md`); a store that never sold anything holds little once closed.
