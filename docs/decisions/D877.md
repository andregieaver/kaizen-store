# D877: One structure for the platform's navigation: PLATFORM_SECTIONS, from which the tabs, the sidebars, the areas and the pag

**The platform's navigation is one structure, `PLATFORM_SECTIONS` in `src/lib/platform-nav.ts`, in the shape of `STORE_SECTIONS`, and
all that was assembled by hand is derived from it (code review step 1.18, finding FN9; 23 November 2026).** Gone: the four item arrays,
the hand-written tab list, the hub titles and intros, `PLATFORM_PAGE_AREAS` and the areas `platform-access.ts` rebuilt from the arrays.

A section has a key, label, icon, start, hub, intro (a hub's) and groups of items, each naming its permission area as D851 has it.
Two fields are the platform's own: `area`, for the section's own page (a hub, or Themes, Stores, Customers and Access requests, which
have no sidebar), equal to the item its tab opens where it opens one (a test); and `also`, addresses no item holds (the docs folder,
Website's). Derived: `platformTabs()` (counts by key; each tab names its `section`, as a store's, so its addresses are sent once),
`platformAreas()`, `platformNav()` for the layout, which lists no area, the two hub pages, and `platformNavAreas()`, which
`platformAreaOfPath()` reads. A page's section is the area the frame finds for its address; step 1.19 adds a `platformSectionOf()`
when the admin map takes its groups from here (only a test would read it now, and the unused-code budget holds that).

Orphans: after D873 only the AI manager (`/assistant`, its Autopilot and routes) was in no tab. As a store's, it is Home's
(`PLATFORM_HOME_PATHS`), keeping the owners' area it had by default (D510): the Home tile is marked on it, its header names no section.
No menu item was added. `platform-nav.test.ts` puts each of the 136 folders holding a page or a route in a section or Home.

Unchanged: the menu's tabs, items, order, labels, icons, Norwegian words and hub cards. `platformAreaOfPath()` and the role notice's
answer for each role were recorded for 1,564 addresses (782 page folders, map pages, menu and area addresses with a child and a
grandchild below each, relative and whole) before the change and all answered the same after. The move test moves each of the 57
sidebar pages to the next section of another area. Marks moved only on the AI manager (Home) and on addresses with no page: below the
docs folder (Website) and below the two hubs (none; a hub's tab marks itself alone, as a store's).

Bytes, against the commit before: the role notice finds the area in the browser (D851), so the structure goes with it: each of the
122 platform routes' first scripts grew 1,135 bytes; no other route changed (store Home 781,201, Pages 786,770, Settings 744,776). The
platform home's document fell 1,189 bytes, its tabs no longer sending their addresses. Render modes unchanged. Handing the notice its
areas from the layout would take the navigation's 9.2 KB off every platform route: not done here.
