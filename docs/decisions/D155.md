# D155: Custom grid items and a carousel with controls; the replicator makes grids and carousels from them

**Custom grid items and a carousel with controls; the replicator makes grids and carousels from them.** A content grid (D51) has a fourth source, `custom`: up to 60 items the owner writes, each with a picture, title, text, link, button label, date, badge, a plain-text price and up to three detail lines (`CustomGridItem`, `src/lib/page-content.ts`, `src/lib/custom-grid.ts`).
The price is only words: no `<Price>`, no VAT label, no cart, never a live price; a custom item never reaches structured data, the sitemap, llms.txt, search, feeds, recommendations, the knowledge base or the chat agent (`custom-grid-isolation.test.ts`; `blockText()` is empty for a custom grid).
Links are the menu link kinds by slug (a copied page carries no foreign ids) or safe web addresses; a picture is an https file in this project's Storage or a site path (`custom-picture.ts`), checked in the schema, when drawn, when a template is applied and when items are copied. Categories, tags, filters and sort do not apply to this source.
The editor has the item list (drag to order, duplicate, up and down), a *Copy current items* button that makes items from what a pages, articles or products grid shows now (prices left out, business-only or consumer-only products skipped, pictures that cannot be kept said), and notes on what custom items do not do. Item texts are translated and in the store worklist (detail lines by their own id, not their place), copies get fresh ids, a linked picture-only item is the link for keyboards.
The carousel (shared with testimonials) takes `carousel` settings: arrows, dots, snap, rewind (the next arrow goes back to the first tile, real tiles only) and an opt-in autoplay that never runs under reduced motion, stops for good on any interaction, holds while the pointer is over it or the tab is hidden, and always has a Pause button; without JavaScript the row scrolls.
The replicator (D150, `src/lib/replicate-grid.ts`, `replicate-watch.ts`) builds one grid block of custom items from repeated cards and from script sliders (Swiper, Slick, Splide and similar): cards must share a shape and size and every word and link of each card must fit an item, or the group stays as columns with the reason in the report; cloned slides are dropped, hidden slides read, a loop becomes rewind, arrows, dots and snap are read, autoplay is switched on only when two bounded captures
saw the track move; a grid that scores under 60 % is tried as columns in the same pass and kept only if columns win by more than 2 points; no word in an item is one that was not in the capture (property-tested, and 0 invented words in 1,084 words on 26 real pages). No migration. Not built: a page-studio pattern using custom grids, a browser test of the builder's own editor (e2e has no admin sign-in; the editor's state changes are unit-tested).
Known limits of the replicator, said in `docs/page-replicator.md`: cards inside one column of a side-by-side layout are not looked at (the largest gap: 24 of 25 sliders missed on one site), tablet columns are estimated (only 1440 and 390 px are captured), autoplay "not observed" means not seen in a few seconds, wrapped rows with a centred last row stay columns, and the grid-against-columns figures were measured before the last fixes.
