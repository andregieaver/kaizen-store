# D189: A column dropped between two columns goes between them

**A column dropped between two columns goes between them.** A column dragged onto a row outside any of its columns (the gap between two, the room before the first or after the last) went last in the row's last line, so a new column from the sidebar's Column tile could not be dropped in between.
The place is now the pointer's among the columns (`columnSlotAt()` in `src/lib/column-slot.ts`: the line the pointer is in, the nearest where it is between lines, and the columns whose middle is before it, counting from above where the row stacks), for the Column tile, a saved column and a column moved by its handle (which counts without itself in its own line), shown by the indicator on the column it goes before.

Date: 2026-10-10

Asked for by the owner. No migration. Checked in a real browser: the gaps of a row of one line and of a row of two lines, before the first and after the last, and a moved column.
