# D464: Deals and pipelines

**Deals and pipelines (S2 of `docs/own-books-and-sales.md`; `src/lib/deals.ts`, `src/server/deals.ts`, `DealBoard`, `/admin/{store}/deals`).** A deal exists before any quote ("met them at the fair"): a title, a person or a company of the store (or both), an owner on the team, a pipeline and a stage, an expected close and an estimate in the store's main currency.
It links to quotes, orders and Work assignments, one deal each; once one is linked, the deal's value is theirs before VAT (`dealValue()`: an order's total less VAT, a quote's order or its open price, an assignment's invoiced amount, else its fixed fee or hours at its rate), converted at the store's rates and naming a currency it cannot convert, never counting it as zero. **Won without a person**: the database wins the open deals linked to an order that becomes paid (or the order made from a
linked quote), to an assignment whose Work invoice is issued, or a link made to something already paid or invoiced (`deal_win()`, triggers on `orders`, `work_invoices` and `deal_links`); a closed deal is never won again, a copied order never wins, and each stage entered and each close is kept by a trigger (`deal_moves`), which also restarts the stage's clock. **Homer** gets one pipeline made on his first visit in his language (New, Talking, Quote sent; Won and Lost are a deal's status), a board
with a column per stage, each column's value and the value weighted by the stage's chance, deals dragged on or sent with their own *Move to*, Lost asking why; New deal from the board or from a person's or a company's page, with only what they might buy, who with and an estimate in view. **Lisa** gets several pipelines, stages ordered with a chance and the stage sent quotes go in, an owner and an expected close, the time a deal has sat in a stage.
Deals show on the person's and the company's page and their timeline (kind `deal`).
A merge moves a person's deals, a company with open deals is not deleted, erasure deletes a person's own deals and takes the person off a company's, and the export lists them under a new purpose of the register, `sales` (legitimate interest, marked for review). **Offered step by step**: the feature `sales` needs nothing else (a store selling its work without a shop keeps deals too) and is offered where the platform opened it to the store (`stores.sales_pilot`, set on the platform's page of the
store) or to all (`platform_settings.sales_open`); a store that switched it on keeps it. **Custom fields** (D118) can be on deals, for staff only as a customer's and an order's are: the group editor offers deals while Deals is on, a deal's page fills them in, its person's export lists them and they go with the deal.

`commerce.deal_move_via`). Migration `20261106162810_deal_quote_stage`.
