# D845: Empty screens with a next step: every empty admin list says what it is for and offers one thing to do, a filtered one Clear filters

**An empty admin screen or list is the kit's `EmptyState`: a title in plain words, a line only where it helps, and one next step; a search
or filter that matched nothing has its own title and *Clear filters* (code review step 2.9, finding FS8; 20 November 2026).** Before, 88
hand-written "nothing here" blocks in 70 files (`p-8 text-center` paragraphs, dashed and sunken boxes, cards holding one word) stated a
fact and stopped. Now: products (*Add your first product*), subscriptions (*Go to your products*), stays (*Add rooms and items*), the
store's custom fields (*Make a group for the store*), hosts (*Add a host*, to the form below), inventory (*Add a product*), the analytics
panels that need a setting (that setting) and every other list, whose header or nearby form already holds the step, so the box adds none:
two links of one name confuse people and tests. *Clear filters* (`kit.empty.clearFilters`, en and nb) is offered where the search bar has
no way back (content entries, the product catalogue, the platform's docs feedback); where it has one (*Clear*, *Show every list*, the
filter tabs) the box does not repeat it. A list in a card is `compact`; a phone's plain line (D820's rounds) is kept with `className`.
A person with no store gets who to ask (their email for a store's owner) and *Start a store* to the sign-up in their language, on the
control center and on `/admin/stores`. Platform: the same change on Kaizena Flow's pages (referrals, plan reminders, A/B tests, requests,
notices, apps, API, endpoints, workflows, domains, docs feedback and more), the owner's level and the host's area. Scan:
`adminFiles.emptyBlocks` in the ratchet (`scripts/lib/ratchet-count.mjs`, `handMadeEmptyBlocks()`: a centred box with `p-6`+, a thin
dashed box with muted words, a card holding only a word of nothing; alerts, chart pictures and picture frames aside) is 4 in 3 files,
each named in `KEPT_EMPTY_BLOCKS`: the page builder's two drop targets, the two-page editor's empty half, and the page finder, whose
line would put about 0.9 KB of `EmptyState` into every admin route's first scripts (as D829 kept Sign out). `adminFiles.emptyLines`
(a word of nothing alone in a bare line) fell from 287 in 231 files to 185 in 144 and only falls: the panels' lines move as their
screens are touched. Existing words are the titles, as D818 did; new ones only for steps and lines. Copies: near lines 22,466 to
22,386, twins 36 as before; `directLinks` 381 to 379. Against a build of the commit before, no route outside the admin changed; the
three editors whose client part draws the box (Work's invoice, a field group, support replies) rose about 0.7 KB, 149 admin routes 27
to 155 bytes, and 12 fell (Products draws the catalogue's no-match box on the server and hands it in). Tests: `ratchet.scan.test.ts` (the shapes and the kept list),
`ui.test.ts`, `stores-list.test.ts`, the returns queue and subscriptions views', and `e2e/signed-in/empty-states.spec.ts` (Products
none and no match, the store's fields, the platform's docs feedback). Left: the 185 lines, and the no-access page in a browser (no
account without a store can sign in).
