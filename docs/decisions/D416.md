# D416: The briefing's one-press actions

**The briefing's one-press actions (asked for with D415).** Every finding of the briefing, by email and on the home page, names one thing to do and links to a page with it prepared (`alertAction()`, `checkupAction()`, `src/lib/briefing-actions.ts`): stock running out opens *Prepare reorder*, the purchase order form filled in from the reorder rule (`reorderDraft()`: 30 days' sales, 30 days to cover, 7 for delivery, the variant's cost price, the supplier last used); customers coming back opens
*Create campaign*, a broadcast from the thank-you template; a change in the figures opens *Ask the AI manager*, the home page with `?ask={alert}` opening the AI manager with the alert's words and figures in its box (`AskOnArrival`); the rest keep their page. Nothing is saved or sent until the person presses the page's own button.

Date: 2026-11-01

*Why a page and not a one-press action in the email:* mail programs and their scanners follow links on their own, so a link that saved a purchase order or sent a campaign would do so unasked; one press on the page that opens is the same effort and is the person's. *Why these three:* they are the findings with one obvious next step; the others already link to the page that acts on them.
