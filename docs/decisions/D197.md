# D197: Table colours and section dividers; negative margins

**Table colours and section dividers; negative margins.** A table's header row and shaded rows each take a text and a background colour (only the ones chosen are drawn). A divider, with an optional title, can be put above any body row to group long tables (the ― button beside a row); dividers follow their rows when rows are added or removed and are translatable. A margin in the settings modals may be negative (−240 to 240 px), to pull a part over its neighbour; padding stays 0 or more.
