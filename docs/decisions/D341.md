# D341: Blogs in depth

**Blogs in depth (wave 7 unit G, `docs/wave-7-storefront.md` section 4, G); built.** A store has several blogs (`commerce.blogs`; the first stays at `/blog`, the others at `/blogs/{handle}`, each with its own title per language, list or chosen page, categories, sitemap and llms.txt entries); an article names its blog and author (`blogId`, `authorId`, kept by `savePage()` only when they are the store's), and asked for under another blog's address it moves to its own (308).
Authors (`blog_authors`: name, a library picture, a short text, a checked link) show on the article and as a `Person` in its JSON-LD. Each blog has an Atom feed at `{blog}/feed.atom` (published articles only, cached with the store's pages).
Comments (`blog_comments`) are posted to `/api/blog/comments` with no cookie or storage, limited per visitor and store by a keyed hash, checked for spam in code, held for moderation unless the blog shows clean ones at once, shown as plain text only when approved, and are personal data (export, erasure, 30-day retention).
Pages and articles can be scheduled (`page_schedules`, written only by `src/server/page-schedules.ts`, published by `runSchedules()` through `savePage()` in the five-minute dispatcher, in the scheduler's name read again, never into a running A/B test). The AI manager reads blogs and comment counts (never a comment's words) and proposes `moderate_blog_comment` and `schedule_article` (gate `public`).

Date: 2026-10-27

*Why:* `storefront.blog-multiple-blogs-authors-comments-rss` (with `customers.blog-and-content-marketing` merged into it). The feed is `{blog}/feed.atom` rather than `/blog/{blog}.atom`, as one dynamic segment cannot serve both a page and a file. `blogs` is reserved in code only, so a store with a page at that address does not break the migration. Other blogs and authors are not copied with a store; their articles fall to the copy's first blog. A comment's words never reach a model.
Migration versions: `20261027161338_blogs`, `20261027161403_blogs_sql` (applied by CI in the `release` job on 27 October 2026 at 18:39 UTC; advisors checked after: no warning, INFO only (RLS on with no policy, new indexes not used yet)).
