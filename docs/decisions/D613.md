# D613: A checkout question can be a number, a date, an email address or a phone number

**A checkout question can be a number, a date, an email address or a phone number (step 2 of task 4 of `docs/checkout-fields-tasks.md`; 14 November 2026).** Four kinds join the six (`CHECKOUT_FIELD_KINDS`).
A `number` is whole, with an optional lowest and highest value; a `date` has an optional earliest and latest day counted from the store's own today (0 to 730 days; `storeToday()`, the store's time zone), which the cart's date input gets as `min` and `max`; an `email` is checked for its shape; a `phone` is read by `e164Phone()` in the basket's country when typed without its code and kept in international form.
`checkout_fields.bounds` (`{ min?, max? }`, only for `number` and `date`: `boundsProblems()` and the database's check) is kept by a change without bounds while the kind stays, and dropped when the kind changes, so a number's values never become a date's days. The answers are kept as text answers (every reader already shows them).
One given but not of its shape, or outside its bounds, stops the order even when the question is optional (nothing wrong is kept), checked by `cleanAnswers()` when the cart keeps the answers and again by `placeOrder()`, so a date that was tomorrow's earliest yesterday is refused today. The cart draws the browser's own number, date, email and phone inputs; its problem sentence names them in four languages.
The editor asks a number's lowest and highest, or a date's earliest and latest in days from today, right under the question. Admin API contract 1.21 (`bounds`). The AI manager's tool keeps its kinds, as D601. Tests: `checkout-rules.test.ts`, `checkout-presets.test.ts`, `checkout-rules.int.test.ts`, `commerce.test.ts`.
