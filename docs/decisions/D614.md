# D614: The 100-day challenge's console

**The 100-day challenge's console (`docs/100-day-challenge.md` H2; 15 November 2026, `/admin/platform/challenges`, `src/server/challenge-console.ts`, `src/lib/challenge-console.ts`).** Platform → Settings → *100-day challenge* lists every store with a challenge set, running first, then in rehearsal, upcoming and ended.
Each card says its phase and day (Day 1 to 100), the dates, whether the owner paused it, what holds it up (the stall watch's own stalls, A6, in the words of its email), what waits on the owner and how long the oldest has waited (calls the autopilot kept, escalated customers, customers queued after a fault), the autopilot's AI cost this calendar month against the challenge's budget (and the calls no price covers), what people did since challenge mode came on by class (refunds, escalated answers,
platform fixes, anything else, A5; each of the last listed), and the three ratios over Day 1 to today or Day 100 (contribution margin, profit per krone of marketing, revenue per visitor, from the analytics' own totals as `profit_overview` reads them; one that cannot be measured says so, never 0), with net sales, marketing and orders beside them.
An owner of the platform sets a challenge on a store by its address and three dates, the budgets one step away with the plan's defaults, changes one from its card and ends it (`saveChallenge()`, `endChallenge()`, through `platformAction()` in the settings area); every role reads. Setting the challenge is audited as a platform fix, and so now is any of the platform's own audit actions on a store (`platform.…`, such as its approval: `classOfAction()`), whoever pressed.
Store-only by nature: Kaizena Flow's own store is not run by the autopilot for the challenge, and the console is the platform's already. No migration.
