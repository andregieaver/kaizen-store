# D904: The octopus for the whole of Kaizena Flow, and Projects as an extension that stands alone

**Every part of Kaizena Flow stands alone, and each part added makes the whole more coherent and more intelligent (the owner, 26 November
2026).** In the owner's words: an octopus losing its arms in reverse. The more arms you take away, the less coherent and intelligent it
becomes; so each arm added makes it more so. It was the paid extensions' rule (D748, `docs/extensions.md` 1 and 3.1); it now holds for all
of Kaizena Flow and is weighed in every major architectural decision (`docs/platform.md` P9):

- An arm works on its own: an account can have only it (a standalone account's workspace, D748 E7), and it needs nothing of another arm.
- Two arms that meet do more together, and that only lights up when both are there (the pairs of `docs/extensions.md` 3.1).
- An arm taken away leaves the others whole: the system knows less, nothing breaks, nothing is left pointing at it.
- The AI manager reaches every arm the account has, and only those, so it grows smarter with each arm.

**Projects becomes a paid extension that stands alone**, which replaces D903's first answer (a free store feature on every plan). An
account subscribes to it as to the others (its own trial, grace, read-only and price, a setting the owner sets); an account with only
Projects uses it in its workspace with no store; and with other arms it does more: a won deal starts a project from a template (Deals,
P6.3); time and assignments live on tasks (Work, P6.1); a support conversation becomes a task (Support desk); an order, a return or a
review can make a task (the shop); the AI manager plans and works tasks (P5). Built in the steps of `docs/projects.md` P8.

**Its price (the owner, 26 November 2026):** €25 a month, €19 founding, five people included and €4 a person beyond; included in Grow,
Advanced and Plus, and in the Everything bundle. Built as P8.1.

Idle cost: none for the principle. Projects as an extension adds one call of `commerce.extension_on()` per store in Home's control center
and per due task in the five-minute repeat job, which stays one statement on its partial index. Its three AI manager tools (P8.3c) are sent
only to stores with Projects; elsewhere `load_tools` names them (30 characters).
