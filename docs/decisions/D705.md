# D705: Forms as their own records

**Forms as their own records: an editor, kept responses, the sign-up as a form, and form templates (`docs/forms.md`; F1 to F5 built 17 November 2026).** A form becomes a row of `commerce.forms` (a store's, or Kaizena Flow's with no store), of kind `message` or `signup` (the newsletter sign-up), made, edited, archived and deleted under Website → Forms (and Platform → Forms), and a page's new `form` component points at one by id and keeps only its look; the old `emailForm` and `newsletter`
components move to forms by a migration (F2). Responses are kept in `commerce.form_responses` for the form's `keep_months` (1 to 36, 12 unless chosen), listed and exported by people who may read customers, and in the person's export and erasure (D162) from the first step; the emails and the support inbox (D93, D382) stay. Forms share one question engine with the checkout's questions (`src/lib/questions.ts`, out of `checkout-rules.ts`) and add conditions on earlier answers (`showWhen`).
Templates (`commerce.form_templates`): Kaizena Flow's own to start from, and the owner's own made from any form, shared as D125's (private, the owner's stores, the marketplace) and hidden by a platform admin; using one copies it, and a template never changes a form already made. A form on a page cannot be deleted, only archived.
