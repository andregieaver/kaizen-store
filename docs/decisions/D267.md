# D267: Signature themes

**Signature themes: themes made from scratch, each a world with its own demo store (`docs/signature-themes.md` steps 1 and 2).** A launch theme may be a *signature* theme (`LaunchTheme.signature`): its own header, footer, product page and stylesheet in its snapshot, and a content pack of its own whose home and about pages are designed for it (`ContentPack.design`, `PACK_DESIGNS`) and whose pictures are the theme's art (`ContentPack.art`), drawn by code from a seed in Chromium
(`scripts/theme-art.mjs`, recipes in `scripts/theme-art/`), kept as WebP in `public/demo/themes/{key}/` and placed into the pack store's library once by `syncContentPacks()` (so nothing waits for the picture model).
`signatureCheck()` (`src/lib/signature-themes.ts`) holds what code can tell of the bar the owner set (unique, artistic, beautiful, converting): its own layouts and CSS, search and the cart in the header, the title, price, gallery and buy part in the product page's first row, the footer the law asks for, art for every picture with alt text, and no claim `findClaims()` stops in any of its words or its pages'.
Both syncs dress a signature theme's demo store in its theme once both exist (`dressSignaturePacks()`: applied the first time, updated in place after, so nothing piles up in its library) and make it the store template's recommended look. A path written in a store's page, header, footer or product layout (`/products`, `/category/planets`, `/about`) is the store's own: `PageRowView` draws it under the market shown (`withStoreLinks()`, `src/lib/store-links.ts`), as the builder already promised.
The first theme is **Orbit** (space: a drifting star field, Unbounded over Manrope, starlight gold, glass cards, a header over a planet's sunrise, a glowing product page) with its demo store *Far Light* (eight framed night-sky prints in three collections) and the presets *Nebula* and *Mars*.
