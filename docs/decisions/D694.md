# D694: D1 step 1

**D1 step 1: the AI reads what each Meta campaign brings and makes paused copies to test (`docs/100-day-challenge.md` D1; 16 November 2026, `adResults()`, `copyAd()` in `src/server/ad-accounts.ts`, `campaignResults()` in `src/lib/ad-budgets.ts`, `src/server/ad-budget-tools.ts`, the Ads page).** The AI could move a budget (D585) but could not see what a campaign brought, nor try a variation.
`adResults()` reads each campaign's spend, impressions, clicks, purchases and their value for the last 7 or 30 days or this month, as Meta attributes them; Meta names one purchase several ways at once, so `campaignResults()` takes the first of `PURCHASE_ACTIONS` the answer holds, never their sum, and a figure it cannot read gives no result rather than a guess; the return on spend is worked out in code.
`copyAd()` makes a paused copy (`status_option=PAUSED`, deep) of a campaign or ad set, only one of the account's own (checked against its lists first), audited as `marketing.ad_copied`. `list_ad_budgets` takes an optional `results` period and then answers them too (one tool fewer for a turn's budget), and a new tool, `copy_ad` (gate `spend`, area money, owner only); a paused copy spends nothing, so money still moves only through `set_ad_budget` under the owner's limit and largest raise.
The Ads page shows the last 7 days per campaign, folded under the budgets (Lisa's step). Meta's own words never reach the model: campaign names are the owner's, marked as data, and a refusal is a code. Tests: `ad-budgets.test.ts`, `ad-accounts.int.test.ts` (a purchase counted once under two names, a copy of another account's object refused, the copy's form).
