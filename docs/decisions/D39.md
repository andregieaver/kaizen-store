# D39: A store's admin has a fixed header with its main sections, and a sidebar with its settings in groups

**A store's admin has a fixed header with its main sections, and a sidebar with its settings in groups.** The header stays at the top and slides away while scrolling down, back when scrolling up, like the storefront's. It shows Kaizen, the store's name, View store and the account's links, and the main sections as tabs: Overview, Orders, Subscriptions, Products, Coupons, Wishlists.
The sidebar groups the rest: Sales (Customers, Cart reminders, Shipping, Payments), Store (SEO & Reach, Header and footer) and Account (Billing, Team). The page being viewed, or one inside its section, is marked in both. On phones the tabs scroll sideways, and a menu button slides out every page, in the same groups, with the account's links. Pages are named as in the navigation: discount codes are coupons in the admin, the Plan page is Billing, Staff is Team and Search is SEO & Reach.
Addresses are unchanged.

Date: 2026-09-25

Asked for by the owner, who placed Customers and Cart reminders under Sales. The rest of the admin (platform, account, store list) keeps its simple header. Shoppers still see discount codes as "rabattkode"; only the admin says coupon.
