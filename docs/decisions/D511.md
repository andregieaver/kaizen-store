# D511: Kaizen Life shows Work from Kaizena Flow, once the accounts are linked

**Kaizen Life shows Work from Kaizena Flow, once the accounts are linked (the Flow side, 9 November 2026, `docs/life-work-summary.md`, `src/server/kaizen-life-accounts.ts`, `src/server/kaizen-life-doors.ts`, `src/lib/kaizen-life-jwt.ts`, `src/lib/work-summary.ts`, `src/server/work-summary.ts`, `/admin/sign-in/kaizen-life`).** The owner: Work now lives in Kaizena Flow, so Kaizen Life drops its own Work (its personal Finances stay) and shows instead a summary of the person's Work across their
stores, with links back; information and navigation between the two must be intuitive, relevant and seamless. Someone who uses only Kaizen Life sees no Work at all; as soon as the person has accounts on both, they are linked by themselves and the summary appears (until then Life's `/app/work` invites them to sign up to Kaizena Flow). One company runs both apps (the owner, 9 November 2026), so linking by a verified email needs no extra consent; it is told to the person and undone in either app.
Built here: **the link** (`commerce.kaizen_life_accounts`, one live link per account and per Life user, one row per pair so an undone pair is never linked again by itself, only by the person's own sign-in with the other app; made by a sign-in with Kaizen Life, after any sign-in or confirmed self-serve sign-up when Life has the same verified email, or when Life asks for its user who verified the address there and the account has signed in here; Life keeps a copy written in the same call, a link
it did not take is taken back; one email when linked, *Kaizen Life ↗* in the account menu, *Unlink* on Connected apps and assistants); **the channel** (server to server, both ways, ES256-signed requests bound to the door, the body's hash and 60 seconds, each `jti` taken once; keys `KAIZEN_LIFE_LINK_PRIVATE_KEY` and `KAIZEN_LIFE_LINK_PUBLIC_KEY`, without which nothing links; Flow's doors `/api/kaizen-life/v1/{link,unlink,status,work-summary}`, Life's
`/api/kaizena-flow/v1/{lookup,link,unlink}`); **the summary** (`workSummaryOf()`: what needs the person, urgent first, money per currency in integer minor units, each store's figures, a client named only on a line about one, every link through the one door from Life; also the owner MCP's `work_summary`, WP13's, over the caller's stores, scope `settings:read`); **the door** (`/admin/sign-in/kaizen-life?next=…`: straight through when signed in, the second step when held, else Sign in with Kaizen
Life with `next` kept; a linked account is admitted through Kaizen Life whatever its role). Under `/admin/sign-in`, not `/admin/from`, because `from` is not a reserved store address. A session that is gone now keeps the page asked for on the way to sign in (`signInFor()` in `SessionRecovery`; a page's own `requireAccount()` still sends to the plain sign-in page, as the server does not know the path).
Life's side is built in Kaizen Life (its copy `kaizena_flow_links`, its doors, the invitation, the Today card and the summary page; `docs/work-moves-to-kaizena-flow.md` there), at kaizenalife.com, `KAIZEN_LIFE_URL`'s default. Not built yet: the one run at launch for those already on both, and skipping Flow's confirmation email for a sign-up that comes verified from Kaizen Life. Nothing happens in production until both keys are set and `KAIZEN_LIFE_SSO` is on.
Kaizena Flow's own store is covered like any store the person belongs to.
