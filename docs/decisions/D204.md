# D204: Brands

**Brands: logos with names, as a grid or a carousel.** A component of uploaded brand logos (`BrandsBlock`, `src/components/brands-view.tsx`, `brands-carousel.tsx`, `src/lib/brands.ts`) with an optional name under each. Logos in a row (in view, in a carousel) are set per screen size through `at.*.columns` (five, four on Medium and two on Small until set, `brandColumns()`).
A carousel either keeps moving (CSS only; seconds a logo takes to pass, 1 to 30) or steps a logo at a time (every 1 to 15 seconds); both pause under the pointer or focus, stop for visitors who prefer less motion and have a Pause button. The tallest a logo may be is a setting (16 to 300 px). The step field is `movement`, since `motion` is a part's entrance effect. Also: D203's table box now fits inside the pop-up's panel, so the header stays fixed at all times.
