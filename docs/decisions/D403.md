# D403: Invoices and credit notes as EN 16931 XML

**Invoices and credit notes as EN 16931 XML (wave 13 unit C, `docs/wave-13-loyalty-rules-einvoices.md` C): built.** `invoiceUbl()` and `creditNoteUbl()` (`src/lib/einvoice.ts`, pure) write a document's frozen snapshot (D159) as UBL 2.1 with Peppol BIS Billing 3.0's customisation: the lines at their net after their own discounts (a unit price with up to six decimals so quantity times price rounds back to the net), shipping as a document-level charge, the VAT breakdown from the snapshot's buckets
by UNCL5305 category (S, Z, E, AE, and O for a seller not registered, with VATEX reasons), the VAT in the seller's currency as `TaxCurrencyCode` where it differs, what was paid before as the prepaid amount and terms' due date; a credit note is one line per VAT bucket referring to its invoice (BG-3). Electronic addresses are the organisation number under its country's EAS (0192 Norway, 0007 Sweden, 0184 Denmark), else the email (`EM`).
Nothing is worked out again: a document whose figures would not add up as BR-CO-10 and 13 to 16 ask, or that lacks a seller or buyer country, or a seller VAT number where VAT was charged, is refused with the reason (422), never sent half right.
Staff fetch it beside the PDF (`/admin/{store}/invoices/{id}/xml`, `…/credit-notes/{id}/xml`, `orders:read`, `documentUbl()` in `src/server/einvoices.ts`), apps through the Admin API (`GET /invoices/{id}/ubl`, `/credit-notes/{id}/ubl`, scope `orders:read`). Not built: sending through a Peppol access point, and a check against the official schematron (the rules are held by `einvoice.test.ts` and `einvoices.int.test.ts` on real issued documents). No migration.
