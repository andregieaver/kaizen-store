# D625: Challenge stores protected from deploys

**Challenge stores protected from deploys (`docs/100-day-challenge.md` F5; 15 November 2026, `src/lib/challenge-pin.ts`, `src/server/challenge-pin.ts`, `src/server/challenge-smoke.ts`, `/api/health/smoke`, `scripts/await-deploy.mjs`, `docs/runbooks/challenge-rollback.md`).** **The pin:** what a challenge store's AI runs on (each agent's prompt version, the tools' version and the text model, its own or the platform's) is pinned on the challenge when it is set (`pinChallenge()`, from the console's
save; the watch pins one not pinned yet). **The watch:** the dispatcher's job `challengeVersions` (`watchChallengeVersions()`) compares every five minutes what each store in challenge mode runs on with what it saw last, and logs each change on the challenge with its time (`versionLog`, the newest 200); the console shows the pin and the last five changes, each a platform fix.
It records, it does not enforce: a deploy that changes a prompt reaches the store, and the log is what the referee reads (the plan's rule, bug fixes only during the run, stays the team's). **The smoke:** after every deploy `await-deploy.mjs` asks production for the challenge stores' pages (`/api/health/smoke`: each store's front page and cart in its main market, full https addresses, at most 40) and requests them with `SMOKE_PAGES`; one that does not answer 200 fails the job.
The route is public, as those pages are, and reaches nothing of the AI (`challenge-smoke.ts` is its own module). Not done: a test checkout in the smoke (it would make a payment session on a real store), and paging (F3). Changing the challenge's dates keeps the pin and its log, as it keeps the original's periods. Store-only by nature. No migration.
