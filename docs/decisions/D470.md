# D470: The console's plan analytics, C2 of the plan

**The console's plan analytics, C2 of the plan (6 November 2026, `docs/platform-parity-plan.md` section 6, `src/lib/plan-analytics.ts`, `src/server/plan-analytics.ts`, `commerce.plan_moves`, `/admin/platform/plans/analytics`).** Platform → Plans → Analytics shows what Kaizena Flow's business is: what the stores' running plans bring in every month per currency (a yearly price as a twelfth; active and past due count, a trial not yet), how many stores pay, are late, are trying a plan or are
leaving at their period's end, each plan's stores and revenue, and, for 30, 90 or 365 days, the way from an application to a paying store (asked, approved, opened, paying) and how the monthly revenue moved. Live and test payments are kept apart (production has only test ones yet).
The history is new: `plan_moves_track()` on `store_billing` keeps a move each time what a store pays a month changes (started, upgraded, downgraded, paused while unpaid, resumed, cancelled; a change of currency as one cancelled and one started), and the migration kept every running plan as started that day. Only customers' stores count.

Date: 2026-11-06

The plan's console track, C2 first with C1 (the owner, D466). *Why a history of our own:* Stripe's events are not kept here past 30 days (D465), and a month's movement needs before and after. *Not yet:* targets, alert emails and the morning briefing on the platform home (the store's D348).
