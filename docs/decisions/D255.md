# D255: The theme store's quick fixes

**The theme store's quick fixes (the theme showcase plan, before its phase 1).** The public theme preview (D176) no longer carries the admin's banner: a visitor sees one quiet line saying it is a preview that cannot be pressed, and the frames on a theme's page ask for the store alone (`designPreviewPath(..., embed)`, `embed=1`); platform admins keep their banner (`as=admin`).
A store selling to both kinds of buyer is previewed as a consumer store (no Private / Business switch or business popup over a look that is about the theme). The theme list shows the themes first under one bar of three small menus (`FilterMenu`: kind of store, style, what it includes) that name the choice, with the count and *Clear filters*; the menus are links in `<details>` sharing a name (one open at a time) and keyed by their choice, so choosing closes one.
A launch theme's card shows a photograph of its public preview on the demo store (`launchShot()`: `public/demo/themes/{key}-v{version}.webp`, a 4:3 crop of the top at 1280 by 960 made 800 by 600) where the admin gave the theme no picture, its colours where neither; `theme-store.test.ts` fails for a version without its photograph, so a launch theme changed in code is photographed again.
The demo template's front page, left from a test (a dark-worded photo and typing), became a plain demo home (light words over the darkened photo, the products, a line about the store), data only and only while the test text was there. The signed-in specs install their stand-in fonts with a stylesheet: an empty one 404s, and React then draws a streamed part of the page again in the browser (two previews, the frame's page hidden).

Date: 2026-10-16

"Go with your recommendations, start with the quick fixes": the plan's audit found the preview carrying admin text and a buyer switch, a broken demo page in every preview, filters above the themes and swatches where Shopify shows the theme.
