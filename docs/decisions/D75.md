# D75: Query understanding

**Query understanding (Phase 2, S3): the store's text model reads a search that may hold more than words ("lampe under 500 kr", "billigste notatbok", "hytte for en helg", "på lager") as filters, which drive ordinary SQL; shoppers see what it was read as and can search for their exact words instead.** Only searches of three or more words, or with a digit, a currency or a word about price, order or stock (`worthUnderstanding()`), are sent, so short searches wait for no model.
The model is given the store's categories and tags and the market's currency, and answers with JSON (`understandingMessages()`); `cleanFilters()` keeps only the store's own category and tag slugs, known kinds (`goods`, `appointment`, `stay`, `rental`) and orders (`relevance`, `priceLow`, `priceHigh`, `newest`), sane prices turned into minor units in code (a reversed range put right), and, as words to search for, only words the shopper typed.
The filters (`filterClause()` in `src/server/search.ts`) keep both keyword and meaning candidates to a category or any below it, a tag, a kind, a variant priced in the range in the market (with VAT, as prices are kept), and in stock (goods with physical stock at an active place, or digital; bookings count as available); a search that is only filters lists what meets them, newest first, and an order asked for sorts the results.
Answers are cached per store, text model, market and search, and dropped when the store's categories or tags change (`understandQuery()`); the model is asked at the same time as the search's vector, and a failure or 2.5 seconds without an answer runs the search as typed. The page shows the reading in Kaizen's words (category names, prices formatted in code) with a link to search for exactly what was typed (`exact=1`), and the log keeps the filters (`search_queries.filters`).
The eval (`src/lib/query-eval.ts`): 25 searches in Norwegian, Swedish, Danish and English with each reading a good answer may give; any filter a case does not name must be empty, so invented filters fail; a model passes at 90 %, run with "Check query understanding" on Platform → AI or a store's own AI page.
