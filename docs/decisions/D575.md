# D575: The deploy job requests two pages, and Kaizena Flow's own store is prerendered

**The deploy job requests two pages, and Kaizena Flow's own store is prerendered (bloat review step 0.3; 12 November 2026, `scripts/await-deploy.mjs`, `src/server/shop.ts`, `e2e/house-site.spec.ts`).** Once production runs the commit, `await-deploy.mjs` requests the pages of `SMOKE_PAGES` (a repository variable, paths or full addresses; by default `/,/s/demo/no`: Kaizena Flow's home and the template store's Norwegian front page), one after the other and each twice, with no cookie or token; it
logs each page's status, bytes and `x-vercel-cache`/`x-nextjs-cache`, tries once more after a minute when one fails, and fails the job when a page does not answer 200. `prerenderedShops()` also returns the markets of Kaizena Flow's own store while its site is served from it (`Store.onSite`, D484), read through `getOpenStore()` and guarded so a build never fails on it, so kaizenaflow.com's pages are built with each deploy.
Only CI's `hosts` job has the switch on (`e2e/hosts-seed.mjs`); the e2e and signed-in jobs test the platform's own pages.
`house-site.spec.ts` asks for the store's market at `/<market>` on Kaizena Flow's own host, as a visitor reaches it: `/s/kaizenaflow/<market>` moves to the store's own host and back, which production follows (308, 307, 200) but `pnpm start` on `*.localhost` cannot (its redirect comes back relative and loops). *Why:* the first visitor after each deploy paid the cold draw, and a page that could not load was found by a person (SD-3, step 1 of its fix; warming after opening and the deploy cadence
stay with SD-3).
