# D454: Basic and Advanced views, an icon beside every page, and the admin usability audit

**Basic and Advanced views, an icon beside every page, and the admin usability audit (4 November 2026, `docs/audits/admin-usability-2026-11.md`).** The admin had grown to 104 pages in a store's nine sections (33 in Settings alone), each sidebar a flat list of words, and the rail showed bare icons until a person widened it.
An audit against Nielsen's ten heuristics and what is known of working memory, choice time and recognition found the navigation, not the pages, to be the main maze, and planned the rest in phases.
Built now: a store's admin shows each section's everyday pages in **Basic**, the default for every account, and every page in **Advanced** (`src/lib/admin-view.ts`; `accounts.admin_view`, migration `20261105063932_admin_view`; the switch in the top bar, the phone's menu and Your account; `AdminViewProvider` so the sidebar and the pages of cards follow at once). 40 of the 104 pages are Basic (`StoreItem.basic`, or the page a section's tab leads to); Basic hides links, never pages, and the page
being viewed stays in its sidebar. While Basic is on, each sidebar's foot, each page of cards and Home say how many pages Advanced adds and that it is where to look when something is missing, with one press to switch (`BasicViewHint`).
Every page in a sidebar and on a page of cards, at all three levels, has an icon (`NavIcon`, lucide at the sections' stroke); Settings' cog, drawn as a sun, is lucide's; and the rail shows its names on screens 1280 px and wider until the person narrows it (`RAIL_NAMES_WIDE`). The platform and the owner's level have no views: their people run them daily.
Rejected: hiding pages from Basic outright (a link from Home or the AI manager would 404), a view per store (a person is Homer or Lisa, not their store), and Basic as a separate set of pages (one set of pages, two ways of listing them). Migration version (applied by CI #996, 5 November 2026 09:30 UTC, regenerated after Tripletex's so it runs after them): `20261105063932_admin_view`. Advisors checked after: no new finding.
