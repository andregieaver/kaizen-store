# D887: Flows start when someone enters a segment (email plan M2.3)

Date: 2026-11-24

**A segment starts a flow (24 November 2026, email plan M2.3).** Flows started on events, dates and birthdays, but not on the store's own
saved segments, the groups an owner already uses for broadcasts and discounts (*VIP customers*, *Bought twice, not this month*).

- The trigger `segment.entered` names one of the store's saved segments (`flows.trigger_segment_id`, no foreign key: a deleted segment
  holds nobody, as a broadcast's does). A person on the list who comes into it starts the flow.
- Segments are matched in code over the people the segments' one reader reads (`segmentPeople()`, `matches()`), never stored as a list, so
  the trigger reads the segment once an hour per switched-on flow (`enterSegments()`, in the every-minute flows turn, `segment_checked_at`)
  and keeps who it found (`flow_segment_members`): someone not there at the last read starts the flow (`segment:{subscriber}:{read}`), and
  someone who left is forgotten, so coming back starts it again.
- The first read after the flow is switched on (or saved) only notes who is in already, so switching one on never emails a whole segment.
  A segment that is gone, whose rules no longer read, or that the reader stops at its cap enters nobody and is read again later.
- `flow_segment_members` is personal data with the subscriber: a working list of ids, never shown, exported or copied, deleted with the
  subscriber and with the flow.
- The test's estimate counts those in the segment now who joined the list in the last 30 days, since who will come in cannot be foreseen.

Idle cost: nothing while no segment flow is on; one read of its flows a minute while any flow is on, and one read of a segment's people per
segment flow an hour.
