# D81: Kaizen and every store can have an AI chat agent on their pages, typed or spoken, that answers only about the site from its published pages, articles, products and a knowledge base of the owner's documents, and opens pages for the visitor as it talks

**Kaizen and every store can have an AI chat agent on their pages, typed or spoken, that answers only about the site from its published pages, articles, products and a knowledge base of the owner's documents, and opens pages for the visitor as it talks.** The owner gives it a name, an occupation, a picture, a greeting per language, guidance on tone, a daily message limit and whether visitors may talk (`commerce.chat_agents`, one per site; `/admin/{store}/chat`, `/admin/platform/chat`).
It uses the site's AI (D73): a text model with tools (`chatWithTools()`), and for voice a speech-to-text model, a text-to-speech model and a voice (new columns on `ai_providers`); a recording is written down, answered like a typed message and read out (push to talk, `/api/chat/voice`), not a live audio session.
The tools are the site's own reads: product search (D72, D74) and one product's variants, prices and stock, passages from the knowledge (`knowledge_chunks`: the site's published pages and articles in each language, cut again within five minutes of publishing, and the knowledge base's documents, pasted or read from text, Markdown, PDF or Word files, found by keyword and meaning merged by reciprocal rank), the site's facts (contact details, countries, shipping), and navigate, which only opens the
site's own pages that exist (a product, page, article, category or tag by its address, a search, the cart, account, wishlist).
The rules (`systemPrompt()`) keep it to the site, state products, prices, stock and policies only as the tools gave them, and put the owner's guidance after the rules; its answer is shown as plain text after `cleanReply()` (no Markdown or web addresses, sentences with green, urgency or best-price claims dropped, D76), products it found are drawn as cards by code with the site's own prices (`VatAmount`, B2B), and the page it opened is opened by the widget (`router.push`).
The widget is labelled AI assistant and says answers may be wrong and not to share personal data. Conversations are kept only in the visitor's tab (`kaizen_chat` in sessionStorage, necessary) and sent with each message; nothing is stored on the server but counts: 30 messages per visitor per ten minutes (a daily-changing hash of the address) and the site's daily limit (`chat_usage`, pruned after two days). Requests from other sites are refused.

Date: 2026-09-28

Asked for by the owner. Tools over the site's own reads keep the model grounded (the conventions' AI rule) and let the same code answer for any store; drawing products and opening pages in code means the model never states a price or makes up an address. Push to talk with the site's configured models keeps voice on the same provider choice, key and EU settings as everything else (D73) and works with any OpenAI-compatible API, where a realtime audio session would tie the chat to one provider.
Keeping conversations in the visitor's tab keeps no personal data on the server. The AI Act asks that people are told they talk to an AI.
