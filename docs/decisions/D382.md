# D382: Contact messages in the support inbox

**Contact messages in the support inbox (wave 11 unit C, `docs/wave-11-core-and-psi.md` 2.C): built.** A store's email form sends its message into the store's support desk as a conversation of source `form` (`formToInbox()`, `src/server/support-forms.ts`, from `submitForm()`): the form's first email answer as its contact and its name answer as the contact's name, both as typed and never proof, the answers as customer messages through `support_add_message()` (cut at whole words into the inbox's
length, at most eight parts; a message cut further is emailed in full as well), the subject, the page it was sent from and the store's market in the visitor's language. People answer it as any conversation and their answers go back by email from the desk (`sendReplyEmails()`, as a conversation by email, D332), a reply continuing it while receiving is set up.
The desk's `form_messages` (Support → Settings, a store's desk only): `inbox_and_email` (default: the form's recipients are still emailed), `inbox`, or `email` (as before). The inbox's context shows *A customer has this address: not verified* with a link to that customer, only to people who may read customers (`addressCustomer`); nothing is tied by it.
Export and erasure take such a conversation by the person's own proven addresses, as conversations by email. **Course changes.** (1) A form's message reaches the inbox only while the desk is on (AI first or people): the desk's state machine (the database's and `nextState()`) has a desk that is off take no person's claim, and its transcripts go a day after the end, so with support off the form emails its recipients as before; the database refuses a form's conversation on a desk that is off or on
Kaizena Flow's. (2) There is no *AI reads form messages*: a form's conversation is people only on every desk. The database starts it in the queue whatever the mode, never lets it go to the AI (`support_no_ai`) and skips its summary; tone is never scored, and the brief, the silent partner, translating its messages and the AI manager's `get_support_conversation` (which gives no words for it) never send its words to a model; support reports leave it out of what the AI did (`byForm`).
