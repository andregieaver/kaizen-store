# D45: A column moves with everything in it, into any row; and a page shows only its rows

**A column moves with everything in it, into any row; and a page shows only its rows.** A column is dragged by its handle into another row, beside one of its columns (a line shows which side) or onto the row to go last. The row it joins divides its width evenly among its columns (two columns and a new one make three thirds); the row it left does the same with one fewer, and goes if that was its only column. A row holding six columns takes no more.
Within its own row a column still just changes place. The page's title and picture are no longer shown on the page or the canvas: the title stays the page's heading for screen readers and search engines (and its browser tab and search result), and the picture stays the one shared on social sites and shown in lists.

Date: 2026-09-25

Asked for by the owner, who will add title and picture as components later. Pages published before show their rows only from now on.
