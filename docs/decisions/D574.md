# D574: A daily reading of production's statistics

**A daily reading of production's statistics (bloat review step 0.2; 12 November 2026, `src/server/production-reading.ts`, `commerce.platform_readings`).** Once a day the daily route's own job `daily.production-reading` (tracked on its own, so a failed reading shows on the platform home and never fails the route) writes three kinds of row for the UTC day: `table`, one per commerce table, with rows, bytes, seq_scan, seq_tup_read, idx_scan, inserted, updated and deleted from `pg_stat_user_tables`;
`schema`, one per schema, with bytes and tables; and `statement`, the app's own role's 40 costliest and 40 most-called statements from `pg_stat_statements` (at most 80, each marked time, calls or both), their text as Postgres keeps it, values already replaced by $1, $2, cut at 2,000 characters. A day already read is left as it is; rows go after 90 days. The statements are read inside a savepoint, so a database without the view (CI, local) still gets the table and schema rows.
No person, store or account (`NOT_PERSONAL`); a platform-only table with no store twin by design: it measures the whole database, not a store. No screen, flag or briefing line until step 1.13. *Why:* every later step of the bloat review needs a measured 'before' from production, not from one day's guess. Migration versions: `20261112121342_platform_readings`, `20261112121344_platform_readings_rules` (applied by CI's `release` job 12 November 2026 14:18 UTC; production then deployed `325b547`).
Advisors after the deploy: security, only the INFO 'RLS enabled, no policy' every private table has; performance, nothing on the new table.
