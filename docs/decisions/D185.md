# D185: Pages nest under other pages

**Pages nest under other pages.** A page's address (`pages.slug`) may be its parents' and its own part joined by `/` (`projects/project-a`, at most four parts, each at most 80 characters, 240 in all; only pages, not articles, layouts or versions).
The editor's *Parent page* choice (`PageOwnerContext.parents`) and Address field edit the parent and the own part; `savePage()` moves the pages nested under a page with it when its address changes (their `slug` and their drafts' and published copies' `slug`, a redirect for each published one by the existing trigger).
Routes: `[slug]/[...sub]` beside `[slug]` in a store's market and Kaizen's own pages; the platform's reserved addresses and the store's working routes apply to the first part (`pages_slug_not_reserved`, `pages_store_slug_not_reserved` use `split_part`). Menu page links take nested addresses. A page deleted leaves its children at their addresses; an A/B test of a nested page is not served (the version route is one level).
A content grid of pages can show only the pages directly under a parent (`source.parent`, or `@this`).
