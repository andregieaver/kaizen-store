# D415: Profit per order

**Profit per order (asked for by the owner on 1 November 2026, after the comparison with what Shopify merchants struggle with).** Each paid order's profit by the Finance page's own definitions for one order, in its own currency (`orderProfit()`, `src/lib/order-profit.ts`; reads in `src/server/order-profit.ts`): sales without VAT (gift cards left out), less refunds (shared by revenue, change settlements left out), the cost of the goods as frozen at the sale, the payment fee (Stripe's own where
every Stripe payment has it, else the store's estimate), Kaizena Flow's fee, sending (each parcel at its own label's price where kept, else the store's estimate per parcel) and a lost dispute; before ads and fixed costs. A goods line without a cost makes it unknown, and the order page says how many and links to the products.
Shown on the order page (a card, one figure first, how it adds up one step away) to members who may read analytics; on the Finance page as *Profit by order* (the period's orders, their average, how many lost money, how many wait on cost prices, the ten least profitable); and the briefing gains the period's contribution profit.
A booked label's cost is now kept on its parcel (`shipments.label_cost_minor`, `label_cost_carrier`): without VAT in the order's currency, from the carrier's own price for the service booked (Bring's and Helthjem's rates, Porterbuddy's window price), written once by `recordLabelCost()` and never changed (`shipments_guard()`).

Date: 2026-11-01

*Why unknown rather than partial:* a profit that leaves out a missing cost price reads as good news it is not. *Why the order's currency:* the order page shows the order as it was paid; the Finance page converts for its average. *Why after the booking:* a booking is never refused or undone for want of its price.
