# D223: The admin's pages move to the style's type, shapes and components, starting with what they share

**The admin's pages move to the style's type, shapes and components, starting with what they share (`docs/admin-style.md` section 8, step 7).** A codemod over `src/app/admin` and `src/components/admin` changed only exact patterns: the page title (`text-2xl font-semibold` on an `h1`, 205 places) to the display size, which is fluid on a phone (28 to 40 px); every `font-semibold`/`font-bold` to `font-medium` (hierarchy by size and colour); a section's card
(`rounded-lg border border-border bg-background p-5`, `p-6`, `p-8`) to `rounded-card` while smaller boxes inside cards keep their corners; and the main action (`bg-foreground … text-background`) and bordered secondary buttons with `rounded-md` to pills. Left alone, because their look is a store's or a document's: the builder's stand-ins, the theme editor's preview, previews of a store's pages, packing slips and the print routes.
`admin-type.scan.test.ts` refuses a heavy weight, an old page title or a square main action in the admin from now on. The shared overview parts (`Stat`, `Attention`, `Section`) are drawn with the components (KPI tiles; a card of rows whose urgent ones say so in words too), so every page built from them follows; the store's Home (titled with the store's name) and the control center use `PageHeader`, `Card`, `Chip` and `List`, and the store cards use `Chip` in place of fixed amber. No migration.
