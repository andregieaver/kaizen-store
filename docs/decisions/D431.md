# D431: Request a quote, prices on request, and a product question

**Request a quote, prices on request, and a product question.** A store chooses who may ask for a quote under Settings, Orders (`order_settings.quote_requests`: *Off* by default, *For everyone*, *For business customers only*: the store sells only to businesses, the shopper chose Business (D63), or a signed-in customer of a company account or customer group, D108).
A product can be *Price on request* (`products.price_on_request`, a tick in its editor over its prices; goods bought once): no shopper or outside reader sees its price (the product page, cards, grid tiles, wishlist, the chat's tools and cards, marketing emails, `llms.txt`; JSON-LD offers without price or shipping; feeds, the WordPress plugin, the storefront API, the agents' catalogue and a company's catalogue leave it out), it is never put in a cart (`sellableQuantity()`, the cart's and the
checkout's reads) and its buy part offers only *Request a quote*, whatever the setting; its prices stay as staff's guide. Where quotes are offered, the buy part shows *Request a quote* beside Add to cart (or instead of it, chosen on the product layout's buy part) and the cart *Request a quote for these items* (goods sent by post and bought once; the lines read from the cart itself).
The form (name, email always required, phone, company, note; a signed-in customer's own details filled in; a hidden field, the time taken and the forms' hourly limit per visitor, 100 requests a store a day) makes a draft order of `source = 'quote'` (D173) in the shopper's market view, its lines at the list price and a product priced on request at 0 waiting for its price (`price_requested`); staff who get new orders are emailed and pushed (D238, D361), the shopper gets a receipt in their language
that holds nothing they typed (the address is anyone's, so the store's mail must not carry a stranger's words to it). In Orders, Drafts a *Quote requests* filter and a *Quote requested* chip; the draft editor shows the shopper's note, blocks a send until every price is typed, and *Send quote* emails the draft's pay link worded as a quote; paying makes the order as a draft does. Staff can decline with a short reply (emailed, kept on the draft, never sent, gone 30 days later).
The *Product question* is one product layout part with a Kind: *Question* (to the support inbox as a conversation of source `form` with the product, chosen variant and link attached, D382; emailed to the part's copy addresses, else the store's contact address, while support is off) or *Request a quote* (the same request, with the part's own fields); the owner sets the heading, words over it, button, thank-you message and, one step away, which fields are asked or required (the email always is)
and the copy's addresses. No AI manager tool for quotes yet: a new tool needs an eval case and a recorded run, left for a later change.
