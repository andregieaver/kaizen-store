# D285: Themes is a section of its own, and the theme store's pages are built in the page builder

**Themes is a section of its own, and the theme store's pages are built in the page builder.** **The rail.** Themes moved out of Stores into a rail section of its own beneath Website (`platformTabs()`, a palette icon); Stores and Themes have no sidebar, since each was one page. **The theme store's pages.** The list at `/themes` and the page every theme has at `/themes/{theme}` are two more places of Kaizena Flow's pages (D143's `platform_page_roles`, roles `themes` and `theme`,
`THEME_STORE_ROLES`), built from the Themes page's *The theme store's pages* card and never among the other pages: the Pages list leaves them out, its Special pages offer only the first three (`SPECIAL_ROLES`), and either editor address leads to the other (`/admin/platform/themes/pages/{id}`, `themeStorePageHref()`). *Build it in the page builder* makes a draft in its place from a starter with the standard page's words (`createThemeStorePage()`); the standard page shows until it is published,
and after Unpublish or Delete again.
The pages hold a new Kaizena Flow-only component, **Theme store** (`ThemeStoreBlock`, `THEME_STORE_PARTS`, `src/lib/theme-store-parts.ts`): the themes with their filters on any of Kaizena Flow's pages, and on a theme's page its preview, See it live, highlights, pictures, what you get and details, each drawn by `ThemeStoreSection` for the theme the address names (`GridPlace.themeSlug`, given only by the route of a theme's page) and nothing anywhere else; a part's heading and line are the page's
words, drawn only when the part has something for the theme. The standard page draws the same parts (`src/components/theme-page-parts.tsx`), and the theme's structured data stays the route's. **The full-screen preview** has *Previous* and *Next* (`previewNeighbours()`, through `themePreviewNeighbours()` cached per theme): the themes before and after in the theme store's order that have something to preview, going round, opening on their own look with the same page and screen.
