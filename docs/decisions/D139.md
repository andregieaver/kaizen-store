# D139: Product recommendations

**Product recommendations** (`docs/recommendations.md`): upsells, cross-sells and complements picked for each shopper, shown in a product grid that recommends (page builder → content grid) on product pages, the All products page, articles and any page, and used by the chat agent through a `recommend_products` tool.
Candidates come from the store's own data (the owner's pairings, co-purchase, vectors, shared categories and tags, searches, the page's words, sales); hard rules in SQL and code keep out anything not for sale, already bought (account, email or this device's carts; bookings excepted), in the cart or hidden; kinds are decided in code.
The store's AI may only reorder the best candidates and its reasons are checked; the line under a product is a fixed sentence with a title the store holds, never the model's words. What the tab remembers (views, searches, clicks) is in session storage only (`kaizen_rec`, listed as necessary: to confirm per country); the cart, wishlist, account and device orders are read from the request's cookies.
Per store: on or off, AI on or off, a monthly token cap (counted from `ai_usage`), an upsell ceiling, a share of tabs held on the plain ranking, and goes-with / never-with / hide rules. Impressions, clicks, add-to-cart and the revenue of the orders that follow are recorded per ranking so the AI's can be compared with the plain one.
