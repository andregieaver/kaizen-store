# D229: The setup wizard is drawn in the `focus` layout within the store's level

**The setup wizard is drawn in the `focus` layout within the store's level (`docs/admin-style.md` section 8, step 13).** `AdminFrame` takes `focus` (`pattern`, a regular expression's source of the addresses drawn so, `back` and `title`): on those pages it draws no rail, no sidebar, no menu button and no level actions (the AI manager), only a top bar with the way back and what is being done beside the account menu, and the page in one column of 760 px (`AdminMain`'s `focus`,
`data-admin-layout="focus"`). As with `fullWidth`, the choice is made from the address in the browser (`ByPath`, `src/components/admin/by-path.tsx`), because a level's layout stays mounted across its pages. The store's layout asks it for `^/admin/{store}/setup` with *Leave setup* back to the store's Home and *Setting up {store}* as the title.
`SetupFrame` is drawn in the components: the step count as an eyebrow, the steps' bars as pills, the step's title in `text-display`, *Back* a quiet button with a chevron and *Skip for now* a secondary one; it no longer repeats the store's name. `admin-rail.test.ts` holds the focus frame and that the store's other pages keep the rail. No migration.
