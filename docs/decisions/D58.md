# D58: Cookie consent per site, from what the site sets

**Cookie consent per site, from what the site sets.** Kaizen and every store have their own consent (`consent_{storeId}` / `consent_kaizen`, a year) for four categories: necessary (always on), preferences, statistics and marketing. Only necessary cookies need no consent, so a site that sets nothing else shows no banner, only a Cookies page linked from the footer (`/cookies`, `/s/{store}/{market}/cookies`) listing what it sets and why.
Owners can add Google Analytics 4, Google Tag Manager and Meta Pixel (`stores.tracking`, `platform_settings.tracking`, under Cookies and tracking); these load only after consent, with Google Consent Mode v2 defaults denied, and turning one on makes the banner appear by itself.
The banner offers Reject all and Accept all equally, plus a choice per category; the choice can be changed from the Cookies page, withdrawing deletes the cookies it covered, and visitors are asked again when the categories in use change (the version). Each choice is logged anonymously (`commerce.consents`: random visitor id, choices, version, store, time) and deleted after 12 months by a nightly job.
A real browser scans each site (`/api/cron/cookie-scan`, every minute from Supabase's scheduler: an owner's Scan now first, else each open site weekly), in headless Chromium (`@sparticuz/chromium` on Vercel), opening up to six of its pages first without a choice and then with everything allowed, and records every cookie and storage item, first- and third-party (`commerce.cookie_scans`, kept 90 days plus each site's latest).
What it finds that Kaizen knows, or the owner describes (`commerce.cookie_notes`), goes on the cookie page, and an optional category found brings the banner; the admin flags optional items set before a choice and items nobody has described yet. Cookie texts need the same human review as the other legal texts.
