# D422: The Dream Factory in two stages

**The Dream Factory in two stages: the look first, then the rest.** Stage 1 (the brief, the owner's yes, research, setup and the brand, then the first three products and the website) ends on *Your look*: the owner sees the real front page drawn in the store's live theme on the run's page (`DreamFrontPage`, the builder's own drawing, in place of the sketch) and asks for changes, with *Other colours*, *Other fonts*, *Another layout* or their own words, each one turn of the AI limited to the design
and front-page tools (`LOOK_TOOLS`: `design_look`, `make_theme_live`, `get_page`, `edit_page`, `publish_page`, with `get_brand` and `list_themes`), at most `LOOK_ROUNDS` (10), until they say *This is it* (`approveLook()`).
Stage 2 then asks for the business details the description did not give (`detailsQuestions()`; stage 1 asks only for the name, what is sold and the countries), makes the rest of the products in the agreed look, writes the legal pages, and goes on to payments, plan, rehearsal, opening and goals. The demo store's products are archived before the first product is made, so the front page shows only the store's own.
After the website publishes, the brand step's own look (colours, fonts, styles) is laid over the live look the draft made live, keeping its header, footer and menus (`wearBrandLook()`). A test run's look is agreed by the drill.
