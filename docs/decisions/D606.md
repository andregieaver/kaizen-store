# D606: Goals of profit, and the autopilot sees money

**Goals of profit, and the autopilot sees money (`docs/100-day-challenge.md` E2, E3; 14 November 2026, `src/lib/autopilot-goals.ts`, `goalFigures()`, `src/server/profit-tools.ts`, `src/lib/profit-figures.ts`).** Four targets join `GOAL_TARGETS` for a store: `contribution_30d` (contribution profit of the last 30 store days), `margin_30d` (contribution per 100 of net sales), `revenue_since` and `contribution_since` (added up from a first day, the challenge's Day 1; *Counting starts on …* before
it). Each is read with the analytics' own definition (`derive()`: net sales less the cost of goods, payment and platform fees, shipping, marketing and lost disputes), and is *not measured* with the reason while too few goods sold have a cost (under 30 % of sales), or an order's currency has no rate, never 0.
The new read tool `profit_overview` (`analytics:read`, ungated, in the goal review's and events' read tools) gives a period's (7 or 30 days, this month, last month, or since a day) net sales, each cost, gross and contribution profit, contribution margin, profit per krone of marketing (contribution before marketing per krone spent: the challenge's second ratio) and revenue and contribution per visitor, with the month's forecast for 30 days or this month, written out in code.
While a store is in the challenge, every turn the autopilot runs there is told `CHALLENGE_LINES`: it runs the store alone, never makes a question step, judges plans by contribution profit and keeps refunds for the owner. Tests: `autopilot-goals.test.ts`, `profit-goals.int.test.ts`. **The agents' eval must be recorded again** on Platform → AI once this is deployed (the tools' version changed; `agent-eval-replay.int.test.ts` fails until the recording is committed).
