# D909: Guide: the admin shown around a little at a time

Date: 2026-11-26

**Guide shows a person around the admin where they already are, a little at a time (26 November 2026, the owner: "Build it", from the
canvas "Onboarding Guide Wizard").** No long tour on day one: Guide waits for the person to reach a place, says one thing about it, and
keeps the rest in its panel. The rule: `docs/rules/admin/platform.md`, *Guide*.

- **Three ways to speak up, each with a budget.** A spotlight (the page dimmed around one place) only for the welcome on Home, three
  steps, at most one a day. A beacon (a dot after a section's page title, its card opened only when pressed) on the first visits to a core
  section, fading after three visits not pressed. A hint inside an empty page, part of the page and in no budget. "Later" twice and that
  kind stops; with no level chosen Guide is Balanced for two weeks and Quiet after; the person can choose Quiet, Balanced or Hands-on and
  switch each kind off.
- **Never in the way.** Not while typing, with unsaved work, in the first moments on a page, with another dialog open, in a focus task or a
  workspace tool; a phone gets hints only. Whatever was not said waits in the panel at the rail's foot (the phone's menu), every place one
  press away.
- **Its places are the menu's.** Each level's sections as the person's menu offers them, so a new section is a place with nothing to add.
- **For the platform too** (the owner, 6 November 2026): the platform admin gets the same Guide with its own sections, and so does the
  owner's level, the control center (27 November 2026: its stores, inbox, books, work and account; its welcome is the rail, for owners).
  A sandbox store (`/try`) has none.
- **Its record is the person's.** One row per person and level (`commerce.guide_progress`), keys of places and moments only, deleted with
  the account's erasure and with the store, never copied with a store; the person's choice is on their account.

- **Its own route, never in the screen.** Guide's record, places and words come from one GET of `/api/guide` once a screen is
  drawn (which also marks found the place it is in), and its events go there by POST, never as server actions, which Next queues with the
  router's own moves. A screen's document carries three props; its first scripts carry only the rail's button and the hints, the rest
  is a chunk loaded after.

Idle cost: none per store. Per admin screen, nothing in the layout: one GET of Guide's route once it is drawn (one statement, two when the
place is found for the first time), and a POST of one transaction (three statements) when a moment is shown or answered, a few per person in
their first weeks and next to none after. Its runtime's chunk loads once per visit. No job, no email.
