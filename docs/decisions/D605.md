# D605: The margin floor

**The margin floor: the autopilot never prices or discounts a product under the owner's lowest margin without a yes (`docs/100-day-challenge.md` D5; 14 November 2026, `src/lib/margin-floor.ts`, `marginAfterPrices()` and `marginAfterOffer()` in `src/server/product-tools.ts`).** `budgets.marginFloorPercent` (the owner's *Lowest margin*, empty for none, on the Autopilot page's Settings) and the challenge's own (`marginFloorPercent`, 10 % by default) hold `set_prices`, `create_discount` and
`create_campaign`: a call that would leave any product a margin under the floor, measured on the price without the market's VAT for the product's category against the variant's unit cost in the main currency (`toMainOne()`), waits for a yes with the margin in words. A price change measures only the prices it changes; an offer measures every active product with a cost at its current prices less the offer's depth, whatever the offer's own reach (the strictest reading).
Free shipping takes nothing off a price and passes. A margin that cannot be measured (no product with a cost, a price with no exchange rate) asks, so a store without costs never passes the floor by accident. The catalogue is read only when a floor is set. Tests: `margin-floor.test.ts`, `autopilot-challenge.test.ts`, `margin-floor.int.test.ts`.

Date: 2026-11-14

Unit 2 of the platform's preparation for the 100-day challenge: before it, nothing stopped the autopilot pricing below cost. Platform: Kaizena Flow sells plans, not goods with a unit cost, so its own autopilot has no floor.
