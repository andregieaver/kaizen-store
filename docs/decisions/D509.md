# D509: Kaizena Flow's own payouts, and what each carried

**Kaizena Flow's own payouts, and what each carried (the next of D508, 9 November 2026, `commerce.platform_payout_lines`, `src/lib/platform-payouts.ts`, `src/server/platform-payouts.ts`, *Payouts* on Platform → Plans → Analytics and a page for each).** The platform's twin of a store's Payouts (D290), closing the parity row *Payouts*.
Kaizena Flow's own Stripe payouts are now read whether or not books are named, in each mode it has keys for (the dispatcher's `platformBooks` job over the last 40 days; *Read from Stripe* for the year), every status kept up; once one is paid, its balance transactions are read once (at most 2,000, as a store's) and kept as its lines, each payment, refund or dispute tied to the sale it was for by its payment intent (Stripe pays an invoice through one; `invoicePayments.list` by the intent names the
invoice, and an invoice the webhook missed is kept as a sale then). A sale's own line also says what it settled in the payout's currency and Stripe's fee, kept on the sale where Stripe had not said it. The payout's fee is its lines' fees, and D508 now posts a payout only once its lines are read (a manual payout, which has none, at once), so the fee in the books is the real one.
Whether a payout adds up is the store's rule (`reconcilePayout()`): the lines' net against the payout, and money for a sale tied to one. Plans → Analytics lists the year's payouts (live; test folded away): the day it arrives, its status, what reached the bank, Stripe's fees, how many sales it carried and whether it adds up; each opens a page with its lines, the sale and the store each was for.
