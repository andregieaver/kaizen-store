# D176: Design profiles

**Design profiles: a store's look kept to use again, applied to any store (outside the parity waves).** `docs/design-profiles.md` is the contract.
A design profile (`commerce.design_presets`, "design presets" in code, never "template", "starter" or "theme" alone) is a frozen, versioned snapshot (`v: 1`, `designSnapshotSchema`) of a store's look: the theme's settings (D60, mode and visitor switch included), the chosen header and footer (D80) and standard product layout (D79) as rows with their CSS, and the site's CSS (D100); never the store's name, logos (`navigation`), business details, menus' links, pages or products.
A snapshot is cleaned when it is taken (`snapshotLayout()`): the store's field components out, each menu component named by its role (header or footer menu), then D125's `sanitizeTemplate()` (no ids, links into the store, contact details, form recipients or other owner's HTML); pictures keep their addresses until applied.
Platform admins make one from a store they work in, update it from its store, describe, publish (never delete), order and preview it at `/admin/platform/design-profiles` (Stores section), and set a store template's recommended profile (`store_starters.recommended_design`).
`applyDesignPreset()` is the one writer: refused for a store that is not open or not the account's (a platform admin may apply to any store, and an unpublished profile only to a store template); it installs the fonts, copies the pictures into the store's media library as `applyTemplate()` does, checks each page with `pageInput` and `pageRulesProblem()`, then in one transaction keeps the look before as a saved theme "Before {profile} ({date})" and in `design_preset_uses.previous`, makes new
published header, footer and product layout pages (never over the store's own) with the store's own menus by role, chooses them, writes the theme and the CSS; `restoreDesignLook()` puts the look before back, newest first.
Owners apply one from `/admin/{store}/settings/design` (`website:write`), platform admins from `/admin/platform/stores/{store}`; creating a store and sign-up offer the cards after the store template's ("Keep the template's own design" first, a template's recommended profile chosen with it), kept on `access_requests.design_preset_id` only when published, and applied right after the store is made in application code (`createStoreForOwner()`, `approveAccessRequest()`); a failure keeps the store
with its template's look and says so. A public, `noindex`, inert, cached preview (`/admin/account/design-profiles/{profile}/preview?starter=`) draws a published profile on a published store template's front page and one product without writing anything. `design_presets` has no `store_id` (outside `COPY_RULES`); `design_preset_uses` is `never`.
