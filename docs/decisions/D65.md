# D65: Bookings are products of another kind, with one booking module a store switches on

**Bookings are products of another kind, with one booking module a store switches on.** Stores sell appointments (slots of staff time), stays (nights in rooms or homes) and rentals (days or hours of items), besides goods.
The product stays the sellable, with its prices, VAT, pages, cart, checkout and orders; `products.kind` says which, and what is not a product (resources, schedules, availability, bookings, calendar, reminders) is one module shared by all three kinds, with settings per kind in their own tables. Stores choose per service or listing whether it is paid at booking, at the venue, or by a saved card charged later.
Stays can be listed by outside hosts, making the store a marketplace; hosts become Kaizen's connected accounts, since a store cannot be a Stripe platform itself. Design and order of work: `bookings.md`.

Date: 2026-09-26

Products get a VAT category with rates per country, since accommodation and health care do not take the standard rate. Appointments come first (B1), then paying later (B2), stays and rentals (B3), hosts (B4). How money moves to hosts and the store's commission, and the store's reporting duties for hosts (DAC7), are to be confirmed before B4.
