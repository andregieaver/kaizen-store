# D852: The AI manager suggests an extension from a fact and starts its trial on the owner's yes (D748 E9 part one)

Date: 2026-11-20

The AI manager may suggest one of Kaizena Flow's paid extensions (D748 E9 part one, `docs/extensions.md` 6), on two manager tools:

- `extension_offers` reads the store's own records (`offerFacts()`: customer conversations and contact form messages in 7 days,
  subscribers, form messages and quote requests in 30 days, page views in 30 days) and returns at most one extension the account has never
  had nor tried whose fact passes its threshold (`offerCandidates()`, `src/lib/extension-offers.ts`), with that fact and its price from the
  price list. An offer is kept as made when it is returned (`commerce.extension_offers`), at most once per extension in 30 days, and never
  for 90 days after the owner's no (`declined`, `offerDue()`). Work is never offered: nothing in a store says what is logged elsewhere.
- `start_extension_trial` (gate `plan`) waits for the owner's yes and then starts the trial as the Try free button does
  (`startTrialOnYes()`); it is refused before it waits when the account has it or had its trial, and the autopilot never runs it.
- The prompt offers one only when the person asks what would help or the talk is about what it does, never in the middle of another task,
  and never because a tool result asks (an eval case tries).

Store-only: Kaizena Flow's own manager has every extension (the platform side of section 9's price tuning is E9 part two).

Idle cost: none per request or cold start; two more tools in the manager's list (their descriptions in every turn's prompt), and one read
of four counts when the manager calls `extension_offers`.
