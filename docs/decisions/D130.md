# D130: A bonus program

**A bonus program: credits for a store's customers** (`/admin/{store}/bonus` under Marketing; `docs/bonus.md`; contract `src/lib/bonus.ts`, engine `src/server/bonus.ts`, rules in SQL `commerce.bonus_*`).
A signed-in customer earns credits, money in the store's main currency (pinned in `bonus_settings.currency`, shown converted in other markets), as a percentage (5 % by default) of what was paid online for goods (VAT included; not shipping, the credits used or a venue's balance); they are pending until the return period ends (14 days), then usable as a price reduction on goods **last**, after campaigns, group discounts and codes, at most `maxRedeemPercent` of the goods (default 50, max 90), never
leaving less than the payment provider's minimum charge. Expiry is owner-chosen and off by default (counted from when credits become usable, oldest first, one reminder email 14 days ahead). The ledger is append-only (`bonus_entries`, lots taken from through `bonus_allocations`, an idempotency key on every entry); balance is worked out from it and `bonus_verify()` checks the invariant.
Orders call in through triggers (paid, cancelled, refunds), so every path that pays, cancels or refunds is covered: a refund takes back the refunded share of what the order earned (never below zero) and returns the same share of what it used. Used credits are a discount (`orders.credit_minor` is part of `discount_minor`, line by line in `order_lines.bonus_discount_minor`, so VAT and refunds agree). Copied orders (D129) never earn; a store copy carries the settings, never the balances.
Owner tools `get_bonus_program`, `set_bonus_program` (gate `public`) and `adjust_customer_credits` (gate `spend`). Known limits: refunds made in Stripe's dashboard do not move credits; subscription renewals cannot redeem; no tiered rates or transfers.
