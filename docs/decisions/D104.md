# D104: The AI manager gets the skills its owners asked for

**The AI manager gets the skills its owners asked for: analyses, reaching customers and the team, stock, and a hands-free voice mode.** New store tools, all worked out in code from the store's own data: `customer_insights` (who comes back, new and returning, the usual days between orders, loyal, at-risk and lapsed customers), `product_performance` (units, orders and takings per product, wishes and search opens, and what does not sell), `sales_trend` (per day, week or month, with the change),
`sales_funnel` (carts, checkouts left, orders placed and paid, carts won back, searches that found nothing; visits and views are not tracked and are never guessed), `restock_suggestions` (pace from paid sales, days the stock lasts, and how many to order for the days to cover plus the supplier's delivery time), and `list_integrations`.
New changes, each kept for the owner's yes: `email_customer` (a message to one of the store's own customers, in the store's name with its reply-to; AI-written text passes the claims filter, D76; the approval shows the whole text), `resend_order_email` (the confirmation, or the latest shipping notice), `set_stock` (by SKU) and `post_to_slack` (to the store's connected channel, escaped, through the integration log).
Playbooks for restocking, winning back customers, understanding customers and writing to a customer. The store checkup warns of stock that runs out within a week.
Voice mode, after Kaizen Life's hands-free loop (`useVoice`, `src/components/admin/voice-mode.ts`): the microphone's loudness is measured every frame; speech of 0.3 s or more starts an utterance and 0.7 s of silence ends it; typing discards it; what was heard is transcribed with the site's speech-to-text (D73), noise transcripts are dropped, and the answer is spoken sentence by sentence as it streams (`takeSentences()`, `speakable()` in `src/lib/speech-text.ts`) through
`/admin/…/assistant/speak` with the site's voice, then it listens again. Talking over it stops it (barge-in: louder than its echo for 0.28 s, with the browser's echo cancelling). Voice turns tell the model its answer is heard: plain sentences, no lists, links or ids, two to four sentences.
A change waiting for approval can be answered in words (`decide_approval`): only one of the conversation's, kept before the message, and approved only when the message itself plainly says yes (`saysYes()`, several languages, any no word blocks it), whatever the model says; declining is always allowed. The launcher has a microphone that opens the panel talking; the panel stays open on phones while talking; Escape ends voice mode; the screen stays on while it runs.

Date: 2026-09-29

Asked for by the owner, from the gaps the AI manager itself named (notifications, customer analytics, marketing tools, stock replenishment) and "full voice mode like Kaizen Life". Kaizen Life's main voice is one provider's realtime model; Kaizen never names a provider or model (D73), so the hands-free loop runs on each site's own speech models instead, which also keeps its data where the site chose. Speaking sentence by sentence starts the answer seconds sooner than speaking the whole.
Approving by voice needs a code check, not only the model's reading, so that words in data (an order note, an email) can never approve anything. Not done: shipping labels (no carrier is connected; the packing slip and tracking stay), a marketing-consent list (none is collected at checkout; newsletters go through Zapier or Make), product view tracking, and a realtime voice model.
