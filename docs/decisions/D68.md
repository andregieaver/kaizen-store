# D68: Rooms, items and staff are closed by blocks

**Rooms, items and staff are closed by blocks: dates the store sets, and events read from other sites' calendars; each room or item publishes its own taken days at a secret iCal address.** A block (`resource_blocks`) closes the whole resource whatever its capacity, and every booking check counts it: `busyOn()` for the dates and times offered, `commerce.resource_blocked()` under the lock in `hold_booking`, `move_booking` and a late payment.
Calendars from Airbnb, Booking.com and the like (`calendar_feeds`) are read every 15 minutes from the five-minute cron, and on adding or asking; each read makes the feed's blocks what the file says (by the event's UID), and a feed that fails keeps its blocks and shows why. Whole-day events close a room from noon to noon, so the morning's guest leaves and the afternoon's arrives, and an item or a person from midnight to midnight.
The published file (`/api/calendar/{token}.ics`) has whole days marked only Booked or Blocked, never a name or note. A block over an existing booking is allowed and shown as booked twice for the store to sort out.

Date: 2026-09-27

Defaults chosen for the owner (B3b). Only https addresses of public hosts are read, redirects checked one by one, at most 2 MB and 2,000 events; imported events are kept from a day ago to two years ahead. Staff get time off from the same blocks, without sync.
