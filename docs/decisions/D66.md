# D66: Appointments are paid now, with a deposit, or at the venue, chosen per appointment; shoppers cancel and move within the appointment's rule; no-show fees are charged only when staff ask

**Appointments are paid now, with a deposit, or at the venue, chosen per appointment; shoppers cancel and move within the appointment's rule; no-show fees are charged only when staff ask.** Each appointment says how it is paid (all now, the default; a deposit of 1–99 % now, 30 % by default; or all at the venue) and until how many hours before shoppers may cancel or move it themselves (24 by default).
An order keeps its full total and VAT; what is left for the venue is its balance (`orders.balance_minor`, per line `order_lines.venue_minor`), which staff mark paid (card terminal, cash, other) from the order. Stripe is sent only what is due now: each line at that amount with any discount inside it, no coupon and no Stripe invoice (the store invoices the whole at the venue), Kaizen's fee on that amount.
A deposit keeps the card (`setup_future_usage`, a Stripe customer on the store's account) so staff can charge a no-show fee (a percentage of the price, 0 by default, less the deposit). An order with nothing to pay online asks the shopper's name, email and phone on the cart page and is confirmed at once, keyed by a `venue` payment. Subscriptions are ordered apart from appointments paid later.

Date: 2026-09-27

Defaults chosen for the owner (asked to "use sensible defaults"): percentages rather than amounts, so they work in every currency; no automatic charges; shoppers may cancel and move. Refunds cover what Stripe took; money taken at the venue is paid back there. B2 is built in parts: B2a (payment modes, balance), B2b (shoppers cancel and move), B2c (no-show fees).
