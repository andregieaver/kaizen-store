# D691: Browse abandonment

**Browse abandonment: an email about the products a signed-in subscriber looked at (`docs/100-day-challenge.md` D4; 16 November 2026, `src/server/product-views.ts`, `/api/shop/viewed`, `BrowseTracker`, `src/server/automations.ts`, `src/lib/automation-kinds.ts`, migration `20261116180248_dusty_nick_fury`).** Browse abandonment waited for its own privacy decision: it needs a record of what a known person looked at, and visits are anonymous by design (D152).
The owner chose the narrowest rule: a product view is kept only for a customer **signed in** to the store, **subscribed** to its emails, who **allowed marketing cookies** in the store's own consent, only while the store's browse email is **on**, never with Global Privacy Control or Do Not Track, and for **7 days** (`PRODUCT_VIEW_DAYS`).
`commerce.customer_product_views` holds one row per customer and product with the latest view; a product page's `BrowseTracker` posts `{store, market, product}` to `/api/shop/viewed` only when the consent cookie allows marketing (nothing is stored in the browser), and `recordProductView()` checks every condition again on the server (an always-empty 204 says nothing).
Switching the email off deletes the store's views at once; the daily prune deletes older ones; a customer's or product's deletion takes theirs.
A new lifecycle kind, `browse` (*Still thinking about it?*), 1 to 5 days after the latest view (start 1): it goes to a subscriber whose latest view falls in the window, with no paid order since and no browse email in the last 7 days (key `browse:{subscriber}:{day}`), showing up to `PICKS_SHOWN` of the products they looked at, newest first, on sale and in stock now, not bought since; with none it does not go.
Its words are hand-written in Norwegian, Swedish, Danish and English, never naming the products, and the AI writes them with `write_email_automation` like the other kinds (told it does not know which products are shown and never to say it watched). The privacy starter gains a purpose line, only while the email is on (`LegalFacts.browseEmails`, four languages). Registered as personal data (marketing; exported with when, deleted on erasure), never copied with a store.
API contract 1.32. **Platform:** Kaizena Flow sells plans, not products to browse: store-only. **Not done:** recognising a subscriber from an email click (the owner chose against it).
