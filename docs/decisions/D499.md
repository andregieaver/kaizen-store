# D499: An invoice issued elsewhere, recorded in Work

**An invoice issued elsewhere, recorded in Work (8 November 2026, `recordIssuedElsewhere()` in `src/server/work-invoices.ts`, `InvoiceElsewhere` under a draft).** For the whole year in the books (D498), the owner records the invoices the business sent from another system or by hand that no import brought in.
Homer keeps issuing drafts; under a draft, folded away, *Issued elsewhere already?* takes the number it had, the day it was issued and, if known, its due day (else the client's or the store's days to pay).
The draft's client and lines, priced as Work prices them, become an imported invoice (D494's kind: `imported`, `legacy_number`, never numbered from the series, never sent, no PDF of ours), the draft deleted in the same transaction (event `invoice.imported`, `import: by_hand`; audited `work.invoice.recorded_elsewhere`).
Refused: a day after today, a due day before the issue day, a number already here (ours or another's), a draft without lines, and a draft with time logged here on it (that is Work's own invoice: issue it). It posts to the books as any invoice of their days does (D498), and is paid and credited like any other. No migration: the guard already lets an import insert an imported invoice and delete a draft.

Date: 2026-11-08
