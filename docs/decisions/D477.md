# D477: Moving in from Pipedrive

**Moving in from Pipedrive (S7 of `docs/own-books-and-sales.md`), built in steps.** A store brings its CRM from Pipedrive by the account's API token: organisations to companies, persons to customers (D366's rules: no password, email not proven, nothing emailed, nobody subscribed), pipelines and stages, deals, activities and notes; a dry run first, then a count of what came and what did not. **Step 1, reading an account (built).** Pipedrive moved its lists to API v2 (`/api/v2/…`, cursor pages
of at most 500; v1's are marked deprecated with a grace period of at least a year, and a third party gives 31 July 2026 for their end [U: Pipedrive's own date]), so organisations, persons, pipelines, stages, deals and activities are read from v2, and notes and the token's own user from v1, where they still are.
`readPipedriveAccount()` (`src/server/pipedrive-client.ts`) sends the token in the `x-api-token` header (never in an address that could be logged), waits out a rate limit as `Retry-After` says (three times, at most a minute) and stops at its time budget; it only reads.
`src/lib/pipedrive.ts` reads each record leniently into ours (a person's primary email and phone; a deal's value in its currency's minor units, none for a currency the platform does not keep; Pipedrive's activity types to a call, a meeting or a task, its own type kept; a note's HTML as text) and `planImport()` is the dry run: what would come over, and why the rest would not (a person without an email, a deal with nobody or no stage that comes over, an activity without a day or anyone, a note
about no one).
Tests: `pipedrive.test.ts`, `pipedrive-client.test.ts` (a stand-in API: pages by cursor and offset, the token only in the header, a rate limit waited out, a refused token, the budget). **Step 2, the import and its screen (built).** Settings → Moving in has a Pipedrive card while Deals is on (where Deals is offered but off, the one step before it): Homer pastes the API token (*Connect Pipedrive*: asked of Pipedrive whose it is, `/v1/users/me`, and kept encrypted in `commerce.crm_moves` with its
Pipedrive company and shown by its last four), presses *See what comes over*, reads the counts and what is left out and why, and presses *Bring it over*; then *Open Deals*, and later *Bring over what's new*.
A check and an import are one run (`PdRun`, `crm_moves.run`) going feed by feed (the team's users, organisations, persons, pipelines, stages, deals, activities, notes: each needing only what the ones before bring) and page by page within a budget, under a 90-second lease; each page is written and counted in one transaction, so a run that stops picks up at its page, and a page that cannot be written is tried again a few times before the run says so.
The open page's ticks and the dispatcher's `pipedrive` job (40 seconds) move it on, so the owner may leave. Both follow the same rules (`skipOf()` in `src/lib/pipedrive.ts`; `planImport()` is a check of a whole account), so the check's counts are the import's.
`commerce.crm_move_links` links each record brought over to its row here by Pipedrive company, kind and id: a second run brings only what is new and counts the rest as come before (what came over before is the store's now, never overwritten), and a row deleted here, or a person erased, keeps its link and is never brought back (the link holds only Pipedrive's number).
What comes over: an organisation as a company (one of the same name adopted); a person with an email as a customer (D366: no password, not proven, nobody subscribed or emailed; someone the store has keeps what it has, only empty name and phone filled), a member of their organisation's company while it has room; pipelines (one of the same name adopted) and their stages; deals in their stage, with their value and currency (the store's when none), owner, close and reason (closed by a person);
activities, due when Pipedrive says (its times are UTC; one without a time on its day in the store's zone), done or not, and marked told so the push job never reminds of them; notes, kept by a person's email: on their person, else their deal's, else someone of their (deal's) organisation, else left out. Deals and activities go to the team member with the Pipedrive user's email, else to nobody. *Disconnect* forgets the token and the run; what came over stays linked.
Audited as `integration.pipedrive_connected`, `_disconnected`, `_import_started` and `_imported` with counts only. Tests: `pipedrive.test.ts` (a check page by page, what came before, where a note goes, owners by email), `pipedrive.int.test.ts` (against a database with a stand-in Pipedrive two records a page: a check writes nothing and counts what the import then brings; a second import brings nothing twice; a deleted deal stays deleted; a refused token).
