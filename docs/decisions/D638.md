# D638: A product that may run out is an event the autopilot answers

**A product that may run out is an event the autopilot answers (`docs/100-day-challenge.md` C4, step 2; 15 November 2026, `src/server/autopilot-events.ts`, `src/lib/autopilot-events.ts`).** In the first five minutes of every sixth hour (UTC, `stockoutCheckDue()`) each reacting store's own reorder forecast (`restockSuggestions()`, D636, the figures the owner and the AI read) is checked, and each SKU it says to order now with no open purchase order (a draft, or ordered and not received in full) is
kept as a `stockout_risk` event, at most five a store a check (the shortest-lasting first), once per SKU and ISO week, its facts the forecast's own (days it lasts, delivery days, units suggested).
The turn has `restock_suggestions`, `stock_levels`, `list_purchase_orders` and `create_purchase_order`: it orders the suggested units from the supplier earlier purchase orders show, which runs only within the purchasing budget (C3) and otherwise waits for the owner's yes; with no known supplier it orders nothing and says so. A purchase order made before the turn looks closes the event (`stillOpen()`). Twelve such turns a store a day.
