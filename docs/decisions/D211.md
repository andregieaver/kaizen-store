# D211: What a visitor's page loads, kept small

**What a visitor's page loads, kept small (`docs/performance.md`).** PageSpeed found the footer's blurred picture as a phone's largest paint (a footer revealed on scroll was pinned under the page from the start), lazy pictures on the first screen, 466 KB of scripts (zod with its translations, every client component of the route) and an embedded frame without a name.
The footer is pinned only after a scroll near the page's end; a page's or header's first row loads its pictures at once and first, the first grid its first four tiles; a background picture gives a phone the library's 480 px copy (only that when blurred); no visitor script imports zod or the page model (schemas in `*-schema.ts`, held by `storefront-bundle.scan.test.ts`); parts that only some pages draw are `next/dynamic` stand-ins in `components/lazy/` (never a form, whose typed words would be
lost, or a booking's changes); the chat and referral capture wait until the browser is idle; font stylesheets are kept a year under a versioned address; an embedded frame is always named. `pnpm perf` runs Lighthouse; `e2e/performance.spec.ts` holds the first scripts' budget and the eager pictures.
