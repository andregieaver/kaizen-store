# D631: The page builder's settings panels, one structure for every part; their tab and scroll kept from one part to the next; Copy style and Paste style

**The page builder's settings panels, one structure for every part; their tab and scroll kept from one part to the next; Copy style and Paste style (15 November 2026, `SettingsTabs`, `src/components/admin/settings-panel-parts.tsx`, `src/lib/settings-memory.ts`, `src/lib/settings-summary.ts`, `src/lib/style-copy.ts`; drawn on the owner's canvas *Builder Settings Redesign*).** The four tabs General, Style, Motion and Advanced (D48, D128) held their fields out of order: an entrance's delay and
duration were under Advanced while the entrance was under Motion, who sees a part was split between Advanced and the foot of General, Take from field opened General above the text, Style showed some 35 controls at once, a column's General ended with its row's whole layout picker, and Delete column sat beside Done.
Now every row's, column's and component's panel is built by `partTabs()` and `chrome()`: a trail to its row and column (each opens its settings), a ⋯ menu (Copy style, Paste style, Save as reusable, Duplicate, Delete), Done alone in the footer, and four tabs named for a question: **Content** (Layout for rows and columns), **Design**, **Motion**, **Visibility**.
Inside each, the few fields most owners need are open and the rest are folds that say their value while closed; Advanced is the last fold (Content: Take from field and the anchor, which was the Id; Design: classes). Design: the screen size the changes reach, with the four sizes, at the top; the part's own look; then Text (named for what it styles: Button text, Heading text, Caption text), Spacing (one drawing, outside and inside, with linked sides, instead of eight boxes), Border and shadow.
Motion: the effects, then Timing (the entrance's delay and duration, from Advanced). Visibility: a sentence of who sees it, where and when (`visibilityAtGlance()`; a schedule alone is still everyone's), then the screens, Who sees it (was Display), When (was Schedule, at the foot of General) and a row's popup (was its General's last fold).
A button's address is *Link to*: Address, Popup, Email or Phone, each with the one field it needs (the popup picker was a second field below the address); a page picker was left out, as a page's address on a store's own path is not yet certain.
A heading's level is six buttons with one line, its second part behind a switch; an image's link folds under its caption; a row's height is one choice (fits its content, fills the screen, set a height) and its section style is the first thing under Design; a column says its width in words (its share of the row) with Change in row, instead of its row's layout picker.
The panel remembers, while the builder is open, the tab last chosen, how far each kind of part's tab was scrolled and which folds are open (`SettingsMemoryProvider`; a component just added opens on Content, `fresh`).
Copy style keeps a part's Design settings (`styleKeys()`: every part's spacing, frame, typography and classes, and its kind's own, such as a button's look and colour or a row's section style and background) with their values at the smaller sizes, never its content, link, motion, visibility or anchor, in the browser (`kaizen:copied-style`, read back by `readCopiedStyle()`, so it pastes on another page); Paste style lays it on a part of the same kind only, through `patchPart()`, so Undo takes it
back. Lists inside components (an FAQ's questions, testimonials) keep their editors; making each a card with a handle is the next step. The platform's pages use the same builder, so Kaizena Flow's own site has it in the same change. Tests: `style-copy.test.ts`, `settings-summary.test.ts`; `e2e/signed-in/schedule.spec.ts`, `dual-heading.spec.ts`, `builder-animation.spec.ts`, `builder-columns.spec.ts` follow the new tabs.
