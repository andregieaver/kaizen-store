# D804: In the challenge a money-free order change and a market price rule run by themselves (100-day A2)

**In the challenge a money-free order change and a market price rule run by themselves (`docs/100-day-challenge.md` A2; 19 November
2026).** Both stay never automatic outside the challenge. In it, `autonomyDecision()` runs `change_order` itself when the order editor's
preview moves no money (`orderChangeMoneyOf()`: `none`), area `messages`; a refund waits in the refund queue, and a higher total (a link
the customer is asked to pay) waits too. `set_market_price_rule` runs itself at the catalogue's level when the largest change it makes to
a price (`priceRuleAutopilotFacts()` over `ruleEffect()`, the same work `saveMarketPriceRule()` does; a price it ends counts as 100 %)
is within `priceChangePercent` and the lowest margin it leaves a product with a cost is not under the margin floor (D5); taking a rule
away changes no price. A fact that cannot be read waits. Tests: `src/lib/autopilot-challenge.test.ts`,
`src/server/market-prices.int.test.ts`, `src/server/order-change-tools.int.test.ts`. The platform sells plans, not goods: it has no
orders to change or country prices to rule, so nothing of this applies to it.

**A price rule before VAT used the real VAT rates (19 November 2026, found while building A2).** `commerce.vat_rate()` gives a fraction
(0.25) and `derivedPrice()` takes percent (25), and `src/server/market-prices.ts` passed the fraction on: a rule on the price before VAT
took off and put back next to nothing, so a country with another VAT rate than its base got the base country's price with VAT (Norway to
Germany: about 5 % too high). Its two reads now multiply by 100. No price in production was wrong: the three rules there go from Norway to
Sweden, both 25 %. `src/server/market-prices.int.test.ts` holds it with books (0 % in Norway, 6 % in Sweden).
