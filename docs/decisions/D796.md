# D796: Dates shoppers see in the store's own time zone

**Dates shoppers see in the store's own time zone: storefront, shoppers' emails and contracts (code review step 1.20, FS12; 19 November 2026, `src/lib/dates.ts`).** After D786 fixed the admin, a store outside Norway still showed its shoppers Oslo's day, or UTC's on
Vercel, for an evening order, article, blog comment, campaign end, renewal or download link's last day. `dateIn(where, instant, style, locale)` and `calendarDate(day, style, locale)` are D786's pure formatter under names that do not say admin;
`storeZoneOf(row.time_zone)` reads a store's zone from an untyped row. Pages read the zone of the store they already loaded and emails take it from `EmailStore`, so no page or email reads more. Kaizena Flow's own plans page, theme store and mail about plans use
`PLATFORM_TIME_ZONE`; Porterbuddy and the books keep `NORWAY_TIME_ZONE`. The 34 `?? "Europe/Oslo"` fallbacks in `src/server` are gone. Outside the admin, `Europe/Oslo` lines fell from 73 to 15 (constants, the column default, fixtures, two country tables and seven SQL
lines of the platform's own sales, books, payouts and channels reports, none a store's own figure) and zoneless dates from 28 to 2 (drawn only in the browser). `dates.scan.test.ts` holds them, and the 16 UTC cuts left (keys, hashes, labelled UTC), to ceilings that only fall,
with a row in the budgets rule. A store in New York or Auckland is tested for an article, campaign end, parcel, order confirmation, closure email and bonus credits. Next: experiments group exposures by UTC day, the platform activity filter uses UTC days, and 39 admin
dates are formatted with no zone.
