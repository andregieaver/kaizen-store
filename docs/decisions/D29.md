# D29: Purchase options can have a free trial, a sign-up fee and a commitment; subscribers pause, skip and change their subscription themselves, and are reminded before they are charged

**Purchase options can have a free trial, a sign-up fee and a commitment; subscribers pause, skip and change their subscription themselves, and are reminded before they are charged.** A free trial (up to 90 days) charges nothing for what renews until it ends (Stripe's `trial_period_days`), and its first delivery ships free; items bought once in the same checkout pay as usual. A sign-up fee is set per market and charged once with the first order, as its own line.
A commitment (up to 24 payments, the first included) means a cancellation takes effect when the last committed payment's period ends (Stripe's `cancel_at`); it counts payments made, so pauses and skips move it. Pausing holds up to three deliveries at a time (Stripe's `pause_collection`, which voids the invoices due meanwhile); skipping is a pause of one; not during a trial or while a payment is failing.
Changing contents swaps a line to another variant of the same product and delivery, or changes its quantity, or removes it (one must stay); a swapped line takes today's price with the option's discount, a kept one keeps its price; shipping is worked out again, and Stripe charges the new amounts from the next renewal, with nothing prorated. Every change is confirmed by email.
A daily job emails a reminder three days before a trial ends and seven days before renewals of a month or longer, once per charge date. Staff can do all of this from the subscription's page, and also end a subscription at once.

Date: 2026-09-24

Weekly renewals get no reminder: each renewal's confirmation serves. Options with different trials cannot share a checkout, like options with different schedules. Content changes replace the Stripe subscription's items (products are reused by name). Commitment dates are worked out from Stripe's period end by the calendar, so a month-end renewal can end a few days early.
The reminder job needs `CRON_SECRET` in Vercel (Vercel Cron sends it); without it, the route refuses every call and no reminders go out. Reminders, like every email, are only recorded until email is set up (D26).
