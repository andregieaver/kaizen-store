# D411: Notices of illegal content on stores

**Notices of illegal content on stores (DSA art. 16 and 17, PSI REG-12; `docs/dsa.md`).** Anyone reports content on a store at `/report` (and `/no/report`), linked from every store's footer and Kaizena Flow's own as *Report illegal content*: the address, the kind of problem, why it is illegal, the sender's name and email (not needed for offences against children) and a good-faith statement (`reportInput()`, `src/lib/content-reports.ts`; checks in `commerce.content_reports`, written only by
`src/server/content-reports.ts`). The sender gets a receipt and every platform admin an alert; an admin decides each notice at `/admin/platform/reports` (Restricted, with what was done, or No action) with its reason, audited; the sender is told the decision with redress, and the store's owners get a statement of reasons when content was restricted, without the sender's name. The sender is forgotten 12 months after the decision (`pruneContentReports()`, dispatcher job `contentReports`).
`ai_used` and `ai_note` wait for part 2, where an AI reads and drafts and a person decides.

Date: 2026-10-31

The re-score's REG-12 named no DSA notice mechanism and no report button. *Why a person decides:* restricting a store's lawful products by mistake harms a paying customer, art. 20(6) forbids complaint decisions made solely by automated means where it applies, and a notice's words are a stranger's. *Why on Kaizena Flow's site:* Kaizena Flow is the hosting service, so the notice is to it, not to the store.
