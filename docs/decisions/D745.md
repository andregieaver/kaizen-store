# D745: Tag refreshes and store page draws are counted in production

**Tag refreshes and store page draws are counted in production (bloat review step 1.25; 17 November 2026).** `refreshTag()` logs and counts each refresh by the tag's family and subject and by its caller (a job by name, a person's action, a route); a store layout's draw logs `store.draw` with its statements and milliseconds and counts it. The counts go to `commerce.cache_events_daily` in one upsert after the response, buffered per instance, never awaited and never failing what they count.
Usage and limits shows a week, and a job refreshing one subject more than 10 times in a day is flagged (a job should refresh only what changed). The build's prerenders are not counted (fixed the same day: the first deploy counted about 2,100 draws in an hour of about 60 real ones, the rest the build's). Kaizena Flow's own site is a store layout too. Migration versions (applied by CI, 17 November 2026): `20261117185003_cache_events_daily`. Advisors after: INFO only.
