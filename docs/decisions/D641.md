# D641: The admin redesigned, round 14

**The admin redesigned, round 14: Companies, Deals, the activity log (`docs/audits/admin-redesign-round-14.md`; 16 November 2026).** A page behind a feature that is off offers owners *Switch it on* beside *Go to Features* (`FeatureOffPanel`, a plain form to `/admin/{store}/settings/features/on`, which calls `setFeature()` and comes back).
Companies asks first for the organisation number or name and fills in the rest from the register (`CompanyFields` `stepped`), the employees' share and account limit in More settings. Deals with no deal is one card to add the first, with the stages on it; the board comes with the first deal, and the sentence says what is open and worth.
The activity log says the week in a sentence (`activityWeek()`), filters by people and areas as chips with the rest one fold away, groups entries by day and never shows an id. Platform: no companies or deals; its activity log shares the rows. No migration.
