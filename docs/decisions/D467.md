# D467: Kaizena Flow runs on Kaizena Flow, step 2

**Kaizena Flow runs on Kaizena Flow, step 2: owners and applicants are its own store's customers (6 November 2026, `docs/platform-parity-plan.md` step 2, `src/lib/house-customers.ts`, `src/server/house-customers.ts`, `commerce.house_customer_links`).** `syncHouseCustomers()`, the five-minute dispatcher's `houseCustomers`, keeps Kaizena Flow's own store's customers in step with the platform: every account that owns a customer's store and the latest confirmed application of each address (a
self-serve one once its link was opened) is one customer of `kaizenaflow` (made when missing, a name filled in only while empty, so what staff typed stays), linked to the account and the application (`house_customer_links`, only in Kaizena Flow's own store, `house_customer_links_house()`).
The platform keeps its tags true and takes them away when they no longer hold: *Owner*, *Lead* (an application waiting, no store), *Plan: {name}* per plan running, *No plan*, *Trial*, *Overdue*, *Store open*; staff's own tags are never touched, so segments, broadcasts and automations reach Kaizena Flow's customers as any store's reach theirs.
An application opens a deal on a *Sign-ups* pipeline (Asked, Approved, Store opened; made the first time), moved forward as it is approved and its store opened, won when the store's plan is paid with real money (Stripe's live mode; a test card's payment is no sale) and lost when it is declined, closed by Kaizena Flow's own records (`deals.closed_via` `platform`, *by Kaizena Flow's own records* in the deal's history); a deal a person closed or moved to another pipeline is theirs, and none is
moved back.
Nobody is subscribed to anything: the newsletter on Kaizena Flow's own pages now keeps those who ask on its store's list (`src/server/forms.ts`, D295's own consent and confirmation), where they were only emailed to the recipients before. *Forgetting*: an erased account (D377) takes everything of its address in the store with it (subscription, tags, notes, deals, the customer); a person who is neither an owner nor an applicant any more (an application blanked after its period, PSI REG-05) is
forgotten when nothing else of theirs is kept there (no subscription, note, staff tag, conversation, privacy request or other deal), else kept as any store's customer; an erasure in Kaizena Flow's store blanks the person's applications too (`blankApplications()` in `eraseRows()`), so the next run does not bring them back.
Platform → Customers → an account links to the same person in the store. *Customers on a website with Deals*: a store's customer pages were behind the online shop, so Kaizena Flow's store (and any website with Deals) had deals with people it could not list; they now open with the shop or Deals (`CUSTOMERS_FEATURE`).

Date: 2026-11-06

Step 2 of the plan the owner chose (D466): Kaizena Flow's customers, subscribers, deals and privacy requests, with no second build. *Why a link table and not columns on `customers`:* the links are Kaizena Flow's alone, and a store's copy or export never carries them. *Why a sync and not triggers:* it brings in every existing owner and application on its first run, and catches up after any miss. Not yet: a plan's events on the customer's timeline, and a news checkbox at sign-up.
