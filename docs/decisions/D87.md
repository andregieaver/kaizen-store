# D87: A top link in a menu can be a mega menu

**A top link in a menu can be a mega menu: the links under it open side by side across the header's width, in columns, each with a picture of its own and the links under it.** Set on the link in the menu editor (`MenuEntry.mega`: 1–`MEGA_MAX_COLUMNS` columns, optionally centred); the links right under it take an uploaded picture (`MenuEntry.image`), shown above them.
Drawn by `MenuTreeView` in menus laid out side by side: the panel lies along the header's whole bottom edge, whichever column the menu sits in (`data-mega-panel`, placed fixed against the header's moving wrapper by globals.css), or under the row a menu component sits in elsewhere, its links within the content's width, opening on hover or keyboard focus with no script; in columns and the phone's menu the links are listed as usual.
Only a top link can be one; a link moved under another stops being one.
