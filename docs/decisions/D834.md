# D834: One way to search and page a list: Customers, Products, Invoices and Returns page with Pager, search with ListSearch, and say when they are cut

**An admin list shows a hundred rows a page by number, says "100 of 150" with *Next 100* whenever it holds more than it shows, and searches with
the kit's GET form (code review step 1.13, finding FS2; 20 November 2026).** `LIST_PAGE_SIZE`, `listPage()` and `listOffset()` (`src/lib/list-page.ts`)
are the one size and parser; the Stores, Pages and Articles lists use them too. Each read counts what its filters find in the same statement (`count(*)
over ()`) and ends its order on a unique key (email, account id, handle and id, number), so a page's edge loses or repeats no row; a page past the last
opens the first. Customers: `customerPage()`, a typed read (it stopped at 100 with no word); *Tag everyone the search finds* follows its view too, on every
page. Products read every product and searched, filtered and showed sixty at a time in the browser: `src/server/product-list.ts` reads a page with its
count, the views' figures in a second statement (in place of every SKU, so the screen's five statements stay five) and every product for the AI's tool. Its
views (All, On sale, Drafts, Needs stock) are the search form's buttons, so a view keeps the search and the search keeps the view, without script. In the
table the head's box is *Choose all 100 on this page*; only when more match, the foot offers *Choose all 150 products that match, on every page*, which
reads the same filter and order (`matchingProductIds()`, at most 500). Invoices and credit notes (50 a page, "Page 2 of 3") and Returns (25) move onto
`Pager` and `ListSearch`, their other values kept by `invoiceQueryValues()` and `queueQuery()`; their dates and selects are the kit's. Orders keep their
cursor: orders arrive while the list is read. Platform: Kaizena Flow's Customers (`platformCustomerPage()`) page the same; its own customers are its store's
(D467), so they get the store's list; it sells plans, so it has no products, returns or invoices list of its own (a store's last 12 plan invoices are a
card), and the Stores list's filters stay step 2.10. Ratchet: unchecked reads 3084 to 3079, field boxes 295 to 293, native selects 456 to 453; copies and
unused unchanged; `products.ts` 98.5 to 94.2 KB. Against the commit before, the products list's first scripts fell 2.5 KB and no route rose. Tests:
`list-page.test.ts`, the views' tests, `product-list.int.test.ts`, `customer-admin.int.test.ts`, the invoice and returns reads' boundaries, and
`e2e/signed-in/list-pages.spec.ts` (150 customers and 150 products).
