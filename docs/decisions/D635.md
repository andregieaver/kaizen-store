# D635: A late order's and a parcel's customer told in the store's own words

**A late order's and a parcel's customer told in the store's own words (`docs/100-day-challenge.md` C; 15 November 2026, `src/lib/order-update-email.ts`, `src/server/order-updates.ts`).** The order events (C5) left a note and wrote to nobody, so a late order or a parcel in trouble waited for the owner, or the customer waited unanswered.
`tell_customer_about_order` (gate `send`, area messages, counted in `CUSTOMER_EMAIL_TOOLS` with the owner's customer emails a week) sends one of four fixed updates: `delayed`, `parcel_attempted`, `parcel_problem`, `parcel_returning`. The words are the store's, written by hand in nb, sv, da and en (English for other languages), and promise no date, refund or amount; the model chooses only which update, so a customer is never told what a model wrote.
Code checks the records first (`factsFor()`): a delay only for a paid order with goods left to send and not held (a held order is a person's), a parcel's update only while its tracking is in that phase. Each update goes once per order (email key `order-update:{order}:{update}`, kind `order.update`, classed `shopper`), with the parcel's tracking number and link, through `orderNotice()` in the order's language with the store's name and reply address, and is audited as `order.customer_updated`.
The `order_unsent`, `delivery_problem` and `supplier_problem` turns have it and are told when to use it; a held order's and a dispute's turns do not. A plan was the other way the plan named; fixed words were chosen because they cannot state a false fact. No migration.
