# D344: Themes from outside

**Themes from outside (wave 7 unit J, `docs/wave-7-storefront.md` section 4, J); built.** Anyone who may change a store's website (`website:write`: its owner, staff given it, or a designer invited as a collaborator) sends the store's live look as a theme, or as the next version of a theme they are the author of, from Design settings' *Share this look as a theme*; the look is taken by `takeSnapshot()` (the look only, never the store's words, products, pages or menus' links) and kept with the
submission in `commerce.theme_submissions` (written only by `src/server/theme-submissions.ts`; status moved only forward by `theme_submissions_rules()`, the snapshot and who fixed, a rejection with its reason, at most three open an account).
Kaizena Flow's admins review at Themes → Submissions: *Review* makes a draft theme from the look (`createDesign()` with a `look` origin, its author set; an update is written into its theme's draft), *Approve and publish* publishes through `publishDesign()` (the theme checks hold) and gives a new theme its address from its name, *Not accepted* tells the author why and puts the draft theme away; *Hide* is the theme's Archive.
A theme from outside names its author (`design_presets.author_id`, `author_name`) on its page with the number of stores using it, and has no price (`theme_prices_author_free()`). The author's Account → *Your themes* lists their submissions with state and reason (withdraw while open) and their themes with version and stores.
Decision 4: a new install no longer follows its theme's updates (`store_theme_installs.follows_updates` defaults to off; installs that followed keep following), so an update is offered with What's new and made by the owner's press.

Date: 2026-10-27
