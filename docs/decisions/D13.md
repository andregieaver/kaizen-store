# D13: No extra monitoring or analytics accounts until there is a need

**No extra monitoring or analytics accounts until there is a need.**

Date: 2026-09-23

Errors: Vercel's built-in logs. Speed: Vercel Speed Insights. Traffic: Vercel Web Analytics (cookieless). Business events: our own tables in Postgres. Experiments: assignment in our own code. Sentry, PostHog and GrowthBook are optional add-ons, not prerequisites.
