# D786: Dates in the store's own time zone

**Dates in the store's own time zone: one admin date formatter (code review 1.14, FS5; 19 November 2026, `src/lib/admin-date.ts`).** A store outside Norway saw an evening order on the wrong day on some screens: 77 admin lines named `Europe/Oslo` and 67 cut a day from a UTC string. `adminDate(where,
instant, style, lang)` shows a moment in a zone (a zone name, a store, or a row carrying `timeZone`); `adminDay(day, style, lang)` shows a calendar day and never shifts it, nor does `adminDate` a bare `YYYY-MM-DD`. A store's dates use its zone; Kaizena Flow's own figures and an account's records use
`PLATFORM_TIME_ZONE`; Tripletex, Fiken and the platform's books use `NORWAY_TIME_ZONE` (the law's clock). The control center and the platform's store lists show each row on its own store's day; discount and campaign times are read and shown on the store's clock. `admin-dates.scan.test.ts` fails on a
new Oslo literal or UTC cut in admin code (11 kept cuts listed with reasons; a row in the budgets rule), and the ratchet's `osloFiles` is 0. The platform is the same code with its own zone. Next: the dates shoppers see (storefront articles, blog comments, campaign notices, the plans page, shopper,
contract and endpoint emails), the `?? "Europe/Oslo"` fallbacks in `src/server`, and the SQL reports that read days at Oslo time.
