# D792: Mail to reserved test domains is logged, never sent

**Mail to reserved test domains is logged, never sent (19 November 2026).** The owner asked for a year of made-up history in Demo store 2
(formerly Dev store 2) to show the admin; its customers need addresses, and the platform sent to any address it was given (`sendEmail()`
held mail back only for sandboxes, starters and erased people), so a made-up customer on `example.com` would have reached Resend, bounced and
counted against the sending guard (D730). Now an address on `example.org` or `example.net` (or under them; RFC 2606 reserves both for documentation) is kept in full in `email_messages` as `logged` with the
note "not sent: a reserved test address" and never delivered: `sendEmail()` checks after keeping the row, and the broadcast batches before
`deliverBatch()` (the only two paths that hand mail to Resend), each in the insert that keeps the row. Only these two of the reserved names:
`example.com`, `.test`, `.example` and `.invalid` are what about twenty integration tests deliver to through a stubbed Resend, and no test
used `example.org` or `example.net`. The check is pure, `isReservedAddress()` in `src/lib/reserved-address.ts`, tested with look-alikes
(`myexample.org`, `example.org.au`) that stay real, and `reserved-address.int.test.ts` runs both senders with sending set up. Consequence: the seeded store's customers all use `example.org`,
so its automations, review requests, cart reminders and subscriptions can stay switched on in a demo without sending anything; the email
log and each customer's timeline still show what would have gone. Rule: `docs/rules/admin/marketing.md`, Reserved test addresses.

**The year it was for (same day).** `scripts/demo-history.mjs` writes the history as plain SQL from the store's facts (`--context-sql`
prints the query; a seeded random generator, so the same context and end give the same files): 14 months from 15 September 2025 with a
launch ramp, Black Week and Christmas; customers on `example.org` (about 40 % with accounts), orders with their card payments, invoices
dated the day paid (`build_invoice_snapshot()` as `make_order_invoice()` does, with the store's own day), parcels, deliveries recorded as
the carrier's, refunds and returns whose credit notes are re-dated to the refund, stock drawn and restocked through the levels so the
ledger holds; subscribers, carts and visits, reviews, finished support conversations, wishlists, bonus credits, staff-issued gift cards, a
trade pipeline, automations with three months of sends, two flows, a year of newsletters with frozen counts, cart reminders, ad spend and
searches. Every row a job would act on is written finished (alerts handled, refunds succeeded, checkouts reminded) and only `SET LOCAL
session_replication_role = replica` (never `set_config()`, which Supabase refuses) re-dates the append-only rows. It was rehearsed on a
local copy of the store, then run on production for Demo store 2 (`dev-store-2`, renamed from Dev store 2) on 19 November 2026 through
Supabase's query API: 1,505 orders (1004 to 2508), 1,389 invoices, 75 credit notes, 599 customers, 1,067 subscribers, 82,404 visits. Most
of it is append-only and stays; the store is a demo for good. In the first half hour the jobs logged 21 emails to `example.org` and sent none.
