# D82: A variant can have a picture of its own, and shoppers choose variants from a dropdown drawn in the store's theme, with each variant's picture, stock and price

**A variant can have a picture of its own, and shoppers choose variants from a dropdown drawn in the store's theme, with each variant's picture, stock and price.** The picture is kept on the variant (`product_variants.image_url`, `image_thumbnail_url`), chosen in the product editor from the product's pictures or uploaded for it, and copied with the template's variants; the demo mug and notebook have one per variant.
`Dropdown` (`src/components/dropdown.tsx`) is a select-only combobox (WAI-ARIA: arrows, Home and End, Enter and Space, Escape, typing to jump; unavailable choices shown and skipped) used on the product page for a product's variants (one Add to cart for the chosen one, where each variant had a row), in the phone's bottom bar (opening upwards across the bar), and for an appointment's staff and a rental's hours; the page and the bar share the chosen variant (`VariantChoice`).
Appointments' and rentals' variant cards show the pictures too.

Date: 2026-09-28

Asked for by the owner: native selects cannot show pictures or follow the store's theme. A picture on the variant, rather than a link to one of the product's picture rows, survives the editor saving the product's pictures anew; choosing from the product's pictures keeps the usual case one click. One dropdown for the page and the bar keeps what the shopper chose the same in both.
