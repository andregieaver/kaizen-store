# D457: The page builder sandbox

**The page builder sandbox: the real builder on a store of one's own, without signing up (5 November 2026, `docs/builder-sandbox.md`; steps S1 to S4 built; the switch, and the *Try the builder* page waiting as a draft, are the owner's).** *Try the builder* on kaizenaflow.com (and *Try it with this theme* in the theme store) makes a sandbox: a real store, cloned from a demo store offered in the sandbox (`clone_store()`), at a `try-` address, owned by a disabled platform account, private from
birth with no host, domain, payments, orders, email or share links, kept out of every list, count and job that already leaves template stores out (`stores.sandbox`, `commerce.sandboxes`, `store_is_active()` false for it).
The visitor holds no account: a pass (an HttpOnly cookie whose secret is kept only as its SHA-256) opens that one store, in the page builder served from `src/app/admin/try/` outside the admin's gate with its own `PageOwnerContext` (`sandboxPageContext()`), whose actions call `sandboxFor()` first and then the store's own server functions: an allow-list.
Made within a budget per network and for the platform, a daily cap and a platform switch; AI and uploads on a per-sandbox allowance (`limit_overrides`). It lasts 7 days after the last visit, 30 at most; *Start over* makes another. *Keep this store* is self-serve sign-up (the first email asked), whose confirmation adopts the sandbox: its own address, the new owner, the 14-day trial, private until opened.
An expired sandbox is purged by `purge_sandbox()`, which refuses any other store and deletes every row of it, its audit entries too (S3), leaving the bare `stores` row as the tombstone its `sandboxes` row points at. Built in four steps (S1 to S4), the switch on for everyone only after S3.

Date: 2026-11-05

Asked for by the owner, to show the page builder to store owners before they sign up; the owner chose no email at all to start (over landing self-serve sign-ups straight in the builder, which needs a confirmed email first). *Why a store of one's own and not a browser-only builder:* the Theme tab, header and footer, versions, the preview in the store's theme and the AI need a store. *Why a pass and not a guest account in the admin:* an account needs an email, and the admin would need every
account-level screen blocked one by one. *Why private and no share links:* otherwise the sandbox is free anonymous hosting for phishing pages. *Why a store per sandbox and not a reused pool:* a reset that missed a table would show one visitor's work to the next.
The owner then took the recommendations (section 10): AI on a small allowance, 7 days after the last visit and 30 at most, a store per sandbox purged with a tombstone, the Standard store as the default source, 300 a day. *S1 as built* (section 8.1): the rules in the database, `src/server/sandbox.ts`, `/admin/try` with pages, headers and footers, the switch on Platform → Access requests, sandboxes left out wherever template stores are (held by `store-starters.scan.test.ts`) and every email of a
sandbox suppressed. *Found:* templates from "my stores" would have shown one visitor another's saved parts, so a sandbox has the marketplace's only.
