# D330: Brand and look

**Brand and look (`docs/ai-autopilot.md` unit F, section 14), step F1.** The brand set down in words is `commerce.brand_profiles` (a store's, or Kaizena Flow's own with no store), written only by `src/server/brand.ts`: a tagline (through `findClaims()`, as customers may see it), a voice, an audience, up to five values and ten words to avoid, read and kept by the AI manager's `get_brand` and `set_brand` (ungated: staff-only), and read by a goal's review.
A look of the store's own is `design_look` (store only, `website:write`, ungated): the brand's accent (and, optionally, its background, text colour and mode), fonts from the catalogue (`findFont()`, installed first) and Design settings' named choices are laid over the live look or a built-in template by `designedTheme()` (`src/lib/brand-design.ts`, pure), which works out both palettes and moves every pair `themeWarnings()` reads to 4.5:1 (main text 7:1, an accent on the dark background 3:1); it
is added by `addOwnLook()` as an install that is not live (`preset_id` null) with the live look's header, footer, product page and CSS, checked by `checkStoreTheme()` and audited `store.theme_designed`, then tried with `try_theme` and made live with `make_theme_live` (free, the owner's yes or the autopilot at the Design level).
Step F2a: `make_brand_marks` (both scopes, `website:write`, ungated: nothing reaches visitors) draws the logo for light and dark backgrounds and the icon by code, never by the picture model: the brand's name in its font's own outlines (Google Fonts' TrueType file, asked of `fonts.googleapis.com` and fetched only from `fonts.gstatic.com` by `fontOutlines()`, read by `opentype.js`), a missing letter refused, an SVG of paths and fills only (`pathOk()`), coloured from the live theme at 3:1
(`markColour()`, `iconInk()`), rendered by sharp and kept in the media library; `set_header_footer` takes `icon` (a square library picture of at least 256 px with its small copy).
Step F2b: the brand reaches the page studio (`siteFacts().brand`), the product writer (new words, not translations), broadcasts' suggestions and the chat agent (voice and words to avoid only) as guidance below each prompt's rules (`brandGuide()`, `brandVoiceGuide()`), never as facts; its words to avoid are found by code (`findAvoided()`, whole words in any case) and treated as claims: left out of a page's sentences, a broadcast suggestion refused, a product suggestion asked again once and its
remaining words flagged in the editor and left out by `write_product_texts`.
