# D452: Product safety

**Product safety: EU and EEA apart, a rule per destination (3 November 2026, `docs/product-safety-eea.md`).** EU and EEA membership are two classifications in `commerce.country_memberships` (the 27 EU states in both; Norway, Iceland and Liechtenstein in the EEA only; neither worked out from the other).
What the GPSR asks of a manufacturer established elsewhere is configured per destination in `commerce.product_safety_rules` (`eu`, `eea` or `none`, with its status, legal basis, source and day checked), and a product needs a responsible person established in each area the store's countries ask for: an EU one wherever it sells in the EU, never one in an EEA EFTA state instead.
Seeded: the EU states `eu`, in force; NO, IS and LI `none`, not incorporated (EEA-Lex: under scrutiny; Norway's EEA note of 27 January 2026: a change in law and the Storting's consent needed). One rule in `src/lib/product-safety.ts` and the publish trigger (`commerce.responsible_person_areas()`), tested on Norwegian, Icelandic and Liechtenstein makers selling into the EU, the EEA and both, before and after incorporation.
A store selling only to the EEA EFTA states is no longer asked for an EU responsible person. A rule changes by a migration of its row (section 4). Migrations `20261103232029_product_safety_tables` and `20261103232105_product_safety_rules`, applied by CI #951 (`9f5cfc7`); production checked after: 27 EU, 3 EEA-only, NO/IS/LI `none`, no country whose `in_eu` differs; advisors INFO only.
