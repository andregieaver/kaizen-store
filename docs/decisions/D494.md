# D494: Invoices brought in from Tripletex into Work

**Invoices brought in from Tripletex into Work (8 November 2026, `src/lib/tripletex-invoices.ts`, `src/server/tripletex-invoices.ts`, the Tripletex page's *Your invoices in Tripletex*).** A store connected to Tripletex brings the invoices it made in Tripletex into Work, with their lines, clients and payments, so each client's history is in one place.
On the Tripletex page the owner picks a first day (ten years back by default) and presses *See what comes in*: Tripletex's invoice list is read (`GET /invoice` with each invoice's customer, order lines and postings, 100 a page, at most 5 000) and the page says what would come in (*12 invoices to bring in, 3 June 2016 to 16 September 2026 · NOK 312,500.00*, how many paid, voided, new clients, clients filled in, already here, left out and why); *Bring in 12 invoices* reads and plans again and
writes it in one transaction that says it is importing (the Work rules let only such a transaction make imported invoices).
What comes in is history as Life's invoices were (WP15): an *imported* invoice, issued, with Tripletex's own number, lines and amounts, never numbered from the store's series, never emailed, no D41 event, never sent back to Tripletex (the sync and Fiken's leave imported invoices out) and never posted to the ledger; a payment Tripletex has is one payment row (method other, reference Tripletex, dated by the invoice's last payment posting, else its due day, never after today), so its status follows
the money. A line is kept as Tripletex has it (hours when its unit is hours, its discount, its VAT rate; a 0 % line is reverse charge for a foreign business, outside the scope for codes 6 and 7, else exempt); an invoice with a discount line or a line that cannot be read is one line per VAT rate.
A Tripletex customer is the Work client with its organisation number, else the one with its name (without `AS`, `ENK` and the like, and only a client with no other organisation number); a client found so gets what it lacks (organisation number, email, phone, address), never more; any other customer becomes a new client from the card; and every client is linked to its Tripletex card in the sync's own mapping (`adoptTripletexCustomers()`, with the hash of the card the sync would send), so the sync
neither makes a second card nor sends ours over the accountant's. Nothing comes twice: an invoice here with Tripletex's number is the same invoice, and so is one of Life's without a number for the same client and amount within a week of Tripletex's date; that one gets Tripletex's number (a new migration lets an imported invoice without a number take one, once, only while importing) and the payment Tripletex has.
A credit note for the whole of an invoice read makes that invoice void; one for part of it, or for an invoice before the first day, is left out and named, as is an invoice whose number another here has. Every id is uuid v5 of Tripletex's own in the store's namespace and the company's, so a second press adds nothing. The ledger now leaves out the payment of an imported invoice issued after its book opened (that invoice is in Tripletex's books, not these).
Owners only, audited `integration.tripletex_invoices_imported`; Work's settings link to it beside Tripletex. The platform's house store has it as any store connected to Tripletex. Not yet: credit notes in part, invoices in Fiken.
