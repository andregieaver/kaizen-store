# D348: Targets of every kind, alert emails and a briefing

**Targets of every kind, alert emails and a briefing (wave 8 unit D, `docs/wave-8-analytics-ai.md` section 4, D, with its detail); built.** A target (`analytics_targets`, migration `analytics_targets_more`) is of net sales, paid orders, the conversion rate (basis points) or the average order value, over a week, a month, a quarter or chosen days up to two years, one per metric, period and first day, its bounds checked by the database; the first form (`month`, `revenue_target_minor`) stays for a
monthly net-sales target and a trigger keeps the two forms in step, so the code running during the deploy kept working. Progress is `periodProgress()` (sums against the weekday-weighted expectation, levels against the target with minimums before a verdict, conversion not known without visits on every day), set on Analytics → Settings and by the AI manager's `set_target` (ungated, `analytics:write`), read by `list_targets` and drawn on the Overview under the month's own card.
The forecast covers the month's paid orders as well as its revenue (`forecastMonth()` over orders per day).
Two existing pieces were built on rather than beside, as the detail says: alert emails are the staff alert event `analytics` (chosen per member on Staff alerts, in no one's defaults, decision 5), sent by `sendAnalyticsAlertEmails()` once per store day from 20:00 store time, each alert once (`analytics_alert_sends`, claimed first), at or above the store's floor (`staff_alert_settings.alert_floor`), never good news; the briefing is D238's staff summary grown (the period before beside each figure,
the alerts, the store checkup's findings through the new `checkupFindings()`, the targets, an optional note through `checkedNote()`, feature `analytics_briefing`, each item linking to its page) and sends nothing for a quiet period (no paid order and no alert).
Home offers it once to an owner (*Weekly*, *Daily*, *Not now*: `turnOnBriefing()`, `dismissBriefingOffer()`), and each alert on Home and the Overview has *Ask the AI manager*, which opens the panel with a first message written by code from the alert (`askAboutAlert()`).
