# D500: A lost invoice, written off

**A lost invoice, written off (8 November 2026, `work_invoice_losses`, `markInvoiceLost()` and `reverseInvoiceLoss()` in `src/server/work-invoices.ts`, `entryOfInvoiceLoss()` in `src/lib/ledger.ts`, *Write off as lost* on an issued Work invoice).** The owner's: *I have to be able to mark some invoices as lost*. Human Web has 30 invoices unpaid from 2023 to 2026; some will never be paid.
On an issued invoice with something outstanding, owners only, *Write off as lost* takes the amount (all that is outstanding unless less), the day (from the invoice's day to today) and why; the words say it is for a loss that is established (*konstatert*: the client bankrupt, collection failed), since the books take the VAT back.
A loss is a row of its own (`commerce.work_invoice_losses`, append-only, in the invoice's currency, never more than is outstanding: the database's `work_loss_guard()`); what is outstanding is now the total less payments, credit notes and losses (`work_invoice_amounts()` keeps its columns; `work_invoice_lost()` beside it), and the invoice is **`lost`**, a new status, when they cover it and something is lost, `sent` again when a loss is taken back (*Take back*: a negative row taking back one loss
in full, once, today; for a mistake, or money that came after all), each with its history entry (`loss.written_off`, `invoice.lost`, `loss.reversed`).
Posted by the ledger's job in the manual series on the day written off: the receivable credited; the amount shared over the invoice's VAT rates as its lines were; each share's VAT taken back as a deduction on its rate's purchase code and input VAT account (25 % code 1 on 2710, 15 % code 11 on 2711, 12 % code 13 on 2713; no VAT, or a rate without a code, none), the rest a loss on 7830 under the same code, because **Skatteetaten has a lost receivable's VAT reported as a deduction with the
specification `tapPåKrav`, on codes 1, 11, 12 or 13 only** (its validation rule R038 and example `tappåkrav.xml`, github.com/Skatteetaten/mva-meldingen), not as a minus on the sales code. VAT by code keeps a loss's lines as a row of their own (`spec`), and the VAT return writes it as its own line with `<spesifikasjon>tapPåKrav</spesifikasjon>` (the comparison with Tripletex adds a code's rows together); a loss taken back turns the loss's own entry with its codes, so the deduction goes too.
The SAF-T file shows the loss's lines with their VAT like any other. Audited `work.invoice.written_off`, `work.invoice.loss_reversed`. Not yet: a provision for doubtful receivables (*avsetning til tap*, no VAT) before a loss is established; money received on a receivable already written off (*inngått på tidligere avskrevne fordringer*) other than by taking the loss back first; a loss on a shop invoice; a filter for lost invoices in Work's list.
