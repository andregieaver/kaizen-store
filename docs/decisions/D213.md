# D213: Pictures and videos compressed in the browser until they are as small as they can be while they look the same

**Pictures and videos compressed in the browser until they are as small as they can be while they look the same (`docs/media-compression.md`).** Pictures were written at a fixed 85 % and videos uploaded as they were.
Now `compressPicture()` searches the lowest quality whose result passes `looksTheSame()` (SSIM on brightness and colour, measured as seen at half size with a full-size floor; the bar set on photos, a screenshot and a graphic and checked by eye), WebP or, for page pictures, AVIF from a WebAssembly encoder when a tenth smaller; `compressVideo()` re-encodes to at most 1080p H.264 with the browser's WebCodecs (mediabunny), dropping a background's sound, kept only at 80 % or less.
Uploads say what they saved. `media.compressed_at` marks a compressed file; the media library's Compress makes older files smaller one or all at a time, uploads the result beside the original (addresses are cached for a year), moves every editable use to it in one transaction and deletes the original unless the file left the site or a document names it. No new service; nothing leaves the browser but the result.
