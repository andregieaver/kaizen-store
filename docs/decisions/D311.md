# D311: Legal pages are written and kept current by the AI, without a person reviewing each text

**Legal pages are written and kept current by the AI, without a person reviewing each text (decided; built in `docs/ai-autopilot.md` unit L).** This reverses D158's rule that a store's legal pages are hand-written starters and never a model's words. The owner: a strong agent can research and compile legal texts better and more accurately than most store owners or traditional legal teams.
They stay right by how they are made: written from official sources fetched by code (never from a model's memory), each section's quote checked word for word against the fetched text, every fact checked against the store's own settings and details, every required topic in every language, the claims filter, written again when a source or a store fact changes, and every version kept with its sources.
The owner's part is one choice when it is turned on (the store, as the seller, stays responsible for its pages); the hand-written starters remain the fallback. The product's own consumer texts (the withdrawal function, emails, checkout words, `HAND_WRITTEN_ONLY`) are not part of this decision.
The owner's choices on the spec (2026-10-25, `docs/ai-autopilot.md` 8.9): pages are published once they pass the checks (`auto` by default), each says it was prepared with AI from the sources it lists, and sellers in Norway, Sweden and Denmark in nb, sv, da and en come first.
L1 (the sources) is built: EU law from the Publications Office's Cellar (the newest consolidated version), Swedish from Riksdagen's open data, Danish from Retsinformation's XML, Norwegian from Lovdata's open data (NLOD 2.0; it answers, so the recorded passages the owner agreed to as a stopgap were not needed), each read weekly and kept with its version (`src/server/legal-sources.ts`).
L2 (the writer) is built: *Write with AI* on the Legal page makes a draft of one kind in the store's languages, the model writing sentences only, every fact filled and every quote checked by code (`docs/ai-autopilot.md` 8.11); tested with a stand-in model, not yet with a real one.
L3 (kept current) is built: the owner turns it on once with the sentence and the publish choice, the pages are written into their places and published when they pass, a person's page is never written over (a draft beside it until handed back), and the daily upkeep writes a page again when a store fact it uses or a passage it cites changes (`docs/ai-autopilot.md` 8.12).
