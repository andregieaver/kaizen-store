# D281: Kinetic, a signature theme for a sports brand, with women and men athletes in its pictures

**Kinetic, a signature theme for a sports brand, with women and men athletes in its pictures.** Made from scratch as the four before it (`src/lib/signature/kinetic.ts`, `docs/signature-themes.md`): its own header (over a full-screen hero on the front page), footer, product page and stylesheet, bold italic capitals in Barlow Condensed, tartan-track red on chalk white, presets *Night run* (dark, volt) and *Glacier*, eight sections, and its own demo store, *Halden Athletic* (pack `sports`): running
and training wear for women and for men, shoes and gear. Every picture of its pages has a photographer's brief, and the scene pictures show athletes of both sexes: a woman and a man sprinting in the hero, a club of women and men in the story, a female runner on the Women picture, a male sprinter on the Men picture, a woman and a man on the floodlit track, their faces shown (D282).
The art standing in until the photographs are made (`scripts/theme-art/kinetic.js`) draws the same scenes with pictogram runners, a woman with a ponytail and a man, no faces.
The hero's outline button and eyebrow are light over the picture, the footer redefines the palette for its dark band, and on phones the hero's words sit on a soft shade; a word too long for a phone breaks in a heading rather than running off the screen (the theme switch test found *PRODUKTER* 2 px too wide in the fallback font a store sees before its fonts load).

Date: 2026-10-18

The owner: "Programmatically create a new theme for a sports brand. Make sure that images has male and female athletes in them." `signatureCheck()` passes (its layouts, CSS, buy box in the first row, art and a brief for every picture, sections, no claims), as do `themeCheck()` in light, dark and both presets and the packs' and themes' integration tests; looked at by eye at a computer's and a phone's width on a local build.
Reaches production as the others did, on `/admin/platform/themes`: *Add and update Kaizena Flow's themes*, then *Bring it up to date* on the *Starting content* card (the pack sync; *Sync content packs* is its old name), then *Make the pictures* on Halden Athletic's row, a look at them, and Publish.
