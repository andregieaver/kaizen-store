# D914: A pricing table shows all plans or the plans of one product category

Date: 2026-11-27

**A store's pricing table (the Plans component) shows every plan the store sells, or, chosen in the component's settings, only the plans in one
product category or a category under it, so a store selling plans for more than one product gives each product a pricing table of its own; and a
product save keeps a variant that comes back without its id, by its SKU, instead of inserting a second of itself.** Asked for by the owner for
Kaizena Life: "how are actually these plans grouped together in a pricing table block? ... I need to be able to launch plans for other products
without them being grouped with this one", and "I have only one Pro plan. However, im told that these skus are already in use?"

- **Which plans:** `PlansBlock.categoryId` (a product category of the store); none shows every plan, as before. `getStorePlans()` gives each plan
  its categories and every category above them (`StorePlan.categoryIds`), and `plansOfCategory()` keeps the plans of the chosen one, cards,
  comparison and cart button alike. The builder offers *Plans to show* only where a category holds a plan (`planGroups()`, Homer sees nothing new
  until he files plans in categories), and *Plan to recommend* lists the chosen category's plans. Categories, not a new grouping: a store already
  files products in them, and a plan in a category is filed like any product. A copied template lets the category go, as it does the
  recommended plan (`template-content.ts`).
- **A variant without its id:** `saveVariants()` takes a variant sent without its id (an editor holding a copy made before a save, or one that
  made its variants again, such as choosing *A plan* again on a plan without a Billing option) whose SKU one of the same product's variants has,
  and that the save does not name by id, as that variant. Before, it was inserted beside itself and the save failed on the store's unique SKUs
  ("Another product already uses one of these SKUs"). A switched-off variant whose SKU comes back is switched on again the same way.
- **The platform:** Kaizena Flow's own pricing table shows its plans (`commerce.plans`), one set with nothing to choose between; the setting
  is a store's only, and Kaizena Flow's own store sells plans through the platform's table, not plan products.

Idle cost: none. The pricing table's cached read adds one small recursive read of a plan's categories per plan; the save reads its variants' SKUs
with the read it already made.
