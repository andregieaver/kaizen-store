# D158: Legal starters, terms at checkout, accessibility checks, a strict checkout policy, and staff security

**Legal starters, terms at checkout, accessibility checks, a strict checkout policy, and staff security (two-step sign-in, roles, activity log).** (1e) A store makes draft starter pages for terms, privacy, returns, shipping, withdrawal information, an imprint and an accessibility statement in nb, sv, da and en from its own facts (`legalFacts()`, `src/lib/legal-starters/*`), hand-written, each with a review notice and `[[placeholders]]`; they are published only by the owner and are linked roles
(`LEGAL_ROLES`, in `page_roles`, never A/B tested). Checkout shows a terms and privacy link or a required checkbox (`stores.terms_at_checkout`); the pay press records `order_terms` with immutable `legal_snapshots`. The builder checks a draft (alt text, empty links, heading order, contrast, placeholders in every language) and asks before publishing with a blocking issue, recording the choice; CI scans the storefront and the admin's signed-out pages with axe.
The cart, checkout and order routes send a Content-Security-Policy (`src/lib/csp.ts`, with `unsafe-inline` for scripts and Stripe's origins including maps.googleapis.com), draw no consent manager, owner code or chat widget (`OffPayRoutes`), are entered by full page loads, and refuse an HTML block on the checkout page. (1f) TOTP through Supabase Auth with our own single-use recovery codes, required of platform admins and, by the owner's switch, of a store's members; `getAccount()` fails closed
and reads enrolment from the signed claim or a server-side `getUser()`. Roles are `store_roles` of `{section}:{read|write}` keys; every admin page, action and route asks a guard (scan tests against a baseline); `staff:write`, `billing:write` and owner-only pages stay the owner's; collaborators are members with a role and an expiry. `audit_log` gains area, target and allowlisted changes, has a filtered page and an owner's CSV, and is pruned at 24 months. *Why:* Asked for by the owner (wave 1).
Texts need legal review, the accessibility audit and SAQ A are the company's, passkeys and native recovery codes wait. Rating: legal, accessibility and PCI partial (bucket D), two-step partial until the platform admin's enrolment in production is recorded; roles and audit log Full. The two roles rows are one row after the merge (`platform.staff-accounts-and-roles`, Partial until a real two-step enrolment is recorded).
Migrations: `legal_terms`, `legal_terms_rules`, `staff_security`, `staff_security_rules`, `store_role_templates`, `plan_features_wave1`.
