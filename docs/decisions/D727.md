# D727: A sending domain per store, through Resend's EU region, with its records written where we keep the DNS

**A sending domain per store, through Resend's EU region, with its records written where we keep the DNS (email plan M1.1; 17 November 2026, `src/lib/sending-domains.ts`, `src/server/sending-domains.ts`, `SendingDomainCard`, migrations `20261117153106_broad_vulcan`, `20261117153118_sending_domains_rules`).** Every store sent from Kaizena Flow's one address with its own name (D26), so all stores shared one sender reputation and none showed its own address.
`commerce.sending_domains` holds at most one domain a store (and Kaizena Flow's own with no store): added to Resend in `eu-west-1`, its DKIM, SPF and return-path records written into the store's zone through `applyDnsChange()` when Kaizena Flow keeps its DNS (D315), else shown to copy; Resend's tracking record is never used (we count clicks ourselves, D297).
Only a `verified` domain sends: `senderFor()` gives `{from_local}@{domain}` to `sendEmail()` and `runBroadcasts()`, and an email Resend refuses with it is sent again from the shared address. The daily job `daily.sending-domains` asks again about pending and failed domains and each week about verified ones.
The card *Send from your own address* is on Settings, Notifications (one field, the store's own domain filled in; the address and stopping one step away) and on Platform, Emails for Kaizena Flow's own.

Date: 2026-11-17

Adding a domain needs `RESEND_API_KEY` to have full access in Resend; a sending-only key is refused in plain words, and nothing else changes until a domain is added. Not yet: the AI manager's tool for it and a domain-health finding for an unverified sending domain (the plan's next steps).
