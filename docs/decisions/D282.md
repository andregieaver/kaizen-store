# D282: A pack photograph of people shows their faces, as invented models

**A pack photograph of people shows their faces, as invented models.** Every pack picture was told *No recognisable faces* (`PICTURE_RULES`), so Kinetic's first briefs framed the athletes from behind or in shadow.
A `PackPhoto` may now be marked `people`: its prompt keeps the rule against any text, letter, number, logo or watermark (`NO_TEXT`) and asks for natural, expressive faces of invented models, never a real or famous person, in plain clothing without branding (`PEOPLE_RULES`); every other pack picture keeps *no recognisable faces*.
Kinetic's hero, story, Women, Men and floodlit pictures are such photographs, briefed from the front or three-quarter with the athletes' faces focused mid-stride; its products and gear show no one. The pictures are made by the platform's picture model with *Make the pictures*, as every pack's (D270, D271).

Date: 2026-10-18

The owner: "Not faceless athletes, we need actual athletes with faces in action. We need the app's image model to handle the image creation like always." `content-packs.test.ts` holds Kinetic's people photographs to women and men, faces in the brief and none turned away, and every other picture to the old rule.
