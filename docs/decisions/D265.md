# D265: Three highlight pictures per theme, composed from its photographs, with words a person writes or accepts

**Three highlight pictures per theme, composed from its photographs, with words a person writes or accepts (the theme showcase plan, phase 2 step 2, `docs/theme-showcase-phase-2.md`).** Once a theme's own look is photographed at both widths, the shots' job composes *Devices* (the computer and two phones, the second at the product), *Collage* (the page's sections in two staggered columns) and *Close-up* (the product part, photographed at twice the pixels) on a backdrop of the theme's own colours,
all drawn by code with no words in the pictures. Their words are part of the theme's listing: written on its admin page, or suggested by Kaizena Flow's text model from the theme's facts only and kept only when they pass the claims filter and make no superlative, never saved until a person saves them; the launch themes' are written by hand. The theme page shows them under its previews.
