# D632: Retention the AI writes, step 1

**Retention the AI writes, step 1: automations' words and days, revenue after each email, text messages (`docs/100-day-challenge.md` D4; 15 November 2026, `src/server/marketing-tools.ts`, `src/server/automations.ts`).** Four owner tools.
`write_email_automation` (gate `send`, area marketing) has the store's own broadcast writer (`suggestBroadcast()`, given the kind's readers as its audience instead of a segment: who it reaches, when, and for after a purchase that it does not know what they bought) write a lifecycle automation's words in the store's main language from a brief, and saves them with its days through `saveAutomation()`; other languages keep their words, and it never switches the automation (`switch_email_automation`
does). The claims filter and the brand's words to avoid refuse a draft as for broadcasts; days outside the kind's range are refused before the call is kept for a yes. `list_email_automations` and the Automations page now say what followed each kind's emails: the paid orders the reader's address placed within `ORDER_AFTER_DAYS` (7) of an email sent in the last 30 days, each order counted once for the latest email before it, per currency.
It says what followed, not what the email caused, and the tool says so.
Text messages (D565) get `list_text_messages` (read: connected, the month's spend against the cap, subscribers, the latest messages with delivered and cost, never a number), `draft_text_message` (ungated: a draft in the manager's words through `saveSmsCampaign()`, refused on a claim, a word the brand avoids or more than `SMS_PARTS_MAX` parts) and `send_text_message` (gate `send`, NEVER_AUTOMATIC: every text costs money and none can be called back, so in the challenge too a person reads it first;
the preflight prices it with 46elks and refuses no recipients or a send past the cap). Broadcasts within the challenge's budget were A2 already. With them the two largest areas asked together (marketing 45, website 41, the core tools and the manager's own 28: 127) no longer fit 124 tools a round, so `TOOLS_PER_TURN` is 128, the providers' own limit (D333): every tool a caller adds is counted in `always`, so no round sends more; the next time they outgrow it an area is split instead.
