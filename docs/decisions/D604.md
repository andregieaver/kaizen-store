# D604: Challenge mode

**Challenge mode: the autopilot runs a store alone for the 100-day challenge (`docs/100-day-challenge.md` 4A1, 4A2 and F2; 14 November 2026, `src/lib/autopilot-challenge.ts`, `saveChallenge()` in `src/server/autopilot-settings.ts`, migration `20261114202420_overrated_whirlwind`).** `autopilot_settings.challenge` holds a store's challenge: when challenge mode comes on (the rehearsal's first day), Day 1, the last day, and its own budgets (broadcasts a week, support answers a day, the AI a month,
the largest price change, the deepest offer, the largest ad raise). Only a platform admin writes it (`saveChallenge()`, `endChallenge()`, audited `ai.challenge_*`), since the store's owner is a contestant.
While it is on, `autonomyFor()` returns the challenge's preset over the owner's settings (`withChallenge()`): products, website, marketing, messages and money at *Just do it*, design and setup at *Do it and tell me*, plans that start themselves, goals reviewed every three days, ten plans a week, reactions on and a waiting customer looked at after five minutes.
The owner's settings are locked: a save is refused with the day they open again, except the pause, which stays theirs and is audited as `ai.challenge_paused` with `intervention: true`. A broadcast, otherwise never automatic (D334), runs within the challenge's broadcasts a week; an answer on the support desk meets a daily budget of its own instead of the weekly email budget; refunds and every other money back to a customer still wait for the person.
A step that fails three times is skipped with its problem instead of becoming a question, and a plan's question step is skipped, since nobody answers in the challenge.
Two changes reach every store, challenge or not: a plan the month's AI budget paused is marked (`assistant_plans.paused_for = 'budget'`) and the loop starts it again once the budget allows (`resumeAfterBudget()`), never a plan a person paused; and the autopilot's own conversations (a plan's, a review's, the events') are `source = 'autopilot'`, whose turns count against `AUTOPILOT_DAILY_TURNS` (1,000 a store a day) apart from the owner's 300, so a busy autopilot never leaves the owner without the
AI manager. Events are limited per kind a day (48 waiting customers, 12 tests ended, 12 alerts) instead of 12 in all. The Autopilot page says when the challenge or its rehearsal is on, and shows the locked settings without sending them. Tests: `autopilot-challenge.test.ts`, `autopilot-challenge.int.test.ts`. Not yet: a screen for platform admins to set the challenge (the challenge console, H2), the store's own time zone for the dates (UTC now).
