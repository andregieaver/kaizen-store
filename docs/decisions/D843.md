# D843: No scheduled job fails in silence: report() for caught errors, trackedWork() for work after a route's answer (bloat review step 1.26)

**No scheduled job fails in silence (20 November 2026).** A job that caught an error so its other parts could go on left the ledger saying
ok (finding GAP-scheduled-s2): the scan found 99 such catches in the entry functions of 58 of the dispatcher's 80 jobs (the review counted
17 in 11; a catch that only logs counts too, since the run still read ok). `tracked()` now hands each job a `report(error, part)`
(`src/server/job-report.ts`): logged as `cron.job_part_failed`, and the run counted failed once its other parts ran, still answering what
they did. It is also kept in scope as `inJob()` keeps the job's name, so an entry function reads it with `const report = jobReport();`
(`logOnly` outside a run) and no job's signature or dispatcher call changes; the step's brief passed it as a parameter, which would have
changed 58 signatures, many with positional ones. Each of the 99 catches reports, keeping its own log line with its ids. A business outcome
in a catch that also meets faults stays quiet, the catch reporting only the other errors: a declined card or Stripe's refusal (theme and
domain purchases, host commissions), a registrar's refusal, an account Instagram refuses. Four normal states are named in the scan with
their reasons (the owner's choice, 20 November 2026: kept out, named): a status probe finding a part down, an owner's website or page the
chat widget cannot read, and a domain that does not answer the watch's probe or registry. The work seven routes do after answering
(autopilot, page-jobs, broadcasts, pack-pictures, page-looks, theme-shots, cookie-scan) runs inside `trackedWork("route.<name>", …)`,
kept as `route.<name>.work`, which `cronHealth()` never calls late (it runs only when there is work), only ok or failing.
`cron-entry-catches.scan.test.ts` holds both: a new silent catch fails it with its job, file and line, and so does a named normal state
no catch has any more. Jobs that read failing after the deploy are failures that were hidden; they are listed in the step's row, not
silenced. Proposed for CLAUDE.md's Conventions (not edited): "a job reports what it catches (`jobReport()`)".
