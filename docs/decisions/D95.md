# D95: Kaizen Store and Kaizen Life sign each other's users in

**Kaizen Store and Kaizen Life sign each other's users in: each Supabase project is the other's OpenID Connect provider.** Both projects switch on Supabase's OAuth 2.1 server (beta), register the other as a confidential client, and add it as a custom OIDC provider (`custom:kaizen-life` here, `custom:kaizen-store` there).
Here: "Sign in with Kaizen Life" on `/admin/sign-in` (shown once `KAIZEN_LIFE_SSO=on`) admits only an account that owns a store or runs the platform (`admitFromKaizenLife()`: staff and hosts are told to use their email; someone with no account is sent to `/sign-up`, filled in with what Kaizen Life knows, into the usual approval queue).
Accounts are matched by the email Kaizen Life has verified, as for any sign-in link; an owner whose Kaizen Life account uses another email connects it under Your account (Supabase manual identity linking), and can disconnect it and remove apps they signed in to with Kaizen Store (`listGrants`/`revokeGrant`).
When Kaizen Life (or any registered app) asks to sign someone in with Kaizen Store, Supabase sends them to `/admin/oauth/consent`: signed in first if need be (`next` is kept through the sign-in form), owners only, and it says the app learns who they are and nothing of their stores.
On Kaizen Life (its own repository): "Sign in with Kaizen Store" on its login, through its existing OAuth helper, so its phone app opens it in the system browser and returns through its app link; its consent page at `/app/oauth/consent`; and Settings → Connected apps. Signing in shares only identity; the assistants talking to each other is a separate, explicit connection (D96).
