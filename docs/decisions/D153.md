# D153: The EU withdrawal function and a returns flow for goods

**The EU withdrawal function and a returns flow for goods.** A shopper withdraws from a contract at `/s/{store}/{market}/withdraw` (linked from every footer, order page and order email) in the two steps the directive asks for (*Withdraw from contract here*, then *Confirm withdrawal*), without giving a reason; the order number and email must match, the reply is the same whether they do or not, and nothing is a withdrawal until the second button is pressed (an unconfirmed request expires after 24
hours). Confirming records the statement, creates a return (`returns`, status `approved`) for the lines declared and emails the acknowledgement on a durable medium (the store, order, lines, date and time with offset, reference, what happens next), kept in `email_messages`; if the email fails the withdrawal still stands and staff see *acknowledgement not sent* with *Send again*.
Per line, `lineEligibility()` (`src/lib/withdrawal.ts`) says whether the right applies and, if not, why in plain words: excluded by law, digital content with consent, business orders, a booking, already returned. The 14 days count only from a recorded receipt (`orders.delivered_at`, set by staff with *Mark received by the customer*): until then the right is open and no end day is shown.
A store can offer a longer voluntary window (`return_settings.window_days`), whose part beyond 14 days is a *return request* it may decline.
Staff work `/admin/{store}/returns` (queue with refund-due and overdue marks) and a detail page: approve or decline a voluntary return, instructions and return address, mark in transit and received, inspect per line (condition, restock, a deduction for diminished value that only inspection can make, with its note), then refund through the existing `refundOrder()` with the amount from `refundFor()` (what the shopper paid for the units after every discount kind, plus the original standard delivery
only when the whole order is withdrawn, minus return shipping when the shopper pays it; a withdrawal's refund can be raised by staff but not lowered). The refund is claimed on the return before Stripe is called, so two staff cannot pay twice.
Settings are the store's (`return_settings`, `/admin/{store}/settings/returns`, owners): window, transit estimate, who pays return shipping (kept with each order when it is placed, `orders.return_cost_payer`, and the standard delivery cost, `orders.standard_shipping_minor`), when the refund is made, excluded goods, instructions (translatable) and address. Staff can also register a withdrawal received outside the function.
Returns appear in analytics (rate, reasons, products, time to refund, with minimum volumes), in the control center and store Home attention (overdue refunds, acknowledgements not sent), in the AI manager (`list_returns`, `explain_return`, and gated `approve_return`/`decline_return`) and in the store's structured return policy.

Date: 2026-10-03

Asked for by the owner as the first step to Shopify parity (`docs/shopify-parity.md`, gaps 1 and 11): the directive has applied since 19 June 2026 and `docs/plan.md` calls it a launch blocker. The legal act (the confirmed statement) is kept apart from the physical return, so a failing email or a slow refund never undoes a withdrawal.
Matching without enumeration, limits counted in the database by hashed keys (never IP or cookie), and a withdrawal function that sets no cookie or storage keep it inside the privacy rules.
A review by three adversaries (law, security and privacy, money) found 17 defects before shipping, all fixed with regression tests; the open points that need a lawyer (information given before the contract, services, sealed goods, receipt, deductions) are listed in `docs/returns.md` and every consumer text in Norwegian, Swedish, Danish and English needs human review. Not yet: return labels, exchanges, defect complaints, photos, ending subscriptions, and a withdrawal page the builder can edit.
