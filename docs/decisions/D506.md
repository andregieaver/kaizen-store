# D506: The year-end

**The year-end: a year closed, and its figures for the tax return (B5 step 3, 9 November 2026, `src/lib/year-end.ts`, `src/server/year-end.ts`, `YearEndCard` on Settings → Books, `/admin/{store}/settings/books/naering/{year}`).** The owner chose it next, to finish getting 2026 in order. The card shows the first year of the book not closed that has ended, first on the screen (from 1 January, for 2026 until it is closed), else the year under way, lower, to get ready for.
Its list: the year is over; nothing waits to be posted; every bank line of the year is in the books; the bank's own balance on a statement covering 31 December agrees with the books (so the statement covering the year's end must be uploaded); and, only to look at, what customers still owe (write off the lost ones first, D500) and what is owed to suppliers.
With the list done, *Close {year}* (the owner's, what it does said first) posts the year's result to other equity on 31 December, *8960 Overføringer annen egenkapital* against *2050 Annen egenkapital* (NS 4102; a loss the same way), as the year's one standing entry (`source year_end/{year}`, which `ledger_post()` keeps once), and locks the book through the day; a lock already on that day is stepped back for the entry and put back, one in a later year refuses. *Reopen {year}* (one step away)
moves the lock back to the year before and reverses the closing entry, so the year can be put right and closed again. Audited `work.ledger_year_closed`, `_reopened`.
The year's figures by the codes of Skatteetaten's business specification (*næringsspesifikasjon*, the same codes the SAF-T file groups each account under, D497): the profit and loss by code, and the balance on 31 December by code, a result not yet carried to equity counted on other equity so it sums as the books do; shown folded away and downloaded as a spreadsheet for the tax return (filed by hand, or by the accountant, until Skatteetaten's API access).
A sole proprietorship's tax is the owner's, so nothing is posted for tax; a company's tax cost (8300 against 2500), depreciation and private withdrawals are not part of this step: Human Web's first company year-end is 2027's. No migration. Kaizena Flow's own store has the same (D466).
