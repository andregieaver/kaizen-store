# D609: The Meta ad account's spend goes into the books by itself

**The Meta ad account's spend goes into the books by itself (`docs/100-day-challenge.md` D2; 14 November 2026, `syncAdSpend()` and `syncAllAdSpend()` in `src/server/ad-accounts.ts`, `dailySpendRows()` in `src/lib/ad-budgets.ts`, migration `20261114202420_overrated_whirlwind`).** Until now the autopilot could raise Meta budgets (D585) while what the ads cost stayed out of contribution profit unless a person typed it.
Every six hours (`ad_accounts.spend_synced_at`, the dispatcher's job `adSpend`, ten accounts a run) each connected account's spend per campaign and day of the last seven days (Meta corrects a day for a while) is read with Meta's insights (`time_increment=1`) and written as marketing spend: channel `paid_social`, the campaign by Meta's name, the amount in the store's main currency (`toMainOne()`; a day with no rate waits for a later run).
The rows are only its own (`note` *From the Meta ad account*): a campaign that turns out to have spent nothing loses its row, a person's own rows are never written over or removed, and a hand-typed total for `paid_social` beside them would count twice (the Marketing page shows both). Contribution profit, ROAS, MER and `profit_overview`'s profit per krone of marketing now include the ads without anyone typing. Tests: `ad-spend.int.test.ts`.

Date: 2026-11-14

Unit 6 of the preparation for the challenge, judged on profit per krone of marketing. Platform: Kaizena Flow's own ad account is its house store's (D585), so its spend reaches the same books.
