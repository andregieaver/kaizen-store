# D476: Email on the timeline

**Email on the timeline (S5 of `docs/own-books-and-sales.md`; `src/lib/filed-email.ts`, `src/server/email-filing.ts`), step 1 built.** A store's filing address, `bcc-{key}@` the inbound domain support already uses (D332; `email_filing`, made the first time it is shown, under the Deals board for those who may change customers, left out of the store's export as a secret): staff copy (Bcc) or forward mail to it.
It arrives as every received email does (the Resend webhook, `support_inbound_emails` with `purpose` `filing`, `routeOf()`'s `filing` route), and `fileReceived()` files it: only mail sent from the address of a member of the store's team whose access has not ended, only while Deals is on, never an automatic mail or one that fails DMARC.
Its people are everyone it went to and those its forwarded headers name (From, To, Cc, also Fra, Til, Kopi; `participantsOf()`), less the team and the receiving domain; it is filed on those of them who are the store's customers, their companies and their open deals (`filed_emails`, with its words whole, forwarded part included, up to 20 000 characters; once per Message-ID). Mail about nobody the store knows is not kept.
It shows on each person's timeline as *Email filed* (kind email) with the start of its words. Kept for the email bodies' retention rule (12 months, `pruneFiledEmails()` with the inbound rows' daily prune), deleted with any of its people by an erasure, never copied, under the `sales` purpose. No AI reads it.
Tests: `filed-email.test.ts`, `email-filing.int.test.ts` (filed through the real inbound pipeline; refused from outside the team, about nobody, with Deals off; erased). *Step 2 (built):* a deal's and a company's page list the mail filed on them (`FiledEmailsCard`), newest first, each opened to be read whole as text; the deal's says where to send more.
