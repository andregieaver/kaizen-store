# D359: Self-serve sign-up

**Self-serve sign-up (wave 9 unit F, `docs/wave-9-platform.md` section 3, F); built.** A platform switch (`platform_settings.self_serve_signup`, off; Platform → Access requests, written only by `setSelfServe()`, audited `platform.self_serve_signup_set`).
While it is on, `/sign-up` and `/no/sign-up` say the store is made at once, and a sign-up (`startSelfSignup()`, `src/server/self-signup.ts`) is counted first, at most 3 an address a day and 20 a network an hour, as `commerce.chat_usage` rows under keyed hashes (`signupBucket()`, never the address or the network); within them the request is kept as ever (`createAccessRequest()`, merged with one pending for the address, without the "received" email), marked `self_serve` with a new link (32 random
bytes, kept only as its SHA-256 in `confirm_hash`, good 24 hours) and emailed (`platform.access_confirm`, hand-written en and nb, from `siteUrl()`, never the request's Origin). The answer is the same whether the sign-up was counted, limited or merged.
The link opens `/sign-up/confirm/{token}` (`noindex`, `no-referrer`), a page with a button, so a mail scanner opening it makes nothing; the press (`confirmSelfSignup()`) claims the link once (`confirmed_at`, the hash cleared) and, with the switch still on, makes the account and the store through `approveAccessRequest()` with no admin (so `commerce.approve_access_request()`, `decided_by` null; the design applied in the new owner's name), at a free address from the store's name (`freeSlug()`),
private until opened as every new store (D339), with its sign-in link emailed; it then sets `store_billing.trial_ends_at` 14 days on and audits `store.self_signup` in the owner's name.
The trial is shown on Billing, and the first plan chosen while it has two days or more left is first charged at its end (Stripe Checkout's `trial_end`, `trialEndFor()`); since D420 a trial that ends with no plan makes an open store private until a plan starts (`pauseAfterTrial()`), where this row first said its end stopped nothing. Off at the press, or when making the store fails, the confirmed request stays pending for a platform admin and the person is told.
Unconfirmed requests are not on the admin's list (counted beside the switch) and are deleted a week after their link ended (retention step `unconfirmed_signups`). No password is asked at sign-up: the account signs in by the emailed link and sets one on Your account. Payments stay as for any store: test mode at once (D20), live only after Stripe onboarding. `platform.self-serve-sign-up-and-onboarding` stays partial (see its row).
