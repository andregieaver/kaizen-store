# D445: Moving from Shopify

**Moving from Shopify: one button, nothing lost (proposed 3 November 2026, `docs/moving-from-shopify.md`; nothing built).** A Shopify shop is read through Kaizena Flow's own read-only Shopify app (Connect, Check, Copy, then Move) instead of five files, written only by the writers that already own each table (D165, D366, D384, D168, D300, D295), behind private mode with emails silent until the owner presses *Move my shop*; every old address is checked against the old sitemap, counts and order
totals are reconciled per currency, and the switch day, the DNS change and the 14 days after are guided.
Open for the owner: the email list from Shopify's consent record, gift cards reissued with new codes, history in analytics, the app listing, and which platform follows (section 13 of the plan). **Step 1 built (3 November 2026): the old shop's links.** Redirects → *Old shop* (`/admin/{store}/redirects/old-shop`): the owner types the old shop's address and its sitemap is read once through `safeFetch()` (`src/server/old-shop.ts`: `/sitemap.xml` or what robots.txt names, an index's files one by
one on the shop's own host, gzip read, at most 60 files and 20,000 addresses, a language folder before a known section taken off; only the sitemap, never the pages), kept in `commerce.old_shops` (one row a store, written only there, `never` in `COPY_RULES`, not personal); every view works out again where each address leads (`fateOf()` in `src/lib/old-shop.ts` by the resolver's own `followChain()`: live, a redirect to a page, a redirect to nowhere, nothing).
One press, *Fix {n} for me*, makes a redirect for each one whose same thing is live here under our shape (`counterpartOf()`: `/products/{h}` → `/p/{h}`, `/collections/{c}` → `/category/{c}`, `/pages/{p}` → `/{p}`, `/blogs/{b}/{a}` → `/blog/{a}`, WooCommerce's too) through `addRedirects()` (origin `import`), worked out again on the server; the rest get the 404 report's suggestions and its door (`redirectFromReport()`).
The old shop's counts by kind stand beside this store's (8.3, from the sitemap until the API reads Shopify). Audited `redirects.old_shop_read` and `redirects.old_shop_fixed`.
Migration `20261103144045_old_shops`. **Step 2 built (3 November 2026): Connect.** Settings → *Moving in* (owner's): any address of the shop (its `myshopify.com` name found through `/meta.json`, else `Shopify.shop` in its page) and *Connect Shopify* to Kaizena Flow's own read-only app (`shopify/shopify.app.toml`, `shopifyEnv()`: off without `SHOPIFY_CLIENT_ID` and `SHOPIFY_CLIENT_SECRET`); Shopify's authorization code grant with expiring offline tokens (`expiring=1`), the callback checked for
the sealed state, Shopify's HMAC, an anchored shop name and the same owner; the access and refresh tokens encrypted in `commerce.shop_moves` (written only by `src/server/shopify-connect.ts`), refreshed under the row's lock, dropped on a refused refresh, a 401, `app/uninstalled` or *Disconnect* (revoked at Shopify too), the row deleted on `shop/redact`; webhooks answer 401 without Shopify's HMAC. Once connected the page shows Shopify's counts and checks the old links in one press.
Audited `integration.shopify_connected` and `integration.shopify_disconnected`. The app itself, its two variables, `read_all_orders` and protected customer data are the owner's to set up with Shopify (`docs/moving-from-shopify.md` 17).
