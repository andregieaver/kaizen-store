# D300: Gift cards

**Gift cards (wave 5 unit F, `docs/wave-5-marketing.md` 8), built in four steps; F1 the model.** Multi-purpose vouchers only (Directive 2016/1065: no VAT when sold, the goods carry theirs when it is spent), flagged for an accountant's and a lawyer's look.
`commerce.gift_cards` (store, currency fixed, the amount issued, `source` `order` with its order line and unit, or `staff` with who issued it, status `active` or `deactivated`, `test_mode`, the customer and recipient, `send_at`/`sent_at`, `expires_at` from `gift_card_settings.expiry_months`, default 36, 12 to 120 or none) keeps no code: `code_hash` is the HMAC of the normalised code with a key made from `SETTINGS_ENCRYPTION_KEY` for this use alone (`src/server/gift-card-codes.ts`), `last4` what
people see; codes are 16 characters of the generated codes' alphabet (`src/lib/gift-cards.ts`, free of zod for the checkout).
A card's balance is only `commerce.gift_card_balance()` over `gift_card_entries` (`issue`, `hold`, `release`, `spend`, `refund`, `adjust`, `expire`, `void`), append-only, never below zero, written only by `gift_card_issue()` (once per unit of a line), `gift_card_hold()` (active, unexpired, with its code, its own currency and mode, the balance for it), `gift_card_release()`, `gift_card_spend()`, `gift_card_refund()` (up to what the order spent on it, also after expiry), `gift_card_adjust()` (with
a reason), `gift_card_expire()` and `gift_card_void()`, each once per key under the card's lock; triggers refuse any other writer, a change to a card's amount, currency, source or dates, a second code, reactivating, a second send and any change to who it is for but clearing it. The database allows the store feature `gift_cards` (needs the shop); its switch reaches the Features page with F2.
All three tables `never`/`settings` in `COPY_RULES`, the cards personal data (erasure clears the buyer and recipient, keeps the card and its money). **F2, selling one:** a product of kind `gift_card` (offered only while the feature `gift_cards` is on, `kind_offered()`; its variants its amounts, each `digital`; the VAT category `voucher`, 0 % in every country through `set_vat_rate()`, unverified until an accountant reads it, and only on gift cards, both held at commit by
`check_gift_card_product()`), bought in the cart like goods; *Send it to someone* on the product page (`GiftCardBuy`) puts who it is for, from whom, a message and the day (today to a year, `cleanGiftCardRecipient()`, the gift message's limits, refused not cut) on the cart line, which `giftCardKey()` names so two people are two lines, and `placeOrder()` freezes them on the order line.
Nothing comes off a gift card and it counts towards no threshold (campaigns, the group's and the friend's discounts, codes, credits, free shipping), in `cartSummary()` and `placeOrder()` alike (`voucher`); it asks no tick for downloads and is no download.
The cards are issued by the database, one per unit, once the order has a captured payment and is paid (`issue_order_gift_cards()` from deferred triggers on payments and on the order, whichever comes last; never a copy's or a host's; a test payment's are test cards), with no code; `deliverGiftCards()` (`src/server/gift-card-delivery.ts`, after the order's confirmation and from the every-minute marketing job) makes each code, keeps its hash (`gift_card_set_code()`, again only while the card was
never delivered, so a failed email never leaves a card no one can use) and emails it (`gift_card.issued`, required and locked; `sendEmail()`'s `secrets` keep `[code]` in the log) to the buyer at once or to the person it is for from 08:00 on their day, the buyer told it went (`gift_card.on_its_way`), then marks it sent once (`gift_card_mark_sent()`).
A gift card is withdrawn from within 14 days of the purchase, card by card while a card is whole (`lineEligibility()`'s `giftCard`, refusal `gift_card_spent`), and the withdrawal voids those cards with the order (`gift_card_withdraw()`); an order anonymised clears who its cards were for (`orders_gift_card_people()`). The demo `Demo: Gavekort` (500 and 1 000 kr) is the template's; a new store's copy waits as a draft, as every digital product does.
Words are hand-written nb, sv, da and en (`m.giftCard` for the browser, `m.giftCardLine`, `m.giftCardEmail`; the withdrawal sentence and the email `HAND_WRITTEN_ONLY`), flagged for legal review.
Not yet: spending a card (F3, so a store should not switch gift cards on before it), the invoice line's own words and Finance leaving vouchers out of sales (F4). **F3, spending one:** a *Gift card* field beside the discount code at the cart and at checkout (`GiftCardForm`; never the code's field: a card is money, a code is a discount) finds a card by its code's hash (`findGiftCard()`: the store's own, active, delivered, unexpired, of the store's mode, with a balance; any other answer is *This
gift card can't be used*, never whether it exists, except another currency, which is named) and keeps it on the cart by its id alone (`cart_gift_cards`, at most three, in the order added; the code is never kept), each try counted per cart (10 an hour) and per client (20, the keyed address hash VIES uses) in `chat_usage` (`giftcard:*`).
What the cards pay is only `giftCardsPay()` (pure: in the order added, each up to its balance, up to what is due online without the order's own gift cards, leaving at least the provider's smallest charge when anything is left), used by `cartSummary()` (`giftCards`, `dueNowMinor` after them) and `placeOrder()` alike; never for a subscription, a host's listing or an order on terms.
A card pays as a payment, never a discount: the total and VAT are unchanged, and `placeOrder()` writes a pending `payments` row of provider `gift_card` per card (`gift_card_id`; its test mode is the store's, as a venue payment's), which the database holds on the card as it is written (`payments_gift_card()`: never more than the order's total online without its gift cards, never for a change or an exchange, its card, order and amount fixed), spends when the order is paid and releases when it is
cancelled unpaid (`orders_gift_card_payments()`; an order paid after it was cancelled does not take its cards again and records `gift_card.unpaid` for staff). Stripe (or Mollie) is asked only for the rest: one line *Order N* holding the shipping, no coupon.
An order the cards pay in full has no payment page: the cart asks who pays, as for Mollie, with the store's terms of sale (and their tick in `checkbox` mode), and `confirmPaidByGiftCards()` completes it with the first card's payment reference as the order page's key (added to every list that finds an order by its key); a card typed at checkout that pays it all sends the shopper back to the cart for that.
A refund goes back where the money came from, the shopper's money first and the cards last (`refundOrder()`: a card's part is recorded `succeeded` with no provider call, capped by `refunds_manual_cap()`), and the trigger `refunds_gift_card()` writes it on the card, by any refund path, also after expiry.
The invoice names what the cards paid (`paid_gift_card`, with each card's last four, `invoice-text.ts` `paidGiftCard`), the rest paid online; a test card's order is a test (`invoice_eligibility()`); the bonus and referral refund bases count a card's part as paid; the payments report calls it *Gift card*. Words are `m.giftCardPay` (nb, sv, da, en by hand).
Not yet: an order changed or exchanged after it was paid with a card refunds through its own paths without the card (the order edit's and exchange's refundable sums leave it out), and a card added on the cart page after checkout began is taken only when checkout starts again (the open checkout does not compare cards); F4 has the owner's screens and the figures. **F4, the owner's screens and figures:** Products → Gift cards (`/admin/{store}/gift-cards`, `products:read`, kept while the feature
is off: the cards are customers' money) lists what customers hold now per currency and the cards by state (`cardState()`: not sent yet, active, spent, expired, deactivated; spent and expired read from the balance and the date, never stored), searched by the last four, an order number or, for staff who may read customers, the person; a card's page shows its ledger in words and the orders it paid for. *Issue a gift card* (`products:write`, the Products section's, as every change on these pages;
`issueGiftCard()`) makes a card in a currency the store sells in and in the store's payment mode, shows its code once (marked delivered there and then, so the code is fixed; emailed to the person it is for when an address is given, `sendIssuedCard()`), never keeping or logging it; *Change the balance* (`adjustGiftCard()`, a reason, never below zero) and *Deactivate* (`deactivateGiftCard()`) go through the ledger's functions; a bought card whose email failed can be sent again
(`deliverGiftCards({ cardId })`), and a delivered code can never be sent again (only its hash is kept: deactivate and issue a new one). How long new cards last is the settings card (`products:write`, 12 to 120 months or never). The daily job expires what is left on a card past its date, once (`expireGiftCards()`).
Finance leaves gift cards sold out of its sales (`GIFT_CARD_LINE`/`VOUCHER_NET` in `analytics-sql.ts`: out of revenue, gross sales, units and cost coverage; an order of gift cards only is no order; a refund is scaled by what of the order is not gift cards; what gift cards paid is no card payment to estimate a fee on), counts what they buy, and shows a *Gift cards* section (`giftCardLiability()`, `GiftCardsView`): held now and the period's issued, spent, refunded back, changed by staff, expired
and deactivated, per currency, never converted or added across currencies, the cards of the store's payment mode now, CSV `finance.gift_cards` (a currency per row). The invoice marks a gift card line (`voucher` on the snapshot's line, in SQL and in `buildInvoiceSnapshot()`) and says *Gift card: a multi-purpose voucher, VAT is charged when it is spent* (`invoice-text.ts` `voucherLine`, nb, sv, da, en); invoices issued before keep their snapshot.
The AI manager's `gift_card_overview` (read-only, `products:read`) gives counts and what customers hold per currency, never a code or a person. Audit actions are `gift_card.*` (area products) with ids and amounts. Not yet: a custom amount the shopper chooses, opening and closing balances on the liability report, refunds of order changes and exchanges back to a card.
