# D648: The challenge's baseline, frozen before Day 1

**The challenge's baseline, frozen before Day 1 (`docs/100-day-challenge.md` E7 and 3.3; 15 November 2026, `src/lib/challenge-baseline.ts`, `src/server/challenge-scoreboard.ts`).** The plan freezes the baseline on 2 to 3 January 2027 with the prompts and model (F5 pins those).
The baseline is what the score rests on that either side could otherwise move during the run: the clone's own cost estimates the score counts with (the payment fee's percentage and fixed part, shipping and handling per order, D646), the ad money agreed for each store, and the agreed cost prices of the clone's goods, kept as a sha-256 fingerprint of every variant's id and cost (with how many have one) rather than a copy of the catalogue.
A platform admin freezes it on the challenge's scoreboard page ("Freeze the baseline"), only before Day 1 in UTC dates (frozen again replaces it), and only once the store has saved its cost estimates; it is kept with the challenge (`KEPT_RECORDS`, so changing the dates or budgets keeps it) and audited as `ai.challenge_baseline_frozen`.
From then on the scoreboard counts the clone with the frozen estimates and the frozen ad money, and names in its notes any estimate, ad money or cost price changed since, for the referee; while it is not frozen the notes say so. No migration (the challenge is one JSON record).
