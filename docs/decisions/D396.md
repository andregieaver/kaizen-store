# D396: Pre-orders

**Pre-orders (wave 12 unit B, `docs/wave-12-after-the-core.md` B): built.** A goods variant that keeps selling at zero (D172) may be on pre-order until a release date (`product_variants.preorder_until`, a date the database refuses without `stock_policy = 'continue'`; set in the product editor under *Keep selling*, cleared by unticking it).
While the date is in the future `commerce.variant_availability` gives it (`preorder_until`), so it is a pre-order even with units on the shelf: `availabilityOf()` says `preorder`, every unit in a line is pre-ordered (`backorderNote()`'s `until`), structured data says `PreOrder`, the feeds `preorder` with Google's `availability_date`, and the chat says "on pre-order" with the date.
The words are `m.preorder` (page, line, order; nb, sv, da, en by hand, `HAND_WRITTEN_ONLY` for the legal review of the delivery promise), with the date by `releaseDate()` in the market's language, on product cards and content grids (`ProductSummary.preorderUntil`, the earliest date of its variants, read with the cached card, so it may stay up to the cache's day past the date), the product page, the cart, the checkout, the order and confirmation pages, My account, the pay page and the
confirmation email. `insertOrderLine()` keeps the date on the line (`order_lines.preorder_until`) while it is in the future; *To send* leaves out an order whose only units to send are pre-orders still waiting, and the Orders list filters them (`ship=preorder`, *Pre-orders waiting*) and badges each with its earliest date (`preorderFrom`); the admin's order page names each line's date. `e2e/preorder.spec.ts` holds the card, the page, the structured data, the cart and the order page.
It is paid in full at checkout as any goods line (no deposit: a deposit is a second payment the order does not have), and `cartSummary()` and `placeOrder()` agree (two scenarios in `checkout-kinds.int.test.ts`, kroner and euro). Not built: a pre-order limit (`preorder_limit`), a product CSV column and an AI manager field for the date. Migrations `20261029154147_preorders`, `20261029154228_preorders_rules` (applied by CI, run 736; advisors INFO only).
