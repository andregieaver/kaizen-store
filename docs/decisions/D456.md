# D456: Sign-up one step at a time

**Sign-up one step at a time (5 November 2026, `src/app/(platform)/sign-up/sign-up-wizard.tsx`, `src/components/platform-routes/sign-up-looks.tsx`).** `/sign-up` was one long column: every field and every theme with all its presets, demo-store lines, descriptions and Preview links at once, two narrow cards a row, and a send button drawn in the admin's colours, which Kaizena Flow's own pages do not set (it showed as plain text).
Now it asks three things, one per step, in a card with a list of steps: **Your store** (its name, what it will sell, and the Dream Factory's box where sign-up is self-serve), **Look** (only while a theme is published: a page of picture cards, Standard chosen; the chosen card alone opens its variants, its price if premium and the demo store it starts with), **About you** (name and email, the store and look repeated with Change, the send button and the terms).
Every step's fields stay in the one form, so the action, the fields' names and the answers are unchanged, going back keeps what was typed, the Kaizen Life and referral pre-fills and the theme store's `?theme=` still work. Continue checks the step with the browser's own checks and Enter means Continue; a field refused later, by the browser on sending or by the server (`ActionForm`'s `aria-invalid`), brings its step back and focuses it.
Without scripts every step is shown, one under another (`@media (scripting: none)`, the `noscript:` variant). The buttons are the header's pill (`bg-foreground`); `SubmitButton` takes a `className` and `ActionForm` a `successClassName` for pages without the admin's colours.

Date: 2026-11-05

A new sign-up field goes in the step it belongs to (`SignUpStep.content`), not at the end; a step with a required field needs nothing more, as Continue checks it. The admin's new-store form keeps `DesignCards`. No migration.
