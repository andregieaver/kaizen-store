# D290: Payouts and real Stripe fees

**Payouts and real Stripe fees (wave 4 unit C).** `docs/wave-4-payments.md` 5 is the contract.
Each store's Stripe payouts (its own connected account only, never a host's) are kept in `commerce.payouts`, from the connected-account webhook's `payout.*` events and a daily read per store of the last 30 days (`payout_reads`, `runPayoutJobs()`); a paid payout's balance transactions are its `payout_lines` (charge, refund, dispute, fee, adjustment, payout, other; at most 2,000, more marks it incomplete), each tied once to its payment, refund or dispute and so to its order.
Each line's fee is split into Stripe's fee and Kaizena Flow's application fee, and a charge writes its payment's `provider_fee_minor`, `application_fee_minor` and `fee_currency` once (also when the charge's facts are read). The tables are append-only: no delete, identity fixed, ties set once, a line in its payout's currency.
`reconcilePayout()` is the only judge: it adds up (lines' net = amount), it does not (by how much), or it holds money not tied to an order; a manual payout, one not paid yet and one with too many lines say so. Analytics → Payouts lists the period's payouts by arrival day, each in its own currency, with a total of the paid live ones in the main currency, one payout's lines on its own page, and the CSV `payouts.list` (`rowCurrency`).
Finance's payment fees are Stripe's real fee for each order whose every captured Stripe payment has one in a currency the store converts, the estimate for the rest, and the label says *estimated* with how many are real; *Payments by method* likewise per payment.
