# D525: D96's OAuth bridge between the assistants is retired

**D96's OAuth bridge between the assistants is retired (9 November 2026, `src/server/store-mcp.ts`, `src/server/owner-assistant.ts`; Kaizen Life's `kaizen_store` integration and its `/api/mcp` token path).** The owner: *retire the old OAuth bridge now*.
Since D517 the assistants talk only over the signed account link behind one switch, and the bridge kept two other ways in that nobody ever used (0 connections either way in production): Kaizen Life's Supabase tokens on Flow's `/api/mcp` (`KAIZEN_LIFE_CLIENT_ID`, `callerFromClaims()`), and the AI manager's own connection to Kaizen Life under Your account (*Kaizen Life for your assistant*, `src/server/kaizen-life-link.ts`, its callback, `KAIZEN_LIFE_ISSUER`, `KAIZEN_LIFE_OAUTH_*`).

`commerce.kaizen_life_links` (no rows) is dropped in a change of its own once no running code names it (account erasure stopped deleting from it first).
