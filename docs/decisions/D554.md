# D554: Digital Product Passports

**Digital Product Passports: a product's passport, published at a lasting address with a QR code to print (11 November 2026, `src/lib/product-passport.ts`, `src/lib/qr.ts`, `src/server/product-passports.ts`, `/admin/{store}/products/{id}/passport`, `/passport/{id}` and `/passport/{id}/json`).** The Ecodesign for Sustainable Products Regulation (EU 2024/1781) makes a passport, reached through a data carrier on the product and kept for its life, compulsory product group by product group as
delegated acts say what each holds (the battery passport under the Battery Regulation first, from February 2027 for the batteries it covers; textiles and electronics after). Nothing in the platform held one.
Now each product can have a passport (`commerce.product_passports`, one per product, its id fixed for life): materials with their share and recycled share (worked out across the product), origin, substances of concern, care, repair and years of spare parts, expected years of use, end of life, certifications with numbers and links, a carbon footprint only with how it was worked out, and documents (https links only), checked by `passportInput`; the identifiers (GTIN and SKU of the active
variants), the manufacturer and the EU responsible person come from the product itself (D452). Saved as a draft or published (publishing needs materials or a care, repair or end-of-life text; a draft again takes it offline).
Published, anyone reads it at `/passport/{id}` (its own root layout, no cookies, in the store's main language: nb, sv, da or en) and as JSON at `/passport/{id}/json` (`kaizenaflow.passport/1`, open to any origin); `passport` is one of the platform's own addresses on its host. The admin page shows the QR code of that address (`qrSvg()`, error correction M, drawn as one path from `qrcode-generator` 2.0.4, MIT, no dependencies) to download as SVG and print.
The product page links the published passport beside its safety details, as an absolute link so it works on a store's own domain. The environmental-claims check (D552) reads published passports' texts too.
This is the shared base, not any one product group's passport: a group's own fields and its registry (the EU's central registry is not open yet) are added when its delegated act applies, and a store's passports are not kept beyond the store's own closure yet (the regulation asks for the product's life). Store-only: Kaizena Flow sells plans, not products. No parity row: Shopify has none. Migration version: `20261111005248_product_passports`, applied by CI on 11 November 2026 (run 1197).
