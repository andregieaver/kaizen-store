# D419: Watching the Dream Factory build

**Watching the Dream Factory build.** The run's page (`/admin/{store}/dream-factory`) shows the build as it happens: the progress and what the AI is doing now, every step on a timeline with its minutes, a sketch of the store's front page filling in from what has been made (name or logo, pages, tagline, colours, fonts, products with pictures and prices; grey shimmer for what is not made yet), the feed of what was just made and a card for each kind made, then *Your store is ready* with the ways
on. It reads itself again every six seconds while the run goes. A test run (a drill's store belongs to the test address, so its page is not a platform admin's) is watched the same way, read only, at `/admin/platform/ai/dream/{run}`, opened from the scorecard; real owners' runs are not shown there.
Read only from the store's own rows made since the run began (`dreamShowcase()`, `src/server/dream-showcase.ts`); the pure parts (progress, minutes, the feed, colours and fonts made safe for a style) in `src/lib/dream-showcase.ts`; the parts in `src/components/admin/dream/dream-showcase.tsx`.

Date: 2026-11-02

*Why the store's rows and the code's words:* the page must never tell the owner something was made that was not; a step's line says what the step does and the feed names a row that exists, so a model's narration is never needed. *Why a sketch and not the real page in a frame:* the real pages are drafts until published and a frame of the store would load the whole storefront every six seconds; the sketch is drawn from the same records and is light. *Why six seconds:* often enough to watch
products land one a turn, no faster than a minute's step needs.
