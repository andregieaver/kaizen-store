# D134: Posten / Bring is connected

**Posten / Bring is connected** (`docs/shipping-carriers.md`, `src/lib/bring.ts`, `src/server/carriers/bring.ts`, `src/server/bring-shipping.ts`), on the store's own Mybring agreement: a connection check on the carrier's page, and from an order the services and prices for the parcel, a pickup point for service 5800, booking with a label, tracking, and the order marked as sent with the tracking number. The store saves a sender address with the agreement.
In the store's test environment Bring books test shipments and the order is not marked as sent. The label is fetched from Bring when printed, never kept. Delivery options and prices at checkout are not part of it yet (they change what shoppers pay). A booked shipment keeps `carrier_id`, `consignment_number` and `label_url` on `shipments`, and a carrier's last check is on `shipping_carriers`.
