# D53: Stores get the same page builder as Kaizen

**Stores get the same page builder as Kaizen.** One `PageEditor` and `PageBuilder` serve both: the route passes a `PageOwnerContext` (whose pages, admin and site addresses, reserved addresses, upload and server actions) and every page, saved-part and category function takes an owner (a store's id, or null for Kaizen). Store owners and staff edit at `/admin/{store}/pages` (with its own Categories and tags page), with actions bound to the store and checked by `requireMember`.
A store's pages cannot take the addresses of its own storefront routes (`RESERVED_STORE_PAGE_SLUGS`, also a database check); its saved parts, categories and tags are its own; a content grid on its page shows the store's own products, priced in the market and language of the shopper viewing it.
