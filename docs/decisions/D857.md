# D857: Contracts: live preview, full-width draft, versions shown, and a Contracts list under Deals

Date: 2026-11-20

**Asked by the owner (20 November 2026), after D856:** the draft's two halves side by side were too narrow, the preview changed only
after Save, an adjusted contract after a declined one was not visible as a version, and contracts could only be found through their deal.

- **Full width:** a draft's *What it says* and *How it will look* are stacked, each the page's full width (no `max-w-5xl`, no columns).
- **Live preview:** `ContractEditor` draws *How it will look* itself, filling the words as they are typed with `fillContract()` in the browser
  from the facts the server gathered for the deal now (`draftFacts()`: each field's value, the quote's lines, the parties; the title as
  typed). What still stops it is listed live too. Sending fills it again on the server from what was saved, so *Send for signing* is offered
  only when nothing is unsaved (*You have changes that are not saved. Save them, then send.*). Shared, stateless parts:
  `contract-preview.tsx` (`ContractReadiness`, `ContractPaper`), also used by a page drawn on the server.
- **Versions:** the chain was already kept (`replaces_id`, *Make a new version* on a sent, declined or expired contract, which cancels the one
  it replaces once sent). Now each contract says its version (`contractVersions()`, pure, `src/lib/contract-versions.ts`), and with more
  than one a *Versions* card lists them oldest first, each linked, with its status, when it was sent, and why a signer declined, in their own
  words (`contract_signers.decline_reason`, read into `ContractSigner.declineReason`: shown on the admin's contract page only, never to a
  model or in a webhook, as D695 step 4 holds). A signer's reason is shown under them on a declined contract too.
- **Contracts under Deals:** `/admin/{store}/deals/contracts` lists the store's contracts across deals, newest first, at most 500
  (`listContracts()`): title, deal, the customer who signs, version, when and status, each opening the contract; Templates one step away.
  The menu item sits right under Deals (`store-nav.ts`, Norwegian «Kontrakter»), and `useIsCurrent()` marks only the deeper item on its own
  address (a sidebar item whose address is inside another's: Contracts, not Deals), with no extra bytes in the menu (its budget).
- **Platform:** Kaizena Flow's own contracts are its house store's (D466), so it has all of this.

Tests: `src/lib/contract-versions.test.ts`, `src/lib/store-nav.test.ts` (Contracts under Deals), `src/server/contracts.int.test.ts` (the list
and the version number), `e2e/signed-in/contracts.spec.ts` (the preview changes before Save, Send waits for Save, the list).

Idle cost: none per request or run; one page and one menu link more for a store with Deals (the surface budget raised by one link, a basic
link and one in Customers), and the page's line in the AI manager's page catalogue (its rounds raised by that line).
