# D175: Store templates

**Store templates: starting points for new stores, made by the platform (outside the parity waves).** `docs/store-templates.md` is the contract.
A store template is a real store marked `stores.starter` (never also `is_template`, never unmarked, only a store with no orders or customers becomes one), made by `clone_store()` from the default template and set up in the normal store admin, and described by a row of `commerce.store_starters` (title, summary, description, category of seven, picture, position, published; never deleted, only unpublished).
Platform admins make, describe, publish, order, open and preview them at `/admin/platform/store-templates` (the Stores section has a sidebar now); owners choose one, or the Standard store (the default template, always first), on `/admin/stores`, and a requester at `/sign-up` (kept on `access_requests.starter_id`; the platform admin may change it before approving), each card with a Preview that opens the template's storefront in a new window.
What a store is copied from is decided only by `commerce.starter_source()` (a published starter, else the template; anything else refused).
A store made from a starter gets what `clone_store()` copies plus `clone_starter_setup()`'s operational set-up (audience, checkout terms, cart behaviour, languages, currencies, custom CSS, draft pages with their places such as legal drafts, order settings, invoice note, delivery days, customer groups, bonus and referral rules) and never its people, orders, payments, accounts, secrets, tax registration or business details; `stores.made_from_starter` records the template.
A starter is not a real store: every reader of `is_template` that means so leaves starters out (`not (s.is_template or s.starter)`, held by `store-starters.scan.test.ts`), `store_is_active()` is false for it so jobs skip it, `orders_store_open()` refuses its orders (`orders.store_starter`), `sendEmail()` holds back its shopper and sign-in emails, the storefront is `noindex`, out of sitemaps and llms.txt, with a notice and the cart's reason instead of the checkout. No AI manager tool.
