# D910: Where Deals and Projects meet, a deal's next steps are tasks

Date: 2026-11-27

**The weighing of `docs/projects.md` P6.2: the CRM's tasks (activities of kind `task`, D468) stay Deals' own, and where a store has both
Deals and Projects a deal's next steps are Projects tasks.** The owner's two answers decide it together. "One kind of task: a checklist item
is Flow's task" (D903, answer 3) and "every part stands alone" (D904): Deals must work for an account without Projects, so its calls,
meetings and tasks stay in Deals, as Work's tasks stayed in Work (P6.1). Where both arms meet, a person has one kind of task and one way to
make it, which is the octopus' pair for Projects and Deals (`docs/extensions.md` 3.1: "a deal's next steps are tasks").

Production on 27 November 2026 (counts only): 36 activities in one store, every one on a deal and its customer; the 6 tasks among them are
open, each a deal's next step.

- **The link.** `checklist_items.deal_id`, the store's own deal by the composite key, cleared when the deal is deleted while the task stays.
  Beside a task's source, as its customer is (P4.7); a task made on a deal's page names the deal's customer too, so the customer's page lists
  it.
- **On a deal's page** the card *Tasks* (`SourceTasks`, the kind `deal`) lists its tasks and makes one, written in advance as *Next step for
  the deal* (never its title, which may hold a person's name and would reach the AI manager). A task's row on its list links back to the
  deal.
- **One way to make a task.** Where a page's *Tasks* card makes tasks (a deal's or a customer's page, for a person who may change projects),
  its *Activities* card offers a call or a meeting, the *Tasks* card beside it making the task. An activity that is a task already keeps its kind;
  nothing is moved. A company's page has no *Tasks* card and keeps all three kinds. The API, the AI manager's CRM tools and the Pipedrive
  move still write activities of every kind: the narrowing is the screen's, for Homer.
- **The board** counts a deal's open task as its next step beside its activities (the sooner of the two; a task with no date only where no
  activity is left), so a deal whose next step is a task is not marked *No next step*.
- **An arm taken away:** without Projects, a deal's page has no *Tasks* card and the form offers a task again; the tasks stay Projects'
  (read-only in its grace). Without Deals, a task's deal link is not shown and none can be made.
- **The platform** has it at once: Kaizena Flow's deals and projects are its own store's (D467).

Idle cost: one column with a partial index, empty until a task names a deal. A deal's page and the board read the deal's tasks on that index
only while the store has Projects; a list's page reads the deals' titles only when a task names one.
