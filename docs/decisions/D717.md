# D717: The admin's words handed are counted against the words named

**The admin's words handed are counted against the words named (bloat review step 1.28; 17 November 2026).** `src/components/admin/admin-words-handed.scan.test.ts` finds every place the admin hands words to the browser (each `adminTexts()` and `AdminAreas` site: 229 today), and keeps per site the bytes handed in English and in Norwegian and the bytes of the words its client parts name, in a baseline.
A site that hands more than 2,000 bytes or a tenth over its baseline (words added to an area pass; a whole area added does not), or an area nothing under it reads, fails, with the fix said (hand a cut, move the words, or rewrite the baseline so the growth shows in its diff); a new site passes until the next rewrite unless it hands over 50 KB or an area nothing reads, and a baseline as far too high asks to be lowered. (The first version failed at 200 bytes and on every new site, and broke main on
its first day as words were added to the discounts area: loosened the same day.) Today 12.7 MB are handed against 3.9 MB named, 80 sites hand over 50 KB and 63 hand an area nothing names: the list phase 2 works from. The product editor hands 211 KB with 64 KB named. No browser is needed; it runs in about 3 s. The platform's admin is counted the same way. Commit 9d9ad9b5.
