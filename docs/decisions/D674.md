# D674: The dispatcher runs four jobs at a time

**The dispatcher runs four jobs at a time (bloat review step 0.45; 16 November 2026).** `runJobs()` (`src/server/cron-runs.ts`) runs a dispatcher's jobs through `pooled()` (`src/lib/job-pool.ts`): at most four at once (`JOB_LANES`, one fewer than the database pool's five connections, so a job that needs a second connection still finds one), where it used to start all of them together and a slow job made others fail with "timeout exceeded when trying to connect".
A job that has run 60 s (`JOB_LANE_MS`) gives its lane to the next, goes on, and is recorded by its own `tracked()` when it ends; the log says `cron.lane_given_up`. Answers keep their shape and order, and a job that throws still leaves the others' rows. The first round in production (16 November, 12:00 UTC) ran 80 jobs in 3.7 s with no error, about 1.3 s before. Cadences, a registry and per-job budgets are step 3.3. The dispatcher serves the platform and every store alike.
A day on (17 November, 10:10 UTC): no job recorded "timeout exceeded when trying to connect" since the deploy; a round of 81 jobs took 0.5 s, the longest job 229 ms, and no dispatcher job failed in the day. Commit ecc6f773.
