# D398: The storefront API's cart

**The storefront API's cart (wave 12 unit D, `docs/wave-12-after-the-core.md` D): built.** A site's own front end keeps a cart in the store: `POST /carts` makes one (`carts.source = 'api'`) in the token's market and answers its key (`kfc_…`, a secret the caller keeps; the database keeps `carts.api_hash`, its SHA-256); `GET /carts/{cart}` reads it as the quote does (`quoteCart()`: each line's price, `available`, `fitted`, status and the quantity rule, and a subtotal); `POST /carts/{cart}/lines`
adds, `PATCH` and `DELETE /carts/{cart}/lines/{variantId}` set or remove, each under the cart's row lock through the store's own checks (`sellableQuantity()`, `nextQuantity()`, `quantityProblem()`, `settleQuantity()`; goods only, never a gift card, at most 50 lines), written only by `src/server/api-carts.ts`; `POST /carts/{cart}/checkout` turns the same cart into the one-time hand-over (D170) and clears the key in the same statement, so the cart is then the browser's alone and is ordered through
`placeOrder()` like any other. The contract is an OpenAPI 3.1 document at `/api/storefront/v1/openapi.json` (`storefrontOpenApi()`, version `v1`), made from `STOREFRONT_ROUTES` alone, each route with its words, linked from Settings → Apps and API. CORS allows PATCH and DELETE. `available` is never above the line's most (`min(stock, lineMax(rule))`), the same figure the store's quantity picker and cart allow.
Held by `storefront-api.int.test.ts` (a cart made, cut to the stock, refused by its rule, emptied, handed over and placed) and `storefront-api.test.ts`. Not built: a page of its own for the contract (a reserved address and a Norwegian twin), bookings, plans and gift cards in an API cart. Migration `20261029164149_api_carts` (applied by CI, run 737; advisors INFO only).
