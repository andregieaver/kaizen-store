# D239: Exchanges and return labels

**Exchanges and return labels (wave 3).** `docs/wave-3-exchanges.md` is the contract (3.14 records the review fixes). **Exchanges.** Staff add catalogue goods to a return as replacements (`return_exchanges`, `return_exchange_lines`). They are priced by `priceDraft()` in the original order's market view, with no order-level discount and an optional lower price per line.
The server nets them against the return's credit, which is always `refundFor()`'s, into a refund due, an even exchange or a payment to collect. The customer confirms on `/account/exchange/{token}`. Staff may record the agreement for a voluntary return only; a withdrawal's proposal is emailed to the order's own address only, with no share link, and is refused once staff changed the order's email after the withdrawal (`email_changed`) (CRD Art. 13(1)).
Processing makes a new order (`source = 'exchange'`, D141 sequence via `insertOrder()`) and only then holds its stock. The credit moves as a transfer refund on the original order and an `exchange` payment on the new one. The rest is refunded through `refundOrder()` (stock restored once, `restockIf`), paid by a link to Stripe's hosted page, or recorded as paid outside Kaizen. The original order gets one `return_exchange` credit note; the new order gets its own invoice.
Bonus and affiliate reversals count `exchange` payments in the paid base. **Return labels.** Bring's return service is booked through `bookReturn()`, from the return's page or by the five-minute job for withdrawals (`label_auto`), one booking at a time. The shopper gets an email button and a status-page link to a route that fetches the label from Bring with the store's keys and never stores it.
The cost is taken off only at refund, when staff confirm the label was used and the shopper pays, never above the cost and never for a test label. A failed booking leaves the pasted address.
