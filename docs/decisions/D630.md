# D630: The admin redesigned, round 9

**The admin redesigned, round 9: Reports, Coupons, Blog (`docs/audits/admin-redesign-round-9.md`; 15 November 2026).** Reports asks four questions (what sells, where, how they pay, when and whether they come back) with each ready report's answer for the last 30 days streamed beside it (`src/server/report-answers.ts`), saved reports first and *Build your own* as the secondary button.
Coupons says how many codes are live and offers four ready codes (`src/lib/discount-offers.ts`: WELCOME10, FRAKTFRI, NEWS15, TAKK100) whose *Make it* opens New coupon filled in (`?offer=`), nothing saved until Save. The blog is the Pages list's picture cards with a sentence on what is live and the newest article, waiting comments as a line, and Categories and tags, Blogs and Comments under More. *Write with AI* on the blog waits for an article AI route.
Kaizena Flow's own blog and plan coupons get the same as the next step. No migration.
