# D555: eBay as a sales channel

**eBay as a sales channel: listings at the store's price and stock, eBay's orders here, tracking sent back (11 November 2026, `src/lib/ebay.ts`, `src/server/ebay.ts`, `/admin/{store}/integrations/ebay`, `/api/integrations/ebay/callback`).** The fourth of the recommendations the owner chose on 10 November 2026 (selling on marketplaces).
Every marketplace needs a seller account and an approved developer app; eBay was chosen first because it sells across the EU (and the UK and Switzerland) and its Sell APIs are open to any registered developer. Built against eBay's published OpenAPI documents (Sell Inventory v1, Sell Fulfillment v1, read 11 November 2026) and tested against a stand-in (`ebay.int.test.ts`), as Tripletex and Fiken were (D453, D460): it has not yet called eBay itself.
The owner connects the store's eBay seller account on eBay's consent page (Kaizena Flow's app: `EBAY_CLIENT_ID`, `EBAY_CLIENT_SECRET`, `EBAY_RU_NAME`; the state cookie signed as Fiken's), the grant kept encrypted (`commerce.marketplace_connections`, refreshed with eBay's refresh token; refused, it asks to connect again).
The owner gives what eBay asks every offer to name: the marketplace (eBay.de, .at, .fr, .it, .es, .nl, .be, .ie, .pl, .co.uk, .ch), the Business policies for shipping, payment and returns, a location key and a category.
Ticked variants (`commerce.marketplace_listings`) are sent by the five-minute job: `PUT /inventory_item/{sku}` (title cut to 80 characters, https pictures, the GTIN as EAN, condition NEW, the store's stock now) with the marketplace's `Content-Language`, an offer made or updated (fixed price from the store's market in the marketplace's currency, so a store without a euro market cannot list on eBay.de, and says so) and published once; eBay's first error message is kept on the listing and tried
again. eBay's orders not yet sent (`GET /order`, since the last fetch with an hour's overlap) are kept once each (`commerce.marketplace_orders`: lines matched by SKU, the address eBay gives), each drawing its units from the store's own locations in rank order (a correction "Sold on eBay" in the stock history, never below zero; what is short is shown on the order), and the owner marks one sent with a carrier and tracking number, posted to eBay (`POST /order/{id}/shipping_fulfillment`).
An eBay order is not a shop order: eBay takes the payment and gives the buyer's receipt, so it makes no invoice, VAT line or ledger posting here (the store books eBay's payouts from eBay's own reports for now). Every action is the owner's (D41). Not done: Amazon, Zalando and others (each its own partner programme) and eBay's payouts and fees into the books; listings are ended at eBay since D556. Store-only: Kaizena Flow sells plans, not goods. New parity row `orders.marketplace-channels`.
