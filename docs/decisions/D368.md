# D368: Broadcast templates

**Broadcast templates (wave 10 unit E, `docs/wave-10-must-haves.md` 4); built.** *New broadcast* opens with *Start from*: blank, or one of five hand-written templates (`BROADCAST_TEMPLATES` in `src/lib/broadcast-templates.ts`: news, new products, an offer, the season, thank you), each a subject, a preview line, a heading, a paragraph that tells the owner what to write, and a button, in Norwegian, Swedish, Danish and English, never machine translated, with no price, stock, urgency or claim
(`findClaims()`, `broadcast-templates.test.ts`); a product or an offer is added from the store's own facts (D352). A broadcast's words can be kept as one of the store's own templates (*Save as template* on its page; `commerce.broadcast_templates`, at most 50 a store, written only by `src/server/broadcast-templates.ts`, audited `broadcast_template.*`, a picture only the store's own; `never` copied), listed under *Your templates* and deleted there.
The editor starts unsaved with the chosen words in the store's languages that have them (`startingContent()`). `customers.email-marketing-broadcasts-and-lifecycle-automations` moves to full.
