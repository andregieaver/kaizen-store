# D54: Store pages on the storefront, in menus, and as the front page

**Store pages on the storefront, in menus, and as the front page.** A store's published pages are at `/s/{store}/{market}/{slug}` in every market, inside the store's own layout (which lets them span the window), prerendered at build like products, with their own search texts, a canonical address per market, a `WebPage` by the store, and an "Edit page" button for the store's people.
Store menus gain "A page", naming the page by its address (which a store copied from the template keeps) and following a page that moved; it takes the page's title unless given a text, and shows only while the page is published.
A store can choose one of its published pages as its front page (`stores.front_page_id`, kept to its own pages by a foreign key that resets only this column when the page is deleted); the market's front page then shows it, its own address redirects there, and the menus' built-in home link says Home instead of All products. Content grids on a store's pages show its products in the market being viewed.
