# D238: Customer notifications, delivery tracking and staff alerts

**Customer notifications, delivery tracking and staff alerts (wave 3).** `docs/wave-3-notifications.md` is the contract. **Notifications.** The order and return emails are one list (`NOTIFICATIONS`), each with a switch per event (`notification_settings`) asked through `notificationOn()`. The order confirmation, the renewal and change confirmations, the withdrawal acknowledgement and the return decisions are required, and the database refuses them off; the acknowledgement cannot be edited either.
Owners write the subject, heading and intro per language (`email_templates`) with named placeholders only, checked by `templateProblems()` (placeholders, braces, any web address by its shape, `findClaims()`) and checked again at send. `fillTemplate()` fills them in the order's language and currency, falling back per part to the standard text. There is a preview with a sample order and a test email. Texts are copied with a store only when the owner ticks *Email texts* (copy group `emails`).
A refund email switched off still lets its credit note go out. **Tracking.** Tracked parcels are polled every five minutes (`pollTracking()`, `shipment_tracking`; parcels stop being followed after 30 days and their events are cleared 90 days after that). Carrier codes are mapped to phases by `phaseOf()`: Bring verified; PostNord, Helthjem and Porterbuddy not yet, so they are shown only. Shoppers' order pages draw the stored rows.
Out-for-delivery and delivered emails go once per parcel, off by default, are worded per parcel, and are hand-written only. A consignment of several packages is delivered only when every package is, from the last one (`parseTracking()`, `PACKAGE_DELIVERED`), so one package at the door never starts the withdrawal period.
A verified delivered code records `delivered_at` only through `record_carrier_delivery()`: sent in full, every parcel delivered, never over a date, with `delivered_source = 'carrier'`. **Staff.** `store_events` rows, queued by triggers in the paying transaction (also for an order paid after it was cancelled) once per event, feed new-order and return-requested emails to the recipients the owner chose: members whose role may read them, or the contact address; owners by default.
The new-order email shows what was charged and any venue balance. Refund-overdue and low-stock move to the same recipients. There is an optional daily or weekly summary from D152's figures. No staff email carries a shopper's name, email, phone or address. **Rules.** `when an order is paid and {conditions} then tag, notify or hold fulfilment` (`order_rules`, a closed list, at most 50), run once per event and logged in `order_rule_runs`. A held order cannot get a shipment (`shipments_not_held()`).
