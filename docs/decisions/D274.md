# D274: The theme store never shows the Standard store

**The theme store never shows the Standard store.** A theme's page on the theme store shows, previews and photographs only the looks that are shown on a published demo store (`shownLooks()`, its own look first). A look whose demo store is not set up or not published waits for it: it gets no preview frame, no photograph is asked for it (`showcaseStores()` gives it no store) and sets taken on the Standard store before are left out at once (`themeShotsFor()`) and set aside by the next queue.
A theme with no shown look is still listed, with its colours as its card and no preview. The hand-made launch photographs (`LAUNCH_SHOTS`, `public/demo/themes/{key}-v{n}.webp`, D255), all taken on the Standard store, are gone. A theme's page in the platform's admin says, beside each look's demo store, when the look is left out of the theme store and why.

Date: 2026-10-17

Asked for by the owner: the theme store showed the Standard store ("Kaizen Demo", its car pictures) on cards and theme pages, which is not part of any theme. No migration.
