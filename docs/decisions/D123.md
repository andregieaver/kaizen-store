# D123: Work lives at the store owner's level

**Work lives at the store owner's level (the control center), across all their stores.** One place for every income stream: `/admin/account/work` shows the combined overview, clients, time, invoices, reports and settings of every store the account belongs to with Work on; one store's own screens are under `/admin/account/work/s/{store}/…` (`src/lib/work-paths.ts`: `workBase()`, the only way to build a Work link).
The data stays per store, because each store is the legal seller with its own invoice series, VAT, bank details and currency; the combined view groups money per currency and never adds across them, and every read takes only the stores the account's memberships allow (`src/server/work-owner.ts`). The Work switch moved from Features to the owner's Work settings (owners only, audited).
A person has one running timer across all stores (`work_start_timer` stops one anywhere; `getRunningTimer(accountId)`; unique per account). The store level lost its Work tab and sidebar group; old `/admin/{store}/work…` addresses redirect (`LEGACY_WORK_REDIRECTS`). Print routes stay chrome-free under `(print)/account/work/s/{store}`.
