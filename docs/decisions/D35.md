# D35: Admins see their customers

**Admins see their customers: a customer list and a customer page, in each store and on the platform, tightly linked with orders, subscriptions and plan invoices.** A store's customer is one person per email: an order placed while signed in belongs to the account's email, and guests are found by any of their orders.
The list shows orders, active subscriptions and money spent (paid, fulfilled and closed orders, and cancelled orders whose payment was captured), newest order first, searchable by name or email. The customer page shows these figures, every order and subscription, the emails sent to them, their contact details and address, their wishlist, and whether they opted out of cart reminders.
Every order and subscription page carries a bar with the customer's name, account and figures, and a "Customer details" button. The customer's name in the order and subscription lists leads there too, and any order or subscription id redirects to the customer's one page.
On the platform, Kaizen's customers are the people who run stores: the list shows each person's stores, role and plan; the customer page adds each owned store's plan invoices from Stripe, unfinished plan checkouts and the emails Kaizen sent them. A store's platform page shows its owner's bar, its invoices and its people, and each invoice has its own page leading back to the owner and the plan.
