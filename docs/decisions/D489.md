# D489: kaizenaflow.com's menus restored after the switch

**kaizenaflow.com's menus restored after the switch: a store's menu links a page by its slug (7 November 2026, `commerce.menus_store_page_links()`, `menuLabel()`).** The owner's report (*the main menu from before we switched*): since the site was served from Kaizena Flow's own store (D484) its header showed only the menu's "#" groups (Explore and its column titles), none of its 25 page links, and the footer's five page links were gone too. *Found:* the menus themselves were whole, the platform's
(`store_id` null, unchanged since 5 November) and the store's copy alike, 31 items each; but a store's menu links a page by `slug` (`StorePageLink`) and Kaizena Flow's own by `pageId`, and `copy_platform_site()` (D469) moved each id to the copy's page and kept it an id, so the store's menus could not read one page link and dropped them.
And the menus' own English labels were written under `en` (Kaizena Flow's are by language) while the store reads `en-GB`, so *Compare*, *About Flow* and *AI Store Manager* showed their pages' titles in English. *Put right in production (7 November 2026, about 20:15 UTC, by hand, as data, after the three menus were kept as they were):* each of the 31 page links in the store's three menus rewritten to its page's slug (every one a published page of the store), and each English label given its
`en-GB` twin; the live header showed the whole menu once the next deploy began with a fresh cache. *So it cannot happen again:* a store's menu is written with a page's or an article's slug whatever writes it (a trigger on `commerce.menus`: a link by `pageId` to a page of the same store becomes one by its slug; a page the store has not got stays as it was; Kaizena Flow's own keep their ids), and the menus written before are written again by the migration; and a menu item's text for the language
(`en`) serves each of its locales (`en-GB`) before a page's title. *The header too (the owner: "restore the custom header before the switch as well"):* the store's header, last saved at 19:57 that day without its language switcher and its columns' widths, was given Kaizena Flow's own header as published on 6 November at 15:48 (four columns, 25/52/8/15: menu button and logo, the menu, the languages, account and *Start your store*), its menu the store's main menu; draft and published alike, kept
as a version beside the 19:57 one (so it can be undone in the builder) and audited as the header's publish (20:31 UTC, by hand, as data). The store's pages hold no link by id (checked).
