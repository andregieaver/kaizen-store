# D121: A row can be a modal

**A row can be a modal.** A row with a `modal` setting (`RowModal`, `src/lib/page-modal.ts`) is taken out of the page's flow and drawn in a native `<dialog>` by `PageModal`, opened by a link to `#modal-{key}` (a button, a menu link or text), any element with a chosen class, a timer or exit intent (on touch screens a quick scroll up). Its content is an ordinary row, so every block, form and video works inside; in a footer or header it is site-wide.
Auto triggers open at most once per page view, never over another modal, never on working pages or in the builder; it always closes with Escape and a visible control. Frequency (always, once per session, every N days) is kept in browser storage as a `preferences` item (`kaizen_modal_{key}`), only for visitors who allowed preferences. A modal's words are not in the page's description or main heading, but stay in the chat's knowledge.
