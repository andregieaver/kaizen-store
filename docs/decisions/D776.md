# D776: A store page sends its layout once

**A store page sends its layout once (bloat review step 2.2; 18 November 2026; refines D64).** The store's pages and the cart drawer's slot moved into a route group, `src/app/s/[store]/[market]/(shop)/`, so a document carries the store's layout once instead of once for the page and once for the drawer (URLs do not change; the build lists no `(shop)` path). The header's cart link is a plain link from 768 px, and the cart count is read once per render.
Locally on a fresh build: the store home's document from 135,935 to 105,599 bytes, its duplicate payload rows from 27,226 to 1,768 bytes, `/om-oss` 80,744 (110,415 on 12 November). `e2e/performance.spec.ts` holds one `html` element per store document and duplicate bytes under 3,000 (12,000 on the six-row page, whose repeats are its own rows), and the home's document at 112,000.
CI ran it green on 982c8fd7; the e2e job's limit went from 30 to 40 minutes, as a slow runner had cut it off after every test passed. Commits 9c606e65, b1ea6238, a98b47e4, af4ca8f7, b3a90442.
