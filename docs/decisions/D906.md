# D906: The country rules in code as the owner left them, one outreach gate, and keyed do-not-contact hashes (email plan P5.3, P5.6)

Date: 2026-11-26

**The owner answered every point of the outreach draft (26 November 2026, Legal reviews, `outreach-rules`).** The answers are the owner's
positions, each *subject to legal confirmation*; the overall view asks for implementation planning to go on and for sending to stay off until
the rules, controller roles, privacy documents, suppression procedures and safeguards are approved. They are summarised in
`docs/outreach-rules.md` section 6.

- **The rules in code** (`src/lib/outreach-rules.ts`, `COUNTRY_RULES`): Norway, Sweden, Finland and France allow a first email to a company's
  general enquiry address with a way to say no; Denmark and Germany are consent first; a country not on the list is consent first. Each row
  keeps the owner's position and a `legalReading`, null until a lawyer's reading is recorded.
- **One gate** (`outreachGate()`), asked before an email is written and again before it is sent: it takes the country, what the recipient is
  (a legal entity, a sole trader, a person), what the address is (a general one or one naming a person), the do-not-contact list and any
  documented consent, and gives every reason it says no, for the record. It takes no store, so no store, domain, campaign or account can
  loosen it. **While no row has a lawyer's reading, it sends nothing anywhere.** Nothing calls it to send yet: sending is P5.5.
- Find companies says where the searched country stands (`countryStanding()`), and that nothing is sent until a lawyer confirms it.
- **Keyed hashes** (question 4): the do-not-contact list's entries are HMAC-SHA256 under a key derived from the settings key
  (`entryHash()`), so the list cannot be matched against a list of addresses without the key. The list was empty in production, so nothing
  was rehashed. The settings key must not be replaced while the list holds entries: a no kept under the old key would no longer be found.
- The Norway view's last line names Sweden (the same text as Sweden's); it is read as Norway, as the rest of it says, for the owner to confirm.
