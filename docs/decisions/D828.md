# D828: The ledger job's turns: ledger_books.checked_at and behind (bloat review step 3.22)

**The ledger job gives every book its turn, and an idle book costs nothing between hourly looks (19 November 2026).** `runLedgerPosting()`
listed the books by `updated_at`, which only the owner's edits move, so a busy book first in the list could take the whole 40-second budget
every run, and every book was read on every five-minute tick. `commerce.ledger_books` gains `checked_at` (when the job last looked) and
`behind` (the last turn stopped before the book was caught up). `ledgerBooksDue()` lists, in the order `checked_at nulls first, id`, the
books open or closed in the last 60 days (of an account with Work, D748) that were never looked at, are behind, were last looked at over an
hour ago, or have a shop invoice or credit note issued since (`issued_on` bounds each `exists()` to `invoices_issued_idx` and
`credit_notes_issued_idx`; `issued_at` decides). A card payment is captured as its invoice is issued (D159) and a refund given with its
credit note, so they come in the same turn; Work's documents, payments that change state later, payouts, dispute lines and a waiting
document whose day is up are taken at the hourly look. A turn stopped by the round's 100 documents, the comparison's 25 entries or the
deadline leaves the book behind, marked with its turn's end so the books caught up in the same run come first; a caught-up book is marked
with the list's time, so nothing issued during its turn is missed; a turn that threw is not behind. The marks are written in one statement
after the loop (the brief wrote one per book, which put 20 statements on the 20-book busy tick): the busy tick is 53 statements, one more
than before, held as `dispatcher.ledger.busy` in `ratchet.baseline.json`, and the idle tick with 20 books falls from 52 statements and 240
rows read to 2 and 20. Opening the books earlier sets `behind`, so the added days are posted from the next tick. The job never moves
`updated_at`. Kaizena Flow's own books are a book in the same list. Tests: `ledger-posting.int.test.ts` ("the ledger job's turns": three
books caught up within three runs, an idle book listed only after an invoice or an hour, a book opened earlier) and
`query-budget.int.test.ts`. Migration versions (applied by CI): `20261119212320_ledger_book_turns`.
