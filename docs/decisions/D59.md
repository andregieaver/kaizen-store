# D59: Google Fonts, always self-hosted

**Google Fonts, always self-hosted.** Kaizen and every store choose a heading font and a body font from the whole Google Fonts catalogue (`src/lib/fonts/google-fonts.json`, about 1,800 open families with Latin letters, written by `scripts/google-fonts.mjs`), under Fonts in the admin; every text component in the page builder (rich text, heading, button, a picture's caption, a content grid's tiles and their headings) can choose its own, and a button its weight.
The first time a family is chosen, Kaizen downloads its files from Google once (the builder's weights 400–700 and italics 400/700 it has, in the Latin, Greek and Cyrillic scripts the EU's languages need) and keeps them in the database (`commerce.fonts`, `commerce.font_files`, named by a hash of their bytes); sites load them from Kaizen's own address (`/api/fonts/css/{family}`, `/api/fonts/files/{file}`, cached by the CDN for a year), so visitors' browsers never contact Google and no consent is
needed, and pages need no extra connection. The picker shows each family's name in itself through a few-kilobyte subset passed on by Kaizen (`/api/fonts/preview`), so the admin's browser does not contact Google either. A weight a family lacks shows as its nearest real one, never thickened by the browser. New stores copy the template's fonts. The files live in Postgres while they are few; move them to object storage if they grow.
