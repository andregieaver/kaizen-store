# D679: A lost parcel's goods sent again, as an order at 0

**A lost parcel's goods sent again, as an order at 0 (`docs/100-day-challenge.md` B3; 16 November 2026, `src/server/replacements.ts`, `src/lib/replacements.ts`, `src/server/replacement-tools.ts`, `src/lib/autopilot-autonomy.ts`, `src/server/autopilot-gate.ts`, `src/lib/autopilot-events.ts`, `src/server/analytics-totals.ts`, `src/lib/order-profit.ts`, migrations `20261116133216_spotty_stranger` and `20261116133226_replacement_index`).** A parcel the carrier reported in trouble or on its way back
raised a `delivery_problem` event whose turn could only tell the customer and leave a note: sending the goods again was a person's. The owner chose, of the ways to book it, a new order of the same goods at 0.
`send_replacement` (core, `orders:write`, gate `spend`) reads the order's parcels whose last tracking phase is `problem` or `returned_to_sender` (`REPLACEMENT_PHASES`; a missed delivery, `attempted`, never: the carrier tries again), takes each goods line once over them, never more units than the line had (a custom line without a product is left out), and in one transaction under the order's lock makes the order through `insertOrder()` with `source = 'replacement'` and `replaces_order_id` (every
line's price taken off in full: subtotal the goods, discount the same, total and VAT 0), allocates and holds its stock as a checkout does and pays it at once with `complete_order_payment()`, which draws it; a total of 0 has no invoice (`invoice_eligibility()` answers `zero_total`) and no payment row.
Both orders' histories name each other (`order.placed` with `replaces`, `order.replacement_sent`), both order pages link to the other, the order list has the source (badge and filter *Replacement*), and `get_order` says what the order is. At most one replacement that is not cancelled per order: the tool checks under the lock, and the unique index `orders_replacement_live_key` holds it in the database.
The gate: never automatic outside the challenge (`NEVER_AUTOMATIC`); in challenge mode it runs by itself (area money) only when the goods are worth, as the customer paid for them with VAT, no more than the challenge's new budget `replacementMax` in the main currency (`replacementCapProblem()`; none by default, so every replacement waits until the platform sets a cap in the console; an order in another currency waits).
`delivery_problem`'s turn has the tool and is told to use it for those two phases only. **The money:** the replacement has no captured payment, so it is no sale (`PAID` is untouched: no order, revenue, AOV, units, COGS or customer count moves).
Finance gets its own line, *Lost parcels sent again* (`Totals.replacementsMinor`), subtracted before contribution profit: the goods at the cost frozen on the replacement's lines (the variant's cost when it was made), each parcel at its label's price and one without, or none sent yet, at the store's estimate per parcel, by the day the replacement was made; profit per order puts the same on the lost order (line `replacement`), and a replacement line with no cost leaves that order's profit unknown,
never 0. **Not done here:** the customer is told through the existing parcel updates (`tell_customer_about_order`), not with words of their own about the replacement; the replacement order's own shipping email follows when it is sent. **Platform:** Kaizena Flow sells plans, not goods, so there is no parcel to send again: store-only.
The migration is on the hot `orders` table: the constraints are added `not valid` in the first file and the index is built concurrently and the constraints validated in the second, marked to run outside a transaction (D569).
