# D602: The admin's menu, its progress polls and the notice badge cost less

**The admin's menu, its progress polls and the notice badge cost less (bloat review step 0.17; 14 November 2026).** `permissionOfPath()` keeps its answers in a process-wide memo keyed on the access and the cleaned path only (the answer is the same for every member; nothing of a person enters it; cleared at 2,000 entries), and `storeNav()` reads the sections once for the tabs and the sidebars, so a store menu build asks 110 times, not 220, in about 0.2 ms instead of about 125 ms.
`canOpenPath()` and `can()` are not memoised. `RefreshWhile` and `PackPicturesWatch` ask nothing while the tab is hidden and look once when it is shown (`src/lib/progress-poll.ts`, pure). `RefreshWhile` takes an optional `status` (a server action answering a short fingerprint) and `drawn` (the fingerprint the page was drawn from), and redraws only when they differ: the Dream Factory's run and rework pages pass `dreamStateAction()` and `dreamFingerprint(run)`.
The notice feed takes a store's id as its place without looking the store up by name (every read is filtered by the person's own account), and its count is `unreadNotices()` alone: two statements with the account's, four before. The platform's admin has no store menu of this size and its pages have no such polls; its badge already counted for `platform` without a lookup. No migration.
