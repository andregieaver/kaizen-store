# D56: New stores start with the template's pages; Kaizen's saved parts are their starter library; store pages are in search files

**New stores start with the template's pages; Kaizen's saved parts are their starter library; store pages are in search files.** `clone_store()` copies the template's published pages (published, with their translations, their category and tag ids and those in their content grids made the copy's by `clone_page_content()`) and its front page.
The template's front page, Forside, is a Produkter heading over a content grid of the store's products, so a new store opens with its products and can change the page like any other. In a store's builder, the Saved tab also lists Kaizen's saved parts as a library: dragged or pressed, they add a copy (its grids made to show the store's own products, without Kaizen's categories and tags) and cannot be changed there.
A store's sitemap lists its pages open to search engines in every market (not the front page, which is each market's own address), its llms.txt lists those open to AI assistants in the first market's language, and robots.txt closes pages that are not to AI crawlers in every market.

Date: 2026-09-25

Chosen by the owner. The front page's product tiles have no wishlist heart; the product list with hearts is still shown by category and tag lists and by stores without a front page.
