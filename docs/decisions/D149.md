# D149: The admin's look

**The admin's look.** The admin was black and white: clean, but flat and hard to tell apart.
Its colours are still the semantic ones every admin page is written in (`bg-background`, `bg-surface`, `border-border`, `text-muted`, `bg-foreground`), but `src/app/admin/admin.css`, imported only by the admin's root layout and scoped to `html[data-admin]`, now gives them a cool tint (a tinted page under white cards), one brand colour (indigo; `bg-foreground` buttons and badges, the current page, focus and selected states), soft shadows, and motion: fields ease to a brand ring on focus and a red
border once touched and wrong, buttons lift and glow on hover and sink when pressed, links and rows warm up, a bar runs along the top while a page loads (`AdminProgress`), loading placeholders sweep with light, a form that is working shows a spinner on its button, and a page, a dialog, a saved message and an opened disclosure ease in. Light and dark follow the account's choice (D99); every motion stops with `prefers-reduced-motion`; nothing moves layout.
The storefront, Kaizen's own site and the previews of a store's pages drawn in the admin (`data-theme-canvas`, `data-theme-preview`) are untouched, so a new admin page needs no styling of its own and never a fixed colour.
