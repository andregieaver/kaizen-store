# D365: Customer accounts optional, required or off

**Customer accounts optional, required or off (wave 10 unit B, `docs/wave-10-must-haves.md` 3); built.** `stores.customer_accounts` (`optional`, the default and what stores always had; `required`; `off`), chosen on Settings → Checkout (`CustomerAccountsCard`, `settings:write`), written only by `saveAccountsMode()` (`src/server/customer-accounts.ts`, audited `store.customer_accounts` with the change) and read only through `accountsMode()` and its helpers (`src/lib/customer-accounts.ts`).
Required: the cart shows *Sign in to check out* in place of its checkout button and `placeOrder()` refuses a cart without a signed-in customer (`sign_in_required`, worded in the four languages), so every way to an order, express payment included, obeys it.
Off: the store draws no sign-in or account link (the header's part, the standard header and phone menu, a menu's My account link), the account's own pages (`ACCOUNT_ONLY_SEGMENTS`) answer 404 and checkout offers no account, while every link sent after a sale (pay, change, exchange, invoice, documents, review, newsletter, stock, support) still works.
Accounts cannot be switched off while subscriptions, the bonus programme or referrals are on or a company account buys in the store, and those features cannot be switched on while accounts are off (`setFeature()`). `checkout.guest-checkout-and-optional-account` moves to full. Migration versions: `20261028152500_customer_accounts` (applied by CI; the release job of run 710 found nothing left pending).
