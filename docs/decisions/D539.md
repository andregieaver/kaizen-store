# D539: The AI manager's turn has a deadline and a cost ceiling

**The AI manager's turn has a deadline and a cost ceiling (10 November 2026, code review step 0.8, findings FA2 and FA5, `src/lib/turn-budget.ts`, `runTurn()` in `src/server/owner-assistant.ts`, `platformManagerCostToday()` in `src/server/ai-usage.ts`).** A turn could run eleven model calls of up to 60 s each, plus its tools, in a route cut at 300 s: when cut, nothing was saved and the tools that had run were not reported.
Its only cost limit was 300 messages a day per store, the month's allowance was read once at the start, and a platform admin's turns had no limit. Now `nextRound()` decides each round from the time and tokens left: a turn has 240 s, and once 45 s or less is left the model is given no tools and answers from what it has; a round with tools may take only the time that leaves the last round its share, and a tool asked for once that share has begun is answered as not run.
A turn stops after 400,000 tokens in and out (the provider's count, or the estimate usage records), and between rounds it stops when a store on Kaizena Flow's key has used its month's AI or a platform admin has spent US$25 on the platform's AI manager in the last 24 hours (also checked before a turn starts). A stopped turn saves a plain message with the tools that ran, and nothing is learned from it.
A round cut by its time now says *No answer within N seconds* rather than *Something went wrong*. The autopilot's steps, which have 120 s, give their own limits (110 s, the last 30 s without tools). The limits are constants, not settings: no owner has asked to change them, and the platform's can be raised in code if a day's work needs more.
