# D711: Ecosystem plan phase 1

**Ecosystem plan phase 1: quick joins between support, customers, deals, the autopilot and the domain jobs (17 November 2026, `docs/audits/ecosystem-2026-11.md`, `src/server/support-customer.ts`, `src/server/support-reply-to.ts`).** The assessment of the systems as one whole (Shopify's, Pipedrive's, Zendesk's, VWO's, Clarity's and Hotjar's, ActiveCampaign's counterparts, the CRM, domains and the AI agents) found one memory and one brain but no shared nervous system, and planned four phases; this
is the first, eight small joins and one moved. **E1.1:** a signed-in chat's customer is linked in the inbox's Context column (only an email or form's matching address was before), and the column lists the customer's three latest orders with their status and total, each linked (`conversationCustomer()`, on the feed's first answer only, `customers:read` for the link and `orders:read` for the orders; an address match stays marked not verified). **E1.2:** the customer timeline's conversation link
opens the inbox (`/support?c=`), not the JSON feed route. **E1.3:** an order and a draft or quote show the deal they are linked to (`dealOfThing()`, never called before), while Deals are on, for `customers:read`. **E1.4:** with the desk's existing choice *In this inbox only*, replies to the store's emails about orders reach the inbox: emails marked `replyToDesk` (order confirmation and updates, bookings, box deliveries, returns, exchanges) carry the desk's address as Reply-To when the desk is on
and has its address (`deskTakesReplies()`, `deskReplyTo()`, resolved in `sendEmail()` only for an email that is sent); with either other choice they keep the store's contact address, never both, so the desk's AI and the owner's own mailbox do not answer one reply twice.
The settings page says so under the choice. **E1.6:** the autopilot's goal reviews and alert turns may read `page_behaviour`, `version_behaviour` and `speed_report`, its turns on an ended or answered A/B test `version_behaviour` before applying a winner, and `suggest_experiments` answers `where_visitors_get_stuck` from the heatmaps' last 30 days. **E1.7:** the requests the chat assistant passed to the team (`openRequestCount()`, never used before) are counted on the Support tab with the waiting
conversations, at the store and the platform, and on Home (*… requests from the chat assistant are waiting for your decision*, to Support → Requests). **E1.8:** the four daily domain jobs run inside `tracked()` (`daily.domain-renewals`, `-reminders`, `-moves`, `-arrivals`), so a failure shows on the platform home. **E1.9:** docs that no longer matched the code put right. **E1.5 moved:** routing the platform's own forms to its desk is step 6 of `docs/platform-parity-plan.md` (one desk for
visitors and owners), since kaizenaflow.com is served from the house store (D484) and the database refuses form messages on the platform desk by design.

Date: 2026-11-17

The owner asked for the assessment ("is this ecosystem properly aligned for interconnectedness, automation, usability and efficient workflows?") and then to start phase 1. *Found in production on 17 November 2026* (counts only): 60 stores; 0 workflows, order rules, email automations, broadcasts and recordings; 10 deals in 2 stores; the house store's desk off, so kaizenaflow.com's contact forms email their recipients. *Why E1.4 is opt-in:* the default choice sends a form to the inbox and by
email, and a reply sent to both would start a conversation the AI may answer while the owner answers from their mailbox. *The eval:* no prompt or tool description changed (the tools' lists and an answer's fields did), so the recorded run stands. No migration.
