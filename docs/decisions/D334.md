# D334: The autopilot pays for everything it asks for, never broadcasts on its own, and sends customers only so many emails; AI answers by email say so

**The autopilot pays for everything it asks for, never broadcasts on its own, and sends customers only so many emails; AI answers by email say so (PSI 2026-10-26 re-score).** Every model call is recorded with who made it (`ai_usage.acted_by` from `currentActor()`), and page builds, pictures and page looks keep who asked (`page_ai_jobs.acted_by`, `page_looks.acted_by`) and run as them, so a picture or a look an autopilot step asked for counts in its monthly budget whatever its feature, and the
page it saves is audited as the autopilot's. A call no price covers makes the budget unknown, so the autopilot stops with the reason (`aiBudgetProblem()`) while it has a limit of its own. `send_broadcast` is never automatic (a broadcast reaches the whole list and cannot be called back); `email_customer` and `reply_to_support` run without a yes at most `customerEmailsPerWeek` times in seven days (default 5, 0 asks every time, counted from the audit log), a limit on the Autopilot page.
An AI-written answer in a conversation by email is marked *Answer by AI* with a note under it (nb, sv, da, en by hand).

Date: 2026-10-26

*Why:* the PSI agentic re-score (AGT-05, AST-01, AST-05, AST-07): the budget counted only calls tagged `autopilot` and priced an unpriced call at nothing, a page built for it read as a person's in the log, at *Just do it* it could email a whole list on its own, and AI answers by email carried no disclosure (AI Act Art. 50(1)).
