# D222: The scheme preview page

**The scheme preview page: every scheme in light and dark side by side (`docs/admin-style.md` 7.1).** `/admin/account/appearance` (Account's sidebar, the admin map, linked from Your account) holds light or dark and the colour scheme, and draws each offered scheme in both modes with every component of section 5 and a still rail on a sample page (`SchemePreview`), so a person compares them before choosing and a new scheme is looked at before it is offered.
Each scheme's file also applies to `[data-scheme-preview="{id}"]` and, for dark, `[data-preview-mode="dark"]` on the same element, so a page can draw a scheme other than its own; the device's dark never reaches a preview (`admin-schemes.test.ts`). A preview is `inert` and its figure names it in words; its sample header is a `p`, not a second `h1` (`PageHeader`'s `as`). The figures in it are examples. No migration.
