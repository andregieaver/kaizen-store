# D616: A store's slug by its id comes from one cached helper, and the store domains are parsed once

**A store's slug by its id comes from one cached helper, and the store domains are parsed once (bloat review step 0.20; 15 November 2026).** `storeSlugOf(storeId)` moves to `src/server/stores.ts` ('use cache', `cacheLife('days')`, tag `store-id:{id}`); the 23 hand-written `select slug from commerce.stores where id = $1` reads that then asked `getStore()` use it, leaving 3 lines (the helper and two support reads that also ask `store_is_active()`).
The only writer of a slug, `commerce.adopt_sandbox()` through `adoptRequestSandbox()`, stales the tag; nothing in the code renames or deletes a store. `storeHosts()` parses `NEXT_PUBLIC_STORE_HOSTS` once per instance, keyed on its text. Reading a theme's preview neighbours from the shared cached card list was tried and taken back: a theme published after that list was cached had no neighbours on its first visit. Kaizena Flow's own pages have no store slug to look up. No migration.
