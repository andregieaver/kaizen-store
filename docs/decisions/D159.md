# D159: Invoices and credit notes for shop orders in the store's own gap-free series

**Invoices and credit notes for shop orders in the store's own gap-free series.** A paid order is invoiced by `commerce.issue_order_invoice()` called inside `complete_order_payment()` in a sub-block (every paid path: checkout, renewal, standing delivery, venue booking; a failure rolls back only itself, so the number returns and the payment stands).
A succeeded refund, by any path, gets a credit note from a deferred constraint trigger at commit (`refundOrder()`, the new Stripe `refund.created`/`refund.updated` handler `applyStripeRefund()`, D153 returns, a refund made in Stripe, raw SQL), and a return refunded outside Kaizen gets one from the return.
The credit note is allocated by `creditAllocation()` (largest remainder over the invoice's VAT-rate buckets, or the return's own working in `returns.refund_working`) and is never above what the invoice has left per rate (a database trigger); the VAT in the seller's and the main currency is a difference of cumulative conversions, so notes sum to the invoice's.
A refund of a payment that is not on the invoice (a no-show fee) gets none (`payment_on_invoice()`, order event `credit_note.not_invoiced`). Documents are frozen JSON snapshots, immutable except a one-time `pdf_path` and `anonymise_expired_documents()` (unit 1g's hook, 5-year floor), with no delete and no direct insert.
Copied, host, Stripe-test-mode (`payments.test_mode`, frozen at payment for venue payments) and invoicing-off orders get none; a store with incomplete seller details, a non-VAT-registered seller whose order charged VAT, or no exchange rate waits and is issued by the five-minute job with the payment day as supply date. Invoicing is on for new stores and off for stores with live history until the owner turns it on (no backdating).
Content follows Art. 226 and the national lists of NO, SE, DK and DE (sources and gaps in `docs/wave-1b-invoices.md` 1.3), with the VAT in the seller's currency (Art. 230), reverse charge with both numbers and the IOSS number from D157; a non-registered seller's document carries no VAT columns.
The PDF is the same view rendered by Chromium (`src/server/invoice-pdf.ts`, every request refused), stored once in the private `documents` bucket, the print page the fallback; documents are served by long random tokens at `/s/{store}/{market}/account/documents/{token}` (no cookie, noindex, outside the tracking extras), linked in the confirmation, shipped and refund emails with the PDF attached (base64) when it exists. The Stripe invoice option is ignored while Kaizen invoicing is on.
New: refund webhook events, `returns.refund_working`, a base64 email attachment, `refundKey()` independent of webhook-written refund rows. All consumer wording is hand-written nb, sv, da, en and needs legal review.
Rows: `orders.invoices-and-vat-receipts-for-orders`, `orders.credit-notes-for-refunds`, `international.legal-invoices-and-credit-notes-for-orders`, held at Partial until the wording is reviewed and a real PDF is read per country. *Why:* the largest weighted gap after returns; draft orders, order editing, gift cards, payouts and unit 1c all need correct documents.
Migrations: `order_invoices`, `order_invoices_rules`, `order_invoices_audit`, `order_invoices_fixes`, applied to production on 2026-10-04 in parts (versions in the table below); the old append-only triggers were replaced with `CREATE OR REPLACE TRIGGER`, so the tool's cancelled DROPs were not needed; the two redundant indexes `credit_notes_refund_idx` and `invoices_order_idx` are left for the owner to drop.
