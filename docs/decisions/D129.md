# D129: An owner can duplicate one of their stores

**An owner can duplicate one of their stores** (`/admin/stores` → Duplicate, wizard at `/admin/stores/copy/{store}`, progress at `/admin/stores/copies/{id}`; `docs/store-copy.md`).
The owner chooses pages, products and posts (all, selected or none), customers (shoppers only) and order history; a store's settings always come except secrets, and whatever only makes sense for the original never does (payment connection and keys, domains, integrations, AI keys, invoices and numbering, billing and plan, the team, Work, logs, consents, carts, and customers' subscriptions and subscription boxes; a product's subscription plans come with the product).
The content step is one transaction, `commerce.duplicate_store()` (deterministic `clone_id()` mapping; references to things not copied are dropped, scoped campaigns and codes switched off; tracking ids and the store's own code are not copied), then a resumable job (`store_copies`, run by the five-minute cron: pictures and files into the new store's own library and every address rewritten, never left pointing at the original; customers and orders in batches).
Customers come without passwords, sessions, carts, wish lists or consents, and opt-outs stay opted out. Orders are read-only history: numbered `C-{original}`, marked by `copied_from`, status kept, and structurally unable to be paid, refunded, shipped, invoiced, emailed, booked or to reserve stock (triggers), skipped by revenue, analytics, control center, cron and integration events. A data-use confirmation is required with customers or orders.
The new store is owned by the requester only, starts closed with no Stripe account, and `COPY_RULES` (`src/lib/store-copy-rules.ts`) classifies every store-owned table, with a test that fails when a new one is not decided.
