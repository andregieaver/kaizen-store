# D283: Pictures made and made again by prompt, in the site's look

**Pictures made and made again by prompt, in the site's look: in the page builder's picture fields and, one by one, on a theme's Content tab.** In the builder every picture field under `PictureMakerProvider` (the image component, row and column backgrounds, a gallery's pictures, a video's poster, testimonials' and custom grid items' pictures; never a brand's logo, `make={false}`) offers *Make with AI*, or *Make again with AI* where it has a picture: a description (already there when making
again: what it was asked for in this tab, else its alt text), a shape (the one it has) and *In the site's look* (on).
The route `/admin/{store}/media/make` (`website:write`) or `/admin/platform/media/make` (a platform admin), five minutes long so a slow model never cuts it, calls `makeSitePicture()` (`src/server/picture-maker.ts`, feature `page_pictures`): the person's words first, then `pictureDirection()` (`src/lib/picture-direction.ts`: the live theme's photographer's brief when it is a Kaizena Flow theme, its content pack's `look`; the colours it is shown in, its dark palette and a low-key mood when it is
always dark, `colourWords()`; what the site is about), said to set the style, light and colour and never what is in the picture, then `MADE_PICTURE_RULES` (no words; anyone invented). The picture is kept in the media library with its first sentence as alt text (`altFromBrief()`), shown first, and used with *Use this picture*.
On a theme's Content tab (`PackSlotPictures`), each of its content pack's pictures has *Make again*, with its brief to keep or change: the job makes it (`askToRemakePackPicture()` marks the slot `again`, `makeNextPackPicture()` takes such slots after the missing ones), `swapPackPicture()` puts it wherever the store used the one before (`rewriteAddresses()`, with the pages and products marked changed, so the content shows changes since it was published), keeps the one before for *Put back the one
before* and keeps a changed brief with the picture; Stop, or three failures, ends the remakes asked for. A pack picture that is not of people is now told *No people, faces, bodies or hands* (`PICTURE_RULES`), since Kinetic's shoot direction (athletes at night) put runners, a head cropped off by *no recognisable faces*, in its trail shoes' product photograph; and every pack photograph is one frame, never a collage or a split picture (`REAL_PHOTO`), since its gear picture came as two side by side.
