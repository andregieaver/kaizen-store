# D884: Basic and groups on the platform and the account: the platform's Settings in six groups, a Basic view at all three level

**The platform's Settings are in six groups, every platform sidebar and the owner's Account have a Basic view, and the switch is at all
three levels, one choice per person (code review step 2.3, finding FN4; 23 November 2026).** Settings was one heading over 28 links and
Account a flat 15; D454 gave stores alone their views, against the rule that the platform gets what stores get.

Groups, from the audit's six: Site (Header and footer, SEO, Cookies, Google reviews, Instagram); Money and tax (Stripe, VAT rates, Duty
rates, Domains); AI (AI, AI usage, AI prices, 100-day challenge); Apps and connections (Integrations, Workflows, AI assistants and apps,
App listings); Privacy and law (Data retention, Record of processing, Content notices); Running the platform (Status page, Usage and
limits, Emails, Languages, A/B tests, Search test, Team, Change log). Compliance and Operations became the plain words for what is in
them; Team joined Running the platform (two pages alone), and the apps that ask to connect or be listed went with the connections, where a
person looks for them. Account, in its old order: Personal, Billing and usage, Apps and themes. Website (13), Plans (9) and Support (7)
keep one group. The sidebar draws the headings in D873's style; the page of cards uses the same words in `SettingsHub`'s, as a store's.

Basic: `PlatformItem.basic` and `OwnerItem.basic`, as a store's; the page a tab leads to always (`advancedIn()`, now in `admin-view.ts`
for the three levels). Platform 24 of 57: Website 6, the store's everyday parts; Plans 4 (Editor, Accounts, Plan analytics, Discounts);
Support 3 (Inbox, Support reports, Chat assistant); Settings 11 (Header and footer, VAT rates, Domains, AI usage, 100-day challenge, App
listings, Content notices, Status page, Usage and limits, Emails, Team): queues waiting on a person and what the team watches weekly, one
in each group so Basic still shows six; set-once pages are Advanced. Account 5 of 15 (Your account, Kaizena Flow support, Your domains,
Billing, Add to your account).

The choice: `accounts.admin_view`, one for every level, as D454 keeps one for every store: a person is Homer or Lisa, not their store
or level. Both layouts already read the account, so `AdminViewProvider` takes it with no new `cookies()` or `headers()` read. The switch
is in the top bar and the phone's menu; Your account's is the layout's, so the sidebar follows at once.
Rejected: a column for the platform (a migration, and two switches that look alike); hiding cards (D597: Settings lists every setting).

Bytes: the 122 platform routes' first scripts +164 each (flags and headings 462, less the switch's inline icons 298); 332 other admin
routes fell 137 to 298 (store Home 780,903, Pages 786,472, Settings 744,478); no public route changed. The platform home's document
grew 3,708 (109,867 of its 110,808), the switch being in its top bar and phone menu: a note on that home, as a store's Home has, put it
over, so it has none, and the full switch drops the titles the compact one keeps. Render modes unchanged.

Tests: `platform-nav.test.ts` (the groups; the cap: some Basic in every sidebar, at most 12 a section, under half in all, each list by
name, a tab's page always), `owner-nav.test.ts`, `admin-view.test.ts`, `e2e/signed-in/admin-view.spec.ts` (the platform, the owner's
level, one choice across them). Unchanged: who may open what, the map's groups (its tabs, D881), every menu outside these two sidebars.
