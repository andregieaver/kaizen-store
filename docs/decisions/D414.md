# D414: A Timeline component and descriptions on icon list lines, asked for by the owner on 1 November 2026

**A Timeline component and descriptions on icon list lines, asked for by the owner on 1 November 2026.** **Timeline**: events over time in the order listed, each a date as written, a title, a few words and an optional icon (else a dot), along a line; along one side, or alternating sides from the `md` width (one side on a phone).
An ordered list drawn on the server, the line and markers decorative and solid in the accent or a chosen colour. **Icon list**: each line may have an optional description under its words, drawn smaller, translated only where written. Rules: `docs/rules/admin/website.md` (Timeline; Icon list descriptions).
