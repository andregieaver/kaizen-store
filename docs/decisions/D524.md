# D524: Timed plan prices

**Timed plan prices (C5 of `docs/platform-parity-plan.md`, 9 November 2026, `platform_discount_codes.campaign_name`/`starts_at`/`plan_ids`, `src/lib/plan-campaigns.ts`, `src/server/platform-discounts.ts`, Platform → Discounts → *New campaign*).** A store runs campaigns that need no code (D114); Kaizena Flow now runs them on its plans.
A **campaign** is one of its discount codes (D31) with a name, a start and an end, on chosen plans (every plan when none is ticked): a percentage or an amount off per currency, for the first payment, some months or every payment, as the Stripe coupon it already is. **Given without a code:** a new plan chosen while it runs gets the coupon on Stripe's page or the platform's own subscription (`waitingDiscount()`), unless the owner saved a code of their own, which wins (Stripe takes one); a plan
already running is not touched, as a store's campaign does not reprice what was bought. Its code is made from the name and never typed; typed anyway, it works only while the campaign runs. **One at a time:** a second campaign whose time meets an active one's on a shared plan is refused, so the price shown is the price charged.
Unknown plans are refused rather than dropped, since fewer plans than named would mean every plan. **Shown:** the plans pages (D142) carry the running campaign, never its code: a line with its name and last day over the cards, and each card it gives on with its price, for how long and the price after (months on a yearly price are its first payment, up to twelve); words in English, Norwegian, Swedish and Danish. The cache is read again within the hour a campaign starts or ends.
An owner's Billing page says it over the plans while no code waits. Months count from when the plan is chosen, as Stripe counts them, a self-serve trial included. Closes the parity row *Campaigns*; the Marketing section of `docs/platform-parity.md` has nothing left.
