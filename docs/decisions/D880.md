# D880: Use beyond the allowances billed as lines on the account's invoice, with a cap the owner may set (D748 E11)

Date: 2026-11-23

D748's price sheet bills use beyond a size: €0.40 per AI-handled conversation beyond the support desk's 100 a month, €5 a person beyond
Work's three (accountants free) and €8 beyond Deals' three. The owner chose, of the options put to them (23 November 2026): billed as used
with a cap they may set; told at 80 % and once extras begin; as lines on the account's invoice (Stripe's usage meters are not confirmed for
the Accounts v2 customers our subscriptions bill); a person is a team member who can use the extension.

- **Prices** (`commerce.extension_extra_prices`): one a unit, extension and currency, excluding VAT; a change is a new row, the old one
  inactive. Seeded from the sheet in EUR and NOK; Platform → Plans → Extensions, *Beyond what's included*, sets them (empty: never billed).
- **Counting** (`src/server/extras.ts`, kept lean for the shop's chat): AI-handled conversations are those whose first AI answer fell in the
  period, across the account's open stores, resolved or handed over (each costs tokens); Work's people are team members with access to
  Work's screens (`settings:read`) in a store using it, accountant roles free; Deals' are those who can change customers
  (`customers:write`), so a read-only member never counts. Only an extension the account pays for (bought, in a bundle or in its plan)
  has extras: a trial, extensions not yet sold and Kaizena Flow's own never do. What each size includes is `accountSize()`'s.
- **The bill** (`src/server/extras-billing.ts`): when Stripe drafts an account's renewal invoice (`invoice.created`, `subscription_cycle`),
  one line an extension is added for the period just ended ("Work: 2 people beyond the 3 included"), never past the cap
  (`billExtras()`, pure in `src/lib/extras.ts`). Each line's row (`commerce.organisation_extra_charges`) is written first, so a retried
  event adds nothing twice; Stripe's call has an idempotency key of its own. Lines go on before referral credit, which can cover them.
- **The cap** (`organisation_billing.extras_cap_minor`, Account → Billing, under *Monthly cap*): the most a period's extras cost. Once one
  more AI-handled conversation would pass it, a new conversation (chat or email) goes to people (`aiRoomFor()` in `openConversation()`
  and the email desk); people already added stay.
- **Told**: Account → Billing lists each extension's use this period against what is included, marks one at 80 %, and says what is beyond
  and what it costs so far.

Not yet: an email to the owner when extras begin (the billing page says it). Kaizena Flow's own account is never billed, so nothing here
applies to the platform.

Idle cost: none per request; a new support conversation reads the account's cap (one query, and the period's counts only when a cap is
set); the billing page reads the period's counts.

Migration versions: `20261123144334_keen_bloodaxe` (the two tables and the cap), `20261123144343_extension_extras_rules` (RLS, the sheet's
prices).
