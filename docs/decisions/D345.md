# D345: Reports from a closed catalogue

**Reports from a closed catalogue (wave 8 unit A, `docs/wave-8-analytics-ai.md` section 3); built.** Analytics → Reports (`/admin/{store}/analytics/reports`, `analytics:read`; saving, deleting and the CSV `analytics:write`) offers ten ready reports, one press each, and a builder: up to six figures of `METRICS` (net sales, gross sales, discounts, refunds, orders, units sold, average order value, customers, gross profit, VAT) cut by up to two of `DIMENSIONS` (day, week, month, quarter, product,
variant, SKU, category, tag, kind of product, country, currency, channel, device, discount code, payment method, new or returning customer), with filters, the analytics period and comparison, a sort, up to 500 rows and a table, bar or line chart (`src/lib/report-catalogue.ts`, pure; `reportSpecSchema` in `report-catalogue-schema.ts`).
A spec is checked by `specProblems()` and refused in words, never run as free SQL; the builder offers only the cuts every chosen figure allows (`dimensionAllowed()`), and the spec is the address (`q`), so a report is a link.
`src/server/report-query.ts` (`runReport()`) is the only module that turns a spec into SQL: D152's `PAID` orders of the period for the store, the cut keys worked out once in a `cut` CTE, money grouped by currency in SQL (`grouping sets`) and converted in code (`toMainOne`; a currency with no rate left out and counted), counts across currencies; a figure cut by a line (product, variant, SKU, kind) is counted from lines with the line's share of order amounts, and the part of the orders' totals no
line carries is a rest row, so the column adds up to the period's figure; a category or tag cut can count a line twice and says the column does not add up; gross profit is `estimatedCogs()` with its coverage, none under 30 %.
Saved reports are `commerce.saved_reports` (title and spec, at most 200 a store), written only by `src/server/saved-reports.ts` under the store's row lock, audited `analytics.report_saved`/`_deleted`, `never` in `COPY_RULES`, not personal data; a saved spec is checked again when read. The CSV is a POST to `reports/csv` through `writeCsv()`/`amountCell()`, audited `analytics.report_exported` with the keys and never the filter values. Words are the `customReports` catalogue (en, nb).
This closes `analytics.custom-report-builder-and-shopifyql` and `analytics.standard-sales-reports`. Not built: visit figures (sessions, conversion), shipping as a figure, vendor, customer group, company and subscription cuts, a scan test that no other module builds a report query, and annotations.
