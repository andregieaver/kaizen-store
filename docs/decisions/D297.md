# D297: Broadcasts

**Broadcasts (wave 5 unit C, `docs/wave-5-marketing.md` 5).** A store writes an email once, per language, from blocks (heading, text, button, a picture from its own media library as JPEG, PNG or GIF, a line; `broadcastContentSchema`, `src/lib/broadcasts.ts`), and sends it to the addresses on its subscriber list that are `subscribed`: everyone, or a segment's (D296's `segmentPeople()` and `matches()` at the start).
`commerce.broadcasts` moves only draft → scheduled or sending, scheduled → draft or sending, sending → sent or cancelled (`broadcasts_rules()`); its words and audience are fixed once a send starts, and only a draft is deleted.
Starting a send freezes the addresses into `broadcast_recipients` in one transaction; `runBroadcasts()` (every minute from `/api/cron/broadcasts`, and `after()` the press) claims them a batch at a time, skips one that left the list since, keeps each email in `email_messages` under `broadcast:{id}:{subscriber}` so a retried batch never sends twice, and hands them to Resend's batch endpoint (`deliverBatch()`, 100 a call, paced; a batch refused as a whole is sent one by one).
Every email carries the subscriber's own unsubscribe link and `List-Unsubscribe` with `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, and the store's details; a hard bounce or a complaint about any email a store sent unsubscribes that address (`applyEmailEvent()`). Buttons go through `/api/marketing/c/{broadcast}/{link}`, which redirects only to a link the broadcast's content has and counts a press with no person, time or address (`broadcast_link_clicks`); no open-tracking pixel.
The report counts recipients, sent, kept only (no email settings), delivered, bounced, marked as spam, failed, skipped, unsubscribed since and presses per link; recipients go 90 days after the send ended with the figures frozen on the broadcast (`pruneBroadcastRecipients()`, daily).
A test goes to the staff member's own address (`marketing.broadcast.test`). *Write with AI* (feature `marketing_email`) suggests a subject, preview, heading, paragraphs and a button's words from the staff's own words, every text through `findClaims()` (a claim refuses it), never saved or sent.
Marketing (`marketing:read`, every change `marketing:write`, audited `broadcast.*` with the title, never an address); recipients are personal data (export `marketing`, erasure), the rest `NOT_PERSONAL`, none copied. Not built: templates and product blocks, placeholders, A/B subjects, the AI manager drafting a broadcast, and automations (unit D).
