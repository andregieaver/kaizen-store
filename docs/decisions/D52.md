# D52: Categories and tags in menus, leading to filtered lists

**Categories and tags in menus, leading to filtered lists.** A store's menus and Kaizen's gain "A category's products/pages" and "A tag's products/pages" links, naming the category or tag by its address (as product links name products by handle, so a store copied from the template keeps them working); without a text of its own a link shows the category's name, and a link to one that is gone is left out.
A store's lead to its product list filtered: `/s/{store}/{market}/category/{slug}` (with its subcategories, which are offered as links) and `/tag/{slug}`, priced in the market like the front page. Kaizen's lead to `/category/{slug}` and `/tag/{slug}`, which list its published pages as a content grid; `category` and `tag` are reserved page addresses. The seeded demo store has categories (Papir; Hjem with Belysning inside) and a tag (Nyhet), and its footer links to Hjem.

Date: 2026-09-25

Asked for by the owner, choosing filtered lists over dropdown menus or separate category pages. Category and tag names are in one language for now; menu links can say them in each of a store's languages. A list shows at most 48 items until paging is added.
