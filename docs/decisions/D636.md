# D636: What to reorder is a demand forecast

**What to reorder is a demand forecast (`docs/100-day-challenge.md` C4, step 1; 15 November 2026, `src/lib/demand-forecast.ts`, `src/server/demand-forecast.ts`).** `restock_suggestions` (and *Prepare reorder* and the checkup, which read it) divided the last days' sales by their number.
It is now `forecast()`: the units per day from the pace's days with each week counting 0.85 of the week after it; times the season once there is a year of sales (last year's units in the days ahead against last year's in the weeks before them, used from 10 units, clamped to 0.5–2.5); times a price campaign planned in the days ahead, by the product's own lift in the store's past price campaigns over 180 days (used from 7 campaign days and 5 units on them, clamped to 1–3, weighted by the share
of the days ahead it covers). The delivery time is the median days from ordered to received of the variant's received purchase orders in a year (from two), else the days asked; a safety stock of 1.65 standard deviations of the daily sales over the delivery time is added (about 95 %). Each adjustment used is named in the variant's `why`, and `rule` says the whole method, so the AI repeats it and computes nothing.
Campaigns count from their start (or creation) to their end, or until switched off; the price kinds only (percent, multi-buy, amount, tiered, buy X get Y). The tool's description is unchanged, so its version and the recorded eval stay as they are. No migration. Step 2: a stock-out risk event the autopilot answers (a new event kind, a migration).
