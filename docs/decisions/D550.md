# D550: Drop shipping, step 1

**Drop shipping, step 1: suppliers that ship for the store, their own locations and the products they send (10 November 2026, `docs/drop-shipping.md` 3 and 10, `src/lib/drop-shipping.ts`, `src/server/suppliers.ts`, `routingOrder()` and `allocate()` in `src/lib/stock-routing.ts`, Inventory → Suppliers).** The first of D482's five steps, asked for by the owner on 10 November 2026 after the review of missing features ("let's go with your recommendations": drop shipping first).
A supplier is the row of `commerce.suppliers` its bills are booked on (D492) with `drop_ships` on and an order email, the country it ships from (any country; outside the EU it is flagged: customs, import VAT, a longer wait), its orders' language (nb, sv, da, en), dispatch days (1 to 30), how orders reach it (email, its own system, a daily file), a return address and a note on every order; at most 50 a store.
It has one location of its own (`inventory_locations.supplier_id`), named after it and ranked above 100,000, which the Locations page and its changes leave out and a store's copies never take (migration `drop_ship_rules` patches `clone_store()` and `duplicate_store()`). A physical variant names its supplier (`product_variants.supplier_id`).
Corrections to D482: no new `kind` column (the location points at its supplier); one routing rule instead of two modes per variant, **the store's own stock always goes first and the supplier sends the rest** (`routingOrder()` puts suppliers after every own place, nearest-first included; `allocate()` keeps an order together at a supplier only when no own place has a free unit of anything wanted), so a variant always sent by the supplier is one the store keeps no stock of; the supplier's stock is
set on the stock pages at its location until step 3. Staff with `products:write` add, edit and mark; stopping a supplier (its variants back to the store, its location closed, the row kept for its bills) is the owner's. Platform: store-only, as the platform parity rule allows: Kaizena Flow sells plans, not goods, so it has nothing a supplier could send (`docs/platform-parity.md`, store-only list). Parity row `orders.drop-shipping` (app tier on Shopify) is new and Partial.
