# D295: The subscriber list

**The subscriber list (wave 5 unit A, `docs/wave-5-marketing.md` 3).** A store keeps who asked for its marketing email in `commerce.marketing_subscribers` (one row per address: status `pending`, `subscribed` or `unsubscribed`, where they joined (a page's newsletter form, checkout or My account), the words they agreed to and when, when they confirmed by email, when and how they left, and an unsubscribe token) with the history in `marketing_consent_events`, both written only by
`src/server/marketing-subscribers.ts` and the database's rules (`marketing_subscribers_rules()`: only the moves of `mayMove()`, every change of status written into the history in the same statement, the history append-only and gone only with its row; the address, token and customer fixed; a subscribed address keeps its consent).
A typed address waits for its confirmation link (`marketing.confirm`, a page with a button at `/account/news/{token}` so a mail scanner subscribes no one); a newsletter form's own link (D93) is its waiting record and writes the subscriber when opened; only a signed-in customer with a verified email subscribes at once, and an unverified one is never told an address is subscribed already.
Checkout has an unticked box with the words agreed to (the pay press asks, never holding up the payment); My account has the switch; leaving is the email's unsubscribe page or the one-click POST (the cart reminders' routes now look up subscribers' tokens first), My account, or staff for a person who asked.
Marketing → Subscribers lists them with their counts, search, one subscriber's consent and history, *Unsubscribe* (`marketing:write`) and the owner's CSV export (audited with the count only). The customer CSV's `marketing_consent` is the address's place on the list (`none` without one) instead of `not_recorded`; the D162 export has a `marketing` section and erasure deletes the row and its history; unconfirmed sign-ups go 30 days after their link ran out (`pruneSubscribers()`, daily).
Staff never subscribe anyone; Kaizena Flow's own forms keep D93's behaviour. All shopper words are `m.news` (nb, sv, da and en by hand, `HAND_WRITTEN_ONLY`, for legal review).

Date: 2026-10-20

The owner: "start wave 5"; the plan's first item and every later unit (segments, broadcasts, automations) rests on a record of consent, which D165 marked missing. A consent is a record with words and a time, never a flag on a customer, so a broadcast can only ever reach an address that asked.
