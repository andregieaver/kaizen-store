# D118: Owners define their own custom fields, as in WordPress's Advanced Custom Fields, and place them in product layouts and pages

**Owners define their own custom fields, as in WordPress's Advanced Custom Fields, and place them in product layouts and pages.** Groups of fields are the store's (`commerce.field_groups`: name, web name, the kinds of thing they can be on, OR-of-AND location rules over a product's kind, audience, categories (with their parents) and tags, or a page's categories, tags and special page, the fields as one jsonb array, main or side column, on/off); what was entered is `commerce.field_values`, one row
per product, page or article and language (`locale` empty for what is the same in every language), keyed by the field's immutable `id` so a field can be renamed without losing its values, taken away with the thing by triggers.
The pure part is `src/lib/custom-fields.ts` (types, `fieldGroupInput`, location rules, conditional logic in ACF's shape (a field is shown by fields above it in the group; a hidden field is kept but never required), `parseFieldChanges`, presets, export and import), the server `src/server/custom-fields.ts`, the generator `/admin/{store}/fields` (`FieldGroupEditor`), the entry form `FieldsForm`/`EntityFields` in the product and page editors (values travel in the same JSON and the same transaction,
checked against the groups the finished thing gets). Field types: text, text area, number, measurement, email, web address, phone, select, radio, button group, checkboxes, yes/no, rich text (Tiptap JSON, never HTML), picture, gallery, video (YouTube/Vimeo), date, date and time, time, colour. Only texts are per language (`isTranslatable()`), and the main language's text shows where another has none.
A field is **private until the owner makes it public** (`access`), and the site shows only public ones (`shownFieldsFor()`, cached under `fieldsTag`, read inside the product page's own cached read so the page stays prerendered). Product layouts get `fields` and `field` parts and pages a `customField` block; the standard product page shows public groups after the description.
`clone_store()` copies groups (rules following the copied categories and tags) and values; `products.attributes`, which nothing used, was dropped. What phase 2 added is in D119.

Date: 2026-09-29

Asked for by the owner, after research into ACF (`docs/custom-fields.md`). Values by id and one row per thing and language avoid ACF's orphaned values after a rename and its two-meta-rows-per-value bloat; placing fields visually in the builder needs no theme code; private by default follows Shopify's metafields, as a shop's owners keep things they would not want on the site.
