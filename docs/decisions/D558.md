# D558: Free shipping and *You saved* for the shop's templates

**Free shipping and *You saved* for the shop's templates (10 November 2026, `src/lib/shop-notes.ts`, `src/server/shop-notes.ts`, `src/components/shop-notes.tsx`, `STORE_NOTES` in `src/lib/store-parts.ts`).** The owner asked for two components, kept apart, offered only when building templates for categories, tags, products, the cart and the checkout.
They are *notes* (`STORE_NOTES`, shop parts drawn by `StorePartSection` on any of their `routes`: `drawsOn()`), offered in the builder's palette (*Basket notes*) only on a page chosen for the cart, checkout, category or tag role (`noteRolesOf()` of the page's roles), and product parts of the same names (`PRODUCT_PARTS.free_shipping`, `you_saved`) in product layouts. **Free shipping** follows the checkout's rule (`shippingCost()`): the market's flat rate is free from its threshold (Settings,
Shipping), compared with the basket's lines before discounts (`cartSummary().subtotal`); it says how much is left with a bar, that shipping is free, or for an empty cart the threshold; nothing without a threshold or for downloads alone.
A shipping method's own threshold (D237) is not read: the flat rate's is the one every basket has. **You saved** is only ever a genuine reduction (Price Indication Directive Art. 6a): on the cart, checkout, category and tag pages, each line against its variant's lowest price of the last 30 days (`current_prices.prior_30d_minor`, converted as prices are), never for a line priced by a list, a break, a subscription, a booking or a gift card, plus the cart's discounts
(`cartSummary().discountMinor`), shown without VAT to a business buyer; on a product page, its headline price's 30-day reference less the price. Nothing is drawn when nothing is saved. Both read the cart per request (`cartView()`, shared with the cart's pieces), so they sit in `<Suspense>`; Add to cart's `refresh()` redraws them. Words in nb, sv, da and en (`m.shopNotes`). **Not for the platform:** Kaizena Flow has no cart or shipping. Tests: `shop-notes.test.ts`, `store-parts.test.ts`.
No migration.
