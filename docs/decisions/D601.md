# D601: A checkout question can be a dropdown, a choice of one or longer text

**A checkout question can be a dropdown, a choice of one or longer text (step 1 of task 4 of `docs/checkout-fields-tasks.md`; 14 November 2026).** Three kinds join `text`, `checkbox` and `checklist` (`CHECKOUT_FIELD_KINDS`): `select`, one option from a closed list, drawn in the cart with the storefront's `Dropdown` (D82), never a native select, starting on *Choose*, which is no answer; `radio`, one option with all of them shown; and `textarea`, several lines of at most 2,000 characters, its
lines kept (at most one empty line in a row, other control characters made spaces), kept as an answer of kind `text`. A `select` or `radio` question has options as a tick list has (2 to 30, no `none`, `checklistProblems()`), may be `sensitive` (a consent box once an option is chosen, refused by `cleanAnswers()` without it), and the order keeps its answer as kind `choice` (the option in the store's main words, its id and the consent; a gift's packing slip leaves a sensitive one out, as D598).
Options belong only to the three option kinds (`hasOptions()`, and the database's check); a question changed to another kind without new options is checked against the ones it has, so a tick list's None of these never becomes a dropdown's option, and a question made a text loses its options and consent.
The editor's kind list names all six, the options' heading and empty line say *Options to choose from* for the one-choice kinds, and the allergen button and None of these show only for a tick list.
