# D387: Payments

**Payments: MobilePay end to end, and orders on terms counted the day they are paid, paid in part (wave 11 unit H, `docs/wave-11-core-and-psi.md` 2.H): built.** **MobilePay.** `mobilepay.int.test.ts` holds, with the stand-in Stripe, what Kaizena Flow sends: a Danish (DKK) and a Finnish (EUR) checkout leave MobilePay in (no `payment_method_types`, nothing excluded), a Norwegian one names it in `excluded_payment_method_types` once the owner switched it off there (D291), and a Danish one once a
checkout rule hides it (D357); a session Stripe completes whose charge says `mobilepay` ends as a paid order whose payment's `method_type` is `mobilepay` (D288), and its refund through `refundOrder()` is recorded succeeded at Stripe.
Which methods Stripe then shows a shopper in each country is Stripe's own (the row says so). **Orders on terms.** An order on payment terms counts as a sale when its balance is paid in full, not when it was placed: `orders.sold_at` is `infinity` from the moment its terms are recorded and `order_payment_terms.paid_at` when that is set, kept only by the trigger `order_payment_terms_sold()` (existing orders on terms set by the migration); `PAID` leaves out an order whose `sold_at` is `infinity`,
and every analytics reader dates a sale by `SOLD_AT` and filters by `soldIn()` (`analytics-sql.ts`: `placed_at` unless `sold_at` is set, each by its own index, never null), Finance, the reports' compiler (`report-query.ts`), the time, traffic, customers, products, discounts, refunds, returns, payments, geography, subscriptions and insight readers and the tax reconciliation alike, so the reconciliation names an order on terms paid in a later period than its invoice as timing like any other;
`docs/analytics.md` (Paid order, Owed on payment terms, Tax date) defines it.
A balance may be paid in part: money recorded outside Kaizena Flow takes an amount (`recordTermsPaidOutside()`'s `amountMinor`, never above what is owed, `amount`), and any payment of a balance goes through `applyTermsMoney()` under the order's lock: the whole balance or more settles it (`markTermsPaid()`, as before), less lowers `balance_minor` and writes `order.terms_part_paid` with what is left; *Pay now* then asks for what is left.
A store whose checkout is Mollie's (D292) takes the balance on Mollie's page (`startMollieTermsPayment()` through `openMolliePayment()`, one payment per press by its idempotency key, back to the account), settled by Mollie's answer through `applyPaymentAnswer()`'s `terms` purpose with the provider it came from (`settleTerms(…, provider)`, `endTermsSession(…, provider)`).
