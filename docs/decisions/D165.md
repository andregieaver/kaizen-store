# D165: Data in and out

**Data in and out: CSV for products, orders and customers, reports, and bulk editing (wave 2, first run).** `docs/wave-2-data.md` is the contract.
One job table, `commerce.data_jobs` (with `data_job_items`, `data_job_assets` and, for the undo, `bulk_edit_batches` and `bulk_edit_items`), runs a product import (a dry run that lists every row's problem by a stable code, then a resumable apply, one product per transaction, through `getProductForEdit()` and `saveProduct()` so `commerce.set_price` and the editor's checks apply; a Kaizen or a Shopify file; pictures fetched only through `safeFetch()` into the media library; never a delete, never a
handle change, never a compare-at price, never Vendor as manufacturer or Charge tax as the exempt VAT category), a product export, an order export (one row per line, VAT split out, order and main currency, copied history marked, Stripe test-mode orders marked `test_payment`, an erased person's order without personal fields, the end of a range being the store's next local midnight), a customer export (`marketing_consent` is `not_recorded`: Kaizen has no consent record until wave 5) and, in the
products list, bulk actions and a grid with preview and undo. Large exports are jobs delivered by a signed link of 60 seconds to the signed-in owner, never emailed; the files are private and deleted after 7 days, or at an erasure only if they can hold the person (`ExportScope`). Order and customer files are owner-only and minimal by default. Every analytics table has a CSV for the period and comparison on the page, built from the page's own loaders (a scan test fails for a table with none).
All CSV goes through one formula-safe writer, `src/lib/csv.ts`; Excel (Nordic) is a dialect.
