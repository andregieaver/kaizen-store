# D160: Unit price indication

**Unit price indication: the price per kilogram, litre or metre beside the price.** A goods variant carries a total measure (g, kg, ml, cl, l, cm, m, m2, piece) and the owner's preferred comparison base (`product_variants.measure_amount/measure_unit/measure_base`).
The shop shows the price per measure on the product page (following the chosen variant and purchase option), listing cards (for the variant the card's price shows), the cart and slide-out cart, checkout, the order page, the order emails, the chat's product cards, and the subscription and weekly-delivery lists.
The product's JSON-LD carries an `Offer.priceSpecification` (`UnitPriceSpecification` with `referenceQuantity` and `valueReference`); `unitPricingMeasure` is a Merchant Center feed name, not schema.org, and waits for the feed in wave 5. The figure is only ever worked out by `unitPrice()` (`src/lib/unit-price.ts`: integers, half up to the minor unit) from the price as shown.
A euro view or a business buyer's net price therefore gets its own figure, never a converted one (435 cents for 250 g is 1740 per kg, where converting the krone figure would give 1739). It is omitted when it equals the price or the price is 0, and it never changes a total: it is display plus a frozen snapshot on the order line (`order_lines.measure_*`, copied by `placeOrder()`, the subscription renewal and `copy_orders()`).
A product marked `sold_by_measure`, or in a product category marked `requires_unit_price` (or under a marked ancestor), cannot be saved active while an active physical variant has no measure (`commerce.unit_price_required()` plus a deferred constraint trigger); existing products are grandfathered and reported (`productsNeedingMeasure()`). A measure on a service or download is refused.
Basket-level discounts, codes, campaigns, credits and reverse-charge relief do not change a line's unit price; a reduced list price does; the 30-day reference never gets one. A market's country decides the base (`UNIT_PRICE_COUNTRY_RULES`): no country allows 100 g or 100 ml today (Germany, Norway and Sweden read, Denmark unread and closed), so every market compares per kg, l, m, m2 or piece.
