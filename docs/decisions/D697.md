# D697: Pictures in page content get the 800 px copy

**Pictures in page content get the 800 px copy (bloat review step 0.44; 16 November 2026).** A picture component, a Before and after and a row's or column's background picture are offered the library's 800 px copy (step 0.33) beside the 480 px and large files: `withPageAlts()` reads `medium_url` in the same one statement per render that reads alt texts and small copies (`libraryFacts()`), and a picture without a copy, or an outside address, is drawn as before.
A picture in a row's column that stays beside others on a phone says its share of the screen (`fixedSizes(width, share)`, `phoneShare()`): on a phone a full-width picture at 2x and a two-column picture at 3x take the 800 px file where they took the 1,600 px one (a local e2e test holds both). `node scripts/page-pictures.mjs` counts page pictures wider than 800 px whose library row has no middle copy. The platform's pages are drawn by the same code.
In production no picture had a copy yet on the day (none uploaded since step 0.33), so the gain shows as owners upload. Commits 1c2133cf, 83bb4d81, 3859ac22.
