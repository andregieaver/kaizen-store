# D232: White boxes on the canvas are cards

**White boxes on the canvas are cards (`docs/admin-style.md` section 8, step 16).** 134 boxes in about 60 pages and components that lay directly on the canvas as `rounded-panel border border-line bg-card` (or dashed empty states) now draw with `CARD_CLASS` (flat in light, lined in dark), keeping their padding and layout.
Left as panels on purpose: boxes inside a card, in a table, a label or a floating panel, and components a caller may place in a card (`ActionForm`'s notice, Work's report table and invoice list); the builder, the product and page editors and previews keep their own. No scan rule: telling a box on the canvas from one in a card needs the caller, so it is judgement in review.
