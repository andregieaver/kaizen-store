# D859: The sign-up for extensions offers bundles and proposes the one that costs less or little more (D748 E7)

Date: 2026-11-20

The owner asked for upselling on `/sign-up/extensions` (20 November 2026), and chose, of the options put to them: bundles shown with the
parts that need a store marked; a bundle that costs no more chosen for the person with the reason said; a bundle for a little more proposed
within a percent the platform owner sets, 20 by default.

- **Bundles over the extensions.** Each bundle on offer is a box above the extensions sold on their own, with its price a month in the
  currency of the country chosen below (`signUpCurrency()`: the country's when the price list has it, else euros), what it holds, what it
  saves against its parts one by one, and which parts work only once a store is added (A/B testing, and what is not sold on its own, such
  as email automation). Choosing one ticks and holds its extensions below; the form sends the bundle, and the sign-up starts the trial of
  every part it holds (`requestExtensions()`), so the checkout's cheapest cover (E6) buys the bundle when the trials end.
- **Cheaper as a bundle.** When a bundle holds everything chosen for no more than the choice costs, the page chooses it for them and says
  why ("Cheaper as the Everything bundle: 1,190 kr instead of 1,200 kr a month"). Taken off, what it replaced comes back and it is only
  offered, with a button, never chosen for them again.
- **A little more for more.** A bundle that holds everything chosen and more, costing at most `platform_settings.extension_upsell_percent`
  % more (Platform → Plans → Extensions, Bundles at sign-up; 0 proposes none), is proposed with what it adds and a button, but only when it
  adds a part they can use now: a part that needs a store is never what it is sold on to an account without one, and is named as working
  once they add their store. With *Also open an online store* ticked every part counts. A bundle they took off is not proposed.
- The rules are pure (`bundleUpsell()` in `src/lib/bundle-upsell.ts`), worked out in the browser as the person ticks
  (`ExtensionPicker`); the arithmetic is there, never in the words. Without script the boxes are plain checkboxes.

With today's price list and email automation not sold on its own, an account without a store can tick the support desk, Work and deals:
neither bundle holds more that such an account can use, so nothing is proposed for more until a store is ticked or the catalogue changes;
the cheaper-as-a-bundle rule fires once a bundle is chosen and more is ticked (Customers and Work: Everything).

Idle cost: none per request or cold start; one column on the one settings row, read with the page, which is prerendered.
