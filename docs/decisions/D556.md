# D556: Drop shipping and eBay, the parts D553 and D555 left

**Drop shipping and eBay, the parts D553 and D555 left: a supplier's return address, suppliers and marketplaces in the privacy notice, listings ended at eBay (11 November 2026, `supplierReturnAddress()` in `src/server/withdrawals.ts`, `src/lib/legal-starters.ts`, `chooseForEbay()` in `src/server/ebay.ts`).** A return or withdrawal whose every line was sent by one drop-shipping supplier that gave a return address (`suppliers.return_address`, its own words) now carries that address, so the
customer's email and page send the goods back to the supplier; a mixed return, or a supplier without one, keeps the store's (its return settings, else its postal address). A carrier's return label (D239) is still booked to the store's structured return address, which the owner sees on the return before booking: a supplier's free-text address cannot be put on a label.
The privacy starter's recipients now name the store's drop-shipping suppliers (they receive the customer's name and delivery address) and its connected marketplaces, in nb, sv, da and en, from new legal facts (`suppliers`, `marketplaces`), so an owner who has either and writes the legal pages from the starter (or has the AI write them, L2) gets the sentence; existing pages are not rewritten, the legal upkeep (L3) flags them as the facts changed. *Stop listing* on the eBay page now withdraws the
published offer at eBay (`POST /offer/{offerId}/withdraw`, from eBay's OpenAPI document), so the listing ends there too; one eBay refuses keeps its state and eBay's reason, to try again, and choosing it again republishes the same offer.
Still not done: Amazon, Zalando and other marketplaces (each its own partner programme), eBay's payouts and fees in the books (eBay's Finances API needs another scope, so a new consent, and the books need the accountant's answer on how fees and the payout clearing are posted). No migration.
