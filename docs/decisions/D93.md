# D93: Pages have an email form and a newsletter sign-up, which email what visitors send to addresses the owner chooses

**Pages have an email form and a newsletter sign-up, which email what visitors send to addresses the owner chooses.** Both are page builder components (`emailForm`, `newsletter`) with up to five recipients each, kept in the page but never sent to the browser: the site draws them through `FormSection` with `publicForm()`, which leaves out the recipients and the email's subject, and the builder offers stores Kaizen's saved parts without them.
An email form asks the owner's questions (name, short text, email, phone, message, a choice from a list, a tick box; the usual ones named in the page's language unless worded), optionally a consent to tick; a newsletter asks an email address, a name if set, and a consent (the language's usual words unless worded).
Visitors send to `/api/forms`, from the site's own pages only (as the chat, D81); the server finds the form by its id in its owner's published pages (`findForm()`), so only a form the site shows sends, and only to its own recipients.
Answers are checked by the same functions in the browser and on the server (`src/lib/forms.ts`), and emailed once to each recipient in the owner's language, with the visitor's address to reply to; visitors never get a copy, as that would let anyone send email through the site.
A newsletter sign-up is confirmed first unless the owner switches that off (double opt-in): the visitor is emailed a link on the site's own address, its token kept only as a hash and good for 7 days, and only on opening it is the sign-up emailed to the recipients, with the words consented to and when, then the visitor returns to the page, which says so.
Robots are told apart by a hidden field and by sending within two seconds, and are told it worked; a visitor may send 5 times an hour and a form 200 times a day, and the same address signs up to a form once a day. `commerce.form_submissions` counts sends for these limits and holds a waiting sign-up's name and consent until it is confirmed; rows go after 30 days (the five-minute cron). A message's words are only in the emails kept (`email_messages`).
New stores' copies of the template's pages have no recipients (`clone_page_content()`). The owner keeps the list of subscribers where they like: Kaizen emails each sign-up and keeps no list.

Date: 2026-09-28

Asked for by the owner: forms that relay to any email address. Relaying rather than keeping a list keeps Kaizen out of the business of sending newsletters, and double opt-in is the proof of consent GDPR and the ePrivacy rules ask for.
