# D806: Admin redesign round 55: product feeds, order history import and redirect export on a phone

**Admin redesign round 55: Product feeds, Import order history and Export redirects on a phone (`docs/audits/admin-redesign-round-55.md`; 19 November 2026, `src/app/admin/(gated)/[store]/feeds/page.tsx`, `src/app/admin/(gated)/[store]/orders/history-import/page.tsx`, `src/components/admin/customer-import-flow.tsx`, `src/app/admin/(gated)/[store]/redirects/export/page.tsx`).** The tallest store pages on a phone after round 54 that no round had reworked.
Below 640 px: Product feeds says what it is for in a line and makes its three switches (product feeds first while off, shopping agents, the ChatGPT feed) rows of one card, each saying on or off and where it goes, with its one press beside it while off (the card's own action) and its card opening under it.
Import order history says what it brings in in a line, gives the file's limit in one line (`CustomerUpload` takes a shorter phone hint where the words give one), makes what an order history is a row and says in a line that the file is deleted after 30 days.
Export redirects says what the file is in a line, leaves the two choices' hints out and makes About the file a row. From 640 px the pages are as before.
Kaizena Flow sells plans, not goods, so it has no product feeds or order history; its redirects are its own store's (D484), so the platform has that change.
