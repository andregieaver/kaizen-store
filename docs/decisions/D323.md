# D323: Quantity rules and price breaks

**Quantity rules and price breaks (wave 6 unit E, `docs/wave-6-catalogue.md` Unit E); specified.** A variant of goods or a bundle has a minimum, a maximum and a step (`product_variants.min_quantity`, `max_quantity`, `quantity_step`), held by `quantityProblem()` (`src/lib/quantity-rules.ts`) in the cart's add and change, in `placeOrder()` and in the WordPress cart; adding puts in the minimum, then whole steps (`nextQuantity()`), and a line holds up to its maximum, else twenty steps (`lineMax()`,
at most 9,999). A product's price breaks (`price_breaks`: from N units, a percentage off or a unit price per market) and, in E3, a price list's (`price_list_breaks`, `price_list_quantity_rules`) price a line only through `commerce.line_price()`, the lowest of the market price, the breaks the quantity reaches and the buyer's list offers, read by the cart, checkout, the product page's table and the WordPress quote. A break is the price at that quantity, never shown as a reduction (decision 3).

Date: 2026-10-26

*Why:* `catalogue.volume-pricing-and-quantity-rules`: Shopify has volume pricing and quantity rules natively; Kaizena Flow had only tiered campaigns and a fixed line maximum of 20. Built in three steps (E1 rules, E2 breaks, E3 a price list's).

functions. Unit E is complete. *The WordPress plugin's cart (4 November 2026):* plugin 1.2.0 followed the rule in its stepper and quantity field, but its own cart route (`class-kaizen-store-rest.php`) still refused a line of more than 20 units, so a variant sold in 24s could not be priced or taken to checkout from a WordPress site; found while writing the developers' WordPress page.
Plugin 1.2.1 takes a line of up to 9,999 (`Kaizen_Store_Rest::MAX_QUANTITY`, held equal to `MAX_ORDER_QUANTITY` by `wordpress-plugin.test.ts`), the store still fitting each line to the rule and the stock, and its product page's quantity field starts at the first variant's own minimum, step and most before the script runs.
