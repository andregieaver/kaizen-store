# D463: E-invoices through Peppol, the parts that need no vendor

**E-invoices through Peppol, the parts that need no vendor (B1 of `docs/own-books-and-sales.md`; `src/lib/peppol.ts`, `src/lib/work-einvoice.ts`, `src/server/peppol-lookup.ts`, `src/server/access-point.ts`, `src/server/einvoice-sending.ts`).** From 1 January 2027 a Norwegian business must send e-invoices to buyers registered as receivers (ELMA), and the buyer's organisation number must be on sales documents from 1 January 2028 (bokføringsforskriften as amended 29 September 2026, Skatteetaten).
Built: Work's invoices and credit notes as EN 16931 UBL with Peppol BIS Billing 3.0's customisation (`workDocumentUbl()`, beside D403's shop documents: hours as `HUR`, the net price after the line's discount to six decimals, UNCL5305 categories from the line and the seller's registration, `NO…MVA` VAT identifiers, the credit transfer with the account and reference, the credit note's invoice as its billing reference; refused with reasons when a receiver would reject it), downloadable on the
Work invoice page. A buyer is a participant by its country's scheme and organisation number (0192 Norway with its check digit, 0007 Sweden, 0184 Denmark), and the network is asked whether it receives each document type: the SML's NAPTR record at `base32(sha256(lowercase value))` (Peppol Policy for use of Identifiers 4.4.0 POLICY 7, the CNAME-to-NAPTR migration's own test vector holds it; NAPTR is the only record since February 2026), then the SMP (`commerce.peppol_receivers`, a day).
The dispatcher's `einvoices` job looks at each document issued to a business in the last two days once, from 7 November 2026, and keeps its state in `commerce.einvoice_sends` (one row a document, no buyer data: the document has it): `not_valid`, `not_receiver`, `waiting`, `pending`, `sent`, `failed` (six tries, a growing wait), `refused`, `delivered`.
With no access point connected a receiver's document is `waiting`: it went by email as every document does, and is never sent later, so nothing reaches a buyer twice. The access point is an interface (`AccessPoint`: `send`, and `status` where the vendor can tell); `configuredAccessPoint()` is null until the owner signs with one (section 6 of the plan). Each document's line in the reader's words is on the order page and the Work invoice page (`einvoiceLines()`).
The shop's and Work's documents already print the buyer's organisation number when it is known; asking for it at checkout is left for 2028. The Work invoice page's own words (title, client, party lines) are now in the catalogues.
Left for when the vendor is chosen: its adapter and credentials, whether a document sent as EHF is still emailed (a receiver's system may book both), and receiving (B3). **The platform**: Kaizena Flow's own plan invoices are Stripe's (D35) and go by email; an owner's business registered in ELMA is owed EHF from 2027 too, which needs Kaizena Flow's own documents through this sender, decided with its books in the console's C3 (`docs/platform-parity.md`).
