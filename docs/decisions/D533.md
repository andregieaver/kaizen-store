# D533: A header over the page has two stages, each with its own background, blur and text colour

**A header over the page has two stages, each with its own background, blur and text colour (10 November 2026, `HeaderOverlay.top` and `.revealed`, `src/lib/header-stages.ts`, `HeaderStageFields`, `globals.css`).** When *Over the page* is chosen, the owner sets how the header looks **at the top of the page** and **when it comes back on scroll** (after scrolling, as `HidingHeader` slides it in again).
Each stage takes a background (*As the header row has it*, *See-through*, a colour with its opacity, or a gradient with every gradient setting, Radial blur among them), a blur of what is behind (the row's, none, or 1 to 20 px) and a text colour. **The header row's settings are the fallback, never overridden by default:** whatever a stage leaves out draws as before, from the row's own colour, gradient, opacity and blur, so no live header changed.
That was the owner's choice after production was read (10 November 2026): of 73 headers set over the page, 58 rows set a frosted blur, 4 a solid colour (2 of them Kaizena Flow's) and 1 a gradient, all of which still show at the top.
A stage's background replaces the theme's header background and the header rows' own colour and picture, video or gradient (now in a `display: contents` box, `data-row-bg`, on every row, marked `data-row`); its blur replaces the rows' blur; on pages the header does not lie over, none of it applies. The top's text colour, kept as `overlay.textColor` before, is read as the top stage's and moved into it when the stage is edited.
The change between stages keeps the header's 200 ms fade. **The platform:** Kaizena Flow's header uses the same editor and `HidingHeader`, so it has it in the same change.
No migration (pages are JSON checked by zod). **The same day:** its first wording took the admin's home 613 bytes over its first-scripts budget (`e2e/signed-in/admin-scripts.spec.ts`), as every screen carries the English admin messages; the stages now use the row background editor's own words and keep only six of their own (55 bytes more than before, not 909).
