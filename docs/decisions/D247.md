# D247: The theme store, step 1

**The theme store, step 1: the list and a theme's page (`docs/theme-phase-3.md`).** Kaizena Flow's themes are listed at `/themes` on its own site, filtered by kind of store, style and what a theme includes (plain links, an address per filter), and each published theme has a page at `/themes/{slug}` with its summary, *Free*, its preview at computer and phone widths (the public preview in frames), what it suits and includes, its section styles, its changelog from the versions' *What's new*,
structured data as a free `CreativeWork` without a price, and *Start with this theme*, which opens sign-up with it chosen. A theme's address and listing (`design_presets.slug`, `listing`) are saved at once from its Details tab, not as a draft; what it includes is worked out from its published version, so a listing cannot claim more. Launch themes are listed at their key. The sitemap lists the store, and `themes` is reserved from Kaizena's own page addresses.
