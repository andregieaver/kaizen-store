# D483: Labels without a carrier agreement, through Sendcloud

**Labels without a carrier agreement, through Sendcloud (decided 5 November 2026, `docs/sendcloud-labels.md`; nothing built).** Sendcloud becomes a fifth shipping carrier (`CarrierId` `sendcloud`) behind the existing `ShippingCarrierAdapter` (D133), on API v3 only (v2 is closed to accounts made after 13 April 2026): each shop connects its own Sendcloud account with its public and secret key (encrypted as every carrier agreement; Sendcloud has no consent flow for platforms), chooses one of
Sendcloud's sender addresses, and buys labels at Sendcloud's own rates with no carrier agreement, invoiced by Sendcloud; Flow never handles the money. It serves shops shipping from Germany, France, the Netherlands, Belgium, Austria, Italy, Spain and the UK (Denmark, Sweden, Czechia, Poland and Portugal only conditionally; Norway and Finland not at all, so Norwegian shops keep D134 to D138). Test mode books only the free `sendcloud:letter` (Sendcloud has no sandbox) and never marks an order sent.
Built in steps: the connection; booking from the order and *Book labels* through `carrier-booking.ts` with `external_reference_id` against duplicates, a 200 with *Announcement Failed* treated as a refusal, labels fetched never stored, customs lines and tax numbers (D343, D157), a new optional `cancel()` for undo; checkout options `store`-priced first (Sendcloud does not say whether its quotes include VAT) and service points; tracking by polling, webhooks later; return labels.
Flow selling labels under its own account (the "centralized" model) needs Sendcloud's written consent (General Terms 9.2) and Flow's own billing, and is a later commercial step only. Sendcloud's facts were read on its own pages on 5 November 2026; the questions for Sendcloud are in the plan, with a message for the owner to send.
The owner chose the three recommended answers on 5 November 2026: each shop's own account now and Flow's own labels only later with Sendcloud's consent; apply to the Tech Partner programme now; the shop's own checkout prices first. **Step 1 built (5 November 2026):** Sendcloud is the tenth `CarrierId` (the fifth when it was written; Instabox, DHL Express, UPS, DHL Paket and nShift came first, D458, D459), `checkable` before any feature is `available` (`canCheck()`), with the two keys, the sender
address chosen from the shop's own Sendcloud addresses (a `pick` field, read by `sendcloudSenders()`), *Check connection* (the keys work and the chosen address is still there), Flow's optional `SENDCLOUD_PARTNER_ID` and `SENDCLOUD_TEST_OPTION` for step 2's test environment; tested against a stand-in for Sendcloud (`sendcloud.test.ts`, `carriers/sendcloud.test.ts`, `sendcloud-shipping.int.test.ts`), not yet against Sendcloud itself.
Migration `20261107082148_sendcloud_carrier` widens the checks built from `CARRIER_IDS`, `carrier_labels_carrier` among them (written again when the branch reached `main` on 7 November 2026, from the ten carriers; applied by CI the same day, run 1074).
