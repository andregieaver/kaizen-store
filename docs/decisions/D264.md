# D264: A theme's pictures are photographs Kaizena Flow takes after each Publish

**A theme's pictures are photographs Kaizena Flow takes after each Publish (the theme showcase plan, phase 2 step 1, `docs/theme-showcase-phase-2.md`).** Each look of a published theme's latest version is photographed on the store its showcase draws (its content pack's published copy, else the Standard store) at a computer's width (1440) and a phone's (390, its first screen at twice the pixels), through the public preview drawn alone, by a job that runs every minute in a route of its own with
one browser per look; the photographs become the full-length pages, the first screens, an 800 × 600 card and a 1200 × 630 share image, kept as the platform's files (not in the media library) per version and showcase in `commerce.theme_shots`.
The theme store's card, page, share image and structured data, the stage's frames while they load and the design cards at sign-up use them wherever the theme has no picture of its own; a launch theme's photograph by hand is only the fallback until they are made. A look's older set is set aside when the newer one is made and deleted with its files a week later. Platform admins see each look's pictures, what is being made and why one could not be, with *Try again*, on the theme's page.
