# D161: VAT, OSS and IOSS reports read from invoices and credit notes

**VAT, OSS and IOSS reports read from invoices and credit notes.** `/admin/{store}/analytics/tax` has a VAT view per country, rate and basis (net, VAT, gross, documents, orders; reduced and shipping rates, reverse charge and exempt shown as such), a quarterly OSS view (Union scheme parts 2a, 2b, 2d, Part 3 corrections, Part 4, Part 5; non-Union scheme) and a monthly IOSS view, in euro at the ECB reference rate of the period's last day (or the next publication day) kept in
`commerce.ecb_reference_rates`, with an audited owner override, converted once per group in BigInt; the figures come from documents only (`commerce.tax_document_groups()`), not from orders. Orders with no document are counted and itemised by cause in a reconciliation that ties the report to Finance and to sum(`tax_minor`) of paid orders in every currency.
Books mode counts a credit note in its own period; filing mode follows the Commission's OSS guidelines (a later-period credit is a Part 3 correction of the sale's period). Seller country, member state of identification and dispatch country are frozen on the order's `vat_treatment`. Four fixed, formula-safe CSV layouts, an export log with a drift line, deadlines, registration warnings and read-only AI tools.
It is the owner's data for the accountant and Kaizen files nothing; no consumer text is added. Review fixes: a non-EU market is national only for a store established there; the Member State of dispatch is carried for 2b and 2d (Art. 369g(2)); payment-day dating cites Art. 63 and 65, not 66a; ECB fetches are rate-limited; the VAT view and the reconciliation are read in one snapshot.
Rows: `analytics.tax-and-vat-reports-by-rate-and-jurisdiction` Full; `checkout.oss-and-ioss-support` and `international.ioss` stay Partial (bucket B: a registration the owner holds, nothing is filed or checked against a register); stale gap texts of the eu-vat and b2b rows rewritten. *Why:* the VAT total on Finance could not be broken down for the owner's accountant and Shopify has no EU report either. Migrations: `tax_reports`, `tax_reports_rules`, not yet applied to production.
