# D451: Moving from any other platform

**Moving from any other platform: paste your address, we do the rest (proposed 3 November 2026, `docs/moving-from-other-platforms.md`; nothing built).** A shop on a platform with no mover of its own is recognised from its live site (`PLATFORM_SIGNS`: generator tags, headers, known paths and domains), and sent to that platform's mover when one exists; otherwise, only after its owner proves the address is his (a domain the store has verified, a meta tag, or the `_kaizen.{host}` TXT record; checked
again before every read; no "this is mine" box), its public shop is read through `safeFetch()` with robots.txt obeyed (the sitemap, schema.org `Product`/`ProductGroup` and Open Graph, `BreadcrumbList`, pages through the replicator D150, look through D330) and copied by the move engine of D445, behind private mode.
Customers, orders and stock come from any file in any layout: known column names are mapped in code, the model names only the rest from a closed list and never writes a value, and the existing dry run (D165, D366, D384) checks the result; a consent column from a file is never used. Open for the owner: proof without exceptions, page prices read as VAT-inclusive, the next movers (Mystore.no and Quickbutik recommended), and whose AI key maps the files (section 12 of the plan).
