# D257: Content packs

**Content packs (the theme showcase plan, phase 1 step 2, `docs/theme-showcase-phase-1.md`).** Six demo stores, one a trade (Field & Fold for fashion, Lumen Skin for beauty, Harbour Pantry for food and drink, Still House for home, Volt & Wire for electronics, North Hair Studio for services), written in code (`CONTENT_PACKS`) and kept as store templates (`store_starters.pack_key`, `pack_version`, `pack_pictures`), set up from *Content packs* on the platform's Store templates page
(`syncContentPacks()`): each a clean English store of its trade at `/s/pack-{key}` selling where the template does, with its own categories, six to eight products (through the product editor's save), menus, a home page, an about page and an All products page in the core section styles, the template's own demo archived; a pack whose code is newer is written again, its pictures kept.
Its pictures (the home page's two and one a product) are made one a request by Kaizena Flow's picture model (`makeNextPackPicture()`, feature `content_pack`, briefs with no words, logos or faces) from the page's *Make the pictures*, kept in the pack store's library and replaceable there; a pack is published only once every picture is made.

Date: 2026-10-16

The plan's recommendation 3 and 6 (first packs: fashion, beauty, food and drink, home, electronics, services): a preset (step 3) needs a store of its trade to be shown on, and a new store (step 4) a store to start from.
