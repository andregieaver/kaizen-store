# D156: Wave 1 of the parity plan

**Wave 1 of the parity plan: compliance and money correctness.** `docs/wave-1.md` is the contract for the 18 rows of wave 1, in seven units run in two lanes: the money lane (1a tax profile and VAT engine, 1b invoices and credit notes, 1c VAT, OSS and IOSS reports, 1d unit price) and the trust lane (1e legal starters, terms at checkout, accessibility and PCI hardening, 1f staff two-step sign-in, roles and the activity log; 1g GDPR after 1b).
Every rule that comes from a law carries its source and the date it was read and is flagged for an accountant or lawyer; every consumer legal text is hand-written in nb, sv, da and en. Defaults taken: destination VAT for consumers is kept, IOSS above 150 EUR is not handled (marking and reports only), TOTP without passkeys, 24 months of audit log.
