# D40: A store keeps who it is and where to find it on its Company page

**A store keeps who it is and where to find it on its Company page: the business, the office with its hours, and any stores and pickup points.** The page (under Store in the sidebar) holds the business details from setup (the owner's to change), the office's address, phone, directions and opening hours, and a list of physical stores and pickup points, each with a name, address, phone, directions and, if it shows them, opening hours.
Opening hours are the usual week (each day closed or open from one time to another) and exceptions: seasons, a run of dates with their own week (summer hours), and single dates, closed or with their own hours (a public holiday); a single date wins over a season, and a season over the usual week. The office, stores and pickup points are kept in one table, at most one office per store and 50 other places.
