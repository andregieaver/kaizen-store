# D143: Kaizen's own front page, blog and 404 page are chosen from its pages

**Kaizen's own front page, blog and 404 page are chosen from its pages**, as a store's front page and special pages are (D54, D112).
`commerce.platform_page_roles` (`role` front, blog or not_found; one place per page; a trigger keeps it to Kaizen's own pages of type `page`; a deleted page lets its place go) is read by `platformPageForRole()` (cached under the pages' tag): `/` draws the chosen page, `/blog` the chosen blog page, and an address that does not exist the chosen 404 page (still a 404), each with the page's own search and sharing texts; the page's own address then leads to the place's (`permanentRedirect`), and its
address is left out of the sitemap and llms.txt. Chosen at `/admin/platform/pages` under *Special pages*, each with a *Start from a new page* that makes and publishes a starter (the front page's has the standard words, the buttons and the Plans component, D142). Kaizen has no cart, account or catalogue pages, so those places are the stores'.
