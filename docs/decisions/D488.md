# D488: The bank's own fees and interest, posted from the statement with a press

**The bank's own fees and interest, posted from the statement with a press (B4 route 1, step 3, 7 November 2026, `src/lib/bank-own-lines.ts`, `postBankOwnLine()` in `src/server/bank-payments.ts`).** For a fee the bank charged or interest it paid or took, the statement is the voucher and there is no VAT (financial services are outside it), so the owner needs nothing else to post it.
A line the books do not have whose words read as a fee (*gebyr* and its compounds such as *kontoholdsgebyr*, *omkostninger*, a package's monthly price, *fee*, *charges*) or as interest (*rente*, *renter* and their compounds such as *kreditrenter*, *interest*), and not as both, is offered on the Bank card: *A bank fee.
Post it to 7770 Bank- og kortgebyr?* One press posts it in the bank series on the bank's day against 1920: a fee to 7770, interest in to 8050 (interest from bank deposits), interest out to 8150, no VAT code, once for the line (`source_kind` `bank_line`; the account is added to the chart when the book lacks it), matches the line to it and audits it (`work.bank_line_posted`). An invoice offer (D487) comes first when a line has one.
A card purchase or any other cost is never offered: it needs its receipt, and its VAT, which is B3.

Date: 2026-11-07

Most lines left after the invoices are matched are the bank's own, and each month brings the same few; posting them by hand meant typing two rows each. No migration.
