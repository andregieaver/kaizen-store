# D695: Contracts for deals, signed on our own signing page

**Contracts for deals, signed on our own signing page (`docs/contracts.md`, designed, not built).** A contract is made from a store's template (clauses, optional clauses, a closed list of fields filled from the deal, its person and company, the linked quote and the business details), linked to a deal (`DEAL_LINK_KINDS` gains `contract`), frozen when sent as a snapshot and a PDF with its SHA-256 (the invoices' pipeline, D159), and signed on a page of our own (`/s/{store}/{market}/sign/{token}`).
Level 1 first, a simple electronic signature with no vendor: an email code, the name typed, the evidence kept per signer (time, IP, user agent, the hash shown), and a signed PDF with an audit page sealed with Kaizena Flow's certificate. Level 2 later on the same page: BankID and its neighbours through one EU identity broker. A sent contract moves its deal to the pipeline's contract stage; signed by all wins it. Kaizena Flow's own store has Deals, so the platform gets it in the same change.
Built in the five steps of the design's section 7.
