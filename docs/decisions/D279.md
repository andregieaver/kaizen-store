# D279: A blank photograph of a theme is a failed try, never kept

**A blank photograph of a theme is a failed try, never kept.** Orbit's card was white and two of its highlights showed an empty computer screen: its own look and its Nebula preset had been photographed at a computer's width as one flat white, one screen long (the page had not drawn when it was photographed), and Arcade's Vapor at a phone's. `shootLook()` only refused a page without `[data-design-preview]`, so these were stored as made, and nothing tried them again.
Now the job refuses a photograph whose first screen is one flat colour (`pictureIsBlank()` in `theme-shot-files.ts`: every colour channel's standard deviation under 1; a theme's first screen always holds words or a picture) before anything is stored, as a failed try with *The page was still blank when it was photographed* (`BLANK_PAGE`), so the look is photographed again.
The migration puts the three blank units back to waiting, with the highlights composed from them (Orbit's), picked out by a first screen under 5 KB (the blank ones were about 2 KB, every real one 57 KB or more); they are made again under their own addresses, which the bucket serves with a five-minute cache.

Date: 2026-10-18

The owner: "The Orbit theme is still missing its thumbnail and some of the highlight images." Production: every computer and phone photograph checked for one flat colour; these three were the only ones, all from the run at 16:14, when two themes had just been published and runs still overlapped (before D278).
