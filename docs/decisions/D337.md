# D337: Checkout branding

**Checkout branding (wave 7 unit C, `docs/wave-7-storefront.md` section 4); built.** Stripe's payment form takes the store's look: `checkoutAppearanceFor()` works the theme out on the server (both colour sets, the body font's stack, the field and button corners in pixels) and hands it to the checkout's client component as plain data, drawn by `stripeAppearance()` and `stripeFonts()` (`src/lib/checkout-appearance.ts`: no zod, nothing of the server).
The owner's choices are `ThemeSettings.checkout` (`CheckoutLook`: the logo on checkout, the page's background or the surface panel, the pay button's own colour and corners, the fields outlined, filled or underlined), edited in the theme editor's *Checkout look* card and saved with the theme. The body font reaches Stripe's frames as our own stylesheet on the page's own origin (`/api/fonts/css/{slug}?for=stripe`, now served with `Access-Control-Allow-Origin: *`, as the font files already were).

Date: 2026-10-27

*Why:* `checkout.checkout-branding`: the payment form was drawn in fixed black and white whatever the store looked like. The pay routes' policy is unchanged: no new script origin (`docs/pci.md`). No migration: every template carries the defaults, so a stored theme is filled in on read. Not done: a checkout-only background colour (the surface panel is the one choice); the e2e checks the saved setting and the page's variables, not Stripe's frames, which need Stripe keys.
