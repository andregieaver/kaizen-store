# D353: Shopping agents

**Shopping agents (wave 8 unit I, `docs/wave-8-analytics-ai.md` section 4, I); built.** *Discovery*: a store's UCP business profile (`ucpProfile()`, `src/lib/shopping-agents.ts`; UCP version `2026-08-25`, read at ucp.dev on 28 October 2026: the `dev.ucp.shopping` service over MCP, the capabilities `dev.ucp.shopping.catalog.search`, `…catalog.lookup` and `dev.ucp.shopping.cart`, no payment handler) at `/.well-known/ucp` on its own host and `/s/{store}/.well-known/ucp` until then; `/agents.md`
(`agentsMarkdown()`) and a line in its llms.txt point to it. *The endpoint*: `/api/ucp/{store}/mcp`, JSON-RPC over HTTP, apart from `/api/mcp` (Kaizen Life's only).
Tools (`runAgentTool()`, `src/server/shopping-agents.ts`): `search_catalog` (keyword search by `rankedSearch()` without a meaning model, else the newest; at most 10), `lookup_catalog` and `get_product` (the product page's own `getProduct()`: variants, options, pictures, the price with VAT in the country's currency as UCP's minor units, available or not from `getVariantStock()`, `metadata.cartable`), the country from `context.address_country` (`offeredMarketIn()`), ids as
`gid://kaizena-flow/Product|ProductVariant/{uuid}` (a bare uuid read too); `create_cart` checks its lines as the WordPress hand-off does (`quoteCart()` for the subtotal, `createHandoffCart()` with `source = 'agent'`) and answers the store's own `continue_url`, good once for 15 minutes; nothing is ordered or paid there.
No token, no cookie, nothing of a person; a budget per store and per caller (`takeBudget()`, `agent`: 60 a minute per caller, 1,200 per store; the caller a daily hash of the address); counts logged.
It answers only while `agentProblem()` finds nothing: an open, real store, the shop on, not private, AI assistants allowed under SEO & Reach, and its *Shopping agents* switch on (`stores.shopping_agents`, off by default, written only by `src/server/shopping-agent-settings.ts`, audited `store.shopping_agents_*`, never copied).
`/api/` is already closed to every crawler in robots.txt. *Feeds*: `FEED_CHANNELS` gains `openai`, ChatGPT's product feed in OpenAI's spec (read 28 October 2026: CSV, its required columns, `price` as `amount CURRENCY`, variants by `group_id` and `listing_has_variations`, `is_eligible_search` true and `is_eligible_checkout` false), served only while its own switch is on (`feed_settings.channels`, `AGENT_FEED_CHANNELS`, off by default, `switchAgentChannel()`); Perplexity and Copilot wait for their
specs to be read. *Attribution*: the channel `ai_agent` (`CHANNELS`) for a visit from `chatgpt.com`, `chat.openai.com`, `perplexity.ai`, `copilot.microsoft.com` or `gemini.google.com` or a `utm_source` naming one, with no cookie; an order from an agent's cart counts there whatever visit opened it (the channel table, first orders and the report builder's channel). Marketing → Product feeds has the two switches.
Nothing reaches an agent channel's programme until it accepts the store, so `ai.agentic-storefronts-chatgpt-gemini-copilot` stays partial (bucket B); `ai.agent-ready-ucp-or-mcp-endpoints` is full.
