# D293: Payment terms for company accounts

**Payment terms for company accounts (wave 4 unit F).** `docs/wave-4-payments.md` 8 is the contract. A company (D108) has payment terms (15, 30 or 60 days, or none) and an optional credit limit kept in the store's main currency, set on its page by staff with `customers:write` (audited `company.terms_changed`).
Who may buy on terms is only `termsOffer()` (`src/lib/payment-terms.ts`): the business feature on, a signed-in owner or employee of an active company with terms, goods bought once only, something to pay, and the limit (what the company already owes plus this order, converted at the store's rates, a currency with no rate refused) not passed.
The cart offers *Pay by invoice* beside paying now, asking who pays with the billing address and showing the store's terms of sale (recorded before the order is confirmed).
The order is confirmed as a venue booking is (D66): `placeOrder({ terms })` checks again under a lock on the company row and writes `order_payment_terms` (due day = the store's day plus the days) and the whole total as the balance; `confirmOnTerms()` adds a pending `terms` payment (never money: the order page's key and what a cancellation voids) and completes the order, so it is `paid` as a shop status, its stock drawn and its invoice issued with a `due` line (due date, terms, the supply date
the order day). It is not a sale until its money arrives: *Pay now* on the order page opens Stripe's hosted page for the balance (`startTermsPayment()`, `applyPaymentAnswer()`'s `terms` purpose, `settleTerms()`), or staff record it paid outside Kaizena Flow (`recordTermsPaidOutside()`, D173's rules and cash ceilings).
Cancelled before it is paid, the invoice gets a credit note through a `void` refund (no money, no provider, no refund in any figure, no integration event) and the goods go back in stock. The five-minute job sends the reminder three days before and the notice the day after (`order.payment_due`, `order.payment_overdue`, switchable), and the staff alert *Payment overdue* (owners by default).
The control center says how many are overdue, the Orders list filters `terms_due`/`terms_overdue`, and Finance shows what is owed today (docs/analytics.md *Owed on payment terms*). A balance on terms earns no bonus or referral reward. The customer words are hand-written in nb, sv, da and en (`HAND_WRITTEN_ONLY`) and need legal review. Not built: part payments, *Pay now* for a store on Mollie alone, writing off a sent order that is never paid.
