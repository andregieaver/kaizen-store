# D395: Saved reports emailed on a schedule

**Saved reports emailed on a schedule (wave 12 unit A, `docs/wave-12-after-the-core.md` A): built.** A saved report (D345) gets a schedule on its page (*Email this report*, `analytics:write`): daily, weekly on a weekday or monthly on day 1 to 28, from an hour on the store's clock, to members who may read analytics (`reportRecipients()`), at most ten schedules a store and ten people each (`commerce.report_schedules`, written only by `src/server/report-schedules.ts`, audited
`analytics.report_schedule_saved`/`_deleted` with counts, never addresses). `sendScheduledReports()` in the five-minute dispatcher claims the store day first (`last_run_on`), runs the report through `runReport()` alone for its own period as of that day, reads each recipient's membership again, and emails (`staff.report`) the first 25 rows and the total in the recipient's admin language, what the figures leave out, a link and the whole table as a CSV, keyed by schedule, day and account.
An erased account is taken off every list (a schedule left with nobody goes); a store copy carries none (`COPY_RULES` `never`). Migrations `20261029152419_report_schedules`, `20261029153701_report_schedules_rules` (applied by CI, run 735; advisors INFO only).
