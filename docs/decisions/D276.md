# D276: The theme's page sells the theme, and it has a full-screen preview

**The theme's page sells the theme, and it has a full-screen preview (step 2 of `docs/themes-content-and-demo-stores.md`).** A theme's page in the theme store opens with the theme large: its name and sentence, a computer and a phone showing the chosen look on the theme's own content (its photograph at once, the live preview scaled from a computer's and a phone's real width over it), its presets as swatches in their own colours under the frames, and two buttons for the chosen look, *Use this
theme* (sign-up with the look) and *Preview*, with *Already have a store? Add it to your store* under them. The chosen look is kept in the address (`?preset=`), so a shared link opens on it while the page stays prerendered. Then its highlights, its own pictures, *What you get*, and the details (about, kinds, styles, section styles, version, changelog) folded under *Details*.
The full-screen preview is the theme store's own page, `/themes/{slug}/preview` (`/{slug}/preview` on its own host, Kaizena Flow's address moving there): a root layout of its own with a slim bar (back to the theme, the presets, Home or A product, computer or phone, *Use this theme*) over the inert public preview, which now takes `show=home|product`; every choice is a link, so it works without JavaScript. The list's cards show each look as a dot in its colours.
