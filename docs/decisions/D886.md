# D886: Owners guided from trial to plan by service emails: six steps of their own store's journey, every owner, one switch to t

Date: 2026-11-23

**Owners guided from trial to plan (23 November 2026, email plan M4.5, was E4.6).** A new owner heard nothing from Kaizena Flow between
signing up and their trial ending: no word when their store opened or took its first order, and no warning before the trial ran out and
the store went private.

- The owner chose service emails (23 November 2026): every owner gets them because they are about their own store and contract: what
  happened, the next practical step and, near the end, when the trial ends. They carry no offer or promotion, and owners stay off
  Kaizena Flow's marketing list (D467: news only for those who asked).
- Six steps, each once per store, from the store's own state, read by the five-minute dispatcher's `ownerJourney`
  (`src/server/owner-journey.ts`): the trial started, the store published, its first paid order, the trial ending (two to three days
  left with no plan chosen), a plan paid (live), and the store made private after its trial. Each looks back two days only, and its
  email's key (`owner-journey:{step}:{store}`) keeps it to one, so a missed run sends late, never twice.
- Not the flows engine: flows reach people on a subscriber list and are the owner's to change; these reach every owner of a store
  (the first active owner) whatever lists they are on, with words written by hand in English and Norwegian (`src/lib/owner-journey.ts`).
- Each email says why it came and links to the account's settings, where *Emails that help you run your store* turns them off
  (`accounts.owner_journey_off`). Sent from Kaizena Flow as `platform.owner_journey`, a staff email; never to a template, a demo, a
  sandbox or Kaizena Flow's own store.

Idle cost: one statement every five minutes, its look back two days; an email only when a store passes a step.
