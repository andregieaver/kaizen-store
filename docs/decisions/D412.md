# D412: Dual page

**Dual page: two of the builder's documents side by side (`docs/dual-page.md`).** Asked for by the owner on 1 November 2026; the eight questions put back were answered the same day: any two documents of one site (page, article, product layout, header, footer, entry page; mixed kinds; "product" is the product layout), the second in the address (`?with=article:{id}`, `new:article`; `parseDualWith()`); rows, columns and components dragged onto either page from the shared sidebar; a part dragged
from one page to the other is copied with new ids and every setting (global uses stay linked, translations come along under the copy's ids, an A/B test does not), never moved, and refused with the save's own reason where the target kind cannot hold it (`dropProblem()` over `pageRulesProblem()`, moved to `src/lib/page-rules.ts`); within one page a drag still moves.
One `DndContext` for both (`DualFrame`), each canvas's drag ids prefixed with its side; the sidebars, settings, bar, undo and versions are the page last pointed at, outlined; the screen size is one for both; *Scroll together* (by pixels, kept per browser); Custom CSS's *This page* has Left and Right. *Publish both* asks the checker's questions for both in one dialog, saves both as drafts (every server check, nothing live on a refusal), then publishes both; *Save both*; Ctrl/Cmd+S saves both.
*Open beside* picks a document of any kind or a new one; *Create new* asks the kind, then side by side or on its own. Not offered for the staging set's copy or an A/B test's version; nothing is copied while either page is in another language. Both a store's builder and Kaizena Flow's own.
A saved template (row, column, component or page layout) can be one of the two (`?with=template:{id}`, the owner's answer: "template" meant saved templates): edited as the kind of page it is for, saved back with `updatePart` (never published), its own translations kept as the Saved dialog keeps them.
