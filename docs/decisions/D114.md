# D114: Stores run campaigns

**Stores run campaigns: offers without a code, for a time.** A campaign (`commerce.campaigns`, `src/lib/campaigns.ts`, `src/server/campaigns.ts`; `/admin/{store}/campaigns` under Sales) is one of three kinds: *a percentage off*; *buy N, pay for M* (3 for 2: in every full group of N units, over all the lines it reaches together, the N − M cheapest are free); or *a free product* added to the order when the basket comes to an amount per market (in the country's own currency, converted when read
like a code's minimum, D109). Each can be for the whole store or for chosen products and categories or tags (`product_ids`, `term_ids`; a category takes in its subcategories through `termsOfProducts()`; for a gift the choice is what counts towards the amount), and has an optional start and end (Norwegian time) and a switch.
Campaigns take a reduction off *goods bought once* (not appointments, stays, rentals or subscriptions), first: the buyer's group discount (D108) then comes off what is left (`memberOffAfterCampaign()`), then any code (D31; a percentage code on a reduced line comes off what is left, `applyDiscount()`). A unit gets at most one campaign: the campaign giving most on the lines still free wins, ties to the older (`applyCampaigns()`).
A free product is only goods the store ships itself (`giftItems()`: physical, kind goods, no host, not subscription-only, active, priced in the market, in stock for the whole quantity), and only when the order ships anyway and has no host's listing; it is a line of the order at its list price with all of it taken off (`order_lines.gift`, `campaign_discount_minor`, `campaign_id`), so it is held, shipped, invoiced and shown like any other line, and shown as struck through and "Gratis" in the cart,
checkout, order pages and emails. `cartSummary()` and `placeOrder()` both use `evaluateCampaigns()` and agree to the øre (`checkout-kinds.int.test.ts` has a scenario for each kind, with a group, a company, a code and euro); the order keeps the campaigns' part (`orders.campaign_discount_minor`, `campaign_label`), `discountParts()`/`discountNote()` split a discount into campaign, group and code, and Stripe takes it all in the same one-time coupon, named after them.
A gift line is not part of the cart, so the checkout's "cart changed" check leaves it out. Changing or deleting a campaign never changes an order. New stores get none (`clone_store()` does not copy them). Not yet: a notice on product pages and cards (they are cached, and a time-limited notice needs a run-time read), campaigns for a customer group only, a limit on uses, stacking two campaigns on one unit, free shipping and "spend X get Y off" as kinds, and the AI manager's tools for campaigns.

Date: 2026-09-29

Asked for by the owner: scheduled and time-limited campaigns for single products or by category/tag, a free product above a basket amount, and 3 for 2. Taking campaigns off before the group's discount and codes, from goods bought once, keeps the cart, checkout and Stripe agreeing and leaves subscriptions' and bookings' own pricing as it is.
The free product being an ordinary order line at a full discount, rather than a special case, keeps stock, shipping, invoices and returns working unchanged; giving only goods the store ships, and only to orders that ship, avoids consent for downloads, shipping added by a gift and a gift from a host's stock.
