# D876: Email automation's send allowance and contact limit: told at 80 %, new broadcasts wait at the limit with one press to move up (D748 E10 part three)

Date: 2026-11-23

D748 section 10: each size of email automation includes 10 emails per contact a month, so a heavy sender pays for their volume and no tier
loses money; the price sheet's "beyond" for email is the next size. The owner chose, of the options put to them (23 November 2026): ask to
move up. Told at 80 %; at the limit new broadcasts wait with one button, while flows and order emails keep sending; no surprise bills.

- **The size an account has** (`accountSize()`, `src/server/extension-sizes.ts`): the biggest of what it bought on its own, in a bundle or
  on trial (a bundle or a trial without a size is the smallest) and what its plan includes while it is on the plan.
- **Emails a month.** The size's contacts times its emails per contact is the account's `marketing_emails_month` (`readStoreLimits()`, a
  source in place of the plan's number; a platform admin's override still comes first), counted across the account's stores as D748 E4's
  limits are. Broadcasts and flows count; order emails never do.
- **Contacts.** People subscribed on the lists of the account's open stores, each address once.
- **Told and held** (`allowanceVerdict()`, `broadcastRefusal()`, pure in `src/lib/send-allowance.ts`): at 80 % of either, Broadcasts says
  what is used with one press to move up; at the limit it says new newsletters wait. A broadcast does not start while the lists hold more
  contacts than the size, or when it would pass the month's emails; a scheduled one goes back to a draft with the reason, as before. Flows
  are never held here. Account, Extensions shows the usage always, with the same press.
- **Moving up** (`moveUpExtension()`): the next size, bought on its own the cheapest way with everything else the account pays for
  (`accountCover()` with a wanted size: a bundle holds the smallest, so a part moved up leaves the bundle). A size bigger than the plan's
  is bought on its own and is not "in the plan already" (`resolveItems()` and `coverFacts()` compare the sizes' positions). The press is
  the account owner's; anyone else is told who can. At the biggest size it says to write to us.
- Words: the `sendAllowance` catalogue, and the refusals in marketing's problems (`overContacts`, `overEmails`), in contacts rather than
  size labels ("Move up to 10,000 contacts").

Not yet: an email to the owner at 80 % (the admin says it where newsletters are written). Kaizena Flow's own account has every extension
and no billing row, so nothing here applies to the platform.

Idle cost: a few reads on the Broadcasts and Extensions pages and when a broadcast starts; the store's limits read one more union branch,
for stores billed through their account only.
