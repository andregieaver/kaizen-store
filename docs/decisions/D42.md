# D42: Kaizen has its own pages, built from blocks in the platform admin, and its own header and footer, built like a store's

**Kaizen has its own pages, built from blocks in the platform admin, and its own header and footer, built like a store's.** Under Platform → Pages, platform admins list every page (status, address, whether search engines and AI assistants may read it) and create, edit, publish, unpublish and delete pages.
A page has a title, an address made from the title as it is typed (editable; Kaizen's own routes are reserved), a picture with its description (shown at the top and when shared), a search title and description (empty ones use the title and the start of the text), a switch for search engines (off: `noindex` and left out of the sitemap) and one for AI assistants (off: left out of llms.txt, and robots.txt closes the page to AI assistants' and AI training crawlers), and its content: blocks one under
another. The first block is rich text (headings 2–4, paragraphs, bold, italic, underline, links, bulleted and numbered lists, quotes, lines), edited with Tiptap in the browser; blocks are added, edited, deleted (asking first when they hold text) and put in order by dragging (dnd-kit, with a keyboard) or with Up and Down. Save keeps a draft; Publish copies it to what visitors see, so a published page is worked on without changing it until published again (`pages.draft` and `pages.published`).
Pages are served at `/{address}`, prerendered at build and cached until the next publish; the address of a page that was published moves only when it is published again, and the old address then redirects permanently (308) to the new one, a rule in the database (`page_redirects`). A signed-in platform admin sees Edit page on the page itself, worked out in the browser so the page stays cached for everyone.
Kaizen's front page, sign-up and pages get a header, footer, phone bottom bar and slide-out menu like a store's (D30), from a logo, a header menu and a footer menu edited under Platform → Header and footer with the store's menu editor; a menu link goes to a page (by id, so it follows the page to a new address and shows only while the page is published), the front page, sign-up, sign-in or a web address. The footer shows Kaizen's business details, edited on the same page.

Date: 2026-09-25

Asked for by the owner, as the first step of a page and article builder for the platform, then for stores (`pages.store_id` is there for them). Content is stored as the editor's JSON, checked on the server against what Kaizen shows (unknown blocks, marks and link addresses such as `javascript:` are refused) and rendered as elements, never as HTML. There is no revision history yet: publishing replaces what was published. Pages are in English, like the rest of Kaizen's own site.
Tiptap and dnd-kit are libraries running in the admin's browser (MIT); they send nothing anywhere, so the residency register is unchanged. Business details start empty and should be filled in before launch (ehandelsloven § 8).
