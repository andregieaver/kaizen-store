# D672: Paging and an outside heartbeat

**Paging and an outside heartbeat (`docs/100-day-challenge.md` F3; 16 November 2026, `src/server/push.ts`, `src/server/challenge-watch.ts`, `src/lib/cron-health.ts`, `src/app/api/health/cron/route.ts`).** A challenge store's stall (A6, D608) reached the platform team by email only, once a day, and every in-house check runs on the scheduler, so a scheduler that stops takes its own alarms with it (PSI REL-06). **Paging:** the day's first email of a stall (one not sent before under its
`stallKey()`) now also rings the platform admins' phones: `sendPlatformPush()` sends to every device any platform admin turned on, whichever store it was turned on for, each endpoint once, with urgency high, in the admin's language ("Challenge store {store} needs a look" and the stall in words), removing a device the push service says is gone.
A platform admin turns notifications on on Your account for any store they belong to; there is no device list of the platform's own, as a phone is a phone. **Outside heartbeat:** `/api/health/cron` answers 200 while every scheduled route (`route.*`, what the scheduler calls) has finished a run within its allowance (`cronHeartbeat()`, the same rule as the platform home's `cronHealth()`), and 503 when one has gone quiet, when no route is kept at all, or when the ledger cannot be read; it says only
counts (routes, late routes, late and failing dispatcher jobs), never a job's error, and a late or failing dispatcher job does not stop the beat, as it has its own email. An uptime monitor outside Kaizena Flow (the owner's choice, an EU service) polls it every five minutes and pages on a failure: the owner's step.
