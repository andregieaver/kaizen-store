# D269: Three more signature themes

**Three more signature themes: Atelier, Arcade and Botanica (`docs/signature-themes.md` step 3).** Each is a world of its own, made as Orbit was (its header, footer, product page and stylesheet, a content pack with designed home and about pages, art drawn by code, two presets on other packs), and each passes `signatureCheck()` and `themeCheck()`. **Atelier** is quiet editorial luxury: Cormorant Garamond over Jost on warm paper, a centred logo between two quiet rows, small-capital buttons, roman
numerals, a glaze band, a buy box that stays in view; its store *Ostra Studio* sells eight stoneware pieces in three collections, with the presets *Linen* (home pack) and *Noir* (dark, fashion pack). **Arcade** is neon and retro games: Tilt Neon headings that glow over Space Grotesk on a dark screen with scan lines, pixel labels in Silkscreen, "Level" numbers, a blinking cursor; its store *Neon Alley* sells eight neon signs, with the presets *Vapor* (beauty pack) and *Terminal* (electronics
pack). **Botanica** is organic, plants and paper: Fraunces over DM Sans, a floating pill header, arched and leaf-shaped pictures, a swaying sprig, rounded cards; its store *Fernhouse* sells eight house plants, with the presets *Terracotta* (food pack) and *Night garden* (dark, services pack). The pages share their blocks through `src/lib/signature/kit.ts`.
One fix for everyone, found on Atelier's glaze band: a row taller than its content (`height`, D184) with its columns in the middle or at the bottom kept them at the top, because the columns did not fill the row; they now do when the row places them (`rowGrid()`), so the alignment has room, and a row aligned to the top or as tall as its content is unchanged.

Date: 2026-10-17

The owner chose the three worlds proposed ("Let's go with your recommendations"). No migration. Production needs a platform admin to press *Sync content packs* and *Sync launch themes* after the deploy, then publish the Ostra Studio, Neon Alley and Fernhouse store templates.
