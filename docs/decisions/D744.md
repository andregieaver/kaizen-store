# D744: Database flags over two daily readings

**Database flags over two daily readings (bloat review step 1.13; 17 November 2026).** The daily production reading (D574) now keeps the hot tables' indexes and each job's runs (`platform_readings.kind` takes `index`, `job` and `ai`), and `src/lib/platform-flags.ts` raises eight flags from two days of readings: a table read whole many times, a write that rarely changes a row, a statement repeated slow, the scheduler's log growing, an unused index on a hot table, a job running long or often, and
an AI cost out of line (the one tied to the opening date stays off until there is one). Platform, Usage and limits shows the flags in plain words, with the day's figures one step away, and the morning briefing carries them (`database_flag`); a written exception silences one. Flags appear once production has two days of the new readings. The flags read the platform's own database, which every store shares.
