# D492: Suppliers' invoices

**Suppliers' invoices: bills posted as they come and paid from the bank's statement (B3 step 3, 7 November 2026, `src/lib/supplier-invoices.ts`, `src/server/supplier-invoices.ts`, `commerce.suppliers`, `commerce.supplier_invoices`, the *Bills to pay* card on Settings → Books).** A receipt is paid as it is bought (D490); a bill comes first and is paid later, so what the store owes is in its books in between. *Add a bill:* who it is from, its number, its date and due date, the amount to pay with
VAT, what it was (the purchase kinds of D490) and its VAT (*as usual for it* or a rate), and the PDF or photo; the organisation number and the owner's own words one step further. It is posted when added: the cost without VAT and the deductible VAT (`costLinesOf()`, as a receipt) against what the store owes its suppliers, 2400, in a new purchases series `L` on the bill's day, once for the bill; the file is kept first as the entry's voucher (no place to keep it, nothing posted).
The same number from the same supplier is refused as a second bill. *Bills to pay* lists the unpaid ones, the soonest due first, an overdue one marked, each with its *Receipt* link, and their sum. *Paid:* a line of money out the books do not have is matched to an unpaid bill (`matchBills()`: `matchInvoices()` turned round) only when it pays exactly the bill; the bill's number in the line records the payment when the statement is read (the upload's message names the suppliers), the supplier's
name or an amount only one bill has is offered on the Bank card (*Pays the bill 88123 from Telenor Norge AS?*), one press. The payment is posted against the bank (2400 against 1920, in the bank series on the bank's day) and the line matched; a bill changes only so, once, and is never deleted (a trigger). A supplier is kept by name (`lower(name)` unique a store) with its organisation number and the kind it last sold, made as a bill first names it.
Owner only, audited (`work.supplier_invoice_added`, `work.supplier_invoice_paid`).
Kaizena Flow's own books are its own store's (D467). *On the home (8 November 2026):* the owner's *Needs your attention* says how many bills are past their due date and how many fall due within the week, by the store's day, counts only, one press to the books (`StoreFigures.books.billsOverdue`, `billsDue`). *Not yet:* a bill paid in parts, a bill in another currency, e-invoices in by Peppol, and paying a bill from Kaizena Flow (a bill's PDF read by the model came the next day, D493).
