# D736: Statements are counted, and the admin's five screens held

**Statements are counted, and the admin's five screens held (bloat review step 1.9; 17 November 2026).** `src/db/statement-count.ts` counts the statements a block of work sends, with their time and rows, through a wrapper on each pooled connection's `query` (off unless a test or a render asks; it then passes straight through).
The wrapper binds a waiting `pool.connect` callback to its caller's context, so a statement queued for a connection is counted where it was asked (and a request's context is never the releaser's).
`src/server/admin-screens.ts` holds exactly the reads the store shell, the store home, the orders and products lists and the platform home run (each screen calls its function), and `src/server/admin-statements.int.test.ts` holds their counts in a baseline (4, 33, 6, 5, 22): a rise fails with the fix said, `UPDATE_BASELINE=1` takes one on purpose, a fall asks to lower it. Waves of parallel reads are not held: with a pool of five they vary run to run.
Each admin render logs one `admin.render` line with its statements and milliseconds.
Two faults were caught at review before they reached production or were fixed the same day: the render timer read `Date.now()` in prerendered storefront pages (Cache Components failed the build; `performance.now()` now), and the wrapper kept `pg`'s `query` at connect, hiding statements from the tests that spy on `Client.prototype.query` (the idle-run tests of steps 0.27, 0.28 and 0.30 failed in CI; it now looks the method up on each call).
The `admin.render` line was seen in production on 17 November (the store home with its shell 48 statements, a builder page 8). The platform's home is one of the five. Commits 2a888534, 5a1a0f72, 1f7e0b79, 0fe09cbe.
