# D729: Ecosystem plan phase 4

**Ecosystem plan phase 4: closed loops (17 November 2026, `docs/audits/ecosystem-2026-11.md` E4.1 to E4.3; E4.4 to E4.6 moved to the email plan, D720).** **E4.2:** three alerts in `alertsFor()` (`src/lib/analytics-alerts.ts`, thresholds and minimum volumes in `ALERT_RULES`), so Home, the Overview, the alert emails, the briefing, `analytics_alerts` and the autopilot's `alert` events all have them: `page-frustration` (a page's share of views with a dead or rage click up 10 points and to 1.5 times
its own 28 days before, 100 views in the week and 200 before; links to its heatmap), `page-slow` (a kind of page whose p75 LCP, INP or CLS turned poor against the 21 days before, 50 and 100 samples; worked out in SQL) and `domain-problem` (a domain that no longer reaches the store, a failing certificate, a registration lapsed or expiring without renewal, an unconfirmed registrant, one not pointed after two days).
A daily domain watch (`src/server/domain-watch.ts`, dispatcher job `domainWatch`, 20 a run) asks each open store's domains for the store's own `/api/health` and keeps the answer in `store_domains.checks.watch`; `domainHealth()` gains `points_away` and `certificate_failing`; a timeout or a 5xx is never a failure, and no page view looks anything up.
The autopilot reads the event log (E3.1) as the reader `autopilot` for `support.waiting`, `experiment.answered` and a `fulfilment.updated` in a problem phase, with the scans' own `ref`, and keeps scans only where time passing is the fact (unsent orders, late suppliers, failed payments, stockout risk, the daily alert check) or the log has no event yet (a test ended, an order held, a dispute, a staff request, an exchange confirmed).
No prompt or tool changed. **The platform:** the house store raises the frustration and slow-page alerts for kaizenaflow.com's pages and its connected domains are watched; next, the house store's alerts in `platformAlertsFor()` and the briefing, kaizenaflow.com itself in the watch, and the remaining kinds queued as log events (`docs/platform-parity.md`). **E4.1:** when the autopilot itself would publish a changed store page whose own traffic answers a test within 21 days, it starts one instead
(`src/server/autopilot-tests.ts`): the live page against its change, goal orders or else the fastest of cart and checkout; a dispatcher job decides by code (`decideAutopilotTests`): a clear win goes live within the owner's Website level or waits for a yes, anything else keeps the page.
One undecided test a page (`commerce.autopilot_tests`), never stacked, never an event turn; a quiet page, a full test count or the setting off (*Test my page changes first*, on by default) publishes as before under the watch.
Next: the house store's sign-ups per visit as a goal, so kaizenaflow.com's pages can be tested. **E4.3:** a question the shopper chat handed on as `could_not_help`, or answered after knowledge searches that found nothing, is kept masked and grouped by its words (`src/lib/unanswered-questions.ts`, `commerce.unanswered_questions`, 90 days, in the register, export and erasure); Support → *Questions your assistant couldn't answer* offers *Write the answer*, which saves a knowledge document, and
Home counts the open ones. The platform's own chat and desk have the same page. Next: a relevance floor for the meaning search, and the count on the platform's Home.
