# D338: Each country's own

**Each country's own (wave 7 unit D, `docs/wave-7-storefront.md` section 4, D); built.** A store's page or article may be limited to some of its countries (`PageContent.markets`, `pageInMarket()`, the store's own on save): elsewhere it is the route's 404, out of `hreflang`, the sitemap, llms.txt and content grids.
A product's description may have a text per country over its language's (`commerce.product_market_texts`, written only by `saveProduct()`, read by the product page and its structured data; the title stays the language's), and a menu link's label one per country (`{locale}@{COUNTRY}`, read first by `menuLabel()`).
A store that sells in several countries may give one of them (never its own) a domain of its own (`store_domains.market`, `setDomainCountry()`), where the country goes without saying; `countryHostDecision()` moves its addresses there and the link helpers write them on it.
A visitor whose country the store also sells to is offered the same page there (`CountryHintSection`, per request in its own `<Suspense>`), never moved, never a crawler, never on the pay routes; closing it is kept in memory only.

Date: 2026-10-27

*Why:* `international.market-specific-content-adaptation`, `international.domains-and-subfolders-per-market`, `international.geolocation-suggestion`. Product texts per country are a table of their own, not keys in `product_translations`: 68 modules read that by exact language, and a composite key would reach search stemming, fallbacks and copies. A store gives domains to its countries or to its languages (D313), never both (writers and a trigger), so every market has one address.
The suggestion's dismissal is not stored, so it needs no cookie and brings no consent banner. The builder's bar names the view it draws; the Translate page counts the texts written for one country, which are never translated.
