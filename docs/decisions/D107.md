# D107: The admin's three levels

**The admin's three levels (platform, store owner, store) share one shell, and the owner has a control center.** `AdminFrame` (`src/components/admin/admin-frame.tsx`) draws every level: a header that hides while scrolling down with a *level switcher* on the left (`AdminSwitcher`: the control center, the person's stores, the platform for Kaizen's team with its waiting requests, their account) and the level's actions and an account menu (`AccountMenu`: account, colours, sign out) on the right; the
level's daily sections as tabs; the rest in a sidebar under headings (the slide-out menu with the tabs on phones); `AdminMain` gives editors the whole width. The platform admin (`/admin/platform`) has tabs Overview, Requests (with the number waiting), Customers, Stores, Plans and AI manager, and sidebar groups Website, Billing, AI and Communication; its overview (`platformOverview()`) is the operator's first look, and access requests moved to `/admin/platform/requests`.
The owner's level is the control center at `/admin`, also for an owner with one store (before, a single store was opened at once and the level was hidden): `controlCenter()` counts, in a handful of queries for all the stores the account works in, their status, plan, payments, the last 7 days' paid sales per currency against the 7 before, orders waiting to be sent, stock running out and the latest orders; `attentionFor()` turns them into what needs the owner first (late orders, an overdue plan,
payments not ready, a store not open, stock). Its other tabs are Stores (with creating one), Billing (`/admin/account/billing`: the plans of all their stores), AI usage and Account, all under `/admin/account/…` or `/admin/stores` because `/admin/{word}` would take a store address. Staff who own nothing go straight to their one store, or see Stores and Account; a platform admin with no store lands on the platform; a host on their hosting, which has the same shell.
A store's own overview shows its week the same way above the setup checklist. Every page still checks who asks (`requireAccount()`, `requirePlatformAdmin()`, `requireMember()`).

Date: 2026-09-29

Asked for by the owner: the platform admin structured like the store admin, and the store owner's level, which was hard to find and had no structure, made a control center with an operational bird's-eye view of all their running stores. One shell means moving between levels keeps the same places for everything, and the switcher makes each level one click from any other, so nobody needs to remember addresses.
Owners land on the control center even with one store: it is where the business is seen, and the store is a click away. Money is never added across currencies. Not yet: a per-store sales chart, alerts sent by email, and a control center for staff. Design and rules: `docs/admin-navigation.md`.
