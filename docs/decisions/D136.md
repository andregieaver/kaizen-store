# D136: PostNord is connected

**PostNord is connected** for pickup points, tracking and checkout services (`docs/shipping-carriers.md`, `src/lib/postnord.ts`, `src/server/carriers/postnord.ts`), on the store's own API key.
PostNord has no price API, so a carrier's checkout services have two pricing modes (`CHECKOUT_PRICING`): `carrier` (Bring's Shipping Guide price plus VAT plus the store's markup) and `store` (PostNord: the store enters, per country and service, the price with VAT and a free-above value, in `shipping_carriers.checkout_prices`). Several carriers can be on in a country: their services are listed together, named for the carrier, and one that fails leaves the others.
Booking and labels (EDI) are not built because their specification is behind PostNord's login.
