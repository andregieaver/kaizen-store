# D173: Order search, filters, saved views and bulk actions; tags and archive; draft orders with pay links; gift messages

**Order search, filters, saved views and bulk actions; tags and archive; draft orders with pay links; gift messages (wave 3, run 2).** `docs/wave-3-orders.md` is the contract.
The Orders page reads its state from the address (`parseOrderListParams()`: search over number, email, name, line title and SKU, tag and tracking number; filters for status, payment, fulfilment, tag, market, source, gift, archive and a store-day range; keyset paging, 50 a page, a count capped at 10,000; the total sort compares totals converted at the store's rates). Every list condition is built by `orderListWhere()`. Saved views are the store's (30 at most).
Bulk actions (tag, untag, archive, unarchive, mark sent, print slips; 250 at most, slips 100) run order by order in their own transactions and report each refusal. Orders have tags (`order_tags`: 40 characters, 250 per order, case-insensitive) and an archive state (`orders.archived_at`, a visibility flag only; the number sequence is untouched; a return unarchives; automatic archiving is an owner option); both work on copied orders (two guard functions patched).
Draft orders (`draft_orders`, `draft_order_lines`): staff price goods and custom service items with a custom price, a staff discount, shipping and a market through `priceDraft()`, built from the checkout's own VAT, shipping and allocation functions and equal to `cartSummary()` for the same goods.
Send makes the order (`source = 'draft'`) from the one shared insert `src/server/order-insert.ts`, takes its number from D141's sequence and holds stock until the draft's expiry; the buyer pays through a hashed-token link (`/account/pay/{token}`, Stripe's hosted page, one idempotency key per press), or staff record money taken outside Kaizen (a `payments` row with provider `manual`, a method and a received day of at most 31 days back, owner-only unless the owner allows staff, cash ceilings held
as unverified data in `cash-limits.ts`), which the invoice states as paid outside and whose refund is recorded with no Stripe call, capped under the order lock and by the `refunds_manual_cap` trigger, and gets a credit note by the existing trigger. An expired draft cancels its order, releases the stock and leaves no gap. A custom item is sold to businesses only until the legal review decides; a delivery address must be in the market's country. A staff discount is its own kind on the invoice.
Gift messages (store switch, default off): `is_gift`, To, From and a 300-character message on the cart and the order, cleaned by `cleanShopperText()`, refused not cut, frozen after placement, erased with the order, printed on a price-free packing slip, never in a shipping notice and never sent to a third party. Rows: tags and archive, gift messages Full; order list and draft orders Partial (draft orders bucket D until a person has read the consumer texts of section 8).
