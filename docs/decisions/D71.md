# D71: A store can list stays and rentals for outside hosts

**A store can list stays and rentals for outside hosts: hosts sign in to an area of their own, are paid directly through their own Stripe account, and the store keeps a commission; the store is the platform operator for DAC7, and Kaizen prepares its report.** A host (`commerce.hosts`: their account, a display name, the store's commission in basis points, 15 % by default, and whether they are VAT registered) is added by the store's owners and signs in like staff, but is not a store member:
`requireMember()` never admits them, and `requireHost()` gives them `/admin/hosting/{store}` with only their listings (`products.host_id`), their rooms and items (`booking_resources.host_id`) with calendars they keep, and the bookings of those. The store writes the listings; hosts do not edit them. A host who is not VAT registered sells without VAT (a trigger keeps their listings `exempt`).
Payment (B4b): a booking with a host is a direct charge on the host's connected account (the host is the seller; `host_stripe_accounts`, which `connected_accounts` lists with the stores' own for webhooks, refunds and order pages), with Kaizen's fee plus the store's commission (a share of what is paid online, `orders.commission_minor`) as the application fee.
Once paid, the commission is owed to the store (`host_commissions`, one per payment) and sent from Kaizen's balance as a transfer to the store's account, tried again from the five-minute cron until Stripe takes it (the fee must be in Kaizen's available balance, and the store's account is asked for the transfers capability on the first refusal); a refund returns the same share of it, by lowering it or reversing part of the transfer.
A no-show fee charged to the card saved with a host's booking (D66) is charged on the host's account too, with the store's commission of it taken and sent the same way (kind `no_show`); hosts and the store see it next to the booking (hosts also see Kaizen's own fee on each payment, kept as `payments.kaizen_fee_minor` and less its refunded share), and the DAC7 report counts the fee as paid to the host and its commission as the store's.
One checkout pays one host: a cart mixing hosts, or a host and the store, is refused (`host_mix`), and host listings are never subscriptions. Live, hosts open their own Stripe account (full Dashboard, Stripe's fees) with Stripe's embedded screens in their area; in test mode Kaizen makes it (D20).
The store's invoices are not used for hosts' charges; the site's domain is registered on the host's account (`host_stripe_accounts.payment_domains`) as on the store's, so wallets, Link and Klarna show for their bookings too. A part paid at the venue carries no commission.
DAC7 (B4c): hosts give their tax details in their area (`host_tax_details`: a person with their date of birth or a business with its registration number, address, tax identification number and issuing country, VAT number, IBAN) and each room's or home's address and land registry number (`booking_resources`); the store sees what is missing, and owners download the year's report (`/admin/{store}/hosts/dac7`, CSV of sellers and of properties) with, per quarter of payment in the store's time zone,
what shoppers paid less refunds, the store's commission and the number of bookings, and each home's nights; for the store's own records it also gives Kaizen's fee and the host's net earnings per quarter, which the report itself does not ask for. The store files it (in Norway with Skatteetaten) by 31 January; Kaizen's own fee is not in it, as Kaizen is not treated as the operator.

Date: 2026-09-27

Defaults chosen for the owner ("use sensible defaults"). Built in parts: B4a (hosts and their area), B4b (payments), B4c (DAC7). Whether Kaizen is itself a platform operator under DAC7 still needs legal advice.
