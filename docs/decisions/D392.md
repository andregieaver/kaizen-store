button, never by opening the link; `stock_requests.cancel_hash`, the confirmation token's hash kept after it is used), the product page's *Stop* for a signed-in customer's own waiting request (`DELETE /api/stock-requests`), both through `cancelStockRequest()`/`cancelOwnStockRequest()`, the answer the same whether or not a request was found. **Amount steps** (`customers.tiered-and-volume-discounts`): a tiered campaign's steps give a percentage or an amount off per market (`tierGive`, a step's

`commerce.bonus_earn_bps()`, read by `bonus_order_paid()` and the cart alike, never stored), shown on My account with what more the next needs; a credit given once when a customer's email is first proven (`signup`, trigger `customers_bonus_signup`); both carried by store copies.
