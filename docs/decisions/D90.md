# D90: The media library searches a file's name by name alone, finds pictures by their alt texts in every language, and always has English alt texts; files list as a grid or a list

**The media library searches a file's name by name alone, finds pictures by their alt texts in every language, and always has English alt texts; files list as a grid or a list.** A search that looks like a file's name (`isFileNameQuery()`: a file type's ending, or one word of letters and digits such as a stored file's id) finds that file, then names starting with or holding it, and never searches by meaning or likeness.
Vectors are made from a file's alt texts in every language, its name only where it is words (`readableFileName()`: no ids or numbers) and what uses it; a file with none of that has no vector, and fuzzy name matching ignores ids. The AI writes English too where the store sells in none (`AltLanguage.extra`), for people searching in English and for AI assistants; AI texts missing one of the site's languages are written again (a market added), and only a failure or a language left out waits a day.
The view is in the address (`view=list`); Type and Order apply as they change.

Date: 2026-09-28

Reported by the owner: "car" found none of four car pictures (their vectors were of ids, and their alt texts in Nordic languages), and a file's whole name found five files (ids are alike to a model).
