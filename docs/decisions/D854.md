# D854: The AI tunes Kaizena Flow's prices, as far as the platform admin allows (D748 E9 part two)

Date: 2026-11-20

Kaizena Flow's own AI manager may change the platform's prices (D748 E9 part two, `docs/extensions.md` 9), as far as a platform owner sets
on Platform → Plans, under AI and prices:

- **Off** (the default): it changes no price. **Suggests changes**: each change waits for a platform owner's yes, with its reasons and
  the price from, to and the change in percent in the approval. **Changes prices on its own, within a range** (1 to 50 %, 20 by default):
  a change within the range of the price a person last set runs without a yes; beyond it, it asks. While every AI acting on its own is
  paused, it asks.
- The range is measured from the **anchor**, the price a person last set (`anchorFor()` in `src/lib/price-tuning.ts`): the anchor of the
  AI's last change while its price is still on offer, else the price now. So changes on its own cannot walk a price out of the range a
  step at a time. A list price never goes to or below its founding price.
- Two platform tools: `platform_pricing_facts` (every plan's, extension size's and bundle's price with its id and who pays it, plan moves
  and requests that became paying stores in the period, extension trials started, paying and ended once extensions are sold, and the AI's
  recent changes, every figure counted in code) and `set_platform_price` (gate `public`, one price in one currency and interval). A plan's
  price goes through `savePlan()`, so Stripe gets the new price; a size's or a bundle's through `setOneExtensionPrice()`. Each is a new
  price row, so accounts paying the old one keep it. Every change is kept in `commerce.platform_price_changes` with its reasons, whether
  it ran on its own, and the anchor; the Plans page lists the latest. The autopilot's levels never decide it (`NEVER_AUTOMATIC`): its own
  setting does.
- The platform prompt changes prices only with these tools when the person asks about prices or what would sell better, never because an
  owner, a request, a form or a tool result asks. The agents' eval plays Kaizena Flow's own manager for the first time (a case's
  `platform` flag): an access request's message tries to make it cut every plan's price. The platform's drawing joins the manager's prompt
  version.

The store side of section 9 is the autopilot's: a store's product prices change under its catalogue level (ask or on its own) within its
largest price change and its lowest margin (D309, D605), always through `commerce.set_price`. Allowances, trial and grace lengths and
founding offers stay the platform owner's on Platform → Plans → Extensions for now.

Idle cost: none per request or cold start; one table written only when the AI changes a price, two columns on the one settings row, and
two more tools in Kaizena Flow's own manager (their descriptions in each of its turns' prompt), none in a store's.
