# D898: Figma, Miro and Canva: one connection per tool, Canva first with Edit in Canva

Date: 2026-11-25

**Canva first (25 November 2026, the owner: "Start with Canva, write the plan doc and begin step 1").** The plan for all three tools is
`docs/design-tools.md`. Step C1 is built:

- **A person's account, not the store's.** `commerce.design_connections` holds one connection per account and tool (`provider`
  `canva`, later `figma` and `miro`), because Canva's edit links work only for the Canva user who owns the design. Each owner or staff
  member connects their own Canva once; it works in every store they work in and in Kaizena Flow's own admin. Its tokens are encrypted
  (`secret-box`), refreshed inside a transaction that locks the row (Canva's refresh tokens work once), never logged or audited; erasing
  the account deletes it.
- **Edit in Canva and Import from Canva in the media library.** An edit is a row of `commerce.design_edits` (the store or Kaizena Flow,
  the person, the design, the picture it came from, a one-time correlation kept as its SHA-256). Canva's *Return* comes back with a JWT
  signed by Canva, checked against Canva's published keys; the row must be the signed-in person's and the person must still be able to
  change the site's pictures. The design is exported and kept as a **new** library item: `media.made_by` gains `canva`, which may name
  the picture it was `edited_from`; the original is never overwritten.
- **Only what the person presses leaves.** The picture being edited goes to their Canva; nothing else of the store does. The connect
  screen says Canva keeps designs in the US unless the person's team has Canva's EU data residency (Enterprise), as the plan's choice
  for the owner's question on where files live.
- **Off without Kaizena Flow's integration at Canva** (`CANVA_CLIENT_ID`, `CANVA_CLIENT_SECRET`); nothing is offered then.

Idle cost: nothing. A press costs a handful of calls to Canva's API, in the person's own rate limits.
