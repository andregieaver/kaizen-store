# D839: The product taxonomy is traced only where its reader is reached; a product keeps its category's words

**The product taxonomy is traced only where its reader is reached; a product keeps its category's words (bloat review step 2.23;
20 November 2026).** `next.config.ts` no longer adds the product taxonomy to every function (`outputFileTracingIncludes` `'/**'`):
Turbopack traces its five JSON files beside the functions that reach `src/server/product-taxonomy.ts`, whose reads are literal
`path.join(process.cwd(), "src/data/product-taxonomy", "<file>.json")` calls (`product-taxonomy.scan.test.ts`). The brief's way, an
exclude per route, cannot work in Next 16.4.6: Turbopack applies `outputFileTracingExcludes` only to files found by following imports,
never to files an include adds (`crates/next-api/src/nft.rs`), and it matches route names with their groups. After a build
`scripts/trace-sizes.mjs --check` fails if any of eleven routes that read the taxonomy (the product page, store home, category, search,
the product editor, new product, import, the AI manager, the admin API, feeds and the dispatcher) lacks a file; its store-page patterns
now include `(shop)`, so the store home and checkout sizes are held again. Sharp is left to Turbopack's own trace (a list from the
import walker would have missed `next/og`). Measured locally: traces carrying the taxonomy 883 to 747, sharp 776 to 749, the median
trace 64.8 to 55 MB, `/api/health` 15.4 to 2.2 MB, `/api/visit` 16 to 2.6 MB.

A product keeps the words its page shows for its standard category (`products.standard_shown`, migration
`20261120100419_curvy_santa_claus.sql`, add only): `saveProduct()` writes them, the dispatcher's `standardShown` job fills older rows
(up to 200 a run) and refreshes the store's catalogue tag, and `getProduct()` reads the row, so the product page no longer loads the
taxonomy reader. A category the release does not know is kept as its key only, so the job goes idle. Idle cost: one statement per
five-minute dispatcher run (10 rows read with one store, 0 with twenty); none per request or cold start. The surface budget's
dispatcher jobs rise 85 to 86 by this decision. Kaizena Flow has no products; its routes lose the same traces. To read after the
deploy: the functions' sizes in Vercel, a day of logs without ENOENT, the advisors, and no product with a category but no stored words.
