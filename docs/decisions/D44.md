# D44: The page editor's canvas shows the page as the site will, and each part is edited from tools that appear on it

**The page editor's canvas shows the page as the site will, and each part is edited from tools that appear on it.** The canvas renders the title, picture, rows, columns and text with the site's own markup and spacing. Pointing at a row, a column or a block (or tabbing to or tapping it) outlines it and shows its tools: drag handle, edit (wrench), duplicate and delete; only the innermost part under the pointer shows them.
Rows and columns have a thin band around what they hold to point at, taken back by negative margins so the layout is the site's. Editing rich text opens the editor in a dialog (also by double-click, or Enter on the block); a new text block opens it straight away. A row's or column's wrench opens the row's layout. Duplicating a row or block puts a copy right after it; duplicating or deleting a column changes the row to that many equal columns (at most six, at least one).
Deleting anything with text asks first. Columns are dragged within their row, and take the width of their new place. Links in the text do nothing in the editor.
