# D63: Businesses see prices without VAT; VAT is charged unless the order is reverse charge

**Businesses see prices without VAT; VAT is charged unless the order is reverse charge (D157).** A store sells to consumers, businesses or both (`stores.audience`, the Company page). Prices stay kept with VAT (`set_price`, the 30-day reference and checkout's VAT are unchanged); a store selling only to businesses types them without VAT in the product editor, kept as `withVat()`, which `withoutVat()` always turns back into what was typed.
Every order charges the market's VAT, except a reverse-charge order since D157 (`vatTreatment()`: a business buyer with a valid VAT number in another EU country, buying goods from a VAT-registered EU seller, is not charged VAT).
In a store selling to both, each shopper is private or business, chosen with the header's switch or the optional first-visit question (`business_popup`) and kept in the necessary cookie `buyer_{storeId}`; the page's first script marks `<html data-buyer>` so prebuilt pages show each kind its prices (`<Price>` draws both, CSS shows one) and products (`products.audience`: everyone, private only, businesses only; cards carry `for-business`/`for-private`).
Business-only products can be added to the cart only by business buyers (`sellableQuantity`), and an order with one, or any order in a store selling only to businesses, needs the company's name and organisation number (`companyRequired()`, checked in `placeOrder`; numbers checked for Norway, Sweden, Denmark and Finland by `organisationNumber()`).
They are typed in the cart, kept on the cart and the order, put on the Stripe invoice and in the confirmation, and can be saved in My account, which makes the customer a business buyer when signing in. Hiding by CSS is a view, not a lock: the page still holds the other kind's products; the cart and checkout enforce.
