# D458: Instabox, DHL Express and UPS join the carriers

**Instabox, DHL Express and UPS join the carriers (5 November 2026, `src/lib/instabox.ts`, `src/lib/dhl.ts`, `src/lib/ups.ts`, `src/server/carriers/{instabox,dhl,ups}.ts`, `src/server/carrier-delivery-booking.ts`).** Each is built like Helthjem (D138), against the carrier's own published documentation, and connected with the store's own agreement from Integrations. **Instabox** goes through Instabee's integration API (`ApiKey`, `Accept-Version: 1.0.0`, sandbox and live hosts): its lockers in SE,
NO and DK at checkout at prices the store enters (Instabee does not say whose its `deliveryPrice` is), each locker kept as its sort code with the availability token it came with (held 14 days; asked for again when missing), booked and confirmed as packed in one `PUT /orders`, the label fetched as a PDF when printed.
Its parcels are not polled (`FOLLOWED_CARRIERS`): Instabee reads an order by its own id, not the parcel number, so the shopper follows Instabox's page. **DHL Express** goes through the MyDHL API 3.4.1 (Basic auth, `x-version`), the one DHL service a store anywhere in Europe can use with one connection: Address Validate gives the city Rates needs from the shopper's postal code, Rates prices the store's three services (Express, Express 12:00, Economy Select, each a family of DHL's per-lane product
codes) without DHL's own tax (`STTXA`), a booking asks Rates for the lane's product code first, and a parcel across a customs border carries its `exportDeclarations` (D343). DHL's label comes only in the booking's answer, so it is kept in `commerce.carrier_labels` (RLS, server only) and deleted 30 days later by retention kind `carrier_labels` and with the person's orders on erasure.
DHL's checkpoint codes are read from its reference data, so OK (delivered) and WC (with courier) are verified (D238). **UPS** goes through its OAuth client credentials, Rating `Shop` (the negotiated rate first), the Locator for UPS Access Points (booked as Standard held at the point, `ShipmentIndicationType` 01), Shipping v2409 with a paperless commercial invoice across a customs border, Tracking (its status letters are unverified) and Label Recovery for a PDF of the label when it is printed
(Shipping gives only a GIF). The carriers that ship from anywhere in Europe ask for the sender's country (`senderCountry`, `CarrierField.format`, `senderAddress()`), which checkout and booking now read instead of assuming Norway; a carrier's price in another currency than the country's is converted at the store's own rates (dropped when there is none).
Booking goes through one path for the three, `bookCarrierDelivery()`, used by the order page and *Book labels*; UPS and DHL can also book an order whose customer chose the store's own shipping, with a door service staff choose.
