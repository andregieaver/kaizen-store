# D607: The AI answers emails about the sender's own orders

**The AI answers emails about the sender's own orders (`docs/100-day-challenge.md` B1; 14 November 2026, `support_settings.email_orders`, `emailCustomer()` in `src/server/support-inbound.ts`, migration `20261114202420_overrated_whirlwind`).** Until now an email about an order always went to a person, most of a store's support.
With the desk's new switch (*Emails about orders*, off by default, never for a store with sensitive information) or the store in the challenge, an email conversation's own address is matched to the one customer of the store with that address; the AI then gets the first line's order tools (orders, details, parcels, return options) for that customer, and the email prompt says it may answer about their own orders and must send refunds, cancellations and changed addresses to a person.
Safe because every answer goes only to the conversation's address: the email passed DMARC, a thread continues only from that address, so whoever sent it, only the address's owner reads the answer. Two customers with one address, or none, leave it as before. Tests: `support-inbound.int.test.ts`. The chat prompt changed, so the agents' eval is recorded again with D606's.
Not yet: contact forms to the AI (B2; a form's address is typed, and the database keeps forms from the AI), the waiting customer answered in the challenge (B4, with the escalation categories of A4).

Unit 4 of the preparation for the challenge, where a person may only answer escalated support. Platform: Kaizena Flow's own desk serves owners, not customers with orders, so the switch is a store's only.
