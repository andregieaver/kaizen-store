# D418: What the autopilot's changes moved, and the monthly *What the AI did*

**What the autopilot's changes moved, and the monthly *What the AI did*.** Each change the autopilot made itself (`audit_log.acted_by = 'autopilot'`, action `store.assistant.{tool}`) is measured once, two weeks on, by the dispatcher's hourly `autopilotEffects` (`measureAutopilotEffects()`, `src/server/autopilot-effects.ts`, the only writer of `commerce.autopilot_effects`): a change to one product (`create_product`, `update_product`, `set_prices`, `set_product_status`, `write_product_texts`,
found by the reference it gave) on that product's sales, anything else on the store's, over the 14 store days before the change's day and the 14 after (the day itself in neither), as what the goods sold for without VAT (order lines, gift cards left out, before refunds) in the main currency, with the store's own figure over the same days beside it.
A product change is read against the store's trend (`expectedAfter()`, `liftMinor()`, `src/lib/autopilot-effects.ts`); store changes are counted, never summed into money, as their windows overlap.
The Autopilot page has a *Results* tab (this month and last: the changes made, those measured, what the products changed sold before and after and beyond the store's trend, and each change measured); on the first of a month the people whose plans ran get *What the AI did in {month}* (`autopilot_reports.kind = 'monthly'`, sent only when there is something to say); and each goal review is told what the store's latest changes moved (`effectLines()`), so the next plan builds on what worked.
Every text says it is a comparison of before and after, not proof that the change caused it.

Date: 2026-11-01

*Why sales, and the product's own for a product change:* that is the figure such a change is meant to move, and the only one every store has; a goal's own target can be anything. *Why the store beside it:* a season or a campaign moves everything; a product read against the store's trend shows what was its own. *Why no total "earned" for store changes:* two page changes in one fortnight would each claim the same sales. *Why once an hour:* a change waits two weeks anyway, and a store without a
rate for a currency is tried again until 90 days have passed.
