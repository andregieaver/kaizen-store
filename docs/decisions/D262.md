# D262: Page builder maintenance

**Page builder maintenance: Kaizena Flow's own pages drawn in their theme, rows that meet, a shadow's opacity, wider or narrower sidebars, and the rail's names.** (1) `PageRowView` laid a theme under a row only where the place had an owner (a store), so Kaizena Flow's own pages (owner null) were drawn on the live site without their theme while the builder drew them with it: buttons lost the theme's corners and shadow, and rows the theme's full width (what the owner saw as "the background fails
to stretch").
Every row is now drawn with its site's theme (`themeLayersFor(null)` for Kaizena Flow's), a place that brings its layers using them at once; a button shaped Pill or Square keeps its own corners over the theme's radius (`withoutRadius()`). (2) Two rows next to each other that both draw a box (a background of any kind or a border, at every screen size; not a modal or a row shown only at some sizes or to some visitors) meet with no room between them, on the site (`meetsAbove()`, `rowAbove()`: the
row takes back the article's `ROW_GAP`) and on the canvas; plain rows keep the room, and a row's own margin still keeps it apart; a page replicator's row (class `rp`, `MEASURED_ROW_CLASS`) keeps the room its measured margins count on, so copies look as they did. (3) `shadowOpacity` (0 to 100, per screen size, with the theme's elements) sets a shadow's darkest layer and the others in proportion (`shadowCss()`), a slider under Shadow that starts at the shadow's own (`shadowOpacityOf()`); it goes
with the shadow it is of when a theme is laid under a part. (4) The builder's sidebars can be dragged wider or narrower by their edge by the canvas, or with the arrow keys (`RailResizer`, `railTemplate()`, `draggedWidth()`: 240 to 720 px, the canvas keeping 400), kept in the browser (`kaizen_admin_builder_{side}_width`); a double press, Enter or Home gives the usual share back. (5) The admin's rail has a button at its foot (*Widen the sidebar*, named without "name", which every search for a Name
field would also find) that shows the sections' names beside their icons (224 px), kept in the browser (`kaizen_admin_rail`) and set on `<html data-rail="names">` by the admin's first script (`railNamesScript()`) so a page never widens after it is drawn; a workspace's narrowed rail keeps to its icons. Both browser keys, and the sidebars' fold keys of D125 which were missing, are in `KNOWN_COOKIES` as staff-only.
Also: a product of one variant draws no Variants heading or box, only its stock, price and Add to cart; and a theme's presets on the sign-up and new-store forms are rows at least 32 px high, so their radios are far enough apart to press (axe's target size, found once the launch themes' presets were offered).
