# D866: A publish refreshes its own page; list facts refresh the site

**A publish refreshes its own page; list facts refresh the site (bloat review step 2.14; 20 November 2026).** Every read of one page's
document carries the page's own tag, `page:{id}` (`pageTag()` in `src/lib/cache-tags.ts`): `findPublishedPage()`, `publishedPageById()`,
`siteLayoutFor()`, `productLayoutById()`, `siteVersionContent()`, the entry layout in `findEntryPage()` and a recommending grid's words.
`savePage()` says how far a publish reaches: `page` when nothing other pages show changed (`listFacts()`: titles, addresses, thumbnails,
excerpts, search descriptions and picture descriptions in every language, the pictures the sitemaps list, headers and footers, a first
publish, an unpublish), `site` otherwise, and `site` while the store has a published grid of pages sorted by date
(`hasPageGridByDate()`), so no grid shows an old order. `src/server/page-refresh.ts` turns the scope into tags at the eight places that
publish a body; an unknown scope refreshes both. A product layout's document is read once per store, not once per product. Sitemaps and
the feed keep `cacheLife("hours")`, so a body-only publish's date reaches them within the hour. Locally the About page's own tag is on 3
of 267 cached entries and all carry the store's pages tag; refreshes of a whole store's pages tag fell from 43 to 40;
`e2e/signed-in/publish-reach.spec.ts` replays a publish against built pages: the page changes, the home and a product page stay cached,
and a title change reaches the home's menu at once (two test fixes after CI runs 1831 and 1833, d8a94a7f0 and a05117dd8). Live from
20 November (eff1a215b). To read on production: step 1.25's refresh counts, and a title change on a test store. Idle cost: none.
