# D57: Articles

**Articles: a blog built with the same page builder.** Articles are pages of another type (`pages.type`, `page` or `article`), for Kaizen and every store: the same editor, builder, translations, saved parts and preview, edited under Blog (`/admin/platform/articles`, `/admin/{store}/articles`) with their own categories and tags (content type `article`).
They are at `/blog/{slug}` (a store's at `/s/{store}/{market}/blog/{slug}`), so addresses are unique per owner and type, redirects keep their type, `blog` is reserved for pages and `category`, `tag` and `page` for articles. An article has an author, filled in with the name of the person who starts it and changeable (empty shows the store or Kaizen), and keeps the date it was first published (`first_published_at`) when published again.
A store's front page is a page, never an article (a database rule). `/blog` lists articles newest first with filtered lists by category and tag, and each article shows a header (title, date, author, picture) above its rows with `Article` structured data.
Content grids can show articles (with their date); menus link to the blog, an article (a store's by address, Kaizen's by id) or a blog category; sitemaps, llms.txt and robots.txt include the blog and its articles; new stores copy the template's articles.
