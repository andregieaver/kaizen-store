**Copying the site again keeps what was changed in Kaizena Flow's store (6 November 2026, `docs/platform-parity-plan.md` step 5, migrations `house_site_settings` and `house_site_settings_rules`).** Step 3's copy (D469) brought the site's look, logos, SEO, header, footer, menus and front page onto Kaizena Flow's store on every run, over whatever the store's own Settings had made of them: the owner had meanwhile given the store its SEO (title, banner, llms.txt, Google's verification) and a dark
logo, which *Copy it again* would have thrown away. `copy_platform_site()` now notes the live site's settings at each copy (`house_site_copies`, kind `settings`, its source and copy the store itself) and brings a setting only where the store still has it as the last copy left it; one changed in the store stays the store's, and Platform → Website says how many were kept.
