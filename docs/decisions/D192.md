# D192: Staff edit a page's words on the live site

**Staff edit a page's words on the live site.** Signed-in staff who may change the website get *Edit text* beside *Edit page* on a page or an article: a heading or a text of the page's own is pressed and typed in where it stands, then saved (Save, Ctrl+Enter, or Enter in a heading) or cancelled, without opening the page builder.
The words are read and saved by `/api/platform/editor/text` against the revision that was read, into the published page and into the draft where it still has the same words (otherwise the builder's draft keeps its own and the person is told); the visitor's page only gains markers, and the editor is fetched when the mode is turned on.
Not offered for parts shared with other pages or bound to a field, in a modal, on a working page, in a translation, for a page in a running A/B test or one that is not published; headers, footers and product layouts stay in the builder; on a store's own domain the admin's session cannot be seen, which D193 answers.

Date: 2026-10-10

Asked for by the owner. No migration. The server's rules are held by `page-text-edit.int.test.ts`, the markers by `page-article-edit-marks.test.ts`, what visitors get and the route's refusals by `e2e/inline-edit-live.spec.ts`; the editor was driven in a real browser against a stand-in for the route. No test signs in to the admin, so the signed-in path through the real route is checked by hand.
