# D486: Bank statements in the books, by file

**Bank statements in the books, by file (B4 route 1 of `docs/own-books-and-sales.md`, 7 November 2026, `src/lib/bank-statement.ts`, `src/lib/xml-read.ts`, `src/server/bank-statements.ts`, the Bank card on Settings → Books).** The owner uploads the statement the bank exports, and each of its lines is matched to the books. *Read:* ISO 20022 camt.053 (the statement banks in Norway and the EU export for accounting systems; booked entries only, the opening and closing balances, the bank's own
reference, the KID or the payer's reference and the other party), or a netbank's CSV whose columns are found by their Norwegian or English names (one amount, or in and out apart, a decimal comma or point, thousands in groups of three, `31.01.2027` or `2027-01-31`). A file is read whole or refused whole, with a reason the owner can act on, and never in another currency than the books'.
XML is read by a small reader of our own that reads no DTD and expands no entity, so an uploaded file cannot reach outside itself (no dependency was added). *Kept:* `commerce.bank_statements` (the upload: file name, account, days, balances, counts) and `commerce.bank_lines` (day, amount in the book's minor units, text, reference, other party), a line once per book and account on its key (the bank's reference, else what it says and how many identical lines came before it in the file), so a
statement uploaded twice, or two that overlap, add nothing. *Matched:* a line to the entry of the books that moved the same money on the bank's account (1920): the same amount to the øre, at most four days apart, nearest first, one to one (a database index keeps an entry matched once), never to a reversed entry or a reversal; a line matched to an entry later reversed waits again.
Matching runs at the upload and again after the ledger's job posts, as a payout's entry may come after the statement that shows it. *Shown:* how many lines are matched, the bank's closing balance beside what the books hold on 1920 that day, what is in the bank and not in the books (to post by hand, or matched when its document posts) and what is in the books in the statements' days and not in the bank. Nothing is posted from a bank line and nothing is sent to the bank.
Owner only, at most 3 MB (the admin's form takes 4 MB a request), audited with counts. Kaizena Flow's own books are its own store's (D467), so the platform has it the same day. *Not yet:* matching by KID or invoice number when the amount differs (part payments), several bank accounts in one book (the tables carry the account; the screen and the matching use 1920), and routes 2 and 3 (open banking, DNB's bank integration), which land in the same table.
