# D913: A price of nothing is shown as Free, and a free order or subscription never goes to a payment provider

Date: 2026-11-27

**A product's, purchase option's or plan's price of nothing says "Free" (Gratis), where the store, a category, a tag or the product chooses so; an
order that costs nothing is confirmed with no payment provider; and a subscription that costs nothing (a free plan) is Kaizena Flow's own, never a
Stripe subscription.** Asked for by the owner for Kaizena Life's free plan: "i want the '0' price shown as 'Free'", "free products, subscriptions
and plans, should not involve stripe, right?", and "let store owners choose if '0' price is shown as 'Free' hierarchically".

- **Free, chosen at four levels:** Settings > Products (`product_settings.zero_free`, on by default), a category (its subcategories' products too),
  a tag, and the product (`terms.zero_free`, `products.zero_free`, null as the level above), resolved by `commerce.zero_shows_free()`: the product,
  then its tags, then each category's nearest choice, then the store; where tags or categories disagree, the amount wins. The catalogue's prices
  (`PriceView.free`), the pricing table, cart lines, purchase options, the phone's bar and the chat's cards follow it; "From" keeps its amount, and
  a reduction to nothing keeps its 30-day reference. A total, a line's VAT or a reference is a sum and stays one (`VatAmount`'s `free` is opt-in).
- **An order that costs nothing:** the cart asks who it is for (as when gift cards pay all) and places it, even with no payment provider set up;
  `startCheckout()` confirms it with a payment of nothing (provider `free`, the order page's key, never money: it stays pending) and
  `complete_order_payment()`. `payments_amount_positive` allows 0 only for `free` and for a Stripe session with nothing due now, which also mends
  a free trial with no fee or shipping (it ended the checkout in an error).
- **A free subscription** (renewing at nothing, nothing shipped each time): written by `placeOrder()` with provider `none`, switched on when its
  order is paid however it was paid (`activate_free_subscription()`, on `orders`), so a free plan beside paid goods takes a one-time payment. No
  consent to charges is asked; it can only be ended, at once (`changeSubscription()`), and gets no charge reminders. Its events are the database's
  as before (`subscription.started`, `.cancelled`). The cart, the subscriber's page and the emails say it is free until cancelled.
- **The platform:** Kaizena Flow's own pricing table says Free for a plan at nothing; its own plans are billed by the platform, not sold as
  products, so the setting and free orders do not apply there.

Idle cost: none per request or run. One trigger on `orders` (an update of `status` to paid on an order with a subscription); the catalogue's
cached reads and the cart call `zero_shows_free()` once per product.
