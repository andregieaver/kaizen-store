# D907: Added to a team, told by email with the way in, for Kaizena Flow's team and every store's

Date: 2026-11-26

**Someone added to a team heard nothing (26 November 2026, the owner: "Yes, send the invitation email").** Adding a lawyer under Platform →
Team made their account and role and told no one; the owner had to send the way in by hand. A store owner inviting staff under Settings →
Staff was in the same place.

- **Both teams, the same email** (`teamInviteEmail()`, `src/lib/team-invite-email.ts`): who added them, to what (Kaizena Flow's team, or the
  store by its name) and as which role, and a *Sign in* button to the sign-in page with `next` set to where they work: Legal reviews for a
  lawyer, the platform admin for the platform's other roles, the store's admin for a store's staff. It names the sign-in page's own button,
  read from its words (`SIGN_IN_WORDS`), since an added account has no password; for Kaizena Flow's team it says the first sign-in sets up
  two steps with an authenticator app. In the inviter's admin language, the only one known of a new account.
- Sent by `addPlatformAdmin()` (`platform.team_invite`) and `inviteStaff()` (`staff.team_invite`) after the addition is saved and audited,
  best effort: the addition stands whatever the email does. Its idempotency key holds the account and the minute, so a retry sends once and
  someone removed and added again is told again. Both kinds are staff email (`EMAIL_KINDS`).
- A role change or a collaborator's invitation sends nothing yet; a collaborator's (`inviteCollaborator()`) is its next step.

Idle cost: nothing; one email per person added.
