# D584: A new layer over a background starts at a depth

**A new layer over a background starts at a depth (13 November 2026, `GLOW_DEPTH`, `HORIZON_DEPTH`, `PATTERN_DEPTHS`, `PICTURE_DEPTHS`, `newLayer()`, `newPictureLayer()`, `setPattern()` and `setPictureFit()` in `src/lib/background-layers.ts`).** D534's layers each travel at their own speed as the page scrolls, but one added by hand started *With the page*, so a backdrop an owner built layer by layer stayed flat until every slider was moved, and the owner read it as the layers not moving apart at
all.
Each new layer now starts where its kind usually lies, in steps the slider reaches: a glow far behind (-60), a horizon behind (-40), a grid (-20) and dots (-15) a little behind, stars close (50), a placed picture a little closer than the page (30, a cut-out in front) and one that fills the part behind (-30), as the ready-made backdrops already do. **It follows what the layer is:** a pattern changed to stars, or a picture set to fill the part, takes the new kind's depth where the layer still had
the old kind's, and keeps a depth the owner set themselves (and *With the page* where they chose it). Layers already on pages and the ready-made backdrops are as they were. "Reduce motion" still draws everything still, and the builder's canvas shows the travel only with its motion preview on. **The platform:** the same builder and renderer, so Kaizena Flow's own pages have it in the same change. No migration.

Date: 2026-11-13
