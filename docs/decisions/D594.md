# D594: Contact details at checkout

**Contact details at checkout: the phone and a person's name, each off, optional or required (task 1 of `docs/checkout-fields-tasks.md`; 14 November 2026, `src/lib/checkout-contact.ts`, `src/server/checkout-contact.ts`, `ContactFieldsCard`).** Settings, Selling, Checkout rules has a *Contact details* card: the phone and a person's name, each *Not asked* (the default), *Optional* or *Required* (`stores.checkout_phone`, `stores.checkout_name`, written only by `saveCheckoutContact()`,
`settings:write`, audited `store.checkout_contact` with `checkoutPhone` and `checkoutName`). What one order asks is `contactAsked()`: a local delivery always asks the phone, required (D237); the name is asked only when nothing is sent to an address (downloads, bookings, pickup), since the delivery address holds one.
The checkout page on our site asks them (`CheckoutContactOption`), checks them with `cleanContact()` (a phone in E.164 by `e164Phone()`), and `saveCheckoutContactAction()` checks them again against the order and keeps them on the order waiting for payment (`saveOrderContact()`, laid over `orders.billing_address`) before Stripe is asked to pay; a paid session keeps them where it gives no phone or name of its own (`saveCustomer()`).
Stripe is not asked to collect them (only a local delivery's phone also goes to `confirm({ phoneNumber })`, as before), so Stripe and Mollie behave alike: Mollie's cart form always asks the name and requires the phone when the store requires it. Stripe's own page and pay links ask only what Stripe asks. Tests: `checkout-contact.test.ts`, `checkout-contact.int.test.ts`, `checkout-section.test.ts`, `commerce.test.ts`.
