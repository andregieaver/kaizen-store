# D347: Live view and revenue after a search

**Live view and revenue after a search (wave 8 unit C, `docs/wave-8-analytics-ai.md` section 4, C); built.** Analytics → Live (`/admin/{store}/analytics/live`, `analytics:read`, behind the shop) is `liveFigures()` (`src/server/analytics-live.ts`), read on each request and again every 10 seconds while the tab is shown (`LiveRefresh`, decision 2: polling): visitors with a page view in the last 5 minutes and their countries (visits keep no place finer, so a table and no map), today's visits,
today's paid orders and revenue (`periodTotals()` for the store's day, a euro case in `analytics-live.int.test.ts`), carts with something in them changed in the last 30 minutes and checkouts waiting for payment placed in the last `CHECKOUT_MINUTES`; without visit counting the visitor figures say what is missing and link to switching it on.
A search keeps the day's visit (`search_queries.visit_id`, migration `search_visits`): the search page keys the visitor as `/api/visit` does (`visitKeyOf()`, only while counting is on, never for a robot or with a privacy signal) and the search's insert finds the visit row, so nothing new is kept about the person, no cookie is set and nothing goes in the browser; erasure deletes the searches of the person's carts' visits (the register's `search_queries` entry is now `delete`).
The Traffic page's search section gains what searches led to: searches tied to a visit, those followed by a paid order placed from a cart of the same visit at or after the search, those orders each once and their revenue (per currency, converted, a currency with no rate counted), orders per term and day by day; and searches that found something but whose results were rarely opened (`LOW_CLICK_MIN`, `LOW_CLICK_RATE`), linked to a synonym and to search rules (D324).
New CSVs `traffic.low_click_terms`, `traffic.search_days`. Migration versions: `20261027231859_search_visits` (applied by CI at 00:35 UTC on 28 October 2026; advisors checked after: INFO only).
