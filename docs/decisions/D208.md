# D208: An FAQ's answers hold components

**An FAQ's answers hold components.** Under each answer's text an owner can add up to twelve components of their own (text, heading, image, button, two buttons, separator, icon list, table, video, social links), added, ordered, edited and taken out from the question in the FAQ's General tab, and opened in their own settings by pressing them on the canvas.
They are ordinary components inside the FAQ: their own ids, styles, typography, spacing and translations, copied with the FAQ (also as a template or a global part), checked for accessibility, and their words added to the FAQPage data for search engines. They never move, are never global or bound to a field, and show to every visitor, because an answer is what the FAQPage data says it is.
A question shows once it has a text or a component; one answered by components without words (a button alone) is left out of the FAQPage data. Accordions and tabs are unchanged.
