# D442: The docs portal, first step

**The docs portal, first step: its host, its frame and its content as code (wave 14 unit E1 and E2, `docs/wave-14-developer-platform.md` E); built.** *Host:* `NEXT_PUBLIC_DOCS_HOST` (`docs.kaizenaflow.com`, read at build time, `src/lib/docs-host.ts`) and `docsHostRoutes()` in `next.config.ts`, as the theme store's (D249): on the host `/` is the docs' front page, `/owners/…`, `/developers/…`, `/changelog` and `/reference/…` (unit E4's, a 404 until then) their pages, `/no` and
`/no/owners/…` the Norwegian ones, with its own `sitemap.xml`, `robots.txt`, `llms.txt` and `llms-full.txt`; any other address there goes to the same one on Kaizena Flow's site, the API and `public` answer as on every host, and Kaizena Flow's own `/docs/…` and `/no/docs/…` move there for good (308). The host is never a store's (`storeHostRoutes()` takes Kaizena Flow's own hosts as a list now, `storeOfHost()`) and its visits are Kaizena Flow's own.
Without the variable the docs are at `/docs` and `/no/docs` on Kaizena Flow's site, which is how they run now, locally and in CI's `e2e` job; Kaizena Flow's sitemap index names `/docs/sitemap.xml` then, its `llms.txt` links the docs' always, and `docs` is a reserved page slug in the app (`RESERVED_PAGE_SLUGS`; the database's check is left as it is, as for `get-domain`, `holding` and `report`, so a page of that name already in production cannot fail the migration).
Every page also answers as plain Markdown at its address plus `.md` (a rewrite on every host to the route `/docs/md/…`, as no page segment can end in `.md`), with its links made full; no docs page is ever under `/api`. *Frame:* two root layouts of their own, `(docs)` and `(docs-nb)` (`DocsRoot`), drawn in Kaizena Flow's theme with its fonts and its light or dark mode, without the site's header, footer, chat or consent banner: a top bar (the mark, *Store owners*, *Developers*, *API reference*
leading to the developers' track until E4 (`docsReferencePath()`), *Changelog*, *Status* to the status page, a search button switched off until E3 whose ⌘K, Ctrl+K or `/` already says `DOCS_SEARCH_EVENT`, and the language switch on owners' pages that have both languages), the track's tree (sections and pages, the current one marked, folded under a button on a phone from the first paint, so nothing moves when the script arrives), *On this page* (h2, h3), breadcrumbs, previous and next across
sections, the last-checked day, *In the admin:* from the admin map, a link to the page's Markdown, *Was this page helpful?*, print styles, a skip link, landmarks and 24 px targets. Its words are `platformText(lang).docs` in English and Norwegian (the chrome scan holds `src/components/docs/`).
The only scripts are the tree's button, the feedback and the search shortcut. *Content as code:* articles are `content/docs/{track}/{section}/{slug}.{lang}.md` (owners in `en` and `nb`, developers in `en`), sections `_section.{lang}.md`, the changelog `developers/changelog.md` (unit A's, shown at `/docs/changelog`), and `missing-nb.json` the owners' pages still without their Norwegian one, each with its reason.
`compileDocs()` (`src/lib/docs-content.ts`, pure) reads the spec's front matter (`docs-front-matter.ts`, a hand-written subset of YAML: scalars, `[a, b]` lists, comments; `operations` and `tools` added for unit A's names) and the body (`docs-markdown.ts`: headings `##` to `####` with stable ids and `{#own-id}`, paragraphs with strong, emphasis, code and links, nested lists, tables, fenced code coloured by `docs-highlight.ts`, callouts `> [!NOTE]` and the rest, quotes, rules and pictures under
`public/kaizen/docs/`) into a typed `DocPage` with its tree, ordered by `order` then title; HTML is kept as the characters typed and reported, and a link goes only to `/docs/…`, `#heading`, the site's own routes, an admin page (`/admin/{store}/…`, drawn as a link to the admin's front door until the guide's *navigate*, E5) or `https:`/`http:`/`mailto:`; anything else (`javascript:`, `data:`, `//host`, relative) stays as text and is reported.
React draws the tree (`DocsMarkdown`): no HTML string, no sanitizer.
`src/lib/docs-content.test.ts` compiles every file in CI and fails on any problem: front matter, unknown track, kind, area (`DOC_AREAS`, for E3's facets) or section, broken links and anchors, `admin:` and admin links not in the admin map (D107), decisions without a row here, pictures not in `public/`, unknown features (D178), an owners' page in English without its Norwegian page or a reason, and, once `src/lib/admin-api-routes.ts` exists, operations not in `ADMIN_API` (the MCP catalogue's tools
likewise when it ships). `docsIndex()` (`src/server/docs.ts`) reads the files from disk when the docs are built (every address is prerendered from `docsPaths()`) and keeps the index; `outputFileTracingIncludes` carries `content/docs/**` into the docs' routes for an address drawn at run time.
Pushes of `content/**` run CI and deploy (`ci.yml`'s `paths`), unlike the team's own `*.md`. *Feedback:* `commerce.docs_feedback` (path, language, yes or no, an optional sentence of at most 500 characters, the time; no address, account, cookie or browser; RLS on, no policy; in `NOT_PERSONAL`), written only by `src/server/docs-feedback.ts` through `/api/docs/feedback` (`sameSite()`, budget `docs_feedback`: 60 an hour a visitor and 3,000 for the site; a page the docs have; the sentence added by
the answer's id within the hour, cleaned by `cleanShopperText()`, refused when too long), read at `/admin/platform/docs/feedback` (under Website: each page's yes and no with the least helpful first, the answers newest first by page, by yes or no and with a sentence; *Remove the sentence* keeps the vote and is audited). *First pages:* owners' *Create your store* and *Add your first product* (English and Norwegian), developers' *Build on Kaizena Flow* (what is there today and its honest status)
and *Authentication, scopes and limits* (`kf_` tokens, scopes, 120 and 1,200 a minute, pages, `Idempotency-Key`, the error codes), and the changelog's first entry.
Tests: the host's routes through Next's own matcher (`docs-host.test.ts`), front matter, the Markdown subset and its refusals, the highlighter, the compiler, the text outputs, the drawn HTML (`docs-markdown.test.ts`), the feedback's reading; `docs-feedback.int.test.ts`; `e2e/docs.spec.ts` (Kaizena Flow's `/docs`, with axe in light and dark) and `e2e/docs-host.spec.ts` (CI's `hosts` job, built with `NEXT_PUBLIC_DOCS_HOST=docs.localhost:3000`).

Date: 2026-11-03

Asked for by the owner on 3 November 2026: a docs portal at docs.kaizenaflow.com for store owners and developers. *Why a Markdown subset written here and no library:* none was installed; what the docs need is small, and reading it into a typed tree that React draws means no HTML string ever exists to sanitize, while every refusal comes back with its line for the CI check (a library such as `marked` renders HTML that would then need a sanitizer, and its extensions are a wider surface). *Why the
files are read from disk and not imported or generated into a module:* an article is never code and there is no generated copy to keep in step; every page is prerendered at build, and the tracing covers the few run-time reads. *Why roots of their own:* the docs load no chat, banner or site scripts, so they stay fast and the frame's few parts are the only JavaScript. *A person must:* add the `docs` CNAME (`cname.vercel-dns.com.`) at the registrar, add the domain to the Vercel project and set
`NEXT_PUBLIC_DOCS_HOST` (`docs/kaizena-flow-switchover.md` §1.4), and read the Norwegian pages before the docs are announced (the spec's section 5). *Why E4's pages are made at build and not committed:* unit C adds operations, scopes and topics beside it, and a page made from the registries has each the moment it lands, with nothing to keep in step. *Why the examples are checked by the operation's own zod and not only its JSON Schema:* a JSON Schema cannot say a refinement (a variant by its id
or its SKU, not both), and an example a caller copies must be one the API takes. *Why Python's standard library:* the sample runs as copied, and in CI's test, with nothing to install. *Why one section for the reference:* `docs_index.section` takes `owners/…` or `developers/…` alone, so one section of the developers' track needs no migration and lists the reference as a written section is listed. *E6, the first slice of the content (4 November 2026):* the developers' track gets every page but
the apps' (which waits for unit D): the introduction brought up to date (123 operations, 30 topics, the owner MCP open to any assistant, powers), *Stores, money, times and versions* beside *Authentication, scopes and limits*; quickstarts for a first Admin API call, a headless storefront, a webhook receiver and an AI assistant connected over MCP; *Receiving events* and *Verify a delivery's signature*; *The owner MCP server*, *The shopping agents' MCP server* and *Give the AI manager your own
tools*; the WordPress plugin; *Build a theme and submit it* (a theme is a store's look shared for review: there is no package to upload). The owners' track gets *Choose a plan* in English and Norwegian (the Norwegian for the owner to read).
Each page was written from the rules and the code, names its decisions, and its code was run: the signature checks against `signWebhook()`'s signatures in Node.js, Python and PHP, the storefront examples parsed by the contract's own zod, the MCP discovery documents and challenges printed by the pure functions.
Writing them found, and this slice fixes: a resend carried a new `Kaizena-Webhook-Id` while the contract said it keeps the body's id (`attempt()` now sends the payload's `id`); the reference's PHP signature check accepted a delivery signed with an empty key when its variable was unset, and its Python check raised on a non-ASCII header (all three now refuse an empty secret; Python compares bytes); `products.create` and `products.update` still said publishing was the owner's alone (contract 1.5,
changelog).
Found and left for their own change: a kept change's approval often shows only *Run {tool}.* (`approvalSummary()`'s fallback); a store without a domain of its own has an agents' profile address that is not served (`agentProfileUrl()`); the agents' MCP answers only the first ten calls of a batch, silently; the WordPress plugin refuses a cart line over 20 units though a quantity rule allows 9,999; the deliveries list defaults to 25 where the contract says 50; the setup refusal names the *Plan*
page, now *Billing*. *E6, the owners' areas (4 November 2026):* thirteen sections in English and Norwegian, 51 pages: products (variants and options, prices, quantity rules and price breaks, bundles, pictures and video, categories and tags, import and export), stock (stock and locations, pre-orders and backorders), orders (managing, sending, drafts and quotes, returns and withdrawals, refunds and exchanges, invoices and credit notes), payments (setting up, methods, payouts and fees, disputes and
risk), shipping (rates, pickup and local delivery, carriers), countries, languages and tax (countries, languages and currencies, VAT, OSS and IOSS, customs and duties, producer responsibility), the website (the page builder, design and themes, the blog, menus, header and footer, SEO, redirects and domains), marketing (discount codes and campaigns, email marketing, gift cards, reviews, loyalty and referrals, product feeds and ads), customers (accounts, companies and B2B, personal data), AI (the
AI manager, the autopilot, the chat assistant, AI texts and pictures), reports and analytics (analytics; reports, targets and alerts), team and settings (staff and permissions, security, notifications) and troubleshooting (common problems).
Written by ten writers from the rules, the decisions and the code, every label copied from the admin's own words in each language (a screen still in English is named in italics on the Norwegian page); the Norwegian pages wait for the owner's reading before the docs are announced.
