# D201: Icons in table cells

**Icons in table cells.** A cell may hold icons (a tick, a cross, a minus, a star and the rest of the icon list's set), alone or with words. In the table's settings, click in a cell and press an icon in *Icons in cells* (the common ones as buttons, the rest from a list) to put it at the cursor; the Style tab sets their colour. A screen reader hears the icon's name.
