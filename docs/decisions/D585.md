# D585: The AI spends money only through what the owner connected, and first moves ad budgets inside the owner's own ad account

**The AI spends money only through what the owner connected, and first moves ad budgets inside the owner's own ad account (13 November 2026, step 1a of `docs/agent-spending.md`, `commerce.ad_accounts`, `src/lib/ad-budgets.ts`, `src/server/ad-accounts.ts`, the Ads page's *Ad budgets*).** The owner asked how the agent could pay by itself, flexibly, reliably and safely.
The answer, kept in `docs/agent-spending.md`: the agent never holds a card; it asks for one named thing, code checks it against the owner's limits and pays, through account billing where the platform already bills the owner (ads, labels, domains), and only otherwise through a single-use virtual card from a prepaid wallet answered in real time by our own rule (steps 2 to 4, to do). **Step 1a:** a store connects its Meta ad account (the account number with or without `act_`, and a system user's
token with `ads_management`), read with the token first: Meta's name, currency and time zone are kept, the token only encrypted; an account in a currency Meta counts otherwise (HUF, in whole forints) is refused.
The Ads page starts with *Ad budgets*: what the ads spent this month, about where the month ends at today's budgets (each daily budget that is on, for every day left, today included; unknown while a lifetime budget runs), each campaign's or ad set's budget, and the AI's monthly limit, typed in the account's currency (empty: the AI never raises a budget itself); a new token and disconnecting are under *Connection*. Staff with `marketing:read` see it, only the owner changes it.
The autopilot gains *Largest ad budget raise* (`budgets.adRaisePercent`, 20 %). `adChangeProblem()` is the rule the tools will use: a change that cannot spend more always may; a raise needs the limit, stays within the percentage and keeps the projection within the limit. `changeAdBudget()` checks a change against the account as Meta has it now (`previewAdChange()`: the account's own budget, a daily one, a change, 1.00 to 1 000 000.00 a day) and audits its figures.
Meta's own words never reach a page or a prompt. **The platform:** Kaizena Flow's ads are its house store's account, on the house store's Ads page, so it has this in the same change. **Step 1b** (a push of its own after this migration's, as `docs/ci-migrations.md` asks of a tool change): the AI manager's `list_ad_budgets` (`marketing:read`; the figures in the account's currency, written by code) and `set_ad_budget` (gate `spend`, area `money`, the owner's only; a daily amount typed in the
account's currency, a switch, or both; refused before it is kept when Meta's account would refuse it or it changes nothing; the approval's words `adChangeWords()` from Meta's figures, `src/server/ad-budget-tools.ts`). `autopilotDecision()` reads `adBudgetAutopilotProblem()` (the account now, then `adChangeProblem()` with *Largest ad budget raise*), so the autopilot lowers or pauses at the money area's level and raises only within the limit.
