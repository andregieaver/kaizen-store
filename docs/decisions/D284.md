# D284: A theme's photographs are tried again by themselves until they are made

**A theme's photographs are tried again by themselves until they are made.** Kinetic's seven photograph units (its own look and *Night run* and *Glacier*, at a computer's and a phone's width) failed three tries a minute apart right after its content was published at 00:47, with *The browser stopped …* and one load timeout, and stayed `failed` with no thumbnail or highlights until a person pressed *Try again*.
Its page was not the cause: photographed here with the job's own `shootLook()`, it took 8 s and Chromium peaked at 1.23 GB, as Orbit and Botanica do, and given its tries back in production every unit was made on its first try (07:44 to 07:46).
So a unit now gets rounds: three tries in a row, then a rest (`SHOT_RESTS`: 10 minutes, an hour, 6 hours, a day; `afterFailedTry()`), kept as a waiting unit whose claim lies ahead (`claimed_until`), which `claimThemeShot()` passes over; `failed` only after the last round. A run that stops at the end of a round rests the same way (`queueThemeShots()`).
The theme's Pictures panel says when a resting unit is tried again (*Tried again by itself …*, `ShotStatus.retryAt`) and *Try again now* takes resting units too. A failure keeps the browser's own words beside the plain sentence (`shotProblem()`) and is logged, so the next one says what broke.
The queue already wants every published theme's looks on its published content whoever made the theme (a person, Claude or the platform AI to come), so with the rounds every such theme gets its photographs and highlights.

Date: 2026-10-19

The owner: "the kinetic theme has no generated thumbnail or highlight images … Make sure that all themes get these when the theme is initially created by you, by me or by the platform AI." `theme-shots.test.ts` holds the rounds and `shotProblem()`, `theme-shots.int.test.ts` a unit resting after each round, passed over while it rests, failed after the last and taken again on *Try again*, `theme-shots-panel.test.ts` the panel's words.
Kinetic's units were given their tries back in production (as *Try again* does) and were all made.
