# D432: A part's schedule one step from its settings

**A part's schedule one step from its settings.** Every row, column and component has *Show from* and *Show until* at the foot of its General tab: local dates and times in the store's time zone (Oslo time on Kaizena Flow's own pages), either end open; outside them the server leaves the part out of the page.
They are Display's own date condition (D179 phase 4), read and written only through `scheduleOf()`/`withSchedule()`: setting them keeps every other condition (put beside the dates in each group, a plain *Signed in* or *Signed out* too), clearing them takes only the dates away, Never stays Never, and a part whose dates differ between its groups says to change them in Advanced, Visibility. A start after its end is said and not written. The canvas marks a part with dates alone *Scheduled*.

Date: 2026-11-03

Asked for by the owner: "scheduling the visibility of rows, columns and components conditionally based on start and end date". The rule already existed (*Display → Conditional logic → Date and time between*) but two steps deep under Advanced, where Homer would not find it. *Why the same condition and not a field of its own:* one rule the server already draws, which Display shows and edits, and nothing new to copy, translate or keep in step.
