# D469: Kaizena Flow runs on Kaizena Flow, steps 3 and 4

**Kaizena Flow runs on Kaizena Flow, steps 3 and 4: its site copied into its own store, with its own parts (6 November 2026, `docs/platform-parity-plan.md`, `commerce.copy_platform_site()`, `commerce.house_site_copies`, `src/server/house-site.ts`, `HouseSiteCard`).** Platform → Website has a card that copies the platform's own site into Kaizena Flow's own store, as often as wanted: its pages, headers and footers, menus, saved parts (made private there, as Kaizena Flow's own are the
marketplace's), text styles, categories and tags, content types and entries, and the old addresses of its pages; and onto the store its look, fonts, CSS, logos, SEO, tracking, header, footer, standard menus and front page. One transaction; every id inside content is the copy's (`copy_remap()`, as a store's copy does, D129); each source row lands on the same copy every run (`house_site_copies`), so running it again brings the latest and removes the copy of a row that is gone.
Nothing of the live site changes; the copy is looked at in the store's admin and behind its private mode. *Pictures are not copied*: `media.url` is unique, pages keep their addresses, and the library's rows move to the store with the switch (step 5). *Step 4*: the store's builder offers Kaizena Flow's own parts (its plans, a domain search, the list of themes) on Kaizena Flow's own store's pages only, and the list of themes draws there (`ThemeStoreSection`); the plans and the domain search
already drew wherever they stand. Its theme and theme-store pages (`platform_page_roles` `themes`, `theme`) stay the platform's, drawn by the theme store's own routes.
