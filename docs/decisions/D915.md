# D915: Buy now goes straight to payment, past the cart page, where the store, a category, a tag or the product chooses so

Date: 2026-11-27

**A store can let shoppers go from a product's Buy now, or a pricing table's Choose plan, straight to the payment page. It chooses this on
Settings > Checkout, and a category, a tag or the product can choose otherwise. A basket holding any product that needs the cart goes through the
cart.** Asked for by the owner: "Let store owners choose if cart page is skipped as step towards the checkout hierarchically. level 1:
/settings/checkout level 2: per category level 3: per tag level 4: product. If any product in the cart requires a trip through the cart, it takes
precedence." The consents are given by pressing the button (the owner's choice, 27 November 2026).

- **Four levels**: `stores.skip_cart` (off by default), `terms.skip_cart` and `products.skip_cart` (null means as the level above), resolved by
  `commerce.skips_cart()`: the product, then its tags, then each category's nearest choice, then the store. Where tags or categories disagree, the
  cart wins. A basket skips only when every product in it does (`cartSkips()`).
- **What the shopper sees**: a product that skips says Buy now instead of Add to cart. A plan that skips says its terms under its button. The
  press puts the item in the cart as before, and `startDirect()` opens the payment page when nothing must be asked first. The cart is shown
  instead when payments are off, the store uses Mollie, nothing is due (the cart asks who it is for), a company, its terms or rules are involved,
  the store has questions, the shopper must sign in first, or the delivery needs a postal code.
- **The press is the agreement**: what the cart would ask a tick for is said beside the button, in the cart's own words. That covers a download's
  withdrawal, and a subscription's renewals at their price with any trial and commitment. One function writes these words for both
  (`checkoutConsents()`). The button sends what it said, and the server checks it against the basket: if the basket asks anything the button did
  not say (a download already in the basket, renewals at another price, shipping on each renewal, a booked service), the shopper gets the cart.
  The payment page shows the store's terms as for any checkout.
- **Mended with it**: the product editor did not read back a product's choice for a price of nothing (D913, now "At 0, show"), so saving the product reset it to
  "As above". It now reads both choices back.
- **The platform**: Kaizena Flow's own store is a store and has the setting. Kaizena Flow's own plans are billed by the platform from its sign-up,
  not sold through a cart, so there is no cart to skip there.

Idle cost: none per request or run. The catalogue's cached reads call `skips_cart()` once per product, and Buy now reads the basket once more
before it opens the payment page.
