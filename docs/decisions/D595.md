# D595: The admin redesigned, round 1

**The admin redesigned, round 1 (`docs/audits/admin-redesign-round-1.md`; 14 November 2026).** Seven first principles for Homer and Lisa; round 1 sets the dashboard, record and editor patterns.
Step 1, the frame: a search icon in the top bar opens the page finder (⌘K, Ctrl+K or `/`; `PageFinder`, `finderResults()`, searched on the server over the admin map with the member's role and the store's features), the AI manager moves to ⌘J with a secondary button, and phones get a bottom bar of four sections and More (`PhoneTabBar`).
Step 2, the store's Home: a greeting and one sentence, *Next up* with the page's one green button, the week as bars against the week before, latest orders with pictures, *Needs a look*, the setup ring, *Ask your AI manager*, milestones, and the first week's path to opening (`src/lib/home.ts`, `src/server/home.ts`, `src/components/admin/home/`). Step 3, Kaizena Flow's own Home in the same pattern (`src/server/platform-home.ts`).
Step 4, the order page: state chips and a menu in the header, the order's path (`StepPath`), sending first, pictures beside the items, *Refund or cancel* folded, the refund button naming the amount and asking first. Step 5, the product editor: photos first, rare word fields folded, a Draft / Published switch, *What shoppers see* and *Ready to sell* beside it (`ProductPreview`, `readiness()`).

Date: 2026-11-14

The owner chose, 14 November 2026: ship each step to main; the AI manager's button secondary; a plain search icon; the phone's bar and Kaizena Flow's own Home in this round (its Home is step 3, the platform's parity). No migration.
