# D590: Links a press cannot use are not prefetched

**Links a press cannot use are not prefetched (bloat review step 0.12; 14 November 2026).** Two rules (`docs/performance.md`, The rules): a link whose press does not open its href (the view menu's language and currency links: the handler goes to the same page in the other view), or that few visitors press and that leads to another country's layout or a legal page (the country lists, cookies, withdrawal, the legal pages), carries `prefetch={false}`; and so does a link that crosses to another root
layout, because the press is a full page load and a prefetch only pulls the other surface's scripts and stylesheet. The root layouts: the two (platform) groups, the two (docs) groups, the two (theme-preview) groups, admin, s/[store]/(chooser), s/[store]/[market], s/[store]/private/[market], holding, passport/[id], sms, supply and embed.
Changed: the view menu, the store's three country lists, withdrawal, cookies and legal links (`store-layout.tsx`, and the builder's parts in `site-parts.tsx`: countries, cookies, the house store's sign-up, /admin), the front door's two market links, the six links from Kaizena Flow's site to /admin and the one to the demo store, the sign-up page's link to sign in, the signed-out admin's two links to /sign-up and its logo to /, and the admin's View store: 23 attributes, no href, handler or class
changed.
`src/lib/cross-root-links.scan.test.ts` fails, naming file and line, on a literal /admin link outside the admin, a signed-out admin link to / or /sign-up, or a view-menu link without it; `e2e/prefetch.spec.ts` (a prefetch is a request with `next-router-prefetch` or `next-router-segment-prefetch`) holds the budgets: at most 23 prefetches and 0 for other views on the phone home read to the footer (47 and 24 before; 18 and 0 after), 0 at the front door (12), 0 requests to /admin from / (2 answers,
6 scripts, a stylesheet), and no stylesheet after load that the HTML did not link, on four pages. Kept on purpose: search, account, wishlist, cart, the logo, the menus and product tiles stay prefetched (step 2.6 decides them with PolicyLink). No migration.
