# D287: Meridian, a signature theme for a corporate consultancy, and the first premium theme

**Meridian, a signature theme for a corporate consultancy, and the first premium theme.** Made from scratch (`src/lib/signature/meridian.ts`, `docs/signature-themes.md`): ivory and deep navy, Newsreader in light display sizes with brass italics over Hanken Grotesk, a header over a full-screen hero whose words rise in, four principles between hairlines, three practices as tall pictures, engagements to book, a numbered approach, a quote in large italics, a navy footer and a navy band over the
city; presets *Midnight* and *Harbour*; nine sections. Its demo store, Aldren Partners (pack `consulting`), is a strategy and management consultancy whose six engagements are appointments; its photographers' briefs are an annual report's (boardrooms, offices, the city, still lifes; people only in the story), with drawn art standing in (`scripts/theme-art/meridian.js`). `LaunchTheme.prices` give it NOK 3,490, SEK 3,490, DKK 2,490 and EUR 299, set when the sync first makes it.
