# D150: The AI can copy another website's page into a store's page builder, and show its work

**The AI can copy another website's page into a store's page builder, and show its work.** On the store's AI studio page (`/admin/{store}/pages/ai`) the owner gives an address, a number of improving passes (1–10) and confirms they may copy the page; a job (`commerce.page_replications`, one active per store, migration `page_replications`) then runs step by step while the page is open (`tickReplication()`, one unit of work per request, a lock, resumable, stoppable with Abort): the page is opened
in Chromium at computer and phone width (`replicate-open.ts`, `@sparticuz/chromium` on Vercel, the same launcher as the cookie scan), its boxes and styles read (`extractPage()`), the site's AI describes its design from photographs, the text is taken as it is, pictures, videos and Google fonts are downloaded as the store's own, and the page is built as a draft (`buildReplica()`) from what was *measured*: sections become rows, boxes side by side columns, pieces of content blocks, and every size,
space and colour is the original's own, kept as a style model written into the page's CSS (D100). The model never writes structure.
Each pass photographs the draft at both widths through a token-guarded preview page (`/admin/account/replica/{job}`), scores it against the original (`compareRasters()`, a quarter-size comparison in bands), sets spacing right by measuring (`calibrate()`), and lets the AI propose changes from pictures of the weakest places as a short list of declarations on parts that exist (`parsePatchPlan()`, `applyPatchPlan()`: allowed properties, clean values, at most 40 a pass); a copy that is as close as
pixels allow ends early. The owner sees the steps, a bar, a log, the original beside the copy as it grows (scrolling together, with each pass's match), and at the end a summary written in code from the facts (what went well, what failed, the match at both widths) with links to preview and edit the draft.
Safety: only public http(s) addresses (`parseReplicaUrl()`, `isBlockedAddress()`), every fetch through `safeFetch()` (every address of the name checked, the connection pinned to the checked address, redirects rechecked, size and time limits) and the browser's own requests refused the same way; the owner confirms the right to copy; the copy is a draft hidden from search engines. See [`page-replicator.md`](page-replicator.md).
Limits: 50 rows, 100 blocks and 50 000 characters of CSS per page; forms, carousels, fonts outside Google Fonts and pages behind a sign-in are not copied.
