# D540: The admin's English is fetched per area, and a pressed admin link shows it is on its way

**The admin's English is fetched per area, and a pressed admin link shows it is on its way (11 November 2026, PSI PERF-01, code review step 0.10, `src/components/admin/admin-text-later.ts`, `LinkPending` in `src/components/admin/store-admin-nav.tsx`, `e2e/signed-in/admin-text-fallbacks.spec.ts`).** Every admin screen carried the English of all the admin's areas, about 1.1 MB of its first scripts, for `useAdminText()` to fall back on where a page had not handed an area down.
Fetching it per area was tried on 31 October and backed out: a part waiting for its words is not interactive yet, and the content type editor's Save, pressed that early, submitted without its script.
Now every area's English but `common` and `signIn` (read outside the admin too) is fetched per area again, and a fallback is a fault: it is noted on the page (`window.__adminTextFallbacks`) and a signed-in test visits every admin screen without an id in its address, then one screen of each kind its links lead to (an order, a product, a type from a preset), and fails on any.
Its first runs found the platform's docs guide (`website`) and the new content type from a preset and the content type and entry editors (`website`, `builder`), which explains the Save of 31 October; each now hands its areas down, which also draws those parts in Norwegian for a Norwegian account. First scripts: Home 2.46 to 1.28 MB, the builder 4.39 to 3.34 MB; the budgets in `admin-scripts.spec.ts` were set again with about 4 % of room.
With that room, the sidebar's, the section links', the tabs' and the rail's links show a thin bar while the page they lead to loads (`useLinkStatus`), for a page that was not fetched ahead; the page's own outline (`loading.tsx`) follows once the address changes. PERF-01 stays open until the admin's p75 LCP, read again from its speed beacon, is under 2.5 s.
