# D759: A cart with products and the checkout's payment step are measured

**A cart with products and the checkout's payment step are measured (bloat review step 1.7; 18 November 2026).** The Stripe SDK can be pointed at a stand-in (`src/lib/stripe-endpoint.ts`): only with `STRIPE_ALLOW_TEST_URL=1`, only at this machine's own address over http, never on Vercel, as the VIES stand-in. `e2e/fixtures/stripe-stub.mjs` answers the server's calls and `e2e/fixtures/stripe-js-stub.js` the browser's.
A second `pnpm start` of the same build (`E2E_MONEY_PORT`, 3001; not in the hosts or signed-in jobs) is the only e2e server with platform keys, dummy values in Stripe's test shape, so every spec that relies on payments being off is untouched.
On a phone, a store that takes payments in test mode is held for a cart with two lines and the checkout at its payment step, each at its first figure plus 5 %: first scripts 729,906 each; documents 99,324 and 89,441; router requests 19 and 18, none on the settled payment form; no other host but the provider's script on the checkout. Identical in about 20 runs. After phase 2 the checkout's first scripts are at most 670,000 bytes. Commits 873efcd8, 596444af.
