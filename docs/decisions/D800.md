# D800: The builder paints before the rich text editor and a store language

**The builder paints before the rich text editor and a store language (bloat review step 2.29; 19 November 2026).** The rich text
editor (Tiptap, ProseMirror) loads after the builder's first paint behind `src/components/admin/lazy/editors.tsx`: the panel's text
fields are drawn in the browser only, the canvas's inline editors after a press (`React.lazy`, the block's own view held until the
chunk arrives, so a double press still lands on the block), and `warmEditors()` fetches the chunks when the builder is idle. The
admin's browser loads one storefront language, only when a preview needs it (`useSiteWords()`), and never imports `lib/i18n` or
`lib/messages`: rule 5 of `admin-bundle.scan.test.ts` holds it; the draft-order editor gets its unit words from the server. Measured
on a local build: the builder 2,791,016 to 2,007,139 bytes of first-load script (ceiling 2,300,000; 3,303,372 at the review),
ProseMirror in the first load of 26 routes to none, draft orders 1,220,086 to 840,961; the bundle budget was lowered with them.
On production, pressing a heading within a second of the page showing must open the inline editor at the pressed place: a
signed-in check the owner makes. Commits 3c281cc53, ff17f0a12, c50cb63cf, 5f62939f9.
