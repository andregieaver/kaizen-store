# D878: Form fields styled per block: hidden labels behind placeholders, field look and sizes, and animated labels

Date: 2026-11-23

**A form component's fields take their own look (asked by the owner, 23 November 2026, after trying a prototype).** A page's form drew
its fields only in the theme's look, its labels always above. Each of the three form components (`form`, `emailForm`, `newsletter`) now
keeps a `fieldStyle` (`src/lib/form-field-style.ts`; its schema `form-field-style-schema.ts`, apart so the site loads no zod, D211):

- **Labels:** above the field (as before, left out), hidden where a question has a placeholder (the label stays for screen readers), or
  **animated**: the label rests where the placeholder would be, and once the field has focus or an answer it eases above the field, smaller
  and in capitals. A date always shows its own hint, so its label rests above; an empty dropdown's "Choose…" gives way to the label.
  A sign-up's email field keeps its words in the placeholder, so its form offers no label choice.
- **Fields:** outlined (as before), filled or underlined; background, text, border, focus (also ticks and radio buttons), label and
  placeholder colours; border width, corner radius, padding above and below and at the sides, space between questions and text size.
  Every input kind takes them: text, email, phone, number, date, long text, dropdown and the file button.
- **The animation** (only with animated labels): how it turns to capitals (a crossfade of the two spellings, the default, since browsers
  cannot animate the change of case; a switch; or keeping the case), whether the moved label lines up with the field's edge (default) or
  its text, the speed (220 ms), easing (smooth out), moved size (11 px), letter spacing (0.08 em), the space above the field (6 px), and
  whether the placeholder shows once the label has moved (it does). The defaults are those of the prototype the owner chose.

The site draws it with `src/app/form-look.css` (unlayered and scoped to `.kf-fields`, so it wins over the Tailwind utilities the shared
dropdown carries) from the data attributes and `--kf-*` custom properties `fieldStyleAttrs()` gives the form; every property not set falls
back to the theme's, so a form never styled is drawn as before. Only what differs from the defaults is stored (`trimFieldStyle()`). The
builder's form panel shows Homer the labels and the look; **Customise fields** and **Customise the animation** open the rest for Lisa.

Kaizena Flow's own pages use the same components, so its site has the same in the same change. Reduced motion moves labels at once.
Tests: `src/lib/form-field-style.test.ts`, `e2e/signed-in/forms.spec.ts` (the panel, and a label moving on the site).

**Words without weight.** The panel's words are one short group (`blocks.form.fieldWords`), reusing the catalogue's Look, Corners, Speed,
Easing, Letter spacing and the looks' names. To make room under the admin-words ceiling (`admin-text-reach.scan.test.ts`), Kaizena Flow's
screens that take a picture now import their two upload actions from `platform/upload-actions.ts` instead of the platform's whole
`actions.ts`: the article editor no longer reaches self sign-up and the AI manager's words, and falls from 1.85 MB and 24 areas to 1.14 MB
and 15. The ceiling is lowered to the new largest entry, the sandbox's editor (1.64 MB, 26 areas, unchanged).
