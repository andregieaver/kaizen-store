# D289: Authorise now, capture later

**Authorise now, capture later (wave 4 unit B).** `docs/wave-4-payments.md` 4 is the contract. An owner chooses when the card is charged (`order_settings.capture`: when the order is placed, the default; when it is sent; when staff press Capture).
A store that waits makes its checkout sessions with `capture_method: manual` for the orders that may wait (`captureModeFor()`: never subscriptions, bookings, deposits and venue balances, hosts' listings or downloads; with *when sent* never an order with nothing to send; drafts, pay links, order changes, exchanges and subscription boxes are not checkouts and charge at once); the order keeps its own `capture_mode`.
A paid session whose PaymentIntent waits for its capture makes the order `authorized`, a status between `pending_payment` and `paid` (`commerce.authorize_order_payment()`: `capture_before` from Stripe's card, else seven days; stock held to a day past it, the cart converted, the confirmation sent; nothing drawn, invoiced or counted, no staff alert for a paid order yet).
`capturePayment()` charges the whole or less (Stripe first, then `complete_order_payment()` in one transaction with the payment captured, Kaizena Flow's fee scaled to what was taken, and the rest of the hold as a `refunds` row of kind `release` that the credit-note trigger credits); with *when sent* `markSent()`, `markCollected()` and `markOutForDelivery()` capture first and record nothing if it fails. *Cancel and release* (`voidAuthorization()`), Stripe's `payment_intent.canceled` and the
five-minute job (a staff alert `staff.capture_due` a day before the end, once; past it Stripe is asked) cancel through `cancel_unpaid_order()`. The order page has a capture card (amount, Capture, Cancel and release; `orders:write`), the list a pay state *Card held* and filter, *To send* includes a held order to send, the history says *Card held* and *Captured*, and shoppers read *Confirmed* in their four languages.
Trigger functions that fire on `pending_payment → paid` (bonus, affiliate, integrations, staff events) fire on `authorized → paid` too.
