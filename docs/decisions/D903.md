# D903: Projects and checklists, ported from Kaizena Life

**Projects and checklists, ported from Kaizen Life's project management (`docs/projects.md`, the working plan).** A store's team plans
its work where it sells: projects with a status, a health, a next action and a target date; checklists that stand alone or belong to
projects; items with their own status and a stage in each project, a priority, an assignee on the team, start and due dates, repeats,
subtasks, sections and costs, all as rich as in Life. The owner's answers (25 November 2026): a store feature `projects` on every plan,
not a paid extension; store level (every row belongs to a store); a checklist item becomes Flow's one task (Work's tasks move into
items, step P6.1); pushed to `main`. And: everything about lists, items and statuses as rich as in Life.

Homer gets a plain list: a title and a box to tick, one *Add* button. Lisa switches on *Advanced list* for every item field at once,
then takes away what the list has no use for (only a switch turned off is stored, and hiding a field never clears it); her statuses,
stages, sections and views are one step away. Built in the steps of `docs/projects.md` section 5; each step's commit is recorded there.

The platform: Kaizena Flow's own team gets projects through its own store, as Deals (D464); the platform admin's own projects are step
P7.1.
