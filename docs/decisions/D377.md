# D377: Leaving

**Leaving: an owner's or staff member's own account erased, and the whole store as one file (wave 10 unit P, `docs/wave-10-must-haves.md` 4; PSI REG-03, DATA-05); built, the documents part earlier (`docs/security.md`, `docs/runbooks/breach.md`, `docs/dpia-ai.md`).** *Delete your account* (Your account, `/admin/account/delete`, any account) goes only through `eraseOwnAccount()` (`src/server/account-erasure.ts`): the platform is the controller of owners' and staff's data, and they had no way out
(REG-03). The account row stays, because about a hundred and fifty tables name it as who acted (the activity log, authors, Work, invoices); it is made anonymous in place (email `erased-{id}@erased.invalid`, no name, picture or sign-in user, switched off), so every place that names it names no one and the activity log is pseudonymised without being edited.
Deleted: its memberships, the AI manager's conversations (and their messages, approvals and plans), memories, notices, saved skills and follow-ups, push devices, recovery codes, Kaizen Life and WordPress links, support inbox state, alert choices (`forgetAlertRecipientIn()` in `staff-alerts.ts`) and the staff and security emails sent to it; its applications to open a store are emptied; its sign-in user is deleted through Supabase's admin API after the commit (a failure is logged; the account no
longer points at it). A customer with the same email in a store is the store's shopper, a different subject, and is not touched. Refused before anything changes, in the person's words, for a platform admin, the last current owner of a store that is not closed (named), an outside host and the holder of a domain not gone; a sign-in from the last ten minutes and the account's email typed are asked first.
The register (`PERSONAL_DATA`) now says `anonymise` for `accounts` and `access_requests` and `delete` for the AI manager's tables and push devices. *Take your store with you* (Close store, the owner's) is `storeExport()` (`src/server/store-export.ts`, POST `settings/close/export`, same site): the store's row and `STORE_EXPORT_TABLES` (pages and articles with drafts, places, redirects, menus, terms, blogs, content types and entries, saved parts, field groups, installed themes' looks, countries,
currencies, tax profile, places, shipping, returns, order and checkout settings, notifications and email texts, campaigns and codes, customer groups, segments, search merchandising) in one read-only snapshot as JSON, without any column named like a secret, token, hash, password, key or encrypted value (`SECRET_COLUMN`) or naming a person (`*_by`, a code's customers), audited `store.exported` with counts; the card links the product, stock, order and customer CSV files beside it.
Integration tests hold both (refusals change nothing; what goes and what stays; the file holds every table, no other store, no planted secret), and `e2e/signed-in/leaving.spec.ts` the screens. No migration.
