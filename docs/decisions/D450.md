# D450: Moving from Shopware

**Moving from Shopware: the German shop, kept German-proof (proposed 3 November 2026, `docs/moving-from-shopware.md`; nothing built).** A Shopware 6 shop is read through Kaizena Flow's read-only app from the Shopware Store (apps are the one way in that works on Shopware's SaaS too), or through an integration's two keys with a read-only role, and a Shopware 5 shop (no security fixes since July 2024) through its own REST API reader.
Everything goes through D445's engine (Connect, Check, Copy, Move; existing writers only; the address check, the reconciliation, undo, silent until Move).
Shopware's own data replaces D445's compromises: its manufacturers come over as manufacturers, with only missing GPSR addresses asked once per manufacturer; unit prices; every language; gross prices, so no VAT question, and one VAT-category question per tax rate; every old `seo_url` row redirected in one hop; voucher codes kept; reviews; legal pages kept as the owner's own; old invoices kept read-only on copied orders for GoBD; only newsletter recipients who confirmed (`optIn`) subscribed.
The DNS switch changes only the website's records, never nameservers or mail. Open for the owner: `direct` subscribers, old invoices, several storefronts, Shopware 5, hand-written German for the moving screens (section 12 of the plan).
