# D131: An affiliate program on two levels

**An affiliate program on two levels** (`docs/referrals.md`, `docs/affiliates.md`). **Platform** (`src/lib/referrals.ts`, `src/server/referrals.ts`, `src/server/referral-billing.ts`, SQL `commerce.referral_*`): any store owner gets a code and link at `/admin/account/referrals` (`/r/{code}` → `/sign-up?ref=`), a platform admin switches the program on and sets the rate at `/admin/platform/referrals`.
A referred store's plan invoices (amount without VAT, Stripe `invoice.paid`) and Kaizen's sale fee on its orders earn the referrer `commissionBps` (10 %) for `months` (12) from when the referral was made; it is **credit, per currency and never converted**, pending 30 days, then put on the referrer's own Kaizen plan invoices as one negative invoice line when Stripe says `invoice.created` (ledger entry first, Stripe line idempotent, given back if the line cannot be made or the invoice is voided);
refunds and credit notes take back their share, never below zero. The ledger is append-only like the bonus ledger (lots, allocations, idempotency keys, advisory lock per account).
Self-referral (same email, or a member of the new store) and blocked accounts earn nothing; a referrer sees referred stores by name and money only. **Store** (`src/lib/affiliates.ts`, `src/server/affiliates.ts`, SQL `commerce.affiliate_*`): a signed-in customer gets a code and link at `/account/referrals`; the program needs the bonus program on.
The referred friend (signed in, no earlier paid order in the store) gets `friendPercent` (10 %) off the goods of their first order, after campaigns and the group discount and before codes and credits: `orders.referral_discount_minor` is part of `discount_minor`, with each line's share, and `friendState()` is the only place it is decided, so `cartSummary()` and `placeOrder()` agree (`checkout-kinds.int.test.ts`).
The referrer earns bonus credits (`bonus_entries.kind = 'referral'`, `rewardBps` 5 %, for the friend's first `rewardOrders` paid orders, pending as the bonus program's `pendingDays`, a monthly cap per referrer) granted by a trigger when the order is paid and taken back by refunded share or on cancellation; the referral entries carry no order id, so the friend's own bonus maths is untouched.
Guards in SQL: never oneself, never a friend who has ordered, never a blocked referrer, never copied or host orders; one `affiliate_attributions` row per order, append-only. Attribution: `?ref=` is kept in memory and on the cart, and in a cookie (`kaizen_ref`, `kaizen_aff_{storeId}`, marketing) only after consent. Visits are counted by day and code, nothing about the visitor.
The AI manager reads and sets both programs (`get_/set_referral_program`, `get_my_referrals`, `get_/set_affiliate_program`, `block_affiliate`; changes are gated). Known limits: refunds made in Stripe's dashboard, renewals and guests do not count at the store level; a bare refund on a plan invoice without a credit note is not seen; the first subscription invoice is finalised at once and cannot take credit; without consent a reload loses the link.
