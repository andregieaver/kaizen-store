# D209: A gallery of pictures and videos

**A gallery of pictures and videos.** A new Gallery component holds up to 60 pictures and videos mixed (a video uploaded, or on YouTube or Vimeo with a picture of the owner's), in the order they are dragged into, each with its alt text (a video's title) and an optional caption.
It is a grid with its columns set per screen size (three, at most three on Medium and two on Small unless set), or the site's own carousel (D155) with as many in view; tiles take one shape (square, 4:3, 3:2, 16:9, upright 3:4, or each its own). A press on a tile opens it full size in a lightbox, the browser's own modal dialog, with the previous and the next by buttons, the arrow keys or a swipe, going round; without a script a tile is a link to the piece itself.
A YouTube or Vimeo video still asks for its play press there, so nothing is fetched from them before a visitor chooses. With the lightbox off, pictures show as they are and videos play in their tiles. A picture with no alt text, or a video with no title, is the accessibility check's missing alt text.
