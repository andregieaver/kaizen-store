# D577: A site's header and footer are checked when they are set, not again when the other changes

**A site's header and footer are checked when they are set, not again when the other changes (12 November 2026, `commerce.site_layout_is_type()`).** Deleting a header and a footer in one statement had the foreign keys set `header_id` and `footer_id` to null one at a time, and the first of those updates re-checked the other column against a page the same statement had removed.
A sandbox's purge (D457) therefore failed every five minutes from 12 November 16:25 UTC (`sandbox.purge_failed`: 'a site's footer must be one of its footers'), and the house site's sync (D143) would have failed the same way had it removed both. The trigger now checks a column on insert and when the statement changes it: an unchanged value was checked when it was set, and a deleted page lets go through the foreign key.
`src/db/commerce.test.ts` deletes both in one statement for a store and for the platform, and `src/db/sandboxes.test.ts` purges a sandbox with both chosen. Migration version: `20261112174722_site_layout_checks_changed_only` (applied by CI's `release` job 12 November 2026 18:22 UTC; production then deployed `aa488c1`, and the stuck sandbox was purged at 18:25).
Advisors after the deploy: security, only the INFO 'RLS enabled, no policy'; performance, only the four INFO notices it gave before, nothing on the function.
