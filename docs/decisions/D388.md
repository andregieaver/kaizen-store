# D388: Theme submissions with their own pages

**Theme submissions with their own pages (wave 11 unit I, `docs/wave-11-core-and-psi.md` I): built.** A look shared as a theme (D344) may carry the author store's published pages as the theme's demo content: *Send my published pages too* on Share this look (unticked by default).
`pagesToSubmit()` (`src/server/submission-content.ts`) takes up to `SUBMISSION_PAGES_MAX` (12) published pages of type page, the front page first, never a page chosen for a place (`page_roles`: legal pages, the cart, the blog and the like, which the content store has its own of), and copies each as a template is copied when it is sent: `sanitizeTemplate()` with the author store as the foreign store (links, ids and files into it taken out), forms without their recipients (`withoutRecipients()`),
and no categories, tags, countries, author's name, blog or thumbnail; they are kept with the submission (`theme_submissions.pages`, fixed with the rest of what was sent by `theme_submissions_rules()`), so the store can change after.
The review places them (`makeSubmittedContent()`): a store template of their own copied from the default template (`clone_store(starter_source(null))`, marked a starter, unpublished, tied to no theme), each page's pictures copied from the author store's library into its own (`copyToLibrary()`, the template limits), checked as a save is (`pageRulesProblem()`, no file left that is not its own) and saved published, the author's front page its front page; a page that does not fit is left out and
named. The submission keeps it (`content_starter_id`, set once and only for a submission with pages); the submission's page shows how many pages came, *Preview on its pages* (the theme's draft over that store, the admin preview's `starter`) and *Open its pages* (`joinStarter()`).
On approval, once the theme is published, `adoptSubmittedContent()` makes it the theme's content (D275), letting go of the content the theme had as `giveThemeContent()` does, and `publishStarter()` publishes it; a refusal of either is said, never fatal. Rejected or withdrawn, it is archived (`archiveSubmittedContent()`). It is never listed among starting content (`listStarters()`).
Products, customers, settings and secrets are never sent: the content store's products are the default template's. **Course changes.** (1) The pages are taken when the look is sent, not at review, so what is reviewed is what was sent. (2) They are tied to the theme only on approval: an update's theme keeps its content and the theme store its preview until then. (3) Pages chosen for a place stay the author's.
`platform.themes-and-templates-marketplace` stays partial on decision 4 alone (installs that followed updates before D344). Migrations `20261029040156_theme_submission_pages` and `20261029040245_theme_submission_pages_rules` (applied by CI, run 726; advisors INFO only).
