# D112: A store's blog, search page and 404 page are pages built in the page builder, like its front page and All products page

**A store's blog, search page and 404 page are pages built in the page builder, like its front page and All products page.** `commerce.page_roles` (store, role, page; `src/lib/page-roles.ts`, `pageForRole()` and `setPageRole()` in `src/server/pages.ts`, `store.pageRoles`) says which of a store's published pages is its *blog* (`/blog`, `PAGE_ROLES` `blog`), its *search page* (`/search`, `search`) or the page shown when an address is not found (`not_found`); the site's standard page shows until
one is chosen or while the chosen one is unpublished, and deleting the page lets the role go (the database cascades). A page has one place: not the front page, the All products page or another role's (the database keeps the store's own pages, one place each).
Owners choose them on the Pages list under the front page and All products forms (`PageRoleForm`), where *Start from a new page* makes a published starter in the store's main language that looks like the standard page (`starterPage()`: a heading and a grid of articles; a heading and the search; a heading, a search box and a button to the store), in place and opened in the builder (`createRolePage()`).
The pages' own addresses lead to their places (the blog and search pages redirect permanently, the 404 page answers 404) and they are left out of the sitemap and llms.txt as the front page is. The 404 page is drawn by the market's `not-found.tsx`, so it is a real 404 with the store's header and footer. New stores get copies of the template's (`clone_store()`).
A new *Search* component (`SearchBlock`, `SearchSection` in `src/components/search-section.tsx`, in the palette of a store's pages and articles) draws the store's search box and, unless its `results` is off, its results as the standard page does (query understanding, meaning search, filters, click and zero-result logging all as before; it reads the address through `GridPlace.listing`, so a page that holds it searches at its own path); with only the box it leads to the search page, which is what
a 404 page or a header wants. Search belongs in a store's pages and articles (`pageRulesProblem()`). Blog categories and tags at `/blog/category|tag/…` keep the standard list.
Not yet, and the places to take next with the same mechanism: category and tag pages (they need a grid that follows the term in the address), the country chooser at `/s/{store}`, the cart, checkout and order confirmation, My account and its sign-in, the wishlist, the subscription and delivery pages, the cookies page, and Kaizen's own site (its front page, blog, sign-up, cookies and 404 are still written in code, and platform pages have no roles).
