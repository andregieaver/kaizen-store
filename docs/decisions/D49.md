# D49: Borders, corners and shadows on every part; heading and button components

**Borders, corners and shadows on every part; heading and button components.** Every settings dialog's Style tab adds a border (none, solid, dashed or dotted; its width on each side, 0–20 px, and colour), rounded corners (0–200 px) and a shadow (none, small, medium, large, extra large). They go on the row, column or block; a button takes them on the button itself, not its full-width box. Rounded corners clip a background picture and a block's content.
Heading: one line of text at H1–H6, with a size apart from its level (small to huge; by default from the level), a weight (semibold unless chosen), alignment by screen and an optional colour. H1 is the page's main heading, once per page: a page with one no longer has the hidden title heading.
Button: text and address (checked like other links; shown once it has both), optionally opening a new tab (announced to screen readers), filled, outline or text link, small/medium/large, rounded/pill/square, full width, position by screen, and optional button and text colours; it stays usable inside a linked column. Components are Rich text, Heading, Image and Button.

Asked for by the owner. A heading's level (structure for search engines and screen readers) and its size (looks) are separate so a page can look right and read right. A button's few words are left out of the page's excerpt. Still no migration: page JSON only.
