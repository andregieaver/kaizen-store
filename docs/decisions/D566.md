# D566: Combined listings

**Combined listings: separate products shown as one, chosen by a shared option (11 November 2026, `docs/combined-listings.md`, `src/server/product-groups.ts`, `groupsFor()` in `src/server/catalog.ts`, `/admin/{store}/products/{id}/listing`).** The owner chose it as the next step ("go", after the list of what was still missing); decision 6 of `docs/wave-6-catalogue.md`, which had left it (Plus only on Shopify, weight 1), is overtaken.
A listing (`product_groups`) names the option its products share by language; each product carries its own value, an optional colour dot and a place (`products.group_*`), in one listing at most. The product page shows, above the buy part, the option and this product's value, then every offered, active product of the listing as a dot or value linking to its own page (this one marked as the page); a listing card shows up to six of them, as links of their own above the card's.
Each product keeps its own page, canonical, price, stock, variants and reviews, so search, feeds and the cart are unchanged. Staff start a listing from a product, add others with their values, change a value, colour or the option's name, or take one out (fewer than two left ends it); `products:write`, audited `products.group_*`. Store-only, as Kaizena Flow sells plans, not products with options.
Not yet: the words in other languages (the main language's are shown), the listing in the product CSV and content grids' own tiles, one card for a whole listing.
