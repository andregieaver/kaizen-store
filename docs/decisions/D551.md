# D551: Drop shipping, step 2

**Drop shipping, step 2: orders reach the supplier, and its tracking reaches the customer (10 November 2026, `docs/drop-shipping.md` 4 and 5, `src/lib/supplier-orders.ts`, `src/server/supplier-orders.ts`, `/supply/{token}`).** The five-minute dispatcher's `supplierOrders` job makes one supplier order (`commerce.supplier_orders` and its lines) per paid order and supplier from the units the payment drew at the supplier's location (`inventory_movements` of reason `sale`, read through the supplier
locations so the scan stays small; three days back, so a job that was down still finds them), laid over the order's lines of each variant (`supplierLines()`). It waits the store's `order_settings.drop_ship_wait_minutes` (30, 0 to 1,440) from the sale; an order no longer paid by then is cancelled and never sent.
Then the supplier gets one email in its language (nb, sv, da, en, from the store's name): the lines with SKUs, the customer's name and delivery address, the store's note, and a link to its page; only the link token's SHA-256 is kept (60 days), and the token is a `secret` of the email so the log never holds it.
The page (`src/app/supply/[token]`, its own root layout, no cookies, no index, `supply` added to `PLATFORM_ROUTES`) shows that order alone and takes two answers, both plain forms: **Mark as sent** (a carrier from a short list, a tracking number, a link for another carrier), recorded by `markSent()` for exactly the supplier order's lines (claimed first, so a second press records nothing) and emailed to the customer by `sendShipped()` as the shop's own shipped email, the order staying partly sent
while the store's own lines are left; and **Can't send it?**, which marks the supplier order `problem` with the supplier's words (kept as data, shown as text) and emails the store's owners once, refunding nothing. The supplier's admin page lists its supplier orders and their states. The address is shown only while there is something to send.
Personal data: the email kind `supplier.order` is a shopper's (blanked with the person's emails); `supplier_orders.problem` is in the register, kept with the order. Not yet: connected suppliers and daily files (they get the email meanwhile, step 3), the wait on a settings screen (the default holds), late reminders and the owner's choices on a problem (step 4). Store-only, as D550. Migration version: `20261110233739_supplier_orders`, applied by CI on 11 November 2026 (run 1193).
