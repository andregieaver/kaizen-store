# D270: A signature theme's demo store is photographed, not drawn

**A signature theme's demo store is photographed, not drawn.** Every picture its pages and products read is a photograph Kaizena Flow's picture model makes from a photographer's brief: `ContentPack.photos` (a `PackPhoto` per slot: the brief, the shape and the alt text) and the pack's `look`, now the shoot's direction (camera, lens, light, set, palette, grade), so each store's pictures look like one shoot; the prompt is `packPhotoPrompt()` (the brief, the look, "a real photograph, not a 3D
render, illustration, painting or digital art" and `PICTURE_RULES`). They are made one a request by the existing *Make the pictures* on the Store templates page (`makeNextPackPicture()`), which now always asks for the model's highest quality for a pack (`generateImage()`'s `quality`, over the settings' own).
The art drawn by code stays: it stands in for each photograph until it is made (so a pack is never empty and tests need no model), and as the themes' textures (paper, scan lines, stars, the sprig). A briefed slot is missing until its photograph is made (`slotMissing()`, `picturesMissing()`), so a signature pack is published only with its photographs, and a later sync never puts its art back over one.
`signatureCheck()` requires a brief for every picture and passes every brief and alt text through `findClaims()`. Each tile's frame is fixed in its theme's CSS (`aspect-ratio`, `object-fit: cover`), as the model makes 2:3, 3:2 and square pictures.

Date: 2026-10-17

The owner: "They have a fake feel to them. The images should look like they were shot with a real professional camera by a professional product photographer", and chose the platform's picture model over supplying photographs. No migration. Production: set up the packs, then press *Make the pictures* on each signature pack (14 photographs each), look at them, and publish the store templates.
