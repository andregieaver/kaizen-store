# D571: The slide-out cart takes the menu's settings

**The slide-out cart takes the menu's settings (11 November 2026, `src/lib/menu-drawer.ts`, `DrawerPanel`, `DrawerBackground`, `CartDrawer`, `@drawer/(.)cart`).** The header's Cart component has the phone menu's three settings (D570): the slide-out cart's type, the edge it comes from (the right unless set) and its background, with frosted glass.
The cart opens as a page of its own (the intercepted `/cart`, also after Add to cart), so no click carries its look: its drawer page reads the store's published header (`headerDrawer()`, cached with the pages) and draws the cart so; a header under an A/B test gives its original's. The menu and the cart share one panel (`DrawerPanel`: place, swipe to close, background, colour set), and the cart stays a little wider from the left or right.
The look's constants live in `menu-drawer.ts`, which takes only types from the page model, so the visitor's scripts still never reach zod (D211). Store-only: Kaizena Flow sells plans, not goods, and has no cart. *Rejected:* a store setting for it (the Cart component is where the owner looks); data attributes on the cart link (Add to cart opens it too). No migration.
