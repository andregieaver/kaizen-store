# D490: A purchase the bank's statement shows, posted with its receipt

**A purchase the bank's statement shows, posted with its receipt (B3 step 1, 7 November 2026, `src/lib/purchases.ts`, `src/server/purchases.ts`, `commerce.ledger_vouchers`, the Bank card on Settings → Books).** The lines left on the Bank card once invoices, payouts and the bank's own fees are matched (D486 to D488) are mostly purchases, and each needs its receipt.
Under such a line (money out, no other offer) the owner opens *Add the receipt*: says what it was in plain words (goods for resale 4300, office supplies 6800, software and subscriptions 6553, computer equipment 6551, phone and internet 6900, advertising 7320, courses and meetings 6860, accounting 6705, travel 7140, meals with clients 7350, rent 6300, postage 6940, insurance 7500), keeps the VAT *as usual for it* or chooses 25, 15, 12 % or none, may put it in their own words, adds the receipt (a
photo or a PDF, up to 3 MB) and presses *Post the purchase*. The VAT is worked out of the bank's amount in code, to the øre; the cost without VAT and the deductible VAT are posted apart with SAF-T's input codes (1 on 2710, 11 on 2711, 13 on 2713) against the bank, in the bank series on the bank's day, once for the line, and the line is matched.
Entertainment never takes VAT back (the VAT act § 8-3), whatever is chosen. *The receipt is the entry's voucher:* its type known by its first bytes (PDF, JPEG, PNG, WebP, HEIC), kept in the private `documents` bucket at `{store}/vouchers/{id}.{ext}` before anything is posted (no place to keep it, nothing posted; a refused entry, its file removed), and in `commerce.ledger_vouchers` (its entry, its bank line, name, type, size, SHA-256, who), never changed or deleted.
The journal links each entry's *Receipt*, served to a member who may read the books (`/admin/{store}/settings/books/vouchers/{id}`: inline, no cache, sandboxed). Owner only, audited (`work.purchase_posted`).
Kaizena Flow's own books are its own store's (D467). *Next (B3 step 2):* the model that sees pictures (D163) reads the receipt into the choice (supplier, total, VAT by rate), a draft the owner confirms; it is a new way outside text reaches a prompt, so it comes with its eval case and a fresh eval run on Platform → AI.
