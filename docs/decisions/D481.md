# D481: The pricing table

**The pricing table: whole amounts, a switch between monthly and yearly, and the AI a plan includes in euros (6 November 2026).** The Plans component (D142) gets two choices. *Whole amounts without decimals* (`PlansBlock.decimals: "hide_whole"`) writes €20, not €20.00, through `formatMoney()`'s D429 style: only the zeros go, an amount with cents keeps them, so no price is rounded. *Visitors switch* (`PlansBlock.switcher`, starting on `interval`, monthly unless yearly) draws the monthly and
the yearly cards both and two native radio buttons above them; `globals.css` (`data-plan-switch`) shows the set chosen by `:has()`, so the prerendered page works before and without a script and adds nothing for visitors to load.
A card leading with its yearly price now says what it saves, and the switch the most any saves (`bestYearlySaving()`: "Save 17 %", or "Save up to 25 %" when the plans differ); each set of cards names its headings apart. **The AI a plan includes is in euros, not dollars.** The models' prices stay in dollars (D145), but the metric `ai_cost_micros` is now millionths of a euro: `aiCostSql()` sums each store's dollars per UTC day and divides by `commerce.usd_per_euro(day)` (the ECB's rate that day,
the working day before for a weekend, the first stored before any, 1 in a database with none), so the daily measuring and the live check before an AI call count alike. The limit `ai_cost_month_micros` keeps its key and its numbers, now in euros (Basic's 5 is €5), typed in EUR on Platform → Plans → Limits; a sandbox's allowance likewise.
Wherever it is shown it follows the currency in use (`AiMoney`, `formatLimit()`/`formatUsage()`): the Plans component's currency at the ECB's latest rate (`getPublicPlans().euroRates`), a store's Billing and its owner's Usage and limits in the currency the store pays Kaizena Flow in (`storeAiMoney()`, as domains are priced, D315), euros elsewhere (the limits editor, emails, the AI manager's tools, the platform's capacity page); a converted limit is rounded down to a whole unit, never promising
more than the plan gives. The migration turns the days already measured into euros at their own day's rate.
Tests: `plans-view.test.ts`, `plan-offer.test.ts`, `usage-format.test.ts`, `src/db/ai-allowance.test.ts`, `usage-meter.int.test.ts`, `public-plans.int.test.ts`. **Led by the month (10 November 2026):** every card, monthly or yearly, leads with a month's price (a yearly price as its twelfth, rounded to the minor unit) and says under it what a year comes to and how it is paid (*NOK 9,588 per year, billed monthly*, *NOK 7,990 per year, billed yearly*); a monthly price's year counts a campaign's
price for as long as it lasts (`cardDisplay()` in `src/lib/plan-offer.ts`), and a campaign's *then* names the real price and its interval. **Shown first as a toggle (10 November 2026):** the builder's *Prices* list has one *Visitors switch between monthly and yearly*, and under it, while chosen, a *Shown first* toggle (Monthly or Yearly) sets `interval`, where the switch starts; the saved block is unchanged.
