# D343: Customs and duties

**Customs and duties (wave 7 unit I, `docs/wave-7-storefront.md` section 4, I); built in part.** Duty rates are `commerce.duty_rates` (per destination, a country or `EU`, and HS heading prefix: thresholds for duty and for VAT at the sale per consignment or item, a fixed duty per sub-heading, the import VAT rate where it differs, whether shipping counts; each with its source, the day read, `verified` and validity), written only by `src/server/duty-rates.ts` by platform admins at
`/admin/platform/duty-rates`, audited. The estimate is only `dutyEstimate()` (`src/lib/duties.ts`, BigInt, half up per line): a missing HS code, rate or exchange rate gives none and says why, never zero. Who pays is the store's choice per country (`commerce.customs_settings`: DAP without a row, DDP with one, and a VOEC tick), decided once for the cart and the order by `decideCustoms()` in `cartSummary()` and `placeOrder()`.
Under DDP the duties are `orders.duties_minor` beside the total, charged as a line of their own with no Kaizena Flow fee and never paid by a gift card, kept out of the invoice, sales and VAT reports; DDP without an estimate is refused (`customs`). The order keeps `orders.customs` and shows it on the order pages, the checkout and the confirmation.
A carrier booking across a border carries customs lines (`BookingRequest.customs`) and is refused without them (`bookingCustomsProblem()`); Bring posts them to its Customs API. Shopper words are `src/lib/customs-text.ts`, hand-written and flagged for legal review. The AI manager reads it (`customs_duties`), never changes it.
