# D332: Email into the support desk

**Email into the support desk (`docs/support.md` step 16).** Resend receives mail for `SUPPORT_INBOUND_DOMAIN`; each desk has `desk-{key}@…` (`support_settings.email_key`, shown on the support settings page to forward the store's mailbox to) and each conversation `reply-{key}@…` (`support_conversations.reply_key`), the Reply-To of every answer email.
The signed `/api/resend/webhook` keeps `email.received` as a row of `commerce.support_inbound_emails` (once per Resend id) and processes it after the answer and every minute (`processInboundEmails()`, three tries): the words are fetched from Resend, automatic mail, failing DMARC, a desk that is off, a store that is not open, more than 10 an hour from one sender or 500 a day to a desk and an empty email are refused; the quoted history and signature are cut.
A reply from the conversation's own address continues (or reopens within a day) its conversation; anything else starts a conversation of source `email`, never tied to a customer. The AI follows the desk's mode (the owner's choice): with the AI first it answers by email with no tool that needs a signed-in customer or a button and hands anything about an order to a person; with people only it never writes.
Answers go back as `Re: {subject}` with threading headers once settled 30 seconds, 60 a conversation. Rows pruned after 30 days; erasure removes the sender's.
