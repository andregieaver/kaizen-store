# D141: Every store numbers its orders in one sequence, without gaps

**Every store numbers its orders in one sequence, without gaps** (some countries require it). Each store has an `order` series from 1001 (`commerce.document_series`, made with the store); `placeOrder()` and the subscription renewal take the number inside the transaction that inserts the order, so an order that is rolled back gives its number back.
The database now holds the rest: an order keeps its number and is never deleted while its store exists (a cancelled order stays, cancelled); the order, invoice and credit note series keep their prefix, move one at a time, and are not deleted once a document is issued in them; a store with no orders yet may still choose where its numbers start. Orders copied from another store (D129, `C-…`) are history outside the sequence.
`commerce.order_number_audit(store)` checks a store's sequence (gaps, numbers not from the series, a series out of step), `orderNumberAudit()` reads it, and the AI manager's `store_checkup` reports a broken sequence. Production's four stores were checked when this shipped: all in order (the demo store 1001–1064).
