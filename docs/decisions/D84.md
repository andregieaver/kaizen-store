# D84: The product page's gallery shows the chosen variant's picture

**The product page's gallery shows the chosen variant's picture (D82).** Choosing a variant, in the dropdown, the phone's bar or an appointment's or rental's options, moves the gallery to the variant's picture where it is one of the product's; a picture of the variant's own takes the gallery's first place until another variant is chosen, so the count of pictures and their words stay the same. The variant chosen first is shown as soon as scripts run.
A product without pictures of its own shows its variants' pictures. The pickers and the gallery are separate parts of the product's layout (D79), so they meet through a window event per product (`src/components/variant-picture.ts`: `showVariantPicture()`, `onVariantPicture()`, which also gives the latest to a gallery that starts later).

Date: 2026-09-28

Asked for by the owner. An event keeps product layouts free to put the gallery and the buy part anywhere; replacing the first picture rather than adding one keeps the prerendered gallery and its labels valid.
