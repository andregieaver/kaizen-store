# D622: A store's rows are filtered the way its index can serve

**A store's rows are filtered the way its index can serve (bloat review step 0.23; 15 November 2026).** `owned(storeId, column?)` (`src/server/owned.ts`) is the one way to filter a store's or the platform's rows: `store_id = $1::uuid` for a store and `store_id is null` for Kaizena Flow's own, since Postgres answers `is not distinct from` from no btree index and so read every store's rows on pages, cookie_scans and terms. 125 uses in 40 files moved to it; 9 stay, each commented as column to
column (a statement over every store at once, or one row picked by id with no owner in code). The ratchet (`ratchet.scan.test.ts`) counts the old wording per file and only lets it fall; the rule is in CLAUDE.md's conventions.
The cookie scan's due check (`claimScan()`) reads a site's newest scan and newest finished scan through two indexes on `coalesce(store_id, zero uuid)` (`cookie_scans_site_created_idx`, `cookie_scans_site_done_idx`); a failing site retries after an hour and after a day from its third failure in a row, and the site tried longest ago goes first, so one failing site cannot hold the queue. The platform's rows are covered by the same helper (null).
