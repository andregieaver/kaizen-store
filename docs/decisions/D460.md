# D460: Fiken

**Fiken: a store's books follow it, through Fiken's own consent page, on Tripletex's engine (5 November 2026, `docs/fiken.md`, `src/lib/fiken*.ts`, `src/server/fiken.ts`, `src/server/fiken-client.ts`, `src/server/fiken-sync.ts`, `src/lib/accounting-docs.ts`, `src/server/accounting-documents.ts`).** The second accounting connection, chosen first because Fiken is where small Norwegian businesses keep their own books. **Shared with Tripletex** (D453): the documents read into one shape
(`BookingDoc`, now with each line's net and VAT), the buckets, the document loader and the buckets a store used, the daily alert (worded for either system) and the checkup (`accountingAttention()`); Tripletex's modules re-export what moved, so its behaviour is unchanged. **Fiken's own**: Fiken lets a third party in only through OAuth2 (its terms forbid personal API tokens in one), so *Connect Fiken* goes to Fiken's consent page with a signed 15-minute state cookie and a PKCE verifier, and the
callback (`/api/integrations/fiken/callback`) checks both and the owner signed in now before the code is spent; the grant is kept encrypted, refreshed under the row's lock, revoked on Disconnect, and a refresh Fiken refuses stops the connection (`auth_failed`) until the owner connects again, which goes on where it stopped. A grant may open several companies: one is chosen at once, several are offered; one company belongs to one store; a company without Fiken's API module is refused.
A document is booked as **Annet salg** (`external_invoice`), Fiken's own way in for invoices numbered elsewhere: the receivable on the customer (Fiken's ledger, so no receivable account to choose), each line's account, VAT type and the net and VAT the document charged, in øre.
Its **sale number is the document's own number**, looked up before every send: the same sale is adopted (a lost answer, or the owner's own booking by hand), another with the number waits for a person (`number_taken`), so nothing is booked twice. A credit note is a sale with its lines negative on the same customer (to be confirmed in a Fiken test company, `docs/fiken.md` 6).
Customers as Tripletex's: a Work client a contact of its own, a Norwegian business that bought that business, every shopper one contact per store. Calls go one at a time, a quarter of a second apart (Fiken's limits). **One system per store**: Fiken is refused while Tripletex is connected and the other way round.
The page (`/integrations/fiken`) is Homer's: Connect, allow in Fiken, Start; Lisa's accounts and VAT types are under Customise; its words are a server-only area (`fiken`), out of every admin screen's first scripts. Offered once Kaizena Flow's app at Fiken is set up (`FIKEN_CLIENT_ID`, `FIKEN_CLIENT_SECRET`; `docs/fiken.md` 5).
