# D482: Drop shipping

**Drop shipping: a supplier ships it for you (proposed 5 November 2026, `docs/drop-shipping.md`; step 1 built as D550, which corrects the model below).** A supplier (`commerce.suppliers`: name, order email, the country it ships from, language, dispatch days, return address, and how orders reach it: email, a connected system, or a file) is a stock location of a new kind (`inventory_locations.kind` `supplier`), so routing (`allocate()`), holds at checkout, the stock history and the warehouse
connection of D355 work unchanged; a variant is sent *always from the supplier* or *my stock first*.
A paid order's lines at a supplier make one supplier order per supplier, held for the store's wait before sending (30 minutes unless changed, 0 to 24 hours) and then emailed with the packing slip and a signed page where the supplier marks it sent with tracking (no account), or offered to the supplier's own token; tracking goes through `markSent()` and `sendShipped()`, so the customer gets the shop's own shipped email, and an order of own stock and supplier goods goes as two parcels.
A late supplier is reminded once and the owner told; nothing is refunded by itself. The shop stays the seller (VAT, withdrawal, refunds, GPSR as D452); suppliers outside the EU are allowed, with customs and IOSS as D343 and D157 and a longer delivery estimate. Paying suppliers, drop-ship networks (later as apps, D441) and print on demand are not in the first version. The owner chose the three recommended answers on 5 November 2026; the `/selling-drop-shipping` page says it is coming.
