# D899: A found company's door from its whole page and its contact page (email plan P5.2)

Date: 2026-11-25

**Where companies put their address (25 November 2026, the owner: "continue").** Reading a found company's site (D894) looked for its
general address in the page's readable text, which `pageText()` makes without footers, navigation or links. A company's `post@` is usually in
the footer or behind a *Write to us* link, so it was rarely found.

- `pageEmails()` (`src/lib/web-page-text.ts`) reads every address a page shows, footers and `mailto:` links included, entities and
  percent-encoding decoded, folded, at most 30. `readWebPage()` returns them beside the text as `emails`, **for code alone**:
  `readWebPageTool()` lists the fields it gives the AI manager, and `emails` is not one, so no model sees a page's addresses from it.
- `readCompanySite()` chooses from them with `generalAddresses()` as before: a role name on the company's own domain, never a named person's.
- When the front page has none, it reads the company's contact page at the address sites in its country use most (`CONTACT_PATHS`: Norway's
  `/kontakt` then `/contact`, Finland's `/yhteystiedot` then `/contact`), at most two, each a page of the store's day's limit. A page that is
  not there is passed over. Contact forms and phone numbers are still not read.

Idle cost: nothing; at most two more page reads when a front page shows no general address.
