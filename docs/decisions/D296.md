# D296: Segments and customer tags

**Segments and customer tags (wave 5 unit B, `docs/wave-5-marketing.md` 4).** A store's *people* are its email addresses as the Customers page counts them plus its subscriber list, read by one reader, `segmentPeople()` (`src/server/segments.ts`): the account (verified, language, customer group, company), D152's paid orders (count, first and last, the last one's country, net revenue without VAT per currency, never copied, host or erased people's orders), the subscriber's consent and market, and
tags; money is converted to the main currency in code and is *unknown* (matching no money condition, counted on the page) when a currency has no rate; the RFM group is D152's `rfm()` over the people with a paid order, offered only from `RFM_MIN_CUSTOMERS`.
A segment (`commerce.segments`: a title and rules, at most 100 a store, names unique) is never a stored list: its rules (`{ match: all|any, conditions }`, at most 20 from a closed list: consent, orders, spent, last and first order, RFM group, country, language, customer group, company, account, tag; `segmentRulesSchema`) are matched in code by `matches()` and counted each time it is read, with how many want the store's emails.
Marketing → Segments lists them with their counts, *New segment* starts from ready sets (`SEGMENT_STARTERS`) or from everyone and counts as the rules change, and a segment's page shows the first 100 people by last order to staff who may read customers. Staff tag people on their customer page (`commerce.customer_tags`, by address so a guest can be tagged, D173's `normaliseTag()`, 50 a person, audited with the words, never the address); tags are in the person's export and erased with them.
Saving and deleting are `marketing:write`, audited with the name and the number of conditions. Not built: bulk tagging, a tag filter on the Customers list, a segment's CSV, the AI manager writing rules, and segments as an audience (units C to E).
