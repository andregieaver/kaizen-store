# D380: Segments everywhere

**Segments everywhere (wave 11 unit A, `docs/wave-11-core-and-psi.md` 2.A); built.** A segment's customer types are the Customers analytics page's own: `rfmGroups()` (`src/server/analytics-customers-data.ts`) reads the same paid orders, customer key and cap, rounds per customer, month and currency and converts as `fold()` does, and `segmentPeople()` takes its groups from it, so a segment of one type counts exactly the page's (held for every type by `segment-audiences.int.test.ts`; an erased
person's order is its own anonymous customer there and never a person here).
A code (`discount_codes.segment_id`) or a campaign (`campaigns.segment_id`) may be limited to a segment of the store's (a composite foreign key, refused for another store's, and the segment cannot be deleted while one names it: `segmentUses()`, `deleteSegment()` answers `in_use`, the database refuses too); whether the signed-in buyer is in it is only `segmentsOfBuyer()` (`src/server/segment-membership.ts`: the account's own address through `segmentPeople()` for that address alone, `matches()`
now, never a stored list, the store read past the page cache), asked by `findUsableDiscount()` and `runningCampaigns()`, so the cart and `placeOrder()` agree (a kroner and a euro case in `checkout-kinds.int.test.ts`); a segment is any of a code's three ways in (groups, named customers, segment); a guest is told to sign in and an outsider is `not_eligible`; a segment's campaigns stay out of the public notices like a group's; a store's copy switches a code or campaign limited to a segment off
(segments are never copied). Staff with `customers:write` add or take off a tag for everyone the Customers list's search and tag find (`tagMany()`, at most `BULK_TAG_MAX` 1,000, one transaction, a person at 50 tags left as they were, one audit entry with counts).
A segment's people and each customer type (`/segments/rfm/{type}`, linked from every type on the analytics page for staff who may read segments and customers) are listed a page at a time (100) with orders, last order and money spent (none, never 0, where a currency has no rate), with *Save as a segment* and the owner's CSV (`segmentCsv()`, `/segments/csv`, POST, same site, audited with counts).
The AI manager's `count_segment` (read) and `save_segment` (`marketing:write`, no gate: it changes nothing a shopper sees) take rules in the editor's own schema, counted by the store, never naming who.
