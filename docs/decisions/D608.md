# D608: The challenge's people

**The challenge's people: the refund queue, escalations, the intervention log and the stall watch (`docs/100-day-challenge.md` A3 to A6; 14 November 2026, `src/lib/interventions.ts`, `src/server/interventions.ts`, `src/lib/challenge-stalls.ts`, `src/server/challenge-watch.ts`, `sendDecisionPush()`, migration `20261114202420_overrated_whirlwind`).** **A3:** a store's owner now sees on the Autopilot page and decides every call the autopilot kept in the store (a call in an autopilot conversation,
`source = 'autopilot'`), whoever its plan, review or event ran for, so the challenge's refunds reach them; anyone else still decides only their own (`decideApproval()`, `autopilotOverview()`, by `can(…, 'owner')`).
Each such call is pushed to the owners' phones (*Waiting for your yes*, `sendDecisionPush()`, a push device kept for everything else when its person is not an owner). **A4:** `handoff_to_human` takes a category (`ESCALATION_CATEGORIES`: asked for a person, upset, legal, safety, chargeback, privacy, abuse, could not help), kept as `support_conversations.escalation`; `escalationOf()` reads a conversation's (a customer's own press is *asked for a person*, a low tone *upset*); an AI that was off,
failed or reached its limit is no escalation but a fault. **B4 changed course:** the waiting customer is not answered by the autopilot in the challenge, since `reply_to_support` writes in a person's name and handing back to the AI needs a turn per channel; a fault is prevented (the challenge's limits, F1) and paged at once instead (A6), and escalations stay the owner's. **A5:** `interventionsOf(store, from, to)` classes what people did: a refund decided or made by hand (`refund`), an answer on
an escalated conversation (`escalation`), a platform admin's action (`platform`), and everything else, listed one by one (`other`, which the challenge allows none of). **A6:** the dispatcher's `challengeStalls` job emails the platform admins, once a day while it lasts, of each challenge store's stall: the owner's pause, the AI budget, a plan paused for a reason the budget does not lift, a step asking, a refund waiting on the owner over 24 hours, a customer waiting after a fault over 15 minutes,
an escalation waiting over four hours (`platform.challenge_stall`). Tests: `challenge-queues.test.ts`, `challenge-queues.int.test.ts`. The handoff tool changed, so the agents' eval is recorded again with D606's. Not yet: the intervention log and the stalls on a screen (the challenge console, H2).
