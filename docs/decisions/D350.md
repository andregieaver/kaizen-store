# D350: The AI manager tells and remembers

**The AI manager tells and remembers (wave 8 unit F, `docs/wave-8-analytics-ai.md` section 4, F); built.** *Notices*: `commerce.assistant_notices` (account, store or null for Kaizena Flow's own, kind of `NOTICE_KINDS`: `page_built`, `follow_up`, `plan_question`, `plan_done`, `approval_waiting`, `report_ready`, a reference, a few facts, an admin path of the person's own place, `read_at`), written only by `src/server/assistant-notices.ts`, once per account, kind and reference (`addNotice()`, never
throwing): a page build finished or given up (`runPageJobs()`), a follow-up answered or failed (`runFollowUps()`), a gated call kept for a yes in a scheduled or autopilot turn, an autopilot step or review or event, or from Kaizen Life (`noticeApproval()`), the autopilot's questions and finished plans swept after each run of its loop (`noticeAutopilot()`), and its digest and weekly report (`report_ready`).
A count shows on the AI manager's launcher (its place's) and in the account menu (every place), asked every minute while the tab is shown and when it is shown again (polling, `useNoticeCount()`); the panel's *Notices* lists them and each opens its conversation or page, marked read as it opens; `/admin/account/notices` lists every place's. Read and marked only by the person (`/admin/account/notices/feed`, same site for marking). Kept 60 days (`pruneNotices()`, daily).
The emails the jobs send are unchanged.
Personal data register: a staff member's own (`staff`, no export section), `never` copied. *Saved skills*: `commerce.assistant_skills` (account, store or null, key from the title, title, when to use it, steps of at most 2,000 characters, `shared`), written only by `src/server/assistant-saved-skills.ts`, at most 30 a person and place, after `skillProblem()` (`src/lib/saved-skills.ts`: never a built-in playbook's key, never an email address, a long number or a password (`PERSONAL_OR_SECRET`, the
memory's rule), never a green, urgency or best-price claim (`findClaims()`; prices and stock may be named, a store's work is about them)); an owner may share one with the store (the database refuses sharing without a store) and may take a colleague's shared one off; audited `ai.skill_saved`, `ai.skill_changed`, `ai.skill_deleted` by key, never the words.
The prompt lists them after the built-in ones (`savedSkillsText()`), `use_skill` loads one after the built-ins as words only (the turn's tools and gates unchanged), and the manager tools `save_skill` and `delete_skill` are ungated, the person's own. The AI manager's page has a Skills tab (list, edit, delete, share for owners, the built-in playbooks listed) and every request in a conversation has *Save as skill*.
