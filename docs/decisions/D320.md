# D320: Variants at scale

**Variants at scale (wave 6 unit B, `docs/wave-6-catalogue.md` Unit B); B1 to B3 built: 2,048 variants, one save in a few statements, reads that do not grow per variant, a grid in the editor and a list per option in the shop.** A product has at most 2,048 variants over 3 options (`MAX_VARIANTS`, as Shopify), read by `productInput`, the CSV import and the AI manager's tools, and held by the database at commit (`commerce.product_variants_limit()`, a deferred constraint trigger counting variants
for sale once per product and transaction; switched-off variants are history and do not count), its refusal in the owner's words. `saveVariants()` writes variants, prices (`commerce.set_price` over a list) and stock (`writeStock()`'s rules, one statement per reason, levels locked in variant order) in a fixed number of statements: 50 for a new product whether it has 2 variants or 2,048, where the old save sent 22,556 for 2,048.
`variants-scale.int.test.ts` holds the save to a time budget and the same statement count at both sizes, and the product page, its stock, a card, the listing, search, adding to the cart, the cart's summary and the feed to the same statements at 2 and 2,048 variants.

B2 and B3 (2026-10-26, no migration): the editor draws only the variant rows on screen from 13 variants (`useVirtualRows()`), filters them by an option's value and sets price, stock, a SKU pattern, weight and for sale for all shown (`setForShown()`); the shop offers a list per option from a number of variants the buy part sets (`optionsFrom`, 12 unless set) and marks the values that make no buyable variant with the others chosen (`buyableValues()`), each distinct price drawn once.
