# D896: Find companies in Finland too, from PRH's open register (email plan P5.1)

Date: 2026-11-25

**Finland beside Norway (25 November 2026, the owner: "continue").** P5.1 names Norway's, Denmark's and Finland's registers first. Finland's
PRH serves its open data without a key or agreement (`avoindata.prh.fi/opendata-ytj-api/v3/companies`); Denmark's CVR needs an access
agreement with Erhvervsstyrelsen first, so it waits.

- **A country in the filters** (`ProspectFilters.country`, `NO` unless asked): the model chooses Finland when the owner names it or a
  Finnish place, and Customise has a country to pick.
- **The same industries.** Norway's SN2025 and Finland's TOL 2025 share the 2025 EU classification's divisions and groups, so the bundled
  list serves both (`56.1` is PRH's `561`).
- **Finland's places** are Statistics Finland's 308 municipalities of 2026, added to `src/data/no-register-codes.json`, named in Finnish.
- **What PRH cannot do, said plainly.** It takes one industry and one town a search, has no headcount (size filters are dropped for
  Finland), and ignores its own company-form filter, so code reads each company (`readFiCompany()`): only limited companies, public limited
  companies, partnerships, cooperatives, foreign branches and European companies (`FI_FORMS`); none ceased (`endDate`, a trade-register
  status other than registered) and none in liquidation or bankrupt (an open `companySituations` entry). Its total counts the ones left
  out, which the page says. PRH answers 100 a page; a page of ours is a fifth of one.
- A Finnish company links to its page on `tietopalvelu.ytj.fi`, its site can be read (D894), and the do-not-contact list (D893) applies
  as in Norway.

Idle cost: nothing; one request to PRH when a Finnish search is opened.
