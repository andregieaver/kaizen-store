# D401: Loyalty rewards and a birthday credit

**Loyalty rewards and a birthday credit (wave 13 unit A, `docs/wave-13-loyalty-rules-einvoices.md` A, `docs/bonus.md` "Rewards and the birthday credit"): built.** A store's rewards (`commerce.bonus_rewards`, written only by `src/server/bonus-rewards.ts`, at most 10) are free shipping, one unit of a product free or an amount off per country, each owning one hidden discount code (`discount_codes.reward`, `max_units` for the one unit, the dearest first in `applyDiscount()`), so a reward takes off a
basket only what the code engine says, in `cartSummary()` and `placeOrder()` alike (`checkout-kinds.int.test.ts`, kroner and euro). A signed-in customer redeems one on My account: `commerce.bonus_redeem_reward()` takes the cost exactly (ledger kind `reward`) and makes a generated code for them alone in the same transaction (`discount_code_items.customer_id`, `ends_at`; `availability()` refuses another customer, a guest and an ended code).
The birthday credit (`bonus_settings.birthday_minor`) is given once a year on the store's own date by `commerce.bonus_birthday_grants()` from the bonus jobs, to a proven email whose birthday (month and day only, `customers.birthday`) was given at least 30 days before. Not built, by the house rule: credits for a review or a newsletter consent.
Reward codes are never copied with a store (`duplicate_store()`), the birthday credit is (with the settings); the birthday is in the export's profile and the register, a redeemed code's tie to the customer is cut with the account. `customers.loyalty-and-rewards` stays partial: its second criterion names points for a newsletter confirmation and a review, which the rules refuse (proposed criteria change in the row).
