# D191: A heading or text is edited where it stands in the page builder

**A heading or text is edited where it stands in the page builder.** Pressing a heading or a rich text once on the canvas puts the caret in it and lets it be typed in, as the site will draw it: a heading as its own element (plain text, its inline markup as typed, one line), a text in the panel's editor with its formatting bar floating over it. Each change goes into the page at once; Escape puts the words back, pressing elsewhere ends it.
A double press and the wrench still open the settings (and end the in-place editing); blocks bound to a custom field and the translating view keep to the settings.

Date: 2026-10-10

Asked for by the owner. No migration. Checked in a real browser: typing, Enter, Escape, bold, the bar and pressing elsewhere.
