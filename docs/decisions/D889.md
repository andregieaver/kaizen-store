# D889: The AI manager writes and changes flows from a sentence (email plan M3.2)

Date: 2026-11-24

**A flow from a sentence (24 November 2026, email plan M3.2).** The owner could start a flow from a goal or build one on the canvas, but not
say it to the AI manager.

- `create_flow` takes the model's draft of the owner's words: what starts it (any flow trigger, with its days, segment or time) and up to six
  emails, each the days after the one before and, if asked, only for those who have or have not ordered or pressed a link. Code, never the
  model, builds the steps (`draftSteps()`, `src/lib/flow-draft.ts`), checks every word for claims and the brand's words to avoid, finds the
  segment and the time on the store's clock, and saves the flow switched off and untested. The answer reads it back as a story with who it
  would reach and the contact policy that holds it.
- `change_flow` changes one by its name: its name, what starts it, or all its emails, as a new version off until tested.
- Switching a flow on stays the owner's press on its page; no tool does it. Three eval cases (a sentence, a flow's name and a form answer that
  ask for changes) were held with the tools and released with them; the eval is run on Platform → AI after the release.

Idle cost: none until the owner asks; the manager's round carries the two tools' descriptions (about 4,600 characters).
