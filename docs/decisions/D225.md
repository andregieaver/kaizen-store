# D225: Signed-in admin pages are titled by `PageHeader`, and section cards are `Card`

**Signed-in admin pages are titled by `PageHeader`, and section cards are `Card` (`docs/admin-style.md` section 8, step 9).** `PageHeader` gained `back` (a chevron link to the page this one belongs under, in place of the hand-made back links above titles) and `description` (a sentence or two in ink-2 at a 75-character reading measure, which keeps the rule that admin pages use their whole width).
A codemod moved 178 title blocks (a title, an optional back link, an optional single description) and 49 section cards whose first child was their own `h2` (`compact` keeps their padding); each moved only where the block held exactly those pieces and the card's id was used nowhere else. Descriptions kept their JSX, line breaks joined by JSX's own whitespace rule (rebuilt from the previous source after a first pass put spaces next to links).
Thirteen detail pages were moved by hand: badges to `status`, a customer's or design profile's facts to `meta`, Work's buttons to `actions`, an avatar or a code in the title. `admin-type.scan.test.ts` refuses a signed-in page's own `h1`, except compact tools and notices drawn so on purpose and screen-reader titles. Left for later: 35 cards with an action beside their title, and the signed-out pages until the `focus` layout. No migration.
