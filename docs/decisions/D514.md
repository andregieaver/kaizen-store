# D514: The threshold watch

**The threshold watch (section 4.4 of `docs/own-books-and-sales.md`, 9 November 2026, `commerce.turnover_thresholds`, `src/lib/turnover-watch.ts`, `src/server/turnover-watch.ts`, `TurnoverWatchCard`).** The owner: *start with 1 and 2* (the threshold watch, then C4).
A store's turnover over the last twelve months, read from its own books (what the income accounts, class 3, were credited net of what was debited back; the year-end's closing entry never touches them), against the thresholds that change its duties. **The law's** come with the country pack, Norway's in kroner: NOK 50 000 (VAT registration, and the end of the small business exemptions) and, for a limited company only (the books' legal name ends in AS or ASA, as the legal form is not kept
otherwise), NOK 7 million (an AS may skip the audit only below it). **The owner's own** are an amount and what they will do before it (Human Web's NOK 5 million: take an accountant), added and taken away on Settings → Books by a member who keeps the books (`books:write`), audited. **Said** from 85 % of a threshold (*near*) and once it is passed: a legal one only in the month after (a business long past VAT registration is not told so again), an owner's own until they take it away; only
thresholds in the books' currency, and nothing for a store without open books in a country with a pack.
It is said on the store home's attention list (to members who may read the books, in their language), in the store checkup (one finding, `turnover`, so the AI manager's checkup and the staff briefing carry it) and on a card on Settings → Books with the turnover, the law's thresholds and the owner's own. **The platform** keeps its money in the books of the store it names (D508), so that store's watch is the platform's. **Not yet:** an ENK's size limits for annual accounts and audit (their
numbers not yet read from the act, section 4.4), and other countries' packs.
