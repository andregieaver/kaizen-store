# D301: Product reviews

**Product reviews (wave 5 unit G, `docs/wave-5-marketing.md` 9), built in four steps; G1 the model and writing, G2 checking and showing.** A review is a buyer's own stars (1 to 5) and words about a product they bought and received in the store, so every written review is a verified purchase by construction; an imported one is marked imported and never verified.
`commerce.product_reviews` (store, product, variant, `source` `buyer` with its order, order line and `buyer_key` (`c:{customer}`, or for G3's guests `e:` and the hash of the order's email), or `import` with where and when it was written; the name shown, stars, title, text, language, `status` `pending`, `published` or `hidden` with `hidden_reason` (`not_about_product`, `offensive`, `personal_data`, `spam`, `other`), the store's `answer`); one review per order line and one per product and buyer.
The database's `product_reviews_rules()`: a buyer's line must be of that order and of a variant of that product; who and what it is about are fixed; the stars and words change only inside the author's write (`kaizen.review_author`), which only `src/server/reviews.ts` sets, so staff can never change a word or a star; the state moves `pending → published | hidden`, `published ↔ hidden`, and back to `pending` only in the author's write; a review is deleted only where `kaizen.review_delete` is
set (the author, erasure) or by its product's or customer's own deletion.
What can be reviewed is only `reviewableLines()`: a sold order of the signed-in customer's (D298's `SOLD`), a variant of a product that is not a gift card, received (goods when the order has `delivered_at`, a download once paid, a service when its booking ended, confirmed and not a no-show) within 365 days; the newest receipt of each product. *Reviews* in My account (`/account/reviews`, *Write a review* beside each such line of an order) writes and edits through `writeReview()` (checked again
under the line's lock, 20 writes a customer a day in `chat_usage`, words cleaned by `cleanReview()`, refused not cut) and deletes with `deleteOwnReview()`. The store's `review_settings.publish`: `after_check` (default, a new or edited review waits) or `at_once` (published when written; an edit of a shown one stays shown, a hidden one is checked again).
Feature `reviews` (Customers, needs the shop, off by default; the Features page counts twelve). **G2:** Products → Reviews (`/admin/{store}/reviews`, `products:read`; publish, hide with a reason (*Hiding a review because it is negative is not allowed*), answer and the setting `products:write`; `src/server/reviews-admin.ts`; audit `review.published`, `review.hidden`, `review.answered`, `review.settings`, area products) with the queue first.
The product parts `rating` (stars, average to one decimal, the count, linking down) and `reviews` (average, how many gave each number of stars, the newest ten with *Show more* from `/api/reviews`, *How reviews are checked*) draw only with a published review (`productPartShows()`), from the cached `reviewFacts()` (tag `reviews:{store}`, refreshed by every decision, by an author's write that changes what is shown and by erasure); both are in the default layout and the builder's palette while the
feature is on. The product's JSON-LD carries `aggregateRating` and the newest five `review`s only with a published review. Review words are text in their own language, never HTML and never translated. Personal data: `product_reviews` (export section `reviews`, erasure deletes); `never` copied, `review_settings` copied as settings.
Shopper words are `m.reviews` (nb, sv, da, en by hand; the account intro and *How reviews are checked* `HAND_WRITTEN_ONLY`), flagged for legal review (Directive 2005/29/EC Art. 7(6), Annex I 23b and 23c). Not yet: request emails, imports, the AI manager's tool and the control center (G3), pictures (G4), stars on product cards. **G3a (24 October):** review request emails.
Products → Reviews gains *Ask buyers for a review* (`requests_on`, `request_days` 3 to 60, `saveRequestSettings()`; switching on sets `requests_enabled_at`, staying on keeps it, `products:write`, audit `review.settings`).
`runReviewRequests()` (`src/server/review-requests.ts`, the every-minute marketing job after the automations) asks each sold order (D298's `SOLD`, not a gift card, never a copy) once, when its newest received reviewable line was received the days ago, after the switch and within D298's `CATCH_UP_DAYS`, with something still unreviewed by the buyer, **only when the order's address is `subscribed`** (asking for a review is marketing, BGH VI ZR 225/17): one `commerce.review_requests` row per order
(claimed before the email, the hash of a 32-byte token, 90 days, the outcome; one table where the spec named two, `review_invitations` folded in), then the email through `marketingEmail()` (the subscriber's unsubscribe link and `List-Unsubscribe` headers, the order's language and market, kind `marketing.review_request`, counted in `MARKETING_EMAIL_KINDS` against the plan's month), naming up to five products.
Its link `/account/review/{token}` (a no-extras path, `isReviewLinkPath()`) lists the order's reviewable lines and writes them without signing in through `writeReviewAs()` with an `invitation` `Reviewer` (`reviewableLinesFor()` for that order only; the buyer key the order's account's, else `e:` the hash of the order's address, as erasure finds it); an unknown, other store's or spent link says the same.
Rows go on the buyer's erasure and after 400 days (`pruneReviewRequests()`, daily); personal-data register `review_requests` (export section emails), `never` copied. *How reviews are checked* now says buyers review from their account or the store's emailed link (four languages). Words `m.reviews.request` (nb, sv, da, en by hand).
Migrations `20261024155112_review_requests`, `20261024155140_review_requests_rls` (applied by CI run 40112083083, 24 October 2026; the table with RLS confirmed in production; advisors: no new warning, INFO only). **G3b (24 October):** reviews in and out, the AI manager's tool and the control center. *Import reviews* (`/admin/{store}/reviews/import`, `products:write`, the feature on) reads a CSV with `product_handle`, `rating`, `author`, `date` and optionally `title`, `body`, `source` (Shopify's
and Judge.me's exports as they are) by `readReviewFile()` (`src/lib/review-import.ts`, pure: the words cleaned as a buyer's, a formula escape undone, dates ISO or day.month.year, never in the future or before 2000, 10,000 rows and 3.5 MB at most, a row refused with its line and column and never a quoted cell) and `importReviews()` (`src/server/review-import.ts`): only the store's own products by handle (not gift cards), each an `import` review (`imported_from` the row's source or the form's,
`written_on` its date, never verified, no variant), published or waiting as chosen, in one transaction with the database's `import` writer; *Only check the file* (on by default) writes nothing; a review already imported (same product, stars, author, date, source and words) is left as it is, so a file imported twice adds nothing; audit `review.imported` with counts.
Not a D165 job, unlike the spec: a review file is small, has no pictures to fetch and is read in one request, as the 404 report's and analytics' files are. *Download as CSV* (POST `/admin/{store}/reviews/export`, `products:read`, same site) writes the published and waiting reviews through `writeCsv()` with whether each is verified; hidden ones stay out.
The AI manager's `reviews_overview` (`products:read`, feature `reviews`): counts by state, how many imported, the average and the best and worst ten products with at least three published reviews, averaged in code, never an author or a review's words. The control center says *n reviews are waiting to be checked* (`reviewsWaiting`, for members who may read products, with the feature on).
The admin's words for reviews are now plain templates and `{ one, many }` pairs (no functions), as the catalogues are. **G4 (26 October):** pictures in reviews.
A buyer adds up to three to their review in its form: each made smaller in the browser by D213's `compressPicture()` without AVIF (1600 px, tried again at 1280 and 1024 px while over 600 KB, and a 480 px copy), sent with the review and checked again on the server from its bytes by `checkPicture()` (`src/lib/review-pictures.ts`: WebP, or JPEG from a browser that cannot write WebP, the two of one kind, 600 KB and 150 KB, 1600 and 480 px).
`commerce.review_pictures` (review, place 0 to 2, kind, size, bytes, `hidden_at`/`hidden_by`; the database's `review_pictures_rules()`: added and taken out only inside the review author's write, on a buyer's review, never hidden from the start, otherwise only hidden or shown, gone with the review).
`writeReviewAs()` checks them before anything is written, keeps or takes out the review's own (`keep`) and adds the new ones at the free places, uploading their files inside the transaction (a refused upload undoes the whole write); a store without the bucket takes none.
Files are in the private bucket `review-pictures` named by the picture's id (`src/server/review-picture-store.ts`); `/api/reviews/picture/{id}` serves one (or `?size=480`) while its review is published and it is not hidden (cached five minutes) and to its signed-in author, never cached; staff see every picture through `/admin/{store}/reviews/picture/{id}` (`products:read`) and hide or show one alone (`setPictureHidden()`, `products:write`, audit `review.picture_hidden`/`review.picture_shown`).
The product page draws the shown pictures under each review as small copies that open the picture. The files go after the rows: an edit that takes one out, the author's delete and erasure remove them; the daily job sweeps files no row names (older than an hour). Personal data `review_pictures` and `storage:review-pictures` (export section `reviews`, each picture by its address), `never` copied. Words `m.reviews.picture*` (nb, sv, da, en by hand).
