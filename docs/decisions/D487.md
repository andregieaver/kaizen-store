# D487: A client's payment on the bank statement pays the invoice it names, a Work invoice or a shop's order on terms

**A client's payment on the bank statement pays the invoice it names, a Work invoice or a shop's order on terms (B4 route 1, step 2, 7 November 2026, `src/lib/bank-invoices.ts`, `src/server/bank-payments.ts`, the Bank card on Settings → Books).** A Work invoice's payment reference is its number (printed on it and in the EHF's PaymentID; Kaizena Flow makes no KID, which needs the owner's KID agreement with the bank), so a client's bank writes the number in the line.
Each line of money in not matched to the books is compared with the store's open invoices (issued on or before the day the money came, in the books' currency, something outstanding), in the order the money came, each line taking what it pays off its invoice before the next is looked at. *Certain:* the invoice's printed number (or its number in the series as a run of digits of its own, never inside a longer one such as a KID) is in the line, the line pays exactly what is outstanding, and no other
open invoice's number is in it: the payment is recorded when the owner uploads the statement, and the upload's message names the invoices paid. *Offered, one press each:* the number is in the line and it pays no more than is outstanding (a part payment); the line pays exactly what one invoice has outstanding and the bank names its client (a word of the name, not its legal form, Nordic letters or not); or exactly what one open invoice has outstanding and no other.
Two invoices of the same amount and no name: nothing is guessed. A payment recorded so is an ordinary Work payment (by bank, on the bank's day, with what the bank says of it as its reference), so the invoice's status, its reminders and the payment register follow as for one typed by hand, and it is reversed the same way; it is posted to the books at once and matched to its line. Owner only, audited (`work.bank_payment_recorded`); the ledger's job never records one.
Kaizena Flow's own books are its own store's (D467). *A shop's orders on payment terms (D293), the same day:* an order with a balance on terms is an open document too, named by its invoice's number (or that number in its series) or the order's own number, owed its balance; a payment is recorded through `recordTermsPaidOutside()` as money received by bank transfer on the bank's day (it takes money of the last 31 days, so an older line is not offered one), lowering the balance or settling the
order, and posted at once (`postShopPaymentNow()`); the offer says *invoice F-4 for order 1001*. *Not yet:* a payment covering two invoices, and money out to suppliers (B3).

Date: 2026-11-07
