# D328: The autopilot keeps going

**The autopilot keeps going (`docs/ai-autopilot.md` unit D, section 12).** With *Keep working toward my goals* on (off by default, Settings of the Autopilot page), each active goal is looked at again when a plan for it ended (at least an hour after its last review), every `reviewDays` (default 7) when nothing else brought it up, and six hours after a review that failed; never while a plan for the goal is a draft, running or paused, never on a store that is not active or whose autopilot is paused
or whose AI budget is spent, at most six reviews a store a day and two a run.
A review (`runGoalReviews()` in `/api/cron/autopilot`, after the steps; rows of `commerce.autopilot_reviews`, one running per goal) runs in the name of the goal's maker read again (an owner of the store, else skipped), as a headless turn in the goal's own conversation that may only read (`REVIEW_READ_TOOLS`) and make one plan (`make_autopilot_plan`, the plan kept in the goal's conversation); the message carries the goal's progress written by code and its last plans' results.
Code then ties the plan to the goal (others the turn made are stopped) and, by `startDecision()`, starts it as the autopilot when the owner chose *Starts at once* and fewer than `plansPerWeek` (default 3) started in seven days, or keeps its start for the owner's yes on the Decisions page. Starting a plan gives the autopilot no power it did not have: its steps still meet the levels and budgets, and `start_autopilot_plan`/`resume_autopilot_plan` stay never automatic as tools the model calls.

Date: 2026-10-25

The owner's goal for the AI: research, build, market, *manage and grow the business over time* without a person; the rating of 25 October 2026 named plans that end and wait for a person as the largest gap. "lets start the work on the Highest-value next steps".
