# D538: An Animation component, and a logo that is an animation

**An Animation component, and a logo that is an animation (10 November 2026, `src/lib/animation.ts`, `src/lib/animation-make.ts`, `AnimationBlock` and `SiteBlock.animation` in `src/lib/page-content.ts`, `src/components/animation-view.tsx`, `src/components/animated-logo.tsx`, `src/components/admin/animation-fields.tsx`).** D536 let a video play once without controls, which made the owner's animated logo work in the Video component, in Chromium.
Safari ignores a WebM's transparency and draws it on black, so every iPhone showed a black box, and a video's settings (controls, start by itself, loop, a picture before) are not what an animation needs. The owner asked whether an animation should be a component of its own, and for the header's logo to take one. It is one, in the builder's sidebar beside Video, on a store's pages and on Kaizena Flow's own. **What the owner does:** one button.
They choose the WebM their animation program gave them, with its see-through background, and it is on the page. **What their browser does (`makeAnimation()`, nothing on our servers):** no browser but Chromium's shows a WebM's transparency and none shows an MP4's, so the file visitors get is an ordinary H.264 MP4 twice the picture's height: the picture as it looks on black in the top half and its mask (white where it is solid) in the bottom half.
The WebM is read with its transparency (mediabunny's `CanvasSink`, loaded only when a file is chosen), each frame is drawn above its mask on a canvas, and the pair is written with the browser's own encoder (WebCodecs), as `compressVideo()` writes a video. The last frame is kept as a see-through picture at the source's size, 1600 pixels at most, as a page's pictures are.
The two go up the ways a picture and a video already do: `uploadPicture()`, and `sendVideo()` (D536's two tries, and a video that never arrives is forgotten), marked as compressed so the library never makes it smaller again, which would move the picture off its mask. In the library the video is named *(animation: picture and mask)*, because it is odd to look at by itself.
Picture and mask together must fit what every phone's decoder takes (H.264 levels 4 to 4.2: 8,704 blocks of 16 pixels a frame), so `stackedPictureSize()` makes a 1920 × 1080 source 1394 × 784 inside the video; the still is 1600 × 900, so what it rests on is the sharper of the two.
A file that cannot be an animation is refused in the owner's words before anything is uploaded: not a WebM; over 200 MB; no see-through background (use the Video component); longer than 20 seconds (use the Video component); too small; a browser that cannot prepare it (Safari and Firefox cannot read a WebM's transparency back: use Chrome or Edge on a computer or an Android phone); unreadable. **What a visitor gets (`AnimationPlayer`):** a canvas with a see-through background, drawn from the video
by two small shaders (WebGL), and over it the last frame as a picture with the description. Nothing shows until it starts, as an animation opens on its first frame; it starts when a quarter of it is on the screen, plays without sound or controls, and then the picture is laid over the canvas's last frame and the video is let go, so it rests sharp at any size and holds no decoder. *Play again and again* (Style) keeps it going round instead.
The picture is shown at once, and no video is fetched, for visitors who prefer less motion and without scripts (both by the picture's own classes and a `noscript` copy, no script of ours), in a browser that cannot draw it, where the browser will not start it (a phone saving power), and on a line that has not started it in 8 seconds.
The player is a lazy view (`lazy/blocks`), so its script comes only to pages that have one. **As a picture otherwise:** a description (the publish check asks for one, as for a picture), an optional link, and under Style its width and position by screen size (`ImageSizeFields`). The description is translated with the page. Copied to another store or saved as a template, its two files go together, and it is left empty where either cannot.
Added from the sidebar its settings open, as every component's that is nothing until it is filled. **The logo (`SiteBlock.animation`, `AnimatedLogo`, `AnimatedLogoFields`):** a header's or footer's logo part has the same one button, *Animated logo*. `Brand` (a store's and Kaizena Flow's) then draws the animation in the logo's place, as tall as the logo is (the part's height, else the usual one by screen size) and as wide as its shape makes it, so it must be framed closely on the logo.
It plays once a visit: the tab's storage remembers it (`kz-animation:logo:{store}`), and a script in the page itself, right after it (`animationSeenScript()`, the colour mode's way, D99), marks it as seen before anything is drawn, so every later page has the logo at once and never waits for the page's scripts. The site's logo in Settings stays what everything else shows (emails, documents, sharing, the admin).
One animation serves light and dark: there is no second one for dark backgrounds. **The admin's words:** every admin screen's first scripts carry the catalogues' English (D533), and the home screen had 234 bytes of room. The animation's words are a server-only area (`animation` in `SERVER_AREAS`, D534's way): English in `src/lib/animation-words.ts`, which comes with the fields, and a page in another language hands them down (`useHandedText("animation")`).
The three words the builder itself needs (the component's name, *this animation*, *No animation yet.*) were paid for by saying *Double-click or use the wrench.* once (`emptyHow`, added by `emptyWords()` for the components whose settings open when added) instead of in fifteen `emptyBlock` texts. **Not done, and said to the owner:** it was tested in Chromium here, not on an iPhone; the player uses only what Safari has (H.264 and WebGL).
A looping animation has no pause button, as a row's background video has none: a visitor's setting for less motion is the way to stop it (WCAG 2.2.2 would ask for a control on what moves for more than five seconds). The AI manager cannot make an animation. The Video component still shows a see-through WebM on black in Safari and does not yet say to use this component instead.
Tests: `animation.test.ts`, `animation-block.test.ts`, `animation-view.test.ts`, `e2e/animation.spec.ts`, `e2e/signed-in/builder-animation.spec.ts`. No migration.
