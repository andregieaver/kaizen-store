# D826: Every storefront link states what it may fetch ahead

**Every storefront link states what it may fetch ahead (bloat review step 2.6; 19 November 2026).** A storefront link is a
`PolicyLink` (`src/components/policy-link.tsx`) with a policy: sight (fetched when it scrolls into view, Next's default), intent
(when a pointer, a touch or focus reaches it) or never. A header's top links are fetched on sight; the footers, the phone menu,
sub-links, the header's tools and the bottom bar on intent. The admin's links go through `AdminLink` (intent unless told
otherwise), so an admin screen at rest fetches nothing ahead. A ratchet (`directLinks` in `src/server/ratchet.baseline.json`)
lets direct imports of `next/link` only fall (396 to 384), and `e2e/prefetch.spec.ts` holds a budget of router requests per page.
Measured on a phone read to the footer: the front page 19 to 3 router requests, a product 18 to 3, a content page 20 to 3, a listing
20 to 4, the cart 20 to 5, the first Add to cart 32 to 7; the money path's two-line cart 15 to 7 and checkout 18 to 3 (budgets 9 and
5); the eight budgeted admin screens 0 at rest. CI's e2e and signed-in jobs held these on 19 November. The written policy is in
`docs/performance.md` (What the router fetches ahead). Commits 3d2e4fbde, b9a482b2c, 3862626ac, 90743236b, 8170d5f66, 26ed9b957,
8445130cb.
