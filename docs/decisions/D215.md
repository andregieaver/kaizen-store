# D215: The page builder's columns scroll on their own, and every shop component has its picture

**The page builder's columns scroll on their own, and every shop component has its picture.** On a computer the building blocks, the canvas and the settings are three scroll areas side by side, fitted between where the builder starts (under the admin's header) and the editor's bar at the foot of the screen (`useStandaloneColumns()`: `--builder-h`, measured again when the window or the bar changes; the bar is `data-editor-bar`), each `overscroll-contain` so a column at its end does not move the
page; phones keep one scroll. Shop components and pieces are drawn with their own icon (`SHOP_PART_ICONS` in `src/components/admin/shop-part-icons.tsx`, keyed by every part, so a new one needs its picture): a cookie for Cookies, a cart, a card, a receipt and so on, instead of one bag that read as a bin; a shop component being dragged shows its own name and picture. No migration.
