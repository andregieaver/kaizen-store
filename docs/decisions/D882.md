# D882: Topics and a preference page: a store's own topics, consent per topic kept as a record, how often and a month's pause ch

Date: 2026-11-23

**Topics and a preference page (23 November 2026, email plan M4.2).** The design is `docs/email-topics.md`: a store's own topics, every
topic choice kept as a record, a broadcast about one topic reaching only the readers in it, and (slice 2) a page every email links to
where a reader picks topics, how often, or a month's pause.

- `commerce.marketing_topics` (names and lines per language, on unless turned off, the owner's order, archived not deleted, at most 20)
  and `commerce.marketing_topic_choices` (append only: in or out, when, from where, the words seen), written only by
  `src/server/marketing-topics.ts`. A reader is in a topic by their newest choice, else its default: `inTopic()`, the one rule the count,
  the frozen list and the page read. Choices that change nothing are not kept.
- A broadcast's *About* is asked only where the store has topics, *Everything* unless one is chosen (`broadcasts.topic_id`); a topic
  archived later still reaches those in it, a deleted one stops the send with words.
- The topics sit on the subscriber list as one card with each topic's reach now (`topicCounts()`, one statement). A store copy starts
  with none (its list is empty); the whole-store file carries the topics; the choices are personal data, exported with the subscriber and
  deleted with them. Kaizena Flow's own list is its house store's, so the platform has topics too.

Idle cost: none until a store adds a topic; then one indexed lookup per reader when a broadcast about it is counted or started, and one
statement on the subscriber list.
