# D407: The right of withdrawal checked against the law

**The right of withdrawal checked against the law (consolidation, `docs/consolidation.md` 4, `docs/legal/check-2026-10-29.md`).** Step 1: a booked service is exempt only where the owner marks the product `dated_service` (accommodation, car rental, catering or leisure on a set date: CRD Art. 16(l), angrerettloven § 22 m, distansavtalslagen 2 kap. 11 § 12, forbrugeraftaleloven § 18 stk. 2 nr. 12), offered in the product editor for bookings only (`productInput` refuses it on goods); stays start
exempt (migration `stays_dated_service`, the editor when a product becomes a stay), appointments and rentals keep the right.
`lineWithdrawal()` records the product's choice on the line; `lineEligibility()` gives a service line the right within 14 days of the order (`serviceEligibility()`, facts from `SERVICE_LINE_FACTS`), refused once fully performed at the customer's request (`service_performed`), and the withdrawal function cancels its booking, clears its part at the venue and tells the staff; a refund never counts a booking's venue part.
Step 2: checkout asks the express request and acknowledgment for a booking inside the 14 days (`needsServiceRequest()`, the cart's tick, `orders.service_request_at`); the server records it and refuses nothing, since without it the customer keeps the whole right and owes nothing (Art. 14(4)(a)(ii)); a service partly provided at the request is paid for in proportion to the time passed (`serviceShare()`, the return line's deduction, Art. 14(3)).
Step 3: sealed goods say the right ends when the seal is broken after delivery. Step 5: the last day moves off a Saturday, Sunday or holiday to the next working day (`periodEnd()` over `lastWorkingDay()` in `src/lib/withdrawal-days.ts`, from the order's country): Denmark's and Sweden's days as their laws list them, weekends only for the rest of the EU (Reg. 1182/71 Art. 3(4)), Norway unchanged (no rule read).
Step 4: the order confirmation carries the withdrawal information and the model form in the email itself (`withdrawalInformation()` in `src/lib/withdrawal-email.ts` over the withdrawal-information starter's own sentences, `withdrawalInfoBlocks()`), with the words of any consent the customer ticked, since a link is not a durable medium (CJEU C-49/11, CRD Art. 8(7)).
Step 6: newspapers are a VAT category of their own (`newspapers`: Denmark 0 % by momsloven § 34 stk. 1 nr. 18, Norway 0 % by mval § 6-1, Sweden 6 % by Skatteverket; every other country the rate of its periodicals row, marked unverified), and the words of `periodicals` and `culture_events` say where Norway's and Sweden's rates do not hold (migration `vat_newspapers`). The words are hand-written in four languages and kept out of the AI catalogue.

Date: 2026-10-29

*Why:* every booking was refused withdrawal, which the law allows only for those kinds on a set date (Commission guidance 2021/C 525/01, 5.11.6). Lines sold before stay as recorded.
