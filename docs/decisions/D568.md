# D568: The admin on a phone

**The admin on a phone (11 November 2026, `src/app/admin/admin.css`, `src/components/admin/orders/order-table.tsx`, `send-parcel.tsx`, the order page, `team-view.tsx`, `e2e/signed-in/admin-phone.spec.ts`).** The third step the owner chose ("go"): a pass over the screens owners use every day, at 390 px. Every admin page without parameters was opened on a phone: five were wider than the screen.
The causes and the fixes: words for screen readers only (`sr-only`) in a table that scrolls in its card escaped the card's clipping and widened the page (Inventory, 777 px), so the admin pins them to their containing block's start; a choice whose longest option is a sentence held its form and fieldset at that width (Team, AI settings), so a select is never wider than its space, its own box shrinks with it, a fieldset may shrink below its content, and the team's invite cards sit in a
`minmax(0, 1fr)` grid. And where nothing overflowed but what mattered was out of view: the orders list on a phone is each order's number with its customer, payment and sending under it, and its total (its other columns from Small up, no 40 rem minimum); an order's items keep their quantity and total in view (the SKU under the product's name); sending a parcel keeps ordered, to send and this parcel (sent and withdrawn from Small up).
Only the page builder's canvas is wider than a phone, as a desktop tool. Row `platform.mobile-admin-app` criterion 4 is held by the new test (orders list, one order, products, inventory and the team at 390 px, nothing past the edge). No migration.
