# D637: The admin redesigned, round 12

**The admin redesigned, round 12: Returns, Gift cards, Reviews (`docs/audits/admin-redesign-round-12.md`; 16 November 2026).** Returns says what waits in its header, shows counters only when not zero and filters once there is something to filter, and keeps the law in a fold; an empty queue offers *Find the order* for one that came by email or phone.
Gift cards and Reviews say whether they are off and switch on from the page in one press (`switchOnFromPageAction`, owners, as on Features); Gift cards then offers *Make a gift card* (a new product) and shows what customers hold once cards exist; Reviews counts what waits, puts checking and asking buyers as rows with their forms in place, and import and export under More. Platform: none apply (it sells plans). No migration.
