# D352: Marketing email from the store's own facts

**Marketing email from the store's own facts (wave 8 unit H, `docs/wave-8-analytics-ai.md` section 4, H); built.** A broadcast's content takes two more blocks (`broadcastBlockSchema`): `product` (a product's id and address, `/p/{handle}`, with the title the editor shows) and `campaign` (a campaign's id).
Neither holds a price, a picture or an offer: each email draws them from what code read for its recipient (`EmailFacts`, `emailFactsFor()` in `src/server/broadcast-facts.ts`, `factsForEmail()` in the marketing email): the product's title, picture and price as the storefront shows it in the recipient's country and the email's language (`listGridProducts()`, the price by `wordpressPrice()`'s rules: the amount, the VAT label, a reduction's 30-day price, the unit price), its link a click address to
its page, and a campaign's offer in the shop's words (`campaignNotices()`, `noticeText()`) with its end; a product the market does not sell, or a campaign that ended, is left out.
Broadcasts read the facts once per broadcast, market and language in a batch; automations and tests read them too. *Write with AI* (the editor, and the AI manager's `draft_broadcast`) asks the model with two read-only tools, `find_products` (by keyword through `rankedSearch()`, never a meaning model, else the newest; at most 8) and `running_campaigns`, read in the store's own country in the email's language, in up to four rounds (`chatWithTools()`, `UNTRUSTED_TEXT` for what they return); the
answer names products and campaigns by id and `keepHanded()` keeps only those the tools handed it (at most 6 and 2), put in by `suggestionBlocks()`. A price, discount, stock or urgency claim in the model's words refuses the suggestion, as before (`findClaims()`, the brand's words to avoid). The segment chosen shapes the words: its name and rules in words (`describeRules()`), never its people.
The editor also has *Find a product or a campaign* (`findBroadcastFactsAction`, `marketing:write`, nothing saved). Sending is unchanged: the person's press or the gated `send_broadcast`, to subscribed addresses with the unsubscribe link. Product pictures are the product's own (as in order emails); a mail app that shows no WebP shows the alt text. No migration. Closes `ai.ai-written-marketing-email` in the tracker.
