# D354: Events to the owner's own endpoints, signed

**Events to the owner's own endpoints, signed (wave 9 unit A, `docs/wave-9-platform.md` section 3, A); built.** An owner adds up to ten endpoints of their own at Integrations → Your own endpoints (owners only, as the services: they get customers' details): a name, an HTTPS address on a host name (`checkEndpointUrl()`; the address guard `hostProblem()` at save, and `safeFetch()` again at every send: a public address, no redirect, ten seconds, a small answer read) and the topics.
They are `commerce.webhook_endpoints` (a table of its own rather than a provider of `store_integrations`, as one store keeps several), written only by `src/server/webhook-endpoints.ts` (audited `integration.endpoint_*`, `never` copied, `NOT_PERSONAL`): the address and a signing secret (`whsec_` and 32 random bytes) kept encrypted, the secret shown once on adding and on *New secret*.
The database queues each event for every endpoint that is on and asks for it (`commerce.queue_integration_event()`, a delivery of provider `webhook` with its `endpoint_id`; a second change of the same subject before the delivery is built adds none).
New topics, queued by triggers where they happen and offered to endpoints only (the services keep the events they had, `SERVICE_EVENTS`): `return.requested`, `withdrawal.received`, `product.created`, `product.updated`, `stock.low`, `discount.created`.
Each delivery is `{ id, topic, version: 1, store, created_at, data }` (`endpointPayload()`, built when sent), with the headers `Kaizena-Webhook-Id`, `-Timestamp`, `-Topic` and `-Signature: v1={hex HMAC-SHA256 of "{timestamp}.{body}"}` (`signWebhook()`); `verifyWebhook()` (pure, `src/lib/webhook-signature.ts`) is what a receiver does and refuses a timestamp more than five minutes off.
Retries as the services' (about 8.6 hours); a delivery that arrives clears the endpoint's `failing_since`, and an endpoint where every delivery failed for three days is switched off (`disabled_reason = 'failing'`), its queue failed, and its owners emailed once (`integration.endpoint_off`, its name and address hint, never a payload). A test is signed as any other; a failed or delivered delivery is sent again with the same payload id. Closes `platform.outgoing-webhooks`.
Migration versions: `20261028084018_webhook_endpoints` (applied by CI on 28 October 2026; advisors INFO only).
