# D182: Theme elements, content width and resizing in the page builder

**Theme elements, content width and resizing in the page builder.** `docs/theme-elements.md` is the contract. A Theme tab beside Building blocks edits, on the store's theme, what rows, H1 to H6, paragraphs, lists and buttons are (`ThemeSettings.elements`: Typography, spacing, frame, a row's colour, per screen size), the content width in pixels (`layout.maxWidth`) and the body background; a row sets its own content width (`PageRow.contentMax`, per size).
The theme is laid under the parts before they are drawn (`themedRows()`, a part's own setting wins at the sizes it sets it at; a rich text's own paragraphs, headings and lists take it as `themeInner`), never saved into pages, on the site in `PageRowView` and on the canvas with the unsaved value. On the canvas a row's side edges and the edges between its columns are dragged (content width in steps of 8 px; column shares of 100, `setColumnShares()`), at the size being edited.
The row, column and component settings are a floating panel, moved and resized by the editor, remembered in the browser, with the page behind it in use.
