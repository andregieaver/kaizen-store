# D322: Bundles

**Bundles (wave 6 unit D, `docs/wave-6-catalogue.md` Unit D); specified.** A product of kind `bundle` with one variant and components (`bundle_components`: goods variants of its VAT category, a quantity each, at most 20, two units at least), priced as its own or as the components' sum less a percentage (written through `set_price()` by the database, `refresh_bundle_prices()`), with stock the lowest whole number of bundles its components allow (inside `variant_availability`, never on backorder).
`placeOrder()` holds the components' stock, the order line keeps them (`order_line_components`, frozen), payment draws them and `planRestock()` puts them back.

Date: 2026-10-26

*Why:* `catalogue.bundles`: Shopify sells bundles natively; Kaizena Flow only discounted a mix the shopper put together. Built in two steps (D1 the bundle and its sale, D2 after the sale and around the store). D1 built (2026-10-26): kind, components and their rules, computed price, derived stock, the editor's Components card, the cart, checkout's holds and draw, frozen components on the order line, restock as components, orders with a bundle not changed.
Migration versions: D1 `20261026102721` (bundles), `20261026102754` (bundles_sql), applied by CI on 2026-10-26; after that deploy the advisors show for the new tables only RLS with no policy (by design for the private schema) and indexes not used yet. D2 built (2026-10-26): the demo bundle, components on the order pages, slips and pick list, *What's inside* on the product page, the AI manager's bundle tools.
A change to a component is no longer refused for its bundle's sake: the bundle simply cannot be bought until put right. Migration versions: D2 `20261026125949` (bundles_demo), `20261026130020` (bundles_relax), applied by CI on 2026-10-26. Unit D is complete.
