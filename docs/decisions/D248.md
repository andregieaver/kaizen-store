# D248: The theme store, step 2

**The theme store, step 2: trying a theme in your store (`docs/theme-phase-3.md`).** A theme can be added to a store's theme library without going live: `installDesignPreset()` is `applyDesignPreset()`'s own body (`placeDesign()`, so the same checks, fonts, pictures and new layout pages) with the look kept in the install's row and the store's columns untouched, no `design_preset_uses` row and the audit `store.theme_installed`. *Try it in your store* on a theme's page goes through
`/themes/{slug}/try` (a route that knows who is signed in; sign in and back) to `/admin/account/themes/try/{slug}`, which lists the person's stores where they hold `website:write`, says where the theme is already, and adds it through the store's own `installThemeAction`; Design settings' themes offer *Add to my themes* beside *Apply*. Both open the install's page (D244) with a note that visitors see nothing yet and a *Switch to it* card.
A theme with installs counts as used, so it cannot be deleted.
