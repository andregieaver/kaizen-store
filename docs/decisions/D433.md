# D433: A product priced on request in bundles

**A product priced on request in bundles.** A product priced on request (D431) may be a part of a bundle with a price of its own (`bundle_pricing = 'fixed'`, D322), never of one priced from its products' prices (`computed`).
The editor says so before anything is written: the bundle's list of parts marks such a part while *The products' prices less a percentage* is chosen, `componentProblems()` refuses the save in words that say what to do ("… is priced on request, so the bundle cannot take its price from it: give the bundle a price of its own, or untick Price on request on … to show its price."), and `saveProduct()` refuses ticking *Price on request* on a product in such a bundle, naming the bundles
(`computedBundlesHolding()`, `onRequestInBundlesProblem()`); the product file's import (D165, D434) writes through `saveProduct()` and so gets the same refusal per product.
The database holds the rule at commit, as the other bundle rules: `bundle_components_on_request` refuses a part priced on request written into a computed bundle, `products_bundle_on_request` a bundle switched to computed while it holds one and a product priced on request while one of its variants is in a computed bundle (the bundles' handles in the error's detail), both through `bundle_on_request_problem()`, judged on what a save leaves (a save that changes the pricing and the parts together
passes when the result obeys). A computed bundle that already held such a part is given a price of its own by the migration, keeping the price it had. Cart and checkout read only a line's own product (`not p.price_on_request` in `sellableQuantity()`, `getCart()` and `placeOrder()`), so a bundle with a price of its own holding such a part is bought as any bundle, at the bundle's price, while the part alone is never put in a cart.
Store copies carry bundles that already obey the rule, and their checks pass at the copy's commit. Asked for by the owner. *Why:* a computed bundle's price is its products' list prices less a percentage (`refresh_bundle_prices()`), so a product priced on request inside one would be sold at the price its owner keeps for quotes, and anyone could work that price out from the bundle's.
A bundle with a price of its own gives no part's price away, so the product can still be sold in a set at a price the owner chose. *Why refused and not left unbuyable,* as a part whose VAT category moves is (D322): the bundle would go on showing a price made from the hidden one; a refusal at the save tells the owner the two ways out at the moment they choose.
