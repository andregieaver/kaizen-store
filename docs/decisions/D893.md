# D893: Finding companies from the register and one do-not-contact list (email plan P5.1, P5.6)

Date: 2026-11-25

**Find companies, and a no that every store keeps (25 November 2026, the owner: "go ahead" with the parts of phase 5 that contact nobody).**
Phase 5 waits on a lawyer's reading of each country's rules (P5.3, `docs/outreach-rules.md`, Legal reviews D892). Finding companies and
keeping a list of those who said no contact nobody, so they are built first, for Kaizena Flow's own store (P5.8).

- **Find companies** (`/admin/[store]/companies/find`, Customers): the owner writes whom they want to reach (*cafés in Oslo with 5 to 50
  employees*). The model turns it into filters (`filtersFromWords()`), choosing industries only from SN2025's divisions and groups and
  naming places; code keeps only codes and names on the lists bundled in `src/data/no-register-codes.json` (SSB Klass 6 and Kartverket's
  municipalities and counties), and the page moves to an address that holds the filters, so paging asks the model nothing. Lisa sets the
  same filters herself under Customise, which also works with no AI.
- **Companies, not people.** Code asks Brønnøysund's open Enhetsregisteret (`searchUrl()`, no key, NLOD) for limited companies and the like
  (`ORG_FORMS`), never a sole trader, never one bankrupt or being wound up, 20 a page. Each shows its industry, size, place, registration
  day, website and a link to the register. **Nothing is kept**: the register is asked each time the page is opened, so there is no prospect
  data to keep a record of yet (P5.4 comes with saving prospects, P5.7).
- **Do not contact, everywhere** (`platform_do_not_contact`): an address or a company's domain that said no, kept as a SHA-256 hash of its
  folded form (`foldAddress()`, `foldDomain()`, `entryHash()`), never in clear, for every store and the platform alike; nothing removes an
  entry. `mayContact()` checks an address and its domain, and every outreach will call it before sending (P5.5); today Find companies marks a
  company whose website's domain is on the list and hides its link. A member who may change customers adds an entry by hand (someone asked
  by phone or letter) through a route handler, audited as `outreach.do_not_contact_added` with the kind only. A no from an outreach email's
  reply, link or complaint will add itself when P5.5 sends. It is a suppression list kept so a no is honoured (GDPR art. 21(3)); the
  lawyer's question 4 asks whether hashes shared across stores are acceptable.
- **Only Kaizena Flow's store for now** (the menu item is `house`, the page and the route check `store.house`). Stores get it after P5.3's
  reading, with the countries the lawyer has read; Denmark's CVR and Finland's PRH follow as their own registers.

Idle cost: nothing; one request to Brønnøysund and one indexed read when the page is opened with a search.
