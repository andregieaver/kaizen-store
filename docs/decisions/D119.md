# D119: Custom fields grow

**Custom fields grow: groups and repeaters, links, relations and files, fields on variants and on categories and tags, binding, filters, search, structured data, chat, translation and the AI manager.** A group and a repeater hold sub fields (structure and non-text cells shared, translatable texts per language, repeater rows with stable ids so a translation follows its row); links point at a page, product, category, tag or web address; product, page and category or tag relations and files (own
bucket `field-files`, uploaded from the browser) hold ids and addresses that the server checks belong to the store and resolves on read, leaving out what is gone. Variants have fields by SKU in the product editor, drawn per variant on the product page as the picker changes, and categories and tags in their manager and on their page (`field_values.entity` widened, triggers, `clone_store()`).
A public field can be a listing filter (`f.<name>`), part of keyword search (`commerce.field_search`, one row per product and language, stemmed like titles), a fact for the chat agent and, for simple types, a structured-data property. A heading, rich text, image or button can take its content from a field (`bind`, resolved before drawing).
Field texts are in the store translation worklist and the AI manager reads, fills and makes groups (`set_fields` and `create_field_group` kept for the owner's yes). Left for phase 3: the store and customers as things, money, loop and flexible content, listing tile bindings, number ranges as filters, variants and categories in search.
