# D278: Theme photographs are taken one look at a time across the job's runs

**Theme photographs are taken one look at a time across the job's runs.** The theme store's Highlights for Hearth, Orbit, Atelier, Arcade and Botanica never appeared: a look's highlights are composed only once its computer and phone photographs are made, and 14 of those had failed three times with *The browser stopped while it was looking at the page*.
The job starts every minute and a run takes looks for up to 100 seconds (Publish starts one in `after()` too), so runs overlapped, and two Chromium browsers at once in one function ran it out of memory; the signature themes' photographed pages, the heaviest, failed most, and some looks only came through on a second or third try.
`claimThemeShot()` now takes a unit only while no other is being photographed (a unit `running` with a live claim), deciding under a transaction advisory lock of its own so two runs never both see none; a run that finds one busy ends at once and the run holding it carries on. A run that dies holds the others back only until its claim runs out (three minutes). The migration puts the failed units back to waiting with their tries started over.

Date: 2026-10-18

The owner: "Some of the theme pages are missing a highlight section." Production: every failure was the browser stopping, none a page or upload problem, and the same looks sometimes came through on a later try (Atelier's Noir on its third, Orbit's own computer on its second). Migration version (applied by CI, ledger `supabase_migrations.kaizen_files`, 18 October 2026 21:56 UTC): `20261018212436_theme_shots_retry`.
It ran minutes before the new code was live, so the old overlapping runs took the units first: 12 came through and two (Atelier's own computer photograph, which held its highlights back, and Botanica's Night garden computer one) failed three times again. Put back once more at 22:05 (as *Try again* does), both came through on the first try under one look at a time, and every theme's page then showed its highlights.
Measured with the serverless Chromium on the heaviest page (Atelier at 1440 px: 8075 px long, 17 photographs), one look peaks at about 630 MB, so one browser fits and two did not. If a look still stops alone, the next step is a smaller full-length photograph. Advisors checked after: no new finding.
