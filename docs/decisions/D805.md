# D805: Store pages with nothing per visitor are static files

**Store pages with nothing per visitor are static files (bloat review step 2.4; 19 November 2026).** A store page whose content is
the same for every visitor (the home, a product, the blog, a page, the legal pages) is prerendered and served from the cache; what
is the visitor's own comes in one answer, `/api/shop/mine` (private, no-store): the cart's count, the saved products and the
country hint, asked only when the marker cookie says the visitor has one of them. Add to cart answers the new count (one request,
104 bytes) and redraws the page only when its form asks for it. On production on 19 November, two hours after the deploy, the
demo store's home, cookies page, a product, the blog and About us all answered prerendered, complete and not postponed.

The cart drawer's slot lost its catch-all (`(shop)/@drawer/[...rest]`): a param the build cannot know stays unknown when a static
page is made again, so its metadata was held back and the payload cut off, and a link to such a page ended in React error 412 (the
first landing was reverted for it, 4fb09512c). The drawer now shows only on the cart's address (`isCartAddress()`), the render-modes
check fails hard on such a param, and an e2e test forces a page to be made again and opens it by a link. A large store's home made
cold costs 22 statements, once (`scripts/server-budget.json`, at most 24).

What it changes: D34, the hearts on a saved product come from `/api/shop/mine`, not from the page; D64, the slide-out cart opens on
the cart's address only; D338, the country hint is drawn in the browser from the same answer; D558, the basket's notes follow the
count Add to cart answers; D592, `optimisticRouting: false` was written for the catch-all's endless prefetch, which is gone, so the
setting may go (a follow-up; `e2e/prefetch.spec.ts` says how). Step 2.4 stays In progress until commit 4 (taking out
`/wishlist/saved`) lands in a deploy of its own. Commits 0663977fa, 197e20763, ec7e950cd, 97a95b1d1, a067d25be.
