# D70: Stays and rentals are priced night by night through seasons, with a fee per booking; the booking is one line at its whole price

**Stays and rentals are priced night by night through seasons, with a fee per booking; the booking is one line at its whole price.** A season (`booking_seasons`) recurs every year between two days (`MM-DD`, across the new year too, or all year), on chosen weekdays (a night counts as the day it starts), and changes the price by a percentage (−90 to +500); seasons that meet multiply, and each date's price is rounded to whole units.
A fee per booking (`appointment_settings.booking_fee`, per market, VAT-inclusive, taxed as the product) is a stay's final cleaning or a rental's fee. The cart and the order hold a stay or rental as quantity 1 at its whole price (nights at their seasons' prices plus the fee), with the nights, days or hours on the booking (`CartLine.booking.count`, the parts in `booking.price`), so totals, discounts, VAT, deposits and refunds need nothing new; hours are priced by the date they fall on.
The product page lists the fee and each season's price, drawn for the shopper's kind (with or without VAT).

Date: 2026-09-27

Defaults chosen for the owner (B3c): percentages rather than a price table per season, so they cover every variant and market at once; no minimum stay per season. The demo cabin is 30 % dearer from 15 June to 15 August and 20 % on Friday and Saturday nights, with 500 kr (DK 375 kr) cleaning.
