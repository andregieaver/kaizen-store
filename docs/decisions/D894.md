# D894: Reading a found company's site: what it does, a fit worked out in code, and only its own door (email plan P5.2, P5.9)

Date: 2026-11-25

**Read their site (25 November 2026, the owner: "continue" after D893).** A company found in the register (D893) is a name, an industry and a
size; whether it is worth writing to is on its own website. Reading it contacts nobody, so it is built before P5.3's legal reading.

- **What only the site can tell.** When the owner's words are turned into filters, the model also lists what the register cannot filter by
  (*serves vegan food*, *sells online*): at most four, each weighted 1 to 3 (`cleanCriteria()`), kept in the page's address beside the
  filters (`c=`), and said on the page.
- **Read on request, one company at a time.** *Read their site* on a found company's card reads its front page through `readWebPage()`
  (the safe fetch, the store's day's page limit, kept as a research source) and asks the model for one sentence on what the company does and
  a score of 1 to 5 per criterion from the page alone. **The fit is worked out in code** (`compareOptions()`, the weighted total as a
  percentage), never said by the model. Nothing is kept; a company on the do-not-contact list is never read.
- **Only the company's own door.** Its general addresses are found in code (`generalAddresses()`): on its own domain or a subdomain, and named
  as a role (`ROLE_NAMES`: `post@`, `kontakt@`, `info@` …), at most two, shown and not kept. A named person's address on the page is never
  shown, never guessed from a pattern. A contact form and a phone number are not read yet.
- **Safety (P5.9).** The page is untrusted text under `UNTRUSTED_TEXT`, with the reminder that it may ask for things; the model's sentence
  loses any address, phone number or link in code (`plainSentence()`); scores for names that are not the owner's criteria are dropped and
  odd ones count as 1. `prospect-site.int.test.ts` reads a page that hides instructions and asks to be written to at a person's address,
  with the model played as one that obeyed. The prompts are not the agents' (`agent-eval.ts` plays the chat and the AI manager), so their
  cases are tests in code; an eval case for an invented fact waits for the outreach email (P5.5), the first place a claim reaches anyone.

Idle cost: nothing; a page read and one model call each time the owner asks to read a site.
