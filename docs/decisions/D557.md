# D557: Stock left and Add to cart on product tiles

**Stock left and Add to cart on product tiles (10 November 2026, `src/lib/shop-tiles.ts`, `src/server/shop-tiles.ts`, `src/server/product-settings.ts`, `/api/shop/tiles`, `TileShop`, Settings → Products).** The owner asked for a setting to show or hide quantities on the content grid's product tiles, overridable per grid; asked what *quantities* meant, they chose both: the stock left and a quantity chooser with Add to cart, as two switches. **Store setting:** `commerce.product_settings` (one
row a store, made when first saved; `tile_stock`, `tile_quantity`, both off), edited at `/admin/{store}/settings/products` (Settings, Selling, *Products*; `settings:write`; audited as `product.settings_changed`). **Per grid:** `ContentGridBlock.shopTiles` (`stock`, `quantity`: `show` or `hide`; absent follows the store), chosen in the grid's settings (*Stock and buying on the tiles*), for a store's own product grids, recommending ones too (added 11 November 2026: a recommending grid's tiles get
the same island, and an Add to cart pressed on one is credited to the recommendation as a click on the tile is, with the tab's `rec` attribution sent with the add). **Drawing:** the tiles are prerendered with the catalogue and stock is never cached, so neither is drawn on the server: a lazy island in each product tile (`TileShop`, `lazy/blocks`) names its product, the ids of a tick go in one `GET /api/shop/tiles` (same site only, no cookie, a website or a private store's locked page answers
nothing, at most 96 ids), and the answer carries the store's settings, each product's stock as the product page words it (`tileStockText()`: in stock, *Only 3 left* at five or fewer summed over the variants that can be sold, sold out, a backorder's days, a pre-order's date; nothing for products with no stock to count) and, for a product bought without choosing anything (one variant, goods or a bundle, no subscription it must have, a price shown, not sold out), its variant and quantity rule.
Add to cart is the product page's own `AddToCart` with the store's chooser (− and + unless *box*), so quantity rules, capping at stock, the shop's events and the phone's slide-out cart behave as there. A product with several variants shows its stock and keeps linking to its page. Without scripts nothing is drawn. **Not for the platform:** Kaizena Flow sells plans through Stripe, with no stock and no cart, so this stays store-only.
