# D552: Environmental claims on the live site are found and listed for the owner

**Environmental claims on the live site are found and listed for the owner (11 November 2026, `src/lib/green-claims.ts`, `src/server/green-claims.ts`, Products → Environmental claims at `/admin/{store}/products/claims`, the checkup's `green_claims`).** The Empowering Consumers Directive (EU 2024/825) applies from 27 September 2026: a generic environmental claim ("miljøvennlig", "sustainable", "klimanøytral") is banned unless the trader shows recognised excellent environmental performance,
and a claim of neutral or reduced impact that rests on offsetting emissions is banned outright. The claims filter (D76) already kept them out of AI-written text; nothing looked at what people wrote.
Now `greenClaimsReport()` reads the store's active products in every language (title, description, excerpt, SEO texts, safety information) and its published pages and articles (`textOfContent()` walks a page's rows, blocks and rich text, never its ids, addresses or styles), and lists each claim with the words around it and a link to edit it; one resting on offsetting is marked as banned in every case.
The store checkup (the AI manager's `store_checkup` and the briefing) carries it as `green_claims`. The filter's green words gained the offsetting ones in nb, sv, da, en and de ("klimakompensert", "carbon offset", …), so AI text is held to the same. A finding is a word to look at, not a verdict: a colour or a certified label may be fine, and the owner decides; the page says so and how to rewrite (a specific, provable fact).
Platform: Kaizena Flow's own site is its store's pages since D484, so its admin has the same page and finding. Words checked against the directive's text as summarised, not by a lawyer (see the owner's tasks). No migration.
