# D435: A quote's pay page reads as a quote

**A quote's pay page reads as a quote.** The pay link of a draft that is a shopper's request for a quote (D431, `draft_orders.source = 'quote'`) opens the same page as a staff draft's (D173), worded as the quote: the heading *Your quote {number} from {store}* (the order's number, as the quote's email names it), a line that this is the quote they asked for and that paying accepts it and places the order, the summary named *Your quote*, *The quote is valid until …*, the button *Accept and pay
{amount}*, and its own sentences when it is paid (*You have accepted this quote and paid*), no longer open (*This quote can no longer be accepted*) or still being paid. The seller, the lines, the VAT, the right of withdrawal, the terms and Stripe's note are the same. A staff draft's page keeps its words exactly.
`payPageFor()` reads the draft's `source` with a typed read (`draftByToken()` moved to `typedRow()`); the words are `m.draftPay.quote`, hand-written in nb, sv, da and en and kept under `ui:draftPay.` (`HAND_WRITTEN_ONLY`), so no other language gets them by machine.

Date: 2026-11-03

Asked for by the owner. The quote's email says "your quote" and "accept it by paying", and the page it opened said "Order 1042: the store has made this order for you", which reads as a different thing than the shopper asked for. *Why the order's number:* the email's subject already names the quote by it, and it is the number the shopper's receipt carries once paid; the draft's `D-` number is staff's. *Why hand-written only:* the page is an offer to a buyer, as the rest of it (D173).
