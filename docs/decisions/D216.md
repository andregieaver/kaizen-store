# D216: Support

**Support: live chat with an AI first line, a shared inbox and a silent partner (`docs/support.md`), built in steps.** Step 1 (the foundation, nothing visible): the pure rules (`support-state.ts`, tone, summaries, hours, the partner's tools, actions) and seven tables in the private `commerce` schema; messages are written only by `commerce.support_add_message()` (numbered without gaps; an AI reply after a person took over is dropped) and a conversation's state only by
`commerce.support_transition()`, the database's copy of `nextState()` (`src/db/support.test.ts` compares the two over 500 cases). Migration versions (applied by CI in the `release` job, 13 October 2026 at 17:15 UTC): `20261013163732_support_tables`, `20261013163816_support_foundation`.
