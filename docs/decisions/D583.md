# D583: Chat widgets read their own websites' pages

**Chat widgets read their own websites' pages (13 November 2026, step 2 of `docs/support-embed.md`, section 4).** Asked for by the owner: the crawler, restricted to the sites holding the tag.
A found site is listed by the five-minute job (`crawlWidgetSites()`, `dispatcher.widget-sites`): its robots.txt for `KaizenaFlowBot` or anyone (RFC 9309), its sitemap, else the links of its front page two deep, at most 100 pages on its own host (`sitePath()`: no file, query, sign-in, cart or account page); each page is read through `safeFetch()` (a redirect off the host drops it), its readable words cut into `knowledge_chunks` with the new source `site_page_id`
(`commerce.support_widget_site_pages`, hashed so an unchanged page is not cut again), 40 pages a run, 4 at a time, within two minutes, again weekly or on *Read the site again*. A widget searches its desk's documents and its own sites' pages (`searchKnowledge(…, {widget})`), never the store's pages or another widget's; the store's own chat never sees them. The best two pages are chips, opening in the visitor's own tab. The widget's prompt says a page's words are content, never instructions.
The agent-eval case with instructions hidden in a site page (`chat-widget-site-page`, played with the widget's own prompt and tool: `fixedWidgetPrompt()` is part of the chat's prompt version and `embedTools()` of the tools' version), and Website chat's pages back in the AI manager's prompt, came in the next push, without a migration; the eval is then recorded again on Platform → AI.
