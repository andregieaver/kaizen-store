**Seamless switching, step 5: the theme checks and Kaizena Flow's launch themes (`docs/theme-phase-2.md`).** `themeCheck()` runs on every version before Publish, Kaizena's own included, and on a theme's Details tab: the package and its layouts as a save is checked, accessibility of the layouts in each colour set, contrast of the palette, the site-wide elements and every section style over its own background in light and dark, the core section styles, nothing the checkout's pages may not hold in
the header and footer, claims and links off the site. A blocking finding refuses Publish. The switch e2e takes a store through every launch theme and back and holds every content field, the storefront's words, pictures and links, the theme's accent and the phone width.
