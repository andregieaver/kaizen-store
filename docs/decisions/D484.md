# D484: Kaizena Flow runs on Kaizena Flow, step 5

**Kaizena Flow runs on Kaizena Flow, step 5: the switch that serves kaizenaflow.com from its own store, built and left off (6 November 2026, `docs/platform-parity-plan.md` step 5, `platform_settings.site_from_store`, `commerce.set_site_from_store()`, `src/lib/house-routing.ts`, `src/server/house-switch.ts`, `SiteSwitchCard`).** Platform → Website has, under the copy (D469), a card that serves kaizenaflow.com from Kaizena Flow's own store, or back: three steps (copy again, look at the copy,
switch) and one button that asks only that the copy was looked at; on, it says since when and by whom, links to the store's Pages and switches back. **At run time, never a deployment:** the proxy reads the switch every few seconds (`siteStore()`, job 6 of `src/proxy.ts`); on Kaizena Flow's own host a page request whose first part is no platform route (`PLATFORM_ROUTES`: the admin, API, sign-in, OAuth, sign-up, the theme store, docs, status, report, get-domain, support, unsubscribe, holding
pages, referral links, `.well-known` and the platform's files; under `/no/` sign-up, get-domain, report, themes, status and docs) is decided as a request to the store's own host and served from `/s/kaizenaflow/…`; a test holds the lists to the folders of `src/app`. The store's own host sends its visitors to kaizenaflow.com (307, as the switch can go back).
The store's addresses are Kaizena Flow's while it is on (`Store.onSite`, remembered by `getStore()` as `rememberSiteStore()`, read by `storeOrigin()`, `storeBase()` and `storeOrigins()`), so canonical addresses, links, emails and the visit counter's own-host check are kaizenaflow.com's. **Norwegian stays at `/no/`:** the store's address shape gains an alias (`StoreAddress.aliases`, `HOUSE_ALIASES` `{ no: "nb" }`, only for the house store, in Norway, whose own language is not Norwegian): `/no/x`
is served from `no-nb`, `/nb/x` and `/no-nb/x` move there (308), and `marketPath()` writes Norwegian links as `/no/x`. **The same hreflang as before:** the house store's pages, articles, entries and front page name their languages as Kaizena Flow's site has (`en`, `nb`, `x-default` English; `houseLanguageAlternates()`), not a store's `en-NO`, which would say the English pages are for Norway alone; its sitemap likewise. **What Kaizena Flow's host draws once** reads the switch from a tagged cache
(`siteStoreCached()`, `SITE_STORE_TAG`): `sitemap-kaizen.xml` lists sign-up and the theme store in every language and then every address of the store's own sitemap; robots.txt closes the store's pages to AI crawlers where they say so; llms.txt lists the store's pages and articles; the house store is never listed as a store beside the others. **One consent and one chat:** while on, the platform's own routes ask with the store's consent (its id, tracking and code), so a visitor answers once on one
host; the store's pages keep Kaizena Flow's chat (`KaizenChat`) until step 6 joins the desks. **Rules in the database** (`20261106163515_house_switch_rules`, tested in `src/db/house-switch.test.ts`): the switch turns on only while the house store is open and public; `set_site_from_store()` makes the store public as it turns on and private again as it turns off, in one transaction, keeping when and by whom; the house store cannot be made private while it is on (Settings → Private mode says
why). **While it is on** the copy is not made again (it would put the platform's old pages over the live ones), and the platform's own pages and articles refuse to save (`SITE_IN_STORE`: edit them in the store), except the theme store's pages, which `/themes` still draws; the header and footer of the platform's own routes stay the platform's.
`scripts/site-compare.mjs` reads every address of `sitemap-kaizen.xml` (status, redirect, canonical, hreflang, title, robots) before the switch and checks the same addresses after it, failing on any that answers differently.
