# D599: The AI wallet

**The AI wallet: single-use cards in the owner's own Airwallex account (14 November 2026, step 2a of `docs/agent-spending.md`, `commerce.card_wallets`, `commerce.agent_purchases`, `commerce.agent_purchase_charges`, `src/lib/card-wallet.ts`, `src/server/card-wallet.ts`, `src/server/agent-purchases.ts`, Autopilot → Settings → *AI wallet*).** Stripe Issuing cannot be used: its local commercial issuing excludes Norway (and Sweden, Denmark, Poland), and Issuing on Connect needs connected accounts
without a Stripe Dashboard, which stores' are not. Kaizena Flow does not run a card programme; the owner connects their own business account, and of the providers compared (section 5.1) Airwallex alone has Norway by name, an OAuth partner connection with card scopes, native single-use cards and a real-time hook; the owner chose it, and chose to pay with the card in their own app, so Kaizena Flow never sees a card number and stays outside PCI DSS.
Kaizena Flow's partner app (`AIRWALLEX_CLIENT_ID`, `AIRWALLEX_CLIENT_SECRET`, `AIRWALLEX_MODE`, `/api/integrations/airwallex/callback`; scopes for cards, cardholders and transactions only) takes the owner's grant (encrypted, the five-minute access token refreshed under the row's lock as Fiken's, a refused refresh `auth_failed`), whose Airwallex app the cards appear in (one ready cardholder chosen at once), the wallet's currency (the store's main one), a monthly limit and a pause.
On the owner's yes (`makePurchase()`, the AI manager's tool in step 2b) the wallet is checked under its row's lock (on, a cardholder, not paused, a limit, room left this month: open purchases at their whole amount, others at their spend, in the store's time zone), so two yeses never pass one limit together, and one card is made: virtual, one charge, at most the amount in the wallet's currency, open 48 hours, closed by Airwallex the day after, named `Kaizena Flow: {what}`, the purchase's id as
the request's id. The dispatcher's `agentPurchases` job reads each open card's transactions, keeps them once each, works out the spend and closes the card once cleared or unused when its window ends. The page: Homer connects with one button and types one amount; each purchase shows what, from whom, up to how much, its card's last four digits and where it stands, with Cancel; the cardholder choice, the pause (closing every open card) and disconnecting are one step away.
Owners only. **The platform:** Kaizena Flow's wallet is its house store's, on that store's Autopilot page. **Step 2b** (a push of its own after this migration's): the AI manager's `wallet_status`, `request_purchase` (gate `spend`, in `NEVER_AUTOMATIC`, the owner's only; refused before it is kept when no card could be made; the approval worded by `purchaseWords()` from the wallet: the amount in its currency, 48 hours, whose app), `list_purchases` and `cancel_purchase`
(`src/server/purchase-tools.ts`); the owner MCP's catalogue gains them (contract 1.18); the eval gains `manager-page-asks-purchase` (a page the agent reads asks it to buy), recorded again on Platform → AI after it deploys. Not yet: Airwallex's remote authorisation (to be confirmed for connected accounts), purchases in the books, Revolut.
