# D92: The AI builds pages

**The AI builds pages: it interviews, plans, writes and makes the pictures, and saves a draft.** Pictures come from the site's AI (D73) through a picture model named in the AI settings (`ai_providers.image_model`), never in code: a better model is a new name there. It may come from another provider than the rest, with its own address and key (`image_provider`, `image_base_url`, `image_api_key_encrypted`, kept like the other key), and a quality where the model takes one.
`generateImage()` asks the OpenAI-compatible `/images/generations`, which OpenAI and others offer, for a shape (landscape, portrait or square, as each model's nearest size); a size or quality a model refuses is left out and remembered, as for text.
A picture is shrunk on the server as the browser shrinks uploads (`sharp`: WebP, at most 1600 pixels, and a 480-pixel copy, `src/server/ai-pictures.ts`) and kept in the media library with its alt text, marked as the AI's, so the alt-text run adds the other languages. The AI settings test the picture model with one small picture, shown and not kept.
The studio (Pages, Create with AI, for a store's members and Kaizen's platform admins; `PageStudio`, `src/server/page-ai.ts`, pure parts in `src/lib/page-ai.ts`) works in steps, each its own request under the page's `maxDuration`: an interview (typed, or spoken through the speech-to-text model, replies read out if a voice is set) that keeps a brief with the facts the owner gave; a plan of sections from a catalogue of designs (`PATTERNS`: opening, features, checklist, text and picture, text,
steps, questions, tabs, products, articles, Google reviews, pictures, video, call to action), shown to change by asking or to build; building, where each section's words are written (a few at once), code turns designs and words into the builder's own rows, columns and blocks (`buildPage()`: spacing, heading levels and alignment by design, one main heading, tinted sections only where the theme has a single colour scheme) and the page is saved as a draft, never published; then its pictures one by
one into their places under a row lock (`fillPicture()`), the first the page's own. The model never writes the page's structure, so every page passes `pageInput`.
Grounding: it is told the site's facts (name, language, what it says about itself, contact, categories, products in their own words, never prices or stock) and every address it may link to; links and buttons go only to those (`checkPlan()`, `buildPage()`), a video only at an address the owner typed, product grids show the catalogue's own products and prices, reviews come only from Google, and it never writes testimonials; every text passes the claims filter, asked once more without the claim and
then without the sentence, with a note to the owner. The conversation lives only in the tab.

Date: 2026-09-28

Asked for by the owner, who wants the picture model easy to swap when a better one comes. OpenAI's picture models answer through the same API as the rest; naming the model in the settings, and letting it come from another provider, keeps Kaizen independent of any one.
