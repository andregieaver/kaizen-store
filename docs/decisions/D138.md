# D138: Helthjem is connected

**Helthjem is connected** for service points, checkout services, booking, labels and tracking (`docs/shipping-carriers.md`, `src/lib/helthjem.ts`, `src/server/carriers/helthjem.ts`, `src/server/helthjem-shipping.ts`), on the store's own client credentials (a token kept until it expires). Like PostNord it has no price API, so its two services (home delivery, service point) are `store`-priced, each with the weight it takes at most (`maxWeightGrams`, applied to the cart at quote time).
What is booked is the store's own transport solution for each service; home delivery is checked for coverage of the full address before it is booked. Booking and labels of Porterbuddy and Helthjem share one admin component (`ChosenDeliveryBooking`) and the label route (`carrierLabel()`).
