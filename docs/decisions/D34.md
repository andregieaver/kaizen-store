# D34: Shoppers keep wishlists

**Shoppers keep wishlists: a heart on every product card and product page, several lists, and all or some of a list into the cart.** The heart saves a product in the shopper's first list (made on the first save) and, tapped again, takes it out of every list; the header's heart shows how many are saved and leads to the wishlist page.
There the shopper switches between lists, makes, renames and deletes them, chooses each item's variant and quantity, selects all or some items, and adds them to the cart, moves them to another list (a product already there stays once) or removes them. Each list remembers whether items stay in it once added to the cart or leave it. A product with one variant needs no choice; a product sold only by subscription is chosen on its page.
Shoppers need not sign in: their lists belong to the browser (a random token in a cookie, stored only as a hash) and join the account at sign-in, merging a list with the same name. Signed-in customers' lists are the account's, and go when the account is deleted. Product pages stay the same for everyone and cached: the hearts learn what this shopper saved from one small request after the page shows.

Date: 2026-09-25

Asked for by the owner. At most 20 lists and 200 items per list. Lists are private: there is no sharing yet.
