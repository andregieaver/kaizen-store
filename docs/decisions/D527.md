**The Dream Factory changes stores that exist (9 November 2026, `dream_runs.kind`, `src/lib/dream-factory.ts` `REWORK_STEPS`, `src/server/dream-factory.ts`, `docs/dream-factory.md` *Changing a store that exists*).** Until now a run built a store before it opened and refused an open one, because its AI acts without asking on the promise that nothing is public yet.
A second kind of run, a **rework**, takes the owner's words for what to change (the look, pages changed or new, new products, changes to products) and keeps one rule instead: nothing a visitor sees changes before the owner's *Make it live*.
The plan is one answer of the model held to the store's own pages and products by code, agreed by the owner; the changes are prepared out of sight with tools that only draft (a look in the theme library, page drafts, draft products) at *Ask me first*, so a change to a product on the site is kept for a yes by the existing gate; the review lists what is prepared in the code's words (what each page's draft changes, the look's preview, the kept changes) and offers *Make it live*, one more change in
the owner's words, or *Discard these changes*, which declines what was kept. Going live is code after the press, through the builder's Publish, the theme switch, the product editor's check and the approvals' own decision, each part said.
Chosen over routing the owner to the AI manager (many approvals, no one review) and over a staging set (one open a store, and it holds no products). **Not changed by a rework:** prices and stock of products on sale, shipping, payments, countries, discounts, emails, orders, the business's details, menus; the plan names what was asked for of these. **Store-only**, as the Dream Factory is (`docs/platform-parity.md`). **In the agents' eval (10 November 2026):** the plan's prompt is versioned (agent
`rework`) with the case `rework-plan` (titles that ask for more products and pages add none), titles reach the model through `withoutInstructions()`, and the admin map's line for the page says it changes stores that exist; the recording was made again for both. **Header and footer, and where to find it (10 November 2026):** the owner could not find the page (a line on Home was its only way in on an open store), so the Dream Factory is first in the Website section's sidebar; and a rework now
drafts a new header and footer too (step `frame`: `draft_header_footer`, with `use_header_footer` kept for the yes the review gives).
