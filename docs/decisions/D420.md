# D420: A free trial for every Dream Factory store, its end, and drill stores the asking admin can open

**A free trial for every Dream Factory store, its end, and drill stores the asking admin can open.** Every Dream Factory run gives its store the 14-day trial self-serve sign-up gives (`grantTrial()`, `src/server/store-trial.ts`, once a store: a store that had a trial or is on a plan keeps what it has), and the run's plan step counts it, so the owner looks at the store, changes it and may open it before choosing a plan.
When a trial ends with no plan (Stripe's status not trialing, active or past due), the five-minute dispatcher's `trials` job makes an open store private (`pauseAfterTrial()`, `store_billing.trial_paused_at` kept): its admin says why with a link to the Plan page, the owner cannot make it public and opening is refused until a plan starts, when the job makes it public again (`reopenAfterPlan()`), only ever a store it made private.
The platform admin who asks for the Dream Factory drill is made an owner of the drill's store for 14 days (`addDrillOwner()`, audited `store.drill_owner_added`; the test account stays the first owner, which every "the owner" read takes), so the store is on their Your stores and the watch page offers *See the store* and *Open the store's admin*. *See your store* on a finished run goes through the members' preview (`/api/private/preview`), since a store not yet open is private.
A store that began on a trial and later cancels its plan is made private the same way when the plan ends (the owner's decision of 5 November 2026: such a store needs a running plan to be public, so a month paid cannot keep it public for good); Billing says so while the plan is ending, and the banner says the plan ended, not the trial (`pausedAfter()`). A store that never had a trial is left as it is.

Date: 2026-11-02

Asked for by the owner after watching a drill: they could not reach the drill's store or its admin, and want to look at and change a store before choosing a plan. *Why private and not closed at the trial's end:* nothing is lost, and a plan undoes it at once. *Why a member for 14 days:* a drill's store belongs to the test address so the sign-up path stays real; a lasting membership would leave every admin in every drill store.
