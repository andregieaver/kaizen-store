# D537: A picture as a layer over a background

**A picture as a layer over a background (10 November 2026, `PictureLayer`, `layerDraw()`, `mapLayerPictures()` and `withLayerSmallCopies()` in `src/lib/background-layers.ts`, `LayerPicture` in `src/components/page-parts.tsx`, `[data-fx-pic]` in `src/app/motion.css`, `backgroundImageUrls()` and `withSmallCopies()` in `src/lib/page-images.ts`, `mapTemplateMedia()`, the picture adder in `src/components/admin/backdrop-fields.tsx`).** The fourth kind of layer of D534, beside a glow, a pattern and a
horizon: the owner's own picture, which is what a cut-out with a see-through background is for (a product, a cloud, a shape) and what a texture over a gradient is. **Two ways to sit:** *Placed* (the default: its centre at `x`, `y` per cent of the part, `width` per cent of the part's width from 5 to 300, its height following) or *Fills the part* (cut to fit as a background picture is, `x`, `y` saying which part of it shows).
It takes what every layer takes: opacity, blur (D535), its travel on scroll, twinkle or breathe, and a fade towards the edges, the bottom or the top, as a pattern does.
The hue of D534 does not turn it: a picture is drawn as it is, and alone it gives the hue slider nothing to show. **Chosen as every picture in the builder is:** *Choose a picture* in the layers panel is the builder's own picture button, so it uploads (compressed in the browser, with its small copy), takes one from the media library, makes one by prompt (D283) and, on one already there, removes its background (D413). **Drawn as the element that draws:** the layer's inner element is the `<img>`
itself, decoration with an empty alt text inside the frame that is hidden from screen readers; the blur is on it, so D535's *painted once, only moved* holds.
Filling the part, it reaches past the frame by its travel and by twice its blur, so no edge shows. **A phone gets the small copy:** `backgroundImageUrls()` and `withSmallCopies()` now walk the layers of rows, columns and a Columns component's columns, so a picture layer has the library's 480 px copy beside it by width, and a blurred one is sent only that (`backgroundSources()`); in the page's first row it loads at once, elsewhere as it comes near.
The small copy is never saved. **Carried with its part:** a template or a part copied to another store takes its picture layers through `mapTemplateMedia()` (the copy's address, without the old small copy); a layer whose picture cannot be copied is left out and the other layers stay, and `leftoverStorageUrls()` still finds nothing of the other store's. **Nothing an owner types becomes CSS or markup:** the address is checked as a web address by the schema and again when drawn (`layerDraw()`
leaves out anything that is not `http(s)`), and only its place and size reach the page as custom properties. The page check does not measure text over it, as with every layer and as a background picture is only warned about. **The platform:** the same builder and renderer, so Kaizena Flow's own pages have it in the same change. No migration. **Not done:** a picture in a ready-made backdrop (they stay files-free), per screen size placing, and the AI manager's design tools adding one.
