# D871: A form's own report: views counted per form and day (email plan M4.3)

Date: 2026-11-23

**A form's own report: how often each saved form is seen, and what share answer it (23 November 2026, email plan M4.3).** The forms
list said how many responses a form had, but not how many people saw it, so a popup that annoys a thousand visitors to win two sign-ups
looked the same as one that wins two of ten.

- `commerce.form_view_counts` keeps one counter per form and day in its store's time zone (`PLATFORM_TIME_ZONE` for Kaizena Flow's own
  forms), nothing about who saw it: not personal data, no consent needed, as a broadcast's link counts are not (D297).
- `SiteForm` sends one beacon (`/api/forms/seen`, `navigator.sendBeacon`, no cookie or storage) per page load when half of a saved form
  first comes into view, which is also when its popup opens. A page's own form (a block id) is not counted.
- `recordFormSeen()` is the only writer: a robot or a visitor sending Global Privacy Control or Do Not Track is not counted, as a page
  view is not (D152); only an active form of the store named. The route answers an empty 204 whether counted or not.
- The forms list (a store's and the platform's) says per form: *Seen 1,204 times in 30 days, 3.2 % answered*, answers being a message
  form's responses and a sign-up form's sign-ups (subscribers with the form's id, D850).
- A store copy starts its counts at nothing; the whole-store file keeps them as a record.

Idle cost: none until a saved form is on a page; then one small insert or update per page load that shows it, and one statement on the
forms list.
