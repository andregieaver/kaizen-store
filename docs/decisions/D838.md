# D838: Built pages' links stay in the app for an address of the app

**Built pages' links stay in the app for an address of the app (bloat review step 2.7; 20 November 2026).** A built page's product
tile (its title, button and both picture links), button, dual button, picture, animation, icon-list item and column link go
through `BlockLink` (`src/components/block-link.tsx`). An address of the app is `PolicyLink` with the intent policy (D826), so a
press draws the next page without a new document. A pay path (`isPayPath()`, `docs/pci.md`), a file, `/api/`, `/admin`,
`/auth/`, `/oauth/`, `/r/`, another site, a hash, a new tab, an outside custom item and the builder's canvas stay plain anchors,
every attribute unchanged. Measured locally on the phone home's first tile: 1 document and 29 requests before (the brief said 42),
0 documents and 7 to 8 after (held at 8, `e2e/prefetch.spec.ts`); a block's link to the cart is 1 document, and
`pay-routes.graph.test.ts` holds that a built page never client-navigates to a pay path. On production after the deploy: a tile
pressed on the demo store's home made 0 documents and 3 requests; a builder button on kaizenaflow.com's front page opened
`/page-builder` with 0 documents and 7 requests. Kaizena Flow's pages are built pages, so the platform has it in the same change.
The combined listing's swatch links are left to step 2.8. Commit c351d3ab5.
