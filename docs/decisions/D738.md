# D738: Forms F6

**Forms F6: hidden fields from the address, file uploads and payments in a form (`docs/forms.md` 15; 17 November 2026).** The three things D705's plan left for later, asked for by the owner, each shipped on its own, then the AI manager's tools and its words for *New form* with one eval run. **F6a, hidden fields** (15.1, built): a form's question of kind `hidden` is never asked; it keeps the value of one part of the address the form is sent on (`utm_campaign` unless the owner names another) as an
answer named by the owner, read on the server from the sent path by `fromAddress()`, never from what the browser sends, and left out when the address has none. Only the page the form is on counts: carrying a campaign across pages would need the first visit, kept only with statistics consent (D303). A page's own email form has no such kind.
Kaizena Flow's own forms have it in the same change. **F6b, file uploads** (15.2, built): a `file` question takes one picture or PDF; with no malware scanner at hand, a picture is drawn again in the browser as a WebP of at most 2,000 pixels and checked from its bytes, a PDF is refused if it or its opened streams name a script, launch or file of its own; kept in the private bucket `form-files`, downloaded only as an attachment by those who may read customers, swept within a day of its response.
**F6c, payments** (15.3, built; the owner chose a real order paid at once): a store's message form names one of its products (`forms.payment_variant_id`); after sending, the response is kept, the product goes into the visitor's cart in the page's market and they go on to the store's own checkout, so price, VAT, right of withdrawal, terms, order number, invoice and books are any order's (a typed amount on a custom line would have no right of withdrawal for a private buyer); `form_payments` links
the response to its cart, and the response shows *Paid · order n* once that cart's order is paid. Kaizena Flow's own forms take no payments: it sells only plans.
