# D217: The admin's colours are roles set by a scheme; `meadow` is the first

**The admin's colours are roles set by a scheme; `meadow` is the first (`docs/admin-style.md`, step 1).** A scheme is a stylesheet `src/app/admin/schemes/{id}.css` that sets every role of `ADMIN_ROLES` (`src/lib/admin-schemes.ts`: surfaces and text, brand and actions, five signals with `-solid`, `-text`, `-soft`, `-line`, charts and tones, two shadows) in light and dark, scoped to `html[data-admin]` and `[data-admin-scheme]` (the default also to an `<html>` naming none).
`admin.css` reads roles only and maps the older names onto them (`--background` the card, `--surface` sunken, `--border` line, `--foreground` ink, the main action `--brand` with `--brand-ink` words, since white on the meadow green would be 1.86:1); so pages in the existing tokens change look untouched. Cards lose their shadow and keep a hairline for now; headings are lighter; the header's gradient line goes; the current sidebar item is the brand's tint.
`admin-schemes.test.ts` holds every registered scheme to `CONTRAST_RULES` (text 4.5:1, focus 3:1) in both modes and `admin.css` to no colour of its own. No migration.
