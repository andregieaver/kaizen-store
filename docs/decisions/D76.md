# D76: AI product texts

**AI product texts (Phase 2, S4): in the product editor, staff can ask the store's text model to write a description, improve one, write search texts, or translate the main language's texts into another; the suggestion is shown to edit, is used only when staff copy it in, and is published only when they save the product.
It cannot be copied in while the claims filter finds generic environmental claims, false urgency or scarcity, best- or lowest-price claims, amounts of money or stock levels in it.** The panel ("Write with AI", `AiWriter`) shows when the store's AI has a text model (`EditorContext.aiWriting`), beside each language's texts.
The model is given only the product's own words from the editor (title, description, search texts, category and tag names, options; never prices, stock or anything outside the product) and told not to add facts, prices, stock, environmental claims, urgency or comparisons, and to write plain text (`writingMessages()`, `src/lib/product-writing.ts`); its answer is cleaned to plain text within each field's limit (`cleanWritten()`).
The claims filter (`findClaims()`, `src/lib/claims.ts`) matches whole words with their endings in Norwegian, Swedish, Danish, English and German, and runs again in the browser as staff edit a suggestion. Each suggestion is written to the audit log (`product.ai_suggested`: who, which product, what kind, which model), never its text.

Asked for by the owner ("continue with S4"). Human approval by construction: the model never writes to a product, and nothing it suggests is live until staff save. The Empowering Consumers Directive applies from 27 September 2026 and bans generic environmental claims without recognised excellent environmental performance, which a text model cannot know, so the filter blocks them in AI copy rather than warning; it lists, and staff decide (a colour called "grønn" is flagged too).
Texts edited and published under the store's editorial responsibility need no AI label (AI Act Art. 50(4)). Attributes, FAQ blocks and review summaries in the plan wait for fields to hold them; the filter is ready for them.
