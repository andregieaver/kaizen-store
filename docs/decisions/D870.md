# D870: The AI social media manager: adviser, copy and graphics, posting; a paid extension

**The AI social media manager: adviser, copy and graphics, posting; a paid extension (the owner, 23 November 2026; plan in
`docs/social-manager.md`).** Kaizena Flow gets an AI social media manager as a new `social` area of the AI manager and the autopilot
(goals, plans, gates, budgets and reports reused), built in three phases: an adviser that reads the store's goals and figures and each
channel's posts and insights and gives a status-quo report, recommendations as plan steps, a content calendar and a weekly review;
copy and graphics, where captions come from the brand-aware writer through `findClaims()` and `findAvoided()` and pictures are composed
from the real product photo, a brand template and text and prices set by code (the picture model only makes backgrounds, scenes and
reframes; a made picture of a product is never posted); and posting on a schedule to Instagram and Facebook, then Pinterest, TikTok
and LinkedIn (X left out). It optimises for revenue and margin per post: every link it makes is tagged and attributed to visits, carts
and orders. Every post is approved by a person at first; the owner's toggle *Post on its own within my rules* (off by default, per
store and for the platform, audited) lets the autopilot publish what passes the rules, and anything outside them still waits for a yes.
It is a paid extension (the sixth of D748), bought on its own or granted by a plan; the platform has it for Kaizena Flow's own
accounts in the same change. Models: Claude Opus 5.5 for strategy and reviews, Claude Haiku 5.5 for sorting and extraction, Claude
Sonnet 5.5 for captions and the vision check, the AI settings' picture model for scenes, code for every figure and the schedule.
Comments and DMs, paid boosting and generative video are later steps. Not built yet; the price and plan grants are settled when S1.6
starts.


**Amended 25 November 2026 (the owner):** the post creator comes first, for posting by hand: a caption, hashtags, the price line set by code and
the tagged link per channel, to copy (`/admin/{store}/social`, `src/server/social-posts.ts`, C1), its pictures next (C2); then the adviser, then
posting by itself. Until the extension exists (S1.6) the creator is open to every store, its AI metered as any feature's. Idle cost: one table
(`social_posts`) and one admin link (Marketing, Channels); no job, and no read until the page is opened. S1.2 (25 November): one table (`social_post_insights`) and one
dispatcher job (`socialInsights`), which reads nothing for a store without an Instagram account that granted insights.
