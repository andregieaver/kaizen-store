# D440: The Admin API's breadth and more webhook topics

**The Admin API's breadth and more webhook topics (wave 14 unit C, `docs/wave-14-developer-platform.md` C1 to C4), built a slice at a time on unit A's registry; C1 to C4 built.** *Every operation:* an entry of `ADMIN_API` (each slice's in its own file, `src/lib/admin-api-catalogue.ts`, spread in; shared shapes in `admin-api-shapes.ts`) with the staff `permission` (D158) its screen asks and, where it has one, the store `feature` (D178): `/api/admin/v1` refuses a permission the token's owner does
not hold now as `forbidden` (`memberCan()`, so a store that is not open allows no change, as the admin) and a feature off as the new code `feature_off` (403), before the handler runs; both are in the OpenAPI document (`x-permission`, `x-feature`). Handlers share `src/server/admin-api-kit.ts`.
Every list takes `?updated_after=` (an order's change time is `ORDER_UPDATED`: placed, or its timeline's, parcels' and tags' latest), and answers gained `updated_at` (orders, customers, discount codes); a page's cursor keeps its row's time to the microsecond (`cursorAt()`), since rows made in one statement shared a millisecond and the next page skipped them. *Powers:* a scope that is no read or write of an area (`products:publish`, `orders:refund`, `gift_cards:write`, `content:publish`,
`webhooks:write`) is what the AI manager asks the owner's yes for; it is a token scope and an OAuth scope (`POWER_SCOPES`, resting on a permission, offered on the consent screen only under *Read and change*, never a key of a role or of a grant's cap), worded on the token screen, the consent screen and in the document (`SCOPE_POWERS`). **C1, the catalogue (contract 1.1):** categories and tags (list, get, create, change, delete through `createTerm()`/`updateTerm()`/`deleteTerm()`; a product put in
or out through `updateProductTool()`, never a category filled by rules); pictures through `POST /media/uploads` (a row of the new `commerce.api_uploads`, written only by `src/server/api-uploads.ts`, and a one-time signed address in the private `imports` bucket, two hours) and `POST /media` (claimed once, the file read from its bytes as a JPEG, PNG, WebP or AVIF, at most 20 MB, shrunk by `shrinkPicture()` as the admin's browser shrinks uploads, counted against storage, kept by `storePicture()`,
staff's alt by `describeMedia()`; the waiting file goes then, and rows a day after they end with `pruneDataJobs()`); `GET /media`; a product's gallery read, added to, ordered and taken from through `updateProductTool()` (pictures only; videos and 3D models keep their places); publish, unpublish (`setProductStatusTool()`), archive and bring back (`setArchived()`) with `products:publish`, which also lets `PATCH /products/{id}` and a gallery, category or tag change reach a product on the site
(without it, `gated` as before); price lists' fixed prices read and set in minor units through `setPrices()`.
Topics `inventory.updated` (a variant's stock on hand moved, queued by a trigger on `inventory_levels` and held to the next whole minute by `queue_integration_event()`, so a variant's moves within it are one delivery built as the stock is then) and `product.archived` (the product trigger, as its status becomes archived), both endpoints only, typed in `WEBHOOK_TOPICS` (subject `inventory`) and workflow triggers with their fields. **C2, orders (contract 1.2):** draft orders and requests for a
quote listed (`?quotes=true`), read with their total and what blocks a send, made (`createDraft()`, then saved), changed against their `version` through the editor's own state (`formFromDraft()` with the API's change laid over it, `inputFromForm()`, `saveDraft()`; lines' prices by SKU as D437's tool), sent by `sendDraft()` (the pay link emailed, or answered once with `create_link`) and a request declined by `declineQuote()`; refunds only through `refundOrder()` with the power `orders:refund` (to
the payment or as store credit, whose code is never answered), and an order's refunds read; returns listed, read, approved, declined and received through the returns page's steps; notes (`addOrderNote()`, named `API: {token}`) and tags (`changeOrderTags()`); an order waiting for payment cancelled by the new `cancelUnpaidByStaff()` (its payment pages closed first, as *Reopen* closes them; a weekly delivery through `cancelOrder()`).
Topics `order.created` (an order made, paid or not), `order.updated` (an edit applied, `edited_at`), `fulfilment.created` and `fulfilment.updated` (a parcel recorded, its tracking moved on or undone), `refund.created` (money or store credit that went through; the bookkeeping kinds are no refund), `return.updated` (a status), `quote.requested` and `draft.sent`, each queued by a trigger where it happens, copied history never; subjects `fulfilment`, `refund` and `draft`. **C3, customers and
marketing (contract 1.3):** customers made and changed through `createCustomer()`/`updateCustomer()` (the form's whole state from `staffFormValues()` with the change laid over it) and found by email (`?email=` on the list); a customer's store credit (gift cards from refunds, D364) and bonus credits (`customerBonus()`) read; segments read with how many they hold (`evaluateSegment()`), never who; a discount code read, changed and switched off through `saveDiscount()` over its stored form (what it
reaches and who may use it untouched; a loyalty reward's code refused); gift cards listed, read, issued (`issueGiftCard()`) and deactivated (`deactivateGiftCard()`) with the power `gift_cards:write`; subscriptions read (never their manage link), paused and cancelled through `changeSubscription()` as staff, the subscriber emailed.
What is shown once (a gift card's code, a draft's pay link) is answered once and never kept for a replay (`ApiAnswer.replay`: only it is kept under the `Idempotency-Key`).
Topics `customer.updated` (a customer's own details, by a trigger), `customer.erased` (queued by `eraseSubject()` in its transaction; the account's id alone, built without reading anything), `subscription.renewed` (a renewal order paid), `subscription.payment_failed` (past due), `gift_card.issued` (never its code) and `review.created` (a buyer's, not an import's); subjects `erasure`, `gift_card` and `review`. **C4, content, the store and operations (contract 1.4):** new scopes `content:read`,
`content:write`, `content:publish`, `settings:read`, `reports:read` and `webhooks:write` (a token may hold every scope: `api_tokens_scopes` up to 40); pages and blog articles read, written as drafts through `savePage()` and published with `content:publish` (the checker's blocking issues acknowledged by id, else `conflict` naming them); content types and entries read, entries written through `saveEntry()` and published through `publishEntry()`; menus and the store's settings read (markets,
currencies, languages, shipping rates and methods, tax profile, price display); saved reports listed and run by `runReport()`, nothing written; the owner's webhook endpoints listed, added (the secret answered once), changed, switched, removed, their deliveries read and sent again through the Integrations page's writers with `webhooks:write` (an `owner` permission); the product file of the Data page exported (inline up to 2,000 rows, else a job), imported through a one-time upload address,
checked (the dry run), applied, polled and downloaded through the pipeline's own steps.
Left in the admin, by decision: order and customer files (owner-only, personal data) and the other imports; settings are read, never written. **The review (contract 1.4):** a scope allows exactly what its words say, and a token made before D440 gains no change it could not make (it reads what D440 added to its own area, as the scope's words now say: `orders:read` draft orders, returns and subscriptions, `customers:read` store credit, bonus credits and segments, `products:read` categories, tags,
media and price lists; data of the same kind the owner already shared with it, so no new consent is asked): every operation of D355/D438 keeps its scope (`SCOPES_BEFORE_D440`), every write D440 added has a scope no older token holds, and what an owner tool asks the owner's yes for (`public`, `send`, `spend`) is a power (`API_POWERS`, under *Customise* on the token screen; no OAuth level grants one, so the consent screen no longer names them).
Scopes: `products:read` (products, categories and tags, media and galleries, price lists, the product file's jobs), `products:write` (make products, change drafts), `products:manage` (make categories and tags, upload pictures, import the product file as drafts, a draft's categories, tags and gallery), `inventory:read`/`:write`, `orders:read` (orders, documents, refunds, notes, drafts and quotes, returns, subscriptions), `orders:write` (parcels with tracking), `orders:manage` (draft orders made
and changed, notes and tags), `customers:read` (customers, store credit, bonus credits, segments), `customers:write` (tags), `customers:manage` (add, change name, phone, address, company), `discounts:read`, `discounts:write` (make codes), `checkout:read`/`:write`, `content:read`, `content:write` (drafts), `settings:read`, `reports:read`; powers `products:publish` (publish, unpublish, archive, unarchive, a product on the site, categories and tags renamed or deleted, price lists' prices, an import
that does any of these), `orders:send` (drafts sent, quotes declined, returns approved, declined and received, subscriptions paused and cancelled: each emails the customer), `orders:refund` (refunds, an unpaid order cancelled), `customers:email` (the email a customer signs in with), `discounts:publish` (codes changed and switched off), `gift_cards:write`, `content:publish` (publishing, a published page's or entry's address, pages moved under a never-published one), `webhooks:write`.
Every scope of an area reads it (`grants()`).
Also: an import by a token without `products:publish` is checked drafts only (`ImportOptions.draftsOnly`, `product.on_site`), counted (`dry.onShop`) and refused at apply (cancelled); a draft carrying `globalEdits` is refused; an `Idempotency-Key` stands for the method, whole path and body; picture uploads have their own private bucket `api-uploads` (picture types, 20 MiB, the API's limit) and a 50-megapixel limit read before decoding; upload addresses are never replayed; an erasure blanks the
person's deliveries (`integration_deliveries.erased_at`) in its transaction and they are never resent; `inventory.levels` pages by variant and location; the inventory dedup has a partial index; the new topics' money readers use `typedRow()`. The docs say what a store that is not open keeps (orders, customers, reports, the owner's pages) and which lists take `?updated_after=`.
