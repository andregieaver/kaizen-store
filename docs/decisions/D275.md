# D275: A theme has its own content; demo stores become live stores

**A theme has its own content; demo stores become live stores (step 1 of `docs/themes-content-and-demo-stores.md`).** Every look of a theme (its own and each preset) is shown on one store template, the theme's own content (`store_starters.design_preset_id`, one per theme): its preview, its photographs and every new store started from it read it, never a demo store chosen per look and never the Standard store.
Kaizena Flow's eight themes take one content pack each (Calm Fashion, Hearth Food, Signal Electronics, Fjord Home, Orbit Astronomy, Atelier Ceramics, Arcade Neon, Botanica Plants; Beauty and Services are left for demo stores), linked by a migration and by both syncs.
Any other theme gets a copy of published content (starting content, another theme's, a demo store's; never an owner's store) made on its new **Content** tab or when it is made; it is edited in its own store admin and published with the theme. The per-look demo store choice (`ThemeListing.pack`, `presetPacks`, the theme's Demo stores card) is gone, and the Themes page's demo stores card is *Starting content*.
Steps 2 to 4 (the theme's page, D276; demo stores at `{address}.kaizenaflow.com`; publishing a demo store's look) follow.

Date: 2026-10-18

Asked for by the owner: themes should show off their layouts, fonts, text and images regardless of demo stores, and new stores start with what the theme showed (their agreement to the spec, 18 October). Migration versions: `20261018120218_theme_content`, `20261018120240_theme_content_launch`.
