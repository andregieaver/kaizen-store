# D220: The platform and the owner's level in the `rail` layout too

**The platform and the owner's level in the `rail` layout too (`docs/admin-style.md` section 8, step 4).** `LEVEL_LAYOUTS` gives `platform` and `control` the rail. The platform's sections are `platformTabs()` (`src/lib/platform-nav.ts`, moved out of its layout so they can be tested), with the waiting access requests and Kaizen's own support conversations on their tiles and Settings at the foot; the owner's are `ownerTabs()` with Account at the foot (`foot`).
The sidebars of sections with pages of their own (the platform's Website, Stores, Plans, Support, Settings; the owner's Account) stay beside the rail. Hosting, one page with no sections, stays `classic`.
