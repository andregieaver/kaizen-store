# D383: Stock transfers, purchase orders, cost history, locations kept out of online orders

**Stock transfers, purchase orders, cost history, locations kept out of online orders (wave 11 unit D1, `docs/wave-11-core-and-psi.md` 2.D): built.** A transfer (`commerce.stock_transfers`, lines in `stock_transfer_lines`) is written only by `src/server/stock-transfers.ts`: a draft from SKUs of the store's own shipped goods and two of its active locations; *Send* takes each line's units off the origin, all or none, never the units live checkouts hold there (a short line refuses the send and says
how many are free), as `transfer_out` movements naming the transfer (`job_id`); *Count in* adds what arrived at the destination in parts as `transfer_in`, a figure above what is to come counting what is to come; *Cancel* on a sent transfer puts what was not counted in back at the origin. Units on their way (`incomingStock()`) are shown as incoming on the Locations page and are never for sale.
A purchase order (`commerce.purchase_orders`, `purchase_order_lines`) is written only by `src/server/purchase-orders.ts`: supplier, reference, the currency it bills in, where the stock arrives, an expected date and each line's units and unit cost (read by `parsePrice()`), fixed once marked as ordered; counting in adds `received` movements naming the order and sets each received variant's `cost_minor` to the order's unit cost converted into the main currency at the store's rates (a currency with
no rate leaves the cost and says how many, `costsKept`). Every change of a variant's cost, from any writer, is a row of `commerce.variant_cost_history` written by the trigger `variant_cost_keep()` (the transaction's `kaizen.cost_source` names a purchase order, its currency and its own figure; anything else reads `editor`), never changed or deleted; order lines keep the cost they froze (D152).
The database moves both documents only forward (`stock_documents_rules()`: draft to sent or ordered or cancelled, open to received or cancelled; a closed one stays as it closed; only a draft is deleted) and their lines only as a draft or, once out, their `received` up (`stock_document_lines_rules()`). `products:write` changes them, `products:read` reads them (Inventory → Transfers, Purchase orders); each change is audited without a list of SKUs.
A location may be kept out of online orders (`inventory_locations.fulfils_online`, the owner's, `setFulfilsOnline()`): `variant_availability` and `allocate()`'s locations leave it out, its stock is still counted, moved and received at, and one active location taking online orders always stays (deactivating the last one is refused too); store copies keep the setting. The AI manager reads `list_transfers` and `list_purchase_orders`.
