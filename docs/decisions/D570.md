# D570: The phone menu button's menu

**The phone menu button's menu: its type, edge and background (11 November 2026, `src/lib/menu-drawer.ts`, `MenuDrawer` on `SiteBlock.drawer`, `MobileMenu`, `MenuDrawerFields`).** A header's menu button now says how the phone menu it opens looks: a side panel (the standard), the whole screen, a panel dropping under the header or a floating card; from the left (the standard), right, top or bottom (a dropdown always from the top); on a background of its own, a column's (colour, picture or
gradient, backdrops, layers and hue, D534; no video, no effects), with frosted glass where what is behind is blurred (D86). The menu stays one dialog per page: the button carries its kind and edge on `data-menu-type`/`data-menu-side` and its background as hidden markup (`data-menu-look`) that the menu copies behind its links as it opens, so its pictures load only then and the bottom bar's Menu and the standard header open it as before.
Text over the background reads in its colour set (`kz-on-light`/`dark`, D390). A swipe back towards the edge closes it; up or down only from its top bar, so the links still scroll. Kaizena Flow's own header has the same component, so the platform has it in the same change. *Rejected:* a setting per store for the menu (the button that opens it is where the owner looks for it); videos behind the menu (as a column's). No migration.
