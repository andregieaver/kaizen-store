# D335: Markets in depth

**Markets in depth (wave 7 unit A, `docs/wave-7-storefront.md` section 3); built.** What a store sells in each country is apart from its price: `commerce.market_availability` switches a product or a variant off in one market without touching `commerce.prices`, read through `commerce.current_prices` (which leaves such a variant out, so every shopper-facing read treats it as not sold there) while the admin's price reads use `commerce.kept_prices`.
A market may take its prices from another by a standing rule (`commerce.market_price_rules`: base market, percentage, before or with VAT, rounding step and ending), worked out only by `derivedPrice()` and written only by `src/server/market-prices.ts` through `commerce.set_price` with `prices.derived`, so the 30-day reference stays genuine; a price typed by hand is fixed and overrides the rule.

Date: 2026-10-27

*Why:* `international.market-specific-catalogs` and `international.fixed-per-market-prices-and-adjustment-rules`: taking a product out of one country lost its price, and every country's prices were typed by hand. The wave's decisions 1 to 6 are for later units; decision 7 (before or with VAT) is a choice per rule.
Built in three steps (A1 availability, A2 price rules, A3 the bulk bar's *Sell in* / *Stop selling in*, the tracker and an e2e); as built: a rule between two currencies needs both rates (`rateProblem()`) and a missing rate never ends a rule's prices.
