# D807: Invoices' and credit notes' PDFs are made ahead: the dispatcher asks the PDF route when a document waits

**Invoices' and credit notes' PDFs are made ahead: the dispatcher asks the PDF route when a document waits (19 November 2026).** The
owner chose on 12 November (bloat review step 3.7) that PDFs are made ahead by the scheduled job, so the Waiting tab's "tried again
within a few minutes" is true; but nothing called `/api/cron/document-pdfs`, so an invoice's PDF was made only on its first download,
and step 3.7 waits for step 3.1's one-minute tick (`commerce.cron_due()`), not yet built. The owner asked for it now. A new job of the
five-minute dispatcher, `documentPdfs` (`runDocumentPdfAsk()`, `src/server/document-pdf-ask.ts`, no Chromium), asks one question:
whether an invoice or credit note waits for its PDF as `pdfJob()` picks one (no file, not anonymised, fewer than `PDF_ATTEMPTS`
failures, none in the last five minutes) or a signed contract waits for its seal as `signedPdfJob()` picks one; only then does it ask
the PDF route (with the cron bearer, waiting up to 110 s). A document that failed five times is not asked for again until *Try again*.
The contracts' job (D795) keeps its upkeep and hands its seal question to this job, so one job asks the route for all three kinds.
Idle cost: one statement and no rows a run (`dispatcher.document-pdfs` in the query budget); the dispatcher holds 80 jobs. No
migration: step 3.7's two partial indexes and the move into `cron_due()` come with step 3.1; the question stops at the first waiting
document, and the tables are small. Step 3.7's other parts (a copied AI picture keeping its mark, the backfill's retirement) are not
done here. Tests: `invoice-documents.int.test.ts` (an issued invoice waits; a failure a minute ago and five failures do not; *Try again*
makes it wait again; made, it waits no more) and `contract-signing.int.test.ts` (a seal waiting has the route asked).

Date: 2026-11-19
