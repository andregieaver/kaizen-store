# D462: A customer's timeline

**A customer's timeline (S1 of `docs/own-books-and-sales.md`; `src/lib/customer-timeline.ts`, `src/server/customer-timeline.ts`, `CustomerTimeline`).** A person's page and a company's page show what happened with them as one list, newest first under the days they fell on in the store's time zone: orders (as the page's order list has them, copied history included), quote requests (asked, sent, declined), support conversations, emails sent and not delivered, staff's notes, returns and withdrawals,
buyers' reviews, bookings, and the Work invoices (issued and paid) of a Work client linked to the customer or the company. A company's is its people's, each naming the person, and its own Work invoices. Nothing is stored: one read per kind (`READERS`), through `typedRows()`, at most one row more than the page shows, merged by `mergeTimeline()`.
A kind is read only for a viewer who may open the page it comes from (`visibleKinds()`): orders, emails and notes with `customers:read`, as the customer page already showed them; quotes, returns and withdrawals with `orders:read`; conversations with `support:read`; reviews with `products:read` and Reviews on; bookings with `bookings:read` and appointments or bookings on; Work invoices with `settings:read` and Work on; an email links to its own page only with `orders:read`.
As on every customer screen, an order whose personal data is restricted or anonymised (D162) is left out with what hangs on it, and so is an anonymised conversation. Homer sees the latest 12 and *Show everything* (up to 200, `?full=1`); the filter by kind (`?show=`) appears once two kinds are there, as plain links, so the page needs no JavaScript for it. What someone typed (a note, a quote request's message) is shown as text on one line of at most 140 characters.
The timeline replaces the customer page's list of emails.
