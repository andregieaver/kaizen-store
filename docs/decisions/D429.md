# D429: Prices with or without decimals, and the owner's words around a product's price

**Prices with or without decimals, and the owner's words around a product's price.** A store chooses whether whole amounts show their decimals (`stores.price_decimals`: `always`, the default and what every store had, or `hide_whole`: 499 kr, while 499,50 kr keeps its decimals), and each country may differ (`markets.price_decimals`, null for the store's). It is never a rounding: `formatMoney(minor, currency, locale, style)` leaves off only zeros, so what is shown is what is charged.
Every amount a shopper sees follows the market's style (`Market.priceStyle`, built by `localized()` from the store's row): product pages, cards and grids, search, the phone's bar, the cart, checkout, the account pages, the chat's cards and answers, and the store's emails to shoppers (with the notifications' preview); invoices, credit notes, UBL, tax reports, feeds and the admin keep their decimals.
The owner's words before and after a product's price (`stores.price_words` by language, a language without its own taking the main language's; a product may follow them, show none or have its own, `products.price_words`) are put around the price by the catalogue's reads (`styledPrice()`), so every place that shows a product's price shows them, and never beside a cart line, a total, the 30-day reference, an invoice or a feed; the automatic labels (VAT, "From") stay.
A word is at most 24 characters of plain text with no percentage and nothing `findClaims()` finds (`priceWordProblem()`, checked in the browser, by the server and, for length and tags, by the database). One settings page, *Prices and quantity* (`/admin/{store}/settings/prices`): Homer's decimals and the main language's words with a preview; each country's decimals and the other languages' words in the Advanced fold; a product's own words in its editor's Advanced fold.
A store copy (D129) and a store made from a template (D175) bring the settings and a product's words. Migrations: `20261103005650_price_display` and `20261103013420_price_display_copy` (custom: `duplicate_store()` and `clone_starter_setup()` copy the new columns; `20261103022656_price_words_template_copy`, with D431, makes `clone_store()` carry a product's words too).

Asked for by the owner, "with the most degree of flexibility for the store owner": Norwegian shops write "499,-" or "Kun 499 kr" and many hide ",00". *Why never rounded:* a price shown rounded would differ from what is charged, which consumer law forbids; leaving off zeros changes nothing. *Why per country:* a store selling in Norway and Germany may write 499 kr there and 49,90 € here. *Why the words only around a product's price:* a total or a line is a sum, and words such as "Kun" beside it
would read as a claim about the sum; the 30-day reference must stay a plain figure (`prior_30d_minor`, the Omnibus rule). *Why the claims filter:* "Beste pris" or "Kun i dag" before a price is the same claim the filter keeps out of AI texts (D76).
