# D271: A content pack's pictures are made by a job, not by the open page

**A content pack's pictures are made by a job, not by the open page.** *Make the pictures* (and *Make every picture* for every pack that lacks some) only asks: `askForPackPictures()` records who asked and when (`store_starters.pictures_wanted_by`, `pictures_wanted_at`), and the job `runPackPictures()` makes them in that person's name (asked again each time whether they are still a platform admin), one per pack at a time through `makeNextPackPicture()`, two packs at most at once
(`picture_claimed_until`, taken under an advisory lock, held four minutes, longer than the picture model's 150 seconds and the placing), starting no picture after two minutes of a run. It runs every minute from Supabase's scheduler (`/api/cron/pack-pictures`, `maxDuration` 300, answering at once and working in `after()`) and at once after the press.
A failure holds the pack two minutes before the next try; after `PICTURE_TRIES` (3) in a row the pack is no longer asked for and the page says why (`picture_problem`), and asking again starts over. *Stop* clears the wish; a picture being made is finished and kept. The page shows *Making the pictures: n of m made. You can leave this page.* and looks again every 15 seconds while one is asked for (`PackPicturesWatch`).

Date: 2026-10-17

A hundred pictures a minute or two each cannot depend on a page staying open.
