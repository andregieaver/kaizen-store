# D41: A store connects Zapier or Make on its Integrations page, and Kaizen sends them its events by webhook

**A store connects Zapier or Make on its Integrations page, and Kaizen sends them its events by webhook.** The page (under Store in the sidebar) lists every integration: Zapier and Make to set up, Tripletex as coming soon.
For each, the owner pastes the webhook address the service gives, chooses the events to send (order paid, sent, cancelled or refunded; new customer account; subscription started or cancelled), switches it on, and can send a test (the latest order, or a sample) to see it arrive.
Only the services' own hook addresses are taken (https on hooks.zapier.com, or hook.*.make.com), so a store cannot point Kaizen at any other server; the address is kept encrypted, and the page shows only its host and last four characters. Events are queued by the database in the same transaction as what happened, so none is missed; every minute a scheduled job sends what is due, the same content each try, trying again after 1 and 5 minutes, half an hour, 2 and 6 hours before giving up.
The page shows what was sent in the last 30 days and how it went; older deliveries are removed. Switching off stops new events; disconnecting also drops those waiting. Only owners can connect, change or disconnect.

Date: 2026-09-25

Asked for by the owner. Sending customer data to a service makes it the store's processor, so the page reminds the owner to have a data processing agreement with it. Webhooks need no app review at Zapier or Make; native apps in their directories can follow. Saving needs `SETTINGS_ENCRYPTION_KEY`, as payment settings do.
