# D51: A content grid shows pages or a store's products as tiles

**A content grid shows pages or a store's products as tiles.** A component (Components tab) whose General tab chooses what it shows: Kaizen's published pages, or a store's active products priced in one of its markets (the market sets currency, VAT-inclusive prices and language; the template store is offered first); only items in chosen categories (a category includes its subcategories) and tags (with both, an item needs one of each; none chosen shows all); the order (newest, oldest, title, or
price for products) and how many (up to 48); columns on phones (1–2), tablets (1–4) and computers (1–6); which parts each tile has (picture, heading, excerpt, price for products, button), the button's text (else Read more or View product in the content's language) and a text for when nothing matches.
The Style tab styles the tiles like the components they resemble: picture crop (landscape unless chosen), heading level (H2–H6) and size, excerpt lines, button look, tile background, padding, border, corners and shadow, and the space between tiles; plus the grid's own spacing and frame. A grid of pages leaves out the page it is on.
Items are looked up on the server and cached with their content's tags (pages, catalogue, categories and tags); the canvas asks for them again when what the grid shows changes. Tile buttons are named with their item ("Read more: …").

Asked for by the owner. Built for the kinds of content to come: the source is a kind plus, for products, a store and market, so articles and a store's own pages slot in; a store's page builder will default to its own store. Still page JSON only: no migration.
