# D180: Text colour and opacity in the page builder

**Text colour and opacity in the page builder.** `docs/text-colour.md` is the contract and the record of what was built.
Every kind of text of every component, row and column has a colour (`#rrggbb`) and an opacity (0–100, solid unless set) in its Typography group, per screen size like D179's settings, written by the part stylesheet as `:where()` rules with `!important` (as the inline styles they replace were) and passed down from rows and columns; the panel opens with Colour, the one colour field (`ColorField`: picker, hex, ×, opacity slider, the theme's colours as swatches, Kaizen's own on its pages).
One helper writes any colour with an opacity (`colourCss()`, `src/lib/colour.ts`), backgrounds included. The text colours kept on their own before (a heading's, a button's, each of a dual button's two, a grid's and a form's button's) are folded in on read; a header overlay's text colour, a social link's custom colour and an icon list's icon colour stay (not a text's colour).
Rich text has a colour mark (`textStyle`, kept only as `#rrggbb` and 0–100, drawn as a style); inline markup stays classes only. The accessibility check blends a see-through colour over its background before the contrast ratio, at every screen size, inheriting a row's or column's colour, and checks rich text's marks and dual buttons.
The parity check (`e2e/responsive-parity.spec.ts`, now with `color` and `opacity` and every old text colour) against a build of the commit before: 40 page widths, 7,147 elements, 300,174 values, no difference.
