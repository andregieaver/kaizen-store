# D288: What a payment was, Stripe's risk and disputes

**What a payment was, Stripe's risk and disputes (wave 4 unit A).** `docs/wave-4-payments.md` 3 is the contract.
A captured Stripe payment's charge is read once through the store's connected account (`readPaymentFacts()` in `after()` the payment, and the five-minute job for any of the last day still unread) and its method, wallet, card brand, Stripe's risk level and score and the charge id are kept on `commerce.payments` as Stripe gave them (`factsOfCharge()`); nothing is guessed, an unread payment is *Unknown*.
The order page has a *Payment* card (the method, *Stripe: elevated risk* or *highest risk* with the score, each dispute with its reason, status, evidence deadline in the store's time zone and a link to the dispute in the store's Stripe Dashboard); the list shows *Disputed* and risk badges and filters by them (`pay=disputed`, `pay=elevated_risk`); an automation rule may hold an order by Stripe's risk (condition `risk`, never met while unread, the rule's facts read the charge first).
Disputes are `commerce.payment_disputes`, written only by `applyStripeDispute()` from the connected account's `charge.dispute.*` events (added to `WEBHOOK_EVENTS`, so older endpoints get them through `ensureSubscriptionEvents()`), one row per dispute, its identity fixed and a closed dispute kept as it closed by a trigger, never deleted; a dispute on a payment the store does not have changes nothing.
Staff are emailed once when one opens and once three days before its deadline (staff alert event `dispute`, owners by default and added to stores that saved their choices). A lost dispute is money out in the Finance bridge (*Lost disputes*, without VAT, with the fee where known, in the period it closed), never a refund. The Finance page lists *Payments by method* (with VAT, rows adding up to the money received, CSV `finance.payment_methods`).
The five-minute job's reported `tracking` and `summaries` were the support summaries' and actions' results by a misaligned list; corrected.
