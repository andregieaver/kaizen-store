# D495: An invoice brought in twice kept once, and old bank statements settling old invoices

**An invoice brought in twice kept once, and old bank statements settling old invoices (8 November 2026, `src/lib/tripletex-invoices.ts`, `src/server/tripletex-invoices.ts`, `src/server/bank-payments.ts`, `src/server/bank-statements.ts`, `bank_lines.payment_ref`).** Human Web's first *Bring in* (D494) brought Tripletex's 2153 and 2155 in beside the same invoices from Life, which Life had dated 9 and 25 days away (same client, amount and lines; the matcher looked within a week).
One of Life's without a number is now the same invoice when it has the same client, currency and amount within a week, or within two months with as many lines; and an invoice already here twice is kept once on the next *Bring in*: Life's copy (its time and payments are on it) takes Tripletex's number and the payment Tripletex has, and the copy the import made is taken back, its lines with it (event `invoice.merged`).
The guard allows deleting an issued invoice only for that: imported, no payment, no credit note, no time on its lines, inside a transaction that says it is importing (new migration). The preview says how many are here twice. Old bank statements: a statement may hold lines from before the books opened (Human Web's years of Tripletex invoices, paid before its books here begin).
Such a line's money is in the opening balance, so it never waits for an entry (it is listed apart on Settings → Books, *Before your books opened*, with the count of the rest), and the bank's balance is compared only from a statement that ends on or after the opening day; but a client's payment among them still marks an invoice paid, on reading when it names the invoice and by one press when it is offered.
A payment received before the books opened, or for an imported invoice not in these books, is never posted, and the line that paid it is settled by `bank_lines.payment_ref` (`work_payment:{id}` or `shop_payment:{id}`): never offered again (before this a line whose payment could not be posted stayed offered and could pay a second invoice of the same amount), never waiting.
Human Web's 30 invoices Tripletex's list showed unpaid stay unpaid until their payments are matched from the bank, the owner's choice.
