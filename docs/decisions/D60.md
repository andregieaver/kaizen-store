# D60: Design themes for stores

**Design themes for stores.** Every style setting of a storefront is a theme (`stores.theme`: the template it started from, the saved theme it came from, and its settings; `src/lib/theme.ts`): colour mode (the visitor's device, always light, always dark) with a light and a dark set of seven colours (background, surface, text, secondary text, lines, accent, text on accent), heading and body fonts (D59, now part of the theme), heading weight and capitals, filled or outlined buttons and their
corners, corners of cards, pictures and fields, page width, the header's logo (left or centred) and background (like the page, surface, accent, or inverted in the text colour), and product cards (picture shape, plain/bordered/raised, left or centred).
Themes are only validated settings, never CSS: `themeCss()` writes them as the variables the storefront's classes already read (`--accent`, `--radius-lg`, `--content-width`…, on `<html data-store-theme>`), and globals.css draws the choices from data attributes (`button-primary`, `product-card`, `font-heading`).
Three built-in templates: **Minimal**, the demo store's look collected as it was; **Warm classic** (cream and terracotta, Playfair Display over Lora, centred logo, bordered portrait cards); and **Bold modern** (an inverted black header, square corners everywhere, an orange accent #ff4f00 with black text on it, Archivo headings in bold spaced capitals over Inter, a wide page and edge-to-edge portrait product pictures; it follows the visitor's light or dark mode).
A dark header or dark mode can hide a dark logo, so stores and Kaizen can add a second logo for dark backgrounds (`navigation.logoDark`, under Header and footer): `darkBehindLogo()` works out, from the theme, whether the header and the page are dark for visitors in light and in dark mode, and `LogoPicture` shows the right logo in each, letting the browser choose by the device's mode (`<picture>` with a `prefers-color-scheme` source), so no script is needed.
The Design page says when a logo would sit on a dark background without one. Owners edit it under Store → Design with a live preview (light and dark) and readability warnings (WCAG AA contrast), switch between templates and their own saved themes (`commerce.store_themes`, named per store), update or delete those, and reset to the template's defaults; nothing changes on the store until Save.
Product grids in the page builder follow the theme's cards unless they choose their own picture shape, and the builder's canvas shows the store's theme. New stores copy the template store's theme. `stores.fonts` is no longer read and can be dropped later.
