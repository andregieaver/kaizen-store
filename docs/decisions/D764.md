# D764: One contact policy across email and texts

**One contact policy across email and texts (email plan M1.5).** At most 3 marketing messages per person a week, 20 hours apart, automation emails in the reader's daytime (`commerce.contact_policies`, the owner's to change on Marketing). Broadcasts, automation emails, texts and cart reminders count; a welcome, a cart reminder and back in stock always go; order emails never count. A held one is suppressed with `cap` or `spacing`. Idle cost: none; one read per message sent.
