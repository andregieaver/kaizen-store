# D417: The chargeback assistant, and customers who return a lot

**The chargeback assistant, and customers who return a lot.** A Stripe dispute that asks for an answer has *Prepare the evidence* on the order's Payment card, which opens `/admin/{store}/orders/{order}/dispute/{dispute}`: Stripe's evidence text fields drafted by code from the order's own records (`draftEvidence()`, `src/lib/dispute-evidence.ts`; read in `evidenceFacts()`, `src/server/dispute-evidence.ts`): who bought and the addresses, what was bought, the parcels with their carrier, tracking
and dates, how it was delivered (the carrier's tracking, staff or a handover), the terms accepted at checkout (D158), the return window, refunds made, earlier orders and disputes of the same customer, and the customer's own support messages (the latest 20). Why no refund was due is never drafted: only the owner knows. The fields a bank looks at first for the dispute's reason come first (`keyFields()`), the rest one step away.
The owner's version is kept as a draft (`commerce.dispute_evidence`, written only by `saveDisputeEvidence()`); *Send to Stripe* needs the tick that the person read it and it is true, and only then calls `disputes.update(…, { evidence, submit: true })` on the connected account; a send is claimed in the row first, given back when Stripe refuses (the draft kept), and audited (`order.dispute_evidence_sent`) with an order event.
Sent evidence is kept as sent (`dispute_evidence_guard()`), emptied when its order is anonymised (`dispute_evidence_forget()`), exported with the order and registered in `PERSONAL_DATA`. A Mollie chargeback, a closed dispute or one already answered shows what stands, with nothing to press.
A customer whose last year of paid orders has at least three with a return or a refund, making at least half of them (`returnsOften()`, `customerReturns()`), carries a *Returns often* chip on the customer bar of their orders, subscriptions and wishlists, on their customer page and on the evidence page.

Date: 2026-11-01

*Why the owner sends it:* Stripe takes one submission per dispute and it is final; evidence a bank reads must be true, so it is drafted from facts and never sent by itself, by the AI manager or by the autopilot. *Why text fields only:* every field the assistant fills is a fact the store holds as text; files (a receipt, a signature) are the owner's to add in the Stripe Dashboard, linked from the page. *Why those thresholds:* three returns rule out a one-off, and half of the orders keeps a
frequent buyer who returns now and then from being marked.
