# D250: The theme store's pictures

**The theme store's pictures (`docs/theme-phase-3.md`, after step 3).** A theme's listing holds up to six pictures beside its main one, chosen on its *Theme store listing* card and shown on its page; a launch theme's pictures stay the admin's when the code's listing is written again. A theme without a main picture is shared with a picture drawn from its published colours (`/themes/{slug}/og.png`). The header's home link on the themes host leads to the list on purpose.

Date: 2026-10-15

The gaps named after step 3 ("Lets go with your recommendation"). The pictures live in the listing's JSON, so no migration.
