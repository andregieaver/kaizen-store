# D523: Kaizena Flow's sign-ups and paid plans as ad conversions

**Kaizena Flow's sign-ups and paid plans as ad conversions (the last step of C2 of `docs/platform-parity-plan.md`, 9 November 2026, `access_requests.ad_consent_at`/`ad_signals`, `commerce.platform_ad_conversions`, `src/lib/platform-ad-conversions.ts`, `src/server/platform-ad-conversions.ts`).** A store sends its checkouts and purchases to the ad platforms server-side (D303); Kaizena Flow now sends its own two: a **sign-up** (Meta's and TikTok's *CompleteRegistration*, GA4's *sign_up*,
Pinterest's *signup*) and the **first paid plan** of the store a request made (*Subscribe*, GA4's *purchase*, Pinterest's *checkout*), through the ad platforms connected to **Kaizena Flow's store** on its Ads and conversions page, the one place they are connected, with the store's payload builders (`adRequest()`, widened to these events without changing a store's payloads). **Consent:** the sign-up form keeps the ad platforms' ids (`_fbp`, `_fbc`, Google's click id from `_gcl_aw`, `_ttp`,
`_epik`, each by its shape) only when the visitor's choice on Kaizena Flow's site (the house store's consent cookie) allowed marketing and was made with marketing among the choices; without it nothing is kept and the sign-up never sends, and the database refuses ids without consent. **Nothing raw:** an email leaves only as a SHA-256 hash, and only when the connection is *Enhanced*; no IP address or user agent (kept by neither).
The plan's value is what Kaizena Flow was paid, net of VAT (D508), its sale the event id; one person's events share GA4's client id (the request's id), as `_ga` is never read (D303). **When:** queued every minute with the store's (a sign-up within 7 days; the first live plan within 7 days of its payment and 90 of the request), once per platform, tried three times; the consent and the ids leave the request after 90 days (daily) and with the person (erasure, retention), so a plan paid later sends
nothing. A later withdrawal on the site reaches no request (no cookie names it), which the 90 days bound. Plans → Analytics shows what was sent per platform in the last 30 days, counts only, with the way to connect them. Closes the parity row *Ads and conversions*; C2 is built but its reports.
