# D475: A leads inbox

**A leads inbox (S4 of `docs/own-books-and-sales.md`; `src/lib/leads.ts`, `src/server/leads.ts`, `LeadsList`), built.** People who asked a store for something in the last 90 days but are not its customers yet, read where their requests are: quote requests (quote drafts still open or sent), questions and contact forms (`form_submissions` of kind message or quote, with an address), chats with an address, and new subscribers.
One row a person (`leadsFrom()`), the newest first, with what they asked for and its latest words; a person who has paid for an order (restricted orders left out) or is in an open deal is no lead.
The inbox is the Deals page's second view (*Board* · *Leads (n)*, `?view=leads`, so no new page in the admin map and no prompt change). *Make a deal* makes the person a customer if they are not one (D366's rules), opens a deal about them in the first stage of the first pipeline owned by whoever pressed it, named after them and what they asked for last, links the quote they asked for (so its value counts, with `orders:read`), and opens it; *Dismiss* hides them until they ask for something again.
Each decision is kept by address (`commerce.lead_decisions`, one row a person and store, the latest), deleted with the person by an erasure, never copied, under the `sales` purpose; written by `customers:write` while Deals is on, audited as `lead.*` with ids only. Tests: `leads.test.ts`, `leads.int.test.ts`. (D474 is left to the parallel session, which was told it is its next.)
