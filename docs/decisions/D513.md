# D513: The platform home's figures, targets, alerts and morning briefing

**The platform home's figures, targets, alerts and morning briefing (C2 of `docs/platform-parity-plan.md`, 9 November 2026, `commerce.platform_targets`, `platform_settings.briefing`, `src/lib/platform-briefing.ts`, `src/server/platform-briefing.ts`).** The owner: *start with 1 and 2* (B6, then C2's home).
The store's targets, alerts and briefing (D348) for Kaizena Flow's business of plans. **The home** now shows this month: recurring revenue (live) and how it moved since the month began, paying and trying stores, sales before VAT against last month to the same day, and who asked for a store and opened one today; the month's targets with their progress. **Targets** are set on Plans → Analytics for the month (recurring revenue and sales in a currency, paying and new stores as counts; 0 takes one
away), by an owner or the finance role (the plans area, D510): a sum (sales, new stores) is held against an even share of the month, a level (recurring revenue, paying stores) against the target itself. **Alerts** are worked out in code from the figures: a request waiting two days or more, stores that have not paid (urgent from three), recurring revenue falling over seven days, a target behind, trials ending within three days; the home lists those its attention list does not already say. **The
morning briefing** is off until turned on: offered once on the home (*Weekly*, *Daily*, *Not now*) and set on Plans → Analytics; at seven in Oslo (a weekly one on Mondays) the dispatcher's `platformBriefing` job claims the period and emails the platform's owners and its finance role, each in their language: the period's requests, stores opened and plans started and cancelled, recurring revenue and sales, what needs a look and the targets, with a link to the platform. **Not yet:** cohorts and
lifetime value of owners, channels and cost per new owner, reports, and a sign-up or paid plan sent as an ad conversion (the parity rows Customers, Marketing, Reports and Ads).
