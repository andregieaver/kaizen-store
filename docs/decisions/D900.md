# D900: Market check: the store against itself, market by market, then its rivals

Date: 2026-11-25

**A market check, not a SWOT text (25 November 2026, the owner: "would it make sense to incorporate running of competitor analysis, swot
analysis, or other to gain insight for identifying gaps and to better position a store in its market?", then "write the spec first, then
build step one").** The spec is [`docs/market-check.md`](../market-check.md).

- **Facts by code, findings by code, words by the model.** A SWOT a model writes from memory is the ungrounded output the AI rule forbids;
  the market check reads each fact with its source, works out findings with minimum volumes in code (`marketFindings()`), and the model
  only words a plan for the owner's yes. The SWOT is a view of those findings (M2), never a text of its own.
- **In four steps.** M1, built here: the store against itself, country by country, from its own data only (visits, orders, conversion,
  searches that found nothing, prices and text per country). M2: rivals confirmed by the owner, their facts read from machine-readable
  page data only, through `readWebPage()` honouring `robots.txt`. M3: rivals watched monthly in the goal reviews, a change an autopilot
  event. M4: Kaizena Flow against other store platforms.
- **Homer and Lisa.** Analytics → Markets opens on three findings of three kinds, each with **Make a plan** (the AI manager opened with
  the finding's words written by code; nothing is sent until the person sends it) and a link to the page that fixes it. Every country's
  figures side by side are one step away.
- **Never a price that follows a rival's by itself** (M3): a change in answer to a rival's move always waits for a yes. Prices that
  follow each other by algorithm are a competition-law concern and a race to the bottom.
- **The platform.** Kaizena Flow's own store gets the same page in the same change, with what applies to a store that sells plans (visits,
  searches, text by language). Its sign-ups keep no country, so its countries' conversion is the named next step, **M1b**:
  `access_requests.market_code`, kept from the page the sign-up was sent from.

Idle cost: none. M1 is read when the page or the tool is opened; it calls no model.
