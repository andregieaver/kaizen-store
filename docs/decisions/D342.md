# D342: Producer responsibility

**Producer responsibility (wave 7 unit H, `docs/wave-7-storefront.md` section 4, H); built.** A store's registrations under the producer responsibility schemes (scheme, country, number or an exemption's reason, authority, valid from and to, whether the number is shown) are `commerce.producer_registrations`, written only by `src/server/producer-registrations.ts` and edited at Settings → *Producer responsibility*.
`commerce.registration_gaps()` lists every product under a scheme with nothing valid on the store's own day, marked `recorded` when the store has any registration or exemption for that scheme and country; `commerce.registration_blocks()` (every gap, since the owner's decision of 27 October 2026 below; a store template or demo store never blocks) is the rule that stops a sale, read through `commerce.line_unregistered()` by the cart and `placeOrder()` alike (a bundle by its components): a
registration that ends covers its last day, so sales stop from the next. The number is shown on the product page's safety part where the owner keeps it shown (`SHOW_NUMBER_RULES` the default, for legal review). Copies carry no registration.

Date: 2026-10-27

*Why:* `international.packaging-and-epr-registers`.
As first built, a scheme and country with no registration ever recorded was only warned of, because every new product was ticked as packaging by default (`emptyProduct()`). **The owner decided on 27 October 2026 to untick by default and make the rule strict**: new products and content packs tick nothing, the demo catalogue carries no scheme, and every gap stops the sale; the migration `producer_strict` unticked first what a deploy would have stopped (templates' and demo stores', packaging
everywhere and `demo-` products where nothing is recorded; production had no registration and no real product so ticked), so nothing went off sale. Not done: the number on invoices (`buildInvoiceSnapshot()` and its SQL twin are held equal; the invoice note is the stopgap).
Migration versions: `20261027135524_producer_registrations`, `20261027135554_producer_registrations_sql` (applied by CI in the `release` job on 27 October 2026 at 18:39 UTC; advisors checked after: no warning, INFO only (RLS on with no policy, new indexes not used yet)); the strict rule `20261027200756_producer_strict` (applied by CI on 27 October 2026 at 21:29 UTC; it changes a function and a view and removes default ticks, no new table).
