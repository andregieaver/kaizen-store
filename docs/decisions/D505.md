# D505: Budget against actual, and a forecast

**Budget against actual, and a forecast (subscriptions step 4, 9 November 2026, `commerce.ledger_budgets`, `src/lib/budget.ts`, `src/server/budget.ts`, `BudgetCard` on Settings → Books).** The last step of the owner's ask (D502): *budget, forecast and register* the costs.
The Books screen shows the year: for each result account with a budget, money this year or a subscription, its budget, what the books hold so far, the forecast for the whole year and how far it misses the budget (*over*/*under* for a cost, *short*/*ahead* for income), with income, costs and the result below.
The rest of the year is forecast per account from its budget where one is set, else from the subscriptions posted to it (D503: a monthly one every month it runs, a yearly one in the month it falls due, each as the books will post it, without the VAT taken back, all of it when bought abroad, D502), else from what its months so far cost on average; the month under way counts what it holds or what was expected, whichever is more.
The owner sets an account's budget for the year as one amount a month, or month by month one step away; 0 removes it (audited `work.ledger_budget_set`, `_removed`). Nothing in a budget is posted, and it is never copied to another store. A total is set against the budget only where every line in it has one; the accounts the purchase kinds post to can have a budget before the chart has them. Kaizena Flow's own store has the same (D466).
