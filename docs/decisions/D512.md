# D512: Accountants

**Accountants (B6 of `docs/own-books-and-sales.md`, 9 November 2026, `books:read`/`books:write`, `ROLE_TEMPLATES.accountant`, `ledger_books.lock_held`, `src/lib/books-clients.ts`, `src/server/books-clients.ts`, Your account → Books).** The owner: *start with 1 and 2* (B6 and C2's home).
The store's own books become an area of their own, so an accountant may keep them without the rest of Settings: Settings → Books and every route under it ask `books:read` (every admin by default, as they read Settings before; a custom role that read Settings is given it by the migration) and every change asks `books:write` (it was the owner's alone): the owner holds it, a custom role may grant it, and the new starting role **Accountant** holds it with orders, customers and analytics to read.
Opening the books, opening them earlier and their opening balance from Tripletex stay the owner's. **The accountant's lock:** a lock (or a closed year) set by a member who keeps the books without owning the store is held (`lock_held`): the owner may lock further but never move it back, open it or reopen the year, and is told to ask the accountant; the accountant moves it back or opens it.
Every move is audited with who made it. **Across clients:** someone who keeps or reads the books of a store they do not own, or of two or more stores, gets a Books tab at Your account: each store with what its books need now, the most pressing first: the VAT term to report (the last ended and not yet locked through) with its due day and the amount from the books, late in red; supplier invoices due; the bank's lines and the documents waiting; receipts in the books' inbox; and how far the books
are locked and by whom; each opens the store's Books. An owner invites an accountant on the store's Team page in the Accountant role (an invitation as any member's). **Not yet:** a period the accountant locks through by VAT term in one press, and the threshold watch's warning before Human Web's turnover passes NOK 5 million (section 4.4).
