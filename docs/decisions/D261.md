# D261: A demo store's home page for an existing store, with undo

**A demo store's home page for an existing store, with undo (the theme showcase plan, phase 1 step 4c, `docs/theme-showcase-phase-1.md`).** The Pages list offers each published content pack's home page (*Home pages from the demo stores*: look at it, *Use this home page*).
The press copies the page as a template is copied (cleaned of what points into the demo store, its pictures copied into the store's library, checked as a save), publishes it as *Home page* and makes it the front page in one transaction, keeping the front page before in `commerce.store_home_takes`. *Put back your front page* chooses that one again unless the owner chose another since, and deletes the copy unless they changed it, chose it for a place of its own or put it in an A/B test, saying
what stayed. On the Pages list rather than Design settings, which never touches a store's words.
