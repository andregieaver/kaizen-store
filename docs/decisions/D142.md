# D142: A Plans component for Kaizen's own pages

**A Plans component for Kaizen's own pages** (`PlansBlock`, `src/lib/plan-offer.ts`, `src/server/public-plans.ts`, `PlansView`/`PlansSection`): a card for each active plan with its price (without VAT, the yearly price and what it saves under the monthly one, or only one of them), its fee per sale and what it includes (D132), and if wanted the comparison table under them; a plan can be marked Most popular, the currency chosen, and the buttons lead to sign-up unless told otherwise.
It reads `commerce.plans`, `plan_prices` and `plan_features` where the page is shown (cached under the `plans` tag, which the plan editor and the comparison refresh), so a change to a plan shows on the page with no edit to it. It is Kaizen's pages only: plans are sold through Kaizen's billing (D18), never a store's checkout, and a store's page that holds it is refused.
