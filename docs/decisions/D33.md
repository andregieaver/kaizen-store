# D33: Stores can email reminders about carts left at checkout, and Kaizen about plans left unpaid

**Stores can email reminders about carts left at checkout, and Kaizen about plans left unpaid.** A store turns them on under Cart reminders and starts with three (after an hour, a day and three days), each editable: when it goes (30 minutes to 30 days after the email was typed), on or off, an optional discount code, and a subject, heading, message and button in each of the store's languages, beside a live preview and a test sent to the staff member.
While they are on, checkout keeps the email of a shopper who is not signed in as soon as a valid address is typed, with the cart as ordered; right under the email field it says a reminder may come, with a link to say no (and to undo that). Each reminder shows the cart and its total, and has a button that puts the same cart back in the shopper's browser (with the step's code) and a link to stop reminders, with List-Unsubscribe one-click headers.
At most one reminder per cart per run, the latest due, so a late run never sends several; each is claimed before it is sent and keyed, so none goes twice. Reminders stop when the cart is paid, when the same email buys anything, when the shopper opts out, and 30 days after capture; the email and cart are erased on opt-out and 60 days after capture. The admin shows the last 30 days (carts with an email, reminded, bought after a reminder, and those sales) and the latest carts.
The same applies to Kaizen's own checkout, the plans stores pay for: under Platform → Plan reminders, platform admins turn on and edit reminders (in English, like the admin, with Kaizen's plan discount codes) to owners who went to Stripe to pay for a plan and did not finish.
The owner is signed in, so the attempt is kept when they go to pay (the latest per store); the Plan page and Stripe's page say a reminder may come, and the Plan page and every reminder let the owner say no (for all of Kaizen's plan reminders). The reminder's button takes the owner straight back to paying for the same plan, with the code applied; reminders stop once the store is on a plan, however it got there.
Supabase's scheduler (pg_cron and pg_net) calls Kaizen every five minutes, and daily for subscription reminders (D29), with a token made in the database and kept in Supabase Vault.

Date: 2026-09-25

Asked for by the owner. Whether a cart reminder is marketing is not settled: under markedsføringsloven § 15 (and the Swedish and Danish rules) electronic marketing to consumers needs consent, except to existing customers whose address was collected at a sale with a clear, free opt-out then and in every message.
Kaizen gives the notice and opt-out at collection and in every reminder, keeps reminders to the cart, and leaves it off until the store turns it on; each store is responsible for its use and should say so in its privacy policy (the admin gives the wording). Signed-in customers are not captured. The scheduler's address is Kaizen's production site. Vercel Cron stays for subscription reminders where CRON_SECRET is set; each reminder goes once whoever calls.
