# D366: Customers in the admin

**Customers in the admin: added and changed by staff, notes, a tag filter, merging two, and a customer import (wave 10 unit C, `docs/wave-10-must-haves.md` 4); built.** Staff with `customers:write` add a customer (`/customers/new`) and change one's name, email, phone, address and company (the customer page's *Edit details*) through the one writer `src/server/customer-staff.ts` (`createCustomerIn()`/`updateCustomerIn()`, the form's fields checked by `cleanCustomerInput()` in
`src/lib/customer-staff.ts`, shared with the import): an account with no password and an email not proven (the person signs in with a code, D32; nothing is emailed and nobody is subscribed), an email another customer of the store has is refused (`taken`), a changed email is unproven again and the person's tags and notes move with it, the address is the default saved one (D304), a company needs its name and a valid organisation number, and a refusal found after a write undoes the transaction.
A guest is given an account by their own address (`addGuestAsCustomer()`). Notes are `commerce.customer_notes` (by the store and the person's address, with author and time, never edited by a database rule, deleted by staff; personal data: export, erasure, `PERSONAL_DATA`, `never` in `COPY_RULES`); the activity log keeps a note's id and length, never its words, and a customer's change as `{ changed: true }` per field. The list filters by tag (`?tag=`, `listCustomers({ tag })`).
A merge (`src/server/customer-merge.ts`, `/customers/{id}/merge`) folds another account into the one kept by the email staff type: orders (copied ones only lose the account), subscriptions, cancelled subscription box lists, open drafts, carts, wishlists, saved addresses, support conversations, Work clients, codes named for the customer, custom field values the kept one lacks, tags, notes and the bonus balance (one entry granted to the kept account; the removed account's ledger is deleted with
it, as an erasure's is, so a taking entry would lose its allocations before the ledger's check at commit) move, then the other account is deleted (its sessions, back-in-stock requests and buyer roles at a company location go with it); it is refused before anything changes, with the reason staff read (`MERGE_REFUSALS`), for a company the kept one is not in, subscription box lists not cancelled, reviews, gift cards, the referral program, credits still pending, or more addresses or tags than one
person may hold; the preview runs the same plan in a transaction it rolls back, and the merge reads it again under both rows' locks.
The owner imports customers (`/customers/import`, a D165 job `customer_import`, the stock import's pipeline: dry run, per-row findings that name a column and never quote a cell, a problems CSV, resumable): our own customer file or Shopify's (`readHeader()` in `src/lib/customer-import.ts`), a row finding its customer by email, an empty cell keeping what the store has, tags added, never a password set and never anyone subscribed (marketing and password columns are noted and not read), each row its
own transaction through the staff writer. Words are the `customers` catalogue's `staff` (en, nb), the import's shared words the `inventory` catalogue's. The admin-strings scan no longer reads the `>` of `=> Promise<…>` as a tag (its baseline shrank). `customers.customer-profiles-notes-csv-import-and-export` moves to full. Migration versions: `20261028183302_customer_admin`, `20261028183325_customer_admin_rules` (applied by CI, run 713).
