# D370: Inventory

**Inventory: the export's `on_hand_was`, a first alert for a new variant, the pages in a browser (wave 10 unit G, `docs/wave-10-must-haves.md` 4); built.** The stock export writes `on_hand_was` (the stock it was made from), so a file counted from an export and brought back is checked against it and a row whose stock moved since is a conflict, as the import already read the column.
A variant created at or below its low-stock level, or given a level it is already at or below (`off` to `low`), is a crossing at once: `nextAlertState()` and `commerce.refresh_stock_alert()` still write such a crossing with `notified_at` equal to `crossed_at` (the same transaction's instant), and the job reads that, or null, as not yet told (`untold()`, `UNTOLD_SQL`), tells it once and marks it with its own time.
The 20-line order guard's flakiness had a cause: after other tests had made many small stores, the planner's statistics gave the store one variant and one product and scanned every variant per product; the cart page's read and `placeOrder()` now take the cart's lines first (`cartLinesFirst()` in `cart.ts`), and `inventory-perf.int.test.ts` times the cart page's read too.
`e2e/signed-in/inventory.spec.ts` opens the Inventory page, reviews and saves a counted figure with its reason, follows it into the variant's history and makes a stock file. `catalogue.inventory-tracking-with-reservations` moves to full. No migration.
