# D101: Stores connect Slack on their Integrations page, and get their events as messages in a channel

**Stores connect Slack on their Integrations page, and get their events as messages in a channel.** Slack is a third integration beside Zapier and Make (D41), with the same events, queue, retries, test and log.
Two ways to connect: "Add to Slack", Kaizen's own Slack app with only the `incoming-webhook` scope (`SLACK_CLIENT_ID`/`_SECRET`, redirect `/api/integrations/slack/callback`; the state is bound to the store and account in the `kaizen_slack_connect` cookie, and only an owner's return is kept), where the owner picks the channel in Slack; or pasting an incoming webhook the owner made (only `https://hooks.slack.com/services/…`).
Either way Kaizen keeps just the channel's webhook, encrypted like the others, and no Slack token: it can post to that channel and nothing else. Messages are built from the same content as Zapier's (`slackMessage()` in `src/lib/slack.ts`): what happened, the amount in the store's main language, the products for a new order, tracking for a sent one, and a link to it in the admin.
Shoppers' emails, phone numbers and addresses are never sent to Slack, and everything shoppers or staff wrote is escaped, so it cannot become a link or a mention. What is kept for a delivery is the message Slack got. Slack's answers that mean the webhook, channel or app is gone (400, 403, 404, 410) are not tried again.
