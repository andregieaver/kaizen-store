# D313: A domain per language for stores, optional

**A domain per language for stores, optional (`docs/language-domains.md`).** An owner may make any of the store's domains one language's own address (Settings → Domains, *Use for one language*; `store_domains.language`, one domain a language, never the store's primary, held by the database), from the languages the store keeps but its own country's own.
On that domain a market without a language is in it, so its addresses leave the language out (`/home` is the store in English on `nordic-en.com`); the proxy reads the language from the host (`languageHostDecision()`, before D181's rules) and moves a request for a market whose language lives on another origin there (308).
Links follow in one place: `marketPath()` leaves the domain's language out, full addresses are `marketUrl()` on the market's origin (emails, Stripe, sitemap, canonical, `hreflang`, structured data; the market layout's `metadataBase`), and the language choice links across origins with `marketLink()`. Carts, sign-in and cookie choices stay per site, as browsers keep them.
