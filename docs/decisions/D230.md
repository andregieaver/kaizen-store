# D230: Tools with columns of their own are drawn in the `workspace` layout

**Tools with columns of their own are drawn in the `workspace` layout (`docs/admin-style.md` section 8, step 14).** A level's `fullWidth` addresses (the page editors, now also the media library) and its new `workspaceLinks` addresses (the support inbox) are the workspace: the rail narrows to 64 px (`AdminRail`'s `collapseOn`, `data-collapsed`), no sidebar, and the page takes the whole width with less room at the sides (`data-admin-layout="workspace"`).
The inbox's own section pages (Requests, Reports, Saved replies, Settings, and the platform's Stores), which lived in the sidebar it no longer has, are a row of links in the top bar from `lg` up (`AreaLinks`; the slide-out menu has them below); the media library's section (Website) has too many pages for a top bar and keeps them in the rail's section and the menu, as the builder does.
Store: `/media`, the page editors and `/support`; platform: `/media`, its editors and `/support`; the owner's level: the combined inbox `/account/support`. The inbox takes the height the workspace leaves it (`100dvh - 7rem`, was 10 rem) and the card look. `workspace` is registered in `ADMIN_LAYOUTS`; `admin-rail.test.ts` holds the collapsed rail, the section links on the inbox only, and the inbox's other pages keeping the full rail and sidebar. No migration.
