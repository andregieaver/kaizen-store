# D357: Checkout rules and questions

**Checkout rules and questions (wave 9 unit D, `docs/wave-9-platform.md` section 3, D); built.** Settings → Checkout rules (`/admin/{store}/settings/checkout`, `settings:read` to see, `settings:write` to change; Selling, with the shop on).
A rule (`commerce.checkout_rules`, at most 30, written only by `src/server/checkout-rules.ts`, saved off, audited `shipping.checkout_rule_*`, `never` copied) is conditions on the basket that must all hold (country; basket value at least or below an amount in one currency, compared only in that currency; weight of what is shipped; includes or not a product, a category or a tag, the collections filled by rules and parent categories counted as `termsOfProducts()` reads them; the buyer's customer
group) and one effect: hide delivery options (the flat rate, a method, a pickup place or a local area, by key), rename one (words per language), leave payment methods out (never cards) or stop the order with the owner's words per language.
`applyCheckoutRules()` (`src/lib/checkout-rules.ts`, pure, no zod: the cart is a pay route) is the one evaluator, reached only through `checkoutRuleOutcome()`, which reads the basket's facts from the cart's lines; `cartSummary()` reads it once and hands it to `resolveDelivery()`, and `placeOrder()` does the same in its transaction, so the cart and the order agree (the delivery page, the choice and the pay press's check read it too, given the signed-in buyer).
Rules run in order: hidden options and methods add up, the first rename and the first stop count. A rule that names something gone or cannot be read is left out whole, never half applied, and counted in `commerce.checkout_rule_skips` (on its own connection, so a refused order keeps the count), shown on the rule until it is saved again. A rule never changes a price, VAT or a legal page; a carrier's services and a weekly box (made by the store's job) are not touched.
What a rule left out of payment is kept on the order (`orders.rule_hidden_methods`) and passed by `excludedPaymentMethods()` with the owner's own choices to Stripe and Mollie; the rules that applied are kept too (`orders.checkout_rules_applied`). A stopped basket: the cart shows the owner's words and its button is off, `placeOrder()` refuses `checkout_rule`.
Questions (`commerce.checkout_fields`, at most 10: a text or a tick box, required or not, words per language) are asked above the cart's checkout button, kept on the cart as answered (`carts.checkout_answers`, through `cleanAnswers()`), checked again and copied by `placeOrder()` (`orders.checkout_answers`, the question in the store's main words and the answer; `checkout_fields` when a required one is empty), shown on the order page and in the order file's Full profile (`checkout_answers`) and
the person's export; personal data, cleared when the cart's retention runs, when the person is erased and when the order is anonymised. Shopper words (`problemCheckoutRule`, `checkoutQuestions`) are hand-written in nb, sv, da and en. Moves `platform.custom-logic-functions` to partial (no pricing rule, by choice: discounts stay native) and keeps `checkout.checkout-extensibility-ui-extensions-functions` partial (no Admin API route for the rules yet).

checkout and the order's answers follow `position` as before. No migration.
