# D50: Categories and tags, for each owner and kind of content

**Categories and tags, for each owner and kind of content.** One table, `commerce.terms`, holds categories (which nest: a category can hold subcategories, never itself or one of its own, checked in the database) and tags (a flat list), each for one kind of content (`page`, `article`, `product`) of Kaizen's (`store_id` null) or a store's; an address (slug) is unique per owner, kind of content and category-or-tag. An item can be in several of each.
Pages keep theirs in their content (`categories`, `tags`, by id), so they go live when the page is published; products in `commerce.product_terms`, whose keys make a product take only its own store's product terms. Kaizen's page terms are managed at `/admin/platform/pages/categories`, a store's product terms at `/admin/{store}/products/categories`, and both are chosen (or quickly added) in the page and product editors. Deleting a category moves its subcategories up.
A new store gets copies of the template's product terms with its products (`clone_store`).
