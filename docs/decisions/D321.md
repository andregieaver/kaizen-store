# D321: B2B in depth

**B2B in depth (wave 6 unit C, `docs/wave-6-catalogue.md` Unit C); C1 built: price lists.** A price list (`commerce.price_lists`: a name, an optional whole percentage off the market price, on or off) is for customer groups and companies (`price_list_assignments`) and holds fixed prices per variant and currency with VAT (`price_list_prices`); its percentage reaches the products and categories in its scope (`price_list_products`, every product while empty).
A buyer's lists are those of their active group, their company (active, *Sell to businesses* on, owner or employee) and the company's group; the lowest price they give, below the market price, wins, worked out only by `commerce.buyer_price()`, which the cart, checkout and the product page's buy part all read.
Goods and appointments bought once only (`LIST_PRICED`); a list-priced line takes no group or company percentage on top; the buyer sees *Your price*, never a reduction; the order line keeps the list (`order_lines.price_list_id`). Admin at Customers → Price lists; the AI manager reads them (`list_price_lists`).
