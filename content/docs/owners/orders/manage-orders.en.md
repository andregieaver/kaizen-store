## The orders list

Open **Orders** in the menu. [Orders](/admin/{store}/orders) lists your orders, newest first, 50 to a page. An order appears as soon
as it is paid.

The views at the top are the quickest way in:

- **All**: every order except unfinished checkouts and the archive.
- **To send**: paid orders with goods still to send. [Send orders](/docs/owners/orders/send-orders) says how.
- **Waiting for stock**: orders with items sold on backorder that have not come in yet.
- **To hand over** and **Local deliveries**: orders collected at your places, or taken out by your own driver.
- **Unfinished checkouts**: checkouts a shopper started and did not pay.
- **Archived**: orders you have moved out of the way.

Orders are part of the online shop, which is on unless you switched it off under Settings, Features.

## Find an order

Type in **Search**: an order number, an email address, a name, a product, a SKU, a tag or a tracking number. A search finds archived
orders too. Only the first five words are used.

To narrow the list, press **Filters, sorting and columns**. You can filter by **Payment**, **Fulfilment**, **Order status**, tag
(**Tagged**), the day it was **Placed**, **Market** and **Made by**, show **Gift orders only**, choose the **Sort** and choose the
**Columns** the list shows. Press **Apply**. The filters that are on show above the list; remove them one by one, or press
**Clear all**.

The search and filters are kept in the page's address, so a bookmark or a link you send a colleague opens the same list.

### Save a view

For a list you come back to, press **Save this list as a view**, give it a name of up to 40 characters and press **Save view**. It keeps
the search, the filters, the sort and the columns. **Manage saved views** renames, moves and deletes them. A store has up to 30 saved
views.

> [!NOTE]
> Everyone on your team who can read orders can open a saved view. Do not save a search for a person's name.

## One order's page

Press an order to open it. The page shows its items and totals, the payment, the customer, sending, refunds, its invoice and credit
notes, returns, and its **History**: everything that happened to the order, each email it sent included.

- **Send confirmation again** sends the order confirmation to the customer once more.
- **Change email or address**, under **Customer**, corrects the customer's email, address or phone.
- **Add a note** keeps a note in the order's history. Only staff see it.

## Tags

Tags group orders your own way, such as *vip* or *wholesale*. On the order, type them under **Tags**, separated by commas, and press
**Add**. A tag is up to 40 characters, and capital letters do not matter: *VIP* and *vip* are one tag. Customers never see tags, so do
not write about a person in one.

Filter the list by a tag with **Tagged**, or search for it.

## Archive

Archive an order you are done with: press **Archive** on its page. It leaves the default list but changes nothing else: no amount,
number, invoice or figure. Find it under **Archived** or with a search, and press **Unarchive** to bring it back. A return made on an
archived order brings it back by itself.

An order cannot be archived while it is paid and still has goods to send, was never paid, or has a return open.

To archive for you, switch on **Automatic archiving** under [Settings, Orders](/admin/{store}/settings/orders). It is off by default.
It archives orders that are sent (or have nothing to send), have no return open, and have had no news for the days you choose, from
14 to 365.

## Several orders at once

Tick orders in the list, or tick the whole page and press **Select all … matching**. The bar that opens offers **Add tags**,
**Remove tags**, **Archive**, **Unarchive**, **Mark as sent**, **Print packing slips**, **Print pick list**, **Ready for pickup** and
**Book labels**.

Up to 250 orders go at a time, and 100 for printing and labels. Each order is handled on its own: one that cannot be changed is listed
afterwards with the reason, and does not stop the others.

## Change an order's items

A paid order that is not sent yet can be changed. On its page, under **Change the items**, press **Edit items**. You can lower a
quantity (0 takes the item off), add products at the market's price or one you type, and change the shipping. Choose a reason, and
whether removed items go back in stock.

The summary shows the order before and after:

- **A lower total** is refunded to the customer at once.
- **A higher total** waits for the customer: send them a pay link, or record that they paid you another way. The order stays as it was
  until they pay, and the added items are held for 7 days.

The order keeps its number. Items kept keep the price and discounts they were sold with. An order with an invoice gets a credit note for
what is taken off and an additional invoice for what is added.

If the order cannot be changed, the card says why: for example something is already sent, or it holds a subscription or a booking. Then
refund instead, as [Refunds, cancellations and exchanges](/docs/owners/orders/refunds-and-exchanges) shows.

## Gift messages

Switch on **Gift messages** under [Settings, Orders](/admin/{store}/settings/orders) to let shoppers mark an order as a gift, with To,
From and a message of up to 300 characters. It is off by default.

The order then shows a **Gift** card with the message. **Print gift slip** prints it on a packing slip without prices, for the parcel.
Kaizena Flow never sends the message to anyone and never puts it in the shipping email.

## Emails to customers

Press **Emails to customers** on Orders to see every email the store sent and whether it arrived: sent, delivered, failed, or bounced
because the address does not take email. Each order's **History** lists its own.

Which emails customers get is chosen under [Settings, Notifications](/admin/{store}/settings/notifications). The order confirmation is
always sent. [Notifications](/docs/owners/team-and-settings/notifications) explains the rest, and your own wording.

## Order numbers

Each store numbers its orders in one series without gaps, as some countries require: in a new store from 1001. An order is never
deleted. A cancelled order stays, cancelled, with its number, and a changed order keeps its number.

> [!TIP]
> Orders brought in from another shop as history are numbered **C-** and their old name. They are outside your series and are never
> paid, sent or invoiced.
