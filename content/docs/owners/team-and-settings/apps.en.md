An app is software another company makes for many stores: a stock sync, an accounting link, a shipping tool. You install it on one store,
and it works in that store as you: it can never do more than your own role allows.

## Install an app

Start from the app's own website, which sends you to Kaizena Flow. Sign in, and you see the app, the address it comes from, and in plain
words what it asks to do. Choose **one store** and press **Install**.

- Only stores where your role allows everything the app asks for are offered.
- What the app may do *without asking you each time* is listed on its own: publishing products, refunding orders and the like.
- An app that is not *verified* by Kaizena Flow says so. Install only apps you trust.

## See what is installed

Open **Settings**, **Apps and API**. Under **Installed apps** each app shows who installed it, when, and when it was last used.

## When an app asks for more

When the company behind an app adds something it needs, the app keeps only what you gave it until you agree. Its row says **Asks for
more**. Open **Review** to read what it now asks for, and press **Give it this**. Only the person who installed it can be given more:
if their role does not allow it all, have someone whose role does install the app again.

## Remove an app

Press **Remove** beside the app (owners only). It stops at once: its access ends and it is sent nothing more about your store. It is told
it was removed, and 48 hours later asked to erase what it keeps of your store. If you install it again within those 48 hours, it is not
asked to erase anything.

An app also stops when the person who installed it leaves the store or erases their account, and when Kaizena Flow blocks it.
