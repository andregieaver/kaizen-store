## Make a bundle

A bundle is one product made of several of your products, such as two mugs and a tote bag sold as a gift set. It has a page, a picture
and a price of its own, but no stock of its own: it is in stock as far as all its products are.

1. Open **Products** and press **Add product**. Write the title and description and add a picture, as for any product.
2. Under **What is it?**, choose **A bundle**.
3. Under **What's in the bundle**, type a product's name or SKU in **Find a product** and press **Add** beside the one you want. Add the
   others the same way.
4. Set **How many** of each. The arrows change their order; **×** takes one out.
5. Choose the **Price** (below) and press **Save**.

A bundle holds up to 20 products and at least two units in all: two products, or two of one. Only goods you send by post can go in a
bundle, and they need the same **VAT category** as the bundle (under **Advanced**).

> [!NOTE]
> You choose **A bundle** when you make the product. A saved product cannot become a bundle, and a bundle cannot become another kind of
> product.

## Price it

Under **Price**, choose one:

- **The products' prices less a percentage** (the usual choice): type the **Percent off**, 0 to 90. The editor shows what the bundle
  comes to in each country, beside what the products cost on their own. The bundle's price follows theirs: when one of their prices
  changes, the bundle's does too.
- **A price of its own**: type the bundle's price for each country, as for any product.

If a product in the bundle has no price in a country, a bundle priced from its products keeps the price it had there, and the editor
says so.

## Stock

**Bundles you can sell** shows how many bundles the products' stock allows: the lowest whole number. With 10 mugs and 3 tote bags, a
bundle of two mugs and one tote has 3. You never set a bundle's stock yourself, and a bundle is never sold on backorder.

A bundle cannot be sold while one of its products is archived or switched off, or has another VAT category than the bundle. The editor
marks that product in the bundle's list and says what to do: bring it back, give them one VAT category, or take it out. You can still
change the products themselves: the bundle simply waits until it is put right.

The bundle is not sold in a country where one of its products is not sold.

### The bundle's own details

Under **The bundle's own details**, give its **SKU**, **Barcode (GTIN/EAN)** and **Weight in grams**.

## What shoppers see

A bundle is shown and bought like any product. Its page shows *What's inside*, above the description, with a link to each product that
is on sale. Campaigns, discount codes, price lists and price breaks work on it as on any product: see
[Quantity rules and price breaks](/docs/owners/products/quantity-rules-and-price-breaks).

A bundle is never a campaign's free product, and it cannot go in a subscription box, a draft order or an exchange.

## After the sale

- **The order** shows the products under the bundle's line, such as *Includes 2 × Mug, 1 × Tote bag*: on your order page, the
  customer's and the packing slip.
- **The pick list** counts the products to take from the shelf, never the bundle.
- **Stock**: the products' stock is held when the order is placed and drawn when it is paid. A refund, a cancellation or a return puts
  the products back.
- **Changing the order**: an order with a bundle cannot be changed yet. Refund or cancel it instead. See
  [Refunds and exchanges](/docs/owners/orders/refunds-and-exchanges).

## Bundles and price on request

A product with **Price on request** can only be in a bundle with **A price of its own**. A bundle priced from its products would show and
charge the price you keep as your guide for quotes, so:

- while **The products' prices less a percentage** is chosen, the bundle's list marks such a product, and the bundle is not saved until
  you give it a price of its own or show that product's price;
- ticking **Price on request** on a product that is in such a bundle is refused, and the editor names the bundles.

A bundle with a price of its own is bought at that price. The product priced on request is still sold on its own only by quote: see
[Prices](/docs/owners/products/prices).
