## Sign up

Go to [Start your store](/sign-up) and follow its three short steps:

1. **Your store.** Give it a name and, if you like, say what you will sell. You can change the name later.
2. **Look.** Choose how your store looks, or keep Standard. Your store starts with the look's demo products and pages; you can change the
   look any time.
3. **About you.** Your name and your email address, where we write to you.

You can go back to a step at any time; what you typed is kept.

What happens next depends on how sign-up is open when you come:

- **When you can sign up on your own**, we email you a link to confirm your address. Open it and press **Start my store**: your store is
  made at once, with demo products to try. It is free for 14 days.
- **Otherwise** your request waits for Kaizena Flow's team. When your store is ready, you get an email with a link to sign in.

> [!NOTE]
> Opening the link does not make the store by itself: the store is made when you press the button on the page the link opens.

## Follow the setup steps

Until your store is open, signing in takes you to its [setup steps](/admin/{store}/setup). You can skip any step and finish it later from
the overview. What is done is read from your store as it is, so the list stays right however you change a setting.

1. **What you sell.** Kaizena Flow switches on what your store needs and keeps the rest out of your way. You can change it later under
   Settings, Features.
2. **Your business.** The legal name, organisation number, contact email and address that shoppers see in the footer, the terms of sale
   and every order confirmation. The law requires them, so fill in the business that sells.
3. **Where you sell.** Each country gets its own storefront in its own language and currency. A product shows in a country once it has a
   price there.
4. **Bookings**, only if you take appointments or rent out rooms and items.
5. **Payments.** You can test your whole checkout at once: while test payments are on, pay with the card `4242 4242 4242 4242`. Real
   payments need your own Stripe account, which you connect when you are ready to open.
6. **Products.** Your store starts with demo products. Keep them while you try things out, or remove them, and
   [add your first product](/docs/owners/getting-started/your-first-product).
7. **Open your store.** Check what is done, and open it.

## Before you open

Until you open it, your store says on every page that it is a preview and not open yet. Try it as a shopper would: add something to the
cart and pay with the test card.

A store you made on your own has a free trial of 14 days. Choose a plan before it ends and the plan is free until then. If the trial ends
without a plan, an open store is made private until you choose one; nothing in it is deleted.
