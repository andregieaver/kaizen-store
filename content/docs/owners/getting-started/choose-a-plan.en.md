## Your free trial

A store you made at sign-up, or one the Dream Factory built for you, starts with a free trial of 14 days, as
[Before you open](/docs/owners/getting-started/create-your-store#before-you-open) says. During the trial you can look at your store,
change it and open it without a plan. While you have no plan, **Billing** shows the day the trial ends.

- **Choose a plan with two days or more of the trial left**, and it costs nothing until the trial ends: the first payment is taken on
  that day.
- **Choose it in the last two days**, and the first payment is taken when you choose.

### If the trial ends without a plan

Nothing is deleted. An open store is made private: visitors see its landing page, and everything you made is kept. A banner in your
admin says why, with a link: **Choose a plan**. Until you have a plan, you cannot make the store public, and a store that is not open
yet cannot be opened.

When your plan starts, a store made private at the trial's end is public again within a few minutes.

## Find the plans

Open **Settings** in the menu and choose [Billing](/admin/{store}/billing), under Account. Only the store's owners can open it. Each
store has its own plan.

The top of the page shows your plan, its price, its status and when it renews, or *No plan yet*. It also shows **Kaizena Flow's fee**:
the share of each sale that Kaizena Flow takes when the shopper pays.

Under **Choose a plan**, each plan has a card with:

- its price a month or a year, or both, and what paying yearly saves;
- its fee per sale;
- a button to choose it paid yearly, and one paid monthly.

Prices are without VAT. A store in Norway pays 25 % Norwegian VAT (MVA) on top; a store in another country is invoiced without VAT,
under the reverse charge. The country is the one your business is registered in, under [Company](/admin/{store}/settings/company);
until it is there, Billing asks for it and no plan can be chosen. Prices are in your store's main currency when the plans are offered
in it.

Once Kaizena Flow has filled it in, **Compare plans**, under the cards, shows feature by feature what each plan includes.

## Choose and pay

1. **A discount code from Kaizena Flow?** Type it under **Discount code** and press **Apply** first: each card then shows what the plan
   costs with the code, and for how long. Without a code saved, Stripe's payment page has its own field for one.
2. Press **Choose · yearly** or **Choose · monthly** on the plan you want.
3. Pay by card on Stripe's secure page. You come back to Billing, which shows your new plan.

> [!NOTE]
> If you go to pay and do not finish, Kaizena Flow may email you a reminder. To stop them, press **Do not send me reminders** on
> Billing.

## Change your plan

Once you are on a plan, the cards' buttons say **Upgrade**, **Downgrade** or **Switch**; **Switch** is your own plan paid the other way,
yearly or monthly. The button for what you have now reads *Current plan*. A change applies at once, with no payment page: the
difference is credited or charged on your next invoice.

## Invoices and your card

Press **Invoices and payment details**, under **Invoices and payment**, to open Stripe's page for your plan. There you see all of
Kaizena Flow's invoices, pay one that is open, or change the card you pay with.

A plan that Kaizena Flow's team started for you is paid by invoice instead: Stripe emails you one each period, due in 14 days.

Premium themes you bought are listed under **Themes** on the same page: each is paid once, with its own invoice.

## Cancel your plan

Press **Cancel at the end of the period**. The plan runs to the end of the period you have paid for, the day shown beside *Ends*;
until then, **Keep my plan** undoes it.

When the plan has ended, a store that began with a free trial is made private, as at the trial's end, until you choose a plan again.
Billing says so beside the plan while it is ending, and the banner afterwards says that the plan ended.
To stop selling altogether, [Close store](/admin/{store}/settings/close) under Settings also ends the plan at the end of its period.

## Usage and limits

A plan can limit how much your store uses: storage, products, staff and domains, and each month marketing emails, AI, visits and
bandwidth. **Usage this month**, on Billing, shows what your store has used against what its plan includes;
[Usage and limits](/admin/account/limits), in your account, shows every store you own.

- **From 80 %** a line says *Almost used*, and the store's owners get an email. They get another when it is *Used up*.
- **At a limit, only the action that would pass it is stopped**: an upload, a new product, an invitation to staff, a new domain,
  another store, a marketing email send, or AI on Kaizena Flow's key. The message says what to do: remove something, or choose a plan
  with more.
- **Visits and bandwidth only warn.** Your shop stays open, and you are never charged for being busy.
- **Emails about orders are never limited**, nor any email the law requires.

> [!TIP]
> Planning a big sale? Tell Kaizena Flow when, under **Planning a big sale?** on Billing, for up to 14 days. Traffic warnings wait
> while it runs.
