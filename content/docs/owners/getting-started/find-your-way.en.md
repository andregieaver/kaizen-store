## The sections

Your store's admin has a section for each part of the business: **Orders**, **Products**, **Customers**, **Marketing**, **Analytics**,
**Website**, **Support** and **Settings**, with **Bookings** when you take appointments or rent out rooms and items. They are down the left
of the screen, each with its icon; on a phone, press the menu button at the top. Inside a section, its pages are listed beside the page
you are on, each with an icon of its own.

## Basic and Advanced view

The admin starts in **Basic view**: in each section it shows the pages most owners use every day, such as orders, products, discount
codes, pages, payments and shipping. Everything else (reports, rules, tools and the finer settings) is in **Advanced view**.

Switch with **Basic | Advanced** at the top of the screen, or on a phone at the foot of the menu. The choice is kept with your account,
so it is the same on every device you sign in on. You can also change it under [Your account](/admin/account).

> [!TIP]
> Can't find a page? While Basic view is on, the foot of each section's list says how many more pages Advanced view has, with a button
> that switches to it.

Basic view hides only links, never pages: a link from your home page, the setup steps or the AI manager still opens any page, and the list
beside it then shows where that page lives.

## Ask the AI manager

The AI manager knows every page of the admin. Ask it where something is ("where do I change the return period?") and it opens the page
for you.
