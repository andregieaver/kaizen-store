## Start a product

Open **Products** in the menu and press **Add product**. A new product starts as a draft: shoppers do not see it until you publish it, so
you can save it as often as you like on the way.

## What to fill in

1. **Title and description.** Write them in your store's main language. Each other language your store sells in has its own text; leave
   one empty and shoppers there see the main language's.
2. **Pictures and videos.** The first picture is the one shoppers see in lists. Pictures are made smaller in your browser before they are
   uploaded, so large files are fine. Describe each picture in a few words, for shoppers who cannot see it.
3. **How it is delivered.** *Physical* for goods you ship, *Digital* for files shoppers download after paying.
4. **Price and stock.** A price for each country you sell in, with VAT included. A country without a price does not sell the product.
   If the product comes in sizes or colours, tick that it comes in options and each variant gets its own price, SKU and stock.
5. **Product safety**, for physical goods: the manufacturer, and where the manufacturer is outside the EU, a responsible person in the EU.
   EU product-safety rules require them on the listing.

> [!TIP]
> When you change a price later, Kaizena Flow keeps its history. A lower price is shown as a reduction only against the lowest price of
> the last 30 days, as the law requires.

## Publish it

Set the status to **Published** and press **Save**. A product can be published once it has:

- at least one picture,
- a price in at least one country,
- for physical goods, a manufacturer (and an EU responsible person when the manufacturer is outside the EU),
- for digital goods, a file to download.

If something is missing, nothing is saved yet and the editor lists what to fix. Then press **See it in your store** to look at its page.

## When the price is not fixed

Goods bought once whose price depends on the order can be *Price on request*, a tick over the product's prices: shoppers then see no
price and ask for a quote rather than adding it to the cart. The prices you enter stay as your guide when you price the quote; they are
never shown.
