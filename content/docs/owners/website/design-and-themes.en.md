## Your store's look

Open **Website** in the menu and choose [Design](/admin/{store}/settings/design). Your store's look is a theme: its colours, fonts,
buttons and corners, light and dark, a header, a footer, the product page's layout and the site's CSS. The pages you build use it too.

A theme never holds your content. Changing or switching it never touches your products, pages, words, pictures, videos, menus, logo or
business details.

## Change colours and fonts

Further down Design, your live theme's settings are in groups. Change what you like; the preview beside them shows it at once:

- **Colours**: the background, text, accent (buttons and highlights) and the rest, for light and for dark.
- **Fonts**: one for **Headings** and one for **Body text**. Press **Change** to choose any Google Fonts family.
- **Buttons and corners**, **Layout** (the page width and the header), **Product cards** and, in a shop, **Checkout look**: how
  Stripe's payment form takes your colours, font and corners.

Then press **Save**. The store shows your changes at once.

If a text is hard to read on its colour, a warning under **Colours** says which, and how far it is from the 4.5:1 to aim for.

Fonts are copied to Kaizena Flow when you choose them and load from your own site, so your visitors' browsers never contact Google.

> [!TIP]
> Not happy with what you saved? **Versions** lists your store's earlier looks, the newest 50. **Use this version** puts one back in the
> editor; your store changes only when you save. **Reset to …** starts again from the theme's own settings.

## Light and dark

Under **Colours**, **Light and dark** chooses how your store looks: **Follow the visitor's device**, **Always light** or **Always dark**.
Each has its own colours.

Tick **Let visitors choose light or dark** to add a button to the header that switches to the other colours, remembered in that
visitor's browser. A header you built yourself needs its *Light or dark* component for this.

If your logo is dark, add a light version for dark backgrounds under
[Header and footer](/docs/owners/website/menus-header-and-footer).

## Try another theme

**Add a theme** lists Kaizena Flow's themes. A theme's presets are listed as looks of their own (*Theme · Preset*): the same theme with
its own colours, fonts and buttons.

1. Press **Preview** to see a theme in a new window.
2. Press **Add to my themes**. It goes into your **Theme library** without going live: visitors see nothing of it.
3. In the theme library, press **Customise** on it. You can **Preview on your pages**, read **What changes** for your pages and layouts,
   change its settings, and edit its header, footer and product page.
4. When it is ready, press **Switch to “…”**. It is live at once.

The theme you switch away from stays in your library with every change you made to it, so you can switch back with **Switch to**.
**Remove** takes a theme you no longer want out of the library.

**See every theme in the theme store** opens Kaizena Flow's [theme store](/themes). On a theme's page there,
*Already have a store? Add it to your store* puts it in one of your stores' libraries without making it live.

> [!NOTE]
> **Apply…** on a theme in Add a theme makes it live straight away. It first says exactly what changes: the theme's header, footer and
> product page are added as new pages and used (yours stay, unchosen), and your store's custom CSS is replaced. Your look from before
> stays in your theme library.

### Premium themes

A theme marked *Premium* has a price, shown before you agree to it. Adding one to try costs nothing. Before your store opens, nothing is
paid until it opens with the theme live; once it is open, the price is charged once to your Kaizena Flow billing when you switch to it.

### Switch at a set time

On a theme's **Customise** page, **Make it live later** switches to it at a time on your store's clock, and can put the theme before
back at another. It works once your store is open. A premium theme your store does not have yet must be made live by hand once first.

## More options

### Theme updates

When Kaizena Flow publishes a new version of a theme you installed, it can update by itself or wait for you: choose under **How each
theme is updated**. An update keeps your own settings and CSS. A header, footer or product page you edited stays yours, with the new one
added as a draft beside it. **Update now** updates one that waits, and an update can be undone for a while.

### Let pages follow the theme

Pages made before themes could be switched may repeat the theme's settings, such as a heading's size. **Count the settings**, then
**Make pages follow the theme**: every page looks exactly as before, and from then on follows the theme you switch to. Settings that
differ from the theme are yours and stay.

### Your own CSS

In the page builder, **Custom CSS** in the bar at the foot opens a panel with two tabs:

- **This page**: only on this page, saved with it and live when you publish it.
- **Global**: on every page of your store, its header and footer too. It goes live when you press **Save global CSS**, not with the page.

Style parts by the ids and classes you give them under **Advanced** in their settings. Pictures and fonts can come only from your own
site or media library. Each CSS holds up to 50,000 characters. **Versions** in the panel keeps your last 50 saves of the global CSS.

On your live site, signed in, **Custom CSS** beside **Edit text** opens the same panel over the page.

An installed theme that is not live keeps its own CSS, under **Its own CSS** on its Customise page.

### Share your look as a theme

**Share this look as a theme** offers your store's look to other Kaizena Flow stores, free, in the theme store. Kaizena Flow reviews it
first. Your products are never shared, and your pages only if you choose to send them.
