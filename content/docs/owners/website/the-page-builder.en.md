## Start a page

Open **Website** in the menu and choose [Pages](/admin/{store}/pages). Press **New page** and type a title under **Title and address**.
The page's address is made from the title as you type; change it if you like.

A new page is a draft: visitors see nothing of it until you publish it, so save as often as you like on the way.

> [!TIP]
> **Create with AI**, on the Pages list, asks you about the page you want, then writes and builds it as a draft for you to change.

## How a page is built

A page is rows. A row holds columns side by side, and each column holds components: a text, a heading, a picture, a button, a grid of
your products and more. On phones, a row's columns stack one under another.

The builder has three parts:

- **Building blocks** on the left, with the tabs **Components**, **Rows**, **Saved**, **Templates** and **Checks**.
- The canvas in the middle: your page as visitors will see it.
- The page's settings on the right: title and address, picture, search and sharing.

### Add a row

Open **Rows** and drag a layout onto the page: one to six columns, or columns with a sidebar. Or press the small **+** in the corner of a
layout's tile to add it at the end of the page.

### Add a component

Open **Components** and drag one into a column. Or press the small **+** in the corner of its tile: it goes at the end of the column you
last worked in, or in a new row at the end of the page if you have not worked in one yet. Its settings open at once, ready to fill in.

### Change, move and delete

Point at a row, a column or a component, or tap it, and its tools appear: a handle to drag it, the wrench (**Settings**), **Duplicate**
and **Delete**. Deleting anything with text asks first.

The wrench opens the part's settings in four tabs: **General**, **Style**, **Motion** and **Advanced**.

### Edit words where they stand

Press a heading or a text once on the canvas and type: it looks as it will on your site. **Escape** puts the words back, and pressing
elsewhere ends it. Press twice, or use the wrench, to open its settings instead.

## Save and publish

The bar at the foot of the builder has what you need:

- **Save draft** keeps your work. Visitors still see the page as it was last published.
- **Publish** puts the page on your site. Once it is published, the button reads **Publish changes**.
- **Preview draft** opens the draft as your site will show it; **View page** opens the live page.
- **Unpublish** takes the page off the site. It is a draft again.

The Pages list shows each page as *Draft*, *Published* or *Published, draft has changes*.

When you change the address of a published page, publishing makes the old address lead to the new one for good, so links and search
results keep working.

If the **Checks** tab finds a problem that blocks publishing, you are asked to **Fix first** or **Publish anyway**. Publishing anyway is
written in the activity log.

### Publish later

Once the page is saved, press **Schedule** beside Publish. Choose a **Day** and a **Time** on your store's clock, and press **Schedule**:
the draft as it is then is published. For a sale that ends, open **Put the version before back later** first and choose when the page
goes back to how it was. A schedule waits while your store is not open. **Cancel the schedule** stops it.

### Show a draft to someone

**Share** makes a link that shows the draft to anyone who has it, without signing in, for 1, 7 or 30 days. Search engines never see it.
Copy the link when it is made: it is shown only once. **Revoke** stops it at once.

## Undo, redo and versions

The arrows beside Publish are **Undo** and **Redo**, as are Ctrl+Z and Ctrl+Shift+Z (⌘ on a Mac). They go back and forward up to 100
steps.

**Versions** lists what the page was at each save, each publish and each change made on the live site, the newest 50. Press **Use this
version** to bring one into the builder as unsaved changes: nothing changes on your site until you save or publish, and **Undo** takes
it back. **Compare with the editor** says what differs first.

## Show a part only for a while

Every row, column and component has **Show from** and **Show until** at the foot of its **General** tab, in your store's time zone.
Leave one empty for no limit. Outside those times the part is left out of the page, and the canvas marks it *Scheduled*. **Clear the
dates** removes them.

## Choose your front page

On the Pages list, under **Front page**, choose what **Your store opens with**: **All products**, or one of your published pages. Press
**Save**. If that page is unpublished later, shoppers see your products until you publish it again.

To start your front page from a demo store's, use **Home pages from the demo stores** on the same list and press **Use this home page**.
It follows your theme; its words and pictures are the demo store's until you change them. **Put back your front page** brings back the
one you had.

## More options

### Use a part again

- **Save a part.** Open a row's, column's or component's wrench and choose **Save as…**. It is listed under **Saved**, ready to drag
  onto any page. A global one stays the same on every page that uses it: change it in one place and it changes everywhere.
- **Templates.** The **Templates** tab lists the templates you have switched on: Kaizena Flow's own, and those shared from your other
  stores or by other store owners. Press **Preview** to look first, or **Use** to add a copy. **Browse templates** switches more on.
- **A whole page's layout.** **Save as template**, in the bar, keeps the page's rows. Using a page layout asks whether to replace your
  rows or add it after them.
- **Duplicate**, in the bar or on the Pages list, makes a copy titled *Copy of …* as an unpublished draft.

### Screen sizes

Press **Responsive** to edit at a screen size: **Small** (phones), **Medium**, **Large** or **Extra large**. A setting you change then
holds at that size and the smaller ones. To hide a part at a size, open its wrench, then **Advanced**, **Visibility**.

### Languages and countries

In a store with more than one language, a page is built once in the main language. Choose another under **Language** in the page's
settings to translate its words; **Translate with AI** fills in what is missing. A text you do not translate shows in the main language.

In a store that sells in more than one country, **Only some countries** under **Countries** keeps the page to the countries you tick.
Elsewhere it is not shown, listed or found.

### Search engines and sharing

Under **Search and sharing**, give the page its own title and description for search results; empty fields use the title and the start
of the text. **Search engines** and **AI assistants** let them read the page, or ask them to stay away. The store as a whole is set up
under [SEO & Reach](/docs/owners/website/seo-redirects-and-domains).

### Special pages and All products

Under **Special pages** on the Pages list, your blog page, search page, 404 page and working pages such as the cart can be built in the
builder too. Choose one of your published pages for each, or press **Start from a new page**: it makes a page like the standard one,
publishes it in place and opens it. Under **All products page**, one of your pages can take the place of the standard product list.

### Edit text on your live site

Signed in, open one of your published pages on your site and press **Edit text** beside **Edit page**. Press a heading or a text, type,
then press **Save**. Headers, footers, translations and parts shared with other pages are changed in the builder.
