## Change a menu

A menu is a list of links. Your store starts with one for its header and one for its footer, and you can make as many more as you like.

1. Open **Website** in the menu and choose [Menus](/admin/{store}/menus).
2. Under **Select a menu to edit:**, choose the menu. To make a new one, press **create a new menu**, give it a **Menu name** and press
   **Create menu**.
3. Under **Add menu items**, open a list (**Pages**, **Products**, **Product categories**, **Blog articles** and others), tick what you
   want and press **Add to menu**. **Store links** has your front page, All products, the blog, My account and the cart.
   **Custom link** takes any **Web address** with its **Link text**.
4. Under **Menu structure**, drag the links into the order you want. Drag a link to the right to put it under the link above it.
5. Press **Save menu**. Your site shows it at once.

To change a link's words, open it with its arrow and type its **Navigation label**, in each of your store's languages. There you can also
have it **Open the link in a new tab**, move it with the **Move** buttons, or **Remove** it.

A menu holds up to 100 links, three levels deep. A link to a page, article, category or tag shows only while it is published, and follows
it when its address changes.

### Where a menu shows

A menu shows where you choose it: in the standard header or footer (see [Logo, icon and the standard menus](#logo-icon-and-the-standard-menus)),
or wherever you place a **Menu** component in a page, header or footer. **Where it shows**, in the menu editor, lists those places.

**Delete menu** asks first, and says where the menu is used: those places then show no menu.

### A mega menu

Open a top link and tick **Mega menu**: the links under it open side by side across the page's width, in **Columns**, each with a
**Picture** shown above it. A landscape picture (4:3) works best. On phones they are listed as usual.

## Logo, icon and the standard menus

Open **Settings** in the menu and choose [Header and footer](/admin/{store}/settings/navigation), under Site.

- **Logo**: shown in the header instead of your store's name, up to 40 pixels high. A wide PNG with a transparent background works best.
  Without one, the header shows the name.
- **Logo for dark backgrounds**: a light version, shown where the background is dark, such as a black header or dark mode.
- **Icon (favicon)**: shown in browser tabs, bookmarks and on phones' home screens. A square picture of at least 512 pixels works best:
  a simple mark rather than the whole logo. Without one, Kaizena Flow's icon is shown.
- **Menus**: which menu the standard header shows (**Main menu**) and which the standard footer shows (**Footer menu**).

Press **Save**. **View the store** opens your store to look.

The standard header always has the cart, My account and the country choice, so your menu needs no links to them. On phones, the main menu
slides out from a menu button.

The footer always shows your business details, as the law requires: your company name, organisation number, address and contact email
from [Company](/admin/{store}/settings/company), under Settings.

## Build a header or footer of your own

The standard header and footer suit most stores. For a layout of your own:

1. Open **Website** and choose [Headers](/admin/{store}/headers) or [Footers](/admin/{store}/footers). Press **New header** or
   **New footer**: it starts as a copy of the standard one.
2. Build it in the page builder. Besides the usual components, **The site** has your logo, menus, search, the cart, the country choice
   and more; a footer always keeps its business details and the cookies link.
3. Press **Publish**.
4. Back on the list, choose it under **The header in use** (or **The footer in use**) and press **Save**.

Until you choose one, the standard header and footer are shown. If you unpublish the one in use, the standard one shows until you
publish it again. Its logo is the one under Header and footer, and its Menu components show the menus you make under Menus.

> [!TIP]
> A header can lie over the top of a page whose first row has a picture, a video or a colour, see-through until the visitor scrolls. In
> the header's settings, under **Over the page**, choose **Where the header goes**: over every page, over the front page, or over pages in
> chosen categories or tags.
