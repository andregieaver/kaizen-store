## Switch reviews on

Reviews are off until you switch them on: open **Settings**, choose [Features](/admin/{store}/settings/features) and switch on **Product
reviews**.

If you switch them off later, reviews stop showing and customers cannot write new ones. Every review is kept for when you switch them on
again.

## Who can write a review

Only people who bought a product in your store and received it. That makes every review written in your store a **Verified purchase**. A
buyer can review:

- goods, once the order is delivered or collected;
- a download, once it is paid;
- an appointment, a stay or a rental, once it has ended and the customer came;

within a year of receiving it. Gift cards are not reviewed.

Buyers write from **Reviews** in My account, or from **Write a review** beside an item on their order's page: stars from one to five, a
title and text if they like, the name to show, and up to three pictures. One buyer writes one review of a product; buying it again lets
them change it, not add a second.

Nobody else can write a review, and staff cannot change a review's words or stars. Never offer anything in return for a review: Kaizena
Flow has no way to reward one, and the product page says no one is rewarded.

## Check new reviews

1. Open **Products** in the menu and choose [Reviews](/admin/{store}/reviews). Reviews **Waiting to be checked** are first.
2. Read each one, with its stars, words, product, and the order it came from.
3. Press **Publish** to show it, or **Hide** to keep it off your site. To hide one, choose **Why it is not shown**: *Not about the
   product*, *Offensive or hateful*, *Holds personal details*, *Spam or advertising* or *Another reason*.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Hiding a review because it is negative is not allowed. Publish the good and the bad alike, and answer a complaint instead.

To answer a review, write under **Your answer, shown under the review** and press **Save the answer**. You can change or take away an
answer later. A picture can be hidden on its own with **Hide picture**, leaving the review as it is.

Search the list by product or words, and filter it by state or stars.

### Check first, or publish at once

Under **Checking new reviews**, choose:

- **Check each one first** (the default): a new or edited review waits on this page until you publish it;
- **Publish at once**: reviews show as soon as they are written, and you can hide one after.

The choice counts for reviews written or edited from then on.

## What shoppers see

Once a product has a published review, its page shows the stars and the average under the title, and the reviews further down: the
newest ten, with *Show more reviews* for the rest, each with its stars, words, name, date and the variant bought. Before then, nothing
shows. With them, *How reviews are checked* tells shoppers who can write and why a review may not be shown. Search engines get the
average and the count too.

Reviews are shown in the words and language they were written in, never translated. If you made your own product layout, add the parts
**Rating** and **Reviews** to it.

## Ask buyers for a review

Under **Ask buyers for a review**, tick **Send review requests** and choose the **Days after receipt**: from 3 to 60 days, 14 at first.
Each order then gets one email, that many days after everything in it was received, with a link to review it without signing in.

Requests go only to buyers on your [subscriber list](/docs/owners/marketing/email-marketing), because asking for a review is marketing.
They count as marketing emails on your plan. Orders received before you switch this on are not asked. Every buyer can still review from
My account.

## Import and export reviews

To bring in reviews from another service, such as Shopify's Product Reviews, Judge.me or Trustpilot:

1. On the Reviews page, under **Import and export**, press **Import reviews**.
2. Choose a CSV file with the columns `product_handle`, `rating`, `author` and `date`, and `title`, `body` and `source` if you have
   them: at most 10,000 reviews a file. Exports from Shopify's Product Reviews and Judge.me are read as they are.
3. Say **Where they were written**, for rows without a source, and **When they show**: **Show them at once** or **Let me check each one
   first**.
4. Tick **Only check the file, import nothing** and press **Check the file** first. Then untick it and press **Import**.

Each row finds your product by its handle. Imported reviews show *Imported from …* and are never marked as verified purchases. A file
imported twice adds nothing the second time.

**Download as CSV** gives you your published and waiting reviews.

## Reviews and personal data

A review is its writer's personal data: it is in their data export, and erasing the customer deletes their reviews ([Personal
data](/docs/owners/customers/personal-data)).
