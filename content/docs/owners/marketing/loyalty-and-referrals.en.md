## Bonus credits

Signed-in customers earn credits on what they pay, and use them as a price reduction on a later order. A credit is money in your
store's main currency: one credit is worth one krone, euro or whichever your currency is.

### Switch it on

1. Open **Settings**, choose [Features](/admin/{store}/settings/features) and switch on **Bonus program**.
2. Open **Marketing** and choose [Bonus credits](/admin/{store}/bonus).
3. Tick **The bonus program is on**, check the settings below, and press **Save**.

Only the store's owners can change the program. **What shoppers will read** shows your rules as your customers will read them.

Existing customers start at zero: only orders paid from then on earn credits. Guests earn nothing.

### The settings

- **Credits back**: the percentage of what the customer pays online for goods, 5 % at first. Shipping, anything paid at a venue and the
  credits they used do not count, and a free product earns nothing.
- **Wait before credits can be used**: the return period, 14 days at first. Credits from an order wait this long after it is paid, so a
  return does not leave you paying out credits. Use 0 to allow use at once.
- **The most of an order's goods credits can pay**, and **Least credits to use at once**.
- **Credits that are not used expire**, after the number of months you choose. Off, credits last for ever. The oldest are used and
  expire first, and customers get a reminder email before theirs run out.

Credits come off goods only, never shipping, and last of all: after campaigns, group discounts and codes. They always leave something to
pay online. A refund takes back what the refunded part of an order earned.

**Credits at a glance** shows what your store owes in credits now, what is still waiting, and what was earned, used and expired in the
last 30 days. A customer's own credits and their history are on their customer page, where **Adjust credits** adds or takes away
credits, with a reason the history keeps.

If you switch the program off, customers stop earning and using credits. Their credits are kept, and do not expire, until you switch it
on again.

### Levels and extras

All of these are optional, under **Levels and extras**:

- **Levels**: up to four, each with a **Name**, the amount a customer has spent on goods in the last 12 months, less refunds, from which
  it applies, and a higher percentage **Back**. The highest level reached applies, and it moves down by itself as the year passes. My
  account tells customers their level and what more the next one needs.
- **Credits for opening an account**: given once, usable at once.
- **Credits on a customer's birthday**: given once a year on the birthday a customer gave on My account (month and day only), at the
  earliest 30 days after they gave it.

### Rewards

Under **Rewards**, press **Add a reward** to let customers spend their credits on something besides money off. Give it a **Name
customers see**, a **Cost in credits** and **What it gives**: **Free shipping**, **A product free** (one unit) or **An amount off** (per
country). A store can have up to ten.

A signed-in customer chooses a reward on My account and gets a code of their own, to use once. Credits spent on a reward are not given
back if the code is never used. The code works as long as credits do, or a year when credits never expire.

> [!NOTE]
> Credits are never given for a review or for signing up to your emails. Talk to your accountant about how to book credits that are
> still unused.

## The referral program

Customers share a link. A friend's first order gets a welcome discount, and the customer who shared the link earns bonus credits. It
needs the bonus program on.

1. Open **Settings**, choose [Features](/admin/{store}/settings/features) and switch on **Referral program**.
2. Open **Marketing** and choose [Referral program](/admin/{store}/affiliates).
3. Tick **The referral program is on**, check the settings, and press **Save**.

Every signed-in customer gets a link the first time they open **Refer a friend** in My account; nobody has to be approved. The friend
must be signed in to get the discount, and only on their first paid order. Nobody can refer themselves, and a customer who has ordered
before does not count as a friend. A refund or a cancellation takes back the credits it earned.

The settings:

- **The friend's welcome discount**: the **Discount on the goods in their first order**, 10 % at first, and **The most it takes off**.
  It comes off after campaigns and group discounts, before codes and bonus credits.
- **What the customer who shared the link earns**: **Bonus credits** as a percentage of what the friend pays online for goods, and
  whether **Only the friend's first orders earn credits**.
- **The most one customer earns a month**, if you want a limit.
- **How long a visitor's link is remembered**, in days. A link is remembered only for visitors who allow marketing cookies, so your store
  asks about cookies once the program is on.

**Referrers** lists the customers with a link; block one who abuses it, with a reason. **Orders through links** shows each order that
came through a link, its welcome discount and the credits it earned. A customer who shares a link never sees who their friends are.

## Creators

Creators are people with a channel of their own who sell your goods with a code and a link of their own, and earn a commission. It
needs only the online shop.

1. Open **Marketing** and choose [Creators](/admin/{store}/creators).
2. Tick **Creators can apply and earn**, set the **Commission, percent of goods without VAT** (at most 50 %), and press **Save**. Under
   **Categories with their own commission**, a category can earn more.
3. People apply from My account, under **Creator programme**. **Approve** or **Decline** each application; **Stop** a creator later if
   you need to.

A creator earns their share of what their paid orders pay for goods, without VAT, in each order's own currency. A refund or a
cancellation takes it back. Kaizena Flow moves no money: you pay creators yourself, press **Record a payout** for each payment, and
**Download payouts (CSV)** for your bookkeeping. The creator's own page never names a buyer.

Only an owner can change the programme, a creator's own commission and the payouts.
