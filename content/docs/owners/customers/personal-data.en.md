## When someone asks

People may ask what your store holds about them, or ask for it to be erased. The law asks you to answer within one month of receiving
the request. Kaizena Flow keeps each request with its clock under **Customers**, [Privacy requests](/admin/{store}/privacy).

> [!NOTE]
> Kaizena Flow's texts on this help you keep the deadline. They are not legal advice.

### A shopper with an account does it themselves

Under **My account**, **Settings**, **Your data**, a signed-in shopper can press **Download my data** or **Continue to deletion**. Either needs a
sign-in from the last ten minutes, with a code sent to their email or their password. Their request shows in your log as *Asked on the
site*, already answered.

### A request that comes by email, post or phone

1. Open **Customers** in the menu, choose **Privacy requests** and press **Log a request**.
2. Choose what the person asks for: **A copy of their data (export)** or **Their data erased**.
3. Type **Their email address**: the address they wrote from.
4. Set **Date received**. The month runs from the day the request arrived, so set an earlier day if it came before today.
5. Press **Log the request**.

Then open the request and do what the person asked: **Download data** answers a request for a copy, **Erase personal data** a request
to erase.

## Keep the deadline

The list shows each open request with the day it is due and the days left, in red once it is overdue. Filter by **Open**,
**Answered** or **All**.

- **The due day** is the same date in the next month, or the last day of that month when it has no such date: a request received on
  31 January is due on the last day of February. A weekend or a holiday does not move it.
- **Owners are emailed** seven days before a request is due, and once more if it becomes overdue.
- **Need more time?** Press **Extend the answer**, write why, and press **Extend and tell the person**. You may extend once, before the
  due day, by up to two further months. The person is emailed your reason.
- **Not sure who is asking?** Reply to the address you already have and ask for more. **Note that we are waiting** records the date,
  but it does not stop the clock.

## Send a copy of someone's data

Press **Download data** on the request, or on the **Privacy** card of the customer's page. You get one file with what the store holds:
their account, addresses, orders, invoices, returns, subscriptions, wishlists, emails, carts and more.

> [!WARNING]
> The file includes the team's notes about the customer, because they are personal data. Read them first. The file is never emailed:
> send it to the person yourself, by a channel you trust.

## Erase someone's data

Press **Erase personal data**. Erasing takes two steps.

1. **Read what will happen.** A table shows each kind of data the store holds about the person and what happens to it: *Deleted*,
   *Made anonymous*, *Kept restricted* until a date, or *Kept*, each with why. Below it are what else happens and anything to check
   first, for example when the email is also a member of your team.
2. **Confirm.** Type the customer's email address (or ERASE for a customer with none) and press **Confirm**.

What erasing does:

- **The account goes**, with the name, phone, address, password and sign-ins, wishlists, carts, the contents of emails sent and forms
  sent in.
- **Sales stay in your accounts.** Orders the bookkeeping law requires you to keep are cut loose from the person and kept restricted:
  use them only for the accounts. When the bookkeeping period ends, their name, address and email are removed. Nothing is deleted from
  your accounts.
- **Subscriptions are cancelled now**, without a refund. Saved cards are detached, bonus credits are lost, and orders still waiting for
  payment are cancelled. Paid orders and returns that are open continue.
- **The email opt-out is kept**, so the address is not written to again.

Erasing cannot be undone. The person is emailed a confirmation, and the store's owners are told. Stripe's own customer and payment
records on your Stripe account are not deleted here.

## More options

### Close or refuse a request

- **Close as "no data held"**, when the store holds nothing about the person. Tell them.
- **Refuse the request**: choose a reason, add a note if you like, and press **Refuse and tell the person**. They are emailed the
  reason and their right to complain to the supervisory authority.
- **Cancel the request**, when it was logged by mistake. It stays in the log as cancelled: a request is never deleted.

### Who can do this

Anyone whose role can view **Customers** sees the requests and the **Privacy** card. Logging, downloading, erasing and answering need a
role that can change **Customers**. See [Staff and permissions](/docs/owners/team-and-settings/staff-and-permissions).

### Cookie consent

Your store asks visitors about cookies only when it sets optional ones. With necessary cookies alone it shows no banner, only a
cookie page linked from the footer. Tracking tools such as Google Analytics, Google Tag Manager and Meta Pixel load only after a
visitor allows them, and switching one on brings the banner by itself. The banner offers *Reject all* and *Accept all* side by side,
and a choice per kind of cookie. Each choice is kept for 12 months as proof, without a name.

Under **Settings**, [Cookies and tracking](/admin/{store}/settings/cookies), you find the tracking tools, the choices visitors made and
the weekly cookie scan: a real browser opens your pages and lists every cookie it finds. Press **Scan now** after a change.
