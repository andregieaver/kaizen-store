> [!WARNING]
> This is a draft, version 2026-11-04, written for legal review. It will be replaced by the reviewed terms before any app is listed in a
> directory.

By turning on developer mode you agree to these terms for every app you make.

## Your app and your credentials

- You keep your client secret and events secret private, and renew them at once if anyone else may have seen them.
- You are responsible for what your app does with the tokens a store gives it.
- The name, logo and description you give are true, and do not suggest that your app is made, checked or endorsed by Kaizena Flow.

## What your app may do in a store

- Your app acts in a store only as far as its install and the role of the person who installed it allow, and only for the purpose you
  describe.
- It reads, keeps and uses a store's data, and the personal data of the store's customers, only to provide what the store installed it
  for, and keeps it no longer than that needs.
- It does not sell that data, combine it with data from other stores, or use it to contact the store's customers on its own account.

## When a store removes it

- When a store removes your app, its tokens stop working at once. You delete what you hold of that store's data within the time the data
  processing terms set.
- When a person is erased, you delete what you hold of them.

## Kaizena Flow's part

- Kaizena Flow may block an app, end its installs or turn off developer mode for an account that breaks these terms or puts stores or
  their customers at risk.
- Kaizena Flow may change these terms; you are asked to accept a new version before you make or change an app.
